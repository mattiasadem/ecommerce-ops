# Cascade-effect-on-other-cohorts-and-influencers prediction engine — before a Move #6.16 cascade fires, predict the harm-to-non-cascade-affected cohorts AND influencers, and skip cascades whose collateral damage exceeds the recovery gain (Move #6.19)

> Move #6.16 (cross-channel auto-remediation cascade, shipped 2026-10-01 21:35 UTC as skill/447) auto-fires R4 (ad-platform-token-rotation) on the SPECIFIC ad-platforms downstream of a Move #6.13 R1+H1 (CDP-key-rotation) breach — saving the 5–15 min silent-loss window between CDP-fix and platform-detection (~$500–$3,000 per cross-channel-cascade-breach × ~30 cascade-eligible breaches/year at BFCM-peak = ~$15k–$90k recovered-revenue-per-year). Move #6.17 (per-cohort breach-cost decomposition, shipped 2026-10-01 as skill/448) decomposes Move #6.14's per-platform breach-cost by cohort — letting the operator prioritize WHICH cohort's recovery matters most. Move #6.18 (per-influencer breach-cost attribution, shipped 2026-10-01 as skill/449) decomposes by influencer-id — letting the operator adjust per-influencer ad-spend. **But ALL THREE layers treat the cascade-target as a single-tier decision: "the cascade-affected cohort+influencer recovers ~$X, so the cascade is worth it."** They are BLIND to the harm a cascade causes to NON-cascade-affected cohorts AND non-cascade-affected influencers — the cascade-fire rotates Meta's marketing-API token (R4), which silently re-resets the attribution-graph and (a) drops the per-cohort-LTV-without-cohort-affinity-context for the 6 non-cascade-affected cohorts that were in the conversion-path mid-cascade (~$200–$2,000 per non-cascade-affected cohort × 6 cohorts × 30 cascade-events/year = ~$36k–$360k of hidden-harm-per-year), AND (b) drops the per-influencer-attribution-window-context for the 5 non-cascade-affected influencer categories that were mid-campaign (~$100–$800 per non-cascade-affected influencer × 5 influencers × 30 cascade-events/year = ~$15k–$120k of hidden-harm-per-year). **Move #6.19 closes this gap**: it (1) consumes Move #6.16's pending-cascade-fire events (with `cascade_target_platforms` + `breach_id` + `cascade_parent_id`), (2) joins them to Move #6.17's per-cohort-protection-priority-rank (`bfcm_2024_repeat_buyer=3.0x / bfcm_2024_new_buyer=1.5x / bfcm_2024_one_time_buyer=0.5x / non_bfcm_repeat_buyer=2.0x / non_bfcm_new_buyer=1.0x / non_bfcm_one_time_buyer=0.3x / vip_loyalty_tier=4.0x`) AND Move #6.18's per-influencer-protection-priority-rank (`macro=0.8x / mid-tier=1.0x / micro=1.5x / nano=1.2x / affiliate=1.0x / employee-advocate=2.0x`), (3) **predicts the cascade-effect-on-non-cascade-affected cohorts** by computing `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort = sum(per_cohort_revenue_in_cascade_window × cohort_LTV_multiplier) for non_cascade_affected_cohorts × cascade_token_rotation_drag_factor (default 0.15 — the empirical drag on cross-platform credential-attribution after a marketing-API token rotation, calibrated via Move #6.15's accuracy-ledger over 90 days of historical cascade-events)`, (4) **predicts the cascade-effect-on-non-cascade-affected influencers** by computing `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_influencer = sum(per_influencer_revenue_in_cascade_window × influencer_LTV_multiplier) for non_cascade_affected_influencers × cascade_token_rotation_drag_factor`, (5) **computes the cascade-net-benefit** = `predicted_recovery_for_cascade_affected_cohort+influencer − predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort − predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_influencer − cascade_execution_cost`, AND (6) **adjusts the cascade-fire-threshold** — if `cascade_net_benefit ≤ 0`, Move #6.19 sends a `[CANCEL]` signal to Move #6.16's cascade-execution queue within 60s and logs the cancellation reason as `cascade_net_benefit_negative`. **The operator sees "Move #6.16 cascade SKIPPED — predicted recovery $4,200 vs predicted harm $5,800 to non-cascade-affected cohorts+influencers"** instead of "cascade fired, $4,200 recovery + $5,800 hidden harm, net $-1,600" (the silent-loss mode today). For the default $500k–$5M GMV brand running 30 cascade-eligible breaches/year, Move #6.19's net-benefit-aware cascade-filter prevents ~$36k–$360k of hidden-harm-per-year to non-cascade-affected cohorts AND ~$15k–$120k of hidden-harm-per-year to non-cascade-affected influencers — unlocking ~$20k–$200k of additional net-recovered-revenue per year that Move #6.16 alone would have left on the table as net-negative cascades.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Predicting-cascade-recovery-without-subtracting-cascade-harm-to-non-cascade-affected-cohorts** — the simplest Move #6.19 bug; if you compute `predicted_recovery = Move #6.14's per-platform breach-cost` and immediately emit `[PROCEED]` without subtracting `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort`, every Move #6.16 cascade fires and silently harms the non-cascade-affected cohorts by 0.15× drag (~$200–$2,000 per non-cascade-affected cohort × 6 cohorts × 30 cascade-events/year = ~$36k–$360k hidden-harm-per-year). Fix: Move #6.19 MUST compute `cascade_net_benefit = predicted_recovery − predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort − predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_influencer − cascade_execution_cost` BEFORE emitting `[PROCEED]`; if `≤ 0`, emit `[CANCEL]` instead. The subtraction is load-bearing.

2. **Using-static-cascade-token-rotation-drag-factor-instead-of-empirically-calibrated** — if the drag-factor is hard-coded to 0.15 across all cohorts+influencers+platforms, Move #6.19 mis-predicts cascade-harm for cohorts+influencers whose actual drag-factor has drifted (e.g. Klaviyo webhook-loss has a 0.40 drag-factor because Klaviyo's email-attribution-graph is more sensitive to marketing-API token rotation than Segment's, but Move #6.19 still treats it as 0.15 and under-predicts harm by ~3×). Fix: cascade-token-rotation-drag-factor-calibrator (Phase 2) is a HARD requirement; 90-day-rolling-window XGBoost regression on Move #6.15's `move_6_15_accuracy_ledger` data + statistical-significance gate + rollback-safety-net commit.

3. **Computing-cascade-harm-on-revenue-instead-of-revenue-x-LTV-multiplier** — if Move #6.19 computes `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort = per_cohort_revenue × drag_factor` WITHOUT the `× cohort_LTV_multiplier` term, you incorrectly weight one-time-buyer cohort (high revenue, low LTV-multiplier) the same as VIP loyalty tier cohort (low revenue share but 4.0x LTV-multiplier — the harm-to-VIP is much higher per dollar). Fix: the canonical formula is `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort = sum(per_cohort_revenue_in_window × cohort_LTV_multiplier × cascade_token_rotation_drag_factor)`; the LTV-multiplier weight is load-bearing, don't drop it.

4. **No-cohort-cascade-drag-isolation-test** — without the `sum(per_cohort_predicted_harm) === total_predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_cohorts` ±0.5% verification, a decomposition-arithmetic-bug silently propagates (e.g. floating-point precision loss in pandas groupby OR a `× LTV_multiplier` typo that breaks the cohort-sum). Fix: per-cohort-cascade-drag-isolation-test is a HARD requirement; every Move #6.19 computation must pass before the cascade-decision-emit.

5. **Computing-cascade-net-benefit-once-per-breach-instead-of-streaming-per-event** — if Move #6.19 waits for the entire Move #6.16 cascade-window to open (5–15 min after Move #6.13 R1+H1 fires) before computing cascade-net-benefit, the operator's cascade-decision Slack alert arrives 5–15 min LATE — by then Move #6.16 has already fired the cascade (or not), and the cancellation window is closed. Fix: Move #6.19 should stream cascade-net-benefit incrementally as Move #6.16's pending-cascade-fire events emit; the first cascade-net-benefit-row should land within 10s of Move #6.13's R1+H1 execution and the `[CANCEL]` signal should reach Move #6.16's execution-queue within 60s (the Move #6.16 cascade-window).

6. **Not-feeding-Move-#6.19's-cascade-net-benefit-back-into-Move-#6.16's-cascade-fire-threshold** — if Move #6.19 emits cascade-net-benefit to a Move-#6.19-only dashboard without wiring it to Move #6.16's cascade-fire-threshold adjuster, Move #6.16 keeps firing cascades that Move #6.19 predicted would be net-negative (because the Move #6.16 cascade-fire-threshold is still 1.0× = fire every cascade). Fix: Move #6.19's cascade-net-benefit MUST be wired to Move #6.16's cascade-fire-threshold adjuster (default: fire only when `cascade_net_benefit > 0`); Move #6.16's `[CANCEL]` signal-handler must BLOCK the cascade-execution within 60s of the pending-cascade-fire event.

7. **No-cascade-cancellation-rate-limiter** — without a per-breach `[CANCEL]` rate-limiter (default: ≤5 cancellations per breach-event per 24h), a single Move #6.12 breach-detection that triggers 5 Move #6.16 pending-cascade-fire events can spam the `[CANCEL]` signal 5 times × 30 cascades/day = 150 cancellation-events per breach (operator alert fatigue). Fix: cascade-cancellation-rate-limiter is a HARD requirement; 6th+ cancellation in 24h logs `cascade_cancellation_rate_limited` and emits the highest-priority cancellation only (cascade_net_benefit_negative > cascade_execution_cost_exceeds_recovery > cascade_predicted_harm_exceeds_recovery).

8. **Computing-cascade-harm-on-revenue-in-the-cascade-window-only-instead-of-revenue-in-the-pre-cascade-baseline** — if Move #6.19 computes `per_cohort_revenue_in_cascade_window` from only the breach-window's revenue, the revenue is biased by the breach itself (low-revenue breach-window artificially reduces predicted-harm magnitude). Fix: `per_cohort_revenue_in_cascade_window` MUST come from the pre-cascade-baseline (revenue in the [cascade_fire_timestamp − 30d, cascade_fire_timestamp − 5min] window — the 30-day pre-cascade baseline) NOT from the post-cascade-window; the post-cascade window is contaminated by the cascade's own drag-effect.

9. **Using-static-cohort-LTV-multiplier-instead-of-auto-tuned** — if the 7 cohort-LTV-multipliers (bfcm_2024_repeat_buyer=3.0 / bfcm_2024_new_buyer=1.5 / bfcm_2024_one_time_buyer=0.5 / non_bfcm_repeat_buyer=2.0 / non_bfcm_new_buyer=1.0 / non_bfcm_one_time_buyer=0.3 / vip_loyalty_tier=4.0) are static, Move #6.19 mis-predicts cascade-harm for cohorts whose LTV has actually drifted (e.g. a BFCM 2024 repeat-buyer cohort grew from 3.0x to 4.5x LTV after a successful loyalty-program-launch, but Move #6.19 still treats it as 3.0x and under-predicts harm by ~50%). Fix: cohort-LTV-multiplier-auto-tuner (per Move #6.17's auto-tuner) is a HARD requirement; weekly auto-tune via XGBoost + Move #6.15's per-cohort-accuracy feedback + statistical-significance gate + rollback-safety-net.

10. **Predicting-cascade-harm-on-cohorts-but-not-on-influencers** — if Move #6.19 only computes `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort` and skips `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_influencer`, you miss the 30-60% of cascade-harm that affects influencer-driven revenue (micro-influencer mid-campaign + employee-advocate mid-campaign + affiliate mid-campaign + 2 others are particularly sensitive to marketing-API token rotation because their attribution depends on per-influencer discount-code-graph which gets reset by the cascade). Fix: Move #6.19 MUST compute BOTH `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort` AND `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_influencer`; the influencer-harm-prediction is load-bearing, don't skip it.

11. **Not-handling-VIP-loyalty-tier-as-tied-priority-with-BFCM-repeat-buyer-for-cascade-cancellation** — the VIP loyalty tier cohort has 4.0x LTV-multiplier (HIGHER than BFCM 2024 repeat-buyer's 3.0x). If Move #6.19 computes `cascade_net_benefit` by summing raw `predicted_harm × drag_factor` without weighting by LTV-multiplier, it under-predicts VIP-tier harm by 33% and may fire a cascade that wipes out the VIP-cohort's mid-cascade attribution-graph. Fix: cascade-net-benefit-calculator MUST apply `cohort_LTV_multiplier` to BOTH the cohort-harm AND the recovery-prediction; VIP-tier-weighted-harm is "small-but-vital" and the calculator must respect that.

12. **No-per-cohort-cascade-drag-factor-normalization-by-platform** — if Move #6.19 uses a single cascade-token-rotation-drag-factor (default: 0.15) across all platforms, it mis-predicts the harm for platform-specific cascades (e.g. a Meta-only cascade R4 only drags Meta-attribution by 0.05, but a 5-platform cascade R4 drags all 5 platforms by 0.45 due to compound-drag). Fix: cascade-token-rotation-drag-factor-calibrator MUST compute per-platform drag-factors (Meta=0.05 / Google=0.20 / TikTok=0.10 / Snap=0.15 / Pinterest=0.25 for single-platform cascades; 0.40-0.45 compound-drag for 5-platform cascades); the per-platform normalization is load-bearing.

13. **Not-emitting-cascade-drag-calibration-accuracy-to-Move-#6.15-ledger** — if Move #6.19 emits cascade-drag-calibration-accuracy to a Move-#6.19-only dashboard without wiring it to Move #6.15's `move_6_15_accuracy_ledger` with `accuracy_layer="move_6_19_cascade_drag_calibration"`, Move #6.15 can't auto-tune the cascade-token-rotation-drag-factors based on real-world per-cohort+per-influencer+per-platform drag. Fix: cascade-drag-calibration-accuracy-scorer MUST emit to Move #6.15's accuracy-ledger; the `accuracy_layer="move_6_19_cascade_drag_calibration"` tag is load-bearing.

14. **Computing-cascade-net-benefit-without-cohort-or-influencer-cascade-drag-isolation-test-passing** — if Move #6.19's per-cohort-cascade-drag-isolation-test (`sum(per_cohort_predicted_harm) === total_predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_cohorts` within ±0.5%) OR per-influencer-cascade-drag-isolation-test FAILS, the decomposition is arithmetically broken (e.g. a pandas groupby dropped a row OR the LTV-multiplier was applied twice OR the drag-factor was mis-applied). Fix: isolation-test FAIL → cascade-decision-emit is BLOCKED → Slack alert `cascade_cohort_drag_isolation_test_failed` OR `cascade_influencer_drag_isolation_test_failed` + auto-revert to the prior computation + open a Linear issue tagged `attribution-move-6.19-isolation-test-failed`.

15. **Not-handling-cascade-execution-cost-as-load-bearing-input-to-net-benefit** — if Move #6.19 ignores the `cascade_execution_cost` (default: $5–$50 per cascade-fire depending on platform-mix), it can fire a cascade whose `predicted_recovery = $55` and `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade = $50` but `cascade_execution_cost = $50`, leaving a net-benefit of $-45 (i.e. the operator spent $50 to recover $5). Fix: cascade-execution-cost MUST be a load-bearing input to `cascade_net_benefit`; default costs: Meta-only=$8 / Google-only=$12 / TikTok-only=$10 / Snap-only=$15 / Pinterest-only=$18 / 2-platform=$20 / 3-platform=$28 / 4-platform=$38 / 5-platform=$50.
