## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing DC-tier (cadence) with DM-tier (mode).** DC-tier answers HOW OFTEN the disclosure is replayed (real-time / weekly / monthly / quarterly / vintage-onboard); DM-tier answers WHAT FORM the disclosure takes (#ad / Paid-Partnership-Tag / credentials / reactivator / vintage). The two axes are orthogonal and must be carried as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers — collapsing them breaks FTC 16 CFR Part 255.5(b) compliance.
2. **Pitfall — DC5 vintage-disclosed-at-onboard silently skipped because no per-post disclosure surfaces.** A DC5 creator has ONE disclosure at creator-onboard time (the MSA capture); the per-post snippet must NOT auto-inject `#ad` (the disclosure is in the MSA, not in the post). The LLM-judge resonance scorer must down-rank DC5 creators when the cohort expects per-post `#ad` snippets — otherwise the bandit picks DC5 creators whose MSA-disclosure is invisible to the cohort.
3. **Pitfall — DC4 quarterly-disclosure-aggregator accumulates stale disclosures.** A DC4 creator's quarterly-disclosure-aggregator rolls 30 posts into one `#ad`-archive disclosure at quarter-end. In week 1 of a quarter, the disclosure is INVISIBLE per-post — the FTC's "clear and conspicuous" rule applies per-post. **Fix:** DC4 creators must surface a low-confidence interim disclosure (e.g., "see [archive-link] for full quarterly disclosure") on weeks 1–12 of the quarter; auto-inject only on weeks 13+.
4. **Pitfall — DC1 real-time-per-post creators with broken Paid-Partnership-Tag fail FTC posture.** Meta Brand Collabs Manager's Paid-Partnership-Tag is the platform-side disclosure; if the tag fails to render (creator's account is on a personal-creator-account instead of a business-account), the per-post `#ad` in the caption is the ONLY FTC-clean signal. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must verify BOTH the in-caption `#ad` AND the Paid-Partnership-Tag before scoring DC1 × PN1 Instagram-native arms at 1.25x; if either is missing, drop to DC3 monthly-disclosure posture at 1.15x.
5. **Pitfall — DC2 weekly-disclosure-replay collides with creator-content-cadence.** A DC2 creator on a daily-post cadence (7+ posts/week) has 7 disclosure-events/week; the weekly-disclosure-replay-tool must dedupe to 1 disclosure per platform-week, not 7 (Meta's Paid-Partnership-Tag auto-renders once per creator-platform-week). **Fix:** the bandit arm picker must track per-platform-week disclosure-count, not per-post — double-counting inflates DC2 dwell-window-budget by 7x.
6. **Pitfall — DC3 monthly-disclosure-aggregator creators confuse "30-day" with "calendar-month".** A creator who publishes on the 15th of one month through the 14th of the next month has a 30-day rolling disclosure window, not a calendar-month one. **Fix:** the cadence classifier must use rolling-30-day-from-first-post, not calendar-month boundaries — CaptivateA Creator Disclosure Cadence 2026 specifies rolling windows for all DC2-DC4 tiers.
7. **Pitfall — DC5 vintage-disclosed-at-onboard creators fail audit-trail checks after creator-reboard.** When a creator leaves a platform and rejoins (creator-reboard), the MSA-disclosure from the original onboard may not carry over; the new onboard-cycle resets to DC5 vintage-onboard on the new MSA. **Fix:** the disclosure-cadence classifier must RE-CHECK the MSA-disclosure on every creator-reboard event, not assume the historical disclosure persists — otherwise the bandit picks DC5 creators whose MSA-disclosure is stale.
8. **Pitfall — DC4 quarterly-disclosure-aggregator breaks EU UCPD 2026 "clear and conspicuous" rule.** EU UCPD 2026 Art. 7 requires per-post disclosure of paid partnership (commercial communications). DC4's quarterly-archive violates this; only DC1 + DC2 are UCPD-clean. **Fix:** the bandit picker must OVERRIDE DC4 → DC2 for EU cohorts, regardless of the creator's actual DC-tier — otherwise EU-cohort campaigns fail UCPD compliance.
9. **Pitfall — DC1 real-time-per-post creators on TikTok-native with broken TikTok-Creator-Marketplace-tag.** TikTok Creator Marketplace's disclosure-tag is the platform-side disclosure; if the tag fails to render (creator not in active Marketplace deal), the in-caption `#ad` is the only signal — but TikTok's content-policy API may suppress the post. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must verify TikTok-Creator-Marketplace-tag-rendering before scoring DC1 × PN2 TikTok-native arms; if not rendered, drop to DC3 monthly-disclosure posture.
10. **Pitfall — DC5 vintage-disclosed-at-onboard creators with broken MSA-archive-link.** The MSA-disclosure must include an archive-link (URL or PDF) that the cohort can verify; if the link 404s, the disclosure is FTC-non-clean. **Fix:** the bandit picker must verify the MSA-archive-link returns HTTP 200 before scoring DC5 creators at 1.20x — otherwise the cohort cannot audit the disclosure.
11. **Pitfall — DC3 monthly-disclosure-aggregator creators mis-classify affiliate-link vs brand-deal-flat-fee.** Affiliate-link disclosure cadence is typically DC1 (per-post #affiliate + tracking-link-disclosure per FTC 16 CFR Part 255); brand-deal-flat-fee can be DC3 (monthly disclosure). The classifier must NOT collapse the two — affiliate-link creators ALWAYS carry DC1 per-post disclosure. **Fix:** the CMM-tier × DC-tier cross-axis (CMM1 affiliate-link → DC1, CMM2 promo-code → DC2, CMM3 brand-deal-flat-fee → DC3/DC5, CMM4 revenue-share → DC5) is enforced in the LLM-judge scorer.
12. **Pitfall — DC2 weekly-disclosure-replay breaks YouTube-Paid-Promotion-Badge.** YouTube's Paid-Promotion-Badge is a per-video disclosure; YouTube does NOT have a weekly-disclosure-replay tool (unlike Meta Brand Collabs Manager). DC2 × PN3 YouTube-native is therefore NOT a valid cross-axis. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must down-rank DC2 × PN3 YouTube-native arms; auto-route to DC1 (per-video Paid-Promotion-Badge) or DC3 (per-month rollup).
13. **Pitfall — DC4 quarterly-disclosure-aggregator creators on PR4 direct-wire (NET-30 monthly batch) confuse quarterly with monthly.** A PR4 direct-wire creator is paid monthly (NET-30 batch); the disclosure aggregator can be monthly (DC3), not quarterly (DC4). **Fix:** the DC-tier × PR-tier cross-axis must default to PR4 → DC3 (matching the payout-cycle); DC4 × PR4 is a contradiction (the disclosure cannot roll up slower than the payout-cycle).
14. **Pitfall — DC5 vintage-disclosed-at-onboard creators mis-classified as DC1 because of paid-promotion-tag presence.** A creator with a Paid-Partnership-Tag visible on a post but with the disclosure captured only in the MSA is a DC5 creator, not DC1. The presence of a per-post tag is necessary but not sufficient — the disclosure source matters. **Fix:** the cadence classifier must trace the disclosure to EITHER per-post #ad OR MSA-archive-link; if MSA-archive-link is the primary source, classify as DC5 regardless of paid-promotion-tag presence.
15. **Pitfall — DC-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's locale-compliance posture.** EU cohorts require per-post disclosure (UCPD-clean); US cohorts accept quarterly-disclosure-aggregator. A DC4 creator may be FTC-clean but UCPD-non-clean. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply locale-compliance posture BEFORE scoring resonance — DC4 creators score 0 for EU cohorts, regardless of FTC-cleanliness.
