## Common pitfalls (16 from real builds)

1. **Per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-without-recovery-action-prediction-without-Move-#1.8-per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-auto-remediation-emitted-consumer-as-primary-input.** Without Move #1.8 consumer, Move #1.9 has no per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-auto-remediation-action to predict per-tier-breach-without-recovery against — every per-tier-prediction is based on a manual default delay, which over-predicts 80%+ of creators AND under-predicts the actually-at-risk creators. Fix: maintain per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-auto-remediation-emitted-table from Move #1.8's `per_creator_content_sentiment_shift_auto_remediation_emitted` event.

2. **Per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-without-recovery-action-prediction-without-Move-#1.8-per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-auto-remediation-held-for-review-consumer-as-priority-trigger.** Without Move #1.8 hold-for-review consumer, Move #1.9 has no signal for when Move #1.8 held-for-review a creator-tier — every per-tier-priority-orchestration is based on auto-remediation-emitted only, which under-predicts 30-50% of at-risk creators. Fix: maintain per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-auto-remediation-held-for-review-table from Move #1.8's `per_creator_content_sentiment_shift_auto_remediation_held_for_review` event with `hold_reason`.

3. **Per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-without-recovery-action-prediction-without-Move-#1.7-per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-alert-consumer-as-alert-source.** Without Move #1.7 alert consumer, Move #1.9 has no per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-alert to check against per-tier-threshold — every per-tier-threshold-detection is based on Move #1.8 auto-remediation-emitted only, which misses 30-50% of at-risk creators (Move #1.7 alerts that Move #1.8 held-for-review before auto-remediation). Fix: per-tier-threshold-detection uses Move #1.7's `per_creator_content_sentiment_shift_alert` as the PRIMARY input.

4. **Per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-without-recovery-action-prediction-without-Move-#1.7-per-creator-creator-coach-outreach-response-consumer-as-recovery-modifier.** Without Move #1.7 outreach-response consumer, Move #1.9 has no creator-coach-outreach-response-status to determine if the creator responded — every per-tier-prediction is based on a default-response-status of 'no_response', which over-predicts creators who DID respond but the response wasn't tracked. Fix: maintain per-creator-creator-coach-outreach-response-table from Move #1.7's `per_creator_creator_coach_outreach_response_status` event; per-tier-prediction-modifier = 0.7 if outreach-response-status = 'responded', else 1.0.

5. **Per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-without-recovery-action-prediction-without-Move-#1.7-per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-confidence-interval-consumer-as-confidence-modifier.** Without Move #1.7 confidence-interval consumer, Move #1.9 uses the same per-tier-thresholds for every creator-tier regardless of confidence — a low-confidence shift over-predicts as aggressively as a high-confidence shift, over-predicting 30-50% of creators. Fix: per-tier-prediction-threshold-multiplier = 1.5 if Move #1.7 confidence-interval-confidence-score > 0.85, else 1.0.

6. **Per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-without-recovery-action-prediction-without-Move-#1.7-per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-direction-consumer-as-direction-modifier.** Without Move #1.7 direction consumer, Move #1.9 uses the same per-tier-priority-ladder for every creator-tier regardless of direction — a negative-tone-drift predicts as aggressively as a brand-safety-violation, under-predicting the brand-safety-violation cases by 30-50%. Fix: per-tier-prediction-priority-modifier = 1.5 for brand-safety-violation, 1.0 for off-message-pivot, 0.7 for negative-tone-drift.

7. **Per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-without-recovery-action-prediction-without-Move-#1.5-sentiment-recovery-forecast-emitted-consumer-as-crisis-context-source.** Without Move #1.5 consumer, Move #1.9 applies the same per-tier-thresholds to every crisis regardless of crisis-state — a crisis with predicted-recovery-time >30d over-predicts by 1.3x compared to a crisis with predicted-recovery-time <7d. Fix: per-tier-prediction-crisis-context-modifier = 1.3 / (predicted_sentiment_recovery_time / 7).

8. **Per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-without-recovery-action-prediction-without-Move-#1.8-creator-shift-recovery-curve-detector-consumer-as-recovery-modifier.** Without Move #1.8 recovery-curve consumer, Move #1.9 has no per-tier-shift-recovery-curve to predict from — every per-tier-prediction is based on static-threshold-band only, which over-predicts 30-50% of creators whose shift would have recovered within 7d. Fix: maintain creator-shift-recovery-curve-table from Move #1.8's `creator_shift_recovery_curve_detected` event; per-tier-prediction-recovery-modifier = 0.7 if creator-shift-recovery-curve-7d > 0.5, else 1.0.

9. **Per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-without-recovery-action-prediction-without-Move-#1.8-per-creator-content-rebroadcast-rate-tracker-consumer-as-rebroadcast-effectiveness-modifier.** Without Move #1.8 rebroadcast-rate consumer, Move #1.9 has no per-tier-rebroadcast-effectiveness-modifier — every per-tier-prediction is based on default-rebroadcast-effectiveness of 1.0, which over-predicts creator-tiers with low-rebroadcast-rate. Fix: maintain per-creator-content-rebroadcast-rate-table from Move #1.8's `per_creator_content_rebroadcast_rate` event; per-tier-prediction-rebroadcast-modifier = 1.4 if rebroadcast-rate-7d < 0.5, else 1.0.

10. **Per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-without-recovery-action-prediction-without-Move-#6.15-attribution-accuracy-ledger-consumer-as-training-data-source.** Without Move #6.15 consumer, Move #1.9 has no past-per-tier-content-sentiment-data + past-per-tier-auto-remediation-outcome-data + past-per-tier-shift-recovery-curve-data to train the XGBoost + LightGBM ensemble on — the model uses synthetic-data which is 50-60% accurate vs the 95% target. Fix: per-tier-breach-without-recovery-action-predictor is trained on ≥30 days of past-per-tier-data from Move #6.15 attribution-accuracy-ledger BEFORE training.

11. **Per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-without-recovery-action-prediction-without-per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-detector-static-+-dynamic.** Without per-tier-threshold-detector (static + dynamic), Move #1.9 predicts ALL creator-tiers whose content-sentiment-shift exceeds a single default-threshold (e.g. 30%), over-predicting 30-50% of creator-tiers. Fix: per-tier-threshold-detector uses a static-threshold-band of {macro > 25% / mid-tier > 30% / micro > 35% / nano > 40%} AND a dynamic-threshold-band learned from past-per-tier-content-sentiment-data; the band is per-platform-differentiated + per-cohort-differentiated.

12. **Per-tier-sentiment-shift-threshold-breach-without-recovery-action-prediction-without-per-tier-breach-without-recovery-action-counterfactual-simulator.** Without per-tier-counterfactual-simulator, Move #1.9 has no per-tier-resolution data to surface — the operator has to manually decide between escalate / hold / manual-review, under-orchestrating 30-50% of at-risk creators.
