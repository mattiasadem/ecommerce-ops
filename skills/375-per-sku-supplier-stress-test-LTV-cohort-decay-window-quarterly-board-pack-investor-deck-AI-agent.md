## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

**P1. Ship without Move #358.5.7 Pillar 4 8-section-rollup.** The investor-deck Pillar 3 6-section narrative-engine depends on Move #358.5.7 Pillar 4 8-section Q1-Q8 rollup template; shipping without this integration means the investor-deck has NO source-template and ships as a static investor-spreadsheet. **Fix:** wire Pillar 3 to Move #358.5.7 Pillar 4 with a Q-end rollup window (read trailing 13 weeks, anonymize via Pillar 1, generate 6-section investor-deck).

**P2. Ship without counsel-review for A1-A7 anonymization-engine.** The 7-rule A1-A7 anonymization-engine MUST be counsel-reviewed before distribution; shipping without counsel-review means the investor-deck leaks supplier-names / SKU-names / region-names / customer-ids / dollar-amount-precision and triggers SEC Reg D 506(b) material-non-public-information disclosure risk + SEC Reg S-K Item 10(e) MD&A-non-GAAP-measure-disclosure violation + FASB ASC 280 segment-reporting-anonymization-engine non-compliance. **Fix:** require counsel sign-off on A1-A7 ruleset AND SEC Reg D 506(b) + Reg S-K Item 10(e) + FASB ASC 280 alignment before activating Move #358.5.12 (Phase 1 + Phase 4 both require counsel-review).

**P3. Ship without audit-trail (operator-id + counsel-id + section-id + cohort-tier-id + quarter + 7-anonymization-rule-firing-record).** Investor-deck-output is non-reproducible and fails FASB ASC 280 segment-reporting-anonymization-engine audit + SEC Reg D 506(b) due-diligence-recordkeeping requirements. **Fix:** wire audit-trail-logger to every investor-deck-section-output; audit-trail is immutable and retained ≥7 years per SEC standard.

**P4. No IR1-IR6 rollback-coverage validation.** An investor-deck can degrade (confidence-band-drop, anonymization-rule-leak, distribution-SLA-breach, competitive-benchmark-percentile-rank-drop, cohort-LTV-preservation-percentile-rank-drop, operator-rollback-request); shipping without IR1-IR6 means bad investor-decks persist for a full quarter and risk SEC Reg D 506(b) material-non-public-information disclosure. **Fix:** wire IR1-IR6 to Move #182's auto-rollback pipeline with a 24h SLA on IR-firing → regen-execution.

**P5. No Pillar 2 8-dim competitive-benchmark-vector publish-rate gate.** All 8 dims must publish per cohort-tier per quarter; shipping without the publish-rate gate means some dims carry `null` and the investor sees a partial investor-deck. **Fix:** Pillar 2 publish-rate ≥98% per cohort-tier per quarter; null-dims are dropped and logged to Pillar 5 schema-error counter.

**P6. No confidence-band on investor-deck-section.** All 6 sections (N1-N6) treated as equally-trustworthy. **Fix:** publish confidence_band T1-T5 on every section; T1 auto-distribute, T2 require pre-approval, T3-T5 REJECT. Section-level confidence-band is the AGGREGATE of all cell-level confidence-bands in that section (median or 25th-percentile, whichever is more conservative) AND the A1-A7 anonymization-rule-firing-record (a section with A1-A7 firing-coverage <100% drops one confidence-band-tier).

**P7. No Move #358.5.3 Pillar 5 decision-routing-segregation.** Investor-deck must NOT include Move #358.5.3 Pillar 5 decision-routing weights (a separate decision-engine layer); shipping without this scope-check means the investor-deck leaks decision-routing data to the investor that should be operator-only AND may trigger SEC Reg S-K Item 10(e) MD&A-non-GAAP-measure-disclosure violation. **Fix:** investor-deck section filter excludes Move #358.5.3 Pillar 5 fields; only Move #358.5.6 Pillar 4 CRO1-CRO6 weights are surfaced (and only after A7 internal-metric-name-redaction).

**P8. No Move #89 BFCM-peak-multiplier integration.** Q4 quarterly-investor-deck must include Move #89 BFCM-peak-multiplier context (the BFCM-quarter is structurally different from Q1-Q3); shipping without BFCM-multiplier means the Q4 investor-deck under-reports BFCM-driven avoided-loss AND mis-states competitive-benchmark-percentile-rank. **Fix:** wire Q4 investor-deck to Move #89's BFCM-peak-flag and split Q4-section into BFCM-week (Black-Friday → Cyber-Monday + 7d) and BFCM-tail (rest of Q4) sub-sections.

**P9. No Move #154 predictive-LTV-churn integration.** Cohort-LTV-preservation-percentile-rank dim must cross-check Move #154 churn-prediction stream; shipping without this discrimination means a churn-driven LTV-drop is misattributed to the Move #358.5.6 multi-supplier-rollover-AI-agent AND the investor-deck overstates the AI-agent-tuning-driven net-avoided-loss. **Fix:** Pillar 2 dim 4 must classify the LTV-preservation as rollover-driven vs churn-driven vs seasonal-driven; if churn-driven, N6 NEXT-QUARTER-FUNDRAISING-CONTEXT defers to Move #154.

**P10. No A1 supplier-name-anonymization pre-distribution-leak-scanner.** Even with A1 anonymization ruleset shipped, a post-build-leak-scanner is required to catch residual supplier-name-leakage (e.g., a supplier-name embedded in a case-study anecdote or a region-name in a competitive-benchmark-percentile-rank table). **Fix:** post-build-leak-scanner runs pre-distribution on every section; if leak detected, IR2 AUTO-REGENERATE-SECTION + notify counsel.

**P11. No C2 INVESTOR-QUARTERLY-UPDATE distribution-cadence enforcement.** Investor expects quarterly-update on a predictable cadence; shipping without a quarterly-cadence-enforcement means the investor-update ships late or not at all and erodes investor-trust. **Fix:** Pillar 4 C2 distribution-SLA gate; if Q-end + 10d is missed, IR3 fires AUTO-ESCALATE.

**P12. No C5 PRESS-RELEASE-INVESTOR-PARAGRAPH anonymization-relaxation-validation.** C5 press-release-investor-paragraph only requires A1-A4 anonymization (not A5-A7); shipping without a per-channel-anonymization-mandate-validation means C5 accidentally publishes dollar-amount-precision (A5) or customer-id-redaction (A6) details. **Fix:** Pillar 4 per-channel-anonymization-mandate-validation gate; if C5 fires with A5-A6 data present, the section is blocked + IR2 fires AUTO-REGENERATE-SECTION.

**P13. No Triple-Whale 44-field-schema coverage validation.** Downstream consumers (Move #113, Move #115, Move #119, Move #154, Move #180, Move #181, Move #182, Move #185, Move #186, Move #188) silently fail when fields like `quarterly_competitive_benchmark_percentile_rank_anonymized`, `quarterly_anonymization_rule_firing_record`, `quarterly_net_avoided_value_usd` are missing. **Fix:** schema-validation gate fires pre-publish; events missing >2 fields are dropped with a Pillar 5 schema-error counter increment.

**P14. No Q-end + 10d investor-distribution-SLA enforcement.** Investor-quarterly-update must reach the investor within 10d post-quarter-end; shipping without the SLA means the update arrives in week 2 of the new quarter and is stale AND erodes investor-trust. **Fix:** Pillar 4 C2 distribution-SLA gate; if Q-end + 10d is missed, IR3 fires AUTO-ESCALATE.

**P15. No investor-deck-cost-amortization tracking.** Investor-deck profitability unknown. **Fix:** Pillar 5 6-tuple publishes `quarterly_investor_deck_net_avoided_value_usd` per cohort-tier per quarter; investor-board-meeting review checks `net_avoided_value > 0` for ≥80% of cohort-tiers per quarter (where net_avoided_value includes fundraising-uplift-attribution per Pillar 2 dim 8).

**P16. No operator IR-rollback explicit handling.** Operator REJECT-but-not-regenerate confusion (operator rejects investor-deck section but agent continues publishing). **Fix:** IR6 AUTO-REGENERATE-ROLLUP on operator REJECT; agent pauses for 7d before re-publishing the same investor-deck section + flag for counsel re-review.

**P17. No confidence-band boost from prior-quarter history.** Every quarterly-cycle treated as fresh; shipping without history means the investor-deck repeats mistakes from prior quarters AND the competitive-benchmark-percentile-rank drifts without early-warning. **Fix:** Pillar 4 confidence-band lookup includes `prior_quarter_outcomes[cohort_tier_id]`; if last 2 quarters of this cohort-tier had IR-firings, the new quarter's confidence_band is downgraded by 1 tier (T2 → T3).

**P18. No investor-deck-vs-Move-#358.5.7-quarterly-rollup-boundary-validation.** Investor-deck scope-creep (Move #358.5.12 starts writing back to Move #358.5.7 competitive-benchmark-percentile-rank or cohort-LTV-preservation-percentile-rank fields that should be Move #358.5.7's domain). **Fix:** Pillar 6 scope-validation gate enforces investor-deck consumption of Move #358.5.7 Pillar 4 outputs read-only + anonymization-only; if a section requires writing back to Move #358.5.7, the write is BLOCKED and the event is forwarded to Move #358.5.7's next-quarter processing.
