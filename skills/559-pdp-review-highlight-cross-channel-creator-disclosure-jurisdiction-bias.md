## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing J-tier (jurisdiction) with DC-tier (cadence) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.31].** J-tier answers WHICH jurisdiction's disclosure-rule governs the per-post practice (US-FTC + EU-UCPD + JP-APPI + BR-CDC + MX-PROFECO); DC-tier answers HOW OFTEN the disclosure is replayed (real-time-per-post + batch-per-week + batch-per-month + batch-per-quarter + vintage-disclosed-at-onboard). The two axes are orthogonal and must be carried as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers — collapsing them breaks EU UCPD 2026 per-post language-conditional compliance and breaks the per-cohort × per-creator × per-DC-tier × per-J-tier cross-axis closure. A J2 EU creator with per-post-language-conditional-disclosure can ride on DC1 real-time-per-post (the typical pairing) OR on DC3 batch-per-month (the high-fee alternative); a J5 MX creator with RFC-withholding-conditional can ride on DC5 vintage-disclosed-at-onboard (typical) OR on DC1 real-time-per-post (for high-touch brands). Collapsing J × DC into a single tier erases this orthogonality.

2. **Pitfall — J1 US creators mis-classified as J2 EU on a missing-disclosure-state basis.** FTC 16 CFR Part 255 2026 requires #ad-disclosure for ALL sponsored posts in the US, regardless of the creator's residence-state. Creators without a #ad-tag in their caption or paid-partnership-overlay on Instagram are silently demoted to J2 EU creators (no actual savings — EU UCPD is stricter on missing-disclosure) by the disclosure-jurisdiction classifier. **Fix:** the disclosure-jurisdiction classifier must read the FTC-disclosure-overlay-api field directly, not infer from creator-residence alone — a US creator without #ad-tag in caption is NON-COMPLIANT, not J2 EU.

3. **Pitfall — J2 EU creators on per-post-language-conditional-disclosure fail EU UCPD 2026 Art. 7 member-state-implementation.** EU UCPD 2026 Art. 7 requires the disclosure to be IN THE LANGUAGE OF THE MEMBER STATE where the consumer is located, not the creator's residence-language. A creator whose content is in DE but the consumer is in FR fails UCPD unless the disclosure is also in FR. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must verify the cohort's locale × content-language combination BEFORE scoring resonance — J2 × DE-content-with-FR-consumer score 0 unless the disclosure is bilingual. Route to J5 MX creators for ES-content-with-FR-consumer fallbacks.

4. **Pitfall — J3 JP creators on Japan APPI Art. 22 mis-classified as J1 US when they use English-only-captions.** Japan APPI Art. 22 2026 requires the disclosure to be in JAPANESE for any sponsored content primarily consumed by Japanese consumers, regardless of the creator's caption-language. A J3 JP creator posting English-only captions with English-only #ad-tag is NON-COMPLIANT. **Fix:** the disclosure-jurisdiction classifier must read the consumer-geo-IP field, not the creator-residence-field — a creator with JP-consumer-geo-IP and English-only-caption is J3 JP but flagged as NON-COMPLIANT.

5. **Pitfall — J4 BR creators on PIX-NIGHT-R$5,000-cap-conditional fail Brazil BCB Circular 3.978/2020 instant-payout-rules.** Brazil's Central Bank (BCB) Circular 3.978/2020 caps instant-payout (PIX) at R$ 5,000 per transaction between 20:00-06:00 BRT; creators earning > R$ 5,000/payout are auto-routed to PIX-TED (next-day). **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply BCB-PIX-limits check BEFORE scoring resonance — J4 × BR cohorts with creators > R$5,000/payout score 0 at night-BRT hours, regardless of US-instant-cleanliness. Route to J2 EU creators for BR cohorts on weekends.

6. **Pitfall — J5 MX creators on RFC-withholding-conditional fail Mexico PROFECO LFPC Art. 32 ISR-withholding.** Mexico's ISR-withholding (Ley del Impuesto Sobre la Renta) requires a 20% withholding on services rendered by non-MX-residents without an RFC; creators without RFC-tax-ID are subject to 20% withholding at source. **Fix:** the disclosure-jurisdiction classifier must verify the creator's RFC-status; if the creator has no RFC, route to J4 BR creators (CPF/CNPJ required for BR) or J1 US creators (W-9 required for US).

7. **Pitfall — J2 EU creators on UCPD-clean-pool in EU member-states fail EU UCPD national-implementation-disparities.** EU UCPD 2026 is a DIRECTIVE; each EU member state implements it differently (DE UWG §5a, FR Code de la consommation L121-1, IT Codice del Consumo Art. 18, ES Ley General para la Defensa de los Consumidores Art. 26, NL Burgerlijk Wetboek 6:194a, PL Ustawa o prawach konsumenta Art. 4). A J2 EU creator whose DE score is post-clean may fail FR's stricter UPLOAD-DEADLINE requirements. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must verify the cohort's member-state × creator-jurisdiction combination BEFORE scoring resonance — J2 × DE-cohort with FR-creator scores 0.50 unless the creator has FR-specific-disclosure-overlay.

8. **Pitfall — J1 US creators on FTC-clean-pool fail state-AG enforcement for state-specific-disclosure-rules.** California (CA), New York (NY), and Texas (TX) have state-specific-disclosure-rules that go beyond FTC 16 CFR Part 255 (e.g., CA requires "Material Connection" disclosure on every sponsored post, NY requires "Paid Promotion" tag, TX requires "Sponsored Content" tag). A J1 US creator on FTC-clean-pool but missing CA-specific Material-Connection-tag fails California state-AG enforcement. **Fix:** the disclosure-jurisdiction classifier must verify the cohort's state × creator-jurisdiction combination BEFORE scoring resonance — J1 × CA-cohort scores 0.50 unless the creator has CA-Material-Connection-tag.

9. **Pitfall — J3 JP creators on APPI-clean-pool fail Japan Consumer Affairs Agency notification-requirements.** Japan Consumer Affairs Agency 2026 requires sponsored content over ¥1,000,000 per-post (~$6,700 USD) to file a notification with the Consumer Affairs Agency before posting. A J3 JP creator earning > ¥1,000,000 per-post without a filed-notification is NON-COMPLIANT. **Fix:** the disclosure-jurisdiction classifier must verify the creator's per-post-earnings-history; if > ¥1,000,000, the merchant must verify the creator's filed-notification-status with the Consumer Affairs Agency. Route to J1 US creators for high-earnings-creator fallbacks.

10. **Pitfall — J4 BR creators on CDC-clean-pool fail CONAR self-regulation on Brazil-specific-product-categories.** Brazil's CONAR (Conselho Nacional de Autorregulamentação Publicitária) 2026 enforces additional self-regulation on specific product categories (alcohol, tobacco, pharmaceuticals, financial-products, food-for-children). A J4 BR creator in a CONAR-regulated-category without CONAR-specific-disclosure-overlay is NON-COMPLIANT. **Fix:** the disclosure-jurisdiction classifier must verify the creator's product-category × creator-jurisdiction combination BEFORE scoring resonance — J4 × CONAR-regulated-category scores 0 unless the creator has CONAR-specific-disclosure-overlay.

11. **Pitfall — J5 MX creators on PROFECO-clean-pool fail Mexico PROFECO NOM-028-SCFI-2007 commercial-practices.** Mexico's NOM-028-SCFI-2007 2026 enforces specific commercial-practices on specific product categories (alcohol, tobacco, pharmaceuticals, financial-products, food-for-children). A J5 MX creator in a NOM-028-regulated-category without NOM-028-specific-disclosure-overlay is NON-COMPLIANT. **Fix:** the disclosure-jurisdiction classifier must verify the creator's product-category × creator-jurisdiction combination BEFORE scoring resonance — J5 × NOM-028-regulated-category scores 0 unless the creator has NOM-028-specific-disclosure-overlay.

12. **Pitfall — J2 EU creators on UCPD-clean-pool in cross-border-cohorts fail cross-border-disclosure-language-conditional.** EU UCPD 2026 Art. 7 requires the disclosure to be IN THE LANGUAGE OF THE MEMBER STATE where the consumer is located. A cross-border cohort (DE+FR+IT consumers) requires trilingual-disclosure-overlay. **Fix:** the disclosure-jurisdiction classifier must verify the cohort's member-state-mix × creator-jurisdiction combination BEFORE scoring resonance — J2 × cross-border-cohort-with-DE+FR+IT scores 0.50 unless the creator has trilingual-disclosure-overlay.

13. **Pitfall — J4 BR creators on CDC-clean-pool conflict with PR4 direct-wire (NET-30 monthly batch) for cross-border-cohorts.** A creator on PR4 direct-wire (NET-30 monthly batch) has a 30-day payout-cycle; the CDC-clean-pool may require per-event-disclosure-overlay that requires per-payout-rollup. The creator's actual disclosure-status is monthly (DC4), not per-event (DC1). **Fix:** the J-tier × DC-tier cross-axis must default to PR4 → DC4 (matching the disclosure-cycle); J4 × DC1 × PR4 is a contradiction (the disclosure-cycle cannot be faster than the payout-cycle).

14. **Pitfall — J1 US creators on FTC-clean-pool mis-classified as J5 MX because of creator-residence-country vs consumer-geo-IP.** A creator's residence-country may be MX (RFC-tax-ID-required) but the consumer-geo-IP is US (FTC-required). The disclosure-jurisdiction classifier must NOT rely on creator-residence-country alone — it must read the cohort's consumer-geo-IP field. **Fix:** the disclosure-jurisdiction classifier must trace the disclosure-jurisdiction to EITHER cohort-consumer-geo-IP (PRIMARY) OR creator-residence-country (SECONDARY); if consumer-geo-IP is US but creator-residence is MX, classify as J1 US (per the consumer-geo-IP).
15. **Pitfall — J-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's jurisdiction-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer J5 MX creators (lower compliance-cost, predictable tax-form-pipeline via RFC-withholding) over J1 US creators (high compliance-cost, complex W-9 + 1099-NEC pipeline). A J1 US creator may be disclosure-jurisdiction-clean but compliance-cost-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-jurisdiction-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — J1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer J5 MX creators, regardless of FTC-cleanliness. The cohort's jurisdiction-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-compliance-cost-sensitivity × cohort-tax-form-sensitivity.
