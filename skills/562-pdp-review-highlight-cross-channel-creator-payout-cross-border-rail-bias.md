## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing CR-tier (cross-border rail) with PR-tier (payment rail) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.34].** CR-tier answers WHICH cross-border-rail CARRIES the payout (ACH-Stripe-Connect-domestic + Wise-EU-SEPA-Instant + Wise-JP-Zengin + Wise-BR-PIX + Wise-MX-SPEI); PR-tier answers HOW the payment is RAILED (Stripe-Connect + PayPal-Mass-Pay + Wise-Mass-Pay + ACH + Wire). The two axes are orthogonal and must be carried as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers — collapsing them breaks FX-spread compliance AND breaks the per-cohort × per-creator × per-PR-tier × per-CR-tier cross-axis closure. A CR1 USD-domestic creator with ACH-Stripe-Connect can ride on PR1 Stripe-Connect (the typical pairing) OR on PR4 direct-wire (for high-touch brands); a CR2 USD-EU-cross-border creator with Wise-EU-SEPA-Instant can ride on PR3 Wise-Mass-Pay (typical) OR on PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay (for low-friction fallbacks). Collapsing CR × PR into a single tier erases this orthogonality.

2. **Pitfall — CR1 USD-domestic creators mis-classified as CR2 USD-EU-cross-border on a missing-domestic-bank-account basis.** Stripe Connect 2026 + ACH 2026 + Fedwire Funds Service 2026 require US creators to have a valid US-domestic-bank-account (routing-number + account-number) for ACH-rail. Creators without a valid US-domestic-bank-account are NOT eligible for CR1 USD-domestic pool and must be routed to CR2-CR5 cross-border (depending on creator's actual residency). **Fix:** the cross-border-rail classifier must read the creator's bank-account-residency field (US vs non-US) BEFORE assigning CR-tier — a US creator with missing-domestic-bank-account is CR2 USD-EU-cross-border (assuming EU-bank-account), NOT CR1 USD-domestic.

3. **Pitfall — CR2 USD-EU-cross-border creators on Wise-EU-only-pool fail SEPA Instant Credit Transfer 2026 SEPA-Instant-eligibility.** EU SEPA Instant Credit Transfer 2026 requires SEPA-Instant-eligibility (max €100k per-transaction + 24/7/365 + 10-second-settlement); creators claiming SEPA-Instant-eligibility but using standard SEPA-Credit Transfer (which takes 1-2 business-days) are silently demoted to Wise-EU-Standard-pool. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail classifier must verify the SEPA-Instant-eligibility-state; route to CR1 USD-domestic if SEPA-Instant-eligibility-missing.

4. **Pitfall — CR3 USD-JP-cross-border creators on Wise-JP-only-pool fail Zengin System 2026 Zengin-bank-membership-rule.** Zengin System 2026 requires all JP-domestic-banks to be Zengin-member-banks; creators on non-Zengin-member-banks (e.g., the cooperative-financial-institutions-not-on-Zengin) are silently demoted to SWIFT-MT103-pool (slower + more-expensive). **Fix:** the cross-border-rail classifier must verify the creator's Zengin-bank-membership-state; route to CR1 USD-domestic if Zengin-bank-membership-missing.

5. **Pitfall — CR4 USD-BR-cross-border creators on Wise-BR-only-pool fail BCB Circular 3.978/2020 PIX-key-registration-rule.** BCB Circular 3.978/2020 + Brazil PIX 2026 + Brazil SPI 2026 require all BR-domestic-creators to register a valid PIX-key (CPF/CNPJ/email/phone/random-key); creators without a valid PIX-key are silently demoted to Wire-pool (3-5 business-days + intermediary-bank-fee). **Fix:** the cross-border-rail classifier must verify the creator's PIX-key-registration-state; route to CR1 USD-domestic if PIX-key-registration-missing.

6. **Pitfall — CR5 USD-MX-cross-border creators on Wise-MX-only-pool fail Banxico Circular 14/2024 CLABE-registration-rule.** Banxico Circular 14/2024 + Mexico SPEI 2026 require all MX-domestic-creators to register a valid CLABE (18-digit-bank-account-number); creators without a valid CLABE are silently demoted to Wire-pool (2-5 business-days + intermediary-bank-fee). **Fix:** the cross-border-rail classifier must verify the creator's CLABE-registration-state; route to CR1 USD-domestic if CLABE-registration-missing.

7. **Pitfall — CR1 USD-domestic creators on ACH-Stripe-Connect fail Fedwire Funds Service 2026 cutoff-time-rule.** Fedwire Funds Service 2026 requires same-day-rail cutoffs (6:00 PM ET for USD); creators submitting after the cutoff-time are silently rolled to next-day-rail. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail classifier must verify the cohort's submission-cutoff-time-state; route to CR2 USD-EU-cross-border for next-day-rail fallbacks.

8. **Pitfall — CR2 USD-EU-cross-border creators on Wise-EU fail EU DAC7 2026 platform-reporting-requirement.** EU DAC7 2026 requires digital-platform-operators to report non-EU-resident-creator-earnings to EU tax-authorities; creators from non-EU-resident countries on CR2 USD-EU-cross-border may be DOUBLE-REPORTED (EU DAC7 + US 1099-NEC). **Fix:** the cross-border-rail classifier must verify the creator's EU-residency-state; route to CR3 USD-JP-cross-border for non-EU-resident-creators with JP-bank-account.

9. **Pitfall — CR3 USD-JP-cross-border creators on Wise-JP fail Japan NTA 2026 NRA-withholding-rule.** Japan NTA 2026 + Japan NTA Article 226 2026 require JP-domestic-payers to withhold 20.42% NRA-withholding-tax on payments to non-JP-resident-creators; non-compliance triggers Japan NTA penalties. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail classifier must verify the creator's JP-resident-state; route to CR1 USD-domestic if non-JP-resident-without-treaty-claim.

10. **Pitfall — CR4 USD-BR-cross-border creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The Wise-BR-PIX-pool + BCB Circular 3.978/2020 cross-border-rail requires 1 FTE engineer to keep the PIX-rail live + integrate new BRAZIL-domestic-banks + maintain FX-spread-hedging; low-margin cohorts (<$5M annual) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — CR4 score 0 for cohorts with <$5M annual revenue. Route to CR1 USD-domestic or CR2 USD-EU-cross-border creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

11. **Pitfall — CR5 USD-MX-cross-border creators fail 1-FTE-$120k-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The Wise-MX-SPEI-pool + Banxico Circular 14/2024 cross-border-rail requires 1 FTE engineer to keep the SPEI-rail live + integrate new MEXICO-domestic-banks + maintain CLABE-registration; low-margin cohorts cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — CR5 score 0 for cohorts with <$5M annual revenue. Route to CR1 USD-domestic creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

12. **Pitfall — CR-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's cross-border-rail-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer CR1 USD-domestic creators (lower rail-cost, no-FX-spread, ACH-Stripe-Connect-native) over CR5 USD-MX-cross-border creators (high rail-cost, 1.10%-FX-spread, 1-FTE-$120k-maint-cost). A CR1 USD-domestic creator may be cross-border-rail-clean but cross-border-rail-cost-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-cross-border-rail-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — CR1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer CR5 USD-MX-cross-border creators, regardless of ACH-Stripe-Connect-cleanliness. The cohort's cross-border-rail-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-cross-border-rail-cost-sensitivity × cohort-cross-border-rail-residency-preference.

13. **Pitfall — CR-tier cross-axis with PN-tier silently double-rails cross-border payments.** A creator on CR1 USD-domestic with PN2 TikTok-native as secondary platform is double-railed (ACH-Stripe-Connect + TikTok-Creator-Marketplace-rail). The cross-border-rail classifier must not double-count rail on cross-platform-cohorts. **Fix:** the CR-tier × PN-tier cross-axis must apply secondary-platform-rail-conditional — CR1 × PN2 score uses TikTok-Creator-Marketplace-rail as the secondary; the cohort's primary rail is ACH-Stripe-Connect.

14. **Pitfall — CR4 USD-BR-cross-border creators on Wise-BR-PIX mis-classified as CR5 USD-MX-cross-border because of PIX-rail-failure-state.** A creator on CR4 USD-BR-cross-border whose PIX-rail has failed (e.g., PIX-key-invalid, integration broken) is silently demoted to CR5 USD-MX-cross-border creators. The actual state is "PIX-rail-failed — payout not delivered". **Fix:** the cross-border-rail classifier must distinguish between PIX-rail-success / PIX-rail-failed / no-PIX-rail states; route PIX-rail-failed creators to CR1 USD-domestic as a temporary fallback.

15. **Pitfall — CR-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's cross-border-rail-residency-preference.** A cohort with US-domestic-only preference (no EU/JP/BR/MX creators) requires CR1 USD-domestic pool; a cohort with EU-only preference requires CR2 USD-EU-cross-border pool; a cohort with JP-only preference requires CR3 USD-JP-cross-border pool; a cohort with BR-only preference requires CR4 USD-BR-cross-border pool; a cohort with MX-only preference requires CR5 USD-MX-cross-border pool. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-cross-border-rail-residency-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — CR2 × US-domestic-cohort score 0; route to CR1 USD-domestic fallbacks.
