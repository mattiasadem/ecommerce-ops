## Common pitfalls (20 from real builds)

The 20 pitfalls from BFCM 2024 + 2025-Q1/Q2 builds split into 4 categories: 5 BFCM-cohort-LTV-baseline pitfalls (P1–P5), 5 BFCM-per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth-engine pitfalls (P6–P10), 5 BFCM-margin-floor + loyalty-tier + Gorgias-CX-ticket-aware guards pitfalls (P11–P15), and 5 BFCM-A/B-test-framework pitfalls (P16–P20). Each pitfall includes the bug + the canonical fix.

### BFCM-cohort-LTV-baseline pitfalls (P1–P5)

**P1 — Shipping the auto-tune WITHOUT ≥4-week BFCM-cohort-LTV-baseline-data.** Ships per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth recommendations from a <4-week baseline — accuracy is <70% per Triple Whale BFCM 2024 + Polar BFCM 2024. **Fix:** ship 2026-10-10 (today) → enable recompute-cadence 24h for the first 4 weeks → upgrade to 4h recompute-cadence after 4 weeks (2026-11-07) → 1h recompute-cadence after 12 weeks (2026-12-31) → real-time-stream after 24 weeks (2027-03-31). The canonical Nov 2026 ship-window is a 4-week-baseline-floor; the brand should EITHER delay ship to 2027-Q1 OR accept the ≥70% accuracy floor.

**P2 — Treating the BFCM-per-cohort-LTV-overlay as "the same as off-season per-cohort-LTV-overlay."** The off-season Triple Whale / Polar / Northbeam per-cohort-LTV-overlay uses 5-cohort segmentation (high-LTV / mid-LTV-low-AOV / first-time / loyalty / at-risk); the BFCM-per-cohort-LTV-overlay uses 7-cohort segmentation (BFCM-VIP / BFCM-first-time-browse / BFCM-loyalty-active / BFCM-dormant-reactivation / BFCM-no-purchase-yet / BFCM-gift-recipient / BFCM-mobile-browse). The two overlays are NOT interchangeable. **Fix:** ship the canonical 7-cohort BFCM-overlay as a NEW Triple Whale / Polar / Northbeam overlay; do NOT reuse the off-season 5-cohort overlay for BFCM-per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth.

**P3 — Computing BFCM-per-cohort-LTV from <12-month BFCM-cohort-LTV-baseline-window.** BFCM-cohort-LTV has strong year-over-year seasonality (a customer who bought BFCM-2024 has materially different LTV than a customer who bought BFCM-2023 because of the BFCM-buying-frenzy + BFCM-discount-trained + BFCM-loyalty-tier-up). **Fix:** capture ≥12-month BFCM-cohort-LTV-baseline + 24-month BFCM-cohort-LTV-baseline if available; the canonical Triple Whale / Polar / Northbeam BFCM-cohort-LTV-overlay configuration includes a 24-month-baseline-ramp from the first BFCM-cohort on record.

**P4 — Computing BFCM-per-cohort-LTV WITHOUT the BFCM-anchor-promo + BFCM-flex-promo + BFCM-flash-promo context.** BFCM-cohort-LTV is materially different during BFCM-anchor-promo-windows (Nov 28 / Nov 30 / Cyber-5 Nov 28-Dec 2) vs BFCM-flex-promo-windows (Nov 9 / Nov 16 / Nov 23) vs BFCM-flash-promo-windows (Nov 23 6PM-12AM / Nov 25 / Nov 29 6PM-12AM). A BFCM-VIP-cohort-LTV computed from "average across all BFCM-windows" misses the 30–55% peak-window vs the 5–10% baseline-window divergence. **Fix:** compute BFCM-per-cohort-LTV PER BFCM-promo-window-type (BFCM-anchor-promo-LTV / BFCM-flex-promo-LTV / BFCM-flash-promo-LTV); the auto-tune consumes the per-promo-window-type-LTV during the corresponding cadence.

**P5 — Reusing off-season AOV (average-order-value) as BFCM-AOV in the cohort-LTV calculation.** BFCM-AOV is typically 1.2–1.5× off-season AOV per Klaviyo BFCM 2024 + Shopify Plus BFCM 2024 (BFCM brings gift-recipients with higher AOV + BFCM-discount-trained with higher cart-size). **Fix:** the Triple Whale / Polar / Northbeam BFCM-cohort-LTV-overlay must emit BFCM-per-cohort-AOV separately from BFCM-per-cohort-LTV; the auto-tune consumes BFCM-per-cohort-AOV as the AOV-basis for the discount-depth-band calculation.

### BFCM-per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth-engine pitfalls (P6–P10)

**P6 — Computing per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth as a SINGLE-VALUE (not a band).** Returns 35% for BFCM-VIP single-value; misses the ±5pp band-allowance that the auto-tune needs to respect BFCM-discount-cadence-policy + BFCM-margin-floor per-cohort-product-COGS-lock. **Fix:** the engine emits a 5-BFCM-cohort-discount-depth-BAND with P50 + P95 + P99 confidence-band (e.g. BFCM-VIP: P50=35% / P95=33–37% / P99=32–38%); the Klaviyo flow-filters consume the band, not the single-value.

**P7 — Treating the auto-tune as a replacement for skill/612's static-discount BFCM-cohort conditional-split.** The auto-tune sits BENEATH skill/612's static-discount BFCM-cohort conditional-split; it does NOT replace it. The 5-cohort conditional-split (BFCM-VIP / BFCM-first-time-browse / BFCM-loyalty-active / BFCM-dormant-reactivation / BFCM-no-purchase-yet) is the routing layer; the auto-tune is the per-cohort-discount-depth refinement layer. **Fix:** the engine consumes the 5-cohort from skill/612's static-discount conditional-split (the routing layer is unchanged); the engine emits per-cohort-discount-depth-band (the refinement layer is auto-tuned).

**P8 — Recommending a discount-depth that overrides the BFCM-anchor-promo / BFCM-flex-promo / BFCM-flash-promo cadence.** A per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth of 45% during a BFCM-flex-promo-policy-week (the canonical 10–15% off early-bird tier) overrides the BFCM-flex-promo-policy and creates a BFCM-discount-cadence-policy-violation. **Fix:** the engine emits per-cohort-discount-depth-actual = min(per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth-engine-output, BFCM-flex-promo-floor OR BFCM-anchor-promo-ceiling OR BFCM-flash-promo-band-policy during the corresponding BFCM-cadence-window). The engine sits BENEATH Move #46's BFCM-discount-cadence-policy.

**P9 — Recompute-cadence-too-fast (real-time-stream at the floor 4-week baseline).** Recomputes the per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth every 1 minute at the 4-week baseline; recomputes-with-1%-data-delta triggers 100+ per-day coefficient-of-variation-swings; the Klaviyo flow-filters get whiplash. **Fix:** recompute-cadence = 24h at the 4-week-baseline floor; 4h at the 12-week baseline; 1h at the 24-week baseline; the recompute-cadence scales with baseline-window-length.

**P10 — Single-threaded recompute across the 5-BFCM-cohorts.** Recompute one cohort at a time; the BFCM-VIP-cohort recompute takes 30s, then the BFCM-first-time-browse-cohort recompute takes 30s; total recompute-time is 2.5 minutes — exceeds the recompute-cadence and the BFCM-cohort-LTV-signal-real-time-updates break. **Fix:** parallelize the recompute across all 5-BFCM-cohorts using a 5-thread / 5-process worker-pool; total recompute-time ≤30s.

### BFCM-margin-floor + loyalty-tier + Gorgias-CX-ticket-aware guards pitfalls (P11–P15)

**P11 — Per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth NOT per-product-COGS-locked.** Recommends 45% off on a 35% gross-margin SKU; the BFCM-margin-floor (canonical 30–45% gross-margin) is violated AND the SKU eats margin on every BFCM-discounted sale. **Fix:** wire the BFCM-margin-floor-aware per-product-COGS-lock auto-tune that narrows the per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth to ≤30% on the low-margin-SKU subset (the canonical Move #46 BFCM-margin-floor-per-SKU-cohort configuration).

**P12 — Per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth NOT loyalty-tier-discount-multiplier-aware.** A Smile-VIP-customer in BFCM-loyalty-active cohort gets 35% per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth; the Smile-VIP-cohort-discount-multiplier (canonical 2× loyalty-points) gets overridden by the 35% off discount; the BFCM-loyalty-tier-up-customer-incident-rate spikes. **Fix:** wire the loyalty-tier-discount-multiplier-aware auto-tune that narrows the per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth to ≤30% on Smile-VIP / Yotpo-LoyaltyLion-VIP customer segments.

**P13 — Per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth NOT Gorgias-CX-ticket-aware.** A customer with an open CX ticket (the canonical "I emailed customer service 10 min ago and they got another cart-abandon email with 35% off discount") gets a BFCM-VIP-cohort-discount-depth of 38%; the CX-agent didn't authorize this discount; the BFCM-VIP-VIP-CX-dispute-rate spikes. **Fix:** wire the Gorgias-BFCM-tier-routing-aware auto-tune that suppresses per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth when the customer has an open CX ticket.

**P14 — Per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth-guard-violation-rate not measured.** The BFCM-margin-floor-guard fires 50 times across BFCM-week (every low-margin-SKU); the violation-rate exceeds the canonical ≤3% floor; the operator pages Gorgias but the per-violation-cause attribution is missing. **Fix:** wire the BFCM-margin-floor-violation-attribution-dashboard that surfaces the top-5-violation-causes (SKU-COGS-bucket / cohort-LTV-overlay-spike / loyalty-tier-override / Gorgias-CX-suppression / BFCM-discount-cadence-policy-override) per BFCM-week.

**P15 — Per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth emitted as a log-only signal.** The engine emits per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth-recommendation as a log entry but doesn't wire to Klaviyo flow-action; the recommendation sits in the log and doesn't actually drive the BFCM-discount applied. **Fix:** wire the per-cohort-LTV-recommended-discount-depth-engine-output DIRECTLY to the Klaviyo flow-action + Postscript flow-action + Gorgias BFCM-discount-stacking-guard; the recommendation must traverse from the engine to the in-flow-discount in ≤5-minutes.

### BFCM-A/B-test-framework pitfalls (P16–P20)

**P16 — A/B test split at the cohort level (not the brand level).** Spits BFCM-VIP 50/50 between auto-tune and static-discount; the A/B test loses the cohort-level-significance AND the BFCM-VIP is statistically-too-small per Triple Whale BFCM 2024. **Fix:** split at the brand level (50% brand-traffic into auto-tune-cohort, 50% into static-discount-cohort); the cohort-level analysis happens within each brand-traffic-arm; the auto-tune-vs-static-discount-cohort-comparison is statistically-valid.

**P17 — A/B test duration too short (1-day or 3-day).** Runs the A/B test for 1-3 days during BFCM-week; the A/B test loses the BFCM-pre-rave-cohort-LTV-divergence AND the BFCM-week-cohort-LTV-divergence AND the BFCM-post-rave-cohort-LTV-divergence. **Fix:** run the A/B test for the FULL BFCM-7-week window (Oct 27 → Dec 31); the canonical A/B-test-duration-covers-BFCM-pre-rave + BFCM-week + BFCM-post-rave per Klaviyo BFCM 2024.

**P18 — A/B test scorecard missing BFCM-margin-floor-violation-rate + BFCM-discount-stacking-incidents.** Measures only BFCM-revenue and BFCM-cohort-CVR; misses the canonical BFCM-margin-floor-violation-rate AND the canonical BFCM-discount-stacking-incidents; the auto-tune appears to win BFCM-revenue but lose on margin-floor-violations. **Fix:** wire the canonical 7-metric scorecard (BFCM-revenue / BFCM-margin / BFCM-cohort-CVR / BFCM-cohort-AOV / BFCM-cohort-LTV / BFCM-discount-stacking-incidents / BFCM-margin-floor-violations) into the A/B-test-measurement-dashboard.

**P19 — A/B test result interpretation: "the auto-tune won, ship to 100%."** Ships the auto-tune to 100% of BFCM-traffic on day-5 of the A/B test; the A/B test loses statistical-power AND the operator ships BEFORE the BFCM-week. **Fix:** use the canonical A/B-test-stopping-rule (≥20% incremental BFCM-revenue measured at 7-day-window OR BFCM-margin-floor-violation-rate ≤1%) AND ship ONLY after BFCM-week.

**P20 — A/B test NOT re-run for next-BFCM (2027).** The BFCM-cohort-LTV-overlay accuracy drifts year-over-year; the A/B test result for BFCM-2026 does NOT generalize to BFCM-2027 (BFCM-cohort-discount-depth band shifts by ±5pp). **Fix:** re-run the A/B test for next-BFCM (2027) with the BFCM-2026-auto-tune-cohort-results as the new-baseline + the BFCM-2027-auto-tune-cohort-results as the new-candidate; canonical A/B-test-cadence = annual per BFCM-window per Triple Whale BFCM 2024.
