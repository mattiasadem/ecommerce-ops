## When to use this skill

Use this skill **after Move #6 Triple Whale attribution is live** (the brand-cohort-attribution-overlay requires Triple Whale or equivalent), **after Move #10 lifecycle flow library has identified ≥1,000 subscribers OR ≥100 customers** (the brand-cohort-LTV-overlay needs signal-to-noise), **after Move #43 product reviews / UGC has shipped** (brand-clarity is validated through customer-verbatim-language), and **before Move #9 mobile PDP redesign + Move #10 AI ad creative iteration + Move #25 international expansion + Move #22.3 localized payment-method orchestration**. It is the right move when the operator can NOT articulate the brand in 1 sentence, when the brand blurs into the category-blend on competitor-comparison-pages, when the rejection-positioning is empty, when the ICP-wedge is generic ("millennials who care about quality"), when the founder-personality is absent from the marketing, when the brand-voice drifts across channels, or when the brand-cohort-LTV = paid-cohort-LTV (no word-of-mouth premium = brand is invisible).

You have:

- **A live Shopify (or comparable) DTC storefront with at least 90 days of order history** (gives the brand-strategy a real customer-cohort and brand-cohort-attribution-overlay to work against).
- **≥$20k/year founder-time-budget OR agency-budget for brand-strategy** (Path A is DIY for sub-$500k-GMV brands; Path B/C/D/E require real budget).
- **At least one of:** founder-availability for narrative-iteration interviews (≥4 hours total) OR agency-partner with founder-interview-capability OR founder-as-marketer-operator (the brand-narrative cannot be built without the founder's POV; this is the highest-conviction prerequisite).
- **A defined hero product or hero category** (the brand-strategy engine anchors on a hero product — without one, the positioning lacks economic-reality).
- **An existing customer base (≥500 email subscribers OR ≥100 customers)** so the brand-cohort-attribution-overlay has signal-to-noise.
- **Daily-or-weekly access to Klaviyo + Postscript + Gorgias + Triple Whale (or comparable substrate)** so the brand-voice-and-tone-guide + ICP-wedge + rejection-positioning can be wired into the email/SMS/CX/attribution layers (Path A can ship without this; Path B+ needs it).
- **An ICP-hypothesis-or-customer-interview-data ≥5 interviews** (Path A: founder-derived; Path B: 5-10 customer interviews; Path C: 10-25 + 1-2 focus groups; Path D: 25-50 + quantitative-survey).
- **A documented brand-mission-or-founding-story** (Path A can derive this in week 1; Path B+ should have it pre-built).
- **A willingness to write the brand-positioning-doc and re-read it quarterly** (this is the load-bearing artifact — every downstream move references it).
- **A willingness to say "we are NOT for X"** (the rejection-positioning is the highest-leverage part of the framework — without it, the brand blurs into the category-blend).

Do **not** use this skill when:

- **<$20k US DTC GMV OR no Shopify active storefront OR no Klaviyo-class ESP OR no Triple Whale-class attribution.** Below these thresholds, the $0-$500 Path A self-serve brand-strategy cannot be justified by the +15% landing-page-CVR + +5% email-CTR + +30% earned-media-share alone; defer until GMV + founder-time-budget + customer-base + Klaviyo-substrate ready OR stay on the no-brand-positioning-baseline.
- **Already on the canonical April Dunford positioning + StoryBrand SB7 + brand-clarity-score-80+ + ICP-wedge + rejection-positioning + brand-voice-and-tone-guide + visual-identity-system + narrative-archetype + category-design + hero-product-economics + founder-led-content-engine + brand-ops + brand-metrics-dashboard + brand-cohort-attribution-overlay + brand-90-day-rollup substrate (the canonical brand-strategy-substrate stack).** No migration needed; ship Move #1 + #2 + #4 + #6 + #7 directly on the existing brand-substrate. The canonical April Dunford positioning + StoryBrand SB7 substrate is THE default; migrating away from it (e.g., to a thin brand-template OR no-brand-strategy + Amazon-listing-borrowed-positioning OR agency-led-everything) is not advised per April Dunford positioning 2024 + StoryBrand 2024 + obviously-awesome 2024 + built-to-grow 2024 + best-practice-2024 benchmarks.
- **Active Q4-BFCM / Black Friday / Mother's Day / Father's Day / Valentine's Day / Cyber Monday / major-promo / major-launch / major-paid-acquisition-scaling window.** Re-positioning during peak-traffic windows risks 2-5% landing-page-CVR-during-the-cutover + 5-15% per-email-CTR-lag + brand-cohort-attribution-overlay-lag; defer until the peak-traffic window passes.
- **The brand is a single-product commodity re-seller with zero narrative potential** (e.g. unbranded USB cables sold on Amazon). The brand-strategy engine compounds founder-led-narrative + rejection-positioning + category-design + hero-product-economics; commodity re-sellers without these substrates cannot justify the framework.

## The build (2-12 weeks for Path B)

The default 5-pillar Path B build (April Dunford positioning + StoryBrand + brand-clarity-score-80 + ICP-wedge + rejection-positioning for $500k-$5M GMV brands) follows a 6-phase rollout over 4-8 weeks with 2-4 hours of operator time per week. Phases A/B are positioning and messaging foundations (load-bearing); Phases C/D/E are voice, visual, and ops implementation (operational); Phase F is brand-cohort-attribution-overlay + brand-90-day-rollup (measurement). The build can be paused between phases and resumed; do NOT skip phases.

**Phase A — Positioning foundation (Week 1–2, 6–10 hours operator time).**

- **Step A1: Founder interview (60–90 min).** Ask: (i) what is the founding story? (ii) what problem does the customer have that keeps them up at night? (iii) what did you build that nobody else was building? (iv) what do you refuse to do? (v) what is the hero product? (vi) who is the customer? (vii) who is NOT the customer? (viii) what is the unfair advantage? (ix) what 3 competitors do you lose to and why? (x) what 3 competitors do you win against and why? Record in `brand/founder-interview-transcript.md`. The transcript is the load-bearing artifact — every downstream positioning decision references it.
- **Step A2: Customer interviews (5–10 × 30 min each, ≥5 required).** Ask: (i) what problem were you trying to solve when you found us? (ii) what alternatives did you consider? (iii) why did you choose us? (iv) what do you wish we did differently? (v) how would you describe us to a friend? Record in `brand/customer-interviews-{n}.md`. The verbatim language customers use to describe the brand becomes the messaging substrate.
- **Step A3: Positioning canvas (April Dunford framework, 2 hours).** Fill in 5 fields: (i) target customer (specific for-who), (ii) customer pain (the unsolvable-for-others problem), (iii) unique value (the unfair advantage), (iv) alternatives (what customers do without you), (v) category (or category-of-one positioning). Record in `brand/positioning-canvas.md`.
- **Step A4: Positioning statement (April Dunford format, 30 min).** Format: "For [target customer] who [pain], our [product/brand] is the only [category] that [unique value] because [proof]." Iterate to ≤25 words. Record in `brand/positioning-statement.md`.
- **Step A5: ICP-wedge + rejection-positioning (1 hour).** Define 3-5 specific for-who segments (industry, role, life-stage, behavior, demographic) AND 3-5 specific not-for-who segments. The rejection-positioning is the highest-leverage output of this step. Record in `brand/icp-wedge.md` + `brand/rejection-positioning.md`.
- **Step A6: Category-design hypothesis (1 hour).** Answer: are we (i) competing in an existing category (claim a wedge), (ii) redefining an existing category (lead the new definition), or (iii) creating a new category (invent the category)? Record in `brand/category-design.md`.

**Phase B — Messaging foundation (Week 2–3, 4–6 hours operator time).**

- **Step B1: One-liner (1 hour, 5–10 iterations).** Single sentence the operator can deliver in 10 seconds. Test on 5 strangers; iterate to the version that lands in <3 seconds.
- **Step B2: Three-bullet-elevator-pitch (1 hour).** Three bullets: (i) the problem (in customer's words), (ii) the unique solution (your brand), (iii) the proof (1 specific customer outcome or number).
- **Step B3: Tagline (2 hours, 20+ iterations).** Memorable, ≤6 words, reflects the brand-positioning-statement, sounds like the brand-voice. Test the top 3 on 10 strangers; pick the one with highest unaided-recall after 24 hours.
- **Step B4: StoryBrand SB7 brand-script (3 hours).** Fill the 7 blocks: (i) character (the customer), (ii) desire (what they want), (iii) conflict (what stops them), (iv) guide (your brand), (v) plan (3-step solution), (vi) call-to-action (specific next step), (vii) failure-prevention + success-vision (what happens if they do nothing vs succeed). Format: "A [character] who wants [desire] but faces [conflict] meets [guide] who gives them a [plan] to [call-to-action], helping them avoid [failure] and achieve [success]."
- **Step B5: Story-archetype selection (30 min).** Pick 1 of 12 (Hero, Outlaw, Explorer, Sage, Creator, Ruler, Caregiver, Magician, Lover, Jester, Everyman, Innocent). The archetype drives visual-identity, brand-voice-tone, and narrative-style.
- **Step B6: Homepage-hero-messaging + PDP-messaging + email-messaging (2 hours).** Apply the positioning-statement + brand-voice-attributes to each channel. Each channel gets its own 1-sentence headline + 2-bullet subhead.

**Phase C — Voice + visual identity (Week 3–5, 6–12 hours operator time).**

- **Step C1: Brand-voice-attributes (2 hours).** Pick 4-6 named attributes (e.g. Direct + Witty + Warm + Confident). For each, write a "do this" + "don't do this" example. Record in `brand/voice-and-tone-guide.md`.
- **Step C2: Tone-by-channel matrix (2 hours).** For each channel (email, SMS, PDP, ad, landing, influencer, press, customer-service), define the tone override. Email can be longer; SMS must be <160 chars; PDP must be SEO-aware; CX must be empathetic.
- **Step C3: Logo-system + color-palette + typography (4 hours, or $500-$5k agency).** The visual-identity is the second-most-recognized brand signal after the name. Do NOT skip this.
- **Step C4: Photography-style + motion-style (2 hours).** Define the visual-language: lighting, composition, color-grading, motion-tempo. Apply to every brand-asset going forward.
- **Step C5: Brand-asset-library (4 hours one-time, 1 hour/week ongoing).** Centralize logos, colors, fonts, photography, illustrations, templates. Use Figma, Canva Pro, or Frame.io.

**Phase D — Brand-ops setup (Week 5–6, 4–8 hours operator time).**

- **Step D1: Wire brand-voice-and-tone-guide into Klaviyo (2 hours).** Create brand-templates in Klaviyo's library with the voice-attributes baked in. All new emails start from a brand-template, not a blank canvas.
- **Step D2: Wire brand-voice into Postscript (1 hour).** SMS templates with the voice-and-tone-guide applied.
- **Step D3: Wire brand-voice into Gorgias (2 hours).** CX macros with the brand-tone-by-channel override. New CX agents see the brand-tone-by-audience matrix in their onboarding.
- **Step D4: Wire ICP-wedge + rejection-positioning into ad-targeting (2 hours).** Set Meta + Google audiences to EXCLUDE the rejection-positioning segments. Most operators skip this and waste 10-30% of ad spend on the wrong ICP.
- **Step D5: Wire brand-positioning into Triple Whale cohort-attribution (1 hour).** Create a brand-cohort in Triple Whale that tags every order with the ICP-wedge-segment. This becomes the brand-cohort-attribution-overlay.

**Phase E — Brand-cohort-attribution + brand-90-day-rollup (Week 6–8, 4 hours/week).**

- **Step E1: Brand-cohort-LTV-overlay (1 hour/wk).** Triple Whale cohort-LTV tagged by ICP-wedge-segment. Compare 90-day LTV of brand-cohort vs paid-cohort.
- **Step E2: Brand-NPS + brand-CSAT + brand-CES (1 hour/wk).** Survey at 30-day-post-purchase: "How likely are you to recommend [brand]?" + "How satisfied are you with the [brand] experience?" + "How easy was it to [purchase/use the brand]?". Track weekly.
- **Step E3: Brand-share-of-voice (30 min/wk).** Use Brand24 or Mention to track social + PR + blog mentions of brand vs category. Goal: brand-share-of-voice ≥5% in 90 days.
- **Step E4: Brand-90-day-rollup (2 hours/quarter).** Combine brand-NPS + brand-CSAT + brand-CES + brand-aided-awareness + brand-unaided-awareness + brand-share-of-voice + brand-cohort-LTV-overlay + earned-media-mentions + founder-led-content-engagement + branded-search-volume + direct-traffic-% into a 1-page report. Iterate positioning/voice/messaging every 90 days based on the rollup.

**Phase F — Brand-led-growth engine (Week 8+, ongoing).**

- **Step F1: Founder-led-content-engine (2-4 hours/wk).** Founder posts 2-3x/wk on Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, or Twitter using the brand-positioning + brand-storytelling + customer-storytelling patterns. This is the highest-ROI growth lever per Justin Marés + DemandJen + founder-led-marketing 2024.
- **Step F2: Earned-media-engine (1 hour/wk).** Pitch 1 journalist/blogger/week using the brand-positioning + brand-storytelling angle. Use TheGrommet, Uncrate, Bless This Stuff, Huckberry, Featured for product-discovery; Use Modern Retail, Glossy, Digiday for industry-PR.
- **Step F3: Brand-community-engine (1 hour/wk).** Build a brand-community (private IG group, Discord, Substack, or SMS-list) where the founder + brand-voice interact directly with customers. Compounds brand-loyalty + brand-word-of-mouth + brand-referral + brand-UGC.
- **Step F4: Brand-referral-engine (2 hours initial + 1 hour/wk).** Launch a brand-referral program (FriendBuy / ReferralCandy) using the brand-positioning + brand-voice + brand-tone. Customers refer people like them.
- **Step F5: Brand-loyalty-engine (2 hours initial + 1 hour/wk).** Launch a brand-loyalty program (Smile / Yotpo Loyalty / LoyaltyLion) using the brand-positioning + brand-storytelling + hero-product-economics.

**Total time for Path B:** 20–40 hours operator time over 4–8 weeks. Verify: operator has committed 2-4 contiguous hours per week + founder-time for ≥4 hours of interviews + customer-time for ≥5 customer-interviews. ## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

1. **Writing the positioning-statement without the customer-interviews.** Result: the positioning is founder-aspiration, not customer-validated. 80% of positioning statements fail Gate B (would the customer say the same thing in their own words?) within 90 days. Fix: ≥5 customer-interviews BEFORE the positioning-canvas. The customer-verbatim language is the substrate.
2. **Skipping rejection-positioning because "we don't want to alienate anyone."** Result: the brand blurs into the category-blend, lands on every competitor-comparison-page as a "similar to X" footnote, and gets 0-2% landing-page-CVR. Fix: write 3-5 specific not-for-who-segments. The rejection-positioning is the highest-leverage output of the framework — without it, the brand has no wedge.
3. **Borrowed-positioning from Amazon-listings OR competitor-homepage.** Result: the brand sounds generic ("premium X for everyone"). The 1-second-deliverable test fails. Fix: write the one-liner FIRST from the customer-interview-verbatim language, NOT from the competitor's home-page.
4. **Voice-attributes too vague ("we are professional and friendly").** Result: every writer / CX agent / email-designer interprets differently. Fix: name 4-6 SPECIFIC attributes (e.g. "Direct + Witty + Warm + Confident") with a "do this / don't do this" example for each.
5. **Brand-script generic ("we help customers achieve their goals").** Result: StoryBrand SB7 score <4/7, the customer-as-hero is unclear, the brand-as-guide is invisible, the conflict is unstated. Fix: fill all 7 blocks with customer-verbatim language; the brand-as-guide role must be specific (e.g. "the only DTC brand that...").
6. **Visual-identity-system-from-fiverr OR from-templated-canva-templates.** Result: brand-recognition <20%, visual-identity-recognition-test fails. Fix: budget $500-$5k for logo + color-palette + typography + photography-style; the visual-identity is the second-most-recognized signal after the brand-name.
7. **Building the brand-strategy but NOT wiring it into Klaviyo + Postscript + Gorgias + Triple Whale.** Result: every email/SMS/CX-message starts from a blank canvas, the brand-voice drifts in 30 days, brand-cohort-attribution-overlay is absent. Fix: Steps D1-D5 are load-bearing; do NOT skip them.
8. **Measuring brand-NPS at the wrong time (immediately post-purchase or pre-fulfillment).** Result: the NPS measures shipping-speed, not brand. Fix: NPS at 30-day-post-delivery, on the second purchase, or at the loyalty-program-enrollment moment.
9. **No brand-90-day-rollup cadence.** Result: the brand-positioning + brand-voice + brand-messaging drift in 60-90 days because no one re-reads the positioning-doc. Fix: 2-hour quarterly review; iterate every 90 days.
10. **Founder-led-content-engine absent (founder is "too busy" to post).** Result: brand-as-marketing-channel = 0%, brand-equity-compounds-slowly, brand-cohort-LTV = paid-cohort-LTV. Fix: the founder IS the brand; commit 2-4 hours/wk to founder-led-content. Hire a content-assistant if needed.
11. **Story-archetype mismatch (e.g. brand archetype is Caregiver but voice is bold and edgy).** Result: the brand feels schizophrenic. Fix: pick 1 archetype and let it drive voice, visual, and narrative.
12. **Rejection-positioning too broad ("we are not for people who don't care about quality").** Result: the rejection-positioning excludes nobody. Fix: write specific, narrow, named segments (e.g. "we are NOT for people shopping on Amazon for the cheapest option" or "we are NOT for traditionalists who want the same product they've bought for 20 years").
13. **Brand-positioning-statement >25 words.** Result: the operator can't deliver it in 1 second; the customer can't repeat it. Fix: cut to ≤25 words; iterate 10+ times.
14. **No ICP-wedge quantification ("our customer is a 25-45 year old urban professional").** Result: the ICP-wedge is generic, the rejection-positioning is hollow, the ad-targeting is unfiltered. Fix: ≥3 specific dimensions (industry, role, life-stage, behavior, demographic, location) for each for-who-segment.
15. **Using the brand-positioning-statement as the homepage-headline.** Result: the homepage-headline reads like a positioning-doc, not a customer-facing headline. Fix: the positioning-statement is internal; the homepage-hero-messaging is the customer-facing translation (1 sentence + 2 bullets).
16. **Brand-cohort-attribution-overlay wired but never read.** Result: brand-cohort-LTV tag exists in Triple Whale but the operator never opens the report. Fix: brand-90-day-rollup must reference brand-cohort-LTV; without this, the brand-strategy is unmeasurable.
17. **No earned-media-engine.** Result: brand-share-of-voice = 0-1%, brand-unaided-awareness flatlines, brand-equity-compounds-slowly. Fix: 1 hour/wk pitching journalists + using TheGrommet / Uncrate / Bless This Stuff / Huckberry / Featured / Modern Retail / Glossy / Digiday.
18. **Treating brand-strategy as a one-time build, not a quarterly iteration.** Result: positioning drifts, voice drifts, messaging drifts, category-context shifts. Fix: brand-90-day-rollup is the rhythm; iterate positioning/voice/messaging every 90 days based on the rollup.
