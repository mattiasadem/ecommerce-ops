## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing WAT-tier (aggregation-tier) with WR-tier (withholding-rate-tier) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.39].** WAT-tier answers WHICH aggregation-cadence the merchant uses (zero-aggregation-pool + payer-level-rollup-pool + entity-level-rollup-pool + state-level-rollup-pool + IRS-level-rollup-pool); WR-tier answers WHICH withholding-rate-strategy (zero-withholding + treaty-rate-withholding + entity-rate-withholding + state-withholding-only + 30%-backup-withholding). The two axes are orthogonal — a WAT1 zero-aggregation creator may use WR1 zero-withholding (typical, no-rollup-required) OR WR2 treaty-rate-withholding (rare, no-payer-rollup-required); a WAT5 IRS-level-aggregation creator is forced into WR5 30%-backup-withholding (24%-IRS-published-rate-2026-pool). **Fix:** carry WAT-tier and WR-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung precedence ladders (WAT1-WAT2-WAT3-WAT4-WAT5 and WR1-WR2-WR3-WR4-WR5) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-WAT-tier × per-WR-tier 5×5 cross-axis grid that scores 25 distinct WAT×WR pool combinations.

2. **Pitfall — WAT1 zero-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected IRS-payer-level-rollup-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses WAT1 zero-aggregation pool (no-1042-S-no-1099-NEC-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-border-traffic may have a payout that crosses USD-EU-border without a 1042-S-payer-level-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$50k-annual-1042-S-payer-level-rollup-maint + Jan-31-deadline-tracking on the payout event. **Fix:** the withholding-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's cross-border-traffic-share + cohort's aggregation-tolerance — WAT1 score only if cohort-cross-border-share <10% AND cohort-WAT-tolerance = high; WAT2 payer-level-aggregation fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.

3. **Pitfall — WAT2 payer-level-aggregation creators on a high-entity-cohort fail IRS-Form-1042-S-entity-rollup-requirement.** IRS Form 1042-S 2026 requires entity-level-rollup for cross-border-payouts >$600/yr per entity; WAT2 payer-level-aggregation (payer-level only) is insufficient for high-entity-cohorts, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to WAT3 entity-level-aggregation is suddenly required to file IRS Form 1042-S 2026 per-entity rollup with W-8BEN-E-entity-attestation. **Fix:** the withholding-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's entity-cohort-traffic + aggregation-tenor — WAT3 score only if cohort-entity-share >5% AND merchant-is-1042-S-registered; WAT2 payer-level-aggregation fallback for non-1042-S-registered merchants.

4. **Pitfall — WAT3 entity-level-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$70k-annual-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The entity-level-rollup-pool + W-8BEN-E-entity-rollup + 1-rollover-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $70k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the withholding-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — WAT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-US-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to WAT1 zero-aggregation or WAT2 payer-level-aggregation creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

5. **Pitfall — WAT4 state-level-aggregation creators on a high-multi-state-cohort fail cross-state-aggregation-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to WAT4 state-level-aggregation is REQUIRED to track cross-state-W-4-block-rollup for every state-payout event; non-compliance triggers cross-state-aggregation-enforcement and additional-state-penalties. **Fix:** the withholding-aggregation classifier must verify the merchant's cross-state-aggregation-tracking-status — WAT4 score only if merchant-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active; WAT2 payer-level-aggregation fallback for non-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active merchants.

6. **Pitfall — WAT5 IRS-level-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$120k-annual-maint-cost for high-margin-cohorts.** The IRS-level-aggregation-pool + 24%-IRS-published-rate-2026-pool + 365-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $120k/yr; even high-margin cohorts may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout if the cohort's payout-frequency is low. **Fix:** the withholding-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency BEFORE scoring resonance — WAT5 score 0 for cohorts with annual-payout-frequency <500 AND WAT-notional <$1M. Route to WAT3 entity-level-aggregation creators for low-frequency fallbacks.

7. **Pitfall — WAT2 payer-level-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort fail EU DAC7 2026-payer-level-rollup-requirement.** EU DAC7 2026 requires payer-level-rollup-reporting for cross-border-payouts >€2000/yr per payer; WAT2 payer-level-aggregation (1-rollover-per-year) is REQUIRED to report by Jan-31-deadline. **Fix:** the withholding-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's EU-creator-count + annual-payout-frequency — WAT2 score only if cohort-EU-creator-count >30 AND annual-payout-frequency >2,000; WAT1 zero-aggregation fallback for non-DAC7-required cohorts.

8. **Pitfall — WAT3 entity-level-aggregation creators silently demoted to WAT2 payer-level-aggregation because of entity-attestation-staleness-event.** A creator whose entity-attestation was set 90 days ago may have a stale entity-attestation-status that doesn't match the current entity-attestation-status, and the merchant's tax-team may silently mark-down the entity-pool to payer-level (WAT2) on the next rollup-event. **Fix:** the withholding-aggregation classifier must read the live entity-attestation-table (per CaptivateA Withholding Reconciliation Aggregation 2026 + IRS Form 1042-S 2026 + IRS TIN Matching System 2026) and re-derive WAT-tier on every rollup event.

9. **Pitfall — WAT4 state-level-aggregation creators on a multi-state-cohort fail state-AG-enforcement-risk.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to WAT4 state-level-aggregation across multiple states is REQUIRED to file cross-state-aggregation on every state-payout; failure triggers state-AG-enforcement (e.g., CA-FTB-state-W-4-block + NY-DTF-state-W-4-block + TX-Franchise-state-W-4-block + FL-DR-1-state-W-4-block) and potential $10k-$50k state-AG-penalties. **Fix:** the withholding-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's state-distribution-count + state-payout-event-volume — WAT4 score only if cohort-state-distribution-count >5 states AND merchant-state-AG-enforcement-history-clean; WAT2 payer-level-aggregation fallback for risky cohorts.

10. **Pitfall — WAT5 IRS-level-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$120k-annual-maint-cost for multi-jurisdiction-merchants.** The IRS-level-aggregation-pool + 24%-IRS-published-rate-2026-pool + multi-jurisdiction-rollup-maint (IRS Form 1042-S 2026 + IRS TIN Matching System 2026 + IRS B-Notice 2026 + IRS 30%-backup-withholding 2026 + EU DAC7 2026 + UK RDEC 2026 + Japan NTA 2026 + Brazil DARF 2026 + Mexico ISR 2026) requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $120k/yr minimum; cohorts with low-frequency-payout-events to multi-jurisdiction-creators may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout. **Fix:** the withholding-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency + cohort-jurisdiction-diversity BEFORE scoring resonance — WAT5 score 0 for cohorts with jurisdiction-diversity >3 jurisdictions AND annual-payout-frequency <1000.

11. **Pitfall — WAT4 state-level-aggregation creators silently demoted to WAT2 payer-level-aggregation on a state-aggregation-tracking-failed-event.** A creator whose state-aggregation-tracking was tracked monthly may have the monthly-tracking silently fail (e.g., the merchant's state-aggregation-database has been unavailable to execute the monthly-rollup); the merchant is REQUIRED to fall back to WAT2 payer-level-aggregation on next payout. **Fix:** the withholding-aggregation classifier must maintain a `p.wat_monthly_rollup_status` field — set to true on rollup-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to WAT2 on next payout AND adds a state-aggregation-tracking-reconciliation line-item.

12. **Pitfall — WAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's withholding-aggregation-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer WAT1 zero-aggregation creators (low-maint-cost, no-1042-S-no-1099-NEC, no-50-state-tracking) over WAT5 IRS-level-aggregation creators (high-maint-cost, daily-aggregation-tracking, 24%-IRS-published-rate-2026-pool). A WAT1 zero-aggregation creator may be WAT-clean but WAT-maint-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-WAT-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — WAT1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer WAT5 (e.g., cohorts with high multi-jurisdiction-traffic). The cohort's WAT-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-WAT-maint-cost-sensitivity × cohort-multi-jurisdiction-traffic-preference.

13. **Pitfall — WAT-tier cross-axis with PR-tier silently double-counts aggregation-cost on cross-platform payments.** A creator on WAT1 zero-aggregation with PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay as secondary platform is potentially double-charged aggregation-cost (PayPal's auto-aggregation-generation + merchant's WAT-rollup on the same payout). The withholding-aggregation classifier must not double-count aggregation-cost on cross-platform-cohorts. **Fix:** the WAT-tier × PR-tier cross-axis must apply secondary-platform-WAT-conditional — WAT1 × PR2 score uses PayPal-Mass-Pay-WAT as the secondary; the cohort's primary WAT-rule is zero-aggregation.

14. **Pitfall — WAT3 entity-level-aggregation creators mis-classified as WAT2 payer-level-aggregation because of entity-attestation-stale state.** A creator whose entity-attestation was set 90 days ago may have a stale attestation that doesn't match the current entity-attestation-status, and the merchant's tax-team may silently mark-down the entity-pool to payer-level (WAT2) on the next rollup-event. The actual state is "WAT3-pending — entity-attestation-stale". **Fix:** the withholding-aggregation classifier must distinguish between WAT2-confirmed / WAT3-pending / WAT3-confirmed states; route WAT3-pending creators to WAT2 payer-level-aggregation as a temporary fallback AND flag for entity-attestation-refresh.

15. **Pitfall — WAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's withholding-aggregation-residency-preference.** A cohort with US-domestic-only preference (no cross-border-traffic) requires WAT1 zero-aggregation pool; a cohort with EU-only preference requires WAT2 payer-level-aggregation pool (or WAT3 entity-level-aggregation for EU-LLCs); a cohort with JP-only preference requires WAT3 entity-level-aggregation pool (or WAT2 payer-level-aggregation for JP-individuals); a cohort with BR-only preference requires WAT4 state-level-aggregation pool — but the WAT-tier model US-centric-by-default; cohort must use WAT4 state-level-aggregation as BR-equivalent. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-WAT-residency-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — WAT5 × US-domestic-cohort score 0; route to WAT1 zero-aggregation fallbacks. Add a "WAT-tier × cohort-jurisdiction" overlay.
