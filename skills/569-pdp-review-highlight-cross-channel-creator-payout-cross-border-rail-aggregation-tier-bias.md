## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing CRAT-tier (cross-border-rail-aggregation-tier) with CR-tier (cross-border-rail-tier) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.41].** CRAT-tier answers WHICH aggregation-cadence the merchant uses (zero-rail-aggregation-pool + daily-rail-aggregation-pool + weekly-rail-aggregation-pool + monthly-rail-aggregation-pool + quarterly-rail-aggregation-pool); CR-tier answers WHICH cross-border-rail-route (USD-domestic + USD-EU-cross-border + USD-JP-cross-border + USD-BR-cross-border + USD-MX-cross-border). The two axes are orthogonal — a CRAT1 zero-aggregation creator may use CR1 USD-domestic (typical, no-rail-rollup-required) or CR2 USD-EU-cross-border (rare, no-rail-rollup-required); a CRAT5 quarterly-rail-aggregation creator is forced into CR5 USD-MX-cross-border (Wise-MX-quarterly-rail-rollup-pool). **Fix:** carry CRAT-tier and CR-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung precedence ladders (CRAT1-CRAT2-CRAT3-CRAT4-CRAT5 and CR1-CR2-CR3-CR4-CR5) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-CRAT-tier × per-CR-tier 5×5 cross-axis grid that scores 25 distinct CRAT×CR rail combinations.

2. **Pitfall — CRAT1 zero-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected Wise-EU-daily-rail-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses CRAT1 zero-aggregation pool (USD-domestic-no-rail-rollup-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-border-traffic may have a payout that crosses USD-EU-border without a Wise-EU-daily-rail-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$55k-annual-Wise-EU-daily-rail-maint + 365-rollovers-per-year on the payout event. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's cross-border-traffic-share + cohort's aggregation-tolerance — CRAT1 score only if cohort-cross-border-share <10% AND cohort-CRAT-tolerance = high; CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.

3. **Pitfall — CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation creators on a high-JP-cohort fail Wise-JP-weekly-rail-rollup-requirement.** Wise Mass Pay 2026 requires weekly-rail-rollup for cross-border-payouts >$600/yr per JP-entity; CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation (daily-rail only) is insufficient for high-JP-cohorts, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to CRAT3 weekly-rail-aggregation is suddenly required to file Wise-JP-weekly-rail-rollup with EU-EMIR-MiFIR-attestation. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's JP-cohort-traffic + aggregation-tenor — CRAT3 score only if cohort-JP-share >5% AND merchant-is-Wise-Mass-Pay-registered; CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation fallback for non-Wise-Mass-Pay-registered merchants.

4. **Pitfall — CRAT3 weekly-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$75k-annual-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The weekly-rail-rollup-pool + Wise-JP-weekly-rail-rollup + 52-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $75k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — CRAT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-US-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to CRAT1 zero-aggregation or CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

5. **Pitfall — CRAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a high-BR-cohort fail cross-monthly-rail-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to CRAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation is REQUIRED to track cross-monthly-Wise-BR-rail-rollup for every BR-payout event; non-compliance triggers cross-monthly-rail-enforcement and additional-monthly-rail-penalties. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail-aggregation classifier must verify the merchant's cross-monthly-rail-tracking-status — CRAT4 score only if merchant-cross-monthly-rail-tracking-active; CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation fallback for non-cross-monthly-rail-tracking-active merchants.

6. **Pitfall — CRAT5 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for high-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-rail-aggregation-pool + Wise-MX-quarterly-rail-rollup + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $130k/yr; even high-margin cohorts may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout if the cohort's payout-frequency is low. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency BEFORE scoring resonance — CRAT5 score 0 for cohorts with annual-payout-frequency <500 AND CRAT-notional <$1M. Route to CRAT3 weekly-rail-aggregation creators for low-frequency fallbacks.

7. **Pitfall — CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort fail EU EMIR 2026-rail-rollup-requirement.** EU EMIR 2026 requires daily-rail-rollup-reporting for cross-border-payouts >€2000/yr per EU-payer; CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation (365-rollovers-per-year) is REQUIRED to report by T+1-deadline. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's EU-creator-count + annual-payout-frequency — CRAT2 score only if cohort-EU-creator-count >30 AND annual-payout-frequency >2,000; CRAT1 zero-aggregation fallback for non-EMIR-required cohorts.

8. **Pitfall — CRAT3 weekly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation because of Wise-Mass-Pay-staleness-event.** A creator whose Wise-Mass-Pay registration was set 90 days ago may have a stale registration-status that doesn't match the current registration-status, and the merchant's treasury-team may silently mark-down the weekly-rail to daily-rail (CRAT2) on the next rollup-event. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail-aggregation classifier must read the live Wise-Mass-Pay-table (per CaptivateA Cross Border Rail Aggregation 2026 + Wise Mass Pay 2026 + EU EMIR 2026 + EU MiFIR 2026) and re-derive CRAT-tier on every rollup event.

9. **Pitfall — CRAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a multi-state-cohort fail state-rail-enforcement-risk.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to CRAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation across multiple jurisdictions is REQUIRED to file cross-jurisdiction-rail on every monthly-payout; failure triggers jurisdiction-AG-enforcement (e.g., BCB-Circular-3-978-Wise-BR-monthly-rail + BCB-Circular-4-373-monthly-rail + Brazil-CVM-monthly-rail + Banxico-CNBV-monthly-rail) and potential $10k-$50k jurisdiction-AG-penalties. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's jurisdiction-distribution-count + monthly-payout-event-volume — CRAT4 score only if cohort-jurisdiction-distribution-count >5 jurisdictions AND merchant-jurisdiction-AG-enforcement-history-clean; CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation fallback for risky cohorts.

10. **Pitfall — CRAT5 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for multi-jurisdiction-merchants.** The quarterly-rail-aggregation-pool + Wise-MX-quarterly-rail-rollup + multi-jurisdiction-rail-maint (Wise Mass Pay 2026 + Stripe Connect Multi-Currency 2026 + PayPal Mass Pay Multi-Currency 2026 + BCB Circular 3.978/2020 + Mexico Banxico 2026 + Mexico CNBV 2026 + JP FX Margin Rules 2026 + JP FSA 2026 + JP MOF FX 2026) requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $130k/yr minimum; cohorts with low-frequency-payout-events to multi-jurisdiction-creators may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency + cohort-jurisdiction-diversity BEFORE scoring resonance — CRAT5 score 0 for cohorts with jurisdiction-diversity >3 jurisdictions AND annual-payout-frequency <1000.

11. **Pitfall — CRAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation on a monthly-rail-tracking-failed-event.** A creator whose monthly-rail-tracking was tracked monthly may have the monthly-tracking silently fail (e.g., the merchant's monthly-rail-database has been unavailable to execute the monthly-rollup); the merchant is REQUIRED to fall back to CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation on next payout. **Fix:** the cross-border-rail-aggregation classifier must maintain a `p.crat_monthly_rollup_status` field — set to true on rollup-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to CRAT2 on next payout AND adds a monthly-rail-tracking-reconciliation line-item.

12. **Pitfall — CRAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's cross-border-rail-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer CRAT1 zero-aggregation creators (low-maint-cost, USD-domestic-no-rail-rollup, no-50-jurisdiction-tracking) over CRAT5 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators (high-maint-cost, quarterly-rail-tracking, Wise-MX-quarterly-rail-rollup-pool). A CRAT1 zero-aggregation creator may be CRAT-clean but CRAT-maint-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-CRAT-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — CRAT1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer CRAT5 (e.g., cohorts with high multi-jurisdiction-traffic). The cohort's CRAT-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-CRAT-maint-cost-sensitivity × cohort-multi-jurisdiction-traffic-preference.

13. **Pitfall — CRAT-tier cross-axis with PR-tier silently double-counts rail-rollup-cost on cross-platform payments.** A creator on CRAT1 zero-aggregation with PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay as secondary platform is potentially double-charged rail-rollup-cost (PayPal's auto-rail-rollup + merchant's CRAT-rollup on the same payout). The cross-border-rail-aggregation classifier must not double-count rail-rollup-cost on cross-platform-cohorts. **Fix:** the CRAT-tier × PR-tier cross-axis must apply secondary-platform-CRAT-conditional — CRAT1 × PR2 score uses PayPal-Mass-Pay-CRAT as the secondary; the cohort's primary CRAT-rule is zero-aggregation.

14. **Pitfall — CRAT3 weekly-rail-aggregation creators mis-classified as CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation because of Wise-Mass-Pay-stale state.** A creator whose Wise-Mass-Pay registration was set 90 days ago may have a stale registration that doesn't match the current registration-status, and the merchant's treasury-team may silently mark-down the weekly-rail to daily-rail (CRAT2) on the next rollup-event. The actual state is "CRAT3-pending — registration-stale". **Fix:** the cross-border-rail-aggregation classifier must distinguish between CRAT2-confirmed / CRAT3-pending / CRAT3-confirmed states; route CRAT3-pending creators to CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation as a temporary fallback AND flag for registration-refresh.

15. **Pitfall — CRAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's cross-border-rail-residency-preference.** A cohort with US-domestic-only preference (no cross-border-traffic) requires CRAT1 zero-aggregation pool; a cohort with EU-only preference requires CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation pool (or CRAT3 weekly-rail-aggregation for EU-LLCs); a cohort with JP-only preference requires CRAT3 weekly-rail-aggregation pool (or CRAT2 daily-rail-aggregation for JP-individuals); a cohort with BR-only preference requires CRAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation pool — but the CRAT-tier model US-centric-by-default; cohort must use CRAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation as BR-equivalent. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-CRAT-residency-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — CRAT5 × US-domestic-cohort score 0; route to CRAT1 zero-aggregation fallbacks. Add a "CRAT-tier × cohort-jurisdiction" overlay.
