## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

**Cluster A — Per-cohort-LTV-overlay-wiring**

1. **Pitfall: Treating "first-order-AOV" as the conversion-value-passed-to-CAPI** (the canonical "we send Meta a $67 first-order-checkout-AOV and Meta optimizes toward customers who spend $67 first-order instead of $247 predicted-LTV" anti-pattern). The Meta + Google + TikTok algorithm is a mirror — it learns from whatever value you pass. Pass first-order-AOV, it learns to find first-order-spend-maximizers (the canonical refund-and-churn cohort). Pass contribution-margin, it learns to find first-order-margin-positives. Pass pLTV-score, it learns to find 12-month-value-maximizers (the canonical retainable subscription-cohort). **Fix:** Enable pLTV-signal-bidding via Voyantis OR AdZeta ValueBid OR Servo value-layer OR Angler AI OR in-house-BigQuery-ML-model with per-cohort-pLTV-score (12-month + 24-month) with 85%+ accuracy by day-7. Pass pLTV-score as the CAPI value-parameter on Meta + Google + TikTok + Snap + Pinterest + Reddit + Microsoft + Amazon DSP + Walmart Connect. Expected outcome: 20-30% CAC reduction within 60 days + 15% ROAS lift on the signal-upgrade alone + 30-45% higher 12-month repeat-purchase-rate on pLTV-bid-cohorts vs AOV-bid-cohorts per AdZeta 2026 + Servo 2026 + Voyantis 2025 + Angler AI 2025 + Capconvert 2025 benchmarks.

2. **Pitfall: Using 7-day-click / 1-day-view attribution-window as the value-baseline** (the canonical "we send Meta the 7-day-click-1-day-view-purchase-value and Meta optimizes toward 7-day-conversion-pattern-customers instead of 12-month-LTV-customers" anti-pattern). The 7-day-window only sees the first-order. The algorithm has no idea whether the customer will be a 12-month-retainable-subscriber or a 30-day-discount-hunter-refunder. **Fix:** Switch to per-cohort-pLTV-value-passing as the conversion-value (12-month or 24-month predicted revenue), not 7-day-click-1-day-view. Per AdZeta 2026 + Servo 2026 + Voyantis 2025 + Angler AI 2025 benchmarks, brands that pass pLTV-score see 20-30% CAC reduction within 60 days and 30-45% higher 12-month repeat-purchase-rate on pLTV-bid-cohorts vs AOV-bid-cohorts.

3. **Pitfall: Optimizing toward gross-revenue instead of gross-margin** (the canonical "we send Meta the $200 first-order-AOV and Meta optimizes toward big-first-order-orders-but-the-COGS-is-$180-and-the-margin-is-only-$20" anti-pattern). The 7-day-window only sees first-order-revenue, not first-order-margin. A $60 first-order at 70% margin ($42 contribution-margin) is more profitable than a $200 first-order at 10% margin ($20 contribution-margin), but the algorithm treats them identically if you send raw-revenue. **Fix:** Enable contribution-margin-bidding-via-Servo-value-layer or in-house-margin-overlay. Compute per-order contribution-margin (revenue - COGS - shipping - fulfillment - payment-processing-fee - discount - return-cost) and pass as the CAPI value-parameter. Per Servo 2026 benchmarks, brands that pass contribution-margin see 15-25% CAC reduction within 60 days vs AOV-bidding.

4. **Pitfall: Sending revenue-as-value-but-the-product-mix-is-margin-variable** (the canonical "we have a $200 AOV mix of 40% high-margin + 30% mid-margin + 30% low-margin SKUs and Meta routes budget toward low-margin-spending-customers because their first-order-AOV is higher" anti-pattern). The algorithm treats every $1 of AOV identically, regardless of per-SKU-margin. **Fix:** Enable per-SKU-margin-overlay-via-QuickBooks-Online-or-NetSuite-or-Ramp-or-Brex-or-Mercury. Compute per-SKU-margin + per-cohort-by-SKU-margin-mix + per-cohort-margin-overlay. Pass per-cohort-margin as the CAPI value-parameter (or pass per-cohort-pLTV-score with margin-overlay-baked-in).
