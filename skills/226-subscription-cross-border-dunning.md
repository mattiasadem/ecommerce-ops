# Subscription multi-currency + cross-border dunning (EU + UK + AU + CA)

> Move #14.2.2 is the canonical **multi-currency + cross-border dunning layer** every DTC subscription brand with ≥10% non-US subscribers needs AFTER Move #14.2 (dunning) AND Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) are live: deploy a **Recharge + Stay AI + Stripe Billing Smart Retries + Chargebee + Recurly + ProfitWell + Adyen + Checkout.com + Braintree + GoCardless + Airwallex** multi-currency dunning engine that handles **EU SEPA + UK BACS + AU BECS + CA PAD + cross-border 3DS / SCA exemptions + Europay failure-reason-specific copy + multi-currency retry scheduling (per-bank-day-not-per-US-day)** for the **EUR / GBP / AUD / CAD subscriber cohorts** that today absorb **5–18% of involuntary churn** but are recovered at only **14–28% of US recovery rates** (per ProfitWell 2024 + Smart Insights 2024 + Statista 2024 cross-border benchmarks). **Stay AI + Recharge international** is the canonical Path A pick for Shopify-DTC brands with EU/UK/AU/CA subscribers; **Stripe Billing Smart Retries + multi-currency + adaptive 3DS** is the canonical Path B pick for cross-platform subscriptions; **Chargebee Multi-currency + Recurly + ProfitWell international** is the canonical Path C pick for $1M+ GMV brands with multi-entity needs; **Adyen + custom-routing-engine** is the canonical Path D pick for $10M+ GMV engineering-heavy brands. Year-1 ROI band **8:1–26:1** with a default **16:1** at $1M subscription GMV Path A. Ship AFTER Move #14.2 (dunning) shipped ≥60 days AND Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) shipped ≥30 days AND ≥10% non-US subscribers AND ≥$15k non-US MRR, in a 4-phase build over 21–35 days. Companion artifact scope: this skill synthesizes the canonical 6-pillar multi-currency + cross-border dunning framework (Pillar 1 multi-currency-display-+-settlement-+-FX-hedge / Pillar 2 SEPA-BACS-BECS-PAD-failure-reason-specific-copy / Pillar 3 3DS-+-SCA-exemption-engine / Pillar 4 multi-currency-retry-scheduling-+-per-bank-day-cadence / Pillar 5 cross-border-pre-charge-warning-+-multi-language-card-update / Pillar 6 cross-border-involuntary-churn-+-recovery-rate-segmentation) for $30k+ non-US-MRR subscription brands. Distinct from `subscription-dunning` (Move #14.2 — US-centric dunning system with USD retry timing; Move #14.2.2 extends with multi-currency + cross-border + bank-rail-specific failure-reason copy), from `subscription-ai-failure-prediction` (Move #14.2.1 — AI failure scoring engine that works on US-centric card-update data; Move #14.2.2 layers cross-border 3DS exemption + Europay-failure-reason-specific copy on top), from `international-expansion` (Move #25 — full international expansion playbook including shipping + tax + localization; Move #14.2.2 is the SUBSCRIPTION-specific cross-border layer that fires AFTER Move #25 multi-currency-display is live), and from `payments-orchestration-routing` (Move #95 — general payment routing + smart-routing across gateways; Move #14.2.2 is the SUBSCRIPTION + cross-border dunning-specific retry-engine that builds on Move #95's routing substrate).

## When to use this skill

**Use this skill when the operator has shipped Move #14.2 (US-centric dunning) ≥60 days AND Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) ≥30 days AND is observing that non-US subscribers (EU/UK/AU/CA) recover at only 14–28% of US recovery rates (per ProfitWell 2024 + Smart Insights 2024 cross-border benchmarks) AND ≥10% of active subscribers are non-US AND the operator wants to lift cross-border recovery-rate by handling multi-currency retry scheduling + 3DS / SCA exemptions + Europay / BACS / SEPA failure-reason-specific copy.** Multi-currency cross-border dunning is structurally distinct from Move #14.2's US-centric dunning (which optimizes USD retry timing + US-bank failure-reason copy) and from Move #14.2.1's AI failure prediction (which optimizes per-subscription failure-risk score without per-bank-rail or per-currency context). Move #14.2.2 is the **multi-currency + cross-border dunning layer** — the operator should only initiate it when ALL of the following are true:

You have:
- **Shopify (or Ikas / BigCommerce / WooCommerce / headless) DTC store** with admin API access AND a subscription app with multi-currency support (Recharge International / Stay AI / Skio / Bold / Loop Subscriptions / Appstle / Subbly / Smartrr / Okendo Subscriptions / Chargebee Multi-currency / Recurly Multi-currency / Stripe Billing Multi-currency / Paddle / Maxio / Zuora).
- **≥500 active subscribers** AND **≥10% non-US subscribers** (EU + UK + AU + CA combined) — below this threshold, the multi-currency retry-scheduling substrate does not pay back the per-currency-rule-engine complexity; deferred single-currency retry timing + manual currency-specific card-update reminders suffice.
- **Move #14.2 (US-centric dunning) shipped ≥60 days** AND ≥3 baseline Klaviyo segments defined (active_subscriber / past_due_subscriber / at_risk_subscriber / recovered_subscriber) — the multi-currency retry-scheduling substrate depends on Move #14.2's failure-recovery metrics as the post-charge truth-label source AND on Klaviyo + Postscript as the cross-channel reminder substrate.
- **Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) shipped ≥30 days** AND the failure-risk-scoring-engine is fed cross-border failure-history (EU SEPA failures + UK BACS failures + AU BECS failures + CA PAD failures) — without this, Move #14.2.2 cannot route high-risk subscriptions to the per-bank-rail-specific retry schedule.
- **Move #25 (international-expansion) shipped ≥90 days** AND multi-currency display is live AND ≥2 currency settlement wallets active (USD + EUR + GBP + AUD + CAD) — without multi-currency display, the operator cannot test multi-currency retry timing; without settlement wallets, the operator cannot route SEPA Direct Debit vs BACS vs BECS vs PAD rails.
- **≥$15k non-US MRR / ≥$180k non-US ARR** AND ≥$30 AOV AND ≥12 months of historical cross-border payment-failure data in the subscription-app data-warehouse — below these thresholds, the per-bank-rail data is too sparse to train reliable cross-border failure-rate benchmarks; manual per-bank-rail retry rules are sufficient.
- **Documented cross-border dunning decision** — multi-currency retry schedule (per-bank-day not per-US-day) + 3DS exemption strategy (low-value-trailing-exemptions + low-risk-trailing-exemptions + trusted-beneficiary-trailing-exemptions) + per-bank-rail failure-reason-specific copy (SEPA mandate + BACS direct-debit + BECS direct-debit + PAD pre-authorized-debit) + FX-hedge strategy (Airwallex multi-currency-wallet vs Wise Business vs Revolut Business vs merchant-of-record like Paddle). Without this, the operator is reacting to cross-border failures one at a time without a multi-currency rule-engine.

You do NOT have:
- A brand with <10% non-US subscribers — Move #14.2's US-centric retry schedule is sufficient; per-currency retry timing adds complexity without proportional recovery-rate lift.
- A subscription app without multi-currency support (e.g., legacy Recharge without international enabled) — without multi-currency settlement, Move #14.2.2 cannot route per-currency retry schedules; first upgrade Recharge / Stay AI / Chargebee / Recurly / Stripe Billing to multi-currency tiers, then ship Move #14.2.2.
- A regulator-prohibited region (e.g., Brazil-only subscription without international license) — Move #14.2.2 requires multi-currency settlement + tax-registered entities in the target region; ship Move #25 international-expansion first.

## The build (4-phase 21–35 day build)

### Phase 1 — Diagnose + baseline (Days 1–5)

1. **Export 90-day cross-border failed-payment history** per currency (EUR / GBP / AUD / CAD) — pull from Recharge Analytics / Stay AI / Chargebee / Recurly / Stripe Sigma / ProfitWell. Capture per-currency failure-rate + recovery-rate + involuntary churn rate + 3DS challenge rate + 3DS abandonment rate + FX-rate-fluctuation rate + per-bank-rail failure-reason breakdown.
2. **Capture baseline cross-border recovery-rate per currency** — calculate the 90-day recovery-rate per currency (EUR recovery-rate / GBP recovery-rate / AUD recovery-rate / CAD recovery-rate) and the cross-border composite recovery-rate (non-US weighted average). This is the baseline Move #14.2.2 lifts.
3. **Identify top-5 cross-border failure reasons per bank rail** — SEPA mandate-related (mandate_invalid / mandate_cancelled / mandate_expired) / BACS direct-debit-related (DD-issued-fail / DD-rejected / DD-cancelled) / BECS direct-debit-related (declined / dishonored) / PAD pre-authorized-debit-related (PAD-NRPC-01 / PAD-NRPC-02 / PAD-not-honoured) / 3DS / SCA abandonment (authentication_failed / authentication_abandoned). Per-bank-rail failure reasons are NOT the same as US card failure reasons — SEPA mandates require explicit bank-side authorization; BACS direct-debit has 2-day notice + 2-day clearing; BECS has 2-day notice + 3-day clearing; PAD has 1-day notice + 1-day clearing.
4. **Capture baseline 3DS challenge rate + 3DS abandonment rate** — what % of EU + UK subscriptions trigger 3DS challenge (authentication_required) and what % of those challenges are abandoned (authentication_abandoned). Best-in-class: 75–85% exemption-rate + ≤22% abandonment. Baseline median: 0–35% exemption-rate + ≥55% abandonment.
5. **Capture baseline FX-hedge rate** — what % of cross-border revenue is hedged via Airwallex multi-currency-wallet + Wise Business + Revolut Business + Stripe Treasury + merchant-of-record like Paddle. Baseline median: 0–30%; best-in-class: ≥90%.
6. **Snapshot baseline** — capture the 5 metrics above (recovery-rate / involuntary-churn-rate / 3DS-abandonment-rate / FX-hedge-rate / winback-after-cancellation-rate) per currency as the Move #14.2.2 baseline.

### Phase 2 — Multi-currency retry-scheduling + per-bank-day cadence (Days 6–14)

1. **Configure per-currency retry schedule per bank rail** — for EUR subscriptions, schedule retries on Mon/Wed/Fri (SEPA SCT processing-day); for GBP, Tue/Thu/Sat (BACS processing-day); for AUD, Tue/Wed/Thu (BECS processing-day); for CAD, Wed/Thu/Fri (PAD processing-day). Best-in-class: 4 retries over 14 days per currency with per-bank-rail processing-day alignment + per-bank-holiday calendar (German Unity Day + French Bastille Day + Italian Liberation Day + etc.). Floor: 2 retries over 7 days without per-bank-rail alignment.
2. **Configure per-bank-rail processing-day calendar** — export the SEPA SCT processing-day calendar (Mon-Fri excluding TARGET2 holidays) + the BACS processing-day calendar (Mon-Fri excluding UK bank holidays) + the BECS processing-day calendar (Tue-Thu excluding Australian bank holidays) + the PAD processing-day calendar (Mon-Fri excluding Canadian bank holidays). Use the calendar as the per-currency retry-window source.
3. **Configure per-currency failure-reason-specific retry rule** — for SEPA mandate_invalid, retry on next SEPA SCT processing-day with a "please verify your SEPA mandate" CTA; for BACS DD-rejected, retry on next BACS processing-day with a "please confirm your direct-debit details" CTA; for BECS dishonored, retry on next BECS processing-day with a "please update your bank account" CTA; for PAD not-honoured, retry on next PAD processing-day with a "please verify your pre-authorized debit" CTA.
4. **Configure multi-currency retry attempt cap** — best-in-class: 4 retries over 14 days per currency + per-bank-rail processing-day alignment + per-bank-holiday calendar. Floor: 1 retry on USD-style cadence. Stretch: 6 retries over 21 days with per-bank-rail calendar + per-bank-rule-engine.

### Phase 3 — 3DS / SCA exemption + Europay / BACS / SEPA failure-reason-specific copy (Days 15–22)

1. **Configure 3DS / SCA exemption engine** — Stripe 3DS exemption + Adyen 3DS2 exemption + Braintree 3DS exemption + Checkout.com Smart 3DS exemption. Configure 3 exemption types: (a) low-value-trailing exemption (single transaction ≤€30 + cumulative ≤€100 / 5 transactions) — flag subscription transactions below €30 as low-value-trailing-exempt; (b) low-risk-trailing exemption (transaction-risk-analysis-based — issuer-side low-fraud-rate + acquirer-side low-fraud-rate + platform-side low-fraud-rate) — flag transactions from issuers with <0.13% fraud-rate as low-risk-trailing-exempt; (c) trusted-beneficiary-trailing exemption (cardholder-side whitelist — issuer-side allow-list of trusted merchants) — flag recurring subscriptions as trusted-beneficiary-trailing-exempt after the cardholder's first successful 3DS authentication. Best-in-class: 85–95% exemption-rate + ≤22% 3DS abandonment.
2. **Configure per-bank-rail failure-reason-specific copy** — 5-failure-reason-specific email + 2-SMS cadence per bank rail: (a) SEPA mandate_invalid / mandate_cancelled — email subject "Please verify your SEPA mandate to continue your [Brand] subscription" + SMS "Action needed: please verify your SEPA mandate for your [Brand] subscription — [secure URL]"; (b) BACS DD-rejected — email subject "Please confirm your UK direct-debit details for your [Brand] subscription" + SMS "Action needed: please confirm your UK direct-debit details — [secure URL]"; (c) BECS dishonored — email subject "Please update your Australian bank account for your [Brand] subscription" + SMS "Action needed: please update your bank account — [secure URL]"; (d) PAD not-honoured — email subject "Please verify your pre-authorized debit for your [Brand] subscription" + SMS "Action needed: please verify your pre-authorized debit — [secure URL]"; (e) 3DS / SCA authentication_failed — email subject "Please re-authenticate your card for your [Brand] subscription" + SMS "Action needed: please re-authenticate your card — [secure URL]". Localize copy to 6 languages (English / German / French / Spanish / Italian / Dutch).
3. **Configure multi-language card-update page** — deploy a 6-language card-update page (English / German / French / Spanish / Italian / Dutch) with per-currency + per-bank-rail-native copy. Best-in-class: 12-language card-update page with native-flag-icon + per-bank-rail logo (SEPA / BACS / BECS / PAD) + locale-formatted amounts. Floor: single-language English card-update page.
4. **Configure cross-border pre-charge card-expiration warning** — for EUR + GBP + AUD + CAD subscribers, send T-30d / T-14d / T-3d pre-charge card-expiration warning in their billing currency + their locale's language. Best-in-class: T-60d / T-30d / T-14d / T-3d / T-1d cadence in 6 languages.

### Phase 4 — FX-hedge + cross-border involuntary-churn segmentation + verification (Days 23–35)

1. **Configure multi-currency FX-hedge strategy** — best-in-class: Airwallex multi-currency-wallet + Wise Business + Revolut Business with FX-rate-lock-in 24–48 hours. Floor: 0% FX-hedge (floating rate). Stretch: Airwallex + Wise + Revolut + Paddle merchant-of-record multi-layer FX-hedge. Cross-border FX-rate-fluctuation can erode 1.5–3.5% of cross-border revenue annually; an FX-hedge strategy locks in the rate.
2. **Configure cross-border involuntary-churn segmentation** — segment non-US subscribers by: (a) currency (EUR / GBP / AUD / CAD) — separate Klaviyo segments; (b) bank rail (SEPA / BACS / BECS / PAD) — separate recovery flow per rail; (c) 3DS abandonment — high-risk segment requiring dedicated exemption-engine-CTA flow; (d) FX-rate-fluctuation-driven churn — segment where churn correlates with FX-rate-delta. Each segment has its own pre-charge + retry + winback cadence.
3. **Configure cross-border winback-after-cancellation flow** — for cross-border involuntary-cancellations, send a 3-email + 1-SMS winback cadence in the subscriber's locale language with a 30-day pause option. Best-in-class: 30-day / 60-day / 90-day multi-tier pause + 3-step winback-discount-ladder (10% / 20% / 30%) + per-locale-copy.
4. **Verification + rollout** — verify the 7 gates below; capture 30-day post-launch metrics; A/B test multi-currency retry schedule vs Move #14.2's US-style retry; ensure no regression in Move #14.2's US-cohort recovery-rate.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Single-currency retry timing** — using the same US-style retry schedule for EUR / GBP / AUD / CAD ignores per-bank-rail processing-day windows. SEPA SCT processes Mon-Fri excluding TARGET2 holidays; BACS processes Mon-Fri excluding UK bank holidays; BECS processes Tue-Thu excluding Australian bank holidays; PAD processes Mon-Fri excluding Canadian bank holidays. **Fix:** Phase 2 Step 1 — configure per-currency retry schedule per bank rail with per-bank-rail processing-day alignment + per-bank-holiday calendar. Median impact: 8–14pp recovery-rate gap. Top-quartile impact: 18–28pp. Stretch impact: 30–45pp. **Lift target:** +8–18pp cross-border recovery-rate.
2. **No 3DS / SCA exemption engine** — every EU + UK subscription triggers a 3DS challenge even when the transaction is low-value-trailing or low-risk-trailing; 45–65% of 3DS challenges are abandoned. **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 1 — configure 3DS / SCA exemption engine with 3 exemption types (low-value-trailing + low-risk-trailing + trusted-beneficiary-trailing). Median impact: 18–28pp recovery-rate gap. Top-quartile impact: 35–50pp. Stretch impact: 50–70pp. **Lift target:** +18–35pp cross-border recovery-rate.
3. **Generic English dunning copy across all currencies** — sending the same English dunning email to EUR / GBP / AUD / CAD subscribers ignores locale-specific tone-of-voice + per-bank-rail terminology (SEPA mandate vs BACS direct-debit vs BECS direct-debit vs PAD pre-authorized-debit). **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 2 — localize copy to 6 languages with per-bank-rail-specific copy (SEPA mandate-friendly vs BACS direct-debit-friendly). Median impact: 8–18pp recovery-rate gap. Top-quartile impact: 22–35pp. Stretch impact: 38–55pp. **Lift target:** +8–22pp cross-border recovery-rate.
4. **Single-language card-update page** — sending EU / UK / AU / CA subscribers to an English-only card-update page loses 18–35% of cross-border card-update-CTR. **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 3 — deploy 6-language card-update page with per-currency + per-bank-rail-native copy. Median impact: 12–22pp card-update-CTR gap. Top-quartile impact: 28–42pp. Stretch impact: 45–60pp. **Lift target:** +12–28pp cross-border card-update-CTR.
5. **No per-bank-rail failure-reason-specific copy** — sending the same "card declined" message to SEPA mandate_invalid vs BACS DD-rejected vs BECS dishonored vs PAD not-honoured is unhelpful — each bank rail has different fix-mechanism. **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 2 — configure 5-failure-reason-specific email + 2-SMS cadence per bank rail with localized copy. Median impact: 12–22pp recovery-rate gap. Top-quartile impact: 25–38pp. Stretch impact: 40–55pp. **Lift target:** +12–25pp cross-border recovery-rate.
6. **USD-style pre-charge card-expiration warning** — sending T-30d / T-14d / T-3d pre-charge warnings in USD-only ignores cross-border billing-currency + locale-language needs. **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 4 — configure T-30d / T-14d / T-3d pre-charge card-expiration warning in the subscriber's billing currency + their locale's language. Median impact: 5–10pp recovery-rate gap. Top-quartile impact: 12–18pp. Stretch impact: 22–30pp. **Lift target:** +5–12pp cross-border recovery-rate.
7. **No FX-hedge strategy** — letting cross-border FX-rate float erodes 1.5–3.5% of cross-border revenue annually + creates recovery-rate uncertainty when FX-rate-fluctuation triggers unexpected card-issuer-decline-pattern. **Fix:** Phase 4 Step 1 — configure Airwallex multi-currency-wallet + Wise Business + Revolut Business with FX-rate-lock-in 24–48 hours. Median impact: 1.5–3.5% of cross-border revenue eroded. Top-quartile impact: 3.5–5.5%. Stretch impact: 5.5–8.5%. **Lift target:** +1.5–3.5% cross-border revenue retained.
