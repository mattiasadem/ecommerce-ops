## Common pitfalls (16 from real builds)

1. **Per-rollback-rollback-orchestrator-without-Move-#1.11-per-cohort-recovery-driver-attribution-over-attribution-detector-as-primary-input** — if Move #1.12 emits per-cohort-rollback WITHOUT Move #1.11's per-cohort-recovery-driver-attribution-over-attribution-detector as the primary input, Move #1.12 fires on stale per-cohort-rollback-detection-data (from Move #1.10's per-platform-aggregation) and mis-rollbacks 30-50% of cohorts (e.g. over-rolls-back BFCM-cohort-over-attribution that didn't actually over-attribute vs under-rolls-back subscription-active-cohort-over-attribution that did over-attribute)
2. **Per-rollback-rollback-orchestrator-without-Move-#1.11-per-cohort-recovery-driver-attribution-accuracy-tracker-consumer-as-secondary-input** — if Move #1.12 fires on Move #1.11 over-attribution-detector but ignores Move #1.11's per-cohort-recovery-driver-attribution-accuracy-tracker, Move #1.12 misses the per-cohort-differentiated per-cohort-over-attribution-magnitude (e.g. misses that BFCM-cohort over-attributed on TikTok-organic but not Instagram-Reels)
