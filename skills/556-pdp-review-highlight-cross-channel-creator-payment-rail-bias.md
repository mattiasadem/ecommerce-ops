## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **PR1 × T8 micro-niche cohort pairing wrong rail.** PR1 Stripe-Connect instant-payout is for high-velocity T8 creators with low-ticket SKUs ($10-30). Pairing with T6 celebrity or T1 mega-creators yields near-zero lift because their payout cadence is quarterly not per-piece. **Fix:** route T1-T6 creators to PR4 direct-wire (NET-30 / NET-60 batch), reserve PR1 for T7-T8 micro-niche + low-ticket-SKU creators only.
2. **PR2 × cross-border-creators missing EU + UK + JP rails.** PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay has 200+ country coverage but excludes sanctioned states (RU, IR, KP, SY, CU). Add the sanctioned-states exclusion list explicitly OR use PR3 Wise for cross-border fallback. **Fix:** build the sanctioned-states exclusion list into the creator-onboarding flow; route EU + UK + JP creators to PR3 Wise (SEPA + FasterPayments + Zengin coverage is preferred); reserve PR2 for non-EU/non-JP/non-sanctioned international creators.
3. **PR3 × EU + UK + JP creators missing SEPA + FasterPayments + Zengin coverage.** PR3 Wise supports SEPA (EUR), Faster Payments (GBP), Zengin (JPY) but NOT BRL (BR) or INR (IN). For BR + IN + CO + MX + AR + CL, fall back to PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay or PR4 direct-wire. **Fix:** build a country-to-rail lookup table (`country → rail`); route BRL/INR/CLP creators to PR2 + accept the 2.0% PayPal-fee; route MX/CO/AR creators to PR2 or PR4.
4. **PR4 × legacy-monthly-batch creators missing W-9 / W-8BEN / W-8BEN-E + withholding-tax compliance.** PR4 direct-wire requires W-9 (US persons) / W-8BEN (non-US individuals) / W-8BEN-E (non-US entities) + 30% backup-withholding for non-W-9 US persons. Without the W-8 stack, the merchant owes the IRS 24% of every payout to a non-W-9 creator. **Fix:** implement the W-9 collection flow at creator-onboarding; for non-US persons, collect W-8BEN + W-8BEN-E; implement 30% backup-withholding for any non-W-9 US person.
5. **PR1 × instant-payout fee (1.5% of payout) breaks unit economics on $5 SKU commissions.** PR1 charges 1.5% instant-payout fee on top of standard 0.25% Stripe processing. For a $5 commission on a $30 SKU, the $0.075 instant-payout fee + $0.0125 processing + $0.30 platform fee = $0.39 (7.7%) — the merchant needs to budget the per-payout cost explicitly. **Fix:** set a minimum-commission threshold ($10 minimum) for instant-payout eligibility; below $10, fall back to PR4 direct-wire (NET-30) which has a flat $0.10 platform-fee.
6. **PR2 × PayPal-Mass-Pay requires a PayPal-business-account with Mass-Pay API access.** PayPal Mass Pay is gated behind a PayPal Business account + Mass Pay API access (legacy product, requires explicit enable). Without Mass Pay API access, fall back to PR1 Stripe-Connect or PR4 direct-wire. **Fix:** apply for PayPal Mass Pay API access early (4-6 weeks lead time); in the interim, use the PayPal Payouts API (newer product, single-payout only) for sub-50-creator cohorts.
7. **PR3 × Wise has a 7-currency minimum-payout threshold ($1 USD-equivalent) + monthly-payout-cap.** Wise enforces a $1 USD-equivalent minimum per payout + a 20-payouts-per-batch cap. For a 50-creator monthly-batch, batch into 2-3 sub-batches. **Fix:** implement a payout-batching queue that splits the creator-list into sub-batches of ≤20 payouts each; track the monthly-cap per Wise Business account.
8. **PR4 × ACH/wire transfer has a 2-3 business-day settlement window + 0.1-1.0% wire-fee.** Direct-wire transfers take 2-3 business days for domestic + 3-5 for international. Wire fees range $15-50 per transfer (domestic) or $25-75 (international). Budget the wire-fee into the per-payout economics. **Fix:** implement the wire-fee-budget explicitly in the merchant's payout-economics calculator; for sub-$100 payouts, fall back to PR1 or PR3 (which have percentage-based fees that are cheaper at low ticket-sizes).
9. **PR1 × instant-payout failure fallback missing.** Stripe Connect instant-payout requires a verified debit card on file + a 1.5% fee + 30-minute arrival window; when the debit card is invalid, the instant-payout fails and falls back to a standard 2-day ACH transfer at no fee. **Fix:** implement the `payout.failed` webhook to retry on a 30-minute interval + auto-fallback to PR4 ACH after 3 failed retries; notify the creator of the fallback via SMS/email.
10. **PR-tier × per-CMM-tier mismatch breaks the creator-economics.** CMM3 brand-deal-flat-fee creators expect NET-30 / NET-60 payment terms, NOT instant-payout. Pairing PR1 instant-payout with CMM3 brand-deal-flat-fee produces a $0 ATC lift because the creators prefer NET-30 for cash-flow-management. **Fix:** route CMM3 brand-deal-flat-fee creators to PR4 (NET-30 / NET-60 batch) by default; reserve PR1 for CMM1 affiliate-link + CMM2 promo-code creators who expect per-piece instant-payout.
11. **PR-tier × per-cohort-LTV mismatch.** LTV1 first-purchase cohorts cannot perform per-currency-payout (only the creator's home currency). Pairing PR3 Wise with LTV1 first-purchase cohorts is meaningless because the creator's home currency is fixed regardless of the cohort's LTV tier. **Fix:** route LTV1 first-purchase cohorts to PR1 Stripe Connect (USD/EUR/GBP only); reserve PR3 Wise for LTV3-VIP + LTV4-dormant cohorts that justify the per-currency-payout overhead.
12. **PR-tier × per-LG-tier translation.** LG7 ja-JP creators expect Zengin (PR3) not PayPal-Mass-Pay (PR2). Pairing PR2 with LG7 yields a 12% drop in payout-completion because Japanese creators expect Zengin. Fall back to PR3 for LG7. **Fix:** build a language-to-rail lookup table (`LG → preferred rail`); route LG7 ja-JP + LG1 en-US + LG3 de-DE to PR3 Wise (Zengin + SEPA + FasterPayments coverage); reserve PR2 for LG10 es-MX + LG12 zh-CN creators.
13. **PR-tier × per-CR-tier regional-rail.** CR3 JP creators expect Zengin (PR3) + Japan-domestic-rail. CR7 DE creators expect SEPA (PR3). CR1 US creators expect ACH (PR4) + Wire (PR4). The per-CR-tier rail must match the creator's home-bank-rail. **Fix:** build a country-to-rail lookup table (`country_code → preferred rail`); route CR1 US to PR4, CR3 JP + CR5 ES + CR7 DE + CR2 UK to PR3, CR9 BR + CR10 MX + CR11 IN to PR2 (fall-back), all other countries to PR1 (default).
14. **PR-tier × per-CA-tier cohort-affinity-bias.** CA3 Millennial creators prefer instant-payout (PR1) because they have high cash-flow-needs (mortgage / family). CA5 Gen-X creators prefer direct-wire (PR4) because they have lower cash-flow-needs and prefer quarterly-batched payments. **Fix:** route CA1 Gen-Z + CA3 Millennial + CA4 Gen-X-young to PR1 (instant-payout); route CA5 Gen-X + CA6 Boomer to PR4 (NET-30 / NET-60 batch).
15. **PR-tier × per-CV-tier vertical-rail-bias.** CV1 fashion creators prefer PR1 (instant-payout for fast-fashion-turnover). CV4 fitness creators prefer PR4 (NET-30 batch) because fitness-creators have lower payout-frequency. CV8 electronics creators prefer PR3 (Wise for cross-border). **Fix:** route CV1 fashion + CV2 beauty + CV6 pet to PR1 (high-velocity verticals); route CV4 fitness + CV5 food + CV7 lifestyle to PR4 (low-frequency verticals); route CV3 home + CV8 electronics to PR3 (cross-border verticals).
