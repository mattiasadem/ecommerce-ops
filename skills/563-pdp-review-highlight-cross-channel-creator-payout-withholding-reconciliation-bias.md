## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing WR-tier (withholding-reconciliation) with TF-tier (payout-tax-form) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.35].** WR-tier answers WHICH withholding-maint-burden the merchant carries (zero-withholding-pool + treaty-rate-withholding-pool + entity-rate-withholding-pool + state-withholding-only-pool + 30%-backup-withholding-pool); TF-tier answers WHICH tax-form the creator files (W-9-US + W-8BEN-individual + W-8BEN-E-entity + W-9-with-state-tax + no-TIN). The two axes are orthogonal — a WR1 zero-withholding creator may file TF1 W-9-US (typical) OR TF3 W-8BEN-E-entity (for entity-LLCs); a WR5 30%-backup-withholding creator is forced into TF5 no-TIN. **Fix:** carry WR-tier and TF-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 4-rung precedence ladders (WR1-WR2-WR3-WR4-WR5 and TF1-TF2-TF3-TF4-TF5) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-WR-tier × per-TF-tier 5×5 cross-axis grid that scores 25 distinct WR×TF pool combinations.

2. **Pitfall — WR1 zero-withholding creators silently demoted to WR5 30%-backup-withholding on a TIN-mismatch event.** IRS TIN-Matching-System 2026 may return a TIN-mismatch (B-Notice) for a creator's SSN/EIN — the merchant is REQUIRED to withhold 24%-IRS-published-rate-2026 + 1-1.5%-state-rate on subsequent payouts until the creator corrects the TIN. A creator who was WR1 yesterday may silently be WR5 today. **Fix:** the withholding-reconciliation classifier must re-read `p.tin_matching_status` + `p.b_notice_history` + `p.withholding_status` on EVERY payout event; route to WR5 if any B-Notice is active; route back to WR1 only after TIN-correction-confirmed.

3. **Pitfall — WR2 treaty-rate-withholding creators on EU-cohort mis-classified as WR3 entity-rate-withholding because of treaty-claim-failed state.** IRS Form W-8BEN 2026 + IRS Chapter 3-withholding 2026 require the creator to certify treaty-eligibility (country + article + rate); a creator whose treaty-claim has been challenged (e.g., the creator changed residency to non-treaty-country) is silently demoted to WR3 entity-rate-withholding-pool (which has a different rate). **Fix:** the withholding-reconciliation classifier must verify the creator's treaty-claim-status — WR2 score only if treaty-eligibility-confirmed; WR3 fallback if treaty-claim-failed-or-pending.

4. **Pitfall — WR3 entity-rate-withholding creators on a non-US-domicile fail Chapter 4-FATCA-status-verification.** IRS Form W-8BEN-E 2026 + IRS Chapter 4-FATCA 2026 require non-US-entity-creators to declare FATCA-status (Active NFFE / Passive NFFE / Exempt / etc.); an entity without validated FATCA-status is silently demoted to WR5 30%-backup-withholding-pool (the harshest tier) on next payout. **Fix:** the withholding-reconciliation classifier must verify the creator's FATCA-status — WR3 score only if FATCA-status-confirmed; WR5 fallback if FATCA-status-pending-or-missing.

5. **Pitfall — WR4 state-withholding-only creators mis-classified because the W-9 form was filed without a state-W-4-block.** IRS Form W-4 2026 + state-withholding 2026 require US-creators to file a state-W-4-block IF their state imposes state-income-tax-withholding (e.g., CA / NY / TX / FL — note FL has NO state-income-tax); a creator in TX-or-FL or who submitted W-9-without-W-4-block is WR1 zero-withholding, NOT WR4 state-withholding-only. **Fix:** the withholding-reconciliation classifier must verify the creator's state-of-residency + state-W-4-block-presence — WR4 score only if state-with-state-income-tax (CA / NY / etc.) AND state-W-4-block-present; WR1 fallback otherwise.

6. **Pitfall — WR5 30%-backup-withholding creators fail 1-FTE-$120k-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The backup-withholding-pool + B-Notice-correction-tracking + TIN-matching-system-maint requires 1 FTE engineer / finance ops at $120k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$5M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the withholding-reconciliation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — WR5 score 0 for cohorts with <$5M annual revenue. Route to WR1 zero-withholding or WR4 state-withholding-only creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

7. **Pitfall — WR1 zero-withholding creators who are passive-NFFE fail 1-FTE-$80k-maint-cost for international-cohorts.** IRS Chapter 4-FATCA 2026 requires Passive-NFFE creators to disclose substantial-U.S.-owners; mis-classifying a Passive-NFFE creator as WR1 zero-withholding results in 30%-of-payments-equal-to-substantial-US-owner-withholding penalties if not properly tracked. **Fix:** the withholding-reconciliation classifier must verify FATCA-status — WR1 score only if creator is US-person-on-W-9 (not Passive-NFFE-on-W-8BEN-E); WR3 entity-rate-withholding fallback for Passive-NFFE.

8. **Pitfall — WR2 treaty-rate-withholding creators silently demoted to WR5 30%-backup-withholding because of treaty-rate-table-update-event.** IRS treaty-rate-table is updated quarterly; a creator whose treaty-country changed rates (e.g., the US-Germany tax-treaty was renegotiated in 2026 and the rate changed from 0% to 5% on royalties) is silently demoted to the higher rate until the merchant's treaty-rate-table is refreshed. **Fix:** the withholding-reconciliation classifier must read the live treaty-rate-table (per CaptivateA Treaty Rate Reconciliation 2026) and re-derive WR-tier on every payout event.

9. **Pitfall — WR3 entity-rate-withholding creators on the EU-cohort fail EU DAC7 2026 platform-reporting-requirement.** EU DAC7 2026 requires digital-platform-operators to report non-EU-resident-creator-earnings to EU tax-authorities; entities from non-EU-resident-countries on WR3 entity-rate-withholding-pool may be DOUBLE-REPORTED (EU DAC7 + US 1042-S + EU Chapter-4 withholding). **Fix:** the withholding-reconciliation classifier must verify the creator's EU-residency-state; route to WR2 treaty-rate-withholding for non-EU-resident-entities with EU-bank-account.

10. **Pitfall — WR4 state-withholding-only creators fail 1-FTE-$40k-maint-cost for multi-state-merchants.** The state-W-4-block + 50-state-DC-withholding-tracking requires 1 FTE finance ops at $40k/yr minimum; cohorts with low-frequency-payout-events to multi-state-creators may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout. **Fix:** the withholding-reconciliation classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency + cohort-state-diversity BEFORE scoring resonance — WR4 score 0 for cohorts with state-diversity >30 states AND annual-payout-frequency <1000.

11. **Pitfall — WR5 30%-backup-withholding creators silently demoted to WR3 entity-rate-withholding on a Chapter-4-status-update event.** A creator whose TIN-matching-system returned a B-Notice may subsequently file a W-8BEN-E with verified Chapter-4-status; the merchant is REQUIRED to roll back the 30%-backup-withholding AND refund the excess-withheld-tax (with interest) — but the merchant's withholding-system may not auto-rollback without explicit reclassification. **Fix:** the withholding-reconciliation classifier must maintain a `p.withholding_refund_pending` field — set to true on Chapter-4-status-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to WR3 on next payout AND adds a refund-reconciliation line-item.

12. **Pitfall — WR-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's withholding-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer WR1 zero-withholding creators (low-maint-cost, no-NRA-tracking, no-state-50-tracking) over WR5 30%-backup-withholding creators (high-maint-cost, B-Notice-tracking, 1-FTE-$120k-maint). A WR1 zero-withholding creator may be withholding-clean but withholding-maint-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-withholding-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — WR1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer WR5 (e.g., cohorts with high non-US-entity creators). The cohort's withholding-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-withholding-maint-cost-sensitivity × cohort-non-US-entity-preference.

13. **Pitfall — WR-tier cross-axis with PN-tier silently double-withholds cross-platform payments.** A creator on WR1 zero-withholding with PN2 TikTok-native as secondary platform is potentially double-withheld (W-9-1099-NEC + TikTok-Creator-Marketplace-withholding on the same payout). The withholding-reconciliation classifier must not double-count withholding on cross-platform-cohorts. **Fix:** the WR-tier × PN-tier cross-axis must apply secondary-platform-withholding-conditional — WR1 × PN2 score uses TikTok-Creator-Marketplace-withholding as the secondary; the cohort's primary withholding-rule is W-9-1099-NEC.

14. **Pitfall — WR4 state-withholding-only creators mis-classified as WR1 zero-withholding because of W-4-block-omitted state.** A creator in a state-with-state-income-tax (CA / NY / etc.) who filed W-9 without a state-W-4-block is silently reclassified to WR1 zero-withholding. The actual state is "WR4-pending — state-W-4-block-required". **Fix:** the withholding-reconciliation classifier must distinguish between WR1-confirmed / WR4-pending / WR4-confirmed states; route WR4-pending creators to WR1 zero-withholding as a temporary fallback AND flag for state-W-4-block-request.

15. **Pitfall — WR-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's withholding-residency-preference.** A cohort with US-domestic-only preference (no non-US-resident creators) requires WR1 zero-withholding pool; a cohort with EU-only preference requires WR2 treaty-rate-withholding pool (or WR3 entity-rate-withholding for EU-LLCs); a cohort with JP-only preference requires WR3 entity-rate-withholding pool (or WR2 treaty-rate-withholding for JP-individuals); a cohort with BR-only preference requires WR4 state-withholding-only-equivalent-Brazil (no federal withholding; Brazil DARF) — but the WR-tier model US-centric-by-default; cohort must use WR3 entity-rate-withholding as BR-equivalent. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-withholding-residency-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — WR5 × US-domestic-cohort score 0; route to WR1 zero-withholding fallbacks. Add a "WR-tier × cohort-jurisdiction" overlay.
