## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing TFAT-tier (tax-form-aggregation-tier) with TF-tier (tax-form-tier) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.42].** TFAT-tier answers WHICH aggregation-cadence the merchant uses (zero-rail-aggregation-pool + annual-rail-aggregation-pool + quarterly-rail-aggregation-pool + monthly-rail-aggregation-pool + daily-rail-aggregation-pool); TF-tier answers WHICH tax-form (W-9-US-domestic + W-8BEN-individual + W-8BEN-E-entity + W-9-with-state-tax + no-TIN). The two axes are orthogonal — a TFAT1 zero-aggregation creator may use TF1 W-9-US-domestic (typical, no-rail-rollup-required) or TF2 W-8BEN-individual (rare, no-rail-rollup-required); a TFAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creator is forced into TF5 no-TIN (1042-S-quarterly-rail-rollup-pool). **Fix:** carry TFAT-tier and TF-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung precedence ladders (TFAT1-TFAT2-TFAT3-TFAT4-TFAT5 and TF1-TF2-TF3-TF4-TF5) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-TFAT-tier × per-TF-tier 5×5 cross-axis grid that scores 25 distinct TFAT×TF form combinations.

2. **Pitfall — TFAT1 zero-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected W-8BEN-daily-rail-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses TFAT1 zero-aggregation pool (W-9-no-rail-rollup-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-border-traffic may have a payout that crosses USD-EU-border without a W-8BEN-daily-rail-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$55k-annual-W-8BEN-daily-rail-maint + 1-rollover-per-year on the payout event. **Fix:** the tax-form-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's cross-border-traffic-share + cohort's aggregation-tolerance — TFAT1 score only if cohort-cross-border-share <10% AND cohort-TFAT-tolerance = high; TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.

3. **Pitfall — TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-JP-cohort fail W-8BEN-E-quarterly-rail-rollup-requirement.** W-8BEN-E 2026 requires quarterly-rail-rollup for cross-border-payouts >$600/yr per JP-entity; TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (annual-rail-rollup only) is insufficient for high-JP-cohorts, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to TFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation is suddenly required to file W-8BEN-E-quarterly-rail-rollup with EU-DAC7-attestation. **Fix:** the tax-form-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's JP-cohort-traffic + aggregation-tenor — TFAT3 score only if cohort-JP-share >5% AND merchant-is-Wise-Mass-Pay-registered; TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-Wise-Mass-Pay-registered merchants.

4. **Pitfall — TFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-annual-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-rail-rollup-pool + W-8BEN-E-quarterly-rail-rollup + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $80k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the tax-form-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — TFAT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-US-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to TFAT1 zero-aggregation or TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

5. **Pitfall — TFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a high-multi-state-cohort fail cross-state-aggregation-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to TFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation is REQUIRED to track cross-state-W-4-block-rollup for every state-payout event; non-compliance triggers cross-state-aggregation-enforcement and additional-state-penalties. **Fix:** the tax-form-aggregation classifier must verify the merchant's cross-state-aggregation-tracking-status — TFAT4 score only if merchant-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active; TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active merchants.

6. **Pitfall — TFAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for high-margin-cohorts.** The daily-rail-aggregation-pool + 1042-S-quarterly-rail-rollup + 365-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $130k/yr; even high-margin cohorts may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout if the cohort's payout-frequency is low. **Fix:** the tax-form-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency BEFORE scoring resonance — TFAT5 score 0 for cohorts with annual-payout-frequency <500 AND TFAT-notional <$1M. Route to TFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators for low-frequency fallbacks.

7. **Pitfall — TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort fail EU DAC7 2026-annual-rail-rollup-requirement.** EU DAC7 2026 requires annual-rail-rollup-reporting for cross-border-payouts >€2000/yr per payer; TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (1-rollover-per-year) is REQUIRED to report by Jan-31-deadline. **Fix:** the tax-form-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's EU-creator-count + annual-payout-frequency — TFAT2 score only if cohort-EU-creator-count >30 AND annual-payout-frequency >2,000; TFAT1 zero-aggregation fallback for non-DAC7-required cohorts.

8. **Pitfall — TFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation because of entity-attestation-staleness-event.** A creator whose W-8BEN-E-attestation was set 90 days ago may have a stale entity-attestation-status that doesn't match the current entity-attestation-status, and the merchant's tax-team may silently mark-down the entity-pool to annual-rollup (TFAT2) on the next rollup-event. **Fix:** the tax-form-aggregation classifier must read the live W-8BEN-E-attestation-table (per CaptivateA Tax Form Aggregation 2026 + IRS Form W-8BEN-E 2026 + IRS TIN Matching System 2026) and re-derive TFAT-tier on every rollup event.

9. **Pitfall — TFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a multi-state-cohort fail state-AG-enforcement-risk.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to TFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation across multiple states is REQUIRED to file cross-state-aggregation on every state-payout; failure triggers state-AG-enforcement (e.g., CA-FTB-state-W-4-block + NY-DTF-state-W-4-block + TX-Franchise-state-W-4-block + FL-DR-1-state-W-4-block) and potential $10k-$50k state-AG-penalties. **Fix:** the tax-form-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's state-distribution-count + state-payout-event-volume — TFAT4 score only if cohort-state-distribution-count >5 states AND merchant-state-AG-enforcement-history-clean; TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for risky cohorts.

10. **Pitfall — TFAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for multi-jurisdiction-merchants.** The daily-rail-aggregation-pool + 1042-S-quarterly-rail-rollup + multi-jurisdiction-rollup-maint (IRS Form 1042-S 2026 + IRS TIN Matching System 2026 + IRS B-Notice 2026 + IRS 30%-backup-withholding 2026 + EU DAC7 2026 + UK RDEC 2026 + Japan NTA 2026 + Brazil DARF 2026 + Mexico ISR 2026) requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $130k/yr minimum; cohorts with low-frequency-payout-events to multi-jurisdiction-creators may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout. **Fix:** the tax-form-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency + cohort-jurisdiction-diversity BEFORE scoring resonance — TFAT5 score 0 for cohorts with jurisdiction-diversity >3 jurisdictions AND annual-payout-frequency <1000.

11. **Pitfall — TFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation on a state-aggregation-tracking-failed-event.** A creator whose state-aggregation-tracking was tracked monthly may have the monthly-tracking silently fail (e.g., the merchant's state-aggregation-database has been unavailable to execute the monthly-rollup); the merchant is REQUIRED to fall back to TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation on next payout. **Fix:** the tax-form-aggregation classifier must maintain a `p.tfat_monthly_rollup_status` field — set to true on rollup-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to TFAT2 on next payout AND adds a state-aggregation-tracking-reconciliation line-item.

12. **Pitfall — TFAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's tax-form-aggregation-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer TFAT1 zero-aggregation creators (low-maint-cost, W-9-no-rail-rollup, no-50-state-tracking) over TFAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators (high-maint-cost, daily-aggregation-tracking, 1042-S-quarterly-rail-pool). A TFAT1 zero-aggregation creator may be TFAT-clean but TFAT-maint-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-TFAT-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — TFAT1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer TFAT5 (e.g., cohorts with high multi-jurisdiction-traffic). The cohort's TFAT-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-TFAT-maint-cost-sensitivity × cohort-multi-jurisdiction-traffic-preference.

13. **Pitfall — TFAT-tier cross-axis with PR-tier silently double-counts aggregation-cost on cross-platform payments.** A creator on TFAT1 zero-aggregation with PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay as secondary platform is potentially double-charged aggregation-cost (PayPal's auto-aggregation-generation + merchant's TFAT-rollup on the same payout). The tax-form-aggregation classifier must not double-count aggregation-cost on cross-platform-cohorts. **Fix:** the TFAT-tier × PR-tier cross-axis must apply secondary-platform-TFAT-conditional — TFAT1 × PR2 score uses PayPal-Mass-Pay-TFAT as the secondary; the cohort's primary TFAT-rule is zero-aggregation.

14. **Pitfall — TFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators mis-classified as TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation because of W-8BEN-E-attestation-stale state.** A creator whose W-8BEN-E-attestation was set 90 days ago may have a stale attestation that doesn't match the current entity-attestation-status, and the merchant's tax-team may silently mark-down the entity-pool to annual-rollup (TFAT2) on the next rollup-event. The actual state is "TFAT3-pending — W-8BEN-E-attestation-stale". **Fix:** the tax-form-aggregation classifier must distinguish between TFAT2-confirmed / TFAT3-pending / TFAT3-confirmed states; route TFAT3-pending creators to TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation as a temporary fallback AND flag for W-8BEN-E-attestation-refresh.

15. **Pitfall — TFAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's tax-form-aggregation-residency-preference.** A cohort with US-domestic-only preference (no cross-border-traffic) requires TFAT1 zero-aggregation pool; a cohort with EU-only preference requires TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation pool (or TFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation for EU-LLCs); a cohort with JP-only preference requires TFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation pool (or TFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation for JP-individuals); a cohort with BR-only preference requires TFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation pool — but the TFAT-tier model US-centric-by-default; cohort must use TFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation as BR-equivalent. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-TFAT-residency-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — TFAT5 × US-domestic-cohort score 0; route to TFAT1 zero-aggregation fallbacks. Add a "TFAT-tier × cohort-jurisdiction" overlay.
