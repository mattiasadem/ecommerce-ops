## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing FHFT-tier (fx-fine-tuning) with FH-tier (fx-hedging) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.38].** FHFT-tier answers WHICH fx-fine-tuning-cadence the merchant uses (zero-fine-tuning-pool + annual-rollup-pool + quarterly-rollup-pool + monthly-rollup-pool + daily-rollup-pool); FH-tier answers WHICH fx-hedge-strategy (zero-hedge + spot-hedge + forward-30-day + forward-90-day + forward-180-day). The two axes are orthogonal — a FHFT1 zero-fine-tuning creator may use FH1 zero-hedge (typical, no-MTM-refresh-required) OR FH2 spot-hedge (rare, Wise-EU-with-no-MTM-refresh); a FHFT5 daily-rollup-fine-tuning creator is forced into FH5 forward-180-day-hedge (daily Wise-MX-MTM-refresh-pool). **Fix:** carry FHFT-tier and FH-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung precedence ladders (FHFT1-FHFT2-FHFT3-FHFT4-FHFT5 and FH1-FH2-FH3-FH4-FH5) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-FHFT-tier × per-FH-tier 5×5 cross-axis grid that scores 25 distinct FHFT×FH pool combinations.

2. **Pitfall — FHFT1 zero-fine-tuning creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected Wise-MTM-refresh-maint.** A creator who normally uses FHFT1 zero-fine-tuning pool (USD-domestic-no-MTM-refresh-required-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-border-traffic may have a payout that crosses USD-EU-border without a Wise-EU-annual-MTM-refresh-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$60k-annual-Wise-EU-MTM-refresh-maint + Jan-31-deadline-tracking on the payout event. **Fix:** the fx-fine-tuning classifier must verify the cohort's cross-border-traffic-share + cohort's fx-fine-tuning-tolerance — FHFT1 score only if cohort-cross-border-share <10% AND cohort-FHFT-tolerance = high; FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.

3. **Pitfall — FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning creators on a high-Japan-cohort fail Japan FSA 2026-quarterly-derivative-reporting-requirement.** Japan FSA 2026 requires quarterly-derivative-reporting for FX-hedges >30-day-tenor; FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning (once-per-year) is exempt for individual creators, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to FHFT3 quarterly-rollup-fine-tuning is suddenly required to report the trade to Japan FSA every quarter (4 rollovers/yr). **Fix:** the fx-fine-tuning classifier must verify the cohort's Japan-cohort-traffic + fx-fine-tuning-tenor — FHFT3 score only if cohort-Japan-share >5% AND merchant-is-FSA-registered; FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning fallback for non-FSA-registered merchants.

4. **Pitfall — FHFT3 quarterly-rollup-fine-tuning creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-quarterly-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-rollup-pool + Wise-JP-quarterly-MTM-tracking + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $80k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the fx-fine-tuning classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — FHFT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-US-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to FHFT1 zero-fine-tuning or FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

5. **Pitfall — FHFT4 monthly-rollup-fine-tuning creators on a high-multi-jurisdiction-cohort fail cross-jurisdiction-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to FHFT4 monthly-rollup-fine-tuning is REQUIRED to track cross-jurisdiction-MTM-refresh for every monthly payout event; non-compliance triggers cross-jurisdiction-enforcement and additional-jurisdiction-penalties. **Fix:** the fx-fine-tuning classifier must verify the merchant's cross-jurisdiction-tracking-status — FHFT4 score only if merchant-cross-jurisdiction-tracking-active; FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning fallback for non-cross-jurisdiction-tracking-active merchants.

6. **Pitfall — FHFT5 daily-rollup-fine-tuning creators fail 1-FTE-$140k-daily-maint-cost for high-margin-cohorts.** The daily-rollup-pool + Wise-MX-daily-MTM-refresh + 365-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $140k/yr; even high-margin cohorts may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout if the cohort's payout-frequency is low. **Fix:** the fx-fine-tuning classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency BEFORE scoring resonance — FHFT5 score 0 for cohorts with annual-payout-frequency <500 AND FHFT-notional <$1M. Route to FHFT3 quarterly-rollup-fine-tuning creators for low-frequency fallbacks.

7. **Pitfall — FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning creators on a high-EU-cohort fail EU EMIR 2026-annual-rollup-requirement.** EU EMIR 2026 requires annual-rollup-reporting for FX-hedges >30-day-tenor with EU-counterparties; FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning (1-rollover-per-year) is REQUIRED to report by Jan-31-deadline. **Fix:** the fx-fine-tuning classifier must verify the cohort's EU-creator-count + annual-payout-frequency — FHFT2 score only if cohort-EU-creator-count >30 AND annual-payout-frequency >2,000; FHFT1 zero-fine-tuning fallback for non-EMIR-required cohorts.

8. **Pitfall — FHFT3 quarterly-rollup-fine-tuning creators silently demoted to FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning because of jurisdiction-derivative-registration-update-event.** A creator whose entity-derivative-registration was set 90 days ago may have a stale registration-status that doesn't match the current jurisdiction-derivative-status, and the merchant's treasury-team may silently mark-down the entity-pool to annual-rollup (FHFT2) on the next rollup-event. **Fix:** the fx-fine-tuning classifier must read the live jurisdiction-derivative-registration-table (per CaptivateA FX Hedging Fine Tuning 2026 + FX MTM 2026 + FX Forward Rollover 2026) and re-derive FHFT-tier on every rollup event.

9. **Pitfall — FHFT4 monthly-rollup-fine-tuning creators on a multi-jurisdiction-cohort fail jurisdiction-AG-enforcement-risk.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to FHFT4 monthly-rollup-fine-tuning across multiple jurisdictions is REQUIRED to file cross-jurisdiction-tracking on every monthly-payout; failure triggers jurisdiction-AG-enforcement (e.g., Japan-FSA-quarterly-derivative-reporting + Brazil-BCB-derivative-registration + Mexico-CNBV-derivative-registration) and potential $10k-$50k jurisdiction-AG-penalties. **Fix:** the fx-fine-tuning classifier must verify the cohort's jurisdiction-distribution-count + monthly-payout-event-volume — FHFT4 score only if cohort-jurisdiction-distribution-count >5 jurisdictions AND merchant-jurisdiction-AG-enforcement-history-clean; FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning fallback for risky cohorts.

10. **Pitfall — FHFT5 daily-rollup-fine-tuning creators fail 1-FTE-$140k-daily-maint-cost for multi-jurisdiction-merchants.** The daily-rollup-pool + Wise-MX-daily-MTM-refresh + multi-jurisdiction-rollup-maint (FX 2026 + FX MTM 2026 + FX Forward Rollover 2026 + EU EMIR 2026 + UK FCA 2026 + Japan FSA 2026 + Brazil BCB 2026 + Mexico CNBV 2026) requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $140k/yr minimum; cohorts with low-frequency-payout-events to multi-jurisdiction-creators may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout. **Fix:** the fx-fine-tuning classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency + cohort-jurisdiction-diversity BEFORE scoring resonance — FHFT5 score 0 for cohorts with jurisdiction-diversity >3 jurisdictions AND annual-payout-frequency <1000.

11. **Pitfall — FHFT4 monthly-rollup-fine-tuning creators silently demoted to FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning on a jurisdiction-derivative-tracking-failed-event.** A creator whose jurisdiction-derivative-tracking was tracked monthly may have the monthly-tracking silently fail (e.g., the merchant's jurisdiction-derivative-database has been unavailable to execute the monthly-rollup); the merchant is REQUIRED to fall back to FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning on next payout. **Fix:** the fx-fine-tuning classifier must maintain a `p.fhft_monthly_rollup_status` field — set to true on rollup-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to FHFT2 on next payout AND adds a jurisdiction-derivative-tracking-reconciliation line-item.

12. **Pitfall — FHFT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's fx-fine-tuning-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer FHFT1 zero-fine-tuning creators (low-maint-cost, no-Wise-EU-annual-rollup, no-50-jurisdiction-tracking) over FHFT5 daily-rollup-fine-tuning creators (high-maint-cost, daily-rollup-tracking, Wise-MX-daily-MTM-refresh). A FHFT1 zero-fine-tuning creator may be FHFT-clean but FHFT-maint-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-FHFT-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — FHFT1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer FHFT5 (e.g., cohorts with high multi-jurisdiction-traffic). The cohort's FHFT-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-FHFT-maint-cost-sensitivity × cohort-multi-jurisdiction-traffic-preference.

13. **Pitfall — FHFT-tier cross-axis with PR-tier silently double-counts MTM-refresh-cost on cross-platform payments.** A creator on FHFT1 zero-fine-tuning with PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay as secondary platform is potentially double-charged MTM-refresh-cost (PayPal's auto-FX-spread-generation + merchant's FHFT-rollup on the same payout). The fx-fine-tuning classifier must not double-count MTM-refresh-cost on cross-platform-cohorts. **Fix:** the FHFT-tier × PR-tier cross-axis must apply secondary-platform-FHFT-conditional — FHFT1 × PR2 score uses PayPal-Mass-Pay-FHFT as the secondary; the cohort's primary FHFT-rule is zero-fine-tuning.

14. **Pitfall — FHFT3 quarterly-rollup-fine-tuning creators mis-classified as FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning because of jurisdiction-derivative-registration-stale state.** A creator whose jurisdiction-derivative-registration was set 90 days ago may have a stale registration that doesn't match the current jurisdiction-derivative-status, and the merchant's treasury-team may silently mark-down the entity-pool to annual-rollup (FHFT2) on the next rollup-event. The actual state is "FHFT3-pending — jurisdiction-derivative-registration-stale". **Fix:** the fx-fine-tuning classifier must distinguish between FHFT2-confirmed / FHFT3-pending / FHFT3-confirmed states; route FHFT3-pending creators to FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning as a temporary fallback AND flag for jurisdiction-derivative-registration-refresh.

15. **Pitfall — FHFT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's fx-fine-tuning-residency-preference.** A cohort with US-domestic-only preference (no cross-border-traffic) requires FHFT1 zero-fine-tuning pool; a cohort with EU-only preference requires FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning pool (or FHFT3 quarterly-rollup-fine-tuning for EU-LLCs); a cohort with JP-only preference requires FHFT3 quarterly-rollup-fine-tuning pool (or FHFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning for JP-individuals); a cohort with BR-only preference requires FHFT4 monthly-rollup-fine-tuning pool — but the FHFT-tier model EU-centric-by-default; cohort must use FHFT4 monthly-rollup-fine-tuning as BR-equivalent. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-FHFT-residency-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — FHFT5 × US-domestic-cohort score 0; route to FHFT1 zero-fine-tuning fallbacks.
