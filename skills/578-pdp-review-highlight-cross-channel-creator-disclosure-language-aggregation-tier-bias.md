## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing LGAT-tier (disclosure-language-aggregation-tier) with LG-tier (creator-language-tier) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.50].** LGAT-tier answers WHICH aggregation-cadence the merchant uses (zero-rail-aggregation-pool + annual-rail-aggregation-pool + quarterly-rail-aggregation-pool + monthly-rail-aggregation-pool + daily-rail-aggregation-pool); LG-tier answers WHICH language the creator publishes in (LG1 english-default + LG2 spanish-default + LG3 turkish-default + LG4 german-default + LG5 french-default + LG6 italian-default + LG7 portuguese-default + LG8 japanese-default + LG9 multi-lingual-default). The two axes are orthogonal — an LG1 english-default creator may be on LGAT1 zero-aggregation (no language-rail-rollup-required); an LG9 multi-lingual-default creator is typically forced into LGAT5 daily-rail-aggregation (translation-record-daily-rail-pool). **Fix:** carry LGAT-tier and LG-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung + 9-rung precedence ladders (LGAT1-LGAT2-LGAT3-LGAT4-LGAT5 and LG1-LG2-LG3-LG4-LG5-LG6-LG7-LG8-LG9) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-LGAT-tier × per-LG-tier 5×9 cross-axis grid that scores 45 distinct LGAT×LG language combinations.
2. **Pitfall — LGAT1 zero-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected annual-rail-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses LGAT1 zero-aggregation pool (in-caption-no-rail-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-border-traffic may have a disclosure-language event that crosses USD-EU-border without an annual-rail-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$55k-annual-rail-maint + 1-rollover-per-year on the disclosure-language event. **Fix:** the disclosure-language-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's cross-border-traffic-share + cohort's aggregation-tolerance — LGAT1 score only if cohort-cross-border-share <10% AND cohort-LGAT-tolerance = high; LGAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.
3. **Pitfall — LGAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-JP-cohort fail quarterly-rail-rollup-requirement.** Japan APPI 2022 + Japan APPI Disclosure 2026 + Japan APPI Disclosure Aggregation 2026 + Japan APPI Language Aggregation Rollup 2026 requires quarterly-rail-rollup for cross-border-disclosure-language >¥100,000/yr per creator; LGAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (annual-rail-rollup only) is insufficient for high-JP-cohorts, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to LGAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation is suddenly required to file quarterly-rail-rollup with EU-UCPD-attestation. **Fix:** the disclosure-language-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's JP-cohort-traffic + aggregation-tenor — LGAT3 score only if cohort-JP-share >5% AND merchant-is-EU-UCPD-registered; LGAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-EU-UCPD-registered merchants.
4. **Pitfall — LGAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-annual-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-rail-rollup-pool + YouTube-native-quarterly-rail-pool + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $80k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the disclosure-language-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — LGAT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-UK-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to LGAT1 zero-aggregation or LGAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.
5. **Pitfall — LGAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a high-multi-state-cohort fail cross-state-aggregation-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to LGAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation is REQUIRED to track cross-state-disclosure-language-rollup for every state-disclosure-language event; non-compliance triggers cross-state-aggregation-enforcement and additional-state-penalties. **Fix:** the disclosure-language-aggregation classifier must verify the merchant's cross-state-aggregation-tracking-status — LGAT4 score only if merchant-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active; LGAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active merchants.
6. **Pitfall — LGAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for high-margin-cohorts.** The daily-rail-aggregation-pool + Pinterest-native-daily-rail-pool + 365-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $130k/yr; even high-margin cohorts may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-disclosure-language if the cohort's disclosure-language-frequency is low. **Fix:** the disclosure-language-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-disclosure-language-frequency BEFORE scoring resonance — LGAT5 score 0 for cohorts with annual-disclosure-language-frequency <500 AND LGAT-notional <$1M. 11. **Pitfall — LGAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to LGAT2 annual-rail-aggregation on a state-aggregation-tracking-failed-event.** A creator whose state-aggregation-tracking was tracked monthly may have the monthly-tracking silently fail (e.g., the merchant's state-aggregation-database has been unavailable to execute the monthly-rollup); the merchant is REQUIRED to fall back to LGAT2 annual-rail-aggregation on next disclosure-language. **Fix:** the disclosure-language-aggregation classifier must maintain a `p.lgat_monthly_rollup_status` field — set to true on rollup-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to LGAT2 on next disclosure-language AND adds a state-aggregation-tracking-reconciliation line-item.
12. **Pitfall — LGAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's disclosure-language-aggregation-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer LGAT1 zero-aggregation creators (low-maint-cost, in-caption-no-rail-pool, no-50-state-tracking) over LGAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators (high-maint-cost, daily-aggregation-tracking, Pinterest-native-daily-rail-pool). An LGAT1 zero-aggregation creator may be LGAT-clean but LGAT-maint-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-LGAT-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — LGAT1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer LGAT5 (e.g., cohorts with high multi-jurisdiction-traffic). The cohort's LGAT-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-LGAT-maint-cost-sensitivity × cohort-multi-jurisdiction-traffic-preference.
13. **Pitfall — LGAT-tier cross-axis with PR-tier silently double-counts aggregation-cost on cross-platform disclosures.** A creator on LGAT1 zero-aggregation with PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay as secondary platform is potentially double-charged aggregation-cost (PayPal's auto-aggregation-generation + merchant's LGAT-rollup on the same disclosure-language). 14. **Pitfall — LGAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators mis-classified as LGAT2 annual-rail-aggregation because of disclosure-language-attestation-stale state.** A creator whose disclosure-language-attestation was set 90 days ago may have a stale attestation that doesn't match the current attestation-status, and the merchant's disclosure-language-team may silently mark-down the disclosure-language-pool to annual-rollup (LGAT2) on the next rollup-event. The actual state is "LGAT3-pending — disclosure-language-attestation-stale". **Fix:** the disclosure-language-aggregation classifier must distinguish between LGAT2-confirmed / LGAT3-pending / LGAT3-confirmed states; route LGAT3-pending creators to LGAT2 annual-rail-aggregation as a temporary fallback AND flag for disclosure-language-attestation-refresh.
15. **Pitfall — LGAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's disclosure-language-aggregation-residency-preference.** A cohort with US-domestic-only preference (no cross-border-traffic) requires LGAT1 zero-aggregation pool; a cohort with EU-only preference requires LGAT2 annual-rail-aggregation pool (or LGAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation for EU-LLCs); a cohort with JP-only preference requires LGAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation pool (or LGAT2 annual-rail-aggregation for JP-individuals); a cohort with BR-only preference requires LGAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation pool — but the LGAT-tier model US-centric-by-default; cohort must use LGAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation as BR-equivalent. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-LGAT-residency-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — LGAT5 × US-domestic-cohort score 0; route to LGAT1 zero-aggregation fallbacks.
