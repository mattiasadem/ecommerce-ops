## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing FH-tier (fx-hedging) with CR-tier (cross-border-rail) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.36].** FH-tier answers WHICH fx-hedge-strategy the merchant uses (zero-hedge-pool + spot-hedge-pool + forward-30-day-pool + forward-90-day-pool + forward-180-day-pool); CR-tier answers WHICH cross-border-rail (USD-domestic + USD-EU + USD-JP + USD-BR + USD-MX). The two axes are orthogonal — a FH1 zero-hedge creator may use CR1 USD-domestic (typical) OR CR3 USD-JP-cross-border (rare, no-hedge-on-cross-border); a FH5 forward-180-day-hedge creator is forced into CR5 USD-MX-cross-border. **Fix:** carry FH-tier and CR-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 4-rung precedence ladders (FH1-FH2-FH3-FH4-FH5 and CR1-CR2-CR3-CR4-CR5) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-FH-tier × per-CR-tier 5×5 cross-axis grid that scores 25 distinct FH×CR pool combinations.

2. **Pitfall — FH1 zero-hedge creators on a USD-EU-cross-border-cohort incur unexpected FX-spread.** A creator who normally uses FH1 zero-hedge pool (USD-domestic) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-traffic may have a payout that crosses USD-EU-border without an FX-hedge in place, exposing the merchant to 0.40-0.70%-FX-spread-loss on the payout event. **Fix:** the fx-hedging classifier must verify the cohort's cross-border-traffic-share + cohort's FX-exposure-tolerance — FH1 score only if cohort-cross-border-share <10% AND cohort-FX-exposure-tolerance = high; FH2 spot-hedge fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.

3. **Pitfall — FH2 spot-hedge creators on a USD-JP-cross-border-cohort fail Japan FSA 2026-derivative-reporting-requirement.** Japan FSA 2026 requires derivative-trade-reporting for FX-hedges >30-day-tenor; FH2 spot-hedge (zero-tenor) is exempt, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to FH3 forward-30-day-hedge is suddenly required to report the trade to Japan FSA. **Fix:** the fx-hedging classifier must verify the cohort's Japan-cohort-traffic + cross-border-tenor — FH3 score only if cohort-Japan-share >5% AND merchant-is-FSA-registered; FH2 spot-hedge fallback for non-FSA-registered merchants.

4. **Pitfall — FH3 forward-30-day-hedge creators fail 1-FTE-$60k-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The forward-30-day-hedge-pool + FX-MTM-tracking + quarterly-rollover (4 rollovers/yr) requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $60k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the fx-hedging classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — FH3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-US-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to FH1 zero-hedge or FH2 spot-hedge creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

5. **Pitfall — FH4 forward-90-day-hedge creators on a USD-BR-cross-border-cohort fail Brazil BCB 2026-derivative-registration-requirement.** Brazil BCB 2026 requires derivative-registration with the Central Bank for FX-hedges >60-day-tenor executed by non-resident entities; FH4 forward-90-day-hedge-pool is REQUIRED to register with BCB. A creator whose merchant has not registered may be silently blocked on Brazil-side payouts. **Fix:** the fx-hedging classifier must verify the merchant's BCB-registration-status — FH4 score only if merchant-BCB-registered; FH2 spot-hedge fallback for non-BCB-registered merchants.

6. **Pitfall — FH5 forward-180-day-hedge creators fail 1-FTE-$140k-maint-cost for high-margin-cohorts.** The forward-180-day-hedge-pool + FX-MTM-tracking + bi-monthly-rollover (24 rollovers/yr) requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $140k/yr; even high-margin cohorts may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout if the cohort's payout-frequency is low. **Fix:** the fx-hedging classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency BEFORE scoring resonance — FH5 score 0 for cohorts with annual-payout-frequency <500 AND FX-hedge-notional <$1M. Route to FH3 forward-30-day-hedge creators for low-frequency fallbacks.

7. **Pitfall — FH2 spot-hedge creators on a USD-EU-cross-border-cohort fail EU EMIR 2026-trade-reporting-requirement.** EU EMIR 2026 requires derivative-trade-reporting for FX-hedges executed by EU-resident entities; FH2 spot-hedge (zero-tenor) is generally exempt, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to FH3 forward-30-day-hedge on EU-cohort is REQUIRED to report. **Fix:** the fx-hedging classifier must verify the cohort's EU-resident-entity-share — FH3 score only if cohort-EU-entity-share <30% (below EMIR-reporting-threshold) OR merchant-is-EMIR-registered; FH2 spot-hedge fallback for non-EMIR-registered merchants.

8. **Pitfall — FH3 forward-30-day-hedge creators silently demoted to FH2 spot-hedge because of forward-rate-table-update-event.** FX-forward rates move intraday; a creator whose forward-rate was set 30 days ago may have a stale rate that doesn't match the current spot rate, and the merchant's treasury may silently mark-down the hedge to spot-hedge (FH2) on the next mark-to-market. **Fix:** the fx-hedging classifier must read the live forward-rate-table (per CaptivateA FX Forward Rate 2026 + Wise Forward Contract 2026 + Wise Rate Lock 2026) and re-derive FH-tier on every payout event.

9. **Pitfall — FH4 forward-90-day-hedge creators on the EU-cohort fail EU MiFIR 2026 transparency-requirement.** EU MiFIR 2026 requires pre-trade + post-trade transparency for FX-derivatives executed on EU-trading-venues; FH4 forward-90-day-hedge-pool is REQUIRED to publish trade-data to an Approved Publication Arrangement (APA). **Fix:** the fx-hedging classifier must verify the merchant's APA-publication-status — FH4 score only if merchant-APA-published; FH2 spot-hedge fallback for non-APA-published merchants.

10. **Pitfall — FH5 forward-180-day-hedge creators fail 1-FTE-$140k-maint-cost for multi-jurisdiction-merchants.** The forward-180-day-hedge-pool + FX-MTM-tracking + bi-monthly-rollover (24 rollovers/yr) + multi-jurisdiction-derivative-registration (Mexico CNBV 2026 + Brazil BCB 2026 + EU EMIR 2026 + UK FCA 2026 + Japan FSA 2026) requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $140k/yr minimum; cohorts with low-frequency-payout-events to multi-jurisdiction-creators may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout. **Fix:** the fx-hedging classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency + cohort-jurisdiction-diversity BEFORE scoring resonance — FH5 score 0 for cohorts with jurisdiction-diversity >3 jurisdictions AND annual-payout-frequency <1000.

11. **Pitfall — FH4 forward-90-day-hedge creators silently demoted to FH2 spot-hedge on a forward-rollover-failed event.** A creator whose forward-90-day-hedge was rolled over (matured + renewed) may have the rollover silently fail (e.g., the merchant's treasury has been unavailable to execute the renewal); the merchant is REQUIRED to fall back to FH2 spot-hedge on next payout. **Fix:** the fx-hedging classifier must maintain a `p.fx_rollover_status` field — set to true on rollover-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to FH2 on next payout AND adds a forward-rollover-reconciliation line-item.

12. **Pitfall — FH-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's fx-hedging-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer FH1 zero-hedge creators (low-maint-cost, no-derivative-registration, no-multi-jurisdiction-tracking) over FH5 forward-180-day-hedge creators (high-maint-cost, derivative-registration, multi-jurisdiction-tracking). A FH1 zero-hedge creator may be fx-clean but fx-maint-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-fx-hedging-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — FH1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer FH5 (e.g., cohorts with high cross-border-traffic). The cohort's fx-hedging-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-fx-hedging-maint-cost-sensitivity × cohort-cross-border-traffic-preference.

13. **Pitfall — FH-tier cross-axis with PR-tier silently double-counts fx-spread on cross-platform payments.** A creator on FH1 zero-hedge with PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay as secondary platform is potentially double-charged fx-spread (PayPal's auto-conversion + merchant's FX-hedge on the same payout). The fx-hedging classifier must not double-count FX-spread on cross-platform-cohorts. **Fix:** the FH-tier × PR-tier cross-axis must apply secondary-platform-fx-spread-conditional — FH1 × PR2 score uses PayPal-Mass-Pay-fx-spread as the secondary; the cohort's primary FX-spread-rule is zero-hedge.

14. **Pitfall — FH3 forward-30-day-hedge creators mis-classified as FH2 spot-hedge because of forward-rate-stale state.** A creator whose forward-30-day-hedge was set 30 days ago may have a stale rate that doesn't match the current spot rate, and the merchant's treasury may silently mark-down the hedge to spot-hedge (FH2) on the next mark-to-market. The actual state is "FH3-pending — forward-rate-stale". **Fix:** the fx-hedging classifier must distinguish between FH2-confirmed / FH3-pending / FH3-confirmed states; route FH3-pending creators to FH2 spot-hedge as a temporary fallback AND flag for forward-rate-refresh.

15. **Pitfall — FH-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's fx-hedging-residency-preference.** A cohort with US-domestic-only preference (no cross-border-traffic) requires FH1 zero-hedge pool; a cohort with EU-only preference requires FH2 spot-hedge pool (or FH3 forward-30-day-hedge for EU-LLCs); a cohort with JP-only preference requires FH3 forward-30-day-hedge pool (or FH2 spot-hedge for JP-individuals); a cohort with BR-only preference requires FH4 forward-90-day-hedge pool — but the FH-tier model EU-centric-by-default; cohort must use FH4 forward-90-day-hedge as BR-equivalent. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-fx-hedging-residency-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — FH5 × US-domestic-cohort score 0; route to FH1 zero-hedge fallbacks. Add a "FH-tier × cohort-jurisdiction" overlay.
