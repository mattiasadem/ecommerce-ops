# Per-network fee-modeling for Visa VAMP + Mastercard ECM/HECM + Amex RED/ICPR + Discover DMP + MATCH-list risk + acquirer reserve opportunity cost + per-network acquirer pass-through fines + ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE cost projection (Move #167.3)

> A best-in-class **Per-network fee-modeling** layer converts the operator's static chargeback-management flow (Move #167) + CE 3.0 evidence-pack automation (Move #167.1) + Subscription Account Updater audit (Move #167.2) into a **per-network, per-program-tier, per-projected-ratio cost projection** that quantifies the operator's true Year-1 cost of being out-of-compliance vs the Year-1 cost of fixing it. The 2025-2026 card-network rule change created a new class of cost model: **Visa VAMP** (merchant Excessive threshold dropped from 2.2% → 1.50% on 1 Apr 2026 in AP/CA/EU/US per Visa VAMP Fact Sheet 2025; $5-$10 per-dispute fine when acquirer ratio exceeds 0.30%-0.50%, $8 per-VAMP-Count when merchant is identified Excessive per Braintree 2025) + **Mastercard ECM** (1.50%-2.99% ratio + 100-299 chargebacks/month → $1,000-$100,000/month assessment by month 19+ per Mastercard ECP 2025) + **Mastercard HECM** (3.0%+ ratio + 300+ chargebacks → $1,000-$200,000/month assessment) + **Amex RED** (1.0% ratio for 3 months → $25 per chargeback above the threshold) + **Amex ICPR** (Immediate Chargeback Program — Amex skips the inquiry step + code FR4/FR5) + **Discover DMP** (1.0% + 100 chargebacks/month → $25 per chargeback above the limit) + **MATCH-list** (5-year industry shutout, MasterCard MATCH Pro 2025 + Visa MATCH 2024) + **acquirer reserves** (typically 5-15% of monthly processing volume held for 6-12 months for at-risk merchants; opportunity cost is the WACC of the frozen capital, 8-12% annualized for an SMB). At default $1M-$5M GMV with a 1.2%-1.5% dispute ratio (the "danger zone" between safe <0.9% and Excessive >1.5% per Braintree VAMP 2025), the layer projects the operator's **12-month fine + reserve + opportunity-cost exposure at $48,000-$320,000**, then ranks the ROI of each mitigation (CE 3.0 evidence composer at 18:1-55:1 + pre-dispute interceptor at 30:1-60:1 + Account Updater at 8:1-22:1 + checkout-descriptor at 4:1-12:1 + 3DS expansion at 6:1-18:1) so the operator can prioritize the moves that fix the most-acquirer-fee cost per dollar of investment. **Year-1 ROI 5:1-15:1** at the default Path B mid-market case; payback in 4-8 weeks once a non-compliant ratio is detected. The 2025-2026 stack is the canonical "I shipped Move #167 + Move #167.1 + Move #167.2 but my acquirer just sent me a 1-page letter saying I'm at 1.4% VAMP and will incur $8 per chargeback in 30 days — I have no model that quantifies the cost vs the cost of fixing it" anti-pattern. This skill is the **per-network cost-modeling layer** that ships (a) a 4-network fee calculator (VAMP / ECM/HECM / RED/ICPR / DMP per-network per-tier per-fine), (b) a MATCH-list risk calculator (5-year revenue-at-risk × probability of MATCH placement by tier), (c) an acquirer reserve opportunity-cost calculator (5-15% reserve × WACC × hold-period), (d) a per-network acquirer pass-through fattener (the acquirer may charge MORE than the network's published fine — the "pass-through multiplier" is typically 1.0×-2.5×), (e) a 12-month ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE projection (linear + exponential convergence scenarios) that quantifies the projected ratio drop from Move #167.1 (CE 3.0) + Move #167.2 (Account Updater) + Move #6.5/6.6/6.7 (attribution audits), and (f) a per-network cost-vs-mitigation-ROI matrix so the operator can see "investing $X in CE 3.0 saves $Y in VAMP fines + $Z in reserve opportunity cost" on a single dashboard. The skill does NOT change the merchant's actual dispute ratio — that's Move #167.1 + #167.2 + #N.5 — but it makes the cost of the ratio **visible and actionable** in dollar terms the CFO can sign off on.

## When to use this skill

Use this skill the first time any of these is true:

- the operator has **shipped Move #167 (chargeback management)** at Tier-1 + is **NOT modeling the per-network cost of their current dispute ratio** (the canonical "I'm at 1.2% Visa ratio and the acquirer just told me I'll be on the VAMP Warning list at 1.5% but I don't have a model showing the $ cost of crossing the threshold" anti-pattern per Visa VAMP Fact Sheet 2025 + Braintree 2025 + Chargeflow 2026);

- the operator's **dispute ratio is between 0.9% and 2.2%** (the canonical VAMP "danger zone" per Braintree 2025 + Cside 2026 — the 2026 thresholds tighten to 1.5% on 1 Apr 2026, so anything above 0.9% is on watch; anything above 1.5% is Excessive) and is **NOT running a 12-month projection of the per-network fine + reserve cost** (the canonical "I'll just absorb $8 per chargeback as a cost of doing business" reflex that becomes a $40k-$80k/yr problem at default $1M-$5M GMV per Visa VAMP Fact Sheet 2025 + Acquired 2025 + Braintree 2025);

- the operator is **on a 3DS coverage below 60%** AND is **NOT modeling the per-network EFM (Excessive Fraud Merchant) program** — the canonical "Mastercard EFM triggers at 0.50% fraud-to-sales ratio + $50K+ fraud claims + <50% 3DS coverage (regulated) or <10% (non-regulated) + 1,000+ e-commerce transactions, and I'm at 45% 3DS with $30K monthly fraud claims so I'm 6 months from EFM enrollment" anti-pattern per Mastercard EFM 2025 + Antom 2026 + Payments-and-Risk 2026;

- the operator's **acquirer sent a "VAMP Warning" or "ECM enrollment" letter** but the operator **does NOT have a written defense plan** quantifying (a) the projected ratio drop from each mitigation, (b) the projected $ savings, (c) the projected MATCH-list risk reduction, (d) the projected acquirer reserve release timeline (the canonical "the acquirer just told me I'm at 1.4% and they want a 10% reserve ($100k of my working capital is now frozen); I have 30 days to either fix the ratio or accept the reserve" anti-pattern per Chargeback Gurus 2025 + Braintree 2025);

- the operator's **Amex volume is >5% of total card volume** AND is **NOT modeling the RED/ICPR cost** (the canonical "Amex's RED is at 1% (lower than Visa's 1.5%) and my Amex dispute ratio is at 0.8% — I'm 1 bad month from RED enrollment + the $25 per excess chargeback" anti-pattern per Amex RED 2025 + Chargeback Gurus 2025 + Solidgate 2025);

- the operator's **Discover volume is >3% of total card volume** AND is **NOT modeling the DMP cost** (the canonical "Discover's DMP is at 1% + 100 chargebacks/month and I just hit 95 chargebacks last month — the next 6 chargebacks put me on the DMP" anti-pattern per Discover DMP 2025 + Chargeback Gurus 2025 + Chargeflow 2026);

- the operator has **MATCH-list risk exposure** (the canonical "if I go over 2.2% VAMP for 3 months I get MATCH-listed, and the MATCH listing stays for 5 years; every acquirer I apply to in the next 5 years will reject me — that's $X million of revenue-at-risk over 5 years" anti-pattern per Mastercard MATCH Pro 2025 + Visa MATCH 2024 + Chargeback Gurus MATCH 2026 + Redress Compliance 2025);

- the operator's **acquirer is holding a 5-15% rolling reserve** (the canonical "the acquirer told me to keep 10% of my monthly volume in a reserve account for 6 months as a 'risk buffer' — that's $50K of my working capital that I can't use to buy inventory or run Meta ads" anti-pattern per Acquirer Reserve 2025 + Chargeback Gurus 2025);

- the operator has **NOT modeled the per-network acquirer pass-through fines** (the canonical "Visa charges the acquirer $5-$10 per dispute when the acquirer ratio exceeds 0.30%-0.50%, but my acquirer is charging me $20 per dispute to 'recover' their pass-through — the 2.0×-2.5× multiplier is the acquirer's profit margin" anti-pattern per Acquirer Pass-Through Fines 2025 + Visa VAMP FAQ 2025);

- the operator is **building a business case to ship Move #167.1 (CE 3.0)** + Move #167.2 (Account Updater) + Move #6.5/6.6/6.7 (attribution audits) and needs a **per-network cost projection** to justify the $5k-$15k investment to the CFO (the canonical "I need a model that says 'investing $10K in CE 3.0 saves $80K-$200K in VAMP fines + reserve opportunity cost over 12 months' — but the CFO won't approve a $10K chargeback automation without a model" anti-pattern per Verifi 2025 + Stripe CE 3 API 2025 + Chargeflow 2026);

- the operator's **dispute volume is between 50 and 100 chargebacks/month per network** (the canonical ECM "low" tier per Mastercard ECP 2025 — at 100 chargebacks + 1.5% ratio you enter ECM, at 50-99 you're in the "Chargeback-Monitored Merchant" (CMM) warning tier per Antom 2026) and is **NOT modeling the cost of crossing the 100-chargeback threshold** (the canonical "I'm at 87 chargebacks this month; 13 more and I'm in ECM at $1,000-$100,000/month fines" anti-pattern per Mastercard ECP 2025 + Redo 2026 + Multi-Flow 2026);

- the operator's **3DS coverage is between 50% and 80%** AND is **NOT modeling the Mastercard EFM (Excessive Fraud Merchant) trigger** (the canonical "Mastercard EFM triggers when fraud ratio >0.50% + 3DS coverage <50% in regulated markets; I'm at 0.40% fraud ratio + 65% 3DS — I'm 0.10pp away from EFM enrollment" anti-pattern per Mastercard EFM 2025 + Antom 2026 + Payments-and-Risk 2026).

## What "best in class" looks like

A best-in-class Per-network fee-modeling layer has **SEVEN mutually-reinforcing components** running in parallel. Every component has a 2025-2026 network-published threshold + per-row benchmark + verification gate.

**Component 1: 4-network per-tier fee calculator.** The calculator takes the operator's monthly dispute count per network (Visa / Mastercard / Amex / Discover) + the monthly settled-transaction count per network + the projected ratio per network + the projected months-in-program, and outputs the projected annual fine per network per tier. The 4 networks use 4 different denominator conventions (Visa = same-month sales count; Mastercard = prior-month sales count; Amex = same-month gross charges minus credits; Discover = same-month chargeback count), so the calculator MUST normalize the operator's input to each network's convention before applying the threshold.

**Component 2: MATCH-list risk calculator.** The MATCH (Mastercard Alert to Control High-risk Merchants) list + the Visa MATCH (Terminated Merchant File) is a 5-year industry-wide database. Every acquirer checks MATCH before onboarding a new merchant. The risk calculator takes the operator's projected ratio trajectory + projected months-in-Excessive + projected acquirer response (termination / reserve escalation) and outputs (a) the probability of MATCH placement, (b) the 5-year revenue-at-risk (per Visa MATCH 2024 + Mastercard MATCH 2025 + Global Legal 2025 + Chargeback Gurus MATCH 2026), (c) the probability-weighted cost of MATCH placement. Per Redress Compliance 2025, MATCH placement rate increases sharply above 3 months in Excessive (the canonical "acquirers typically give you 3 months to remediate before terminating + MATCH-placing" anti-pattern).

**Component 3: Acquirer reserve opportunity-cost calculator.** Acquirers typically hold 5-15% of monthly processing volume as a "rolling reserve" for at-risk merchants. The hold period is typically 6-12 months. The opportunity cost is the WACC (weighted average cost of capital) of the frozen capital — for an SMB, the WACC is the operator's marginal cost of debt (8-12% APR) + the opportunity cost of inventory/ads that could have been purchased with the capital. The calculator takes the operator's monthly processing volume × reserve percentage × hold period × WACC and outputs the 12-month opportunity cost. Per Acquirer Reserve 2025 + Chargeback Gurus 2025, a $500K/mo processor with a 10% reserve × 6-month hold × 10% WACC has $25,000 of opportunity cost per reserve cycle.

The model takes the network-published fine × the operator's specific acquirer's pass-through multiplier and outputs the effective per-dispute fine. ## The build (3-4 weeks, ~16-24 hours of operator time)

A 6-phase build. Each phase has step-by-step instructions + verification gates.

**Phase 1: Per-network dispute volume + ratio baseline (2-3 hours, Day 1).** Step 1.1 — Export the per-network per-month dispute volume from the last 12 months: Visa (TC15 dispute count + TC40 fraud report count from acquirer portal), Mastercard (chargeback count from acquirer portal or Mastercard Merchant Portal), Amex (chargeback count from Amex Merchant Portal), Discover (chargeback count from Discover Merchant Portal or acquirer). Step 1.2 — Export the per-network per-month settled transaction count from each acquirer portal. Step 1.3 — Compute the per-network ratio per network's convention: Visa = (TC15 + TC40) / same-month sales; Mastercard = (first presentment chargebacks in month N) / (transactions in month N-1); Amex = (chargebacks minus reversals) / (gross charges minus credits); Discover = (chargebacks) / (settled transactions). Step 1.4 — Build a 12-month ratio chart per network, color-coded safe (<0.9%) / warning (0.9-1.5%) / Excessive (>1.5%). Step 1.5 — Identify the network driving the operator's blended ratio (e.g., "Visa is 1.2% but Mastercard is 2.0% — the blended 1.5% is masking the ECM-eligible Mastercard exposure"). Step 1.6 — Verify Phase 1: the dashboard shows 12-month per-network ratio + the network with the highest exposure + the months in warning/Excessive tier.

**Phase 2: 4-network fee calculator + MATCH-risk calculator (4-6 hours, Days 2-4).** Step 2.1 — Build the per-network per-tier per-month-in-program fee calculator. The calculator takes (current_ratio, current_count, current_volume, current_months_in_program, projected_ratio_trajectory) per network and outputs the projected 12-month fine per network per tier using the published threshold tables above. Step 2.2 — Build the MATCH-list risk calculator. The calculator takes (projected_ratio_trajectory, projected_months_in_excessive, projected_acquirer_response) and outputs (probability_of_MATCH_placement, 5-year_revenue_at_risk, probability_weighted_MATCH_cost). Step 2.3 — Build the acquirer reserve opportunity-cost calculator. The calculator takes (monthly_processing_volume × reserve_percentage × hold_period × WACC) and outputs the 12-month opportunity cost. Step 2.4 — Build the acquirer pass-through multiplier table. The table is per-acquirer (e.g., Stripe: 1.0×-1.2× / Adyen: 1.0×-1.5× / Worldpay: 1.5×-2.0× / legacy regional banks: 2.0×-2.5×) and the calculator applies the multiplier to the network-published fine. Step 2.5 — Wire the calculator to the per-network ratio baseline from Phase 1 so the operator sees the live fine exposure. Step 2.6 — Verify Phase 2: a 4-test test suite that asserts (a) the per-network calculator correctly applies each network's published threshold + tier, (b) the MATCH-risk calculator returns the right probability for the 0-3 / 4-6 / 7-12 / 12+ month bands, (c) the reserve calculator applies the right WACC, (d) the pass-through multiplier correctly adjusts the network-published fine.

**Phase 3: 12-month ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE projection (3-4 hours, Days 5-7).** Step 3.1 — Build the linear + exponential convergence scenarios. Linear: ratio drops 0.30-0.50pp over 90 days (CE 3.0 evidence composer + Account Updater) + 0.10-0.20pp over 60 days (Move #6.5/6.6/6.7 attribution audits). Exponential: ratio drops 0.40-0.70pp over 180 days (full stack). Step 3.2 — Build the per-scenario 12-month fine + reserve + MATCH-risk + opportunity-cost projection. Step 3.3 — Build the "crossing the threshold" detector: identifies the projected month in each scenario where the operator crosses below the network's safe threshold (Visa 0.9% / Mastercard 1.0% / Amex 1.0% / Discover 1.0%). Step 3.4 — Build the "what-if" slider: lets the operator adjust the projected ratio drop per mitigation to see the live impact on the 12-month projection. Step 3.5 — Verify Phase 3: a 3-test test suite that asserts (a) the linear scenario correctly drops 0.30-0.50pp over 90 days, (b) the exponential scenario correctly drops 0.40-0.70pp over 180 days, (c) the threshold-crossing detector correctly identifies the projected month.

**Phase 4: Per-network cost-vs-mitigation-ROI matrix (3-4 hours, Days 8-10).** Step 4.1 — Build the per-mitigation annual cost table (CE 3.0 composer $5K-$15K / pre-dispute interceptor $3K-$8K / Account Updater $2K-$5K / 3DS expansion $1K-$3K / checkout descriptor $500-$2K / friendly-fraud deterrent $1K-$3K). Step 4.2 — Build the per-mitigation per-network fine-savings projection (from Move #167.1 worked example + Move #167.2 worked example). Step 4.3 — Build the per-mitigation reserve-release projection (the reserve typically releases after 3-6 months of compliance, and the 12-month opportunity-cost savings are proportional to the reserve percentage × the months-until-release). Step 4.4 — Build the per-mitigation MATCH-risk-reduction projection (each mitigation reduces the probability of MATCH placement by a fixed percentage per the table in Component 6). Step 4.5 — Build the net ROI = (per-network fine savings + reserve release + MATCH-risk reduction) / per-mitigation annual cost. Step 4.6 — Verify Phase 4: a 5-test test suite that asserts (a) the per-mitigation annual cost matches the canonical ranges, (b) the per-network fine savings match the CE 3.0 + Account Updater worked examples, (c) the net ROI matches the canonical ranges (10:1-55:1 for CE 3.0 / 8:1-25:1 for pre-dispute / etc.).

**Phase 5: Per-MID VAMP ratio dashboard (2-3 hours, Days 11-12).** Step 5.1 — Build the per-MID per-network ratio breakdown. For multi-MID merchants (Shopify US + Shopify EU + Amazon US + eBay US + TikTok Shop US), the dashboard shows the per-MID per-network ratio + the per-MID per-network status (safe / warning / Excessive) + the per-MID per-network fine exposure. Step 5.2 — Build the per-MID mitigation priority list: identifies the MID with the highest ratio + the lowest mitigation cost (e.g., "Shopify EU (Visa) at 0.6% — already safe, no action; Amazon US (Visa) at 3.0% — Excessive, ship CE 3.0 here first"). Step 5.3 — Build the "blended vs per-MID" comparison: shows the operator's blended ratio vs the per-MID ratios so they can see "blended 1.5% but Amazon US is 3.0% — the blended number is masking the per-MID exposure". Step 5.4 — Verify Phase 5: a 3-test test suite that asserts (a) the per-MID breakdown correctly identifies the highest-ratio MID, (b) the per-MID mitigation priority list correctly ranks the MIDs, (c) the blended-vs-per-MID comparison correctly surfaces the masking effect.

**Phase 6: CFO-friendly executive summary + business case builder (2-3 hours, Days 13-14).** Step 6.1 — Build the 1-page executive summary: total 12-month cost-exposure (fine + reserve + MATCH + opportunity cost) vs total mitigation cost (full stack) vs net savings + ROI + payback period. Step 6.2 — Build the "ship X to save Y" prioritized action list: ranks the mitigations by net ROI (highest first) and shows the cumulative cost-savings as the operator adds mitigations to the plan. Step 6.3 — Build the PDF/CSV export: lets the operator export the 1-page summary + the per-network ratio chart + the per-mitigation ROI matrix to share with the CFO. Step 6.4 — Wire the dashboard to Slack/email: weekly digest showing the current per-network ratio + the projected 12-month cost-exposure + the top 3 mitigations to ship next. Step 6.5 — Verify Phase 6: a 4-test test suite that asserts (a) the executive summary correctly totals the 12-month cost-exposure, (b) the prioritized action list correctly ranks the mitigations, (c) the PDF/CSV export produces a valid file, (d) the Slack/email digest fires on the right cadence.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

**P1. The calculator uses Visa's same-month sales count as the denominator for ALL networks, but Mastercard uses prior-month sales count — the operator's "blended" ratio is wrong.** Per Mastercard ECP 2025 + Antom 2026: "Mastercard's ratio = (first presentment chargebacks in month N) / (transactions in month N-1) × 10,000 bps." A growing store's prior-month denominator is SMALLER than the same-month denominator, so the Mastercard ratio reads HIGHER than the same-month calculation. The operator who uses Visa's same-month convention for Mastercard will under-report their ratio by 5-15%. **Fix:** The calculator MUST normalize each network's denominator convention. Per-network conventions: Visa = same-month sales; Mastercard = prior-month sales; Amex = same-month gross charges minus credits; Discover = same-month sales.

**P2. The calculator applies the acquirer's pass-through fine at 1.0× but the actual pass-through multiplier is 1.5×-2.5× — the operator under-projects the cost.** Per Acquirer Pass-Through Fines 2025 + Visa VAMP FAQ 2025 + Stripe Chargeback Protection 2025, the acquirer's pass-through multiplier varies by acquirer (Stripe: 1.0×-1.2× / Adyen: 1.0×-1.5× / Worldpay: 1.5×-2.0× / legacy regional banks: 2.0×-2.5×). A calculator that uses 1.0× under-projects by $40K-$200K over 12 months. **Fix:** The calculator MUST have a per-acquirer pass-through multiplier table. The operator enters their specific acquirer, and the calculator applies the matching multiplier.

**P3. The MATCH-risk calculator uses a flat 5% probability of MATCH placement per month, but the probability increases sharply above 3 months in Excessive.** Per Redress Compliance 2025 + Global Legal 2025 + Chargeback Gurus MATCH 2026: the MATCH-placement probability is 5-10% per month in months 0-3, jumps to 15-25% in months 4-6, and to 30-50% in months 7-12. A flat 5% probability under-projects the MATCH risk by 2-5×. **Fix:** The MATCH-risk calculator MUST use the banded probability table (Component 2). The 5% / 15% / 30% bands are per Redress Compliance 2025 + Chargeback Gurus MATCH 2026.

**P4. The reserve opportunity-cost calculator uses the operator's WACC at 0% (or "free capital") — the actual opportunity cost is 8-12% annualized.** Per Acquirer Reserve 2025 + Chargeback Gurus 2025, a $50K reserve held for 6 months at a 10% WACC is $2,500 of opportunity cost. A calculator that uses 0% under-projects the reserve cost by $2,500-$50,000 per reserve cycle. **Fix:** The reserve calculator MUST take a WACC input (default 10% for SMB, configurable for the operator's specific cost of capital). The output is the 12-month opportunity cost = monthly_volume × reserve_percentage × hold_months × WACC.

**P5. The 12-month projection assumes a linear ratio drop of 0.50pp over 90 days, but the actual ratio drop is non-linear (front-loaded in the first 30 days, then asymptotes).** Per Move #167.1 worked example: the CE 3.0 evidence composer typically drops the ratio by 0.30-0.50pp over 90 days, but the front-loaded drop is 0.20-0.30pp in the first 30 days (the easy wins — pre-dispute interception + 3DS exclusion + TC40 scrubber) + 0.10-0.20pp in days 30-90 (the harder wins — representment win-rate uplift + repeat-offender cohort). A linear projection over-estimates the day-90 ratio and under-estimates the day-30 ratio. **Fix:** The projection MUST use the 0.20-0.30pp / 0.10-0.20pp / 0.05-0.10pp staged drop (front-loaded) rather than a linear 0.50pp over 90 days. The model validates against Move #167.1 + Move #167.2 worked examples.

**P6. The per-network cost-vs-mitigation-ROI matrix omits the reserve-release upside — the operator projects the fine savings but not the reserve release.** Per Acquirer Reserve 2025 + Chargeback Gurus 2025, the reserve release is typically 3-6 months after the ratio drops below the network's safe threshold, and the 12-month opportunity-cost savings are typically $20K-$100K for a $500K-$1M/mo processor. A matrix that omits the reserve release under-projects the total ROI by 30-60%. **Fix:** The matrix MUST include the reserve-release column. The 12-month opportunity-cost savings are calculated as (monthly_volume × reserve_percentage × months_until_release × WACC) per Component 3.

**P7. The per-MID dashboard blends all MIDs into a single ratio, masking the per-MID exposure — the operator sees 1.2% blended but one MID is at 3.0%.** Per Move #167.1 Extension 6 + Braintree 2025: VAMP is per-MID, not blended. A merchant with 4 MIDs (Shopify US / Shopify EU / Amazon US / eBay US) is monitored per-MID per-network, and the blended ratio masks the per-MID exposure. An operator who sees "1.2% blended" doesn't realize their Amazon US MID is at 3.0% (Excessive) and is generating the bulk of the fine exposure. **Fix:** The per-MID dashboard MUST break down the ratio per MID per network, and the prioritized action list MUST rank MIDs by highest-ratio × lowest-mitigation-cost.

**P8. The MATCH-risk calculator assumes MATCH placement costs the operator the full $5M-$25M of 5-year revenue, but the actual cost is the probability-weighted portion of that revenue.** Per Mastercard MATCH Pro 2025 + Visa MATCH 2024 + Global Legal 2025, MATCH placement rate is 15-30% at 0-3 months in Excessive, but the operator can apply to a new acquirer post-MATCH (most acquirers reject, but some accept with a higher reserve). The probability-weighted cost is (probability × revenue-at-risk), not the full revenue-at-risk. **Fix:** The MATCH-risk calculator MUST output the probability-weighted cost, not the full revenue-at-risk. The output is a single number ($) that the operator can add to the 12-month cost-exposure.

**P9. The 4-network fee calculator applies the per-network per-tier fine schedule, but the operator's actual network exposure may be region-specific (LAC + AP have different thresholds than US/EU).** Per Visa VAMP Fact Sheet 2025 + LAC Region VAMP 2026: the VAMP thresholds tighten faster in LAC (1.50% on 1 Apr 2026 in AP/CA/EU/US; LAC tightens separately) and the per-dispute fine may be higher in some regions. A calculator that uses only the US/EU thresholds under-projects the cost for LAC/AP operators by 20-40%. **Fix:** The calculator MUST take a region input and apply the region-specific thresholds. The default is US/EU/AP/CA; the operator selects their region to get the matching threshold.

**P10. The CE 3.0 + pre-dispute mitigation ROI assumes 65-80% representment win-rate, but the actual win-rate is closer to 50-65% for first-time CE 3.0 filers.** Per Move #167.1 worked example + Stripe CE 3 API 2025 + Chargeflow 2026: the canonical CE 3.0 win-rate is 65-80% on QUALIFYING disputes, but the qualification rate is only 25-40% of all 10.4 disputes. The actual blended win-rate is 25-40% × 65-80% = 16-32% of all 10.4 disputes. A ROI matrix that uses 65-80% as the blended win-rate over-projects the savings by 2-3×. **Fix:** The ROI matrix MUST use the blended win-rate (qualification rate × CE 3.0 win-rate) and the per-row "qualifying disputes only" qualification rate. The 16-32% blended number is the canonical Move #167.1 + Move #167.2 worked example.

**P11. The 3DS-expansion ROI assumes the issuer accepts the 3DS liability shift, but 5-15% of 3DS-frictionless transactions still receive 10.4 disputes (the issuer didn't accept the frictionless authentication).** Per Visa 3DS 2024 + EMVCo 3DS 2024 + Move #167.1 Extension 3: 5-15% of 3DS-frictionless transactions still generate 10.4 disputes, and these disputes require the Move #167.1 Extension 3 special path (3DS-frictionless CE 3.0 fallback with 3DS authentication data in the evidence pack). A ROI matrix that assumes 100% 3DS-liability-shift over-projects the savings by 5-15%. **Fix:** The 3DS-expansion ROI MUST use the 85-95% 3DS-liability-shift effective rate (i.e., 5-15% of frictionless transactions still generate disputes that need CE 3.0 fallback).

**P12. The MATCH-list risk calculator assumes the operator can re-apply to a new acquirer post-MATCH at a 0% acceptance rate, but the actual acceptance rate is 5-15% (specialty high-risk acquirers accept MATCH-listed merchants at higher reserves + processing fees).** Per Global Legal 2025 + Redress Compliance 2025 + Chargeback Gurus MATCH 2026, the MATCH-list acceptance rate is 5-15% across specialty acquirers (e.g., Durango Merchant Services, Easy Pay Direct, EMBR Merchant Services) at a 2-3× higher reserve + 0.5-1.5% higher processing fees. A calculator that assumes 0% acceptance over-projects the MATCH cost by 10-30%. **Fix:** The MATCH-risk calculator MUST take an acceptance-rate input (default 10%) and a post-MATCH processing-cost premium input (default 1.0% of volume). The output is the probability-weighted net cost (probability × revenue-at-risk × (1 - acceptance_rate × 0.5) - new acquirer processing premium).

**P13. The acquirer reserve opportunity-cost calculator uses the operator's WACC, but most SMB operators don't know their WACC (and conflate it with "interest rate").** Per Acquirer Reserve 2025: the WACC for a SMB is the marginal cost of debt (8-12% APR for an SMB line of credit) + the opportunity cost of capital (the return the operator would have earned by investing the capital in inventory/ads/expansion). A calculator that asks for "WACC" and accepts "5%" under-projects the opportunity cost. **Fix:** The reserve calculator MUST default the WACC to 10% (the canonical SMB WACC) and provide a tooltip explaining the 8-12% range. The operator can override the default if they know their actual WACC.

**P14. The 12-month ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE projection uses the operator's CURRENT ratio as the starting point, but the operator's ratio is volatile (varies 0.20-0.50pp month-to-month) — the projection should use the 3-month or 6-month rolling average.** Per Chargeback Gurus 2025 + Chargeflow 2026: a single month's ratio is noisy (a single large BFCM order with 5 chargebacks can spike the ratio by 0.10-0.30pp). A projection that uses the current month as the starting point over-reacts to the noise and under-projects the true exposure. **Fix:** The projection MUST use the 3-month or 6-month rolling average as the starting point. The model validates the rolling average against the 12-month per-network ratio chart from Phase 1.

**P15. The per-network cost-vs-mitigation-ROI matrix assumes each mitigation is independent, but the mitigations are correlated (CE 3.0 composer + pre-dispute interceptor + Account Updater each reduce the same pool of disputes) — the total ROI is LESS than the sum of the per-mitigation ROIs.** Per Chargeflow 2026 + Justt 2025 + Move #167.1 worked example: the full stack (CE 3.0 + pre-dispute + Account Updater) reduces the ratio by 0.30-0.50pp total, but the per-mitigation drops are NOT additive (CE 3.0 = 0.20-0.30pp, pre-dispute = 0.10-0.20pp, Account Updater = 0.05-0.15pp — but the union is 0.30-0.50pp because some disputes are caught by multiple mitigations). A matrix that sums the per-mitigation savings over-projects the total ROI by 20-40%. **Fix:** The matrix MUST apply a 0.6-0.8× correlation factor to the per-mitigation savings when summing the full stack. The 0.7× correlation factor is the canonical Chargeflow 2026 + Move #167.1 worked example.

## Verification (this skill is "shipped" when...)

**Gate A: 4-network fee calculator is live + matches the operator's current per-network ratio within ±0.05pp.** The calculator's per-network per-tier per-month-in-program output matches the operator's current per-network ratio (from Phase 1) within ±0.05pp. Below this accuracy = the per-network denominator convention (P1) is wrong; above = the network-published thresholds are out of date.

**Gate B: MATCH-risk calculator returns a probability-weighted cost within the canonical 0-3 / 4-6 / 7-12 / 12+ bands.** The probability bands match the Redress Compliance 2025 + Chargeback Gurus MATCH 2026 + Global Legal 2025 canonical 5-10% / 15-25% / 30-50% / 50-80% per-month probabilities. A 0% probability for months 0-3 = the banded probability table (P3) is wrong; a 100% probability for months 0-3 = the banded probability is over-applied.

**Gate C: Reserve opportunity-cost calculator uses the WACC default of 10% and the per-acquirer reserve percentage.** The default WACC is 10% (the canonical SMB WACC per Acquirer Reserve 2025), and the per-acquirer reserve percentage is the canonical 5-15%. A 0% WACC default = the WACC input (P4 + P13) is misconfigured; a 0% reserve default = the per-acquirer reserve percentage is misconfigured.

**Gate D: 12-month ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE projection matches the Move #167.1 + Move #167.2 worked example within ±10%.** The 12-month ratio trajectory for the linear scenario (Move #167.1 + #167.2 only) drops 0.30-0.50pp over 90 days, matching the Move #167.1 worked example (Verifi 2025 + Stripe CE 3 API 2025 + Chargeflow 2026). A drop of <0.20pp = the staged drop (P5) is wrong; a drop of >0.70pp = the staged drop is over-applied.

**Gate E: Per-network cost-vs-mitigation-ROI matrix returns net ROI within the canonical 10:1-55:1 for CE 3.0 / 8:1-25:1 for pre-dispute / 8:1-22:1 for Account Updater / 12:1-40:1 for 3DS expansion / 4:1-12:1 for descriptor + friendly-fraud deterrent.** The per-mitigation net ROI matches the Move #167.1 + Move #167.2 + Move #N.5 worked examples. A ROI of <5:1 for CE 3.0 = the blended win-rate (P10) is wrong; a ROI of >100:1 for any mitigation = the per-network fine savings are over-projected.

**Gate F: Per-MID VAMP ratio dashboard breaks down the operator's MIDs and identifies the highest-ratio MID.** For multi-MID merchants, the dashboard shows the per-MID per-network ratio and ranks MIDs by highest-ratio × lowest-mitigation-cost. A blended-only view (P7) = the per-MID breakdown is misconfigured; a missing per-MID ranking = the prioritized action list (P7) is wrong.

**Gate G: CFO-friendly executive summary totals the 12-month cost-exposure correctly.** The 1-page summary totals (per-network fine + reserve + MATCH-risk + opportunity cost) match the sum of the per-component projections. A mismatch = the per-component projections are out of sync; a missing MATCH-risk or opportunity-cost line = one of the 4 cost components is omitted.

**Gate H: The "ship X to save Y" prioritized action list ranks the mitigations by net ROI and shows the cumulative cost-savings correctly.** The action list ranks CE 3.0 / pre-dispute / Account Updater / 3DS / descriptor / friendly-fraud deterrent by net ROI (highest first), and the cumulative cost-savings applies the 0.7× correlation factor (P15) to avoid double-counting. A cumulative savings that exceeds the per-mitigation sum by >40% = the correlation factor (P15) is misconfigured.
