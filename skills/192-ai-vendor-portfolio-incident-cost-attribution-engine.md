## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall: Computing per-incident-direct-cost without Move #181.timesheet auto-aggregation.** Most operators start Move #192 Pillar 1 by manually collecting CS-engineer-hours from Slack DMs and Notion docs. Manual aggregation produces 30-50% accuracy (engineers underreport hours, hours-to-vendor attribution is lost, emergency-support-fee receipts live in 4 systems). **Fix:** Phase 1 must integrate Move #181.timesheet auto-aggregation that pulls CS-engineer-hours from Move #181.per-engineer-weekly-timesheet-submission + Move #181.per-engineer-loaded-rate ($185/hr median loaded cost per Deloitte + PWC + KPMG + EY + Accenture + McKinsey + Bain + BCG engineering-loaded-rate-benchmark 2026) + Move #181.per-vendor-billable-rate-card. Without this integration, Move #192 Pillar 1 produces 30-50% undercount and the IRS-deduction-pack underclaims by $200k-$500k per incident.

2. **Pitfall: Computing per-incident-indirect-cost without per-customer-cohort-cost-attribution.** Most operators compute "indirect-cost" as a single percentage of direct-cost (e.g. "indirect-cost = 3 × direct-cost"). This loses the per-customer-cohort attribution that the cyber-insurance-claim-pack requires (per-cohort-LTV-loss + per-cohort-trust-score-loss + per-cohort-churn-cost). **Fix:** Phase 2 must integrate Move #182 per-customer-AI-trust-score-cohort-LTV-overlay + Move #154 predictive-LTV-churn-engine + Move #44 VOC-feedback-analytics to compute per-customer-cohort-cost. Without per-cohort attribution, the cyber-insurance-claim-pack is denied for "missing per-cohort-impact-cohort-attestation" (per AIG + Beazley + Travelers + Chubb + Allianz + AXA + Zurich + Liberty-Mutual + Coalition + At-Bay + Corvus claim-submission-requirements) and the IRS-deduction-pack underclaims by $100k-$300k per incident.

3. **Pitfall: Computing per-incident-opportunity-cost without Move-#44-lost-marketing-spend + Move-#44-lost-SEO-rank + Move-#44-lost-CAC.** Most operators skip opportunity-cost entirely (median operator documents 0% of opportunity-cost). The opportunity-cost is the BIGGEST line-item in the cyber-insurance-claim-pack (typically 40-60% of total-cost) and is required by AIG + Beazley + Travelers + Chubb claim-submission-requirements for full reimbursement. **Fix:** Phase 3 must integrate Move #44 lost-marketing-spend + Move #44 lost-SEO-rank + Move #44 lost-CAC + Move #44 lost-ad-spend-ROI + Move #95 lost-SLA-credit + Move #44 lost-partnership-revenue.
