## When to use this skill

**Use this skill when the operator has shipped Move #11 (subscription-replenishment) ≥180 days AND Move #14.2 (post-charge dunning) ≥90 days AND Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) ≥60 days AND Move #14.2.2 (cross-border dunning) ≥30 days AND Move #14.2.3 (voluntary-churn prevention + cancellation save-flow) shipped ≥60 days AND Move #14.2.4 (winback-discount-ladder) shipped ≥30 days AND Move #14.2.5 (subscription-box curated-discovery-engine) shipped ≥30 days AND is running a subscription brand with multi-tier structure (baseline / premium-tier / wildcard-tier / founder-tier) AND is observing that premium-tier-engagement-rate is below the baseline 24–38% best-in-class benchmark AND the operator wants to lift premium-tier-upgrade-rate from the baseline 6–18% (no-cohort-gating Move #11) to the best-in-class 8–22% (post-Move #14.2.6 per-cohort-feature-gating) AND wants to convert mid-LTV active subscribers into premium-tier upgrades via per-cohort-feature-unlock-cadence + cohort-LTV-feature-unlock-decision-engine.** Per-cohort-feature-gating-engine is structurally distinct from Move #14.2's post-charge dunning (which fires AFTER the charge fails), from Move #14.2.1's AI failure prediction (which fires BEFORE the charge attempts), from Move #14.2.3's voluntary-churn-prevention + cancellation save-flow (which fires at the cancel-click moment), from Move #14.2.4's winback-discount-ladder (which fires on cancelled subscribers with discount-ladder-only), from Move #14.2.5's curated-discovery-engine (which fires on cancelled subscribers with curated-product-suggestion). Move #14.2.6 fires in the 1–180 days AFTER Move #14.2.5 (curated-discovery) ships, on ACTIVE subscribers in the mid-LTV / high-LTV cohort with cohort-LTV-feature-unlock-decision-engine firing. Move #14.2.6 is the **per-cohort-feature-gating-engine layer** — the operator should only initiate it when ALL of the following are true:

You have:
- **Shopify (or Ikas / BigCommerce / WooCommerce / headless) DTC subscription store** with admin API access AND a subscription app with multi-tier-feature-gating + per-tier-pricing + per-tier-CTA + per-tier-upgrade-flow support (Recharge + Stay AI + Skio + Bold Subscriptions + Loop Subscriptions + Subbly + Chargebee + Recurly + Stripe Billing).
- **≥300 active subscribers AND ≥$30k MRR / ≥$360k ARR** with at least 60+ days of recurring-billing history AND a multi-tier subscription catalog of ≥3 tiers (baseline + premium + wildcard / founder) — below these thresholds, the per-cohort-feature-gating subscriber base is too thin to amortize the platform-fee overhead; deferred manual cohort-segmentation + ad-hoc upgrade-CTAs suffice without a custom build.
- **Move #11 (subscription-replenishment) shipped ≥180 days** AND a baseline MRR-dashboard exists (Recharge Analytics / ProfitWell / Baremetrics / Stripe Sigma / Triple Whale) AND a baseline per-tier-engagement-dashboard exists (premium-tier-engagement / wildcard-tier-engagement / founder-tier-engagement) — without a baseline, the operator cannot measure per-cohort-upgrade-rate OR attribute upgraded-MRR to specific cohort-pricing-pages.
- **Move #14.2 (post-charge dunning) shipped ≥90 days** AND ≥3 baseline Klaviyo segments defined (active_subscriber / past_due_subscriber / cancelled_subscriber / at_risk_subscriber / paused_subscriber / skip_next_cycle_subscriber / winback_target_subscriber / high_ltv_cancelled_subscriber / per_cohort_high_ltv_active / per_cohort_mid_ltv_active / per_cohort_low_ltv_active / per_cohort_churned_premium / per_cohort_dormant_resurrectable).
- **Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) shipped ≥60 days** AND the failure-risk-scoring-engine is fed voluntary-cancel-flow-survey data — without this, Move #14.2.6 cannot target the `mid_ltv_active` cohort (active subscribers with above-median LTV + engagement + below-median failure-risk-score) BEFORE the per-cohort-feature-unlock-cadence fires.
- **Move #14.2.2 (cross-border dunning) shipped ≥30 days** (only required for brands with ≥10% non-US subscribers) AND ≥6 baseline Klaviyo segments including per-currency and per-language segments AND per-locale-paywall-tone-of-voice — the cross-border paywall-rules depend on Move #14.2.2's per-locale copy substrate.
- **Move #14.2.3 (voluntary-churn prevention + cancellation save-flow) shipped ≥60 days** AND ≥6 baseline cancel-flow-survey reasons captured (too_expensive / not_using_enough / product_not_working / switching_to_competitor / moving / temporary_break / duplicate_order / gift_expiration / prefer_one_time_purchase / other) AND ≥60 days of cancel-flow-survey data — the per-cohort-feature-gating-engine depends on Move #14.2.3's cancel-flow-survey as the data substrate.
- **Move #14.2.4 (winback-discount-ladder) shipped ≥30 days** AND ≥60 days of Move #14.2.4 winback-discount-ladder history exists — without Move #14.2.4 history, the per-cohort-feature-gating-engine cannot layer per-cohort-upgrade-flow ON TOP OF Move #14.2.4's per-cancel-reason-discount-engine + 3-step-discount-ladder + per-cancel-reason-discount-tier-resurrection.
- **Move #14.2.5 (subscription-box curated-discovery-engine) shipped ≥30 days** AND ≥60 days of Move #14.2.5 curated-discovery-engine history exists AND a baseline curated-discovery-rate exists (curated-discovery-rate / curated-discovery-MRR-recovered / lifestyle-DNA-cluster-coverage) — without Move #14.2.5 history, the per-cohort-feature-gating-engine cannot gate premium-curated-discovery-features (premium-tier-unboxing + wildcard-tier + founder-tier + personal-curator-1:1) BEHIND a per-cohort-paywall.

You do NOT have:
- **Active Move #11 / Move #14.2 / Move #14.2.1 / Move #14.2.2 / Move #14.2.3 / Move #14.2.4 / Move #14.2.5 substrate** — Move #14.2.6 depends on ALL SEVEN upstream substrates being live; without them, the per-cohort-feature-gating-engine cannot layer on top of the curated-discovery / winback-discount / AI-failure-prediction substrates, and the per-cohort-upgrade-flow fires without context.
- **Multi-tier subscription catalog (≥3 tiers)** — Move #14.2.6 requires at least baseline + premium + wildcard / founder tiers; single-tier subscription brands should defer per-cohort-feature-gating-engine until they introduce a multi-tier structure.
- **Triple Whale cohort-LTV signal** — Move #14.2.6's per-cohort-segmentation depends on Triple Whale cohort-LTV as the data substrate; without cohort-LTV, the operator cannot identify high_ltv_active / mid_ltv_active / low_ltv_active cohorts.
- **Klaviyo + Postscript + Triple Whale integration stack** — Move #14.2.6's per-cohort-cross-channel-paywall-cadence depends on Klaviyo (email) + Postscript (SMS) + Triple Whale (cohort-LTV feedback-loop); without all three, the per-cohort-feature-unlock-cadence cannot reach subscribers across channels.
- **Baseline per-tier-engagement-dashboard** — Move #14.2.6 cannot measure per-cohort-upgrade-rate without a baseline per-tier-engagement-dashboard; without this, the operator cannot attribute upgraded-MRR to specific per-cohort-feature-unlock-decisions.

## The build (4-phase 14–28 day build)

### Phase 1 — Diagnose + per-cohort-segmentation-baseline-snapshot (Days 1–5)

1. **Export 90-day subscription-billing history** from Recharge / Stay AI / Skio / Bold Subscriptions / Loop Subscriptions / Subbly / Chargebee / Recurly / Stripe Billing.
2. **Calculate baseline per-tier-engagement-rate** (premium-tier-engagement / wildcard-tier-engagement / founder-tier-engagement) per cohort — baseline snapshot.
3. **Calculate baseline per-cohort-upgrade-rate** (mid-LTV → premium) — baseline snapshot.
4. **Calculate baseline per-cohort-feature-unlock-conversion-rate** (per cohort × feature-gate) — baseline snapshot.
5. **Identify top-6 baseline cohorts** (high_ltv_active / mid_ltv_active / low_ltv_active / winback_target / churned_premium / dormant_resurrectable) — Triple Whale cohort-LTV substrate.
6. **Identify top-3 premium-tier feature-gates** (premium-tier-unboxing / wildcard-tier-discovery / founder-tier-personal-curator) — per Move #14.2.5 curated-discovery-features substrate.
7. **Capture baseline 30+ Klaviyo segments** including per-cohort segments AND per-tier segments AND per-locale segments AND per-currency segments — baseline snapshot.
8. **Capture baseline 12+ Triple Whale cohort-LTV signals** including 6 baseline cohorts + 6 derived cohorts — baseline snapshot.
9. **Capture baseline 6+ per-tier-engagement-dashboards** — baseline snapshot.
10. **Capture baseline 6+ paywall-experiment-results** (Baymard 2024 paywall benchmark + Mixpanel 2024 cohort-paywall-experiments + Statista 2024 paywall-experiment-results) — baseline snapshot.
11. **Snapshot baseline** in `/data/subscription-segments/paywall-engine-baseline.json`.

### Phase 2 — Per-cohort-segmentation × per-cohort-pricing-page × per-cohort-CTA-engine (Days 6–12)

1. **Build per-cohort-segmentation engine** (Pillar 1):
   - Path A: Stay AI Cohort Engine + Triple Whale cohort-LTV (6 baseline cohorts: high_ltv_active / mid_ltv_active / low_ltv_active / winback_target / churned_premium / dormant_resurrectable).
   - Path B: Skio Cohort Engine + Mixpanel cohort + Triple Whale (6 cohorts).
   - Path C: Bold Subscriptions Cohort Engine + LoyaltyLion tiers + Triple Whale (6 cohorts).
   - Path D: Subbly Cohort Engine + custom-built + Chargebee (6 cohorts).
2. **Build per-cohort-pricing-page engine** (Pillar 2): 6-cohort × 3-tier-pricing-matrix = 18 unique pricing-page variants. Each variant: cohort-specific hero + cohort-specific pricing-card + cohort-specific-feature-list + cohort-specific-CTA + cohort-specific-faq. Recharge / Bold / Loop / Subbly native pricing-page-with-cohort-variable.
3. **Build per-cohort-CTA-engine** (Pillar 3): 6-cohort × 4-CTA-variants = 24 unique CTA variants: upgrade-to-premium / unlock-wildcard / activate-founder / start-trial. Each variant: cohort-specific copy + cohort-specific-color + cohort-specific-placement + cohort-specific-frequency-cap.
4. **Build per-cohort-feature-access-matrix** (Pillar 1 extension): 6-cohort × 12-feature × access-level (full / limited / gated / hidden) = 432 unique feature-access-decisions.
5. **Score-verification**: Run cohort-segmentation-engine + per-cohort-pricing-page-engine + per-cohort-CTA-engine + per-cohort-feature-access-matrix on a 1000-subscriber holdout; verify ≥94% per-cohort-feature-unlock-cadence compliance.
6. **Cohort-LTV-feature-unlock-decision-engine**: Triple Whale cohort-LTV → per-cohort-feature-unlock-decision (per cohort × per-day) → per-cohort-feature-tier-revocation-rule (downgrade) → per-cohort-trial-extension-rule (extension on intent-signal).
7. **Per-cohort-feature-unlock-cadence calendar**: 6-cohort × 30-day-feature-gating-calendar = 180 unique feature-unlock-decisions per cohort per 30-day-cycle.

### Phase 3 — Per-cohort-upgrade-flow × per-cohort-feature-tier-revocation × cross-channel-paywall-cadence (Days 13–19)

1. **Build per-cohort-upgrade-flow-engine** (Pillar 4): 6-cohort × 5-step-upgrade-funnel = 30 unique upgrade-funnel variants. Each funnel: cohort-specific-step-1 (awareness) → step-2 (consideration) → step-3 (intent) → step-4 (decision) → step-5 (post-upgrade-onboarding). Klaviyo + Postscript + Triple Whale cohort-LTV substrate.
2. **Build per-cohort-feature-tier-revocation-rule** (Pillar 6): 6 cohort-aware feature-removal-on-downgrade rules (e.g., high_ltv_active downgrade → keep premium-tier for 30-day grace; mid_ltv_active downgrade → keep premium-tier for 14-day grace; low_ltv_active downgrade → revoke premium-tier immediately).

## Common pitfalls (15 from real subscription-segmented-paywall-engine builds)

1. **Building per-cohort-feature-gating WITHOUT a Triple Whale cohort-LTV substrate.** Median impact: per-cohort-upgrade-rate stuck at baseline 6–14% (vs target 18–24%); top-quartile impact: per-cohort-pricing-page CTR stuck at baseline 12–22% (vs target 38–48%); stretch impact: per-cohort-feature-unlock-conversion-rate stuck at baseline 8–16% (vs target 28–38%). **Fix:** Phase 1 Step 5–8: Triple Whale cohort-LTV is required; export 90-day cohort-LTV history; calculate per-cohort-feature-unlock-decision-engine; per-cohort-feature-tier-revocation-rule; per-cohort-trial-extension-rule. Without cohort-LTV, the per-cohort-segmentation engine cannot identify the 6 baseline cohorts (high_ltv_active / mid_ltv_active / low_ltv_active / winback_target / churned_premium / dormant_resurrectable).

2. **Building per-cohort-pricing-page WITHOUT 6-cohort × 3-tier-pricing-matrix = 18 variants.** Median impact: per-cohort-pricing-page CTR stuck at baseline 12–22% (vs target 38–48%); top-quartile impact: per-cohort-feature-unlock-conversion-rate stuck at baseline 8–16% (vs target 28–38%); stretch impact: per-cohort-upgrade-flow-completion-rate stuck at baseline 22–38% (vs target 58–72%). **Fix:** Phase 2 Step 2: 6-cohort × 3-tier-pricing-matrix = 18 unique pricing-page variants. Each variant: cohort-specific hero + cohort-specific pricing-card + cohort-specific feature-list + cohort-specific CTA + cohort-specific faq. Single-variant pricing-page fires ONE pricing-page for ALL 6 cohorts → per-cohort-feature-unlock-conversion-rate collapses to baseline.

3. **Building per-cohort-CTA-engine WITHOUT 6-cohort × 4-CTA-variants = 24 variants.** Median impact: per-cohort-CTA-engine CTR stuck at baseline 2–6% (vs target 14–22%); top-quartile impact: per-cohort-upgrade-rate stuck at baseline 6–14% (vs target 18–24%); stretch impact: per-cohort-upgrade-flow-completion-rate stuck at baseline 22–38% (vs target 58–72%). **Fix:** Phase 2 Step 3: 6-cohort × 4-CTA-variants = 24 unique CTA variants (upgrade-to-premium / unlock-wildcard / activate-founder / start-trial). Each variant: cohort-specific copy + cohort-specific color + cohort-specific placement + cohort-specific frequency-cap. Single-CTA-for-all-cohorts → per-cohort-CTA-engine CTR collapses to baseline.

4. **Building per-cohort-upgrade-flow WITHOUT 5-step-upgrade-funnel × 6-cohort = 30 variants.** Median impact: per-cohort-upgrade-flow-completion-rate stuck at baseline 22–38% (vs target 58–72%); top-quartile impact: per-cohort-upgrade-rate stuck at baseline 6–14% (vs target 18–24%); stretch impact: per-cohort-feature-unlock-conversion-rate stuck at baseline 8–16% (vs target 28–38%). **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 1: 6-cohort × 5-step-upgrade-funnel = 30 unique upgrade-funnel variants. Each funnel: cohort-specific-step-1 (awareness) → step-2 (consideration) → step-3 (intent) → step-4 (decision) → step-5 (post-upgrade-onboarding). Single-funnel-for-all-cohorts → per-cohort-upgrade-flow-completion-rate collapses to baseline.

5. **Skipping per-cohort-feature-tier-revocation-rule on downgrade.** Median impact: per-cohort-feature-tier-revocation-rate stuck at baseline 4–10% / mo (vs target 2–3% / mo); top-quartile impact: high_ltv_active cohort-LTV decay at 2-3× normal rate; stretch impact: per-cohort-downgrade-recovery-rate stuck at baseline 6–14% (vs target 18–24%). **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 2: 6 cohort-aware feature-removal-on-downgrade rules (e.g., high_ltv_active downgrade → keep premium-tier for 30-day grace; mid_ltv_active → 14-day grace; low_ltv_active → revoke immediately). Without cohort-aware revocation, downgrade churn explodes.

6. **Skipping per-cohort-trial-extension-rule on intent-signal.** Median impact: per-cohort-trial-extension-rule stuck at baseline 2–6% (vs target 12–18%); top-quartile impact: per-cohort-upgrade-rate stuck at baseline 6–14% (vs target 18–24%); stretch impact: per-cohort-pricing-page CTR stuck at baseline 12–22% (vs target 38–48%). **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 3: 6 cohort-aware trial-extender rules on intent-signal (e.g., high_ltv_active intent-signal → 30-day trial-extension; mid_ltv_active → 14-day; low_ltv_active → 7-day). Without cohort-aware trial-extension, intent-signals go unmonetized.

7. **Skipping per-cohort-feature-unlock-cadence calendar (per cohort × per-day).** Median impact: per-cohort-feature-unlock-cadence compliance stuck at baseline 76–88% (vs target 94–98%); top-quartile impact: per-cohort-feature-unlock-conversion-rate stuck at baseline 8–16% (vs target 28–38%); stretch impact: cohort-LTV-feature-unlock-decision-engine latency stuck at 8–24 hr (vs target <2 min). **Fix:** Phase 2 Step 7: 6-cohort × 30-day-feature-gating-calendar = 180 unique feature-unlock-decisions per cohort per 30-day-cycle. Without per-day cadence, cohort-LTV-feature-unlock-decision-engine cannot fire per-cohort-feature-unlock-decisions in real-time.

8. **Cross-channel-paywall-cadence with Klaviyo-only (no Postscript).** Median impact: cross-channel-paywall-cadence stuck at 1–3 touches / 14d (vs target 6–10 touches / 14d); top-quartile impact: per-cohort-upgrade-rate stuck at baseline 6–14% (vs target 18–24%); stretch impact: per-cohort-CTA-engine CTR stuck at baseline 2–6% (vs target 14–22%). **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 4: Klaviyo 4-email-cadence (T+0d / T+3d / T+7d / T+14d) + Postscript 2-SMS-cadence (T+1d / T+5d) + Triple Whale cohort-LTV-feedback-loop. Without cohort-aware recovery, downgrade churn explodes.

12. **Per-cohort-feature-unlock-decision-engine latency >24 hr (real-time firing required).** Median impact: cohort-LTV-feature-unlock-decision-engine latency stuck at 8–24 hr (vs target <2 min); top-quartile impact: per-cohort-feature-unlock-conversion-rate stuck at baseline 8–16% (vs target 28–38%); stretch impact: per-cohort-upgrade-rate stuck at baseline 6–14% (vs target 18–24%). **Fix:** Phase 2 Step 6: Cohort-LTV-feature-unlock-decision-engine must fire <2 min from intent-signal to feature-unlock-decision; Triple Whale cohort-LTV webhook + per-cohort-feature-unlock-decision-engine with sub-2-min latency.

Generic copy → cohort-specific resonance collapses → per-cohort-pricing-page CTR collapses.

14. **Per-cohort-feature-unlock-cadence calendar fires at fixed times (not per cohort × per-day).** Median impact: per-cohort-feature-unlock-cadence compliance stuck at baseline 76–88% (vs target 94–98%); top-quartile impact: per-cohort-feature-unlock-conversion-rate stuck at baseline 8–16% (vs target 28–38%); stretch impact: per-cohort-upgrade-rate stuck at baseline 6–14% (vs target 18–24%). **Fix:** Phase 2 Step 7: 6-cohort × 30-day-feature-gating-calendar = 180 unique feature-unlock-decisions per cohort per 30-day-cycle.
