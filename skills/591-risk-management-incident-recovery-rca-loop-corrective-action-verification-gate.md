## What "best in class" looks like

A best-in-class Risk Management Recovery-RCA-loop with Verification-gate implementation has **8 distinguishing primitives**:

1. **Per-corrective-action 5-action-item-type taxonomy** — every corrective-action from every post-mortem gets categorized into exactly one of (per TruPath Labs Postmortem Discipline 2024):
   - **`rule`** — a documented rule in a `.claude/rules/.md` file or equivalent (e.g. "Pre-checks must be read-only — they cannot mutate config files"). Verification artifact = the rule's file path + last-modified timestamp.
   - **`hook`** — a mechanical pre/post-tool-use hook or equivalent (e.g. "pre-commit hook blocks any commit touching the alert-config without verification"). Verification artifact = the hook's commit hash.
   - **`test`** — an automated test that catches the failure (e.g. "test_chargeback_alert_threshold_re_raises.py verifies the alert fires at 0.3%"). Verification artifact = the test name + currently-passing run.
   - **`criterion`** — a sprint-contract row or release-gate that gates the next ship (e.g. "Holdout overlap with training: 0 (verified by test_X)"). Verification artifact = the contract-row-template path with the new criterion.
   - **`memory`** — a long-term memory entry that loads at session-start (e.g. "F1 > 0.99 on a non-trivial set is suspicious — re-verify"). Verification artifact = the memory file path + last-modified timestamp.

   The taxonomy is intentionally SMALL — every type has a verifiable shipping artifact (a rule has a file path, a hook has a commit hash, a test has a name + passing run, a criterion has a contract row, a memory has a last-modified timestamp). A corrective-action that cannot be categorized into one of the 5 types is "too vague to ship" → re-write it specifically enough to fit. Reference: TruPath Labs Postmortem Discipline 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 (62% recurrence-rate reduction) + Google SRE Workbook 2024.

2. **Per-corrective-action 30/60/90-day verification-cadence** — every corrective-action lives through a fixed cadence where the verification-evidence MUST be presented at each milestone (per Cuts Clothing 2024 + Atlassian Incident Postmortem 2024 + Beefed RCA Action Tracking 2024):
   - **Day-30 (immediate verification)** — owner presents the verification-evidence (e.g. "commit-hash abcd1234 merged, test_alert_threshold_re_raises.py passes, hook fires in pre-commit on the next 3 commits"). If day-30 evidence is NOT presented, surface rose-tone alert "Day-30 verification MISSING — corrective-action at risk".
   - **Day-60 (interim-milestone verification)** — owner presents the impact-evidence (e.g. "alert fired 2× in the last 30 days at 0.3% threshold, no chargeback-spike in the last 60 days"). If day-60 evidence is NOT presented, surface amber-tone alert "Day-60 interim verification MISSING — corrective-action lagging".
   - **Day-90 (recurrence-prevention verification)** — owner presents the recurrence-prevention-evidence (e.g. "no same-RCA-category incident in the last 90 days OR if a recurrence fired, the 2nd-generation-incident-tracker auto-raised it"). If day-90 evidence is NOT presented, surface sky-tone alert "Day-90 verification MISSING — corrective-action closure-deferred".

   The cadence is rigid because corrective-actions that pass day-30 BUT fail day-90 are the canonical "scheduled, not verified-shipped" anti-pattern — the corrective-action was filed + closed on the JIRA board but never actually prevented the recurrence. Reference: Cuts Clothing 2024 (62% recurrence-rate reduction over 18 months via 48-hour + 90-day cadence) + Atlassian Incident Postmortem 2024 + Beefed RCA Action Tracking 2024 + Google SRE Workbook 2024.

3. **Per-corrective-action owner-assignment with single-person accountability** — every corrective-action has EXACTLY ONE owner (not a team-owner, not a role-owner) + an explicit P0/P1/P2 priority + a Jira/LINEAR/Asana ticket created DURING the post-mortem meeting (not after) + a deadline at day-30 (e.g. "ship the hook"). Per Suheb Ghare Incident Response Playbook: P0/P1 corrective-actions get 7-day SLO for closure (expedite); P2 corrective-actions get 30-day SLO; P3 corrective-actions get 90-day SLO. Action-item-closure-rate target: ≥90% for P0/P1 within SLO window. Reference: Atlassian Incident Postmortem 2024 + Suheb Ghare Incident Response Playbook + Beefed RCA Action Tracking 2024 + Google SRE Workbook 2024.

4. **Per-corrective-action 2nd-generation-incident-raise-ratchet** — IF the same RCA-category incident fires AGAIN within 24 months of the original incident, the corrective-action that was "closed" on day-90 gets AUTO-RE-RAISED as a 2nd-generation incident for the same RCA-category AND the day's-90 verification-gate status flips to `failed-recurrence-prevention`. The ratchet is at the RCA-category level (e.g. "chargeback-spike-from-cohort-#3", "ad-account-disabled-Meta", "3PL-pallet-loss-facility-#2"), not at the per-incident level. The ratchet is necessary because "I had a Q3 2025 chargeback-spike AND a Q1 2027 chargeback-spike" is a recurrence — even if the second one was caused by a different contributing-factor, the recurrence-prevention-score for the RCA-category drops from ≥80 (pass) to 0 (fail). Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Verizon DBIR 2024 + Woodruff Sawyer Cyber Claims 2024.

5. **Per-corrective-action recurrence-prevention-score** — every RCA-category (post Move #N.99.1's 12 incident-types × the canonical 8-band recovery taxonomy) gets a `recurrencePreventionScore` from 0-100:
   - **100** — never re-occurred in 24 months
   - **80-99** — re-occurred ONCE in 24 months AND the corrective-action was verified-shipped at day-90 (acceptable)
   - **40-79** — re-occurred 2× in 24 months OR corrective-action failed day-90 verification-gate
   - **0-39** — re-occurred 3+× in 24 months OR corrective-action never shipped

   The score feeds Move #N.99.1's risk-mitigation health band (per Move #N.99.1 §8) — if a pillar's RCA-category scores <40, the pillar flips from `moderate-risk` (sky) to `critical-risk` (rose) regardless of the incident-count. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Gartner Incident Cost 2024.

6. **Per-pillar corrective-action-rollup with verification-rate** — for each pillar (Acquisition / Conversion / Retention / Attribution + Subscription), surface:
   - **`open-corrective-actions`** — count of corrective-actions that have not yet passed day-30
   - **`in-progress-corrective-actions`** — count of corrective-actions that have passed day-30 but not day-90
   - **`verified-corrective-actions`** — count of corrective-actions that passed day-90 with verified-shipped evidence
   - **`failed-corrective-actions`** — count of corrective-actions that failed day-90 verification (rose-tone)
   - **`2nd-gen-incidents-raised`** — count of 2nd-generation incidents auto-raised in this pillar
   - **`verification-rate`** — `verified / (verified + failed)` × 100 — feeds Move #N.24's per-pillar health band

   The rollup renders as a 4-band tone (emerald ≥90% verification-rate · sky 70-89% · amber 50-69% · rose <50%) per pillar + a 12-month-forward `corrective-action-forecast` (how many corrective-actions are projected to verify by month-end). Reference: Atlassian Incident Postmortem 2024 + Google SRE Workbook 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024.

7. **Cross-incident combined-corrective-action verification** — for combined-multi-incident events (Move #N.99.2), the combined-corrective-actions are verified as a STACK, not individually. Example: the 3PL warehouse fire + cascading chargeback-spike + email-deliverability-collapse trilogy produces a combined-corrective-action-set of 12 corrective-actions (4 per incident). The combined-rollup shows: "9 of 12 verified-shipped at day-90, 1 failed at day-30 (chargeback-alert-threshold change never merged because it was a duplicate of an existing alert), 2 pending — recurrence-prevention-score for the combined-RCA-category drops to 67 (amber) — schedule a 2nd-generation combined-incident-event-raise if ANY of the 3 underlying incidents re-occur in 18 months". Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Beazley Breach Response 2024 + Allbirds Warehouse Fire 2024 + Glossier 3PL Outage 2024.

8. **Verification-evidence-pack auto-generator with carrier-grade audit trail** — every verified-shipped corrective-action gets a `verificationEvidencePack` containing: (a) the action-item-type (rule/hook/test/criterion/memory), (b) the shipping-artifact (file path + commit hash / test name + passing run / contract-row path / memory-file + last-modified-timestamp), (c) the day-30 / day-60 / day-90 cadence-evidence, (d) the recurrence-prevention-score, (e) the 2nd-gen-incident-tracker-status. The pack renders as a single PDF + JSON sidecar at `/incidents/[id]/corrective-actions/[actionId]/verification` and attaches to the cyber-insurance renewal-readiness pack (Move #N.99.8) as evidence the carrier can audit. The carrier sees "corrective-action X verified-shipped at day-30 (commit-hash abcd1234 merged), verified at day-60 (alert fired 2× in 30 days), verified at day-90 (no recurrence), recurrence-prevention-score 87 (emerald) — accept" instead of the canonical "corrective-action X closed on Jira 2025-08-15 — accept on good-faith". Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024 + Beazley Breach Response 2024 + Woodruff Sawyer Cyber Claims 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024.

## Common pitfalls (16 from real builds)

1. **Don't accept vague corrective-actions like "be more careful with chargeback alerts" — force the 5-type taxonomy.** Without the rule/hook/test/criterion/memory taxonomy, the corrective-actions are "too vague to ship" per TruPath Labs Postmortem Discipline 2024. Every corrective-action MUST categorize into one of the 5 types or be re-written. The categorization is the load-bearing first-step of the verification-gate — a corrective-action without a type has no shipping-artifact, no commit-hash, no test-name, no contract-row, no memory-file. The day-30 verification cadence then has no verifiable artifact to check.

2. **Don't accept "closed on Jira" as verification-evidence — the verification-evidence MUST be the actual shipping-artifact.** A corrective-action marked "closed" on the JIRA board but never actually merged into the codebase is the canonical "scheduled, not verified-shipped" anti-pattern per TruPath Labs Postmortem Discipline 2024. The verification-evidence is the commit-hash + merged-PR-link for `hook` types, the test-name + currently-passing-run for `test` types, the rule-file-path + last-modified-timestamp for `rule` types, the contract-row-template-path for `criterion` types, the memory-file-path + last-modified-timestamp for `memory` types. "Closed on Jira" is bookkeeping, not verification.

3. **Don't skip the day-30 verification milestone — it's the load-bearing first gate.** The day-30 verification catches the canonical "the corrective-action was filed but never shipped" anti-pattern. A corrective-action that passes day-90 without passing day-30 is "scheduled, not verified-shipped" — the operator's story is "we marked it closed on Jira 3 months later". The day-30 gate is the EVIDENCE that the corrective-action actually shipped, BEFORE the operator forgets about it. Reference: Cuts Clothing 2024 (62% recurrence-rate reduction via 48-hour + 90-day cadence) + Atlassian 2024 + Suheb Ghare 2024.

4. **Don't run the 30/60/90-day cadence on a `team-owner` — require a single-person owner.** A corrective-action owned by "the platform team" or "the on-call rotation" is owned by nobody — when day-30 arrives, the Slack reminder goes to a channel, nobody picks it up, and the corrective-action silently slips past day-30 → day-60 → day-90 with no individual accountable. Always require ONE person with `@handle` ownership. Reference: Suheb Ghare Incident Response Playbook + Atlassian 2024 + Google SRE Workbook 2024.

5. **Don't auto-raise the 2nd-generation-incident on a per-incident-level match — raise at the RCA-category level.** The 2nd-gen-incident-ratchet must compare at the `rcaCategory` level (e.g. "chargeback-spike-cohort-LTV-tier-1"), NOT at the per-incident level. Two chargeback-spikes from DIFFERENT cohorts are still a chargeback-spike recurrence — the operator's preventive-control-layer (alert-threshold change) needs to be evaluated against both incidents, not just the first one. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Verizon DBIR 2024.

6. **Don't conflate the per-corrective-action recurrence-prevention-score with the per-RCA-category recurrence-prevention-score.** The corrective-action-level score measures whether THIS corrective-action verified-shipped (pass/fail day-30/60/90). The RCA-category-level score measures whether the SAME-RCA-category incident RE-OCCURRED (independent of the corrective-action). A corrective-action that passed day-90 on a chargeback-spike is still a "failed-recurrence-prevention" if the same RCA-category fired again in 24 months. Use separate keys: `ecom-ops:corrective-action-ledger:v1` (per-action) and `ecom-ops:second-gen-incident-tracker:v1` (per-RCA-category). Reference: Suheb Ghare 2024 + Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024.

7. **Don't ship a corrective-action without a verifiable shipping-artifact unique to its action-type.** The 5-type taxonomy is intentionally small BECAUSE every type has a verifiable shipping-artifact (rule → file path · hook → commit hash · test → test name + passing run · criterion → contract row path · memory → memory file path + last-modified timestamp). A corrective-action that targets "the agent should remember to" is unverifiable per TruPath Labs 2024 — re-write it to target one of the 5 layers (`.claude/rules/.md` / `.claude/hooks/*.sh` / `tests/test_X.py` / sprint-contract-template / `memory/feedback/*.md`).

8. **Don't run the day-90 cadence without the recurrence-prevention-evidence requirement.** A corrective-action that passes day-30 (shipped) + day-60 (impact) without passing day-90 (recurrence-prevention) is the canonical "verification-without-recurrence-prevention-check" anti-pattern. The day-90 gate is specifically the "did the SAME-RCA-category incident re-occur in 90 days?" check — if it did, the corrective-action auto-raises a 2nd-gen-incident AND flips day-90 status to `failed-recurrence-prevention`. Reference: Cuts Clothing 2024 + TruPath Labs 2024 + Coalition Cyber Claims 2024.

9. **Don't accept a corrective-action's owner assignment AFTER the post-mortem meeting — assign during the meeting.** A corrective-action whose Jira ticket is created 2 weeks after the post-mortem has lost the data-owners-from-the-meeting context: the engineer who debugged the incident has moved on, the IC has handed off, and the corrective-action is assigned to "team-platform" by default. Always create the Jira ticket DURING the post-mortem meeting so the data-owners (Support / SRE / Product / Comms) are in the room and the owner is one of them. Reference: Atlassian Incident Postmortem 2024 + Beefed RCA Action Tracking 2024 + Suheb Ghare 2024.

10. **Don't run the 30/60/90-day cadence silently — emit a Slack / e-mail / dashboard notification at each milestone.** A corrective-action that passes day-30 with no Slack notification is silently "closed" without the carrier ever knowing it shipped. The notification is the AUDIT TRAIL — the cyber-insurance carrier asks "how do I know the corrective-action shipped?" and the operator replies "here's the day-30 Slack notification, here's the day-60 impact notification, here's the day-90 recurrence-prevention notification". Reference: Cuts Clothing 2024 + Atlassian 2024 + Suheb Ghare 2024.

11. **Don't bucket 3rd-party vendor corrective-actions (e.g. 3PL-claim-lodging, Stripe-representment) into the same 5-type taxonomy without a vendor-witness layer.** A corrective-action like "file 3PL-claim within 30 days" is NOT a `rule` or `hook` — it's a vendor-external action that requires a vendor-witness (the 3PL's claim-confirmation email) as the verification-evidence. The 5-type taxonomy covers INTERNAL corrective-actions; for vendor-external corrective-actions, the verification-evidence is the vendor's confirmation email / claim-acknowledgment / representment-status-update. Reference: Bombas 3PL Claim 2024 + Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Beazley Breach Response 2024.

12. **Don't accept a same-day-30 + same-day-60 + same-day-90 cadence for ALL corrective-actions — priority dictates SLO.** A P3 (documentation/process) corrective-action has a 90-day SLO for closure per Atlassian 2024 + Suheb Ghare 2024. A P0/P1 (service outage / data loss) corrective-action has a 7-day SLO. Forcing the same 30/60/90-day cadence on a P0 corrective-action is over-engineering — by day-7 the P0 corrective-action should already be verified-shipped + recurrence-prevention-verified (or the corrective-action failed and a 2nd-gen-incident was auto-raised). Reference: Atlassian 2024 + Suheb Ghare 2024 + Google SRE Workbook 2024.

13. **Don't run the recurrence-prevention-score computation against the operator's CURRENT incident-history only — include ALL incidents in the last 24 months.** A corrective-action that passes day-90 today but the operator had a same-RCA-category incident 23 months ago is `failed-recurrence-prevention`, not `pass-day-90`. The score window must be 24 months (the default `CANONICAL_SECOND_GEN_INCIDENT_WINDOW_MONTHS`) — not "incidents since this corrective-action was filed". Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Verizon DBIR 2024.

14. **Don't surface a 4-band tone without the per-pillar verification-rate as the discriminator.** A pillar with 10 corrective-actions × 8 verified + 2 failed = 80% verification-rate is `sky` (70-89%). A pillar with 10 corrective-actions × 9 verified + 1 failed = 90% is `emerald`. A pillar with 5 corrective-actions × 2 verified + 3 failed = 40% is `rose`. The verification-rate is the canonical discriminator — not raw counts, not success-rate, not verification-gate-pass-rate. Reference: Atlassian 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Verizon DBIR 2024.

15. **Don't separate the corrective-action-rollup from the per-pillar risk-overlay (Move #N.99.1 §7) — wire them.** The per-pillar risk-overlay on Move #N.99.1 shows `pillar risk-band` (emerald <10% risk · sky 10-25% · amber 25-50% · rose >50%); the per-pillar corrective-action-rollup on Move #N.99.3 shows `pillar verification-rate` (emerald ≥90% · sky 70-89% · amber 50-69% · rose <50%). These two tones are COMPLEMENTARY — if either is rose, the pillar gets a `combined-pillar-risk-band = rose` with auto-CTA "freeze spend on this pillar". Reference: Atlassian Incident Postmortem 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Verizon DBIR 2024.

16. **Don't ship Move #N.99.3 without wiring to Move #N.99.8 (insurance-renewal-readiness-pack auto-generator).** The verification-evidence-pack on Move #N.99.3 auto-attaches to the renewal-readiness-pack on Move #N.99.8 — the carrier sees "verification-evidence for corrective-actions X, Y, Z with carrier-grade audit trail". Without this wire, the carrier asks "how do I know the corrective-action shipped?" and the operator says "I marked it closed on Jira" — and the renewal-premium goes up 15-40%. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + At-Bay 2024 + Woodruff Sawyer 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024.
