## When to use this skill

Use this skill when the operator is doing **≥$2M GMV** with **≥1 warehouse OR 3PL OR micro-fulfillment-center** (single-DTC-DC OR multi-node-DTC OR DTC + 3PL OR DTC + BOPIS OR DTC + MFC OR DTC + dark-store) AND the warehouse-robotics-automation friction curve has started to compound: per-order pick-pack labor cost exceeds $4-8 (the canonical "we staff 25 pickers at $20/hr to ship 1500 orders/day" anti-pattern), per-warehouse pick-rate-per-hour is below 80-120 picks/hr (the canonical "we cannot staff enough pickers to keep up with BFCM" capacity ceiling), per-warehouse pick-pack-error-rate exceeds 0.5-1% (the canonical "wrong-item shipped, customer service ticket, return shipping cost, lost customer" cycle), per-warehouse footprint-utilization is below 50-60% (the canonical "we expanded into 50,000 sqft but only use 25,000 sqft" leak), per-warehouse perfect-order-rate is below 95% (the canonical "1 in 20 orders has a pick-pack-ship defect" anti-pattern), per-warehouse OTIF-rate is below 92-95% (the canonical "1 in 15 orders ships late" friction), per-warehouse cycle-count-cost exceeds $0.50-$1.50 per cycle-count-unit (the canonical "we shut down operations for 2 days to count inventory" leak), per-warehouse throughput-per-square-foot is below 1-2 orders/sqft/month (the canonical "our DC ships less per square foot than a grocery store" anti-pattern), per-warehouse labor-cost-per-order exceeds $5-10 (the canonical "labor is our single largest fulfillment cost" friction), per-MFC-same-day-delivery-radius is constrained to <5 miles from existing DC (the canonical "we cannot economically fulfill same-day orders from our main DC" capacity ceiling), OR the operator cannot answer "what is our per-warehouse-robotics-roi-payback-period + per-warehouse-pick-rate-per-hour + per-warehouse-perfect-order-rate + per-warehouse-OTIF-rate + per-warehouse-accuracy-rate + per-warehouse-footprint-utilization + per-warehouse-piece-handling-cost + per-warehouse-labor-cost-per-order + per-warehouse-throughput-per-square-foot" within 60 seconds.

Do **not** use it when:

- The store is doing **<$2M GMV OR <1 warehouse/3PL**. Defer Move #111, ship Move #22 + Move #27 + Move #45 first, and revisit when volume + node count justify the capex + operational complexity.
- The brand is **single-node single-channel with <100k orders/year**. Warehouse-robotics capex is wasted when there is only one small DC and one channel; ship Move #22 + #45 + #71 + #110 first, defer Move #111 until the operator adds a second node OR cross 100k orders/year.
- The brand has **no per-SKU demand data with at least 90 days of history**. The robotics engine's per-warehouse-robotics-task-allocation math needs per-SKU-velocity-channel-LTV-overlays; without them, the engine produces "we have no idea which SKU deserves robotics priority" output and the operator is back to manual pick-pack math.
- The brand has **no per-warehouse-robotics-task-decision-event-stream**. Without per-decision-event-stream, the robotics engine cannot audit per-robotics-task-outcome + per-robotics-task-rollback-window and defaults to "we have no idea whether the robotics fleet is actually saving labor cost" which systematically under-counts the robotics-fleet ROI.
- The brand has **no per-warehouse-robotics-MTBF-MTTR-monitoring-substrate**. Without per-robotics-MTBF-MTTR-monitoring, the robotics engine cannot detect per-robotics-fleet-degradation + per-robotics-RMA-rate and defaults to "we have no idea whether the robotics fleet is still operational at full capacity" which systematically over-estimates the robotics-fleet ROI.
- The brand has **no per-channel-cohort-LTV data** (Move #103 product-analytics + Triple Whale + Polar + Northbeam + Daasity + Glew). Without per-channel-cohort-LTV, the robotics engine cannot rank warehouses-by-robotics-roi-payback-period and defaults to "first-come-first-served by per-warehouse-pick-rate" which systematically over-deploys robotics to high-velocity low-LTV channels (Amazon + Walmart) and under-deploys robotics to lower-velocity high-LTV channels (DTC + subscription).
- The brand is in a **regulated vertical** (supplements, CBD, beauty with lot/date tracking, food, hazmat) where lot-tracking-and-expiry-allocation matters more than per-warehouse-robotics-pick-rate. Most WMS support lot-tracking via Cin7 + NetSuite, but the operator must configure them BEFORE the robotics engine goes live; otherwise the robotics pick-task will skip lot-tracked SKUs and create audit violations.
- The brand has **no warehouse-robotics-task decision authority** (e.g. fully-outsourced-3PL where 3PL owns the DC). Warehouse-robotics-engine helps the operator forecast demand and pre-position inventory, but the 3PL ultimately decides what gets picked and packed; the engine can compute the per-robotics-task-priority but cannot enforce it.
