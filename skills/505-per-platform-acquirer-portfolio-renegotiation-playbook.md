## The build (time estimate: 7-9 days for the full 8-component per-platform acquirer portfolio renegotiation playbook layer)

**Phase 1: Per-acquirer contract review + benchmark pull (Day 0-7; 4-6 hours per per-acquirer contract × 5 acquirers = 20-30 hours total).** Phase 1 pulls the per-acquirer current contract + the per-acquirer last 12 months of invoices + the per-acquirer pass-through benchmark from Move #167.5.9 (Component 7) + the per-acquirer reserve data from Move #167.5.8 + the per-acquirer MATCH data from Move #167.5.7. The deliverable is the per-acquirer contract + invoice data + benchmark data, all formatted for the per-acquirer brief.

**Phase 2: Per-acquirer brief assembly (Day 7-14; 3-4 hours per per-acquirer brief × 5 acquirers = 15-20 hours total).** Phase 2 assembles the per-acquirer brief (Component 1) for each of the 5 acquirers. The per-acquirer brief is a 1-pager that documents the per-acquirer current terms + the per-acquirer current total-fee + the per-acquirer benchmark + the per-acquirer ask. The deliverable is 5 per-acquirer briefs (1 per acquirer).

**Phase 3: Per-acquirer leverage matrix (Day 14-21; 2-3 hours per per-acquirer × 5 acquirers = 10-15 hours total).** Phase 3 builds the per-acquirer leverage matrix (Component 2) for each of the 5 acquirers. The matrix scores the operator's leverage across 5-7 dimensions using Move #167.5.10 + #167.5.8 + #167.5.7 + #167.5.9 + #167.5.1 data. The deliverable is 5 per-acquirer leverage matrices.

**Phase 4: Per-acquirer ask ladder (Day 21-28; 3-4 hours per per-acquirer × 5 acquirers = 15-20 hours total).** Phase 4 builds the per-acquirer ask ladder (Component 3) for each of the 5 acquirers. The ladder has 5-7 ranked per-acquirer asks with target / walk-away / acceptable values. The deliverable is 5 per-acquirer ask ladders.

**Phase 5: Per-acquirer letter library (Day 28-35; 6-8 hours to draft 7 letter templates).** Phase 5 drafts the per-acquirer letter library (Component 4): 7 letter templates (A-G) for the 7 most common per-acquirer renegotiation scenarios. Each letter is 1-7 pages and follows the canonical 7-paragraph structure. The deliverable is 7 per-acquirer letter templates.

**Phase 6: Per-acquirer counter-proposal + BAFO playbooks (Day 35-42; 4-6 hours to document the 5-7 step workflows).** Phase 6 documents the per-acquirer counter-proposal playbook (Component 5: 7 steps) and the per-acquirer BAFO playbook (Component 6: 7 steps). The playbooks are 2-3 page workflow documents that the operator's CFO + Head of Payments follow when the per-acquirer responds. The deliverable is 2 per-acquirer workflow documents.

**Phase 7: Per-acquirer renegotiation ROI calculator (Day 42-49; 6-8 hours to build the 5-acquirer × 6-col calculator).** Phase 7 builds the per-acquirer renegotiation ROI calculator (Component 7). The calculator is a 5-acquirer × 6-col spreadsheet that computes the per-acquirer Year-1 savings + 3-year NPV + break-even point for each of the 5 acquirers. The deliverable is 1 per-acquirer ROI calculator (spreadsheet + PDF).

**Phase 8: Per-acquirer renegotiation SOP (Day 49-56; 4-6 hours to document the 9-step × 120-150 day workflow).** Phase 8 documents the per-acquirer renegotiation SOP (Component 8). The SOP is a 9-step × 30-60-90 day workflow that orchestrates Components 1-7 into a per-acquirer total-fee reduction. The deliverable is 1 per-acquirer SOP document.

**Phase 9: Per-acquirer letter send + counter-proposal + BAFO (Day 56-150; per-acquirer renegotiation cycle in production).** Phase 9 is the EXECUTION of the per-acquirer renegotiation: send Letter B to each of the 5 acquirers, handle the per-acquirer counter-proposals, handle the per-acquirer BAFOs, and close the per-acquirer amended contracts. The deliverable is 5 per-acquirer amended contracts + the per-acquirer Year-1 savings tracked against the Component 7 ROI calculator.

**Total build time: 56-66 hours of focused work (7-9 days at 8 hours/day) for the full 8-component per-acquirer renegotiation playbook layer.** The per-acquirer renegotiation cycle (Phase 9) takes an additional 60-90 days to complete in production.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Treating the per-acquirer pass-through letter (Move #167.5.9) as a "complete" per-acquirer renegotiation.** The Move #167.5.9 letter is a 1-page single-issue letter that addresses only the per-acquirer pass-through MULTIPLIER (1 of 15-25 per-acquirer fee line items). The per-acquirer total-fee spend includes the per-acquirer processing fee + the per-acquirer transaction fee + the per-acquirer monthly minimum + the per-acquirer PCI DSS fee + the per-acquirer chargeback handling fee + the per-acquirer early termination fee + the per-acquirer rate-ratchet clause + the per-acquirer rate-notice period + the per-acquirer auto-renew clause + the per-acquirer interchange-plus-pricing model + the per-acquirer AVS surcharge + the per-acquirer batch fee + the per-acquirer voice auth fee + the per-acquirer retrieval request fee + the per-acquirer ACH return fee + the per-acquirer data-breach liability cap + the per-acquirer network-program-update pass-through clause — that's 15-25 per-acquirer fee line items per per-acquirer contract × 5 acquirers = 75-125 total per-acquirer fee line items. The Move #167.5.9 letter addresses 1 of 75-125. The 5-15% per-acquirer total-fee reduction opportunity is on the OTHER 74-124 line items. **Fix: Always run the full Move #167.5.11 per-acquirer renegotiation playbook (Letter B: comprehensive 7-page letter covering 15-25 line items) in the per-acquirer renewal cycle, not just the Move #167.5.9 per-acquirer pass-through letter.**

2. **Not documenting the per-acquirer current terms before sending Letter B.** The operator's per-acquirer contract is 30-80 pages of legalese with 15-25 per-acquirer fee line items scattered across the per-acquirer fee schedule + the per-acquirer terms of service + the per-acquirer data security addendum + the per-acquirer GDPR/DPA addendum. Without the per-acquirer brief (Component 1: the 1-pager that extracts the per-acquirer current terms), the operator cannot identify the per-acquirer walk-away value + the per-acquirer target value + the per-acquirer acceptable value for each of the 5-7 per-acquirer ask-ladder items. The result: Letter B is sent with vague asks (e.g., "we want a lower per-acquirer processing fee") instead of specific asks (e.g., "we want per-acquirer processing fee reduced from $0.18/txn to $0.10/txn, with a per-acquirer walk-away of $0.15/txn"). **Fix: Always assemble the per-acquirer brief (Component 1) BEFORE drafting Letter B. The per-acquirer brief is the INPUT to Letter B.**

3. **Not using the per-acquirer migration threat (Move #167.5.10) as leverage in the per-acquirer renegotiation.** The per-acquirer migration orchestrator (Move #167.5.10) is the operator's HIGHEST-LEVERAGE per-acquirer renegotiation tool — the operator who can credibly threaten to migrate within 60-180 days gets a 20-40% per-acquirer total-fee reduction, vs 5-10% for the operator without a per-acquirer migration option. Many operators do NOT use the per-acquirer migration threat in Letter B because they're afraid of damaging the per-acquirer relationship. The per-acquirer relationship is preserved by the per-acquirer BAFO outcome (the acquirer will either match the per-acquirer walk-away value or accept the per-acquirer migration). **Fix: Always include the per-acquirer migration threat in Letter B (Letter C variant: renewal-vs-migration) — the per-acquirer migration threat converts the per-acquirer total-fee reduction from 5-10% to 20-40% per Acquirer Counter-Proposal 2025 + Acquirer BAFO 2025.**

4. **Sending Letter B at the wrong time in the per-acquirer renewal cycle.** Letter B is most effective when sent 90-120 days before the per-acquirer auto-renew date. If sent too early (>180 days), the per-acquirer treats it as a "wish list" and the per-acquirer counter-proposal arrives 60-90 days later with a low acceptance rate. If sent too late (<60 days), the per-acquirer treats it as a "take it or leave it" and the per-acquirer counter-proposal is at the acquirer's BAFO value, not the per-acquirer walk-away value. **Fix: Send Letter B 90-120 days before the per-acquirer auto-renew date. If the per-acquirer auto-renew is >180 days away, send a "preliminary inquiry" letter that opens the per-acquirer renegotiation conversation without triggering the per-acquirer formal BAFO cycle.**

5. **Treating the per-acquirer counter-proposal as the per-acquirer final offer.** The per-acquirer counter-proposal is the acquirer's FIRST response to Letter B, not the per-acquirer final offer. The per-acquirer counter-proposal is typically 50-70% of the per-acquirer ask-ladder target values (the acquirer gives some, but not all). The per-acquirer counter-counter-proposal is where the operator extracts the remaining 20-30%. Many operators accept the per-acquirer counter-proposal as-is and miss 30-50% of the per-acquirer total-fee reduction. **Fix: Always run the per-acquirer counter-proposal playbook (Component 5: 7 steps) — diff the per-acquirer counter-proposal vs the per-acquirer ask ladder, identify the 1-3 per-acquirer counter-proposal gaps, draft the per-acquirer counter-counter-proposal, and schedule the per-acquirer counter-counter-proposal call. The per-acquirer counter-counter-proposal cycle extracts the 20-30% that the per-acquirer counter-proposal leaves on the table.**

6. **Not having a per-acquirer BAFO decision matrix BEFORE the per-acquirer BAFO arrives.** The per-acquirer BAFO arrives in a 30-60 minute call + a follow-up email, and the operator must decide accept / reject / counter-BAFO within 24-48 hours. Without a pre-built per-acquirer BAFO decision matrix (Component 6), the operator makes a per-acquirer BAFO decision on emotion, not data, and either (a) accepts a per-acquirer BAFO that's below the per-acquirer walk-away value, or (b) rejects a per-acquirer BAFO that's at or above the per-acquirer walk-away value because the operator doesn't have the per-acquirer walk-away value documented. **Fix: Always pre-build the per-acquirer BAFO decision matrix (Component 6) using the per-acquirer ask ladder's walk-away values + the per-acquirer ROI calculator's break-even point + the per-acquirer migration feasibility from Move #167.5.10. The per-acquirer BAFO decision matrix is the OPERATOR's pre-commitment that the per-acquirer BAFO decision is data-driven, not emotion-driven.**

7. **Not documenting the per-acquirer Year-1 savings + 3-year NPV before sending Letter B.** The per-acquirer renegotiation ROI calculator (Component 7) is the SINGLE source of truth for the per-acquirer total-fee reduction opportunity. Without the per-acquirer ROI calculator, the operator cannot justify the per-acquirer renegotiation cost ($5K-$25K in legal + management time) to the operator's CFO + General Counsel + Head of Payments, and the per-acquirer renegotiation is rejected as "not worth the per-acquirer relationship risk". **Fix: Always build the per-acquirer ROI calculator (Component 7) BEFORE sending Letter B. The per-acquirer ROI calculator is the BUSINESS CASE for the per-acquirer renegotiation — it shows the per-acquirer Year-1 savings ($25K-$500K) + the per-acquirer 3-year NPV ($75K-$1.5M) + the per-acquirer break-even point (1-3 months) that justifies the per-acquirer renegotiation cost.**

8. **Not running the per-acquirer renegotiation SOP on a per-acquirer-per-renewal-cycle cadence.** The per-acquirer renegotiation is NOT a one-time event — it's a per-acquirer-per-renewal-cycle event (every 12-36 months). The operator who runs the per-acquirer renegotiation once and then forgets about it for 3 years misses 2-3 per-acquirer renegotiation cycles and loses $50K-$1.5M of cumulative per-acquirer total-fee reduction. **Fix: Set a per-acquirer renewal-calendar reminder 120-180 days before each per-acquirer auto-renew date. The per-acquirer renewal calendar triggers Phase 1 of the per-acquirer renegotiation SOP (Component 8) automatically.**

9. **Treating the per-acquirer early termination fee as a hard cost.** The per-acquirer early termination fee is a NEGOTIABLE term, not a hard cost. The operator who treats the per-acquirer early termination fee as a hard cost and refuses to terminate a per-acquirer contract that's below the per-acquirer walk-away value loses $25K-$500K/yr of per-acquirer total-fee reduction. **Fix: The per-acquirer ask ladder includes the per-acquirer early termination fee as Ask #5 (target: $0 / walk-away: 90-day notice / acceptable: $250). The per-acquirer early termination fee is included in Letter B alongside the other 4-6 per-acquirer asks, and the per-acquirer counter-proposal typically includes a per-acquirer early termination fee reduction from $500 to $250 (or 90-day notice instead of a fixed fee) — a 50-100% per-acquirer early termination fee reduction that the operator would have missed by treating it as hard.**

10. **Not separating the per-acquirer PCI DSS non-compliance fee from the per-acquirer PCI DSS fee.** The per-acquirer PCI DSS fee is a monthly fee the acquirer charges for the per-acquirer PCI DSS compliance service (typically $5-$50/month). The per-acquirer PCI DSS non-compliance fee is a separate fee the acquirer charges when the operator FAILS to provide the per-acquirer PCI DSS compliance attestation (typically $50-$500/month). The operator who addresses the per-acquirer PCI DSS fee in Letter B but misses the per-acquirer PCI DSS non-compliance fee loses $600-$6,000/yr in avoidable per-acquirer cost. **Fix: List the per-acquirer PCI DSS fee AND the per-acquirer PCI DSS non-compliance fee as separate per-acquirer ask-ladder items in the per-acquirer brief. The per-acquirer PCI DSS non-compliance fee is typically waiver-able (target: $0 / walk-away: 50% reduction / acceptable: 25% reduction) if the operator provides the per-acquirer PCI DSS attestation within 30 days.**

11. **Not having a per-acquirer rate-ratchet reversal clause in the per-acquirer amended contract.** The per-acquirer rate-ratchet clause ('rates may change with 30 days notice') is a per-acquirer poison-pill clause that allows the acquirer to increase per-acquirer rates every 6-12 months without the operator's consent. The operator who doesn't reverse the per-acquirer rate-ratchet in Letter B is exposed to $5K-$20K/yr in per-acquirer rate increases that the operator cannot prevent. **Fix: Include the per-acquirer rate-ratchet reversal as Ask #6 in the per-acquirer ask ladder. The target: 90-day rate-notice + 1.0× pass-through cap for 24 months. The walk-away: 60-day rate-notice. The acceptable: 60-day rate-notice. The per-acquirer rate-ratchet reversal is typically 60-80% accepted in the per-acquirer counter-proposal cycle.**

12. **Not having a per-acquirer auto-renew opt-out clause in the per-acquirer amended contract.** The per-acquirer auto-renew clause ('auto-renews unless 30-day notice to terminate') is a per-acquirer poison-pill clause that locks the operator into a 12-36 month per-acquirer contract unless the operator serves a per-acquirer termination notice within the per-acquirer auto-renew opt-out window. The operator who misses the per-acquirer auto-renew opt-out window is locked into another 12-36 months of per-acquirer unfavorable terms. **Fix: Include the per-acquirer auto-renew opt-out as Ask #7 in the per-acquirer ask ladder. The target: 60-day opt-out. The walk-away: 30-day opt-out (status quo). The acceptable: 30-day opt-out. Set a per-acquirer renewal-calendar reminder 90-120 days before each per-acquirer auto-renew date to avoid missing the per-acquirer auto-renew opt-out window.**

13. **Not using the per-acquirer reserve risk (Move #167.5.8) as leverage in the per-acquirer renegotiation.** The per-acquirer reserve ($20K-$100K held by the acquirer) is the operator's working capital that the acquirer holds for 6-18 months. The operator who can credibly threaten to migrate the per-acquirer reserve OUT of the acquirer (by switching to a new acquirer via Move #167.5.10) gets a 10-20% per-acquirer total-fee reduction as the acquirer tries to retain the per-acquirer reserve balance. Many operators do NOT use the per-acquirer reserve risk in Letter B because they don't think the acquirer cares about losing the per-acquirer reserve. **Fix: Include the per-acquirer reserve release (Move #167.5.8) as a per-acquirer leverage point in Component 2's per-acquirer leverage matrix. The per-acquirer reserve is $20K-$100K of working capital the acquirer holds interest-free; the per-acquirer reserve release threat is a high-leverage per-acquirer renegotiation tool.**

14. **Not including the per-acquirer MATCH-list risk (Move #167.5.7) as leverage in the per-acquirer renegotiation.** The per-acquirer MATCH-list risk is the acquirer's WORST-CASE outcome — a 5-year industry shutout that excludes the operator from ALL acquirers in the network. The operator who can credibly threaten to migrate BEFORE the per-acquirer MATCH-list risk materializes (by switching to a new acquirer via Move #167.5.10 BEFORE the per-acquirer ratio crosses the per-acquirer MATCH threshold) gets a 5-10% per-acquirer total-fee reduction as the acquirer tries to retain the operator. **Fix: Include the per-acquirer MATCH-list risk (Move #167.5.7) as a per-acquirer leverage point in Component 2's per-acquirer leverage matrix. The per-acquirer MATCH-list risk is the operator's "atomic option" — the acquirer knows that if the per-acquirer MATCH-list fires, the operator will leave AND the acquirer loses the per-acquirer reserve + the per-acquirer portfolio relationship.**

15. **Not tracking the per-acquirer Year-1 savings + 3-year NPV AFTER the per-acquirer renegotiation closes.** The per-acquirer renegotiation ROI calculator (Component 7) is the PLAN. The per-acquirer post-renegotiation monitoring is the TRACKING. Many operators run the per-acquirer renegotiation cycle, sign the per-acquirer amended contract, and never check whether the per-acquirer actually delivered on the per-acquirer amended terms. The result: the per-acquirer quietly reverses 1-2 per-acquirer amended terms in the per-acquirer first invoice (e.g., the per-acquirer processing fee is $0.13/txn instead of the $0.10/txn the operator negotiated), and the operator doesn't catch the per-acquirer reversal for 6-12 months. **Fix: Implement a per-acquirer post-renegotiation monitoring workflow: pull the per-acquirer first invoice under the per-acquirer amended terms, diff vs the per-acquirer amended terms, identify any per-acquirer reversals, and trigger a per-acquirer follow-up letter if the per-acquirer reversal is >5% of the per-acquirer amended value. The per-acquirer post-renegotiation monitoring is the LAST step of the per-acquirer renegotiation SOP (Component 8) and is the difference between a per-acquirer renegotiation that "looks good on paper" and a per-acquirer renegotiation that actually delivers the per-acquirer Year-1 savings + 3-year NPV.**
