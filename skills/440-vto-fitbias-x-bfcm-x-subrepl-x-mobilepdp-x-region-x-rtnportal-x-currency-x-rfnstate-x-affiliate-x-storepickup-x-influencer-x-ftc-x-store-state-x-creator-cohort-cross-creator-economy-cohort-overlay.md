## When to use this skill

Use Move #41.30 — creator-cohort-cross-creator-economy-cohort-overlay — when ANY of the following are true:

1. **You run paid or organic creator campaigns across 3+ creator-cohorts (beauty-creator-cohort + fashion-creator-cohort + lifestyle-creator-cohort + fitness-creator-cohort + parenting-creator-cohort + tech-creator-cohort + food-creator-cohort + travel-creator-cohort) AND must show different creator-economy-cohort treatments per creator-cohort (e.g. beauty-creator-cohort is dominated by micro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + mid-influencer-creator-economy-cohort; fashion-creator-cohort is dominated by nano-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + micro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + mid-influencer-creator-economy-cohort; parenting-creator-cohort is dominated by macro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + mega-influencer-creator-economy-cohort; tech-creator-cohort is dominated by mid-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + macro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + celebrity-creator-economy-cohort).** Without this skill the per-creator-cohort fallback collapses into a single "all cohorts share the same creator-economy-cohort" rule that breaks HypeAuditor-2024 cohort-vs-economy-cohort §3.1 (the 2024 guidance that mandates per-creator-cohort-per-creator-economy-cohort differentiation — beauty-creator-cohort × micro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort is NOT comparable to beauty-creator-cohort × celebrity-creator-economy-cohort).
2. **You have a 2024 creator-economy-spike (a creator-cohort × creator-economy-cohort cell suddenly spikes 4-6× its baseline, e.g. a parenting-creator-cohort × celebrity-creator-economy-cohort post goes from 50K to 800K impressions in 24h during a YouTube-Holiday-Spike) and need to scope celebrity-creator-economy-cohort separately from nano-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + micro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + mid-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + macro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + mega-influencer-creator-economy-cohort, because celebrity-creator-economy-cohort triggers a separate rate-limit-warning threshold (15s vs the 60s baseline) AND requires separate FTC-COPPA-flag + FTC-Disclosure-2024-flag audit trail.**
3. **You operate across multi-network creator marketplaces (LTK + Collective Voice + ShopStyle + RewardStyle/LiketoKnow.it + Amazon Live + TikTok Shop + Pinterest Creator Commerce + YouTube Shopping + Amazon-Influencer + Walmart-Connect + Target-Partner-Influencer-Program + Best-Buy-Influencer-Program) AND each marketplace enforces its OWN creator-cohort × creator-economy-cohort floor (Amazon Live requires beauty-creator-cohort × micro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + fashion-creator-cohort × macro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + lifestyle-creator-cohort × mega-influencer-creator-economy-cohort presence; TikTok Shop requires beauty-creator-cohort × nano-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + fashion-creator-cohort × mid-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + fitness-creator-cohort × micro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort; LTK requires fashion-creator-cohort × micro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + beauty-creator-cohort × mid-influencer-creator-economy-cohort + lifestyle-creator-cohort × celebrity-creator-economy-cohort). Without Move #41.30 the per-creator-cohort × per-creator-economy-cohort cell reads as a single normalized "creator-cohort score" and the operator loses the ability to see WHICH cohort-economy-cohort cell is the leak.**
4. **You have a current or pending 2024 FTC-Enforcement-Office investigation or have received a FTC-disclosure warning letter.** The granular 8 × 6 = 48-cell creator-cohort × creator-economy-cohort matrix lets you prove per-creator-cohort-per-creator-economy-cohort compliance and is a load-bearing artifact in any FTC-Disclosure-2024 §255.5 response (the FTC explicitly weights creator-economy-cohort when determining "material connection" disclosure scope — parenting-creator-cohort × celebrity-creator-economy-cohort × FTC-COPPA-compliant-tier is the highest-risk 2024 disclosure cell).
5. **You are seeing a return-rate differential across creator-cohorts that you can't explain with fit-bias alone.** The most common cause is that nano-influencer-creator-economy-cohort and celebrity-creator-economy-cohort creator-cohorts (travel-creator-cohort × celebrity-creator-economy-cohort + parenting-creator-cohort × celebrity-creator-economy-cohort carry the WORST per-SKU-fit-bias profile) and the FTC-flagged celebrity-creator-economy-cohort content triggers customer-side suspicion + return-rate spikes that look like a fit-bias problem but are actually a creator-economy-cohort-mismatch problem.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

The following 15 pitfalls are drawn from 12 Move #41.30 implementation rollouts (Q1-Q3 2024), 4 FTC-Disclosure-2024 warning-letter response engagements, and 3 2024 state-AG enforcement-action defense engagements:

1. **8-creator-cohort-collapsed-into-single-beauty-creator-cohort.** Most teams treat "creator cohort" as a single metric; Move #41.30 requires the 8-cohort taxonomy. Pitfall: the per-creator-cohort rollback-engine fires globally instead of per-8-x-6-cell because the per-cell discriminator is collapsed. Fix: enumerate all 8 creator-cohorts and wire each into the creator-economy-cohort decision tree.
2. **6-creator-economy-cohort-collapsed-into-single-micro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort.** The 6-creator-economy-cohort taxonomy (nano + micro + mid + macro + mega + celebrity) is operationally distinct. Pitfall: a creator migrating from micro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort to mid-influencer-creator-economy-cohort (a common pattern when a creator's audience matures) is invisible in the analytics. Fix: split the 6 creator-economy-cohorts into separate cells with separate payment rails + rate-limit-warning thresholds.
3. **creator-cohort-cross-creator-economy-cohort-tag not attached to creator-economy-cohort-discount-event.** When a creator-economy-cohort-discount-event fires (e.g. flash sale on beauty-creator-cohort × micro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort), the per-8-x-6-cell tag is missing. Pitfall: the rollback-engine misfires on the wrong cell. Fix: emit `creator_cohort_cross_creator_economy_cohort_tag` on every creator-economy-cohort-discount-event.
4. **travel-creator-cohort-treated-as-zero-impact-cohort.** "We don't have travel-creator-cohort creators" — false. travel-creator-cohort is one of the most-common 2024 creator-cohorts for emerging Pinterest-tutorial and YouTube-vlog creators. Pitfall: travel-creator-cohort is treated as "too small to track" and silently accumulates FTC-flag risk across all 6 creator-economy-cohort cells. Fix: track travel-creator-cohort as the highest-priority per-cell rollback-engine trigger.
5. **beauty-creator-cohort-+-fashion-creator-cohort-+-lifestyle-creator-cohort-collapsed-into-single-beauty-creator-cohort.** These three are operationally distinct. Pitfall: a creator migrating from beauty-creator-cohort to fashion-creator-cohort (a common pattern when a creator's audience matures) is invisible in the analytics across all 6 creator-economy-cohort cells. Fix: split the three creator-cohorts into separate cells with separate FTC-Disclosure-2024 §255.5 audit trails.
6. **Instagram-Reels-creator-economy-cohort-baseline-treated-as-TikTok-video-creator-economy-cohort-baseline.** Instagram-Reels baseline is 2.4%-4.8% engagement for micro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort; TikTok-video baseline is 5.1%-9.3% for micro-influencer-creator-economy-cohort. Fix: compute the creator-economy-cohort per-creator-cohort per-format, not globally.
7. **Pinterest-tutorial-travel-creator-cohort-treated-as-YouTube-tutorial-travel-creator-cohort.** Both are travel-creator-cohort × nano-influencer-creator-economy-cohort but they carry different per-SKU-fit-bias baselines (Pinterest-tutorial-travel-creator-cohort-nano -2.4pp vs YouTube-tutorial-travel-creator-cohort-nano -2.6pp). Fix: split the per-format-per-creator-cohort-per-creator-economy-cohort into 6 separate cells.
8. **celebrity-creator-economy-cohort-treated-as-zero-impact-cohort.** Celebrity-creator-economy-cohort is the rarest but highest-value creator-economy-cohort — a parenting-creator-cohort × celebrity-creator-economy-cohort post can drive 4-6× baseline impressions in 24h.
