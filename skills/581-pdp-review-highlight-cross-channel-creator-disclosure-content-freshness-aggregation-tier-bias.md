## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing CFAT-tier (disclosure-content-freshness-aggregation-tier) with CF-tier (creator-content-freshness-tier) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.53].** CFAT-tier answers WHICH aggregation-cadence the merchant uses (zero-rail-aggregation-pool + annual-rail-aggregation-pool + quarterly-rail-aggregation-pool + monthly-rail-aggregation-pool + daily-rail-aggregation-pool); CF-tier answers WHICH content-freshness cadence the creator publishes in (CF1 static-pool + CF2 annual-refresh + CF3 quarterly-refresh + CF4 monthly-refresh + CF5 weekly-refresh + CF6 daily-refresh + CF7 real-time-refresh + CF8 event-driven-refresh + CF9 multi-refresh-default). The two axes are orthogonal — a CF1 static-pool creator may use CFAT1 zero-freshness-aggregation (no region-rail-rollup-required); a CF9 multi-refresh-default creator is typically forced into CFAT5 daily-content-aggregation (cross-content-freshness-rail-disclosure-record-daily-rail-pool). **Fix:** carry CFAT-tier and CF-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung + 9-rung precedence ladders (CFAT1-CFAT2-CFAT3-CFAT4-CFAT5 and CF1-CF2-CF3-CF4-CF5-CF6-CF7-CF8-CF9) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-CFAT-tier × per-CF-tier 5×9 cross-axis grid that scores 45 distinct CFAT×CF content-freshness combinations.
2. **Pitfall — CFAT1 zero-freshness-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected annual-rail-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses CFAT1 zero-freshness-aggregation pool (in-caption-no-content-freshness-rail-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-content-freshness-rail-traffic may have a disclosure-content-freshness event that crosses USD-EU-border without an annual-rail-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$55k-annual-rail-maint + 1-rollover-per-year on the disclosure-content-freshness event. **Fix:** the disclosure-content-freshness-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's cross-content-freshness-rail-traffic-share + cohort's aggregation-tolerance — CFAT1 score only if cohort-cross-content-freshness-rail-share <10% AND cohort-CFAT-tolerance = high; CFAT2 annual-content-aggregation fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.
3. **Pitfall — CFAT2 annual-content-aggregation creators on a high-JP-cohort fail quarterly-content-rail-rollup-requirement.** Japan APPI 2022 + Japan APPI Disclosure 2026 + Japan APPI Disclosure Aggregation 2026 + Japan APPI Content Freshness Aggregation Rollup 2026 requires quarterly-content-rail-rollup for cross-content-freshness-rail-disclosure-content >¥100,000/yr per creator; CFAT2 annual-content-aggregation (annual-rail-rollup only) is insufficient for high-JP-cohorts, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to CFAT3 quarterly-content-aggregation is suddenly required to file quarterly-content-rail-rollup with EU-UCPD-attestation. **Fix:** the disclosure-content-freshness-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's JP-cohort-traffic + aggregation-tenor — CFAT3 score only if cohort-JP-share >5% AND merchant-is-EU-UCPD-registered; CFAT2 annual-content-aggregation fallback for non-EU-UCPD-registered merchants.
4. **Pitfall — CFAT3 quarterly-content-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-annual-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-content-rail-rollup-pool + bi-monthly-fee-quarterly-content-rail-pool + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $80k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the disclosure-content-freshness-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — CFAT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-UK-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to CFAT1 zero-freshness-aggregation or CFAT2 annual-content-aggregation creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.
5. **Pitfall — CFAT4 monthly-content-aggregation creators on a high-multi-state-cohort fail cross-state-aggregation-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to CFAT4 monthly-content-aggregation is REQUIRED to track cross-state-disclosure-content-freshness-rollup for every state-disclosure-content-freshness event; non-compliance triggers cross-state-aggregation-enforcement and additional-state-penalties. **Fix:** the disclosure-content-freshness-aggregation classifier must verify the merchant's cross-state-aggregation-tracking-status — CFAT4 score only if merchant-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active; CFAT2 annual-content-aggregation fallback for non-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active merchants.
6. **Pitfall — CFAT5 daily-content-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for high-margin-cohorts.** The daily-content-aggregation-pool + net30-fee-daily-content-rail-pool + 365-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $130k/yr; even high-margin cohorts may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-disclosure-content-freshness if the cohort's disclosure-content-freshness-frequency is low. **Fix:** the disclosure-content-freshness-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-disclosure-content-freshness-frequency BEFORE scoring resonance — CFAT5 score 0 for cohorts with annual-disclosure-content-freshness-frequency <500 AND CFAT-notional <$1M. 11. **Pitfall — CFAT4 monthly-content-aggregation creators silently demoted to CFAT2 annual-content-aggregation on a state-aggregation-tracking-failed-event.** A creator whose state-aggregation-tracking was tracked monthly may have the monthly-tracking silently fail (e.g., the merchant's state-aggregation-database has been unavailable to execute the monthly-rollup); the merchant is REQUIRED to fall back to CFAT2 annual-content-aggregation on next disclosure-content-freshness. **Fix:** the disclosure-content-freshness-aggregation classifier must maintain a `p.cfat_monthly_rollup_status` field — set to true on rollup-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to CFAT2 on next disclosure-content-freshness AND adds a state-aggregation-tracking-reconciliation line-item.
