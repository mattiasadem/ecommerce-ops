## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds, each with corrective **Fix:**)

1. **Skip the bot-vs-human-disclosure-script + AB-3030 + EU-AI-Act-Article-50 compliance** — operators often assume the AI-agent is "obviously" a bot to the customer and skip the disclosure-policy. In California (AB-3030), EU (EU-AI-Act-Article-50), and under FTC AI Marketing Disclosure 2026 guidance, this is a per-message disclosure obligation with specific cadence + script + language requirements. A brand that skips this faces regulatory-fines ($2.5k-$50k per-violation in California) + reputational-damage + customer-trust-decay. **Fix:** Implement the disclosure-script on EVERY AI-enabled channel from Day 1–2 of Phase 1. Use a canonical disclosure-script per-channel: "I'm [Bot Name], an AI assistant here to help. I'll do my best, but I may make mistakes — a human can take over any time." For high-stakes (refund-issued / policy-violation / personal-data), use the per-stakes disclosure-depth (every-message + human-handoff-option). Validate with the AB-3030 + EU-AI-Act-Article-50 + FTC-AI-marketing-disclosure compliance checklist.

2. **No per-customer-AI-trust-score-decay-engine** — operators often measure per-vendor metrics (per-vendor hallucination-rate, per-vendor deflection-rate, per-vendor brand-voice-drift-rate) but skip the per-customer metric that actually drives LTV-protection. Without the per-customer-AI-trust-score, the operator cannot target recovery-gestures to the highest-risk-customers (high-LTV-VIP-who-just-had-a-bad-AI-interaction) and over-spends on lowest-risk-customers (low-LTV-first-time-buyer-who-is-fine-with-a-coupon). **Fix:** Build the per-customer-AI-trust-score-decay-engine on Day 5–7 of Phase 1. Persist the trust-score to the CDP (Move #100 CDP-identity-resolution). Build the per-cohort-AI-trust-score-cohort-LTV-overlay (Triple Whale cohort-LTV × per-customer-AI-trust-score-band). The high-AI-trust-score-cohort should have +5 to +25% LTV-uplift vs low-AI-trust-score-cohort.

3. **No AI-agent-wrong-answer-recall-system** — operators often discover an AI-hallucination-rate-exposed-to-customer event 1–7 days AFTER it happens (via social-media-monitoring or a customer-complaint). By then, 100–10,000 customers have already seen the bad AI response and the brand has a #YourBrandSucks incident on Twitter. The recall-window has closed. **Fix:** Build the AI-agent-wrong-answer-recall-system on Day 3–4 of Phase 1. Wire the recall-pipeline to per-AI-vendor-observability-pipeline (from Move #181). Recall-time must be <24 hours from incident-detection to affected-customer-notification. Use the per-incident-class recall-templates (6 classes: refund-issued-by-AI-hallucination + policy-violation-AI + wrong-info-AI + tone-misfire-AI + channel-mismatch-AI + promise-overshoot-AI + personal-data-exfil-AI).

4. **Single recovery-gesture for all customers** — operators often default to "10% off next order" as the universal recovery-gesture. This over-spends on low-LTV-customers and under-spends on high-LTV-VIPs. A $2k-LTV-VIP-who-just-had-a-bad-AI-interaction needs a $100-store-credit + phone-call-from-CSO + loyalty-points-boost to recover; a $50-LTV-first-time-buyer is fine with a 10%-off-coupon. **Fix:** Build the per-customer-AI-recovery-playbook with 24 variants (6 incident-classes × 4 customer-tiers) on Day 8–10 of Phase 2. Define per-variant recovery-gesture × tone × delivery-channel × escalation-routing × recovery-budget-cap. Wire to per-customer-tier from Move #100 CDP-identity-resolution.

5. **No per-customer-recovery-budget-cap** — operators often issue recovery-gestures without budget-caps, leading to $50k+/mo over-spend during a high-AI-incident month. A single bad AI-incident can trigger 1,000 recovery-gestures × $100 average = $100k. **Fix:** Define the per-customer-recovery-budget-cap on Day 11–12 of Phase 2. Per-customer-cap: $5 first-time-buyer → $25 repeat-buyer → $100 VIP → $500 CXO-escalation. Per-month-cap: $5k/mo default. Per-quarter-cap: $15k/quarter default. Wire the budget-cap to the recovery-gesture-delivery engine (auto-block if over-budget, escalate to human).

6. **No post-incident-RCA-canonical-pattern** — operators often firefight the AI-incident (recovery-gesture sent, customer happy) but skip the post-incident-root-cause-analysis. Three weeks later, the same AI-incident-class happens again (refund-issued-by-AI-hallucination), the operator firefights again, and the cycle repeats forever. **Fix:** Build the post-incident-RCA-canonical-pattern on Day 15–17 of Phase 3. Use the 5-why root-cause-analysis template (per-AI-vendor attribution + per-prompt-pattern root-cause + per-guardrail-rotation-action-item + cross-vendor-rotation-cost). RCA-completion-rate target: 100% within 7 days. Wire the RCA-action-items to per-AI-vendor-eval-pipeline (from Move #181) so the eval-test-case is added and the same incident-class is prevented next time.

7. **No cross-vendor-AI-trust-recovery-orchestration** — operators often have 3–10 AI-vendors (Gorgias AI + Zendesk AI + Intercom Fin + Klaviyo AI + Triple Whale Moby + Segment AI + Amplitude AI + Mixpanel AI) but no canonical-orchestration-layer that surfaces per-vendor trust-recovery-PnL. The operator cannot answer "which AI-vendor has the highest recall-time?" or "which AI-vendor has the lowest recovery-ROI?". **Fix:** Build the cross-vendor-AI-trust-recovery-orchestration-engine on Day 18–21 of Phase 3. One canonical-page that surfaces per-AI-vendor trust-recovery-PnL + per-vendor recall-time + per-vendor recovery-ROI + per-vendor incident-count + per-vendor incident-class-breakdown. Compounds Move #181 ai-vendor-orchestration-governance.

8. **Recovery-gesture delivered too late (≥72 hours after incident)** — operators often queue the recovery-gesture in a manual workflow (CS-agent-drafts → CS-manager-approves → email-sends) that takes 72+ hours. By then, the customer has already churned. **Fix:** Wire the recovery-gesture-delivery to the customer-AI-interaction-event-stream on Day 13–14 of Phase 2. Auto-trigger within 24 hours of incident-detection. Use per-channel reliability thresholds (99% email + 95% SMS + 90% chat + 99% loyalty-points). For high-stakes incidents (refund-issued-by-AI-hallucination for VIP-tier), auto-escalate to phone-call-from-CSO within 4 hours.

9. **No recovery-gesture-delivery-success-rate measurement** — operators often assume the recovery-gesture was delivered but skip the delivery-success-rate measurement. 10–30% of recovery-gestures fail to deliver (bounced email + unsubscribed SMS + blocked chat). The customer never gets the gesture, never knows the brand cared, and the AI-trust-score-decay compounds. **Fix:** Wire the recovery-gesture-delivery-success-rate to per-channel observability on Day 13–14 of Phase 2. Track delivery-success-rate per channel (email: 99% target, SMS: 95% target, chat: 90% target, loyalty-points: 99% target). Auto-retry on delivery-failure (different channel). Surface delivery-success-rate on the AI-trust-recovery-portfolio-dashboard.

The recovery-engine is running but the operator has no signal on whether it's actually working. **Fix:** Wire the per-incident-VOC-pipeline to the recovery-gesture-delivery on Day 14 of Phase 2. Per-incident-CSAT-recovery-flow (CSAT-survey-sent-3-days-after-recovery-gesture + CSAT-recovery-target-+20-to-+40-points). Per-incident-NPS-recovery-flow (NPS-survey-sent-7-days-after-recovery-gesture + NPS-recovery-target-+10-to-+30-points). Wire the CSAT/NPS-recovery-rate to the AI-trust-recovery-portfolio-dashboard.

11. **Per-customer-AI-trust-score-cohort-LTV-overlay not built** — operators often persist the per-customer-AI-trust-score to the CDP but skip the cohort-LTV-overlay that proves the score is actually predictive of LTV. Without the overlay, the operator cannot defend the trust-score-engine to finance / security / legal / marketing and the engine is "deprioritized" in the next quarterly planning. **Fix:** Build the per-customer-AI-trust-score-cohort-LTV-overlay on Day 5–7 of Phase 1 (alongside the trust-score-decay-engine). Use Triple Whale cohort-LTV × per-customer-AI-trust-score-band (high-trust-score-cohort LTV vs low-trust-score-cohort LTV vs no-AI-trust-score-cohort LTV). Target: high-AI-trust-score-cohort has +5 to +25% LTV-uplift vs low-AI-trust-score-cohort. Surface the overlay on the AI-trust-recovery-portfolio-dashboard.

12. **No escalation-routing-L1/L2/L3-or-CXO** — operators often auto-send the recovery-gesture without routing the incident to the right escalation-tier. A refund-issued-by-AI-hallucination for a CXO-escalation-tier customer (LTV $2k+) needs a CXO-direct-call within 4 hours, not a L1-CS-agent-draft. **Fix:** Define the escalation-routing on Day 10 of Phase 2. L1-CS (default, first-time-buyer) + L2-CS (repeat-buyer) + L3-CS / CSM (VIP) + CXO-direct-call (CXO-escalation-tier). Per-incident-class escalation-routing: refund-issued-by-AI-hallucination for CXO-tier → CXO-direct-call within 4 hours. Wire to Gorgias + Zendesk + Intercom + Kustomer routing.

13. **No cross-channel-AI-trust-recovery-orchestration** — operators often handle the recovery-gesture on the channel where the AI-incident happened (email-AI-hallucination → email-recovery) but skip the cross-channel orchestration (email-AI-hallucination + SMS-AI-misfire + CS-bot-hallucination all happening to the same customer in the same week → no canonical-orchestration-layer). **Fix:** Build the cross-channel-AI-trust-recovery-orchestration on Day 21 of Phase 3. One canonical-orchestration-engine that handles multi-channel-AI-incidents per customer. Per-customer cross-channel-AI-trust-score-band (consolidates per-channel scores into one canonical score per customer). Wire to all AI-vendors.
