## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing WFT-tier (withholding-fine-tuning) with WR-tier (withholding-reconciliation) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.37].** WFT-tier answers WHICH withholding-fine-tuning-cadence the merchant uses (zero-fine-tuning-pool + annual-rollup-pool + quarterly-rollup-pool + monthly-rollup-pool + daily-rollup-pool); WR-tier answers WHICH withholding-reconciliation-tier (zero-withholding + treaty-rate-withholding + entity-rate-withholding + state-withholding-only + 30%-backup-withholding). The two axes are orthogonal — a WFT1 zero-fine-tuning creator may use WR1 zero-withholding (typical, no-Correction-required-event) OR WR2 treaty-rate-withholding (rare, NRA-with-annual-rollup); a WFT5 daily-rollup-fine-tuning creator is forced into WR5 30%-backup-withholding (daily B-Notice-correction-pool). **Fix:** carry WFT-tier and WR-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung precedence ladders (WFT1-WFT2-WFT3-WFT4-WFT5 and WR1-WR2-WR3-WR4-WR5) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-WFT-tier × per-WR-tier 5×5 cross-axis grid that scores 25 distinct WFT×WR pool combinations.

2. **Pitfall — WFT1 zero-fine-tuning creators on a high-EUCohort-treaty-rate-withholding-cohort incur unexpected NRA-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses WFT1 zero-fine-tuning pool (W-9-1099-NEC-no-Correction-required-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-treaty-rate-withholding-traffic may have a payout that crosses US-EU-border without a 1042-S-annual-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$60k-annual-NRA-rollup-maint + Apr-30/May-15-deadline-tracking on the payout event. **Fix:** the withholding-fine-tuning classifier must verify the cohort's NRA-traffic-share + cohort's withholding-fine-tuning-tolerance — WFT1 score only if cohort-NRA-share <10% AND cohort-WFT-tolerance = high; WFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning fallback for high-NRA-cohort-traffic cohorts.

3. **Pitfall — WFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning creators on a high-entity-rate-withholding-cohort fail Chapter-4-FATCA-quarterly-reporting-requirement.** Chapter-4-FATCA requires quarterly-FATCA-reporting for NRA-entity-creators receiving US-source-income; WFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning (once-per-year) is exempt for individual creators, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to WFT3 quarterly-rollup-fine-tuning is suddenly required to report the FATCA-status to IRS every quarter (4 rollovers/yr). **Fix:** the withholding-fine-tuning classifier must verify the cohort's NRA-entity-traffic + withholding-fine-tuning-tenor — WFT3 score only if cohort-NRA-entity-share >5% AND merchant-is-FATCA-registered; WFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning fallback for non-FATCA-registered merchants.

4. **Pitfall — WFT3 quarterly-rollup-fine-tuning creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-quarterly-rollup-maint for low-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-rollup-pool + entity-1042-S-rollup + Chapter-4-FATCA-quarterly-tracking + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE tax-accountant / treasury at $80k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the withholding-fine-tuning classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — WFT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-US-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to WFT1 zero-fine-tuning or WFT2 annual-rollup-fine-tuning creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.
