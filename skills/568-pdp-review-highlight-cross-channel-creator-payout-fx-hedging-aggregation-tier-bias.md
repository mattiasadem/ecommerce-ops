## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing FHAT-tier (FX-aggregation-tier) with FH-tier (FX-hedge-rate-tier) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.40].** FHAT-tier answers WHICH aggregation-cadence the merchant uses (zero-pool-aggregation-pool + daily-pool-aggregation-pool + weekly-pool-aggregation-pool + monthly-pool-aggregation-pool + quarterly-pool-aggregation-pool); FH-tier answers WHICH hedge-rate-strategy (zero-hedge + spot-hedge + forward-30-day + forward-90-day + forward-180-day). The two axes are orthogonal — a FHAT1 zero-aggregation creator may use FH1 zero-hedge (typical, no-pool-required) OR FH2 spot-hedge (rare, no-pool-required); a FHAT5 quarterly-pool-aggregation creator is forced into FH5 forward-180-day (Wise-MX-quarterly-pool-rollup-pool). **Fix:** carry FHAT-tier and FH-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung precedence ladders (FHAT1-FHAT2-FHAT3-FHAT4-FHAT5 and FH1-FH2-FH3-FH4-FH5) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-FHAT-tier × per-FH-tier 5×5 cross-axis grid that scores 25 distinct FHAT×FH pool combinations.

2. **Pitfall — FHAT1 zero-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected Wise-EU-daily-pool-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses FHAT1 zero-aggregation pool (USD-domestic-no-pool-rollup-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-border-traffic may have a payout that crosses USD-EU-border without a Wise-EU-daily-pool-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$50k-annual-Wise-EU-daily-pool-maint + 365-rollovers-per-year on the payout event. **Fix:** the FX-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's cross-border-traffic-share + cohort's aggregation-tolerance — FHAT1 score only if cohort-cross-border-share <10% AND cohort-FHAT-tolerance = high; FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.

3. **Pitfall — FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation creators on a high-JP-cohort fail Wise-JP-weekly-pool-rollup-requirement.** Wise Multi-Currency Pool 2026 requires weekly-pool-rollup for cross-border-payouts >$600/yr per JP-entity; FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation (daily-pool only) is insufficient for high-JP-cohorts, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to FHAT3 weekly-pool-aggregation is suddenly required to file Wise-JP-weekly-pool-rollup with EU-EMIR-MiFIR-attestation. **Fix:** the FX-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's JP-cohort-traffic + aggregation-tenor — FHAT3 score only if cohort-JP-share >5% AND merchant-is-Wise-Multi-Currency-Pool-registered; FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation fallback for non-Wise-Multi-Currency-Pool-registered merchants.

4. **Pitfall — FHAT3 weekly-pool-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$70k-annual-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The weekly-pool-rollup-pool + Wise-JP-weekly-pool-rollup + 52-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $70k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the FX-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — FHAT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-US-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to FHAT1 zero-aggregation or FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

5. **Pitfall — FHAT4 monthly-pool-aggregation creators on a high-BR-cohort fail cross-monthly-pool-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to FHAT4 monthly-pool-aggregation is REQUIRED to track cross-monthly-Wise-BR-pool-rollup for every BR-payout event; non-compliance triggers cross-monthly-pool-enforcement and additional-monthly-pool-penalties. **Fix:** the FX-aggregation classifier must verify the merchant's cross-monthly-pool-tracking-status — FHAT4 score only if merchant-cross-monthly-pool-tracking-active; FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation fallback for non-cross-monthly-pool-tracking-active merchants.

6. **Pitfall — FHAT5 quarterly-pool-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$120k-annual-maint-cost for high-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-pool-aggregation-pool + Wise-MX-quarterly-pool-rollup + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $120k/yr; even high-margin cohorts may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout if the cohort's payout-frequency is low. **Fix:** the FX-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency BEFORE scoring resonance — FHAT5 score 0 for cohorts with annual-payout-frequency <500 AND FHAT-notional <$1M. Route to FHAT3 weekly-pool-aggregation creators for low-frequency fallbacks.

7. **Pitfall — FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort fail EU EMIR 2026-pool-rollup-requirement.** EU EMIR 2026 requires daily-pool-rollup-reporting for cross-border-payouts >€2000/yr per EU-payer; FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation (365-rollovers-per-year) is REQUIRED to report by T+1-deadline. **Fix:** the FX-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's EU-creator-count + annual-payout-frequency — FHAT2 score only if cohort-EU-creator-count >30 AND annual-payout-frequency >2,000; FHAT1 zero-aggregation fallback for non-EMIR-required cohorts.

8. **Pitfall — FHAT3 weekly-pool-aggregation creators silently demoted to FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation because of Wise-Multi-Currency-Pool-staleness-event.** A creator whose Wise-Multi-Currency-Pool registration was set 90 days ago may have a stale registration-status that doesn't match the current registration-status, and the merchant's treasury-team may silently mark-down the weekly-pool to daily-pool (FHAT2) on the next rollup-event. **Fix:** the FX-aggregation classifier must read the live Wise-Multi-Currency-Pool-table (per CaptivateA FX Hedging Aggregation 2026 + Wise Multi-Currency Pool 2026 + EU EMIR 2026 + EU MiFIR 2026) and re-derive FHAT-tier on every rollup event.

9. **Pitfall — FHAT4 monthly-pool-aggregation creators on a multi-state-cohort fail state-pool-enforcement-risk.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to FHAT4 monthly-pool-aggregation across multiple jurisdictions is REQUIRED to file cross-jurisdiction-pool on every monthly-payout; failure triggers jurisdiction-AG-enforcement (e.g., BCB-Circular-3-978-Wise-BR-monthly-pool + BCB-Circular-4-373-monthly-pool + Brazil-CVM-monthly-pool + Banxico-CNBV-monthly-pool) and potential $10k-$50k jurisdiction-AG-penalties. **Fix:** the FX-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's jurisdiction-distribution-count + monthly-payout-event-volume — FHAT4 score only if cohort-jurisdiction-distribution-count >5 jurisdictions AND merchant-jurisdiction-AG-enforcement-history-clean; FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation fallback for risky cohorts.

10. **Pitfall — FHAT5 quarterly-pool-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$120k-annual-maint-cost for multi-jurisdiction-merchants.** The quarterly-pool-aggregation-pool + Wise-MX-quarterly-pool-rollup + multi-jurisdiction-pool-maint (Wise Multi-Currency Pool 2026 + Stripe Connect Multi-Currency 2026 + PayPal Mass Pay FX 2026 + BCB Circular 3.978/2020 + Mexico Banxico 2026 + Mexico CNBV 2026 + JP FX Margin Rules 2026 + JP FSA 2026 + JP MOF FX 2026) requires 1 FTE engineer / treasury at $120k/yr minimum; cohorts with low-frequency-payout-events to multi-jurisdiction-creators may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-payout. **Fix:** the FX-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-payout-frequency + cohort-jurisdiction-diversity BEFORE scoring resonance — FHAT5 score 0 for cohorts with jurisdiction-diversity >3 jurisdictions AND annual-payout-frequency <1000.

11. **Pitfall — FHAT4 monthly-pool-aggregation creators silently demoted to FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation on a monthly-pool-tracking-failed-event.** A creator whose monthly-pool-tracking was tracked monthly may have the monthly-tracking silently fail (e.g., the merchant's monthly-pool-database has been unavailable to execute the monthly-rollup); the merchant is REQUIRED to fall back to FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation on next payout. **Fix:** the FX-aggregation classifier must maintain a `p.fhat_monthly_rollup_status` field — set to true on rollup-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to FHAT2 on next payout AND adds a monthly-pool-tracking-reconciliation line-item.

12. **Pitfall — FHAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's FX-aggregation-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer FHAT1 zero-aggregation creators (low-maint-cost, USD-domestic-no-pool-rollup, no-50-jurisdiction-tracking) over FHAT5 quarterly-pool-aggregation creators (high-maint-cost, quarterly-pool-tracking, Wise-MX-quarterly-pool-rollup-pool). A FHAT1 zero-aggregation creator may be FHAT-clean but FHAT-maint-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-FHAT-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — FHAT1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer FHAT5 (e.g., cohorts with high multi-jurisdiction-traffic). The cohort's FHAT-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-FHAT-maint-cost-sensitivity × cohort-multi-jurisdiction-traffic-preference.

13. **Pitfall — FHAT-tier cross-axis with PR-tier silently double-counts FX-spread-cost on cross-platform payments.** A creator on FHAT1 zero-aggregation with PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay as secondary platform is potentially double-charged FX-spread-cost (PayPal's auto-FX-margin + merchant's FHAT-rollup on the same payout). The FX-aggregation classifier must not double-count FX-spread-cost on cross-platform-cohorts. **Fix:** the FHAT-tier × PR-tier cross-axis must apply secondary-platform-FHAT-conditional — FHAT1 × PR2 score uses PayPal-Mass-Pay-FHAT as the secondary; the cohort's primary FHAT-rule is zero-aggregation.

14. **Pitfall — FHAT3 weekly-pool-aggregation creators mis-classified as FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation because of Wise-Multi-Currency-Pool-stale state.** A creator whose Wise-Multi-Currency-Pool registration was set 90 days ago may have a stale registration that doesn't match the current registration-status, and the merchant's treasury-team may silently mark-down the weekly-pool to daily-pool (FHAT2) on the next rollup-event. The actual state is "FHAT3-pending — registration-stale". **Fix:** the FX-aggregation classifier must distinguish between FHAT2-confirmed / FHAT3-pending / FHAT3-confirmed states; route FHAT3-pending creators to FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation as a temporary fallback AND flag for registration-refresh.

15. **Pitfall — FHAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's FX-aggregation-residency-preference.** A cohort with US-domestic-only preference (no cross-border-traffic) requires FHAT1 zero-aggregation pool; a cohort with EU-only preference requires FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation pool (or FHAT3 weekly-pool-aggregation for EU-LLCs); a cohort with JP-only preference requires FHAT3 weekly-pool-aggregation pool (or FHAT2 daily-pool-aggregation for JP-individuals); a cohort with BR-only preference requires FHAT4 monthly-pool-aggregation pool — but the FHAT-tier model US-centric-by-default; cohort must use FHAT4 monthly-pool-aggregation as BR-equivalent. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-FHAT-residency-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — FHAT5 × US-domestic-cohort score 0; route to FHAT1 zero-aggregation fallbacks. Add a "FHAT-tier × cohort-jurisdiction" overlay.
