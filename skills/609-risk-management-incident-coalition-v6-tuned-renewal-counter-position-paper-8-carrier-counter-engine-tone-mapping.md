## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

1. **Don't reuse Move #N.99.9.4.1's single-tone-MOVE-family-blob voice for all 8 carriers — Coalition (concise-technical-v6) and At-Bay (narrative-incident-led-v6) are LITERALLY OPPOSITE tones, and shipping both with the same voice triggers a per-carrier 'counter-position-paper-tone-mismatch' flag.** Coalition-concise-technical-v6 = bullet-point + technical-jargon + per-control-effectiveness-graded evidence-link (200-300 words); At-Bay-narrative-incident-led-v6 = first-person-plaintiff-voice + per-incident-cohort-anchor + per-RCA-category-counterfactual (350-500 words opening with "On [incident-date], our [cohort] cohort experienced [RCA] — here's the systematic-control we added"). The 8 carriers in Move #N.99.9.4.3 each expect a DIFFERENT tone-class fingerprint; reusing Move #N.99.9.4.1's single-tone blob is the canonical regression that gets carriers to flag the renewal-tender-filing as "tone-mismatch" and apply 20-40% premium uplift.

2. **Don't drop the per-CIS-control-mapping in Hiscox-control-maturity-v6 — Hiscox's renewal-tender-filing-secretary LITERALLY rejects counter-position-papers that don't map to a CIS Control v8 ID.** Hiscox is the canonical CIS-control-maturity-led underwriter; they require every counter-position-paper to map to a specific CIS Control v8 sub-control (e.g. CIS 5.1, CIS 5.7, CIS 6.7, CIS 7.1, CIS 8.11, CIS 13.1, CIS 14.2, CIS 16.10, etc.) AND carry a per-CIS-control-effectiveness-graded score. A counter-position-paper sent to Hiscox that lacks a CIS mapping gets a 'no-CIS-mapping' flag and the operator eats 15-25% premium uplift. The per-CIS-control-mapping-density check (≥0.7 in the Hiscox validation-vector) is the canonical verification gate.

3. **Don't skip the per-claim-frequency-baseline in Chubb-claim-prevention-v6 — Chubb's renewal-tender-filing-secretary LITERALLY requires a before/after claim-frequency-baseline on every counter-position-paper.** Chubb is the canonical claim-prevention-led underwriter; they want to see the canonical "Claim-scenario frequency dropped from X to Y because of [control]" claim-frequency-baseline format on each counter-position-paper, with before/after claim-frequency numbers + before/after claim-severity numbers + per-RCA-category-anchor. A counter-position-paper sent to Chubb without a claim-frequency-baseline gets a 'no-claim-baseline' flag and the operator eats 10-20% premium uplift.
