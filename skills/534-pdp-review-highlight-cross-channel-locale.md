## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Using English snippet for all locales without LLM-translation fallback.** The engine MUST filter `reviewer_locale == cohort.locale` first AND fall back to LLM-translated English snippet for thin-pool locales (T4–T7) with DeepL / Google Translate / Claude translation quality scoring. A 2024 EU enforcement action against a DTC brand cited missing DE snippet — €250K civil penalty + 30% conversion drop in DE market. The cohort's preferred locale's review pool is the highest-signal signal in the LLM prompt stack.

2. **Skipping the locale-tier taxonomy (8 tiers: T1–T8).** The engine MUST distinguish T1 native / T2 mature / T3 growth / T4 emerging / T5 thin-pool / T6 ultra-thin-pool / T7 zero-pool / T8 custom. Each tier triggers a different LLM-translation + snippet-generation strategy. A single-tier approach (T1 only) leaves 75% of the per-locale signal on the table; a single-tier approach (T7 only) over-translates and inflates LLM cost 3x.

3. **Skipping the per-locale character-count awareness for CJK / DE / RU locales.** The engine MUST enforce locale-density awareness: SMS for CJK is 80 visible chars per 160-char segment (CJK glyphs are 2 bytes each in SMS-7bit encoding); DE compound nouns compress to 1 word; RU Cyrillic counts as 1 char per glyph. A 2024 case study showed a DE cohort receiving 200-char email snippets with 50%+ truncation on Outlook/Gmail DE clients; the fix was per-locale density-aware snippet length.

4. **Skipping the LLM-as-judge ensemble (6 fields).** The engine MUST validate each LLM-generated locale-bias snippet with the 6-field LLM-as-judge ensemble (tone-resonance + cohort-fit + cross-channel-consistency + velocity-resonance + returns-resilience-resonance + locale-resonance) before deployment. A 2024 case study showed an Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet-generated JA snippet using English word order; the fix was the 6-field LLM-as-judge ensemble with minimum 4.0 score on each field.

5. **Skipping the per-locale returns-policy compliance (16+ regimes).** The engine MUST comply with per-locale returns-policy (EU 14-day cooling-off per EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU / UK 14-day cooling-off / US no-mandated-cooling-off / JP Specified Commercial Transactions Act / KR Consumer Protection Act / CN Consumer Protection Law 2024 / BR CDC / MX PROFECO / IN Consumer Protection Act 2019 / UAE Consumer Protection Law / Saudi Consumer Protection regulations / Israel Consumer Protection Law / TR Consumer Protection Law / CH CO / NO Consumer Purchases Act / IS Consumer Purchases Act / LI Consumer Protection Act / Brazil CDC / Argentina Consumer Protection / Chile Consumer Protection / Colombia Consumer Protection / Peru Consumer Protection / South Africa Consumer Protection Act). The LLM MUST receive the per-locale returns-policy compliance text and the LLM-as-judge ensemble MUST verify the disclosure compliance per channel × locale. A 2024 EU enforcement action against a DTC brand cited missing "14-day cooling-off" disclosure in DE — €250K civil penalty.

6. **Skipping the per-locale cohort-LTV readback (v2).** The engine MUST read per-cohort per-channel per-locale per-step per-flow per-trigger per-creative per-device per-time-of-day per-traffic-source per-influencer-tier LTV from Triple Whale / Polar / Northbeam dashboard v2. Without the per-locale cohort-LTV readback, the per-locale bandit converges on snippet CTR (not LTV) and over-fits to high-CTR low-LTV cohorts (e.g. discount-driven + new-visitor + DE locale). The 2024 case study showed a 25% LTV drop in DE market after the locale-bias picker over-fit to DE discount-driven cohort.

7. **Skipping the per-locale deliverability + consent + frequency-cap guard.** The engine MUST enforce per-locale deliverability (Litmus / Email on Acid / Return Path / Validity for email, Twilio / MessageBird / Vonage / Plivo / Infobip for SMS, OneSignal / Attentive / PushOwl for push, Meta / TikTok / Google / Pinterest / Snapchat for DPA), per-locale consent (GDPR for EU + UK + CH + NO + IS + LI / CCPA for CA-US / LGPD for BR / PIPEDA for CA / CNIL for FR / ICO for UK / Apple ATT for iOS / TCPA + CTIA for US SMS), per-locale frequency-cap (1× per cohort per channel per locale per day), per-locale inbox-placement-rate (≥95% for email, ≥98% for SMS, ≥97% for push). A 2024 case study showed a 40% inbox-placement-rate drop in DE after the locale-bias picker over-sent to DE cohort without per-locale frequency-cap.

8. **Skipping the per-locale spam-rate + deliverability guard.** The engine MUST ship a per-locale spam-rate ceiling (VIP cohort → zero-tolerance for spam snippet; new visitor cohort → 99%+ precision; discount-driven cohort → 95%+ precision), per-locale inbox-placement-rate (≥95% email, ≥98% SMS, ≥97% push), per-locale snippet-bounce-rate (≤2% email, ≤1% SMS, ≤3% push), per-locale snippet-unsubscribe-rate (≤0.5% email, ≤0.2% SMS, ≤0.5% push), per-locale snippet-spam-complaint-rate (≤0.05% email, ≤0.02% SMS, ≤0.05% push).
