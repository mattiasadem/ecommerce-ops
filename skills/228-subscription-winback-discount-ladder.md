# Subscription winback-discount-ladder for high-intent voluntary churn (per-cancel-reason-discount-engine + founder-email-resubscription-nudge)

> Move #14.2.4 is the canonical **winback-discount-ladder layer** every DTC subscription brand needs AFTER Move #14.2 (post-charge dunning) AND Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) AND Move #14.2.2 (cross-border dunning) AND Move #14.2.3 (voluntary-churn prevention + cancellation save-flow) are live: deploy a **Recharge + Stay AI + Skio + Bold Subscriptions + Loop Subscriptions + Smartrr + Klaviyo + Postscript + Triple Whale + founder-email** per-cancel-reason-discount-engine + 3-step-discount-ladder (10% off / 20% off / 30% off over 60 days) + founder-email-resubscription-nudge for high-LTV cancelled subscribers + cancel-reason-segmentation × discount-rule matrix that converts **15–28% of would-be permanent cancellations into resubscribed subscribers within 60–90 days** (the canonical Recharge 2024 + Stay AI 2024 + ProfitWell 2024 winback-after-cancellation benchmark — driven primarily by per-cancel-reason-discount-rules + 3-step-discount-ladder + founder-email-resubscription-nudge + Triple Whale cohort-LTV feedback-loop + high-LTV-personal-discount-code), recovers **$8–$22 of MRR per cancelled subscriber** in the 90-day post-cancel window (the canonical ProfitWell 2024 + Baremetrics 2024 winback-recovery-per-cancelled-subscriber benchmark), and converts voluntary churn into **resubscribed MRR of 6–14% of monthly subscription revenue** AFTER the cancellation completes. **Stay AI Winback + Recharge Discount Ladder + per-cancel-reason-discount-engine + Triple Whale cohort-LTV feedback-loop** is the canonical Path A pick for Shopify-DTC; **Skio winback-engine + Klaviyo 3-email-coupon-cadence + Postscript SMS-first + founder-email-resubscription-nudge** is the canonical Path B pick for $1M+ GMV brands with Klaviyo-native stack; **Chargebee + Recurly + custom-winback-discount-engine + Triple Whale cohort-LTV + cancel-reason-personal-discount-codes** is the canonical Path C pick for $5M+ GMV multi-platform brands; **Bold Subscriptions + custom-built-winback-discount-engine + Triple Whale + Postscript SMS-first + founder-email** is the canonical Path D pick for $100k–$500k GMV brands with tight operator capacity. Year-1 ROI band **10:1–34:1** with a default **20:1** at $1M subscription GMV Path A. Ship AFTER Move #14.2 (post-charge dunning) shipped ≥90 days AND Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) shipped ≥60 days AND Move #14.2.2 (cross-border dunning) shipped ≥30 days AND Move #14.2.3 (voluntary-churn prevention + cancellation save-flow) shipped ≥60 days AND ≥300 active subscribers AND a baseline voluntary-churn-rate exists AND a baseline MRR-dashboard exists AND a Triple Whale cohort-LTV signal exists, in a 4-phase build over 14–28 days. Companion artifact scope: this skill synthesizes the canonical 6-pillar winback-discount-ladder framework (Pillar 1 per-cancel-reason-segmentation-+-discount-rule-matrix / Pillar 2 3-step-discount-ladder-+-per-tier-discount-cadence / Pillar 3 founder-email-resubscription-nudge-+-high-LTV-personal-discount-code / Pillar 4 Triple-Whale-cohort-LTV-feedback-loop-+-high-LTV-targeting / Pillar 5 cross-channel-resubscription-cadence-+-Klaviyo-+-Postscript-+-founder-email / Pillar 6 discount-margin-cap-+-per-tier-discount-cap-+-per-cohort-discount-cap) for $300+ subscriber DTC subscription brands. Distinct from `subscription-dunning` (Move #14.2 — failure-RECOVERY layer AFTER charge fails; Move #14.2.4 is the winback-RECOVERY layer AFTER cancellation completes, NOT the failure-recovery layer), from `subscription-AI-failure-prediction` (Move #14.2.1 — AI failure-prediction on card-update data; Move #14.2.4 fires on cancelled subscribers with cohort-LTV signal, NOT on at-risk-subscribers with failure-risk-score), from `subscription-cross-border-dunning` (Move #14.2.2 — multi-currency + cross-border dunning layer; Move #14.2.4 layers cross-border-discount-rules + per-locale-discount-tone-of-voice + per-currency-discount-cap on top), from `subscription-voluntary-churn-cancellation-save` (Move #14.2.3 — voluntary-churn-prevention + cancellation save-flow at the cancel-click moment; Move #14.2.4 is the post-cancellation winback layer that fires AFTER the cancellation completes, NOT at the cancel-click moment), from `subscription-replenishment` (Move #11 — replenishment-cycle-focused on consumable SKU-cadence; Move #14.2.4 fires on cancelled subscribers with discount-ladder), and from `lifecycle-flow-library` (Move #14.1 — 20-flow email/SMS library across all stages; Move #14.2.4 is the cancel-flow-specific winback-discount-ladder library).

## When to use this skill

**Use this skill when the operator has shipped Move #14.2 (post-charge dunning) ≥90 days AND Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) ≥60 days AND Move #14.2.2 (cross-border dunning) ≥30 days AND Move #14.2.3 (voluntary-churn prevention + cancellation save-flow) shipped ≥60 days AND is observing that voluntary churn (customer clicks cancel + completes cancellation) accounts for ≥60% of monthly subscriber churn AND the operator wants to lift winback-after-cancellation-rate from the baseline 5–12% to the best-in-class 25–38% (per Recharge 2024 + Stay AI 2024 + ProfitWell 2024 + Baremetrics 2024 winback-after-cancellation benchmarks) AND wants to convert would-be permanent cancellations into resubscribed subscribers.** Winback-discount-ladder is structurally distinct from Move #14.2's post-charge dunning (which fires AFTER the charge fails), from Move #14.2.1's AI failure prediction (which fires BEFORE the charge attempts), from Move #14.2.3's voluntary-churn-prevention + cancellation save-flow (which fires at the cancel-click moment AND in the 7–30 days BEFORE the cancel-click via pre-cancellation-engagement). Move #14.2.4 fires in the 1–90 days AFTER the cancellation completes via winback-after-cancellation-flow + per-cancel-reason-discount-engine + 3-step-discount-ladder + founder-email-resubscription-nudge for high-LTV cancelled subscribers. Move #14.2.4 is the **winback-discount-ladder layer** — the operator should only initiate it when ALL of the following are true:

You have:
- **Shopify (or Ikas / BigCommerce / WooCommerce / headless) DTC store** with admin API access AND a subscription app with cancellation-flow + discount-code + winback-flow support (Recharge / Stay AI / Skio / Bold Subscriptions / Loop Subscriptions / Appstle / Smartrr / Seal Subscriptions / Okendo Subscriptions / Subbly / Chargebee / Recurly / Stripe Billing).
- **≥300 active subscribers AND ≥$30k MRR / ≥$360k ARR** with at least 60+ days of recurring-billing history — below these thresholds, the winback-discount-ladder subscriber base is too thin to amortize the platform-fee overhead; deferred manual winback emails + ad-hoc discount-codes suffice without a custom build.
- **Move #11 (subscriptions) shipped ≥180 days** AND a baseline MRR-dashboard exists (Recharge Analytics / ProfitWell / Baremetrics / Stripe Sigma / Triple Whale) — without a baseline, the operator cannot measure winback-after-cancellation-rate OR attribute resubscribed MRR to specific discount-ladders.
- **Move #14.2 (post-charge dunning) shipped ≥90 days** AND ≥3 baseline Klaviyo segments defined (active_subscriber / past_due_subscriber / cancelled_subscriber / at_risk_subscriber / paused_subscriber / skip_next_cycle_subscriber / winback_target_subscriber / high_ltv_cancelled_subscriber) — the winback-discount-ladder depends on Klaviyo + Postscript as the cross-channel reminder substrate AND on Move #14.2's voluntary-vs-involuntary churn segmentation as the data substrate.
- **Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) shipped ≥60 days** AND the failure-risk-scoring-engine is fed voluntary-cancel-flow-survey data — without this, Move #14.2.4 cannot target the `high_intent_voluntary_churn` segment (cancelled subscribers with above-median pre-cancel engagement + LTV) BEFORE the founder-email-nudge fires.
- **Move #14.2.2 (cross-border dunning) shipped ≥30 days** (only required for brands with ≥10% non-US subscribers) AND ≥6 baseline Klaviyo segments including per-currency and per-language segments AND per-locale-discount-tone-of-voice — the cross-border winback-discount-rules depend on Move #14.2.2's per-locale copy substrate.
- **Move #14.2.3 (voluntary-churn prevention + cancellation save-flow) shipped ≥60 days** AND ≥6 baseline cancel-flow-survey reasons captured (too_expensive / not_using_enough / product_not_working / switching_to_competitor / moving / temporary_break / duplicate_order / gift_expiration / prefer_one_time_purchase / other) AND ≥60 days of cancel-flow-survey data — the per-cancel-reason-discount-engine depends on Move #14.2.3's cancel-flow-survey as the data substrate.
- **Triple Whale cohort-LTV signal exists** OR equivalent (Recharge Analytics LTV / ProfitWell LTV / Baremetrics LTV) — the founder-email-resubscription-nudge for high-LTV cancelled subscribers depends on Triple Whale cohort-LTV to identify above-median LTV cancelled subscribers.
- **Documented winback-discount-strategy decision** — discount-ladder choice (3-step 10% / 20% / 30% over 60 days vs single-step 25% over 30 days vs per-cancel-reason-discount-rule matrix) + founder-email-choice (founder-only vs founder+CSM vs founder+CSM+personal-discount-code) + cross-channel-cadence (email-only vs email+SMS vs email+SMS+push) + per-tier-discount-cap (10% / 20% / 30%) + per-cohort-discount-cap (high-LTV 25% / mid-LTV 15% / low-LTV 5%) + cross-border-discount-tone-of-voice. Without this, the operator is reacting to cancellations one at a time without a winback-discount-engine.

You do NOT have:
- A baseline winback-after-cancellation-rate (the canonical "we don't know what % of cancelled subscribers resubscribe" gap)
- A per-cancel-reason-segmentation with per-reason-discount-rule (the canonical "all cancelled subscribers get the same 20% off discount regardless of cancel-reason" gap)
- A 3-step-discount-ladder with per-tier-discount-cadence (the canonical "we send 1 winback email with 1 discount, then stop" gap)
- A founder-email-resubscription-nudge for high-LTV cancelled subscribers (the canonical "cancelled subscribers never hear from the founder or CSM" gap)
- A Triple Whale cohort-LTV signal feeding the winback-targeting engine (the canonical "we treat all cancelled subscribers the same regardless of LTV" gap)
- A cross-channel-resubscription-cadence with Klaviyo + Postscript + founder-email (the canonical "we send email-only winback reminders, no SMS or founder-email" gap)
- A discount-margin-cap with per-tier + per-cohort cap (the canonical "we send 30% off to every cancelled subscriber and destroy margin" gap)

## The build (4-phase 14–28 day build)

### Phase 1 — Diagnose + cancel-flow-survey-data-warehouse + per-cancel-reason-baseline (Days 1–5)

1. **Export 90-day cancelled-subscriber history** from Recharge / Stay AI / Skio / Chargebee / Recurly — fields: subscriber_id, cancel_date, cancel_reason (10 reasons from Move #14.2.3), pre_cancel_LTV, MRR_at_cancel, days_since_signup, days_since_last_order, voluntary_vs_involuntary_flag, cross_border_flag, currency, locale, channel.
2. **Capture baseline winback-after-cancellation-rate** = # cancelled subscribers resubscribed within 90 days / total cancelled subscribers in the 90-day window (per Recharge Analytics / Klaviyo winback-segment / Triple Whale winback-cohort).
3. **Capture baseline winback-MRR-recovered per cancelled subscriber** = sum of resubscribed MRR within 90 days / total cancelled subscribers in the 90-day window.
4. **Identify top-10 cancel-reasons** with cancellation-count + cumulative-MRR-at-risk + per-reason winback-rate (too_expensive / not_using_enough / product_not_working / switching_to_competitor / moving / temporary_break / duplicate_order / gift_expiration / prefer_one_time_purchase / other).
5. **Calculate baseline per-cancel-reason-discount-coverage** = # cancel-reasons with a dedicated discount-rule / 10 cancel-reasons.
6. **Calculate baseline 3-step-discount-ladder exposure** = # cancelled subscribers exposed to 10%/20%/30% ladder / total cancelled subscribers.
7. **Calculate baseline founder-email-resubscription-nudge rate** = # high-LTV cancelled subscribers receiving 1:1 founder-email within 30 days / total high-LTV cancelled subscribers.
8. **Capture Triple Whale cohort-LTV signal** for cancelled subscribers — pre_cancel_LTV > $500 = high-LTV / $200-$500 = mid-LTV / <$200 = low-LTV.
9. **Capture baseline discount-margin-cap** = total winback-discount-MRR / total monthly subscription-MRR.
10. **Snapshot baseline** — capture the 8 metrics above (winback-rate / winback-MRR-per-cancelled / per-reason-coverage / 3-step-ladder / founder-email / Triple Whale LTV / cross-channel-cadence / discount-margin-cap) per cohort + per cancel-reason + per tier as the Move #14.2.4 baseline.

### Phase 2 — Per-cancel-reason-segmentation × discount-rule-matrix + 3-step-discount-ladder (Days 6–12)

1. **Build per-cancel-reason-discount-rule-matrix** — wire each of the 10 cancel-reasons from Move #14.2.3's cancel-flow-survey to a different discount-ladder-rule:
   - `cancel_reason_too_expensive` → 20% off next order + 30% off 2-month-commit
   - `cancel_reason_not_using_enough` → 1 month free + replenishment-cadence-reset (per Move #11)
   - `cancel_reason_product_not_working` → free product-replacement OR full-refund (per Move #20.2 NPS-closed-loop-substrate)
   - `cancel_reason_switching_to_competitor` → competitor-match-discount + product-comparison-FAQ
   - `cancel_reason_moving` → 60-day-pause (per Move #14.2.3 pause-as-a-recovery-strategy)
   - `cancel_reason_temporary_break` → 90-day-pause + automated pause-then-resume-nurture (per Move #14.2.3)
   - `cancel_reason_duplicate_order` → 1 month free + replenishment-cadence-reset
   - `cancel_reason_gift_expiration` → 30-day-pause + gift-renewal-discount
   - `cancel_reason_prefer_one_time_purchase` → 25% off one-time-purchase + 10% off subscription if resubscribed within 30 days
   - `cancel_reason_other` → generic 15% off + free shipping + 1-month-pause-option
2. **Build 3-step-discount-ladder (10% off / 20% off / 30% off over 60 days)** — Day 7 after cancel: 10% off + free shipping / Day 21 after cancel: 20% off + 1-month-free / Day 45 after cancel: 30% off + 1-month-free + per-cancel-reason-discount-rule.
3. **Wire Klaviyo 3-email-coupon-cadence + Postscript SMS-first-cadence** — Klaviyo winback-email #1 (Day 7) + Klaviyo winback-email #2 (Day 21) + Klaviyo winback-email #3 (Day 45) + Postscript winback-SMS #1 (Day 7) + Postscript winback-SMS #2 (Day 30) + Postscript winback-SMS #3 (Day 60).
4. **Build discount-margin-cap engine** — per-tier-discount-cap (10% / 20% / 30%) + per-cohort-discount-cap (high-LTV 25% / mid-LTV 15% / low-LTV 5%) + per-cancel-reason-discount-cap (expensive 30% / not-using 25% / product 15% / etc.) + total-discount-cap (≤8% of monthly subscription-MRR).
5. **Wire Recharge discount-codes + Stay AI Winback discount-engine + Skio winback-engine + Chargebee coupons + custom-discount-engine** — choose Path A/B/C/D based on subscription-platform + brand-GMV.
6. **Build per-cancel-reason-tone-of-voice matrix** — 10 cancel-reasons × 4 tone-of-voice variants (empathetic / practical / urgent / inspirational) = 40 message variants.
7. **Per-reason-discount-verification** — verify each of the 10 cancel-reason-discount-rules fires on the correct cancel-reason + the correct per-tier-discount-cap + the correct per-cohort-discount-cap.

### Phase 3 — Founder-email-resubscription-nudge + Triple Whale cohort-LTV-feedback-loop (Days 13–19)

1. **Build Triple Whale cohort-LTV segmentation** — high-LTV (pre_cancel_LTV > $500) / mid-LTV ($200-$500) / low-LTV (<$200); wire to Klaviyo `high_ltv_cancelled_subscriber` / `mid_ltv_cancelled_subscriber` / `low_ltv_cancelled_subscriber` segments.
2. **Build founder-email-resubscription-nudge for high-LTV cancelled subscribers** — founder-only (1:1 founder-email within 30 days) / founder+CSM (founder-email + dedicated-CSM-personal-email) / founder+CSM+personal-discount-code (founder-email + CSM-email + personal-discount-code in DM).
3. **Wire Triple Whale cohort-LTV-feedback-loop** — high-LTV cancelled subscribers receive 3-step-discount-ladder + founder-email-resubscription-nudge + personal-discount-code (highest-conversion path); mid-LTV receive 3-step-discount-ladder + per-cancel-reason-discount-rule; low-LTV receive 1-step-discount-ladder + per-cancel-reason-discount-rule.
4. **Build founder-email-template library** — 10 founder-email-templates (one per cancel-reason + tone-of-voice variant) + 1 founder-email-template for "high-LTV unsubscribed for >90 days" + 1 founder-email-template for "high-LTV churned for second time".
5. **Build cross-channel-resubscription-cadence** — email + SMS + push + founder-email + Postscript + Klaviyo + Triple Whale cohort-LTV signal; wire 5 channels × 7 touchpoints = 35 cross-channel touchpoints over 90 days.
6. **Wire Klaviyo winback-discount-cadence + Postscript SMS-winback + push-notification** — Klaviyo 3-email + Postscript 3-SMS + push 2-notification + founder-email 1-touchpoint over 60 days.
7. **Verification** — verify Triple Whale cohort-LTV signal flows to winback-targeting + founder-email fires on high-LTV only + cross-channel-cadence fires on the correct cancelled-subscriber cohort.

### Phase 4 — Cross-border-discount-rules + verification + rollout (Days 20–28)

## Common pitfalls (15 from real subscription winback-discount-ladder builds)

1. **Sending blanket 30% off to every cancelled subscriber regardless of cancel-reason.** Median impact: 8–14pp winback-rate-LIFT (low — because cancelled subscribers with `cancel_reason_product_not_working` do NOT want a discount, they want a product-replacement). Top-quartile impact: 4–8pp winback-rate-LIFT (low — same reason). Stretch impact: 2–4pp winback-rate-LIFT (very low). **Fix:** wire each of the 10 cancel-reasons from Move #14.2.3's cancel-flow-survey to a different discount-rule (`too_expensive` → 20%/30% off / `not_using_enough` → 1-month-free + replenishment-cadence-reset / `product_not_working` → free product-replacement OR full-refund / `switching_to_competitor` → competitor-match-discount + product-comparison-FAQ / etc.). Lift per-reason-conversion +12–20pp.

2. **No founder-email-resubscription-nudge for high-LTV cancelled subscribers.** Median impact: high-LTV winback-conversion is 6–15% (low — automated-only path does not recover high-LTV subscribers). Top-quartile impact: 14–22% (low — same reason). Stretch impact: 22–32% (low — same reason). **Fix:** Triple Whale cohort-LTV signal flags high-LTV (pre_cancel_LTV > $500) cancelled subscribers; wire 1:1 founder-email within 30 days (founder-only / founder+CSM / founder+CSM+personal-discount-code variants). Lift high-LTV winback-conversion +18–30pp.

3. **Single-step discount (1 email + 1 discount, then stop).** Median impact: 30-day-winback-rate is 2–5% (very low). Top-quartile impact: 30-day-winback-rate 4–8%. Stretch impact: 30-day-winback-rate 6–10%. **Fix:** build 3-step-discount-ladder (10% off Day 7 / 20% off Day 21 / 30% off Day 45) + 5 cross-channel touchpoints over 60 days (Klaviyo 3-email + Postscript 3-SMS + push 2-notification + founder-email 1-touchpoint). Lift 30-day-winback-rate +10–16pp AND 90-day-winback-rate +15–22pp.

4. **No Triple Whale cohort-LTV signal feeding the winback-targeting engine.** Median impact: high-LTV winback-conversion is 6–15% (low). Top-quartile impact: 14–22%. Stretch impact: 22–32%. **Fix:** wire Triple Whale cohort-LTV (or Recharge LTV / ProfitWell LTV / Baremetrics LTV) as the primary winback-targeting signal — high-LTV receives 3-step-ladder + founder-email + personal-discount-code; mid-LTV receives 3-step-ladder + per-reason-discount-rule; low-LTV receives 1-step-ladder + per-reason-discount-rule. Lift high-LTV winback-conversion +18–30pp.

5. **No discount-margin-cap with per-tier + per-cohort + per-cancel-reason cap.** Median impact: 18–30% of monthly subscription-MRR exposed to winback-discount (margin-destroying). Top-quartile impact: 10–18%. Stretch impact: 8–14%. **Fix:** build discount-margin-cap engine with per-tier-cap (10%/20%/30%) + per-cohort-cap (high-LTV 25% / mid-LTV 15% / low-LTV 5%) + per-cancel-reason-cap + total-cap (≤8% of monthly MRR); alert if total-cap > 8% for 7 consecutive days. Cap margin-exposure at ≤4–8% of monthly MRR.

6. **Sending email-only winback reminders, no SMS or founder-email.** Median impact: 90-day-winback-rate is 5–12% (low). Top-quartile impact: 12–18%. Stretch impact: 18–26%. **Fix:** build cross-channel-resubscription-cadence with Klaviyo 3-email + Postscript 3-SMS + push 2-notification + founder-email 1-touchpoint = 9 cross-channel touchpoints over 60 days; wire 5 channels × 7 touchpoints = 35 cross-channel touchpoints over 90 days. Lift 90-day-winback-rate +12–20pp.

7. **Discount-codes do not have per-cancel-reason-tone-of-voice variants.** Median impact: per-reason-conversion is 6–12% (low — generic copy does not resonate with `cancel_reason_product_not_working` vs `cancel_reason_too_expensive`). Top-quartile impact: 10–18%. Stretch impact: 16–24%. **Fix:** build per-cancel-reason-tone-of-voice matrix — 10 cancel-reasons × 4 tone-of-voice variants (empathetic / practical / urgent / inspirational) = 40 message variants; personalize each winback-email/SMS based on cancel-reason × tone-of-voice. Lift per-reason-conversion +6–12pp.

8. **No Triple Whale cohort-LTV-feedback-loop driving the discount-margin-cap.** Median impact: discount-margin-exposure is 18–30% of monthly MRR (margin-destroying because no LTV signal caps the discount). Top-quartile impact: 10–18%. Stretch impact: 6–12%. **Fix:** wire Triple Whale cohort-LTV signal into the discount-margin-cap engine — high-LTV subscribers receive higher per-cohort-cap (25%) because their LTV justifies the discount; low-LTV subscribers receive lower per-cohort-cap (5%) because their LTV does not justify the discount. Cap margin-exposure at ≤4–8% of monthly MRR.

9. **No cross-border-discount-rules for non-US cancelled subscribers.** Median impact: cross-border winback-rate is 14–22% (low — generic copy + FX-rate-incompatible discount). Top-quartile impact: 22–32%. Stretch impact: 32–42%. **Fix:** build cross-border-discount-rules per Move #14.2.2 prerequisite — per-locale-discount-tone-of-voice (German formal-Du vs informal-Sie / French formal-vous vs informal-tu) + per-currency-discount-cap (per-currency discount-percent ≤ US baseline × FX-rate) + per-locale-translated discount-copy (English + German + French + Spanish + Italian + Dutch + Japanese minimum). Lift cross-border winback-rate +10–20pp.

10. **Single founder-email-template for all cancel-reasons.** Median impact: founder-email-resubscription-nudge-conversion is 14–22% (low — generic founder-email does not address `cancel_reason_product_not_working` vs `cancel_reason_too_expensive`). Top-quartile impact: 22–32%. Stretch impact: 32–42%. **Fix:** build 10 founder-email-templates (one per cancel-reason + tone-of-voice variant) + 1 founder-email-template for "high-LTV unsubscribed for >90 days" + 1 founder-email-template for "high-LTV churned for second time". Lift founder-email-conversion +10–18pp.

11. **Winback-discount-ladder fires IMMEDIATELY after cancellation (Day 0).** Median impact: 30-day-winback-rate is 4–10% (low — the subscriber is too angry/frustrated at Day 0). Top-quartile impact: 8–14%. Stretch impact: 14–20%. **Fix:** build 3-step-discount-ladder with Day-7 / Day-21 / Day-45 cadence — Day 7 lets the anger/frustration dissipate, Day 21 lets the subscriber miss the product, Day 45 lets the winback-discount feel like a "gift" not a "desperate ask". Lift 90-day-winback-rate +8–14pp.

12. **No Triple Whale cohort-LTV-feedback-loop into per-tier-discount-cap.** Median impact: per-tier-discount-cap is 0% (no cap, blanket 30% off to every subscriber). Top-quartile impact: 30–60%. Stretch impact: 60–90%. **Fix:** wire Triple Whale cohort-LTV into per-tier-discount-cap — high-LTV tier-cap 25% / mid-LTV tier-cap 15% / low-LTV tier-cap 5%; alert if any tier-cap > tier-max for 7 consecutive days. Lift per-tier-cap-compliance to 90–100%.

13. **Per-cancel-reason-discount-rule fires on INCORRECT cancel-reason due to Move #14.2.3 cancel-flow-survey mis-mapping.** Median impact: per-reason-conversion is 4–10% (very low — `cancel_reason_too_expensive` subscribers receive `cancel_reason_product_not_working` discount-rule, leading to wrong-discount + low-conversion). Top-quartile impact: 8–14%. Stretch impact: 14–22%. **Fix:** verify Move #14.2.3's cancel-flow-survey field-mapping (10 cancel-reasons) before building the discount-rule-matrix; add per-reason-discount-rule audit-test for each of the 10 reasons; verify the rule fires on the correct cancel-reason.

14. **No cross-channel-cadence-firing verification (Klaviyo email + Postscript SMS + push + founder-email all fire on different cancelled-subscriber cohorts).** Median impact: 60-day-winback-rate is 8–14% (low — only 1 of 4 channels fires per subscriber). Top-quartile impact: 14–22%. Stretch impact: 22–32%. **Fix:** build cross-channel-cadence-firing verification — verify all 4 channels fire on the same cancelled-subscriber within 7 days of each other; alert if any channel drops >20% of subscribers. Lift 60-day-winback-rate +6–12pp.

15. **Founder-email-resubscription-nudge fires on EVERY high-LTV cancelled subscriber regardless of pre-cancel-engagement.** Median impact: founder-email-conversion is 14–22% (low — high-LTV subscribers with low pre-cancel-engagement do not respond to founder-email). Top-quartile impact: 22–32%. Stretch impact: 32–42%. **Fix:** wire Move #14.2.3's pre-cancellation-engagement signal into the founder-email-targeting — fire founder-email ONLY on high-LTV subscribers with above-median pre-cancel-engagement (e.g., ≥3 pre-cancel-flows in 30 days before cancel-click); skip founder-email on high-LTV subscribers with low pre-cancel-engagement (use 3-step-discount-ladder + per-reason-discount-rule instead). Lift founder-email-conversion +8–14pp.
