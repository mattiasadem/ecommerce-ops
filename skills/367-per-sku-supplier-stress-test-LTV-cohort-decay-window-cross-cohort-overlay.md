## When to use this skill

Use this skill when ALL of the following are true:

1. **Move #358.5.3 per-cohort-LTV-tier-decision-engine has been live for ≥30 days** (the per-cohort-decision-approval-table 5-band is firing daily, ≥100 decisions approved/reviewed per cohort per month).
2. **Move #358.5.2 per-cohort-LTV-tier-decay-window has been live for ≥60 days** (the per-cohort-LTV-tier-decay-window-table 5-band is published daily, ≥95% of cohorts have stable DW1-DW6 firing).
3. **Move #358.5.1 per-cohort-affinity-extension has been live for ≥90 days** (the per-cohort × per-stress-scenario scenario-leak-attribution is computing daily, ≥80% of cohorts have stable leak-rate-vector 7-dim).
4. **Move #358.5 per-region supplier-stress-test-engine has been live for ≥120 days** (the per-region cross-supplier-pool-allocation-engine 5-tuple is recomputed weekly from T-90 days pre-BFCM, ≥80% of regions have stable 5-tuple).
5. **Move #358.4 BFCM-stress-test-engine has been live for ≥150 days** (the per-region BFCM-peak-demand-forecast-vector is computing daily, ≥80% of regions have stable forecast).
6. **Move #358.3 supplier-drop-ship-engine has been live for ≥180 days** (the per-region supplier-drop-ship-pool is recomputed weekly, ≥80% of SKUs have drop-ship-eligibility).
7. **Move #357 per-SKU-OTB-formula has been live for ≥210 days** (the per-SKU OTB budget is published weekly, ≥80% of SKUs have stable OTB).
8. **Move #356 hero-SKU-strategy has been live for ≥240 days** (the per-SKU cohort-affinity attribution is computing daily, ≥80% of SKUs have stable cohort-affinity).
9. **Move #89 BFCM-peak-multiplier has been live for ≥365 days** (the BFCM-peak-multiplier is calibrated from ≥1 full BFCM cycle).
10. **Triple Whale per-cohort attribution is configured ≥80%** (≥80% of orders have per-cohort LTV tier, decay-rate, decay-window-band, decision-routing-band, decision-approval-band, decision-rollback-band, cross-cohort-overlay-band populated).
11. **Polar per-cohort attribution is configured ≥70%** (≥70% of orders have per-cohort LTV tier + cross-cohort-decay-overlay-band populated in Polar's per-cohort view).
12. **Northbeam per-cohort attribution is configured ≥70%** (≥70% of orders have per-cohort LTV tier + cross-cohort-decay-overlay-band populated in Northbeam's per-cohort view).
13. **Operator has detected ≥1 cross-cohort-decision-collision in the last 30 days** (high-LTV-cohort and low-LTV-cohort decision-routings colliding on the same SKU under supplier-stress-test pressure, OR mid-LTV-cohort and low-LTV-cohort decision-routings colliding, OR subscription-cohort and BFCM-peak-cohort decision-routings colliding, OR international-cohort and domestic-cohort decision-routings colliding — i.e. the per-cohort-LTV-tier-decision-engine is firing decisions in conflict on the same SKU).
14. **≥6 distinct LTV-cohort-tiers are active** (high-LTV / mid-LTV / low-LTV / BFCM-peak / subscription / international OR equivalent segmentation with ≥6 tiers — fewer tiers means the cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine has insufficient cells to detect collisions).
15. **≥$3M GMV/year OR ≥$50k MRR OR ≥6k orders/year** (Move #357 OTB engine scale check).

**Do NOT use this skill if** any of the following: (a) Move #358.5.3 has been live for <30 days (insufficient decision data to detect cross-cohort-collisions), (b) operator has <6 distinct LTV-cohort-tiers (the 6 CCD1-CCD6 collision patterns require ≥6 tiers to differentiate), (c) Triple Whale per-cohort attribution is configured <80% (the 38-field event-stream requires ≥80% per-cohort field coverage), (d) Move #181 AI-vendor-orchestration-governance-engine is not live (the cross-cohort-overlay-rebalancing-routing requires AI-vendor-governance validation), (e) Move #182 AI-agent-trust-recovery-engine is not live (the cross-cohort-overlay-decision-rollback requires AI-agent-trust-recovery validation).

## What "best in class" looks like

A best-in-class per-SKU supplier-stress-test-LTV-cohort-decay-window-cross-cohort-overlay has 6 functional pillars:

### Pillar 1 — Per-cohort-LTV-tier cross-cohort-decay-collision-detection-engine 6-pattern CCD1-CCD6

The detection engine continuously scans the Move #358.5.3 Pillar 2 per-cohort-decision-routing + Move #358.5.3 Pillar 3 per-cohort-decision-approval-table 5-band output for cross-cohort-collisions on the same SKU under supplier-stress-test pressure, classifying each collision into 1 of 6 patterns:

| Pattern | Name | Trigger condition | Default severity band |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CCD1** | high-vs-low-decision-band-collision | high-LTV-cohort AUTO-APPROVE + low-LTV-cohort OPERATOR-REVIEW fire on the same SKU within the same Move #358.5.3 Pillar 4 6-sub-rule DW1-DW6 firing window | HARD-COLLISION |
| **CCD2** | mid-vs-low-decision-band-collision | mid-LTV-cohort ONE-CLICK-APPROVE + low-LTV-cohort OPERATOR-REVIEW fire on the same SKU within the same DW1-DW6 window | MEDIUM-COLLISION |
| **CCD3** | subscription-vs-BFCM-peak-decision-band-collision | subscription-cohort AUTO-APPROVE (subscription-ULTRA-SLOW-perpetual-window per Move #358.5.2 Pillar 4 DW5) + BFCM-peak-cohort BFCM-PEAK-FAST-APPROVE (Move #358.5.3 BFCM-PEAK-FAST-APPROVE band) fire on the same SKU within the same DW1-DW6 window | CRITICAL-COLLISION (subscription-cohort perpetual-window must not be overridden by BFCM-peak window-extension) |
| **CCD4** | high-vs-mid-decision-band-collision | high-LTV-cohort AUTO-APPROVE + mid-LTV-cohort ONE-CLICK-APPROVE fire on the same SKU within the same DW1-DW6 window | SOFT-COLLISION (both bands allow automated approval, minimal conflict) |
| **CCD5** | international-vs-domestic-decision-band-collision | international-cohort OPERATOR-REVIEW (Move #25 international-decision-operator-review-routing) + domestic-cohort AUTO-APPROVE fire on the same SKU within the same DW1-DW6 window | MEDIUM-COLLISION (international-decision requires operator-review regardless of LTV tier) |
| **CCD6** | emergency-vs-emergency-decision-band-collision | both cohorts hit EMERGENCY-FREEZE (supplier-stress-test-firing during supplier-outage) on the same SKU within the same DW1-DW6 window | CRITICAL-COLLISION (both cohorts frozen, supplier-stress-test halted — no rebalancing possible) |

The detection engine runs **continuously** (sub-second) on the Move #358.5.3 Pillar 2 decision-routing stream + Move #358.5.2 Pillar 4 DW1-DW6 tuner output, flags each collision with its CCD1-CCD6 classification + default severity band, and feeds the per-cohort × per-supplier × per-region × per-SKU × per-cohort-pair cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine for rebalancing.

### Pillar 2 — Per-cohort × per-supplier × per-region × per-SKU × per-cohort-pair cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine (≥6 cohort-pair bands × 6 CCD1-CCD6 patterns × N LTV-cohort-tiers)

The overlay engine decomposes the Move #358.5.3 Pillar 2 decision-routing + Move #358.5.3 Pillar 3 decision-approval-table output into per-cohort × per-supplier × per-region × per-SKU × per-cohort-pair cells. With ≥6 cohort-pair bands (high-vs-low / high-vs-mid / mid-vs-low / subscription-vs-BFCM-peak / high-vs-international / emergency-vs-emergency) × 6 CCD1-CCD6 patterns × N LTV-cohort-tiers (≥6 tiers), the engine produces at minimum **216 cells** (6 cohort-pair bands × 6 patterns × 6 tiers), but in practice produces **C(N,2) × 6 × 6 = 1080+ cells** (≥6 cohorts → 15 cohort-pairs × 6 patterns × 6 tiers × 1 region × 1 supplier × 1 SKU). Each cell carries the CCD1-CCD6 classification + default severity band + cross-cohort-decay-overlay-band (NO-COLLISION / SOFT-COLLISION / MEDIUM-COLLISION / HARD-COLLISION / CRITICAL-COLLISION) + the recommended cross-cohort-overlay-rebalancing-engine 6-sub-rule CRO1-CRO6 chain.

The engine recomputes **per Move #358.5.3 Pillar 4 6-sub-rule DW1-DW6 firing** (i.e. daily at minimum, more frequently during BFCM-peak or supplier-outage scenarios), and stores the **1080+ cells** to the per-cohort-LTV-tier cross-cohort-decay-overlay-table 5-band (Pillar 3).

### Pillar 3 — Per-cohort-LTV-tier cross-cohort-decay-overlay-table 5-band

The overlay-table classifies each cell's collision severity into 1 of 5 bands:

| Band | Definition | Default CRO routing |
|---|---|---|
| **NO-COLLISION** | 0% of cohort-pair decisions collide on the same SKU | (no rebalancing) |
| **SOFT-COLLISION** | 1-25% of cohort-pair decisions collide | CRO2 (mid-LTV-blend) for soft-conflicts, no escalation |
| **MEDIUM-COLLISION** | 25-50% of cohort-pair decisions collide | CRO2+CRO3 blend + operator-dashboard-amber-flag |
| **HARD-COLLISION** | 50-75% of cohort-pair decisions collide | CRO1+CRO3 split (high-LTV-protect / low-LTV-yield) + operator-dashboard-red-flag + Triple-Whale-event-stream |
| **CRITICAL-COLLISION** | >75% of cohort-pair decisions collide | CRO6 immediate-freeze (supplier-stress-test halted) + CFO-board-pack-alert + Move #182 AI-agent-trust-recovery-trigger |

The table publishes **per cohort-pair × per CCD1-CCD6 pattern × per LTV-cohort-tier × per region × per supplier × per SKU** with a confidence-band on each cell's collision-severity classification (default: ≥95% CI).

### Pillar 4 — Per-cohort-LTV-tier cross-cohort-decay-overlay-rebalancing-engine 6-sub-rule CRO1-CRO6

The rebalancing engine fires per CCD1-CCD6 collision-pattern detection and applies a 6-sub-rule chain to resolve the collision:

| Sub-rule | Name | Fires when | Default action chain |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CRO1** | high-LTV-cohort-protect-routing | CCD1, CCD4, CCD5 high-LTV cohort side | high-LTV-cohort-decision-protect 60-70% of Move #358.5.3 Pillar 4 DW1-DW6 firing weight, suppress mid/low-LTV override on same SKU |
| **CRO2** | mid-LTV-cohort-blend-routing | CCD2, CCD4 mid-LTV cohort side | mid-LTV-cohort-decision-blend 30-50% of DW1-DW6 firing weight, one-click-approve + operator-dashboard-yellow-flag |
| **CRO3** | low-LTV-cohort-yield-routing | CCD1, CCD2 low-LTV cohort side | low-LTV-cohort-decision-yield 40-50% of DW1-DW6 firing weight, operator-review-100% + Move #107 competitive-price-intelligence-validation |
| **CRO4** | subscription-cohort-perpetual-routing | CCD3 subscription cohort side | subscription-cohort-decision-perpetual-window 70% of DW1-DW6 firing weight, suppress BFCM-peak override (subscription-ULTRA-SLOW-perpetual-window per Move #358.5.2 Pillar 4 DW5) |

### Pillar 5 — Per-cohort-LTV-tier cross-cohort-decay-overlay-cost-amortization-engine 5-tuple

The cost-amortization engine computes the **net economic impact** of the cross-cohort-overlay-rebalancing per cohort-pair per CCD1-CCD6 pattern per LTV-cohort-tier per region per supplier per SKU, producing a 5-tuple:

1. **overlay_capital_locked_usd** — capital locked by the CRO1-CRO6 rebalancing (high-LTV-protect 60% × per-SKU-OTB-budget × cohort-LTV-multiplier, etc.)
2. **overlay_daily_amortized_cost** — daily amortized cost of the locked capital (capital_locked_usd × daily-amortization-rate)
3. **overlay_recovery_capital** — capital recovered from the loser-cohort (low-LTV-yield 40% × per-SKU-OTB-budget × cohort-LTV-multiplier in CCD1, etc.)
4. **overlay_net_avoided_loss** — net LTV-loss avoided by the CRO1-CRO6 rebalancing vs. no-rebalancing baseline (computed via Move #154 predictive-LTV-churn-engine + Move #107 competitive-price-intelligence + Move #103 per-SKU-margin)

## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

### P1 — ship-without-per-cohort-LTV-tier-cross-cohort-decay-collision-detection-engine-6-pattern-CCD1-CCD6

**Symptom:** The cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine fires CRO1-CRO6 rebalancing without first detecting the CCD1-CCD6 collision-pattern classification, leading to misrouted rebalancing (e.g. CCD1 high-vs-low firing CRO4+CRO5 instead of CRO1+CRO3). The 6 CCD1-CCD6 patterns (CCD1 high-vs-low / CCD2 mid-vs-low / CCD3 subscription-vs-BFCM-peak / CCD4 high-vs-mid / CCD5 international-vs-domestic / CCD6 emergency-vs-emergency) MUST be classified BEFORE CRO1-CRO6 fires.

**Fix:** Build Pillar 1 cross-cohort-decay-collision-detection-engine 6-pattern CCD1-CCD6 as the FIRST pipeline stage, ingest Move #358.5.3 Pillar 2 decision-routing + Move #358.5.3 Pillar 3 decision-approval-table output continuously, classify each collision into CCD1-CCD6, THEN hand off to Pillar 4 cross-cohort-decay-overlay-rebalancing-engine 6-sub-rule CRO1-CRO6.

### P2 — ship-without-N-cohort-LTV-tiers-min-6

**Symptom:** The cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine has insufficient cells to detect the 6 CCD1-CCD6 collision patterns because the operator has <6 distinct LTV-cohort-tiers (e.g. only high/mid/low = 3 tiers). The 6 CCD1-CCD6 patterns require ≥6 distinct tiers to differentiate (CCD1 high-vs-low needs high AND low distinct tiers, CCD3 subscription-vs-BFCM-peak needs subscription AND BFCM-peak distinct tiers, CCD5 international-vs-domestic needs international AND domestic distinct tiers).

**Fix:** Enforce the ≥6 LTV-cohort-tiers threshold in the cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine configuration — if the operator has <6 tiers, refuse to ship (return `tier_count_below_threshold_error`). Move #154 predictive-LTV-churn-engine + Move #119 per-cohort-attribution-decision-engine provide the LTV-cohort-tier segmentation.

### P3 — ship-without-per-cohort × per-supplier × per-region × per-SKU × per-cohort-pair-cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine-1080+-cells

**Symptom:** The cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine has fewer than 1080 cells (the canonical ≥6 cohort-pair bands × 6 CCD1-CCD6 patterns × N LTV-cohort-tiers × 1 region × 1 supplier × 1 SKU decomposition). Operators with 6+ tiers, 6+ cohort-pair bands, 6+ CCD1-CCD6 patterns need 1080+ cells minimum. Insufficient cells = blind spots in cross-cohort-decay-collision-detection.

**Fix:** Build Pillar 2 cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine with the canonical 216+ cell minimum (6 cohort-pair bands × 6 patterns × 6 tiers) and validate ≥1080 cells in practice (≥6 cohorts → C(6,2) = 15 cohort-pairs × 6 patterns × 6 tiers × 1 region × 1 supplier × 1 SKU = 540 cells minimum, more with multi-region/multi-supplier).

### P4 — no-CCD3-subscription-vs-BFCM-peak-decision-band-collision-CRO4+CRO5-priority-lock-routing

**Symptom:** The cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine detects CCD3 subscription-vs-BFCM-peak-decision-band-collision (the canonical subscription-ULTRA-SLOW-perpetual-window per Move #358.5.2 Pillar 4 DW5 + BFCM-PEAK-FAST-APPROVE per Move #358.5.3 Pillar 3 colliding on the same SKU) but fails to fire CRO4+CRO5 priority-lock (subscription-perpetual 70% / BFCM-peak-fast 30%). Instead, the engine treats CCD3 as a SOFT-COLLISION and applies CRO2+CRO3 blend, which destroys the subscription-cohort perpetual-window guarantee.

**Fix:** CCD3 is a CRITICAL-COLLISION by default (subscription-cohort perpetual-window must not be overridden by BFCM-peak window-extension). The CRO4+CRO5 priority-lock is MANDATORY for CCD3 — CRO4 subscription-cohort-perpetual-routing takes 70% of DW1-DW6 firing weight, CRO5 BFCM-peak-cohort-fast-routing takes 30%, no override of CRO4's subscription-perpetual-window.

### P5 — no-CCD6-emergency-vs-emergency-decision-band-collision-CRO6-immediate-freeze-routing

**Symptom:** The cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine detects CCD6 emergency-vs-emergency-decision-band-collision (both cohorts hit EMERGENCY-FREEZE per Move #358.5.3 Pillar 3 on the same SKU) but fails to fire CRO6 immediate-freeze (100% supplier-stress-test halt). Instead, the engine attempts to rebalance emergency-vs-emergency, which is impossible (both cohorts frozen) and creates a deadlock.

**Fix:** CCD6 is a CRITICAL-COLLISION with NO rebalancing possible. CRO6 immediate-freeze is MANDATORY for CCD6 — 100% supplier-stress-test halt, both cohorts frozen, Move #358.5 Pillar 5 supplier-stress-test-halt triggered, Move #181 AI-vendor-orchestration-validation required before unfreezing.

### P6 — no-confidence-band-on-cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine-cell-severity-classification

**Symptom:** The cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine publishes the per-cohort × per-cohort-pair × per-CCD1-CCD6-pattern cell severity classification (HARD-COLLISION / MEDIUM-COLLISION / etc.) without a confidence-band. Operators can't distinguish high-confidence collisions (≥95% CI) from low-confidence ones (75% CI), leading to over-routing on noise (CCD4 SOFT-COLLISION firing CRO1+CRO2 cascade with 75% CI is misleading).

**Fix:** Add a confidence-band (default ≥95% CI) on each cell's severity classification. Ingest the confidence-band from Move #358.5.2 Pillar 1 per-cohort-LTV-tier-decay-rate-vector 6-dim + Move #358.5.1 Pillar 4 per-cohort supplier-stress-test-leak-rate-vector 7-dim + Move #358.5.3 Pillar 2 decision-routing-confidence. If confidence < 80%, downgrade the cell to NO-COLLISION (do not fire CRO1-CRO6 on noise).

### P7 — no-Move-#358.5.3-per-cohort-LTV-tier-decision-engine-integration

**Symptom:** The cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine fires CRO1-CRO6 without ingesting the Move #358.5.3 Pillar 2 per-cohort-decision-routing + Move #358.5.3 Pillar 3 per-cohort-decision-approval-table 5-band output. Without this integration, the cross-cohort-overlay-engine has no per-cohort-decision-band signal to detect collisions on (CCD1-CCD6 trigger conditions all reference Move #358.5.3 Pillar 2/3).

**Fix:** Wire the cross-cohort-decay-collision-detection-engine to Move #358.5.3 Pillar 2 decision-routing + Pillar 3 decision-approval-table 5-band. The CCD1-CCD6 trigger conditions are: (a) both cohort-decision-bands appear on the same SKU, (b) within the same Move #358.5.3 Pillar 4 6-sub-rule DW1-DW6 firing window, (c) under supplier-stress-test pressure.

### P8 — no-Move-#358.5.2-per-cohort-LTV-tier-decay-window-integration

**Symptom:** The cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine fires CRO1-CRO6 without ingesting the Move #358.5.2 Pillar 4 6-sub-rule DW1-DW6 decay-rollback-window-tuner output. Without this integration, the cross-cohort-overlay-engine can't time-align CCD1-CCD6 detection to the DW1-DW6 firing window (a CCD1 detected before DW1 fires is a false-positive; a CCD1 detected after DW6 closes is a missed signal).

**Fix:** Wire the cross-cohort-decay-collision-detection-engine to Move #358.5.2 Pillar 4 6-sub-rule DW1-DW6 decay-rollback-window-tuner. CCD1-CCD6 detection MUST fire WITHIN the DW1-DW6 firing window (default: same-day, ±24h tolerance). Cross-cohort-overlay-engine publishes a `dw_window_alignment_status` field per cell (aligned / late / missed).

### P9 — no-Move-#358.5.1-per-cohort supplier-stress-test-leak-rate-vector-integration

**Symptom:** The cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine fires CRO1-CRO6 without ingesting the Move #358.5.1 Pillar 4 per-cohort supplier-stress-test-leak-rate-vector 7-dim. Without this integration, the CRO1-CRO6 firing-weight-split (CCD1 CRO1 60% / CRO3 40%) has no per-cohort leak-rate signal to validate the split against (high-leak-rate cohorts may need higher CRO1 protection, low-leak-rate cohorts may tolerate more CRO3 yield-routing).

**Fix:** Wire the cross-cohort-decay-overlay-rebalancing-engine to Move #358.5.1 Pillar 4 per-cohort supplier-stress-test-leak-rate-vector 7-dim. The CRO1-CRO6 firing-weight-split adjusts dynamically: high-leak-rate high-LTV cohort → CRO1 70%/CRO3 30% (more protection); low-leak-rate low-LTV cohort → CRO1 50%/CRO3 50% (more yield).

### P10 — no-Move-#154-predictive-LTV-churn-engine-integration

**Symptom:** The cross-cohort-decay-overlay-cost-amortization-engine 5-tuple computes `overlay_net_avoided_loss` without ingesting the Move #154 predictive-LTV-churn-engine LTV-decay predictions. Without this integration, the cost-amortization-engine under-counts the LTV-loss avoided by CRO1-CRO6 rebalancing (the avoided-loss is the difference between "LTV-loss if no rebalancing" from Move #154 and "LTV-loss with rebalancing" from the actual CRO1-CRO6 outcome).

**Fix:** Wire the cost-amortization-engine to Move #154 predictive-LTV-churn-engine + Move #107 competitive-price-intelligence + Move #103 per-SKU-margin. The `overlay_net_avoided_loss` field MUST reference all three sources (Move #154 for LTV-decay-prediction, Move #107 for competitive-substitution-loss, Move #103 for per-SKU-margin-amortization).

### P11 — no-Move-#89-BFCM-peak-multiplier-integration-on-CCD3-BFCM-peak-side

**Symptom:** The cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine fires CRO4+CRO5 on CCD3 subscription-vs-BFCM-peak-decision-band-collision without applying the Move #89 BFCM-peak-multiplier on the CRO5 BFCM-peak-cohort-fast-routing side. ### P12 — no-Move-#358.5-per-region-supplier-stress-test-engine-integration

### P18 — ship-without-Move-#182-AI-agent-trust-recovery-integration-on-CRO1-CRO6-misfire

**Symptom:** The cross-cohort-decay-overlay-engine fires CRO1-CRO6 rebalancing but fails to detect misfires (e.g. CRO1+CRO3 60/40 firing on CCD1 high-vs-low but the LTV-loss-after-rebalancing > LTV-loss-before-rebalancing).
