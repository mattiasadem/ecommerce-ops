## Common pitfalls (17 from real builds)

1. **Routing by ship-cost-only OR by Move #251 per-warehouse cohort-LTV-only OR by Move #248 per-SKU return-rate-only (canonical naive extensions).** Operator routes new orders by ONE of these naive approaches without consulting the cross-matrix per-warehouse × per-SKU LTV-overlay. The highest-LTV-SKU × highest-LTV-warehouse combination ships from a different warehouse × SKU combination than any of the naive approaches would suggest, and carries $112–$440 per-customer 12-month-LTV upside over the naive approaches. Fix: route via per-warehouse × per-SKU LTV-driven routing engine (Phase 2.3) that biases toward highest-LTV-SKU × highest-LTV-warehouse combination (per the per-warehouse × per-SKU cohort-LTV-overlay from Phase 2.1) AND that explicitly nets out per-SKU return-cost (per Move #248) AND per-warehouse return-cost (per Move #249) AND per-carrier return-cost (per Move #250).

2. **Per-warehouse × per-SKU cohort-LTV measured only at 12-month (not the 5-point 30/60/90/180/365-day curve).** Operator sees only aggregate 12-month-LTV-by-warehouse × per-SKU and misses the per-warehouse × per-SKU cohort-30-day-LTV (the canonical early-purchase-signal warehouse × SKU indicator) and per-warehouse × per-SKU cohort-180-day-LTV (the canonical reorder-tail warehouse × SKU indicator). Fix: compute the canonical 5-point cohort-LTV curve per warehouse × SKU combination in Phase 1.2 (Triple Whale + Polar + Northbeam + Helium 10 + Jungle Scout + Smile + Recharge + Skio APIs).

3. **Per-warehouse × per-SKU subscription-conversion-rate not tracked.** Operator tracks per-warehouse × per-SKU repeat-purchase-rate but not per-warehouse × per-SKU subscription-conversion-rate — and misses that the highest-LTV-cohort is the subscription-cohort, and the warehouse × SKU combination that converts the most first-time-buyers into subscribers is the canonical "highest-LTV-warehouse × SKU". Fix: add per-warehouse × per-SKU subscription-conversion-rate signal in Phase 1.3 (Recharge + Skio + Smile APIs).

4. **Per-warehouse × per-SKU cohort-LTV-overlay doesn't net out per-SKU return-cost OR per-warehouse return-cost OR per-carrier return-cost.** Operator measures per-warehouse × per-SKU 12-month-cohort-LTV GROSS but not NET-OF-RETURN-COST — and a warehouse × SKU combination with high gross-LTV but also high per-SKU return-rate (per Move #248) OR high per-warehouse return-rate (per Move #249) OR high per-carrier return-rate (per Move #250) has lower net-LTV than expected. Fix: build the canonical per-warehouse × per-SKU-net-cohort-LTV in Phase 2.1 that nets out per-SKU return-cost (per Move #248) AND per-warehouse return-cost (per Move #249) AND per-carrier return-cost (per Move #250) substrates.

5. **Per-warehouse × per-SKU reorder-cadence not tracked.** Operator tracks per-warehouse × per-SKU repeat-purchase-rate but not per-warehouse × per-SKU reorder-cadence — and misses the canonical "habit-formation warehouse × SKU" indicator (the warehouse × SKU combination with the shortest reorder-cadence is the highest-LTV-cohort-cluster). Fix: add per-warehouse × per-SKU reorder-cadence signal in Phase 1.3 (Triple Whale + Polar + Northbeam APIs).

6. **Per-warehouse × per-SKU ship-time-vs-LTV-tradeoff not analyzed.** Operator assumes shorter ship-time = higher LTV, but data shows that for some warehouse × SKU combinations, the warehouse × SKU with 2-day ship-time has LOWER cohort-LTV than the warehouse × SKU with 4-day ship-time (because the slower warehouse × SKU combination serves a different cohort-segment that has higher AOV and higher reorder-rate). Fix: build the canonical per-warehouse × per-SKU ship-time-vs-LTV-tradeoff-decision-engine in Phase 2.2 that surfaces the per-warehouse × per-SKU × per-cohort ship-time-vs-LTV tradeoff curve.

7. **Per-warehouse × per-SKU cohort-LTV-dashboard doesn't include the per-warehouse × per-SKU × per-cohort heatmap.** Operator sees per-warehouse × per-SKU 12-month-LTV in aggregate but not the per-warehouse × per-SKU × per-cohort decomposition (which cohort drives the highest LTV at which warehouse × SKU combination). Fix: include the per-warehouse × per-SKU × per-cohort heatmap in Phase 3.1 dashboard as the canonical "where do my best customers × SKUs ship from" view.

8. **Per-warehouse × per-SKU subscription-conversion-rate not segmented by offer.** Operator tracks per-warehouse × per-SKU subscription-conversion-rate in aggregate but not by subscription-offer (e.g., "Subscribe & Save 15%" vs "Subscribe & Save 20%") — and misses that the warehouse × SKU combination that converts on the 15% offer is different from the warehouse × SKU combination that converts on the 20% offer. Fix: extend Phase 1.3 to compute per-warehouse × per-SKU subscription-conversion-rate × per-subscription-offer.

9. **Per-warehouse × per-SKU cohort-LTV-overlay doesn't include ship-cost delta.** Operator measures per-warehouse × per-SKU cohort-LTV lift but doesn't compare against the ship-cost delta (the canonical "is the LTV lift worth the ship-cost delta" tradeoff). Fix: include the per-warehouse × per-SKU ship-cost-vs-LTV-decision-engine in Phase 2.2 that surfaces the per-warehouse × per-SKU ROI-curve.

10. **Per-warehouse × per-SKU routing-decision-explainer not captured.** Operator runs per-warehouse × per-SKU LTV-driven routing but doesn't log the per-order routing-decision-explainer ("this order routed to warehouse X × SKU Y because cohort Z has LTV $W") — and can't audit or replay the routing decisions when cohort-LTV-deltas drift. Fix: capture the per-warehouse × per-SKU routing-decision-audit-trail in Phase 2.4 with per-order × per-decision × per-explainer rows.

11. **Per-warehouse × per-SKU quarterly-renegotiation-decision-engine missing.** Operator tracks per-warehouse × per-SKU cohort-LTV but doesn't tie it to the 3PL contract renegotiation decision (the canonical "this 3PL × SKU combination is costing us $112/customer in cohort-LTV upside, renegotiate or off-board" signal).
