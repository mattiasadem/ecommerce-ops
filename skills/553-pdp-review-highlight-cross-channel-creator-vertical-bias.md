## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — Confusion between creator-tier (T1–T8) and creator-vertical (CV1–CV8).** Creator-tier is the canonical 8-tier follower-count taxonomy (T1 nano <10k + T2 mid 10-50k + T3 mid 50-100k + T4 macro 100k-1M + T5 niche 100k-500k + T6 celebrity 500k-1M + T7 specialist 1M-5M + T8 micro-niche 5M+); creator-vertical is the canonical 8-tier vertical taxonomy (CV1 fashion + CV2 beauty + CV3 home + CV4 fitness + CV5 food + CV6 pet + CV7 lifestyle + CV8 electronics). A CV1 fashion creator on T2 mid tier is a mid-tier fashion creator (different snippet-grammar + ATC-rate than a CV1 fashion creator on T8 micro-niche tier). **Fix:** the canonical 16-source library reads CV-tier and creator-tier as ORTHOGONAL axes; the per-arm 36-dimension readback v14 bundles BOTH; the LLM-judge resonance scorer scores BOTH independently; the bandit picks from a CV-tier × creator-tier cross-axis review pool, not a CV-tier-only pool.

2. **Pitfall — Treating creator-vertical as a single axis when it is orthogonal to creator-tier + CCF-tier + W-tier + DM-tier + PN-tier + ER-tier + AE-tier + LG-tier + CR-tier + CA-tier + FT-tier + CC-tier + CF-tier.** The creator-vertical axis is one of FOURTEEN cross-axes; the per-arm 36-dimension readback v14 bundles ALL FOURTEEN. **Fix:** implement the per-CV-tier × per-CCF-tier + per-CV-tier × per-PN-tier + per-CV-tier × per-DM-tier + per-CV-tier × per-W-tier + per-CV-tier × per-ER-tier + per-CV-tier × per-AE-tier + per-CV-tier × per-LG-tier + per-CV-tier × per-CR-tier + per-CV-tier × per-CA-tier + per-CV-tier × per-FT-tier + per-CV-tier × per-CC-tier + per-CV-tier × per-CF-tier cross-axes ALL at once; the LLM prompt stack injects ALL FOURTEEN cross-axis snippets into the prompt, not just the CV-tier snippet.

3. **Pitfall — Confusing the 8-tier creator-vertical taxonomy with brand-specific product categories.** A fashion-brand SKU might be tagged "CV1 fashion" at the creator-vertical level but tagged "dress" or "shoes" or "outerwear" at the product-category level; these are ORTHOGONAL. **Fix:** the per-arm 36-dimension readback v14 bundles BOTH creator-vertical-tier (CV1–CV8) AND product-category (sku-large-tag) as separate fields; the LLM-judge resonance scorer scores BOTH independently.

4. **Pitfall — Hardcoding CV-tier ATC-rates when they should be per-cohort × per-channel × per-creator-tier dynamic.** The baseline ATC-rate per CV-tier (1.25x / 1.20x / 1.18x / 1.22x / 1.15x / 1.12x / 1.16x / 1.14x) is a baseline; per CaptivateA Vertical ROI 2026 + Lumanu Vertical 2026, the actual ATC-rate varies by cohort × channel × creator-tier × region. **Fix:** the bandit-friendly ATC-rate is `baseline_cv × cohort_correction × channel_correction × creator_tier_correction × region_correction`; the per-arm 36-dimension readback v14 carries the corrections as separate fields.
