# AI orchestration per channel (Move #90)

> Run one **per-channel AI orchestration matrix** that maps every revenue and engagement surface in a DTC operator's stack (24 channels × 6 AI functions × 8 cohorts × 4 LTV tiers = 4,608 cells, of which ~120 are routinely exercised) to a single canonical execution layer: (a) **per-channel-AI-content-variant-engine** (the AI picks subject-line / preview / hero-image / product-card / ATC-copy / PDP-bullet / ad-headline / ad-primary-text / video-script per channel × cohort × LTV-tier), (b) **per-channel-AI-send-time-engine** (the AI picks send-time / bid-floor / frequency-cap / creative-rotation per channel × cohort × LTV-tier), (c) **per-channel-AI-product-recommendation-engine** (the AI picks product-card-set / upsell / cross-sell / bundle per channel × cohort × LTV-tier), (d) **per-channel-AI-cohort-overlay** (the AI wires the channel-output to the cohort-tag in Triple Whale / Polar / Northbeam / Klaviyo / Iterable / Braze / Segment / mParticle / Mixpanel / Amplitude so every AI decision is measurable at the per-cohort × per-LTV-tier level), (e) **per-channel-AI-eval + per-channel-AI-guardrail + per-channel-AI-observability + per-channel-AI-cost-control** (the AI layer is gated by Patronus / DeepEval / Ragas / Braintrust / LangSmith / Arize Phoenix / Maxim eval + Lakera Guard / Prompt Armor / NeMo Guardrails / Azure AI Content Safety / Rebuff / Vigil / Promptfoo / Garak / PyRIT guardrails + Langfuse / Helicone / Arize Phoenix / Honeycomb / Datadog LLM Observability observability + per-token-cost-routing per-tier-model-tier GPT-4o / Claude Sonnet 4.5 / Claude Haiku 4.5 / Gemini 2.0 Flash / Llama 3.3 70B / Mixtral 8x22B / DeepSeek V3 / Qwen 2.5 / Mistral Large 2), and (f) **per-channel-AI-cohort-LTV-overlay** that compounds Move #18 generative-AI-engine + Move #77 agentic-AI-operations + Move #11 AI-CS-automation + Move #34 CX-platform + Move #44 VOC + Move #61 AI-personalization + Move #47 growth-experimentation + Move #36 Move #90 is the canonical **per-channel AI execution matrix** every $1M+ GMV DTC operator needs AFTER Move #1 (cart-abandon) + Move #4 (welcome) + Move #6 (Triple-Whale) + Move #7 (SMS) + Move #9 (mobile-PDP) + Move #11 (AI-CS) + Move #18 (generative-AI) + Move #34 (CX-platform) + Move #47 (experimentation) + Move #61 (personalization) + Move #77 (agentic-AI) are live OR the operator is doing ≥$1M GMV AND has AI-features-turned-on in at least 3 of [Klaviyo AI / Iterable AI / Braze AI / Cordial AI / Attentive AI / HubSpot AI / Salesforce Einstein / Shopify Magic / BigCommerce AI / Magento Adobe Sensei] AND cannot answer "what is our per-channel-AI-content-variant-rate AND what is our per-channel-AI-send-time-personalization-rate AND what is our per-channel-AI-product-recommendation-CTR AND what is our per-channel-AI-cohort-LTV-overlay-rate AND what is our per-channel-AI-token-cost-per-resolution AND what is our per-channel-AI-guardrail-violation-rate?" within 60 seconds.

## When to use this skill

Use this skill the first time any of these is true:

- the operator has **AI-features enabled in ≥3 channels** (Klaviyo AI subject-line / Klaviyo AI send-time / Klaviyo AI product-recommendation / Iterable AI send-time / Braze AI content / Cordial AI / Attentive AI / HubSpot AI content / Salesforce Einstein send-time / Salesforce Einstein engagement-scoring / Shopify Magic PDP-description / Shopify Magic email / BigCommerce AI / Magento Adobe Sensei) but has **no canonical per-channel AI execution matrix** — every channel makes its own AI-decisions in isolation, the AI-output is siloed, the operator cannot tell which channel's AI is producing measurable lift vs which is silently degrading deliverability / CVR / CTR / unsubscribe-rate;
- the operator has **AI-content being generated by LLM** (Move #18 generative-AI-engine — subject-line / preview / hero-image / ad-creative / PDP-bullet / video-script) but the AI-output is **not mapped per-channel × per-cohort × per-LTV-tier** — the same subject-line is sent to a $50-LTV buyer and a $5,000-LTV VIP, the same ad-creative is shown to a 1st-time-visitor and a 30th-time-visitor, the AI is making one-decision-per-piece-of-content instead of one-decision-per-channel-per-cohort-per-LTV-tier;
- the operator has **AI-product-recommendations** being generated (Rebuy / Nosto / Dynamic Yield / LimeSpot / Shopify Magic product-recommendations / Klaviyo AI product-recommendations / Iterable AI product-recommendations / Braze AI product-recommendations) but the recommendations are **not differentiated per-channel** — the same product-card-set is shown on the PDP / in the cart / in the abandoned-cart email / in the SMS / in the push / in the paid-Meta ad / in the paid-Google ad / in the Amazon listing / in the TikTok-Shop listing, when the canonical Move #9 mobile-PDP-redesign + Move #61 AI-personalization + Move #47 growth-experimentation research shows **per-channel product-card-set differentiation lifts conversion 10-25%** vs the same-card-set-on-all-channels baseline;
- the operator has **AI-send-time-personalization** enabled (Klaviyo AI send-time / Iterable AI send-time / Braze AI send-time / Salesforce Einstein send-time) but the send-time-decisions are **not gated by per-channel deliverability-guardrails** — the AI picks 3am send-time for a high-LTV cohort which lifts open-rate but tanks spam-complaint-rate / unsubscribe-rate / sender-reputation, the canonical Move #36 email-deliverability-sender-reputation pitfall;

- the operator has **AI-cohort-detection** (Move #61 AI-personalization + Segment AI audiences / mParticle AI audiences / Amplitude AI audiences / Mixpanel AI audiences / Klaviyo AI segments / Iterable AI segments / Braze AI segments / HubSpot AI segments / Salesforce Einstein engagement-scoring) but the AI-cohort-decisions are **not synced across channels** — Klaviyo thinks the customer is "high-intent-cart-abandon", Meta thinks they're "cold-audience-lookalike", Google thinks they're "in-market-comparator", the AI is making 4-different-cohort-decisions per customer per channel;

## What "best in class" looks like

A best-in-class per-channel AI orchestration engine has **all 6 of these**:

1. **per-channel-AI-content-variant-engine that is gated by per-cohort × per-LTV-tier** — the AI picks subject-line / preview / hero-image / product-card / ATC-copy / PDP-bullet / ad-headline / ad-primary-text / video-script per channel × per-cohort × per-LTV-tier; the same email-subject-line is NOT sent to a $50-LTV buyer and a $5,000-LTV VIP, the same ad-headline is NOT shown to a 1st-time-visitor and a 30th-time-visitor, the AI is making one-decision-per-channel-per-cohort-per-LTV-tier instead of one-decision-per-piece-of-content.
2. **per-channel-AI-send-time-engine that is gated by per-channel deliverability-guardrails** — the AI picks send-time / bid-floor / frequency-cap / creative-rotation per channel × per-cohort × per-LTV-tier; the send-time is gated by the Move #36 email-deliverability-sender-reputation thresholds (no 3am-send-time-on-cold-cohort that triggers spam-rate-spike, no 11pm-send-time-on-VIP-cohort that triggers unsubscribe-rate-spike), the bid-floor is gated by the per-cohort-LTV (no $5-bid-floor on a $50-LTV cohort, no $50-bid-floor on a $5,000-LTV VIP).
3. **per-channel-AI-product-recommendation-engine that is gated by per-channel-funnel-position** — the AI picks product-card-set / upsell / cross-sell / bundle per channel × per-cohort × per-LTV-tier × per-funnel-position (TOFU / MOFU / BOFU); the PDP-cross-sell is different from the cart-cross-sell which is different from the abandoned-cart-email-cross-sell which is different from the post-purchase-cross-sell which is different from the win-back-cross-sell which is different from the VIP-cross-sell; the recommendation-engine reads the per-channel-funnel-position and per-cohort-LTV-tier and outputs a channel-cohort-LTV-position-aware card-set.
4. **per-channel-AI-cohort-overlay that is wired to Triple Whale / Polar / Northbeam / Klaviyo / Iterable / Braze / Segment / mParticle / Mixpanel / Amplitude** — the AI's per-channel-decisions are tagged with the per-customer cohort-LTV-tier in Triple Whale / Polar / Northbeam; the operator can answer "what is the per-channel × per-cohort × per-LTV-tier AI-decision-rate AND what is the per-channel × per-cohort × per-LTV-tier AI-content-CTR AND what is the per-channel × per-cohort × per-LTV-tier AI-send-time-open-rate AND what is the per-channel × per-cohort × per-LTV-tier AI-product-recommendation-CTR AND what is the per-channel × per-cohort × per-LTV-tier AI-token-cost-per-resolution" within 60 seconds.
5. **per-channel-AI-eval + per-channel-AI-guardrail + per-channel-AI-observability + per-channel-AI-cost-control** — every AI-decision is gated by per-channel-eval (Patronus / DeepEval / Ragas / Braintrust / LangSmith / Arize Phoenix / Maxim measuring per-channel-AI-precision / per-channel-AI-recall / per-channel-AI-deflection-rate / per-channel-AI-fallback-rate / per-channel-AI-handoff-context-loss-rate) + per-channel-AI-guardrail (Lakera Guard / Prompt Armor / NeMo Guardrails / Azure AI Content Safety / Rebuff / Vigil / Promptfoo / Garak / PyRIT measuring per-channel-AI-prompt-injection-defense / per-channel-AI-jailbreak-test / per-channel-AI-output-redaction) + per-channel-AI-observability (Langfuse / Helicone / Arize Phoenix / Honeycomb / Datadog LLM Observability measuring per-channel-AI-trace / per-channel-AI-token-cost / per-channel-AI-latency / per-channel-AI-cost-per-resolution) + per-channel-AI-cost-control (per-channel-token-budget + per-channel-cost-per-resolution + per-channel-cost-per-action + per-channel-model-routing-rule + per-tier-model-tier).
6. **per-channel-AI-monthly-quality-scorecard** — the operator runs a monthly scorecard that scores all 24 channels × 6 AI-functions × 8 cohorts × 4 LTV-tiers against per-channel-AI-content-variant-rate / per-channel-AI-send-time-personalization-rate / per-channel-AI-product-recommendation-CTR / per-channel-AI-bid-floor-personalization-rate / per-channel-AI-creative-rotation-rate / per-channel-AI-cohort-LTV-overlay-rate / per-channel-AI-eval-pass-rate / per-channel-AI-guardrail-violation-rate / per-channel-AI-token-cost-per-resolution / per-channel-AI-deflection-rate / per-channel-AI-fallback-rate; the scorecard is the canonical single-pane-of-glass for per-channel AI execution health.
