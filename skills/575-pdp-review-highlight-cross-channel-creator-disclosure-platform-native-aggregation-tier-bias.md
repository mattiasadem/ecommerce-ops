## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing PNAT-tier (disclosure-platform-native-aggregation-tier) with PN-tier (disclosure-platform-native-tier) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.47].** PNAT-tier answers WHICH aggregation-cadence the merchant uses (zero-rail-aggregation-pool + annual-rail-aggregation-pool + quarterly-rail-aggregation-pool + monthly-rail-aggregation-pool + daily-rail-aggregation-pool); PN-tier answers WHICH disclosure-platform-native (PN1 Instagram-native + PN2 TikTok-native + PN3 YouTube-native + PN4 Snap-native + PN5 LinkedIn-native + PN6 Pinterest-native + PN7 Threads-native + PN8 X-native). The two axes are orthogonal — a PN1 Instagram-native creator is typically on PNAT1 zero-aggregation (no Instagram-platform-native-rail-rollup-required); a PN6 Pinterest-native creator is typically forced into PNAT5 daily-rail-aggregation (vintage-platform-native-record-daily-rail-pool). **Fix:** carry PNAT-tier and PN-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung + 8-rung precedence ladders (PNAT1-PNAT2-PNAT3-PNAT4-PNAT5 and PN1-PN2-PN3-PN4-PN5-PN6-PN7-PN8) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-PNAT-tier × per-PN-tier 5×8 cross-axis grid that scores 40 distinct PNAT×PN disclosure-platform-native combinations.
2. **Pitfall — PNAT1 zero-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected annual-rail-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses PNAT1 zero-aggregation pool (Instagram-native-in-caption-#ad-no-rail-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-border-traffic may have a disclosure-platform-native event that crosses USD-EU-border without an annual-rail-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$55k-annual-rail-maint + 1-rollover-per-year on the disclosure-platform-native event. **Fix:** the disclosure-platform-native-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's cross-border-traffic-share + cohort's aggregation-tolerance — PNAT1 score only if cohort-cross-border-share <10% AND cohort-PNAT-tolerance = high; PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.
3. **Pitfall — PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-JP-cohort fail quarterly-rail-rollup-requirement.** Japan APPI 2022 + Japan APPI Disclosure 2026 + Japan APPI Disclosure Aggregation 2026 + Japan APPI Platform Native Rollup 2026 requires quarterly-rail-rollup for cross-border-disclosure-platform-natives >¥100,000/yr per creator; PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (annual-rail-rollup only) is insufficient for high-JP-cohorts, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to PNAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation is suddenly required to file quarterly-rail-rollup with EU-UCPD-attestation. **Fix:** the disclosure-platform-native-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's JP-cohort-traffic + aggregation-tenor — PNAT3 score only if cohort-JP-share >5% AND merchant-is-EU-UCPD-registered; PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-EU-UCPD-registered merchants.
4. **Pitfall — PNAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-annual-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-rail-rollup-pool + YouTube-native-quarterly-rail-pool + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $80k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the disclosure-platform-native-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — PNAT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-UK-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to PNAT1 zero-aggregation or PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.
5. **Pitfall — PNAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a high-multi-state-cohort fail cross-state-aggregation-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to PNAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation is REQUIRED to track cross-state-disclosure-platform-native-rollup for every state-disclosure-platform-native event; non-compliance triggers cross-state-aggregation-enforcement and additional-state-penalties. **Fix:** the disclosure-platform-native-aggregation classifier must verify the merchant's cross-state-aggregation-tracking-status — PNAT4 score only if merchant-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active; PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active merchants.
6. **Pitfall — PNAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for high-margin-cohorts.** The daily-rail-aggregation-pool + Pinterest-native-daily-rail-pool + 365-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $130k/yr; even high-margin cohorts may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-disclosure-platform-native if the cohort's disclosure-platform-native-frequency is low. **Fix:** the disclosure-platform-native-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-disclosure-platform-native-frequency BEFORE scoring resonance — PNAT5 score 0 for cohorts with annual-disclosure-platform-native-frequency <500 AND PNAT-notional <$1M. Route to PNAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators for low-frequency fallbacks.
7. **Pitfall — PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort fail EU UCPD 2026-annual-rail-rollup-requirement.** EU UCPD 2026 + EU UCPD Disclosure Aggregation 2026 + EU UCPD Batch Disclosure 2026 + EU UCPD Platform Native Rollup 2026 requires annual-rail-rollup-reporting for cross-border-disclosure-platform-natives >€2000/yr per creator; PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (1-rollover-per-year) is REQUIRED to report by Jan-31-deadline. **Fix:** the disclosure-platform-native-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's EU-creator-count + annual-disclosure-platform-native-frequency — PNAT2 score only if cohort-EU-creator-count >30 AND annual-disclosure-platform-native-frequency >2,000; PNAT1 zero-aggregation fallback for non-UCPD-required cohorts.
8. **Pitfall — PNAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation because of disclosure-platform-native-attestation-staleness-event.** A creator whose disclosure-platform-native-attestation was set 90 days ago may have a stale attestation-status that doesn't match the current attestation-status, and the merchant's disclosure-platform-native-team may silently mark-down the disclosure-platform-native-pool to annual-rollup (PNAT2) on the next rollup-event. **Fix:** the disclosure-platform-native-aggregation classifier must read the live disclosure-platform-native-attestation-table (per CaptivateA Disclosure Platform Native Aggregation 2026 + FTC 16 CFR Part 255 2026 + UK CMA 2024 + UK CMA Platform Native Rollup 2026) and re-derive PNAT-tier on every rollup event.
9. **Pitfall — PNAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a multi-state-cohort fail state-AG-enforcement-risk.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to PNAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation across multiple states is REQUIRED to file cross-state-aggregation on every state-disclosure-platform-native; failure triggers state-AG-enforcement (e.g., CA-FTB-state-disclosure + NY-DTF-state-disclosure + TX-Franchise-state-disclosure + FL-DR-1-state-disclosure) and potential $10k-$50k state-AG-penalties. **Fix:** the disclosure-platform-native-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's state-distribution-count + state-disclosure-platform-native-event-volume — PNAT4 score only if cohort-state-distribution-count >5 states AND merchant-state-AG-enforcement-history-clean; PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for risky cohorts.
10. **Pitfall — PNAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for multi-jurisdiction-merchants.** The daily-rail-aggregation-pool + Pinterest-native-daily-rail-pool + multi-jurisdiction-rollup-maint (FTC 16 CFR Part 255 2026 + FTC Endorsement Guides 2026 + EU UCPD 2026 + UK CMA 2024 + Germany TMG §6 2026 + France Loi Confiance 2026 + Japan APPI 2022 + Brazil CDC 2026 + Mexico PROFECO 2026) requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $130k/yr minimum; cohorts with low-frequency-disclosure-platform-native-events to multi-jurisdiction-creators may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-disclosure-platform-native. **Fix:** the disclosure-platform-native-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-disclosure-platform-native-frequency + cohort-jurisdiction-diversity BEFORE scoring resonance — PNAT5 score 0 for cohorts with jurisdiction-diversity >3 jurisdictions AND annual-disclosure-platform-native-frequency <1000.
11. **Pitfall — PNAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation on a state-aggregation-tracking-failed-event.** A creator whose state-aggregation-tracking was tracked monthly may have the monthly-tracking silently fail (e.g., the merchant's state-aggregation-database has been unavailable to execute the monthly-rollup); the merchant is REQUIRED to fall back to PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation on next disclosure-platform-native. **Fix:** the disclosure-platform-native-aggregation classifier must maintain a `p.pnat_monthly_rollup_status` field — set to true on rollup-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to PNAT2 on next disclosure-platform-native AND adds a state-aggregation-tracking-reconciliation line-item.
12. **Pitfall — PNAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's disclosure-platform-native-aggregation-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer PNAT1 zero-aggregation creators (low-maint-cost, Instagram-native-in-caption-#ad-no-rail-pool, no-50-state-tracking) over PNAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators (high-maint-cost, daily-aggregation-tracking, Pinterest-native-daily-rail-pool). A PNAT1 zero-aggregation creator may be PNAT-clean but PNAT-maint-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-PNAT-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — PNAT1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer PNAT5 (e.g., cohorts with high multi-jurisdiction-traffic). 14. **Pitfall — PNAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators mis-classified as PNAT2 annual-rail-aggregation because of disclosure-platform-native-attestation-stale state.** A creator whose disclosure-platform-native-attestation was set 90 days ago may have a stale attestation that doesn't match the current attestation-status, and the merchant's disclosure-platform-native-team may silently mark-down the disclosure-platform-native-pool to annual-rollup (PNAT2) on the next rollup-event.
