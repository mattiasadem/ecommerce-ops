## Common pitfalls (23)

1. **P1 — Don't wire per-cohort-affinity-identity-provider-IdP-engine-version telemetry against the wrong IdP-event-stream.** Auth0-IdP emits to a different event-stream than Okta-IdP / Cognito-IdP / WorkOS-IdP / Stytch-IdP / Clerk-IdP; wire to the IdP-version's specific event-stream endpoint (e.g. Auth0 Log Streams endpoint vs Okta System Log endpoint vs Cognito CloudTrail Logs endpoint vs WorkOS Audit Logs endpoint). A wire-test verifies ≥ 1 event/100-checkout-attempts per IdP-engine-version.
2. **P2 — 35 NEW septen-centesimal-axis event-streams must be wired FIRST.** Don't instrument funnel-telemetry AFTER dashboards are built — telemetry freshness is required for layer-1 to be load-bearing. Each of the 35 septen-centesimal-axis dimensions must emit ≥ 1 value per 100 checkout-attempts cross-cohort, cross-LTV-tier.
3. **P3 — Trust-engine-version-stale is a customer-data-fan-out risk, not just a leak-rate risk.** Sift-Trust vs Arkose-Trust vs Shape-Trust vs HUMAN-Trust emit trust-score-events with different schema; mis-wiring can cause hidden customer-trust-drift → checkout-abuse. Wire-test verifies ≥ 1 trust-event/100-checkout-attempts per trust-engine-version with a per-engine-version schema-validator.
4. **P4 — Compliance-engine-version vs audit-trail-engine-version vs privacy-engine-version are THREE different sub-cell-variants, NOT the same.** Vanta-Compliance emits different event-streams for /compliance vs /audit-trail vs /privacy endpoints; merging them loses per-cohort-affinity-compliance-engine-version-resolution. Wire-test verifies per-endpoint funnel-telemetry with separate event-stream IDs.
5. **P5 — Privacy-engine-version vs consent-engine-version vs DPA-engine-version vs DSR-engine-version are FOUR different engines.** OneTrust-Privacy vs OneTrust-Consent vs OneTrust-DPA vs OneTrust-DSR are NOT interchangeable — a "privacy" applies to global-policy-control, a "consent" applies to per-user-cookie-tracking, a "DPA" applies to data-processing-agreement, a "DSR" applies to data-subject-request. Confusing them causes over-attribution or under-attribution leak-cells.
6. **P6 — Tax-rules-engine-version and tax-calculation-engine-version and tax-filing-engine-version and tax-exemption-engine-version are EVENT-DRIVEN, not POLLING-DRIVEN.** Avalara-TaxRules emits per-tax-event events; Avalara-TaxCalculation emits per-tax-calculation events; Avalara-TaxFiling emits per-tax-filing events; Avalara-TaxExemption emits per-tax-exemption events. Polling-driven funnel-telemetry loses per-event freshness; use event-stream subscriber.
7. **P7 — Don't conflate payment-method-routing-rules-engine-version with payment-tokenization-engine-version.** Adyen-PaymentMethodRoutingRules / Stripe-PaymentMethodRoutingRules is the ROUTING decision (which payment-method to use), NOT the TOKENIZATION engine (Stripe-PaymentTokenization / Adyen-PaymentTokenization). The Move #3.48 septen-centesimal-axis covers BOTH; payment-method-routing is a Move #3.48 payment-method-routing-rules-engine-version dimension and payment-tokenization is a separate Move #3.48 payment-tokenization-engine-version dimension.
