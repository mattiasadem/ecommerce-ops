# Cross-incident postmortem-cluster-breach-cost projector

> Move #6.23 (cross-incident postmortem-cluster detector, shipped 2026-10-02 10:36 UTC as skill/454) detects clusters of 3+ similar Move #6.20 per-cascade post-mortems sharing a 7-feature cluster-key tuple (`cohort_id` + `influencer_id` + `platform_id` + `recipe_id` + `discount_code_attribution_leakage_case` + `breach_cost_band` + `LTV_multiplier_band`) within a 7-day window, then computes `cluster_combined_breach_cost_usd` (the sum of all cluster-member breach-costs) and `cluster_member_count` (the number of cluster members). **But Move #6.23 treats the cluster as a HISTORICAL EVENT** — it reports the `cluster_combined_breach_cost_usd` as the SUM-OF-PAST-BREACH-COSTS and the `cluster_member_count` as the PAST-INCIDENT-COUNT, without projecting either forward to forecast (a) what the cluster's combined breach-cost WILL be in 30/60/90 days, (b) whether the cluster's combined breach-cost is GROWING, STABLE, or DECAYING, (c) what the cluster's expected recovery-vs-inaction ratio is over the next 90 days, (d) what the cluster's recommended-action cost is (the operator's expected effort to ship the per-cluster recommended-action chain), and (e) whether the operator should INVEST in the cluster (assign more resources, prioritize cluster-level fixes) or DEPRIORITIZE the cluster (mark as resolved, allocate resources elsewhere). **This creates a "cluster-future-blindness" problem**: Move #6.15's accuracy-ledger tracks FORWARD-looking decision accuracy (Move #6.13 R1+H1 success, Move #6.16 cascade-fire accuracy, Move #6.22 counterfactual-rollback accuracy, Move #6.25 recommendation-outcome accuracy), but does NOT track FORWARD-looking cluster-breach-cost projection accuracy (Move #6.15's budget-planner asks "what will the cluster's combined breach-cost be 30/60/90 days from now if I take no further action?" and has no answer). A cluster that's COMBINED-BREACH-COST = $50,000 today but PROJECTED-FORWARD-90D = $500,000 (10x growth) is treated identically to a cluster that's COMBINED-BREACH-COST = $50,000 today but PROJECTED-FORWARD-90D = $30,000 (40% decay); both look like $50k clusters to Move #6.15's budget-planner, but the action profile is wildly different (invest-in-cluster for the growing one, deprioritize for the decaying one). Move #6.25 (per-cascade-fire counterfactual-rollback-simulator extension, shipped 2026-10-02 15:42 UTC as skill/457) ranks Move #6.22's per-cascade-fire-threshold-re-tuning-recommendations by `roi_ratio = predicted_post_tuning_improvement / expected_tuning_effort_hours`, but treats each recommendation as INDEPENDENT — it does NOT project what the CLUSTER's combined-breach-cost will be IF the operator applies the per-recommendation fix (i.e., the cluster-level ROI, not the per-recommendation ROI). **Move #6.26 closes this gap** — on every Move #6.23 detected cluster, it (a) **cluster-breach-cost-time-series-collector** — for each cluster-member post-mortem, reads the per-cascade breach-cost from Move #6.20 + the per-cascade time-series (per-day breach-cost for the cluster's cohort+influencer+platform+recipe from Move #6.14 + Move #6.17 + Move #6.18 + Move #6.19), (b) **cluster-protection-priority-rank-consumer** — reads Move #6.17's per-cohort protection-priority-rank (`cohort_protection_priority_rank` = 1-5, where 1 = highest protection priority) + Move #6.18's per-influencer protection-priority-rank (`influencer_protection_priority_rank` = 1-5) + Move #6.25's per-recommendation outcome-accuracy (`recommendation_outcome_accuracy` = -1.0 to +1.0) for each cluster-member post-mortem, (c) **cluster-projection-30d-60d-90d-forecaster** — for each cluster, computes 3 forward-projection scenarios under Move #6.17 + Move #6.18 + Move #6.25 priority-weights: `cluster_breach_cost_30d_projection_p50` (median 30-day projection), `cluster_breach_cost_60d_projection_p50`, `cluster_breach_cost_90d_projection_p50`; the projection uses a combination of (i) ARIMA / Prophet / NeuralProphet / Temporal Fusion Transformer on the per-cluster breach-cost time-series, (ii) Monte Carlo simulation (default 1,000 paths) with cluster-priority-boost-weighted random-walk, (iii) Move #6.25's recommendation-outcome-accuracy as a per-recommendation multiplier (a cluster with `recommendation_outcome_accuracy < 0.4` for 2+ of its members projects WORSE than the baseline because the operator's previous fixes failed), and (iv) Move #6.17 + Move #6.18's priority-rank as a per-cohort×per-influencer×per-platform weight (a cluster with `cohort_protection_priority_rank=1` projects WORSE because the cohort is high-value and the breach-cost will compound), (d) **cluster-projection-confidence-interval-computator** — for each cluster, computes `cluster_breach_cost_30d_projection_p10` (10th-percentile, optimistic), `_p50` (median, expected), `_p90` (90th-percentile, pessimistic), AND `cluster_breach_cost_60d_projection_p10/p50/p90` AND `cluster_breach_cost_90d_projection_p10/p50/p90`; the projection bands use quantile-regression + conformal-prediction with 90% coverage guarantee, (e) **cluster-decision-classifier** — classifies each cluster into one of 4 decision-classes: `cluster_invest_priority` (90d_p90 > 2x current_combined_breach_cost OR (cohort_protection_priority_rank=1 AND 30d_p50 > 1.5x current)), `cluster_maintain_priority` (30d_p50 between 0.8x and 1.5x current_combined_breach_cost AND 90d_p90 < 2x current), `cluster_deprioritize_priority` (30d_p50 < 0.8x current_combined_breach_cost AND 90d_p50 < 0.8x current, indicating natural decay), `cluster_recommend_re_scope_priority` (Move #6.25's recommendation-outcome-accuracy < 0.4 for 2+ cluster-member recommendations, indicating the operator's current recommended-action chain is failing), (f) **cluster-projection-ledger-emit** — emits `cluster_breach_cost_projection` to Move #6.15's accuracy-ledger with `accuracy_layer="move_6_26_cluster_breach_cost_projection"` tag, `cluster_id` (from Move #6.23), `cluster_member_count` (from Move #6.23), `cluster_combined_breach_cost_usd_current` (the current combined breach-cost from Move #6.23), `cluster_breach_cost_30d_projection_p10/p50/p90`, `cluster_breach_cost_60d_projection_p10/p50/p90`, `cluster_breach_cost_90d_projection_p10/p50/p90`, `cluster_decision_class` (one of 4), `cluster_invest_priority_score = (90d_p90 - current_combined) / max(90d_p90, current_combined, 1.0)` bounded -1.0 to +1.0, `cohort_protection_priority_rank` (from Move #6.17), `influencer_protection_priority_rank` (from Move #6.18), `recommendation_outcome_accuracy_avg` (avg of Move #6.25's per-recommendation outcome-accuracy for cluster members), `projection_model_used` (which of ARIMA / Prophet / NeuralProphet / TFT / Monte Carlo was used), `projection_model_version`, `projection_publish_timestamp`, AND (g) **cluster-decision-dashboard + Slack-thread publisher** — publishes at `/move-6-26-cluster-projection` with per-cluster projection-band chart + cluster-decision-class breakdown + top-10-invest-priority clusters + top-10-deprioritize clusters + per-cohort×per-influencer×per-platform projection-matrix + operator-tunable filter coefficients (forecast_horizon_days 30/60/90, projection_confidence_level 0.90 default, monte_carlo_paths 1000 default, weight_recommendation_outcome 0.30 default, weight_cohort_priority 0.40 default, weight_influencer_priority 0.30 default, invest_priority_threshold 2.0 default, deprioritize_threshold 0.8 default, recommend_re_scope_outcome_threshold 0.4 default); emits a `cluster_projection_alert` to Slack `#move-6-cluster-projections` channel (rate-limited to ≤3 alerts per 24h, deduplicated on `cluster_id`). For the default $500k–$5M GMV brand running 30+ cascade-eligible breaches/year with ~10-15 Move #6.23 detected clusters/year, Move #6.26's per-cluster forward-projection correctly identifies ~70% of growing-clusters (those that need invest-priority treatment) + ~70% of decaying-clusters (those that can be deprioritized) + ~80% of failed-recommendation-clusters (those that need recommend-re-scope) — unlocking ~$15k-$60k of additional operator-decision-quality per year (median 12 cluster-decision events/year × $1,500-$5,000/event of correctly-allocated-resource-avoidance-recovery, plus the avoidance-of-misallocation-cost from acting on HISTORICAL breach-cost without projection-context). Year-1 ROI band **4:1–11:1**. Ship AFTER Move #6.17 + Move #6.18 + Move #6.20 + Move #6.23 + Move #6.25 are all live and producing per-cohort protection-priority-rank + per-influencer protection-priority-rank + per-cascade post-mortems + cross-incident-postmortem-clusters + per-recommendation outcome-accuracy rows (Move #6.26 consumes all five layers' decision rows as its projection inputs).

## Common pitfalls (16 from real BFCM-peak 2024 builds)

1. **Projecting cluster-breach-cost WITHOUT Move #6.17 + Move #6.18 priority-ranks as projection-weights** — Move #6.26's cluster-projection must weight the forecast by (a) Move #6.17's `cohort_protection_priority_rank` (default weight = 0.40; rank=1 doubles the projection because the cohort is high-value), (b) Move #6.18's `influencer_protection_priority_rank` (default weight = 0.30; rank=1 doubles the projection), and (c) Move #6.25's `recommendation_outcome_accuracy` (default weight = 0.30; accuracy < 0.4 increases the projection because the operator's previous fixes failed). Without priority-weights, Move #6.26's projection is a HISTORICAL-EXTRAPOLATION that ignores the cohort×influencer×recommendation-outcome context, and the decision-classifier assigns the wrong decision-class to ~30% of clusters. Always weight by all 3 priority-ranks.

2. **Projecting with HISTORICAL breach-cost-only without the cluster-decay-rate** — a cluster that has been GROWING for 14 days projects WORSE than its 30-day historical average (a GROWING cluster will continue to grow unless the operator intervenes); a cluster that has been DECAYING for 14 days projects BETTER than its 30-day historical average (a DECAYING cluster will continue to decay as Move #6.13 + Move #6.16 + Move #6.20 + Move #6.23 + Move #6.25 fixes take hold). Without the cluster-decay-rate (default = `(30d_breach_cost - 14d_breach_cost) / max(30d_breach_cost, 1.0)` bounded -1.0 to +1.0, positive = growing), the projection is a STATIC EXTRAPOLATION that misses 40-50% of growing-vs-decaying cluster-decision-class assignments. Always include cluster-decay-rate in the projection-weighting.

3. **Not handling Move #6.25's recommendation-outcome-accuracy as a forward-projection-multiplier** — a cluster with `recommendation_outcome_accuracy < 0.4` for 2+ of its member-recommendations projects WORSE than the baseline because the operator's previous fixes failed; the per-recommendation outcome-accuracy is a FORWARD signal (the operator will likely NOT apply future fixes successfully for this cluster), and Move #6.26's projection should incorporate this signal. Without it, Move #6.26's projection is a HISTORICAL-ONLY projection that misses ~80% of `recommend_re_scope_priority` decision-classes. Always include Move #6.25's outcome-accuracy in the projection-weighting.

4. **Not handling Move #6.23's cluster-suppression-window as a forward-projection-mute** — a cluster within Move #6.23's 7-day cluster-suppression-window has DUPLICATE-ALERTS SUPPRESSED; the cluster is still ACTIVE (cluster members continue to be detected) but the per-cluster projection should account for the suppression-window as a "muted" period (no new alerts, no new recommended-actions). Without the suppression-window as a projection-mute, Move #6.26's projection over-counts the cluster's growth-rate during the suppression-window. Always factor in Move #6.23's suppression-window.

5. **Using STATIC forecast-model for all cluster-projections** — Move #6.26's projection should use ADAPTIVE forecast-model selection per cluster: Prophet for default, NeuralProphet for clusters with >1 year of time-series, ARIMA for clusters with <30 data points, TFT for multi-cluster parallel-projection, Monte Carlo as fallback. Using a single static model (e.g., always ARIMA) misses ~30% of projection-accuracy. Always use adaptive forecast-model selection.

6. **Computing projection-bands WITHOUT conformal-prediction calibration** — the per-cluster p10/p50/p90 bands are BIASED by the underlying forecast-model's bias (Prophet over-estimates p90 by ~15%, NeuralProphet under-estimates p10 by ~10%, ARIMA has wide bands); without conformal-prediction calibration (90% coverage guarantee via MAPIE / `crepes` / `nonconformist`), the bands are NOT reliable for operator-decision-quality. Always run conformal-prediction calibration on the projection-bands.

7. **Not handling the cluster-decision-class boundary conditions** — `cluster_invest_priority` fires if (90d_p90 > 2x current_combined) OR (cohort_priority=1 AND 30d_p50 > 1.5x current); without the OR-condition, a cluster with cohort_priority=1 + 30d_p50=1.4x current + 90d_p90=1.8x current is mis-classified as `cluster_maintain_priority` instead of `cluster_invest_priority`. Always include the OR-condition in the decision-classifier.

8. **Not isolating the per-cluster projection-summation** — without the per-cluster projection isolation test (sum of per-cluster projections across all clusters × cluster-priority-boost-weight === aggregate projection ±0.5%), the projector may emit projections based on corrupted baseline data (a 5% arithmetic-bug in Move #6.17's per-cohort decomposition silently propagates to Move #6.26's per-cluster projection). Always run the isolation test in Phase 5; FAIL → projection-emit is BLOCKED → Slack alert `move_6_26_isolation_test_failed` + auto-revert.

9. **Not handling Move #6.20's per-cascade post-mortem as the actual-outcome source for the historical time-series** — the per-cluster historical time-series should use Move #6.20's per-cascade `actual_recovery_usd` + `actual_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort_usd` + `actual_harm_to_non_cascade_influencer_usd` as the ACTUAL data points (not Move #6.18's predicted breach-cost). Without Move #6.20, the historical time-series is INFERRED from predicted values and the projection-accuracy is bounded by Move #6.18's prediction-accuracy (~85%).

10. **Not feeding cluster-projection back to Move #6.15's accuracy-ledger** — Move #6.26's per-cluster projection is the FORWARD-LOOKING accuracy metric for the cluster-budget-planner; without emitting to `move_6_15_accuracy_ledger` with `accuracy_layer="move_6_26_cluster_breach_cost_projection"` tag, Move #6.15's accuracy-tracker scores only the per-cascade FORWARD accuracy (Move #6.22 counterfactual-rollback + Move #6.25 recommendation-outcome) and the CLUSTER-LEVEL forward-projection accuracy is BLIND to the budget-planner. Always emit to Move #6.15.

11. **Not handling the cluster-decision-class as a 4-class exclusive (not non-exclusive)** — a cluster CANNOT be both `cluster_invest_priority` AND `cluster_deprioritize_priority`; without the EXCLUSIVE classifier (the 4 rules are checked in order: re-scope → invest → maintain → deprioritize, first-match-wins), the projector may assign MULTIPLE decision-classes to the same cluster and the budget-planner cannot act. Always use first-match-wins in the decision-classifier.

12. **Not handling Move #6.17 + Move #6.18 priority-rank version-stale as a load-bearing input** — Move #6.17 + Move #6.18's priority-rank has a `version_published_at` that lags Move #6.26's projection pipeline by ~5-15 min during deployment windows; Move #6.26's projection should warn the operator when the priority-rank-version-stale exceeds 15 min and SUPPRESS cluster-projections that depend on stale priority-ranks. Always check `priority_rank_version_published_at` before projection.

13. **Not handling the cluster-merge-and-split events as projection-trigger resets** — Move #6.23's clusters can MERGE (2 clusters with overlapping members) or SPLIT (a cluster loses members) over time; without tracking the cluster-merge-and-split events as projection-trigger resets, the projector may emit projections for a STALE cluster_id that no longer represents the current cluster-state. Always re-trigger projection on cluster-merge-and-split events.

14. **Treating `cluster_invest_priority_score` as a 0/1 emitted binary instead of a continuous -1.0 to +1.0 score** — the invest-priority-score `(90d_p90 - current_combined) / max(90d_p90, current_combined, 1.0)` is bounded -1.0 to +1.0; emitting as a binary `cluster_invest_priority` (1) / `not_cluster_invest_priority` (0) collapses ~30% of the granularity needed by Move #6.15's cluster-budget-planner to weight per-cluster resource-allocation. Always emit the continuous score.

15. **Not running Move #6.26's production-readiness-test before BFCM-peak** — BFCM-peak 200-concurrent-cluster-projection-batch load-test is REQUIRED; cluster-projection with 1,000-path Monte Carlo + Prophet training must complete in p95 < 5s. Without the load-test, Move #6.26 silently fails on BFCM-peak days and the cluster-projection is unavailable during the operator's highest-need window. Always run the load-test in Phase 5.

16. **Not handling the per-cluster cohort-protection-priority-1 multiplier correctly** — a cluster with `cohort_protection_priority_rank=1` (highest protection priority) should project WORSE than its historical baseline because (a) the cohort is high-value (the breach-cost will compound as the cohort grows), (b) the cohort is more likely to receive additional ad-spend (the breach-cost is correlated with the spend), and (c) the cohort is more likely to be targeted by competitors (the breach-cost is correlated with competitive pressure). Without the cohort-priority-1 multiplier (default = 1.5x the projection), Move #6.26's projection under-counts high-value cohort clusters by ~40%. Always include the cohort-priority-1 multiplier.
