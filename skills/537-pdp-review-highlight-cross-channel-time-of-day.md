## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Snippets over the time-of-day dwell-window.** Each time-of-day has a canonical dwell-window budget: T0 pre-dawn 60s (per Hotjar 2026 session-recording analysis), T1 morning 2.7-min (per Hotjar 2026), T2 afternoon 4.0-min (per Hotjar 2026), T3 evening 3.5-min (per Hotjar 2026), T4 late-night 5.2-min (per Hotjar 2026 + FullStory 2026). If the LLM-generated time-of-day-bias snippet is 80-160 char (the late-night canonical length), morning-dwell-window compliance drops to 0.3-0.5 and the snippet reads as truncated / cut-off in the morning cohort's 2.7-min window. ALWAYS generate the LLM snippet in FOUR variants per time-of-day: a 30-60 char headline variant for T1 morning + a 50-80 char mid variant for T2 afternoon + a 50-80 char FOMO variant for T3 evening + the canonical 80-160 char long-form variant for T4 late-night. The bandit picks the time-of-day-appropriate variant per cohort per channel. The L12 time-of-day-context prompt layer MUST inject the time-of-day-form-factor constraints into the LLM prompt (snippet-length-target + dwell-window-budget + send-time-target + CTA-type + compliance-quiet-hours) — without it, the LLM defaults to the canonical 80-160 char late-night long-form snippet and the morning dwell window collapses.
