## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

1. **Lumping all free-shipping SKUs into a single "free-shipping-positive" bucket.** The canonical free-shipping cohort carries materially different completion-rate-leak-rate profiles across free-shipping-no-min / free-shipping-min-25 / free-shipping-min-50 / free-shipping-min-99 (free-shipping-no-min 80-92%, free-shipping-min-25 76-89%, free-shipping-min-50 72-86%, free-shipping-min-99 60-75%) and requires separate fix-recommendations. Free-shipping-min-99 specifically carries the highest leak-rate (25-40%) among the free-shipping cohorts because customers expect no-minimum free shipping and may abandon cart when seeing $99-minimum-requirement — a counter-fix (e.g. enable-shipping-threshold-display-engine or enable-free-shipping-progress-bar-engine). **Fix:** keep the canonical 4 free-shipping-min cohorts as separate cohorts; do not collapse into "free-shipping-positive" or "no-free-shipping" buckets.

2. **Confusing shipping-policy-cohort with returns-policy-cohort or trust-signal-cohort.** Move #3.15 is shipping-policy-cohort = which-shipping-policy-is-rendered-on-the-checkout (9-cohort taxonomy); returns-policy-cohort = which-returns-policy-is-rendered (the canonical 8-cohort taxonomy, covered by Move #3.14); trust-signal-cohort = which-trust-badge-is-rendered (the canonical 12-cohort taxonomy, covered by Move #3.12). Move #3.15 ≠ Move #3.14 ≠ Move #3.12. **Fix:** keep the canonical 9 shipping-policy-cohorts as a per-rendered-shipping-policy taxonomy, NOT per-source or per-platform.

3. **Treating flat-rate-shipping as the same as weight-based-shipping.** Flat-rate-shipping = customer pays a flat rate (e.g. $7.95 per order) regardless of weight. Weight-based-shipping = customer pays a per-pound rate (e.g. $1.50/lb). Completion-rate differs (flat-rate-shipping 55-70% vs weight-based-shipping 42-58%) because high-AOV customers are deterred by weight-based fees on heavy items (e.g. 5lb at $1.50/lb = $7.50 feels manageable; 50lb at $1.50/lb = $75 feels prohibitive). **Fix:** keep flat-rate-shipping-cohort and weight-based-shipping-cohort as separate cohorts; do not collapse.

4. **Treating carrier-calculated-shipping as the same as flat-rate-shipping.** Carrier-calculated-shipping = shipping rate from carrier API (UPS/FedEx/USPS/DHL) computed at checkout based on weight/zone/dims. Flat-rate-shipping = fixed rate regardless of carrier calculation. Each carries different completion-rate (carrier-calculated-shipping 50-66% vs flat-rate-shipping 55-70%) because carrier-calculated-shipping reveals the true shipping cost (which can be higher than flat-rate for distant zones). **Fix:** keep the canonical carrier-calculated-shipping cohort distinct from flat-rate-shipping.

5. **Treating store-pickup as the same as carrier-curated-shipping.** Store-pickup = customer picks up at local store / BOPIS / curbside (no shipping carrier). Carrier-calculated-shipping = carrier delivery. Each carries different completion-rate-leak-rate profiles (store-pickup 60-78% vs carrier-calculated-shipping 50-66%) because store-pickup-availability and store-hours-affect-customer-confidence. **Fix:** keep the canonical store-pickup cohort distinct from carrier-calculated-shipping; the canonical 9-cohort taxonomy uses store-pickup as the cohort that benefits most from enable-store-pickup-availability-engine + enable-click-collect-engine.

6. **Treating dropship-direct-from-supplier as the same as carrier-calculated-shipping.** Dropship-direct-from-supplier = order ships directly from supplier (no warehouse, longer ETA). Carrier-calculated-shipping = ships from warehouse via carrier. Each carries different completion-rate-leak-rate profiles (dropship-direct-from-supplier 22-40% vs carrier-calculated-shipping 50-66%) and different LTV impact (dropship-direct-from-supplier drives +5-12% LTV due to wider catalog selection but loses 30-50% of cart-abandon-on-long-ETA, carrier-calculated-shipping drives +5-15% LTV). **Fix:** keep the canonical dropship-direct-from-supplier cohort distinct from carrier-calculated-shipping; the canonical 9-cohort taxonomy uses dropship-direct-from-supplier as the cohort that benefits most from enable-supplier-eta-display-engine + enable-dropship-trust-badge-engine.

7. **Treating shipping-policy-cohort as a top-level checkout-element rather than a per-rendered-shipping-policy taxonomy.** Move #3.15's shipping-policy-cohort taxonomy is per-rendered-shipping-policy-on-the-checkout, NOT per-checkout-element. The canonical 9 shipping-policy-cohorts are: free-shipping-no-min + free-shipping-min-25 + free-shipping-min-50 + free-shipping-min-99 + flat-rate-shipping + weight-based-shipping + carrier-calculated-shipping + store-pickup + dropship-direct-from-supplier. Each cohort is rendered at a specific checkout-step (e.g. shipping-policy-summary at cart-step, shipping-policy-fee at shipping-step, shipping-policy-eta at confirmation-step). **Fix:** tag each per-shipping-policy-cohort-encounter with the rendered-checkout-step, not just "checkout-level".

8. **Single-A/B-test against an aggregate shipping-policy-cohort-prominence-engine rather than per-shipping-policy-cohort A/B-test.** The canonical 42 per-shipping-policy-cohort-fix-recommendations are per-cohort-specific (F1 enable-shipping-policy-cohort-prominence-engine targets dropship-direct-from-supplier specifically; F2 enable-shipping-policy-cohort-free-shipping-min-disclosure-engine targets free-shipping-min-99 specifically). A single A/B-test against an aggregate shipping-policy-cohort-prominence-engine would dilute the per-cohort effect and miss the +15-30% completion-rate-uplift on the targeted cohort. **Fix:** run per-shipping-policy-cohort A/B-tests, NOT an aggregate A/B-test.

9. **Treating shipping-policy-cohort A/B-test as a low-power test.** Per-shipping-policy-cohort A/B-tests require minimum-detectable-effect of 3-5% (per Move #47) and runtime of 7-21 days. Low-power A/B-tests (e.g. 100 events per cohort) produce false-positive completions. **Fix:** set per-shipping-policy-cohort A/B-test minimum sample size to ≥1,000 events per cohort; runtime 7-21 days; statistical-significance threshold 95%.

10. **Ignoring shipping-policy-cohort-mobile-cohort-confusion.** Mobile-cohort customers see shipping-policy at 50-75% smaller render area than desktop-cohort customers (per Move #3.4 device-cohort + Triple-Whale-Shipping-Cohort-2024). The shipping-policy-cohort-render-event on mobile-cohort must be tagged with mobile-render-area; if not, the shipping-policy-cohort-mobile-experience-gap-counter fires incorrectly. **Fix:** tag every shipping-policy-cohort-render-event with mobile-render-area + mobile-touch-target-44px-engine-status.

11. **Treating shipping-policy-cohort attribution-double-counting as a global issue.** Shipping-policy-cohort attribution-double-counting happens when a single checkout-step-attempt is tagged with multiple shipping-policy-cohorts (e.g. carrier-calculated-shipping + weight-based-shipping + dropship-direct-from-supplier all rendering simultaneously). Triple-Whale per-shipping-policy-cohort-attribution must apply canonical dedup-rules (last-touch-wins + shipping-policy-cohort-priority-rank). **Fix:** apply canonical shipping-policy-cohort-priority-rank (free-shipping-no-min > free-shipping-min-25 > free-shipping-min-50 > free-shipping-min-99 > flat-rate-shipping > carrier-calculated-shipping > store-pickup > weight-based-shipping > dropship-direct-from-supplier).

12. **Ignoring shipping-policy-cohort-cohort-LTV-decision.** Per-shipping-policy-cohort-cohort-LTV-decision differs from per-shipping-policy-cohort-LTV. Shipping-policy-cohort-cohort-LTV = sum(customer-LTV × cohort-weight) per-shipping-policy-cohort — the canonical 50-cell matrix (per-customer-LTV-tier × per-shipping-policy-cohort). Operators who only track per-shipping-policy-cohort-LTV miss the cohort-mix-shift that drives +10-28% LTV-uplift. **Fix:** track per-shipping-policy-cohort-cohort-LTV explicitly; route to per-cohort-audience-engine via Move #115.

13. **Shipping-policy-cohort-discount-uplift-ceiling-failure.** Discount-uplift-ceiling on shipping-policy-cohort = +5-15% (vs +15-30% on creator-economy-cohort per Move #3.11). Operators who apply creator-economy-cohort-discount-uplift-ceiling-engine to shipping-policy-cohort over-discount and erode margin. **Fix:** apply canonical per-shipping-policy-cohort-discount-uplift-ceiling (5-15% on free-shipping-no-min, 8-15% on store-pickup, etc.).

14. **Shipping-policy-cohort-mobile-cohort-confusion with creator-economy-cohort-mobile-cohort-confusion.** Move #3.15 mobile-cohort-confusion is render-area-driven (shipping-policy-cohort-mobile-render-area); Move #3.11 mobile-cohort-confusion is creator-bio-link-driven (creator-mobile-bio-link-friction). Each requires a separate counter. **Fix:** keep the canonical 2 mobile-cohort-confusion counters separate; Move #3.15 = shipping-policy-cohort-mobile-render-area-confusion-counter; Move #3.11 = creator-bio-link-mobile-confusion-counter.

15. **Ignoring shipping-policy-cohort-cross-border-shipping-display.** Cross-border customers see shipping-policy at different render-priority than domestic customers (per Move #25 international-expansion + Move #74 customs-brokerage-cross-border-customs-operations + Move #79 cross-border-returns-operations). If not handled, the shipping-policy-cohort-cross-border-shipping-display-counter fires and the dropship-direct-from-supplier cohort completion-rate drops 8-15% on cross-border (because customers don't realize cross-border shipping has additional customs duties + longer ETA). **Fix:** tag every shipping-policy-cohort-render-event with cross-border-vs-domestic; apply canonical cross-border-shipping-policy-cohort-render-priority (free-shipping-no-min first, then carrier-calculated-shipping with cross-border-customs-duty-disclosure).

16. **Shipping-policy-cohort-payment-fallback-failure.** When a primary payment-method fails, the shipping-policy-cohort (especially dropship-direct-from-supplier + weight-based-shipping) may be de-prioritized in the fallback-render, leading to a payment-step-leak. **Fix:** apply canonical payment-fallback-shipping-policy-cohort-priority (shipping-policy is ALWAYS rendered, even in payment-fallback path).

17. **Shipping-policy-cohort-eta-display-staleness.** Shipping-policy-cohort-eta from Shopify-Shipping-Policy-API may be stale by 30-300 seconds due to webhook-delivery-lag. A customer who adds a dropship-direct-from-supplier SKU to cart may complete checkout only to receive an email 60 seconds later showing the supplier-eta changed from 5 days to 14 days — a per-checkout-step-leak. **Fix:** wire real-time shipping-policy-cohort-eta validation at checkout-step-entry via Shopify-Shipping-Policy-API + webhook-delivery-monitoring + 30-second-staleness-threshold.

18. **Global-rollback-engine-kills-high-performing-shipping-policy-cohorts.** A global rollback that triggers when ANY shipping-policy-cohort completion-rate drops below benchmark-floor will kill high-performing shipping-policy-cohorts (e.g. free-shipping-no-min + free-shipping-min-25 + store-pickup). Per pitfall #18 the rollback-engine must be per-shipping-policy-cohort-specific (per-shipping-policy-cohort-rollback-decision-engine), not global. **Fix:** scope the rollback-trigger to the specific failing-shipping-policy-cohort + apply canonical Move #3 + Move #3.1-Move #3.14 + Move #25 + Move #59 + Move #74 + Move #79 fallback only to that cohort.
