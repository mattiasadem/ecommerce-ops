1. **Single-network bias.** Treating Amazon Sponsored-Products as the only retail-media-network and ignoring Walmart Connect + Instacart Ads + Target Roundtrip + Kroger Precision Marketing + Criteo Retail Media. The canonical failure: brand allocates 95% of retail-media budget to Amazon + 5% to everything else + never measures per-network-CPC-vs-DSP-CPM-vs-cohort-LTV. The repair: deploy per-network-portfolio-budget-allocation-rule + per-network-cohort-LTV-target + per-network-CPC-vs-DSP-trading-rule before adding the 2nd network.

2. **Sponsored-Products-only (no DSP).** Skipping Amazon-DSP + Walmart-Connect-DSP + Instacart-Carrot-DSP + Roundel-DSP + Kroger-84-51-DSP. The DSP layer is the only way to capture Halo-effect-branding-protection (5–30% Year-1 incremental revenue + 0.5–0.7× CAC vs paid-social per Amazon Ad Business 2024). The repair: launch DSP for at least 2 networks (Amazon + 1 other) within 90 days of Sponsored-Products launch.

3. **No per-network attribution-merge.** Treating each network's first-party attribution as ground truth without de-duping. The canonical failure: Amazon-Attribution says $2M Amazon-attributed, Walmart says $200k Walmart-attributed, Instacart says $50k Instacart-attributed — but the SAME customer bought on Amazon + Walmart + Instacart, each network attributes the full revenue, cohort-LTV is wrong by 300%. The repair: Triple-Whale-retail-media-cohort-LTV-overlay + Polar-retail-media-cohort-LTV-overlay + cross-network-de-dup-engine before scaling budget.

4. **No Halo-defense-creative-assets.** Launching Sponsored-Products without Halo-defense-creative-asset library for DSP-programmatic-launch. The DSP layer needs 5–10 creative-asset-patterns (lifestyle-contextual + competitor-product-targeting + brand-defense + seasonal-event-driven + product-launch-driven) per network. The repair: build 5–10 creative-patterns per network BEFORE DSP-launch.

5. **Budget allocation rule is missing.** Operators allocate budget across networks on a discretionary basis (90% Amazon + 5% Walmart + 5% Instacart) instead of a per-network-cohort-LTV-target + per-network-portfolio-budget-allocation-rule. The repair: build a 4-rule budget-allocation-engine (per-network-cohort-LTV-per-dollar × per-network-DSP-CPM-target × per-network-Halo-defense-rate × per-network-budget-tolerance-band) before scaling past $50k/mo retail-media-budget.

6. **CPC-vs-DSP-CPM optimization gap.** Defaulting to DSP-when-CPC-is-cheaper or defaulting to CPC-when-DSP-CPM-is-cheaper. The rule is per-network: Amazon CPC ($0.75–$2.50) vs Amazon-DSP CPM ($2.50–$6.00) — typically Amazon-DSP-wins on Halo-defense but Sponsored-Products-wins on direct-response. The repair: build a per-network-CPC-vs-DSP-trading-rule with cohort-LTV-overrides (when Halo-defense-rate > direct-response-rate, shift more budget to DSP).

7. **No Amazon-Attribution-post-purchase-email-merge.** Operators use Amazon-Attribution first-party without merging with Klaviyo + Postscript + Triple-Whale. The canonical failure: Amazon-Attribution says 100 orders, Klaviyo says 80 orders — the 20-order gap is the email-channel Amazon-orders-not-attributed-to-Klaviyo-attribution. The repair: Amazon-Attribution-Pro-or-AMC-post-purchase-email-match-instrumentation + Klaviyo-Amazon-source-segment-integration + Postscript-Amazon-source-segment-integration.

8. **Per-network-creative-asset-specs wrong.** Operators use Amazon-Sponsored-Products-banner-specs (1200x300) for Instacart-Carrot-DSP-banner (300x250). Each network has different creative-specs + different aspect-ratios + different text-limits. The repair: per-network-creative-asset-specs-template (Amazon + Walmart + Instacart + Target + Kroger + Best Buy + Costco + Ulta + Sephora + Home Depot + Lowe's + Criteo + CVS + Walgreens) with aspect-ratio + text-limit + animation-supported flags.

9. **Wrong per-network-keyword-research.** Reusing Amazon-keywords for Walmart + Instacart + Target + Kroger. Walmart's keyword universe is smaller than Amazon's; Instacart's keyword universe is grocery-specific; Kroger's keyword universe is health-and-beauty-CPG-specific. The repair: per-network-keyword-research with brand-defensive-keyword-set + competitor-product-targeting-keyword-set + in-market-shoppers-keyword-set + lifestyle-contextual-keyword-set + per-network-keyword-set-volume-benchmark.

10. **No bid-automation.** Manually setting per-keyword bids. The canonical failure: 100 keywords × 5 networks = 500 keyword-bids to manage weekly. Manual management caps at 50 keywords. The repair: Amazon-Ads-Console-AI-bid-automation + Walmart-Connect-AI-bid-automation + Instacart-Ads-AI-bid-automation + Pacvue-managed-service-bid-automation + Tinuiti-managed-service-bid-automation + Helium-10-managed-service-bid-automation.

11. **No AMC-equivalent-cohort-queries on non-Amazon networks.** Treating Walmart-Connect-attribution + Instacart-Carrot-attribution + Kroger-84-51-attribution as black-box. The canonical failure: you know 100 Walmart customers but cannot segment them by acquisition-keyword × cohort × LTV. The repair: Walmart-Connect-cohort-queries + Instacart-Carrot-cohort-queries + Kroger-84-51-cohort-queries + per-network-90-day-cohort-LTV + per-network-180-day-cohort-LTV + per-network-365-day-cohort-LTV.

12. **Wrong keyword-tier bid.** Bidding the same $1 CPC on a $5-margin-keyword and a $50-margin-keyword. The canonical failure: ACoS target is 15% but actual ACoS is 35% because of over-bidding on low-margin-keywords. The repair: per-keyword-AOV-bid-strategy with $1-CPC-on-$5-margin-keyword and $5-CPC-on-$50-margin-keyword (10× CPC differential).

13. **No brand-defensive-keyword.** Letting competitors bid on your brand-name keyword on Amazon + Walmart + Instacart. The canonical failure: competitor's product appears above yours when customer searches your brand-name. The repair: brand-defensive-keyword-set on every network + per-network-brand-defensive-bid ($1.50 minimum) + Brand-Registry trademark-protection + Amazon-Halo-defense-lever.

14. **Per-network-budget-pacing broken.** Spend 100% of monthly-budget by Day 22 then inventory-pause. The canonical failure: ACoS target is 15% but actual ACoS is 28% because Day 22–30's "fill-in" bids are too aggressive. The repair: per-network-budget-pacing with dayparting + per-network-monthly-budget-tolerance-band (90% spend by Day 28, hold 10% for Day 28–30 fill-in).

15. **No per-network-cohort-LTV-dashboard.** Operators check per-network-ACoS in network-console but not per-network-cohort-LTV. The canonical failure: network-ACoS says 15% but cohort-LTV says net-negative because the customer-bought-on-Amazon once and never came back. The repair: Triple-Whale-retail-media-cohort-LTV-dashboard + Polar-retail-media-cohort-LTV-dashboard + Northbeam-retail-media-cohort-LTV-dashboard with per-network-cohort-LTV-at-30/60/90/180/365-day-windows.

16. **Per-network-portfolio-quietly-cannibalizes-Shopify-DTC.** Operators scale retail-media-budget from $50k/mo to $200k/mo + Shopify-DTC-revenue-declines-30%. The canonical failure: customers search "your brand" on Amazon + find Amazon-listing + buy on Amazon instead of Shopify-DTC. The repair: Amazon-Halo-defense-lever + Brand-Registry trademark-protection + canary-defense-loyalty-program + per-network-cannibalization-rate-capping-rule (cap retail-media-budget at 2× Shopify-DTC-budget).

17. **No per-network-real-time-alerts.** Operators check per-network-ACoS weekly but not daily. The canonical failure: a network-exploit (competitor-bomb + keyword-bid-spike) inflates ACoS 5× in 24 hours; weekly check catches it 6 days late. The repair: Triple-Whale-retail-media-real-time-alerts + per-network-cohort-LTV-drop-threshold + per-network-DSP-CPM-spike-threshold + per-network-budget-pacing-alert + per-network-Halo-defense-rate-drop-threshold.

18. **No per-network-budget-rebalancing-quarterly-cadence.** Operators set per-network-budget-allocation-rule once and forget. The canonical failure: per-network-cohort-LTV changes by ±50% quarter-over-quarter but budget-allocation doesn't update. The repair: per-network-quarterly-budget-rebalancing-cadence with cohort-LTV-per-dollar-target + per-network-DSP-CPM-target + per-network-Halo-defense-rate-target updated quarterly.
