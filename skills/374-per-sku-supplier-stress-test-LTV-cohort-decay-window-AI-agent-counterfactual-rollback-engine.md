## What "best in class" looks like

A best-in-class Move #358.5.11 counterfactual-rollback-engine is a 6-pillar framework that turns RR1-RR6 actual-fire-events into Pareto-optimal-rollback-window ±Nd offsets that feed Move #358.5.7 Q6 + Move #358.5.6 RR-agent as retraining-baseline + Move #358.5.10 portfolio-extension as portfolio-rollback-tuning-feedback + Move #358.5.9 stress-test-engine as stress-test-rollback-feedback. The 6 pillars:

- **Pillar 1 — Multi-counterfactual-rollback-decomposition-engine.** Per-cohort-LTV-tier × per-supplier-pair × per-historical-rollback-event × per-counterfactual-rollback-window (RR1-RR6 ±7d / ±14d / ±21d / ±28d / ±35d / ±42d — the 6 fixed offsets that cover the operator's typical late-fire-dwell-hours window) × per-counterfactual-rollback-scenario (CB1 EARLIER-FIRE / CB2 LATER-FIRE / CB3 NEVER-FIRE / CB4 PARTIAL-FIRE / CB5 DIFFERENT-RULE / CB6 RULE-COMBINATION — the 6 scenarios that cover every plausible alternative rollback strategy) cell matrix. Total cells per quarter: N cohort-tiers × N supplier-pairs × ≥4 historical-rollback-events × 6 RR-rules × 6 ±Nd windows × 6 CB scenarios = up to 1,296 × cohort-tiers × supplier-pairs cells per quarter. Each cell carries all 14 counterfactual-rollback-vector dimensions (8 savings + 6 LTV-preservation).

- **Pillar 2 — Counterfactual-rollback-AI-agent-savings-vector 8-dim.** CR1 `cr_avoided_loss_opportunity_cost_usd` (the dollar opportunity cost of the late-fire-dwell-hours — `actual_avoided_loss - counterfactual_avoided_loss`); CR2 `cr_late_fire_dwell_hours` (the cumulative hours between optimal-fire-time and actual-fire-time); CR3 `cr_recovery_capital_delta` (the change in recovery-capital-deployed between actual and counterfactual); CR4 `cr_phantom_inventory_avoided_loss` (the phantom-inventory avoided-loss delta vs actual); CR5 `cr_cross_region_fallback_avoided_loss` (the cross-region-fallback avoided-loss delta vs actual); CR6 `cr_classification_drift_avoided_loss` (the CCD1-CCD6 classification-drift avoided-loss delta); CR7 `cr_rollback_window_pareto_pct` (the Pareto-frontier-coverage percentage of this cell across all 6 ±Nd windows); CR8 `cr_ai_agent_compute_cost` (the per-replay compute cost in USD).

- **Pillar 3 — Counterfactual-rollback-AI-agent-LTV-preservation-vector 6-dim.** CR1 `cr_ltv_pre_usd` (LTV before the counterfactual-rollback); CR2 `cr_ltv_post_usd` (LTV after the counterfactual-rollback); CR3 `cr_ltv_degradation_pct` (LTV degradation % vs pre); CR4 `cr_decile_rank_delta` (cohort-decile-rank shift vs actual); CR5 `cr_decile_rank_delta_vs_actual` (delta vs actual-fire-time baseline — the canonical "would have been better/worse by N deciles"); CR6 `cr_high_performer_loss_pct` (the top-decile cohort-tier loss — anti-pattern-guard, ≤10% Tier-1).

- **Pillar 4 — Counterfactual-rollback-AI-agent-engine 8-stage CRC1-CRC8.** CRC1 SELECT-HISTORICAL-ROLLBACK-EVENT (pick the trailing-quarter RR1-RR6 fire-events from Move #358.5.6 Pillar 5 actual-rollback-event log + filter by ≥4 events + ≥2 quarters); CRC2 EXTRACT-ACTUAL-RR-OUTCOME (read Move #358.5.6 Pillar 6 6-tuple actual-avoided-loss + actual-fire-time + Move #358.5.6 Pillar 4 MR1-MR6 recommendation-bands + Move #358.5.6 Pillar 6 40-field event-stream); CRC3 PROPOSE-COUNTERFACTUAL-ROLLBACK-WINDOW (for each historical-rollback-event, propose 6 counterfactual-rollback-windows: ±7d / ±14d / ±21d / ±28d / ±35d / ±42d); CRC4 REPLAY-WITH-COUNTERFACTUAL-FIRE-TIME (replay the historical-rollback-event at each ±Nd counterfactual-fire-time using Move #358.5.6 Pillar 4 MR1-MR6 replay-engine + apply Move #89 BFCM-peak-flag when the historical-event date falls in BFCM-week); CRC5 COMPUTE-LATE-FIRE-OPPORTUNITY-COST (compute `cr_avoided_loss_opportunity_cost_usd` = `actual_avoided_loss - counterfactual_avoided_loss` across all 14 dims + apply `cr_high_performer_loss_pct ≤ 10%` anti-pattern-guard); CRC6 VALIDATE-AGAINST-PRIOR-CONSENSUS (cross-check the counterfactual-rollback-scenario against Move #182 Pillar 3 confidence-band floor + Move #181 Pillar 2 firing-quota + Move #358.5.6 Pillar 5 multi-supplier-rollover-tuning-confidence-band + Move #358.5.8 CFS7 validation-confidence — reject scenarios that violate any); CRC7 FEED-BACK-TO-MOVE-#358.5.7-Q6-ROLLBACK-ACTIVITY (publish to Move #358.5.7 Q6 ROLLBACK-ACTIVITY as counterfactual-rollback-baseline + the Pareto-optimal ±Nd offset); CRC8 COMPUTE-PARETO-OPTIMAL-ROLLBACK-WINDOW (for each (cohort-tier × supplier-pair) compute the Pareto-frontier across 6 ±Nd windows — the offset that maximizes `cr_avoided_loss_opportunity_cost_usd` while keeping `cr_late_fire_dwell_hours ≤ 48h` AND `cr_classification_drift_avoided_loss ≥ 0` AND `cr_high_performer_loss_pct ≤ 10%`).

## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

**P1. Ship without Move #358.5.7 Pillar 4 Q6 ROLLBACK-ACTIVITY integration.** The counterfactual-rollback-engine must publish to Move #358.5.7 Q6 as counterfactual-rollback-baseline; shipping without this integration means CRC7 returns 0 events and counterfactual-rollback-baselines stay siloed in the counterfactual-rollback-engine and never reach the CFO + cohort-owners. **Fix:** Pillar 5 C4 channel is mandatory for every counterfactual-rollback-replay (T1-T5); counterfactual-rollback-replay not published to Move #358.5.7 Q6 is a CRR3 AUTO-REGENERATE trigger.

**P2. Ship without Move #358.5.6 Pillar 6 multi-supplier-rollover-cost-amortization-engine integration.** The counterfactual-rollback-engine must read Move #358.5.6 Pillar 6 6-tuple actual-avoided-loss + actual-fire-time to compute the late-fire-dwell opportunity cost; shipping without this read means CRC2 EXTRACT-ACTUAL-RR-OUTCOME returns empty and CRC5 COMPUTE-LATE-FIRE-OPPORTUNITY-COST returns NaN. **Fix:** Pillar 1 cell matrix must join on Move #358.5.6 Pillar 6 6-tuple by cohort-LTV-tier × supplier-pair × RR-rule × week-id; counterfactual-rollback-replay with missing actual 6-tuple is rejected pre-publish.

**P3. Ship without ≥4 historical-rollback-events per quarter.** The counterfactual-rollback-engine must have ≥4 RR1-RR6 actual-fire-events to estimate Pareto-optimal ±Nd offset; shipping without ≥4 events means CRC1 SELECT-HISTORICAL-ROLLBACK-EVENT returns 0 events and the counterfactual-rollback-engine ships as a static playbook. **Fix:** Move #358.5.11 prerequisite #11 enforces ≥4 historical-rollback-events per quarter; counterfactual-rollback-engine with <4 events available is REJECTED at the prereq gate.

**P4. No CRC6 validate-against-prior-consensus gating.** The counterfactual-rollback-engine can propose counterfactual-rollback-scenarios that violate Move #182 Pillar 3 confidence-band floor or Move #181 Pillar 2 firing-quota; shipping without CRC6 gating means a counterfactual-rollback-scenario that "would have fired RR3 21d earlier with 100% confidence" but violates Move #181 quota gets accepted as evidence. **Fix:** CRC6 must cross-check Move #182 + Move #181 + Move #358.5.6 Pillar 5 multi-supplier-rollover-tuning-confidence-band + Move #358.5.8 CFS7 validation-confidence; counterfactual-rollback-scenario that violates any of the four is rejected at CRC6 with a CRR2 AUTO-REGENERATE.

**P5. No 6-sub-rule CRR1-CRR6 rollback-coverage validation.** Without CRR1-CRR6, a counterfactual-rollback-replay that drifts to T5 confidence-band or produces a counterfactual-LTV-preservation < 95% of actual can silently propagate to Move #358.5.7 Q6 + Move #358.5.6 RR-agent + Move #358.5.10 portfolio-extension + Move #358.5.9 stress-test-engine; bad counterfactual-rollback-baselines corrupt next-quarter RR-tuning + portfolio-tuning + stress-test-replay. **Fix:** Pillar 6 6-sub-rule CRR1-CRR6 fire on (CRR1 confidence_band T5 / CRR2 mismatch-with-RR-outcome > 2× / CRR3 CFO-board-pack-counterfactual-rollback-SLA-breach / CRR4 counterfactual-rollback-avoided-loss-opportunity-cost < 0 / CRR5 LTV-preservation-drops-below-95%-of-actual / CRR6 operator-rollback-request).

**P6. No confidence-band on counterfactual-rollback-replay-scenario.** All counterfactual-rollback-replays treated as equally-trustworthy. **Fix:** Pillar 5 5-tier confidence-band on every counterfactual-rollback-replay (T1 ≥95% replay-validity / T2 85-95% / T3 70-85% / T4 50-70% / T5 <50% REJECT); counterfactual-rollback-replay confidence-band is the AGGREGATE of CRC6 validation-confidence × Move #358.5.6 actual-confidence-band × counterfactual-rollback-scenario-consistency-check.

**P7. No Move #358.5.7 Pillar 5 decision-routing-segregation.** Counterfactual-rollback-engine must NOT include Move #358.5.7 Pillar 5 decision-routing weights in the counterfactual-rollback-replay (decision-routing is a separate decision-engine layer); shipping without this scope-check means the counterfactual-rollback-replay leaks decision-routing data to the CFO + cohort-owners that should be operator-only. **Fix:** counterfactual-rollback-replay section filter excludes Move #358.5.7 Pillar 5 fields; only Move #358.5.6 Pillar 5 RR1-RR6 fire-times + Move #358.5.6 Pillar 4 MR1-MR6 recommendation-bands are surfaced.
