## What "best in class" looks like

A Tier-1 per-sku-supplier-stress-test-LTV-cohort-decay-window-AI-agent-extension has **all six pillars** live + ≥80% of cells populated:

**Pillar 1 — per-cohort-LTV-tier weekly-cohort-LTV-drift-vector 7-dim.** Every Monday 8 AM (BFCM-week: every day 8 AM) the AI-agent reads the Move #358.5.4 Pillar 6 cross-cohort-decay-overlay-event-stream trailing-7-day window and computes a per-cohort-LTV-tier weekly-cohort-LTV-drift-vector 7-dim: `cohort_ltv_weekly_drift_pct` (delta vs trailing-90-day baseline; positive = LTV up, negative = LTV down; BFCM-week: vs trailing-7-day baseline), `cohort_ltv_weekly_confidence_band` (T1 ≥95% / T2 85-95% / T3 70-85% / T4 50-70% / T5 <50%), `cohort_ltv_weekly_segment_shift` (per-segment delta — new-segment-acquired / existing-segment-retained / existing-segment-degraded / lost-segment; computed by Move #154 Pillar 2 cohort-segmentation-engine), `cohort_ltv_weekly_overlay_rebalance_count` (number of Move #358.5.4 Pillar 4 CRO1-CRO6 rebalancing firings in trailing-7-days, including CCD1-CCD6 pattern × severity-band), `cohort_ltv_weekly_collision_severity_band_shift` (delta of Move #358.5.4 Pillar 3 cross-cohort-decay-overlay-table 5-band assignment — NO-COLLISION → SOFT-COLLISION → MEDIUM-COLLISION → HARD-COLLISION → CRITICAL-COLLISION; computed from Move #358.5.4 Pillar 1 CCD1-CCD6 collision-detection-engine), `cohort_ltv_weekly_substitutability_score_delta` (delta of Move #358.5.1 Pillar 4 per-cohort × per-supplier × per-region × per-SKU × per-stress-scenario substitutability-stress-band — LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL), `cohort_ltv_weekly_recovery_capital_delta` (delta of Move #358.5.4 Pillar 5 cross-cohort-decay-overlay-cost-amortization-engine 5-tuple overlay_recovery_capital_usd — positive = recovery-capital-increased, negative = recovery-capital-decreased; computed against trailing-90-day baseline). All 7 dims populated for ≥80% of cohort-events; confidence_band populated ≥95% of cohort-events with T1+T2 ≥80% of cohort-events. Per-cohort-LTV-tier weekly-cohort-LTV-drift-vector published weekly into a per-cohort × per-week cell table (≥6 cohort-tiers × 52 weeks/year = ≥312 cells/year). ## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

1. **Ship-without-Move-#358.5.4-integration → no per-cohort-LTV-tier cross-cohort-decay-overlay-event-stream to OBSERVE.** The AI-agent-extension depends entirely on Move #358.5.4 Pillar 6 38-field event-stream as the OBSERVE input. Shipping without Move #358.5.4 means no CCD1-CCD6 firing-history, no CRO1-CRO6 rebalancing-history, no overlay-cost-amortization-history → the AI-agent has nothing to tune. **Fix:** ship Move #358.5.4 first; verify ≥80% of cohort-events carry the 38-field schema; only then start the AI-agent-extension build.

2. **Ship-without-Move-#182-AI-agent-trust-recovery-integration → TUN6 COMMIT cannot safely fire without operator-review.** The AI-agent-trust-band T1-T5 from Move #182 Pillar 1 is the load-bearing signal that gates TUN6 COMMIT-with-auto-execute (T1+T2) vs TUN6 COMMIT-with-operator-notification (T2) vs TUN6 DEFER-TO-OPERATOR-REVIEW (T3+). Without Move #182, every TUN6 commit requires operator-review, which negates the 50-70% operator-review-time-reduction ROI pillar. **Fix:** ship Move #182 first; verify AI-agent-trust-band populated ≥95% of cohort-events; integrate into Pillar 4 confidence-band calculation.

3. **Ship-without-Move-#181-AI-vendor-orchestration-governance-integration → AI-agent-tuning violates Move #181 Pillar 2 AI-vendor-firing-quota.** Move #181 limits AI-vendor firings per cohort per day to prevent governance-overrun. The AI-agent-extension's TUN6 COMMIT-action is itself an AI-vendor firing; without Move #181 governance, the AI-agent can overrun the firing-quota and starve other AI-vendors (Move #186 / Move #187 / Move #188). **Fix:** ship Move #181 first; wire AI-vendor-firing-quota into Pillar 4 confidence-band calculation; verify firing-quota usage ≤50% per cohort per day.

4. **No-TUN4-BACKTEST-coverage-validation → recommendations commit without historical-replay-validation.** TUN4 BACKTEST runs the proposed tuning against the trailing-90-day cohort-LTV-drift + CCD-firing-pattern history and emits a backtest_confidence_band T1-T5. Without backtest, recommendations fire on OBSERVE-vector + DIAGNOSE-vector confidence alone, which can be a moving target. **Fix:** validate TUN4 BACKTEST-pass rate ≥85% of recommendations; emit backtest_confidence_band T1-T5 alongside propose_confidence_band; TUN5 VALIDATE compares both.

5. **No-6-sub-rule-RT1-RT6-rollback-coverage-validation → bad recommendations persist indefinitely.** RT1-RT6 are the safety-net that AUTO-ROLLBACK bad TUN6 COMMIT-actions. Missing any of the 6 sub-rules means a class of bad-recommendations persists for the full 14-day trailing window OR until operator manual-rollback. **Fix:** validate all 6 sub-rules RT1-RT6 fire-coverage 100% of TUN6 COMMIT-actions; rollback-rate per cohort per quarter ≤15% Tier-1; verify each sub-rule on at least 1 historical cohort-week.

6. **No-confidence-band-on-recommendation-band → all recommendations treated as equally-trustworthy.** Without Pillar 4 confidence-band T1-T5, the AI-agent either commits everything (over-routes, fires bad rebalancings) or commits nothing (under-routes, never improves overlay-precision). **Fix:** validate confidence-band T1+T2 ≥80% of recommendations; T3+ recommendations DEFER-TO-OPERATOR-REVIEW; T5 recommendations REJECT-immediately.

7. **No-Move-#358.5.3-Pillar-5-decision-routing-segregation → AI-agent-extension commits the Move #358.5.3 Pillar 5 decision-routing weights, not the Move #358.5.4 Pillar 4 CRO weights.** Move #358.5.3 Pillar 5 decision-routing is the operator-approval table (AUTO-APPROVE / ONE-CLICK-APPROVE / OPERATOR-REVIEW / BFCM-PEAK-FAST-APPROVE / EMERGENCY-FREEZE) — operator-controlled, NOT AI-agent-tunable. The AI-agent-extension commits ONLY the Move #358.5.4 Pillar 4 CRO1-CRO6 weights. **Fix:** validate the TUN6 COMMIT-action writes ONLY to Move #358.5.4 Pillar 4 CRO1-CRO6 weights; verify zero writes to Move #358.5.3 Pillar 5 decision-routing-approval-table.

8. **No-Move-#89-BFCM-peak-multiplier-integration → weekly-cadence misfires during BFCM-week.** During BFCM-week (Black Friday → Cyber Monday), the trailing-7-day window is dominated by BFCM-peak noise; the weekly-cohort-LTV-drift-vector's `cohort_ltv_weekly_drift_pct` computation must use trailing-7-day baseline (not trailing-90-day baseline) to avoid false-diagnosis. **Fix:** wire Move #89 BFCM-peak-multiplier into Pillar 1 OBSERVE-vector `cohort_ltv_weekly_drift_pct` baseline-calculation; BFCM-week: trailing-7-day baseline; non-BFCM-week: trailing-90-day baseline.

9. **No-Move-#154-predictive-LTV-churn-integration → cohort-LTV-drift misdiagnosed as cross-cohort-overlay-tuning issue.** Cohort-LTV-drift can be caused by Move #154 Pillar 2 churn-risk-band shift (e.g. a cohort's churn-risk-band went from LOW to MEDIUM due to external factors), NOT by Move #358.5.4 Pillar 4 CRO routing. Without Move #154 integration, AI-agent-extension fires CRO-tuning-recommendations for churn-driven drift, wasting tuning-cycles + missing the real issue. **Fix:** wire Move #154 Pillar 2 churn-risk-band into Pillar 2 DIAGNOSE-vector `ccd_weekly_severity_band_shift` calculation; if churn-risk-band-shift > 1 tier, suppress CRO-tuning-recommendation and emit churn-investigation-recommendation instead.

10. **No-ACTIVATE-DEACTIVATED-CRO-recovery-confidence-band-validation → activated CRO misfires.** When a CRO is firing 0× per cohort per week for ≥4 weeks (Pillar 3 ACTIVATE-DEACTIVATED-CRO trigger), the AI-agent may activate it back without validating that the original deactivation was due to Move #358.5.4 Pillar 4 historical-cro-effectiveness-issue (e.g. CRO-N was deactivated because it was misfiring with confidence_band T5) vs due to transient-cohort-LTV-tier shift. **Fix:** validate recovery-confidence-band ≥85% AND require commit-operator-notification per Move #182 Pillar 2 AI-agent-trust-recovery; verify 0× ACTIVATE-DEACTIVATED-CRO fires in trailing-30-day window before allowing new activation.

11. **No-ADD-CRO-SUB-RULE-naming-convention-validation → new CRO-sub-rules collide with existing CRO1-CRO6.** When Pillar 3 ADD-CRO-SUB-RULE fires, the new sub-rule must follow the Move #358.5.4 Pillar 4 6-sub-rule naming pattern CRO<N>.<M> (e.g. CRO1.1, CRO1.2, CRO2.1). Without naming-convention-validation, the new sub-rule may collide with an existing CRO-sub-rule OR may not be picked up by the Move #358.5.4 Pillar 4 cross-cohort-decay-overlay-rebalancing-engine. **Fix:** validate naming-convention CRO<N>.<M> pattern pre-commit; verify Move #358.5.4 Pillar 4 cross-cohort-decay-overlay-rebalancing-engine picks up the new sub-rule within 24h.

12. **No-ROLLBACK-CCD-CLASSIFICATION-confidence-band-validation → CCD-pattern-classifications get rolled back prematurely.** When Pillar 3 ROLLBACK-CCD-CLASSIFICATION fires, the AI-agent may rollback a CCD-pattern's classification (e.g. CCD3 subscription-vs-BFCM-peak classified as NO-COLLISION for a cohort-pair) without sufficient evidence. **Fix:** validate confidence-band T1+T2 for ROLLBACK-CCD-CLASSIFICATION; require ≥3 weeks of misfiring-history before allowing rollback; emit rollback-recommendation to operator-dashboard for review (DO NOT auto-commit ROLLBACK-CCD-CLASSIFICATION).

13. **No-Triple-Whale-40-field-schema-coverage-validation → downstream consumers silently fail.** Pillar 6 40-field schema is the contract with 12 downstream consumers (Triple Whale + Polar + Northbeam + operator-dashboard + CFO-board-pack + Move #113/115/119/154/180/181/182/183). Missing any field silently breaks a downstream consumer. **Fix:** validate 40-field schema coverage 100% per event with confidence-band populated ≥95%; add schema-coverage-validation as a TUN6 pre-commit gate.

14. **No-2-week-trailing-observation-window-validation → RT1-RT6 rollback-triggers fire too late OR too early.** The 14-day trailing observation window for RT1-RT6 is load-bearing — too short (e.g. 3 days) and natural-LTV-volatility triggers false-rollbacks; too long (e.g. 30 days) and bad recommendations persist for a month. **Fix:** validate trailing-14-day window; RT1-RT6 each fire-coverage 100% of TUN6 COMMIT-actions; rollback-rate per cohort per quarter ≤15% Tier-1.

15. **No-ai-agent-tuning-cost-amortization-tracking → AI-agent-extension profitability unknown.** Pillar 6 6-tuple cost-amortization-engine tracks `overlay_tuning_AI_agent_compute_cost_usd` (~$0.05-$0.50 per cohort per week at $5M GMV) + `overlay_tuning_recovery_capital` + `overlay_tuning_net_avoided_loss`. Without cost-tracking, the AI-agent-extension may be net-negative (compute-cost > recovery-capital). **Fix:** validate `overlay_tuning_net_avoided_loss` > `overlay_tuning_AI_agent_compute_cost_usd` × 100 (i.e. ROI ≥100:1 on AI-agent compute); cost-amortization-payback-period ≤14 weeks Tier-1.

16. **No-operator-rollback-explicit-handling → operator REJECT-but-not-rollback confusion.** RT6 (operator_REVIEW_rejected → AUTO-ROLLBACK) is a specific case where operator marks a TUN6 COMMIT-action as rejected via operator-dashboard. Without explicit RT6 handling, operator-rejection may not translate to actual rollback. **Fix:** wire operator-dashboard-rejection-event into RT6 firing-logic; validate RT6 fires within 24h of operator-rejection; emit rollback-event to Move #182 Pillar 3 AI-agent-rollback-recovery.

17. **No-confidence-band-boost-from-cohort-history → every cohort treated as fresh.** Pillar 4 confidence-band calculation includes a cohort-history factor: if last 4 weekly-tuning-cycles for this cohort had T1+T2 ≥3/4 → confidence-band boost +1; if T3+ ≥3/4 → confidence-band drop -1. Without cohort-history, AI-agent treats every cohort as fresh, losing the cohort-tuning-memory benefit. **Fix:** track last-4-weekly-tuning-cycles per cohort; wire into Pillar 4 confidence-band calculation; validate cohort-history-factor ≥3/4 cohorts have ≥4 weekly-history-entries.

18. **No-cross-cohort-overlay-tuning-vs-cross-cohort-decision-routing-boundary-validation → AI-agent-extension scope-creep.** The AI-agent-extension commits ONLY the Move #358.5.4 Pillar 4 cross-cohort-decay-overlay-rebalancing-engine 6-sub-rule CRO1-CRO6 weights. It does NOT commit the Move #358.5.4 Pillar 1 CCD1-CCD6 collision-detection-engine classification (operator-controlled), nor the Move #358.5.3 Pillar 2 per-cohort-decision-routing-band (operator-controlled). **Fix:** validate the TUN6 COMMIT-action writes ONLY to Move #358.5.4 Pillar 4 CRO weights via a scope-check pre-commit gate; verify zero writes to CCD-classification OR decision-routing-band.
