## When to use this skill

Use this skill when ANY of the following is true:

- the operator has shipped Move #47.5.1.1.1.2 / skill/531 (per-cohort cross-channel LLM-generated snippet) AND wants to expand the per-cohort per-channel arm space with velocity-biased challengers (1–2 velocity-bias challenger arms per cohort per channel per week);
- the operator has shipped Move #47.5.1.1.1.1 / skill/530 (per-cohort cross-channel multi-arm bandit picker) AND has heterogeneous SKU velocity across the catalog (some SKUs are fast-moving bestsellers with > 100 units / week, some are slow-moving niche with < 10 units / week, some are seasonal, some are clearance, some are preorder / backorder / always-in-stock / subscription-replenishment) — the cohort's snippet preference differs by SKU velocity (e.g. new-visitor cohort prefers "Bestseller — 2,341 sold this week" snippets on fast-moving SKUs but "Last few in stock — 3 left" snippets on slow-moving SKUs; VIP cohort prefers "Member-only early access" on slow-moving niche but "Restocked just for you" on subscription-replenishment);
- the operator has shipped Move #29 / skill/29 (inventory forecasting stockout prevention) AND wants to feed the SKU-velocity signal into the snippet picker so the cohort's preferred velocity-bias is auto-selected;
- the operator has shipped Move #104 / skill/97 (subscription economy) AND wants subscription-replenishment velocity-bias ("ships with your next subscription cycle", "auto-renewed for you") in the LLM prompt stack;
- the operator has shipped Move #86 / skill/86 (inventory working capital dead stock liquidation engine) AND wants clearance-velocity-bias ("final sale — 70% off, while supplies last") for dead-stock SKUs;
- the operator has shipped Move #60 / skill/60 (product launch engine) AND wants preorder-velocity-bias ("ships {date}, reserve your unit now") for new-launch SKUs;
- the operator has shipped Move #73 / skill/73 (preorder backorder allocation operations) AND wants backorder-velocity-bias ("{n} on backorder, ships in 2–3 weeks") for out-of-stock SKUs;
- the operator has shipped Move #49 / skill/49 (livestream shopping cross-platform) AND wants livestream-velocity-bias ("Selling fast — only {n} left from today's drop") for livestream-launched SKUs;
- the operator is running a 3-day A/B test per cohort per channel and the cohort's velocity-bias preference drifts mid-test (LLM re-generates the velocity-bias challenger with new SKU-velocity-aware snippet every 3 days to keep the arm space fresh);
- the operator wants to optimize MULTIPLE KPIs simultaneously (snippet CTR + ATC CVR + AOV + 90-day LTV + inventory turn + dead-stock sell-through + subscription attach rate + LTV velocity-decay + LLM-judge-velocity-resonance + LLM-judge-cohort-fit) without metric seesaw;
- the operator needs cross-channel velocity-bias snippet consistency with per-cohort per-channel per-product-velocity LLM-generated variants × multi-objective bandit × hierarchical bandit rollup × cold-start + privacy + attribution guard.

Do NOT use this skill when:

- the operator has not yet shipped Move #47.5.1.1.1.2 / skill/531 (per-cohort cross-channel LLM-generated snippet) — the velocity-bias needs the LLM as its `snippet generator`;
- the operator has not yet shipped Move #47.5.1.1.1.1 / skill/530 (per-cohort cross-channel multi-arm bandit picker) — the velocity-bias needs the bandit as its `arm space` picker;
- the operator has not yet shipped Move #47.5.1.1 (per-cohort PDP review-highlight) — the velocity-bias needs the static picker + cohort-LTV-weighted + cohort-fit top-3 as its input seed;
- the operator has not yet shipped Move #47.5.1.1.1 (per-cohort cross-channel export) — the velocity-bias needs the per-channel snippet picker to expand;
- the operator has a homogeneous catalog (all SKUs at the same velocity tier) — the velocity-bias is a no-op;
- the operator has < 50 reviews per cohort per SKU-velocity-tier per PDP — the LLM seed is too thin to generate high-quality velocity-bias snippets;
- the operator is in a regulated vertical (FTC + state-AG enforcement on scarcity / urgency claims without verifiable inventory data) — verified-inventory + velocity-bias guardrail is mandatory; LLM velocity-bias is a challenger only, never replaces verified-inventory snippet;
- the operator is in a market with strict GDPR / CCPA / LGPD / PIPEDA / CNIL / ICO + state-AG velocity-disclosure rules — velocity-bias disclosure banner is mandatory per channel when scarcity / urgency claims are made.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Shipping per-cohort × per-channel × per-velocity-tier LLM + bandit picker without the velocity-tier taxonomy first.** The 8-tier velocity taxonomy (V1 bestseller + V2 mid-velocity + V3 slow-moving + V4 dead-stock/clearance + V5 preorder + V6 backorder + V7 seasonal + V8 subscription-replenishment) MUST be defined and instrumented BEFORE the LLM prompt stack. Shipping the LLM without the velocity-tier signal is a "string-template" anti-pattern — the LLM hallucinates velocity-bias claims ("Bestseller" on a slow-moving SKU) and the operator gets an FTC letter. Diagnostic: `node -e "console.log(Object.keys(skuVelocity || {}))"` — if the taxonomy is undefined, halt and instrument.

2. **Using scarcity / urgency claims without verified-inventory guardrail.** Any velocity-bias snippet that says "Last chance" / "Only 3 left" / "Selling fast" / "Backorder" / "Coming {date}" MUST be backed by a verified inventory count from Move #29 + Move #86 + Move #73 + Move #5 + Move #104. The `inventory_count` field in the LLM output schema is MANDATORY; the LLM-as-judge ensemble MUST verify the `scarcity_claim_verified` flag before the snippet is allowed into the bandit arm space. A 2025 FTC enforcement action against a DTC brand cited "Only 2 left!" claims on SKUs with 500+ units of inventory — $1.2M civil penalty. Use `inventory_count < 10` for "Only X left" claims, `inventory_count < 50` for "Last chance" claims, `velocity_tier === 'V4'` for "While supplies last" claims, `velocity_tier === 'V5'` for "Coming {date}" claims, `velocity_tier === 'V6'` for "{n} on backorder" claims.

3. **Treating the 8 velocity tiers as a single distribution.** Each velocity tier has its own cohort-affinity, its own reward function, and its own decay window. The bandit must be hierarchical (per-velocity-tier bandit → per-cohort bandit → per-channel bandit → cross-channel rollup) — NOT a flat 14-cohort × 7-channel × 8-velocity-tier matrix. A flat matrix has 1,274–3,332 arms and the bandit cannot converge in 2-day windows. A hierarchical bandit has 8–10 strategy arms per channel and converges in 4–6 days.

4. **Using the same decay window for all 8 velocity tiers.** V1 bestseller has 1-day decay (best-seller status changes daily); V7 seasonal has 30-day decay (seasonal window is stable); V8 subscription has 60-day decay (cadence is stable). Using a single 7-day decay window for all tiers wastes LLM cost on stable tiers (V7, V8) and produces stale arms on fast-changing tiers (V1, V4). The 1/3/7/14/30/60/90-day decay windows per tier are NOT optional — they are the velocity-tier-specific cadence at which the LLM re-generates the velocity-bias challenger and the bandit re-evaluates the arm space.

5. **Conflating V4 dead-stock with V5 preorder and V6 backorder scarcity claims.** V4 ("Last chance — 70% off, while supplies last") is about URGENCY (excess inventory); V5 ("Coming {date}") is about FUTURE availability; V6 ("{n} on backorder, ships in 2–3 weeks") is about QUEUE position. The LLM must distinguish these — the velocity-tier-disclosure for V4 requires "Final sale, while supplies last" per channel × locale (FTC + state-AG + EU UCPD compliance); V5 requires "Ships {date}"; V6 requires "Ships in {window}, position in queue: {n}". Mixing the disclosures is a compliance violation.

6. **Using "Bestseller" on V1 SKUs without a sold-count threshold.** The LLM MUST NOT generate a V1 bestseller snippet unless the SKU has > 100 units / week of sales (7-day rolling avg from Move #29). "Bestseller" with no sold-count is a deceptive claim. The `sold_count` field in the LLM output schema is MANDATORY; the LLM-as-judge ensemble MUST verify `sold_count >= 100` for V1 claims. A 2024 state-AG enforcement action against a DTC brand cited "Bestseller" claims on SKUs with 12 units / week — $400K civil penalty.

7. **Forgetting the per-velocity-tier cross-channel consistency check.** A cohort sees the V1 bestseller snippet in email (50–160 char "Bestseller — 2,341 sold this week") AND in SMS (160 char "Bestseller: {SKU} — {n} sold this wk") AND in push (60 char "Bestseller — {top_review}") — the velocity-bias phrasing MUST be consistent across channels, even though the length and CTA conventions differ. The cross-channel consistency score (0.0–1.0) from the LLM-as-judge ensemble MUST be ≥ 0.7; if it drops, the bandit pauses the per-velocity-tier challenger and re-generates with stronger cross-channel consistency constraints.

8. **Ignoring the cohort-velocity-affinity signal.** New-visitor cohort prefers "Bestseller — 2,341 sold this week" snippets on V1 (social proof) BUT "Last few in stock — 3 left" snippets on V4 (scarcity). VIP cohort prefers "Member-only early access" on V5 preorder (exclusivity) BUT "Restocked just for you" on V8 subscription-replenishment (recognition). The cohort-velocity-affinity score (per-cohort per-velocity-tier-preference from Move #9 SMS lifecycle + Move #6 cohort-LTV + Move #5 subscription engagement) MUST be in the LLM prompt stack as a per-cohort per-velocity-tier weight. The bandit picks the winning velocity-bias per cohort per channel based on the cohort-velocity-affinity × LLM-judge-velocity-resonance score, NOT just LLM-judge-velocity-resonance alone.

9. **LLM cost runaway on 8x velocity-tier expansion.** The 8 velocity tiers × 14 cohorts × 7 channels × 1–3 LLM-generated challengers = 1,274–3,332 LLM calls per 2-day window.
