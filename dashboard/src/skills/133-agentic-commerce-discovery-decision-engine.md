## What "best in class" looks like

A canonical per-user-query × per-agent-channel × per-discovery-stage × per-merchant-catalog × per-product-card-render × per-recommendation-explanation × per-cohort × per-LTV-overlay × per-merchant-readiness-score × per-product-feed-completeness × per-catalog-feed-completeness × per-discovery-fallback-rule × per-discovery-rollback-decision × per-discovery-decision-actor × per-discovery-decision-outcome × per-discovery-decision-trace-id × per-discovery-coverage-benchmark × per-discovery-fairness-benchmark × per-discovery-explanation-latency-benchmark × per-discovery-multi-overlay discovery-decision-engine that:

1. **Auto-emits a canonical discovery-decision for every per-user-query × per-agent-channel × per-discovery-stage query in <30s** — per-discovery-decision-coverage = `(product-impressions-via-canonical-discovery-decision / total-product-impressions) × 100` — operator gets a per-user-query × per-agent-channel × per-discovery-stage × per-merchant-catalog × per-product-card-render × per-recommendation-explanation discovery-decision-card with concrete product-surfacing-reason (matching-score × product-feed-completeness × merchant-readiness-score × catalog-feed-completeness × cohort-LTV-overlay × discovery-fairness-score × discovery-coverage-benchmark) per Product × per-cohort × per-channel × per-discovery-stage.
2. **Auto-emits per-product-discovery-explanation in <60s** — per-product-discovery-explanation-coverage = `(products-with-canonical-discovery-explanation / total-products-surfaced) × 100` — operator gets a per-product-card-render with "why-this-product-for-this-query" explanation (matching-score-attribute × product-feed-attribute × merchant-readiness-attribute × cohort-LTV-attribute × discovery-fairness-attribute).
3. **Auto-emits per-product-discovery-fairness-audit in <60s** — per-discovery-fairness-benchmark = `1 - (max-impressions-by-product) / (total-impressions)` — operator gets a per-discovery-stage × per-product-card-render fairness-card with per-product-impression-share + per-cohort-impression-share + per-channel-impression-share + per-merchant-impression-share; flags when any single product >40% impressions (anti-monopoly floor).
4. **Auto-emits per-product-cohort-LTV-overlay** (high-LTV-cohort → boost-impression × boost-explanation × boost-discovery-trace-id; medium-LTV-cohort → standard-impression × standard-explanation; low-LTV-cohort → suppress-impression × suppress-explanation; non-LTV-cohort → fair-share-impression × no-LTV-overlay) — Move #133's per-product-cohort-LTV-overlay uses Move #119 per-cohort-LTV-overlay-engine as the source of truth.
5. **Auto-emits per-product-merchant-readiness-overlay** (high-readiness → boost-impression × boost-discovery-trace-id × boost-discovery-explanation; medium-readiness → standard-impression; low-readiness → suppress-impression × flag-discovery-rollback-decision-needed; not-yet-onboarded → no-impression × flag-merchant-onboarding-regression) — Move #133's per-product-merchant-readiness-overlay uses Move #123 merchant-readiness-engine as the source of truth.
6. **Auto-emits per-product-catalog-feed-completeness-overlay** (catalog-feed-coverage ≥95% → boost-impression; ≥85% → standard-impression; ≥70% → suppress-impression × flag-catalog-feed-completeness-gap; <70% → no-impression × flag-Move-#124-onboarding-readiness-regression).
7. **Auto-emits per-product-product-feed-completeness-overlay** (per-product-feed-completeness ≥Schema.org-Product+Offer+ShippingDetails+MerchantReturnPolicy+AggregateRating ≥90% → boost-impression; ≥75% → standard-impression; <75% → suppress-impression × flag-product-feed-completeness-gap).
8. **Auto-emits per-product-discovery-fallback-rule** (primary-recommendation-fails → fallback-discovery-decoder-rule; catalog-feed-empty → fallback-catalog-feed-from-Google-Shopping-Graph; Schema-org-missing → fallback-emergency-defaults-from-Move-#127; AI-search-visibility-missing → fallback-canonical-llms-txt-from-Move-#64; merchant-readiness-low → fallback-low-readiness-discovery-decoder-rule).
