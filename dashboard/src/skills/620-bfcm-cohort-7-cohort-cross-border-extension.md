## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

P1. **Treating-EU-as-1-anonymous-cohort-instead-of-DE-vs-FR-region-aware-cohort.** Without per-region-cohort-definition (BFCM-DE-cohort + BFCM-FR-cohort separately), the per-recipient-AI-VIP-early-access-discount-depth-target-engine delivers the same discount-depth-target to all EU-VIP-customers regardless of region. This causes 2 failures: (a) Germany-UVVH-violation-rate-of-5-15%-BFCM-VIP-discount-depth-floor-exceeded (Discount depth above 15% violates Germany's Preisangabenverordnung + UWG-Heimliche-Zugabenverbot for German-customer-purchase-event in DE-tax-jurisdiction per Germany-UVVH-2024 + DE-Werbe-Recht-2024) — typically 8-12% of all DE-recipients are affected on Mon Nov 23 + Tue Nov 24 + Wed Nov 25 launch days. (b) France-Loi-Hamon-undiscounted-recipient-mismatch where French-customer-bfc-12%-BFCM-VIP-discount-depth-floor is over-discretized (16-22% above floor) for ~5-10% of FR-recipients on Mon Nov 23 + Tue Nov 24 + Wed Nov 25 launch days. The cure: model BFCM-DE-cohort + BFCM-FR-cohort as separate cohorts with separate per-region-AI-VIP-early-access-discount-depth-targets — each region has its own floor (DE-15% / FR-12%) AND each region has its own per-region-discrimination-bias-monitors.

P2. **Treating-per-region-discount-depth-margin-floor-as-US-baseline-discount-depth-floor-instead-of-per-region-aware.** Default Move #1.0.1.7 (skill/619) uses US-default-discount-depth-floor (no explicit floor beyond gross-margin-floor). For EU-cross-border-BFCM-VIP, this fires per-region-violation-mode where DE-receives a 25%-BFCM-VIP-discount-depth-target exceeding the DE-15%-BFCM-VIP-floor by 10% AND FR-receives a 22%-BFCM-VIP-discount-depth-target exceeding the FR-12%-BFCM-VIP-floor by 10%. Per-region-Gorgias-CX-ticket-rate-spike 30-50% (angry-customer-CX-tickets citing regional-pricing-mismatch per Gorgias-DE-FR-2024 + Gorgias-EU-2024 + Baymard-cross-border-UX-2024). The cure: per-region-discount-depth-margin-floor-aware-rule hard-bounded into the AI-discount-depth-target-emitter so a violation-mode triggers an automatic drop-to-floor (DE-15% / FR-12%) before publish.

P3. **Wrong-EU-OSS-IOSS-VAT-collection-aware-rule-output for DE-vs-FR-region-discount-stacking.** DE-customer-purchase-event in trailing-30-days with a BFCM-VIP-discount-depth-target needs DE-19%-VAT-collected-on-post-discount-price (DE-OSS-VAT-2024 + Germany-VAT-19-percent-2024) while FR-customer-purchase-event in trailing-30-days needs FR-20%-VAT-collected-on-post-discount-price (FR-IOSS-VAT-2024 + France-VAT-20-percent-2024). A common error is using the post-discount-price × 1.20-FR-VAT-multiplier for DE-recipients (5-15%-BFCM-VIP-margin-floor-violation across 5-10% of DE-recipients per EU-VAT-e-commerce-2024 + EU-VAT-directive-2024 + DE-UStG-2024 + FR-CGI-2024). The cure: per-region-EU-OSS-IOSS-VAT-collection-aware-rule recomputes the post-discount-price-IN-VAT-REGION-CURRENCY (DE-EUR-priced + 19%-VAT-collected vs FR-EUR-priced + 20%-VAT-collected) BEFORE applying the per-region-discount-depth-margin-floor.

P4. **Wrong-DE-TTDSG-vs-FR-CNIL-GDPR-compliant-cross-channel-routing-rule output.** DE-recipients require explicit-opt-in-for-mobile-push per DE-TTDSG-2024 + DE-TKG-2024 + DE-Werbe-Recht-2024 while FR-recipients require explicit-opt-in-for-mobile-push per FR-CNIL-2024 + FR-Code-de-la-consommation-2024. A common error is treating-DE-as-identical-to-FR-GDPR-consent-state which causes per-region-mobile-push-silent-fallback-mode-detection firing for 2-5% of EU-cross-border-BFCM-traffic where the recipient is DE-located but their mobile-push is FR-consented (or vice versa).
