## Common pitfalls (16 from real builds)

P1. **Building per-platform-correlation without Move #1.9 priority-orchestration-emitted as primary input** — when the correlation-engine starts from Move #1.9 priority-orchestration-emitted events but doesn't gate on Move #1.9 priority-class being emitted, the engine over-correlates 80%+ of creators (every Move #1.9 prediction gets a per-platform-per-tier-correlation regardless of whether the Move #1.9 priority-class was emitted). Mitigation: gate the per-platform-per-tier-correlation-engine on `priority_class ∈ {P0, P1, P2}` AND `priority_orchestration_at_timestamp IS NOT NULL`; check the priority-class-emitted gate in the per-platform-per-tier-correlation-engine code; build a Move #1.9 priority-class-emitted-consumer first (Phase 1 step 3) before the per-platform-per-tier-correlation-engine (Phase 2 step 9).
