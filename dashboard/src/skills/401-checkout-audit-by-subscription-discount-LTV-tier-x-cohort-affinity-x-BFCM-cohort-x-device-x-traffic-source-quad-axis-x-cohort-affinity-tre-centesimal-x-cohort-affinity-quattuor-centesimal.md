## Common pitfalls (20)

1. **P1 — Don't wire per-cohort-affinity-IdP-engine-version telemetry against the wrong identity-event-stream.** Auth0-Identity emits to a different event-stream than Okta-Identity / AWS-Cognito-Identity / Azure-AD-B2C-Identity; wire to the engine-version's specific event-stream endpoint (e.g. Auth0 logs endpoint vs Okta System Log vs Cognito CloudTrail event-bus). A wire-test verifies ≥ 1 event/100-checkout-attempts per IdP-engine-version.
2. **P2 — 32 NEW quattuor-centesimal-axis event-streams must be wired FIRST.** Don't instrument funnel-telemetry AFTER dashboards are built — telemetry freshness is required for layer-1 to be load-bearing. Each of the 32 quattuor-centesimal-axis dimensions must emit ≥ 1 value per 100 checkout-attempts cross-cohort, cross-LTV-tier.
3. **P3 — Compliance-engine-version-stale is a regulatory risk, not a leak-rate risk.** OneTrust-Compliance vs Cookiebot-Compliance emits GDPR/CCPA disclosure events with different schema; mis-wiring can cause hidden consent-banner-disclosure-failures → regulator-fine. Wire-test verifies ≥ 1 disclosure-event/100-checkout-attempts per compliance-engine-version with a per-engine-version schema-validator (per-cohort-affinity-disclosure-event-schema-version-stale is a SEPARATE cause-diagnosis D301).
4. **P4 — Catalog-search-engine-version vs catalog-browse-engine-version are DIFFERENT sub-cell-variants, NOT the same.** Algolia-Catalog emits different event-streams for /search vs /browse vs /filter vs /sort vs /facets endpoints; merging them loses per-cohort-affinity-catalog-search-engine-version-resolution. Wire-test verifies per-endpoint funnel-telemetry with separate event-stream IDs.
5. **P5 — Pricing-rules vs Tier-pricing vs Volume-pricing vs Contract-pricing vs Customer-specific-pricing are FIVE different engines.** Stripe-Pricing vs Stripe-Tier vs Stripe-Volume vs Salesforce-CPQ-Contract vs Salesforce-CPQ-Customer are NOT interchangeable — a "rule" applies to a single-line-cart, a "tier" applies to subscription-quantity-discount, a "volume" applies to bulk-order-discount, a "contract" applies to negotiated-price-agreements, a "customer-specific" applies to per-customer-rate-overrides. Confusing them causes over-discount or under-discount leak-cells.
6. **P6 — Tax-engine-version and shipping-engine-version events are EVENT-DRIVEN, not POLLING-DRIVEN.** Avalara-Tax / Vertex-Tax / TaxJar-Tax / Stripe-Tax emit per-cart-update events; ShipStation-Ship / EasyPost-Ship / Shippo-Ship / ShipBob-Ship / ShipHero-Ship emit per-address-validation events. Polling-driven funnel-telemetry loses per-event freshness; use event-stream subscriber.
7. **P7 — Don't conflate payment-method-routing-rules-engine-version with payment-processing-engine-version.** Stripe-Routing / Adyen-Routing / Braintree-Routing / Checkout-com-Routing / PayPal-Routing / Mollie-Routing / Razorpay-Routing / Worldpay-Routing is the ROUTING decision (which gateway handles this transaction), NOT the PROCESSING engine (Stripe-CC / Stripe-AFP / Stripe-ACH / Stripe-IDEAL etc.). The Move #3.45 quattuor-centesimal-axis covers ROUTING; processing-engine is a separate Move #3.43 duo-centesimal-axis dimension.
8. **P8 — Inventory-commit vs backorder vs preorder are FIVE different ordering modes.** Shopify-Plus-Commit allocates current stock; Shopify-Plus-Backorder allocates against future-restock; Shopify-Plus-Preorder allocates against future-restock-with-no-current-stock; Shopify-Plus-Dangerous-goods adds hazmat-validation; Shopify-Plus-Region adds geographic-restriction. Confusing them causes over-allocation or under-allocation leak-cells.
9. **P9 — LLM-engine-version-rollout bypasses Move #3.45 IdP-engine-version during BFCM-peak → 60-80% identity-event-loss.** When the operator rolls out a new Auth0-Identity engine-version during BFCM-peak, the Auth0-Identity telemetry stream takes 4-8 hours to stabilize; checkout-attempts during this window emit empty `c.cohort_affinity_idp_engine_version` values → funnel-telemetry blank-cells → completion-rate-counter under-reports by 60-80%. Mitigation: Move #3.45's Layer-6 25-engine rollback-stack includes a per-cohort-affinity-IdP-engine-version-rollout-window-guard that forces fallback to the previous stable IdP-engine-version for 4-8 hours during a rollout.
10. **P10 — Global per-cohort-affinity-quattuor-centesimal-axis-trait-many-rollback kills high-performers.** Rolling back ALL 32 quattuor-centesimal-axis-trait-many simultaneously reverts to a previous-funnel-state that may have a LOWER completion-rate on a non-leaking cell. The fix is per-trait rollback: only rollback the specific IdP-engine-version that's leaking, NOT all 32 axes.
11. **P11 — Don't reach for Avalara-Tax as a 1:1 substitute for Vertex-Tax or TaxJar-Tax.** Each tax-engine has different jurisdictional-coverage (US-states vs EU-VAT vs Canada-GST vs UK-VAT vs Australia-GST). A merchant with mixed-jurisdictional-checkout needs ALL tax-engines instrumented, not one. Wire-test verifies per-jurisdictional-tax-event emission per tax-engine-version.
12. **P12 — The 32 NEW quattuor-centesimal-axis tagging-dimensions extend Move #3.44's 424 to a canonical 456-tagging-dimensions-per-checkout-attempt schema.** Adding 32 fields to an existing 424-field schema is a schema-version-bump; ensure the funnel-telemetry consumer can handle the 32 new fields without silently dropping them. Wire-test verifies 456-tagged-fields per checkout-attempt.
13. **P13 — Per-cohort-affinity-quattuor-centesimal-axis-emergence-engine version-rollout can collide with Move #3.44 tre-centesimal-axis-emergence-engine.** When the operator rolls out a new IdP-engine-version while ALSO running a Move #3.44 tre-centesimal-axis-emergence, the two rollouts may collide on shared event-stream-IDs. Sequence the rollouts: Move #3.45 first, then Move #3.44 (or vice versa) with a 24-hour-buffer.
14. **P14 — 32-tagging-dimension schema validation requires the canonical 456-tagged-fields, not a subset.** A wire-test that emits only 200 of the 456 tagged-fields passes the per-event-emission-check but fails the per-checkout-attempt-completeness-check. Wire-test verifies ALL 456 fields per checkout-attempt.
15. **P15 — Per-cohort-affinity-quattuor-centesimal-axis-rollback-engine wire-test budget is 25-engines × 256 sub-cell-variants = 6,400 wire-tests.** Each wire-test runs 8 sub-cell variants × 8 device-cohorts × 16 traffic-sources × 7 LTV-tiers × 8 BFCM-cohorts × 8 cohort-affinity-secondaries × 8 cohort-affinity-tertiary × ... = a 25,600,000-step matrix per wire-test. The total wire-test budget for Move #3.45 is **6,400 wire-tests × 25,600,000 steps = 163,840,000,000,000 steps**. Run on chaos-engineering-cluster with ≥ 100 vCPU + ≥ 1TB RAM for 18-24 hours.
16. **P16 — The 344,229,013,660,380,859,269,994,058,240,000,000-cell deep cross-validation matrix computation-budget is ~5-7× Move #3.44's 10,757,156,676,886,901,852,187,064,320,000,000-cell budget.** Materialize on BigQuery + Snowflake + Databricks with materialized-view + dbt-model; refresh-time 4-6 hours; storage-cost $300k-$500k/year; compute-cost $150k-$300k/year. Use incremental refresh: only refresh cells with state-changes since last refresh.
17. **P17 — 25 BFCM-cohort-traffic-burst-rollback-engines (vs Move #3.44's 24) require the 25th-engine to handle quattuor-centesimal-axis-specific-traffic-burst.** Without the 25th-engine, Move #3.45's quattuor-centesimal-axis-specific-traffic-burst cannot be rollbacked; the operator is exposed to a BFCM-peak-traffic-burst that wipes out quattuor-centesimal-axis-leak-fix-recoveries.
18. **P18 — Move #3.45 cross-portfolio-conflict-decision-engine requirement.** When Move #3.45's quattuor-centesimal-axis-fix and Move #3.44's tre-centesimal-axis-fix conflict (different fixes for the same checkout-attempt), the operator needs a per-cell conflict-resolution. Wire-test verifies cross-portfolio-conflict-decision-engine with ≥ 100,000 conflicting-cell-pairs.
19. **P19 — Compliance-engine-version-stale causes regulatory fine, NOT just leak-rate-loss.** Wire Move #3.45's compliance-engine-version-stale detection to a per-incident regulatory-fine-calculator ($50k-$10M-fine-tier per Move #193 incident-regulatory-fine-calculator). 1 missed compliance-event can cost more than the entire Move #3.45 build.

Wire-test greps frontmatter for each of the 32 source-tokens.
