# Loyalty engagement flows — tier-up + tier-down + points-expiry + birthday + anniversary + NPS-detractor (the lifecycle engagement Tier-1 pillar, Move #1.4)

> The loyalty-engagement pillar is the **highest-LTV compounding layer** in the entire lifecycle-marketing library: it converts Move #8 (loyalty program) from a points-issuance system into a **per-customer lifecycle signal that fires on 6 different emotional moments** (tier-up celebration, tier-down warning, points-expiry rescue, birthday celebration, purchase anniversary, NPS-detractor service-recovery). Per Klaviyo 2024 + Smile.io 2024 benchmarks, the 6 flows together convert at **8–22%** (vs the canonical 2–5% of cart-abandon + welcome + browse-abandon), lift 12-month LTV by **40–70%** for the touched cohort, and recover **25–50% of at-risk tier-down customers** + **30–50% of at-risk points-expiry customers** + **20–40% of NPS-detractors** (from detractor to passive). Together they form **Pillar 4** of the canonical 5-pillar lifecycle-marketing framework (research/05): the only pillar that **compounds per-customer LTV** instead of acquiring new buyers. Without tier-up + tier-down + birthday + anniversary + NPS-detractor, the operator captures only the 18–30% of the loyalty program's revenue potential — every Smile-points-issued customer who doesn't get a tier-up celebration will churn 2–3× faster, every NPS-detractor who gets a no-reply email escalates to Twitter 4–6× more often, and every annual-buyer who doesn't get a birthday gift misses the highest-ROI single touchpoint in the database. Ship after Move #1 (cart-abandon) + Move #4 (welcome) + Move #7 (SMS) + Move #8 (loyalty program) + skill/490 (browse-abandon) + skill/491 (winback-sunset) + skill/492 (post-purchase) are all live and producing baseline data; the 6 loyalty-engagement flows reuse the same Klaviyo + Postscript + Smile + Gorgias wiring and the same segmentation hygiene, so they ship in **~5 hours of operator time** and return a Year-1 ROI band of **9:1–30:1** at the default $500k–$10M GMV DTC brand.

## When to use this skill

You have:

- Move #1 (cart-abandon flow) shipped with ≥6-week revenue baseline
- Move #4 (welcome series) shipped with ≥6-week revenue baseline
- Move #7 (SMS welcome + cart-abandon) shipped with ≥4-week revenue baseline
- Move #8 (loyalty program) shipped with Smile.io (or Yotpo / LoyaltyLion) points + tiers + VIP tier + birthday-field + points-expiry configured
- Skill/490 (browse-abandon + consideration flows) shipped with ≥4-week revenue baseline
- Skill/491 (winback + sunset) shipped with ≥4-week revenue baseline
- Skill/492 (post-purchase cross-sell + replenishment + educational) shipped with ≥4-week revenue baseline
- Klaviyo onsite JS installed and `Placed Order` + `Subscribed to List` + `Fulfilled` + `Opened Email` + `Clicked Email` events flowing
- Smile.io events flowing into Klaviyo (via Smile's Klaviyo integration: `points_earned` + `tier_changed` + `vip_status_changed` + `points_expiring` + `birthday` + `anniversary` events)
- Postscript (or Attentive) SMS wired with sender registration + opt-in compliance (TCPA/GDPR)
- NPS survey is live (per `playbook/06-cx-voice-of-customer` or equivalent) with ≥50 NPS responses / month flowing into Klaviyo (the `NPS Score` custom property)
- Gorgias (or Kustomer / Zendesk / Helpscout) wired with macro-tier routing for `intent = NPS-detractor` (per Asset 11 Module 6)
- Klaviyo + Postscript + Smile + Gorgias all on the same customer (CDP-stitched via Segment / RudderStack / Klaviyo Data Platform for cross-tool identity)
- Customer list hygiene: ≥1,000 active customers segmented into ≥3 baseline segments (active customer / lapsed customer / subscriber-not-buyer) + per-tier segments (Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3 / VIP)
- Operator capacity: 5-hour kickoff block + 1.5-hour weekly maintenance block for the first 8 weeks

You do NOT have:

- A **tier-up celebration flow** that emails + SMS's the customer within 1 hour of crossing a tier threshold (the canonical "you've been promoted" moment)
- A **tier-down warning flow** that surfaces a 30-day-prior warning when a customer's points-balance projects below the current tier threshold (the canonical "earn [X] points to keep your tier" rescue)
- A **points-expiry warning flow** that surfaces a 30-day-prior warning when Smile.io points are about to expire (the canonical "use them before they expire" rescue)
- A **birthday flow** that emails + SMS's the customer on their birthday month with a personalized gift / discount
- A **anniversary flow** that emails the customer on the 1-year / 2-year / 5-year anniversary of their first purchase (the canonical "thanks for being a customer" moment)
- A **NPS-detractor follow-up flow** that routes the 0–6 NPS responders to a human CS-rep within 4 hours via Gorgias (not a no-reply email)
- A **NPS-promoter amplification flow** that routes the 9–10 NPS responders to a review-request + referral-invitation dual CTA
- A **NPS-passive nurture flow** that surfaces a "we'd love a 9 or 10" follow-up to the 7–8 NPS responders
- A **per-LTV-tier branching** in the tier-up celebration (Tier 3 → VIP is a much bigger moment than Tier 1 → Tier 2; the copy + perks block must branch)
- A **NPS-detractor → Gorgias macro-tier routing** (the canonical "no-reply email + Twitter escalation" pitfall guard)
- A **Smile.io `points_to_next_tier` dynamic block** in the tier-down warning email (the canonical "earn [X] points to keep your [Tier]" copy)
- A **birthday-field-validation filter** in the birthday flow trigger (else "happy birthday" fires on the account-creation date for customers who never filled in their birthday)
- A **1-discount-per-30-days guard** that prevents tier-up 15% off + birthday 20% off + NPS-promoter 10% off + cart-abandon 10% off + welcome 10% off + winback 15% off + browse-abandon 10% off from all firing on the same customer in 30 days
- A **CDP-stitched identity layer** (Segment / RudderStack / Klaviyo Data Platform) that resolves the same customer across Klaviyo + Postscript + Smile + Gorgias (else the tier-up email fires but the SMS doesn't, or the NPS-detractor email fires but the Gorgias ticket doesn't get created)

## The build (time estimate: ~5 hours total)

This is a 6-phase build: (Phase 1: 1 hour for tier-up celebration flow, Phase 2: 1 hour for tier-down warning flow, Phase 3: 30 min for points-expiry warning flow, Phase 4: 1 hour for birthday + anniversary flows, Phase 5: 1 hour for NPS-detractor + promoter + passive flows, Phase 6: 30 min for verification + monitoring). Each phase is concrete and idempotent; you can pause and resume at any phase boundary.

### Phase 1 — Tier-up celebration flow (~1 hour)

1. **Create the tier-up celebration flow in Klaviyo** (5 min). Navigate to Flows → Create Flow → "Create from scratch" → name it `loyalty_tier_up_v1`. Set the trigger to **"Smile.io `Tier Changed`"** event with a filter of **"new tier is higher than previous tier"** (the canonical "promotion" filter, scoped to upward-only tier changes — downward changes route to the tier-down warning flow in Phase 2). The flow is gated on Move #8 baseline (so the tier-up doesn't fire on the first 30 days of loyalty-program operation when tier changes are still bootstrapping).
2. **Add the 2-email + 1-SMS cadence** (15 min). Email #1 at trigger + 0h ("Welcome to [New Tier]! — here's your [perk]"), Email #2 at +7d ("Your [New Tier] perks — explore them now"), SMS #1 at trigger + 0h ("You're now [New Tier] in [Brand]! 🎉 — [perk] inside"). The 1-hour trigger latency is critical: Klaviyo 2024 benchmark shows tier-up emails sent within 1h of the `tier_changed` event convert 2-3× higher than 24h+ delayed emails. The 0h SMS is the canonical "the moment you got promoted" touchpoint.
3. **Wire the Smile.io dynamic-perks block** (10 min). Each tier-up email needs a Smile.io dynamic block surfacing the customer's new tier's perks (e.g., "Tier 2 perks: free shipping on orders $50+ / 1.5× points earning / early access to drops"). The perks block is the canonical "here's what just unlocked" content; without it, the email is generic and converts 30-50% lower per Smile.io 2024. Use Smile.io's `{{ smile.tier_perks }}` dynamic block fed by the tier's metadata.
4. **Wire the dynamic product block** (10 min). Each tier-up email needs a Klaviyo Product Feed dynamic block surfacing 4 products from the new tier's "free early access" or "tier-restricted" catalog (if the loyalty program has tier-restricted SKUs). Without the dynamic block, the email is perk-list-only and converts 20-35% lower. For programs without tier-restricted SKUs, use a generic "Best sellers in [New Tier]" block.
5. **Set the Smart Sending window to 16 hours** (5 min). Navigate to the flow's Settings → Smart Sending → "Time between messages" → 16 hours. This prevents a single customer from getting tier-up email + cart-abandon email + welcome email + browse-abandon email all in 1 day. 16 hours is the canonical Klaviyo 2024 default; <12 hours is risky for spam complaints.
6. **Set the flow's send-time optimizer to "Smart Sending time"** (5 min). Use Klaviyo's "Send at the recipient's local time" feature (Settings → Send-Time → "Use recipient's local time zone"). The 1-hour-trigger latency is critical for tier-up; the local-time send-time adds another 5-15% open-rate lift.
7. **Add the 3-tier conditional split on Email #1** (10 min). Implement a **conditional split** on the customer's new tier (Tier 1 → Tier 2 / Tier 2 → Tier 3 / Tier 3 → VIP). Each branch gets a tier-specific subject line + perk block + product block. The Tier 3 → VIP branch gets the highest-touch treatment (subject "You're VIP — welcome to the inner circle" + VIP-only perk list + VIP-only product block). Per Klaviyo 2024's A/B test report, the 3-tier split lifts Email #1 open-rate +20-40% and CVR +25-50% vs single-tier copy.
8. **Wire the 1-discount-per-30-days guard** (5 min). Create a Klaviyo segment `discount_redeemed_last_30d` (definition: "has placed order in last 30d" OR "has clicked any tier-up / birthday / NPS-promoter / winback / cart-abandon / welcome / browse-abandon discount email in last 30d" OR "is in welcome series discount redemption in last 30d"). Add this segment as a flow **suppression** filter on the tier-up flow: "Suppress this flow if customer is in `discount_redeemed_last_30d`". This is the canonical anti-discount-stacking guard. The tier-up flow typically does NOT include a discount (it's a perks-only moment), but the guard catches the rare case where the brand offers a "tier-up bonus discount" (e.g., "Tier 3 → VIP gets a 1-time 15% off").

### Phase 2 — Tier-down warning flow (~1 hour)

9. **Create the tier-down warning flow in Klaviyo** (5 min). Navigate to Flows → Create Flow → "Create from scratch" → name it `loyalty_tier_down_v1`. Set the trigger to **"Smile.io `Points Expiring`"** event with a filter of **"current tier is at-risk"** (the canonical "tier-eligibility-window projection" trigger, fired when a customer's points-balance is projected to fall below the current tier threshold within 30 days). The 30-day warning window is the canonical Smile.io 2024 default; <14 days is too late (customer has no time to act), >60 days is too early (warning is forgotten by the time the downgrade fires).
10. **Add the 2-email cadence** (10 min). Email #1 at trigger + 0d ("Your [Tier] status is at risk — earn [X] points by [date] to keep it"), Email #2 at +14d ("Last reminder: [X] points to keep your [Tier] — here's how"). The 2-email cadence is the canonical tier-down warning structure; <2 emails leaves the customer with no second chance to act, >3 emails creates fatigue.
11. **Wire the `points_to_next_tier` dynamic block** (10 min). Each tier-down email needs a Smile.io dynamic block surfacing the customer's EXACT points-gap + the SPECIFIC actions to close the gap (e.g., "Spend $50 to earn 50 points and keep your Tier 2 status" / "Refer a friend to earn 100 points / Buy 2 products to earn 60 points"). The block is the canonical "earn [X] points" copy; without it, the email is generic and converts 40-60% lower per Smile.io 2024.
12. **Add the 3-tier conditional split on Email #1** (10 min). Implement a **conditional split** on the customer's current tier (Tier 2 / Tier 3 / VIP). The VIP → Tier 3 branch gets the highest-touch treatment (subject "Your VIP status is at risk — let's keep you in" + 1:1 concierge outreach + bonus-points offer). Per Klaviyo 2024's A/B test report, the VIP-branch treatment rescues 60-80% of at-risk VIPs vs 25-40% for Tier 2/3.
13. **Set the Smart Sending window to 16 hours** (5 min). Same as Phase 1.5. Tier-down warning emails should not stack with cart-abandon / welcome / browse-abandon / post-purchase / winback / tier-up / birthday flows.
14. **Wire the 1-discount-per-30-days guard** (5 min). Add `discount_redeemed_last_30d` as a flow **suppression** filter (per Phase 1.8). Tier-down warning flows typically include a "earn 2× points this week" offer (a non-discount offer), but the guard catches the rare case where the brand offers a "tier-rescue 15% off".
15. **Wire the in-app + SMS escalation** (15 min). For Tier 3 → Tier 2 downgrades (the highest-LTV-loss moment), wire a Postscript SMS escalation: SMS at +7d ("Your Tier 3 is at risk — earn 2× points this week to keep it"). The SMS escalation is the canonical multi-channel rescue; the email alone rescues 25-40%, the email + SMS rescues 50-70% per Smile.io 2024.

The 2-email cadence is canonical; <2 emails misses the 25-40% who don't act on Email #1, >3 emails creates fatigue.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Tier-up celebration flow fires more than 24h after the `tier_changed` event.** Customer crosses the tier threshold at 2am, the Klaviyo flow trigger is set to "Send at next batch send" instead of "Send immediately", the email fires at 9am the next morning, the customer's "I just got promoted" emotional moment is gone, the email opens at the 25th-percentile benchmark. **Fix: set the flow trigger to "Send immediately" + verify the trigger latency in Klaviyo's flow-analytics dashboard. The 1-hour-trigger latency is the canonical "tier-up moment" win; per Smile.io 2024, immediate triggers convert 2-3× higher than 24h+ delayed triggers. Verification Gate B is the canonical test (target: ≥95% of tier-up emails are sent within 1h of the `tier_changed` event).**
2. **Tier-up celebration uses generic "Welcome to [Tier]!" copy for all tiers.** A Tier 1 → Tier 2 customer and a Tier 3 → VIP customer receive the same email, the VIP customer feels under-celebrated, the Tier 2 customer feels over-celebrated. **Fix: implement the 3-tier conditional split on Email #1 (Phase 1.7) with tier-specific subject lines + perk blocks + product blocks. The Tier 3 → VIP branch gets the highest-touch treatment; verification Gate C is the canonical test (target: ≥3 distinct tier-specific email variants in the live flow).**
3. **Tier-down warning flow triggers on Smile.io's `Tier Changed` event instead of a points-projection.** Customer's points-balance drops below the tier threshold, Smile fires `Tier Changed`, the customer is already downgraded by the time the warning email fires, the warning is too late. **Fix: trigger the flow on Smile.io's `Points Expiring` event + a custom Smile-property "tier-at-risk" computed by Smile's pre-tier-threshold projection (typically `points_balance < tier_threshold AND projected_30d_balance < tier_threshold`). The 30-day-prior projection is the canonical "tier-rescue" trigger; verification Gate F is the canonical test (target: ≥95% of tier-down warnings are sent 30d before the projected tier-loss date).**
4. **Tier-down warning email says "Your tier is at risk" but doesn't tell the customer how to save it.** Customer reads "your tier is at risk", doesn't know how many points they need, doesn't know what actions earn points, ignores the email, loses the tier. **Fix: wire the `points_to_next_tier` dynamic block (Phase 2.11) with the EXACT points-gap + the SPECIFIC actions to close the gap. The block is the canonical "earn [X] points to keep your [Tier]" copy; per Smile.io 2024, the block lifts tier-rescue rate from 25-40% to 50-70%. Verification Gate G is the canonical test (target: every tier-down email includes a dynamic `points_to_next_tier` block with both gap + actions).**
5. **Points-expiry warning flow fires on a 60-day warning instead of 30-day.** Customer's 200 points expire in 60 days, gets the warning, the email is forgotten by day 30, the points expire un-redeemed. **Fix: set the flow trigger to "exactly 30 days before expiry" + add a 7-day-prior reminder (Phase 3.17). The 30-day warning is the canonical Smile.io 2024 default; 60-day is too early. Verification Gate H is the canonical test (target: ≥95% of points-expiry warnings are sent 30d before expiry).**
6. **Points-expiry warning flow excludes customers who have less than 50 points expiring.** Customer has 30 points expiring, the flow filter is "expiring_points > 50", the customer is excluded, loses the 30 points. **Fix: remove the minimum-points filter. Every expiring-points balance is worth the email; per Smile.io 2024, 30-100 point expiries convert at 15-25% redemption rate, 100-500 point expiries convert at 30-50%, 500+ point expiries convert at 50-70%. The aggregate is significant.**
7. **Birthday flow sends to customers who haven't filled in their birthday field.** Customer has `birthday = null` in Klaviyo, the flow trigger is "birthday is today" but the date-property default to the account-creation date, customer receives "Happy birthday from [Brand]!" in their account-creation month, the customer is confused. **Fix: enforce the "birthday field must be valid month-day" filter (Phase 4.21) + add a "fill in your birthday for $5 off" prompt at account creation + first order + loyalty-program enrollment to populate the field. Verification Gate L is the canonical test (target: 0% of birthday emails are sent to customers with empty birthday field).**
8. **Anniversary flow triggers on "approximately 1 year since first order" using a 30-day fuzzy window.** Customer's first order was 2025-01-15, the flow triggers on 2025-12-15 to 2026-01-15, the email fires in mid-December, the customer doesn't see it as a "1-year anniversary" email. **Fix: trigger the flow on the EXACT date (Phase 4.25) using Klaviyo's date-property trigger + a 0-day-fuzzy window. The exact-day trigger is critical; "approximately 1 year" triggers miss the emotional moment. Verification Gate M is the canonical test (target: ≥95% of anniversary emails are sent on the exact day-of-anniversary).**
9. **NPS-detractor follow-up flow sends a no-reply email and the customer escalates to Twitter.** Detractor receives "we'd love to make it right" from `no-reply@brand.com`, takes to Twitter, gets 100× the visibility the no-reply email would have gotten, the brand's PR takes a 5-15pp hit. **Fix: implement the Gorgias macro-tier routing (Phase 5.30) with `intent = NPS-detractor` + auto-assign to CS-rep on duty + SLA 4h. The email is from a human's name + title, not `no-reply`. Per Gorgias 2024, this pattern reduces Twitter-escalation from 12-25% to 2-5%. Verification Gate K is the canonical test (target: 100% of NPS-detractor emails route to a Gorgias ticket + 0% are sent from `no-reply@`).**
10. **NPS-detractor follow-up flow's "we're sorry" perk is a 15% off coupon that stacks with cart-abandon + welcome + winback + tier-up discounts.** Customer receives NPS-detractor 15% off, the same customer is also in the cart-abandon 10% off flow, the codes stack to 25% off, margin destruction. **Fix: enforce the 1-discount-per-30-days guard (Phase 5.36) via the `discount_redeemed_last_30d` suppression segment. The NPS-detractor follow-up's "we're sorry" perk is typically a non-monetary gesture (free shipping / bonus points / complimentary product), not a discount; per Gorgias 2024, non-monetary gestures have 1.5-2× higher CSAT-rescue rate than discount gestures.**
11. **NPS-promoter amplification flow's "Give $20, get $20" CTA uses a static discount code that's shared with the referral program.** Customer uses the static code, refers a friend, the friend uses the same code, both get $20 off, but the brand's accounting can't attribute the second $20 to the original referrer. **Fix: use Smile.io's "Unique referral code" feature to generate a per-customer code; the unique code is single-use-per-customer, tracks attribution, and is 1-discount-per-30-days-guard compatible. The static-code anti-pattern is the canonical NPS-promoter + referral-integration pitfall.**
12. **NPS-passive nurture flow sends the re-survey email to customers who just took the original NPS survey 7 days ago.** Customer takes NPS on day 0, gets a 7 (passive), receives the re-survey email on day 1, is annoyed at the immediate ask. **Fix: set the re-survey trigger to "60 days after the original NPS" (Phase 5.35). The 60-day delay is the canonical Delighted 2024 default; <30 days is too soon, >90 days is too long.**
13. **Birthday flow includes a discount but the customer.birthday field is empty for >50% of the customer base.** Operator has 5,000 customers, 2,500 don't have a birthday field, the birthday flow is fired for the 2,500 who do + the 2,500 fallback-to-account-creation-date. The 50% "fallback" customers mark the email as spam, dragging deliverability. **Fix: enforce the birthday-field-validation filter (Phase 4.21) + run a "fill in your birthday for $5 off" campaign before launching the birthday flow. The campaign should target the 2,500 empty-birthday customers via a 2-email cadence ("we want to send you a birthday gift" + "fill in your birthday for $5 off"). Per Smile.io 2024, this lifts the birthday-field-population rate from 30-50% to 60-80% within 60 days.**
14. **The 6 loyalty-engagement flows are built but Smile.io + Klaviyo identity isn't stitched, so the tier-up email fires but the SMS doesn't (or the NPS-detractor email fires but the Gorgias ticket doesn't get created).** Customer crosses tier, gets the tier-up email, but the Postscript SMS doesn't fire because the customer's Klaviyo profile email and Postscript profile phone aren't stitched. **Fix: implement the CDP-stitched identity layer (Segment / RudderStack / Klaviyo Data Platform) BEFORE the 6 flows ship. The CDP must resolve the same customer across Klaviyo + Postscript + Smile + Gorgias by email + phone + customer-ID. Per Segment 2024, the stitch lifts cross-channel flow effectiveness 25-50% on the first 90 days. Verification Gate N is the canonical test (target: ≥95% of customers in any of the 6 flows have a matching profile in all 4 tools).**
15. **The 6 loyalty-engagement flows are built but the operator never returns to optimize. First-month revenue is $3k, second-month revenue is $3k (no growth), operator assumes "the flows are at their ceiling".** **Fix: schedule a 30-day post-launch flow-performance review (Klaviyo's flow-analytics dashboard + per-flow revenue + per-flow open-rate + per-flow CVR + per-flow unsubscribe rate + deliverability dashboard). Typical first-30-day lift: 30-50% from subject-line + send-time + per-LTV-tier-branch A/B iterations. The weekly maintenance runbook (Phase 6.39) is the canonical 30-day-review trigger.**
