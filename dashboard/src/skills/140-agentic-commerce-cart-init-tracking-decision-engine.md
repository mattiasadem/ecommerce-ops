## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

**Cluster A — Per-cart-init-tracking-decision-router + Per-cart-init-tracking-decision-decision-rule-trace-id-emission**

1. **Per-cart-init × per-tracking-event ingestion SPOF — single Kafka topic + no DLQ + no per-carrier-routing-decoder-id replay-window.** The canonical 2026-Q2 pattern is to wire a single Kafka topic `cart-init-tracking-event-ingest` with one DLQ `cart-init-tracking-event-ingest-dlq`, no per-carrier-routing-decoder-id replay-window, and no per-cart-init × per-tracking-event × per-cohort-id partitioning. Result: a single carrier webhook-burst (e.g. UPS late-evening tracking-deluge) blows up the topic, the DLQ fills with 1M+ unprocessed events, no per-carrier-routing-decoder-id replay-window means no recovery path, and the per-cart-init × per-tracking-decision-rule-coverage silently drops from 95% → 30% for 4-12 hours. **Fix:** wire 3 Kafka topics (`cart-init-tracking-event-ingest-usps` + `cart-init-tracking-event-ingest-fedex` + `cart-init-tracking-event-ingest-ups` + `cart-init-tracking-event-ingest-dhl` + `cart-init-tracking-event-ingest-amazon-shipping` + `cart-init-tracking-event-ingest-walmart-shipping` + `cart-init-tracking-event-ingest-shopify-shipping`), per-carrier DLQ + per-cart-init × per-tracking-event × per-cohort-id partitioning + 7-day replay-window + per-carrier-routing-decoder-id idempotency-key.
