## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing DT-tier (tooling) with DM-tier (mode) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.32].** DT-tier answers WHICH disclosure-TOOLING enforces the per-post practice (Meta-Brand-Collabs-Manager + TikTok-Creator-Marketplace + YouTube-Paid-Promotion-Badge + in-house-aggregator + MSA-archive-link); DM-tier answers HOW the disclosure is MODE-rendered (#ad-disclosure + credentials-disclosure + reactivator-disclosure + #publicidad-disclosure + vintage-disclosed-at-onboard). The two axes are orthogonal and must be carried as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers — collapsing them breaks EU UCPD 2026 member-state language-conditional compliance AND breaks the per-cohort × per-creator × per-DM-tier × per-DT-tier cross-axis closure. A DT1 Meta-Brand-Collabs creator with Paid-Partnership-Tag can ride on DM1 #ad-disclosure (the typical pairing) OR on DM7 reactivator-disclosure (for reactivator-window-content); a DT5 MSA-archive-link creator with vintage-disclosure-record can ride on DM5 vintage-disclosed-at-onboard (typical) OR on DM1 #ad-disclosure (for high-touch brands). Collapsing DT × DM into a single tier erases this orthogonality.

2. **Pitfall — DT1 Meta creators mis-classified as DT2 TikTok on a missing-Paid-Partnership-Tag basis.** Meta Brand Collabs Manager 2026 requires the Paid-Partnership-Tag to be ACTIVE for ALL sponsored posts on Instagram/Facebook, regardless of the creator's follower-count. Creators without an active Paid-Partnership-Tag are silently demoted to DT2 TikTok creators (no actual savings — TikTok Creator Marketplace requires Branded-Mission setup which is more friction). **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling classifier must read the Paid-Partnership-Tag-active-state field directly, not infer from creator-platform alone — a Meta creator without active Paid-Partnership-Tag is NON-COMPLIANT, not DT2 TikTok.

3. **Pitfall — DT2 TikTok creators on Branded-Mission-only-pool fail TikTok Creator Marketplace 2026 Branded-Content-Tag fallback.** TikTok Creator Marketplace 2026 requires Branded-Mission for sponsored content under the marketplace flow; creators under self-service Branded-Content-Tag (the legacy flow) are not technically marketplace-eligible but still get the green-dot-branded-content-tag. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling classifier must distinguish between marketplace-Eligible-Branded-Mission creators and self-service-Branded-Content-Tag creators; route to DT1 Meta for self-service fallbacks.

4. **Pitfall — DT3 YouTube creators on Paid-Promotion-Badge fail YouTube Paid Promotion Badge API 2026 monetization-disclosure-overlay.** YouTube Paid Promotion Badge API 2026 requires BOTH the badge-overlay AND the 'Includes Paid Promotion' shell-text for sponsored videos; creators with only the badge-overlay without the shell-text are silently demoted to DT4 in-house-aggregator creators. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling classifier must verify both the badge-overlay AND the shell-text state — a YouTube creator with badge-only is NON-COMPLIANT.

5. **Pitfall — DT4 in-house-aggregator creators fail cross-platform-rollup-disclosure-aggregator integration.** The in-house-disclosure-rollup-API 2026 requires cross-platform-rollup of branded-content-tags from Meta + TikTok + YouTube + LinkedIn + Pinterest + Snap into a single disclosure-archive. Creators who don't have cross-platform integration are silently demoted to DT5 MSA-archive-link creators. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling classifier must verify the creator's cross-platform-integration-state; route to DT1 Meta creators for single-platform fallbacks.

6. **Pitfall — DT5 MSA-archive-link creators on vintage-disclosure-record fail FTC Endorsement Guides 2026 30-day-archive-retention.** FTC Endorsement Guides 2026 require all sponsored-content-records to be RETAINED for at least 30 days post-posting. A creator whose MSA-archive is older than 30 days is non-compliant. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling classifier must verify the MSA-archive's last-updated-date; if >30 days, route to DT1 Meta creators (Meta-Brand-Collabs auto-archives for 90 days) or DT4 in-house-aggregator creators (configurable retention).

7. **Pitfall — DT1 Meta creators on Paid-Partnership-Tag in cross-platform-cohorts fail cross-platform-disclosure-tag-conditional.** Meta Brand Collabs Manager 2026 Paid-Partnership-Tag is auto-enforced only on Instagram/Facebook. A creator posting TikTok-content with Meta-Brand-Collabs only fails TikTok-Creator-Marketplace compliance. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling classifier must verify the cohort's channel × creator-tooling combination BEFORE scoring resonance — DT1 × TikTok-channel score 0 unless the creator has TikTok-Creator-Marketplace-Branded-Mission as a secondary tooling.

8. **Pitfall — DT2 TikTok creators on Branded-Mission fail TikTok Creator Marketplace 2026 ≥10k-followers-eligibility.** TikTok Creator Marketplace 2026 Branded-Mission is only available to creators with ≥10k followers; creators with <10k followers cannot enroll in Branded-Mission and must use self-service Branded-Content-Tag. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling classifier must verify the creator's follower-count; if <10k, demote to DT4 in-house-aggregator or DT5 MSA-archive-link.

9. **Pitfall — DT3 YouTube creators on Paid-Promotion-Badge fail YouTube Partner Program monetization-disclosure-overlay.** YouTube Paid Promotion Badge 2026 requires monetization-disclosure-overlay for YouTube Partner Program-eligible creators; non-partner creators (with <1k subscribers) can use the badge but cannot monetize. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling classifier must verify the creator's YouTube-Partner-Program-eligibility; if not eligible, route to DT1 Meta creators for monetization-enabled fallbacks.

10. **Pitfall — DT4 in-house-aggregator creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The in-house-disclosure-rollup-API 2026 requires 1 FTE engineer to keep the API live + integrate new platforms + maintain compliance; low-margin cohorts (<$5M annual) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — DT4 score 0 for cohorts with <$5M annual revenue. Route to DT1 Meta creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

11. **Pitfall — DT5 MSA-archive-link creators fail legal-team-$15k-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The MSA-archive-link 2026 requires legal-team-maintenance for vintage-disclosure-record + FTC 30-day-retention-compliance + EU UCPD 7-year-retention-compliance; low-margin cohorts cannot afford legal overhead. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — DT5 score 0 for cohorts with <$5M annual revenue. Route to DT1 Meta creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

12. **Pitfall — DT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's tooling-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer DT1 Meta creators (lower tooling-cost, auto-enforced-by-Meta-platform) over DT5 MSA-archive-link creators (high tooling-cost, legal-team-$15k-maint-cost). A DT1 Meta creator may be disclosure-tooling-clean but tooling-cost-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-tooling-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — DT1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer DT5 MSA-archive-link creators, regardless of Paid-Partnership-Tag-cleanliness. The cohort's tooling-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-tooling-cost-sensitivity × cohort-cohort-follower-count-preference.

13. **Pitfall — DT-tier cross-axis with PN-tier silently double-tags sponsored content.** A creator on DT1 Meta-Brand-Collabs with PN2 TikTok-native as secondary platform is double-tagged (Meta Paid-Partnership-Tag + TikTok Branded-Content-Tag). The disclosure-tooling classifier must not double-count tooling on cross-platform-cohorts. **Fix:** the DT-tier × PN-tier cross-axis must apply secondary-platform-tooling-conditional — DT1 × PN2 score uses TikTok-Branded-Content-Tag as the secondary; the cohort's primary disclosure is Meta.

14. **Pitfall — DT4 in-house-aggregator creators on cross-platform-rollup-disclosure-aggregator mis-classified as DT5 MSA-archive-link because of rollup-failure-state.** A creator on DT4 in-house-aggregator whose cross-platform-rollup has failed (e.g., API down, integration broken) is silently demoted to DT5 MSA-archive-link creators. The actual state is "rollup-failed — disclosure not aggregated". **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling classifier must distinguish between rollup-success / rollup-failed / no-rollup states; route rollup-failed creators to DT5 MSA-archive-link as a temporary fallback.

15. **Pitfall — DT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's follower-count-preference.** A cohort with T1 nano creator preference (1k-10k followers) requires DT2 TikTok-Creator-Marketplace-Branded-Mission eligibility (≥10k followers); T1 nano creators cannot enroll in TikTok-Creator-Marketplace. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-follower-count-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — DT2 × T1-nano-cohort score 0; route to DT1 Meta or DT5 MSA-archive-link fallbacks.
