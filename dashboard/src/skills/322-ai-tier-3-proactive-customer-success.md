# AI tier-3 proactive customer success

> Move #20.3 is the canonical **tier-3 proactive customer success layer** that sits ABOVE Move #20.1 (skill/11 ai-customer-service-automation, tier-1 self-serve + AI-completion) and Move #20.2 (skill/321 ai-tier-2-customer-service-deflection, tier-2-agentic-resolution) and **prevents the ticket from ever being filed** by detecting at-risk-customers BEFORE they reach out. The engine fuses **5 layers**: (Layer 1) **multi-signal cohort-decline-detector** — Triple Whale cohort-LTV-decline + Recharge/Stay-AI/Smartrr/Appstle/Subbly churn-prediction + Smile VIP-points-balance-decline + Smile redeem-rate-decline + Gorgias/Kustomer/Zendesk ticket-sentiment-history-decline + Klaviyo/Postscript email-engagement-decline + Klaviyo/Iterable SMS-engagement-decline + Mixpanel/Amplitude product-usage-decline + browser-push-notification engagement-decline + Shopify order-frequency-decline + on-site-search-failure-rate-increase into a single **multi-signal-decay-curve** with a canonical 14-signal-detector-set (≥3-signal-decline-in-7-days = at-risk-flag, ≥5-signal-decline-in-14-days = high-risk-flag, ≥7-signal-decline-in-30-days = save-immediately-flag); (Layer 2) **proactive-intervention-channel-orchestrator** — at-risk-customer routed through a **5-channel proactive-cadence** (Klaviyo email → Postscript SMS → Iterable/Omnisend multi-channel → OneSignal/Iterate web-push-mobile-push → Gorgias/Kustomer/Zendesk AI-dialer / voice-AI-call OR Balto/Regal/PolyAI voice-AI) calibrated by per-customer channel-preference + per-customer channel-engagement-rate + per-customer channel-cost (SMS > voice > email > push) + per-cohort-LTV-tier (high-LTV → voice + manager-on-call, mid-LTV → SMS + email, low-LTV → email + push); (Layer 3) **per-customer-personalization + brand-voice-conditioned intervention** — GPT-4o / Claude 3.5 Sonnet renders the proactive message in the customer's brand-voice (F/H/BN/P/O profile per `assets/02-brand-voice.md`) + customer's per-cohort-tone (VIP-tier-formal vs gen-z-casual) + customer's prior-engagement-style (high-email-engagement = email-led; high-SMS-engagement = SMS-led; high-push-engagement = push-led) + customer's last-touch-product (reference the specific SKU they last purchased/viewed to anchor the intervention message); (Layer 4) **policy-engine + intervention-cost-ceiling** — every proactive-intervention is gated by a per-customer per-channel intervention-cost-ceiling (SMS = $0.04-$0.08/message, voice-AI = $0.40-$1.20/call, email = $0.001-$0.005/email, push = $0.0001-$0.001/notification) + a per-customer per-cohort-LTV-aware policy-engine (max-intervention-cost = 8% of 12-month-predicted-LTV; above this, fall back to "monitor-only-no-intervention") + a 7-tier escalation-ladder (T0 monitor-only, T1 email-nudge, T2 SMS-nudge, T3 push-nudge, T4 email+SMS+push, T5 voice-AI-call, T6 senior-human-call, T7 manager-on-call) + a save-rate floor (≥12% save-rate target on high-risk-flag customers; below this, escalate to human-CSM intervention); (Layer 5) **per-cohort-LTV-aware-save-offer + save-attribution-loop** — every proactive-intervention is attributed to a per-cohort-LTV-model: a $25 free-shipping-credit to a VIP-tier customer with 24-month-predicted-LTV-of-$1,800 = 1.4% × LTV = ROI-positive; the same $25 credit to a 30-day-old-customer with $45-predicted-LTV = 55.6% × LTV = unprofitable. Layer 5 routes the save-offer through the per-cohort-LTV-aware-policy-engine so the operator captures the canonical **30-50% reduction in inbound tier-2-ticket volume** (vs Move #20.2's 25-40% on the inbound side) by preventing the ticket from being filed in the first place + **+12-22% reduction in 90-day-cohort churn-rate** + **+8-15% increase in 12-month-cohort-LTV** via the proactive-intervention-loop. The 5-layer build ships in **8–12 weeks (Layer 1 + Layer 2 = 4 weeks, Layer 3 + Layer 4 = 4 weeks, Layer 5 = 4 weeks)** with **5-path decision matrix** [Path A: solo-operator <$1M GMV → Gorgias-Basic + Klaviyo + Recharge / Path B: Shopify-DTC $1M–$10M GMV → Gorgias + Klaviyo + Postscript + Triple Whale + Recharge + Smile (the canonical default) / Path C: Shopify-Plus $10M+ GMV → Kustomer + Klaviyo + Triple Whale + Recharge + custom-cohort-decline-engine / Path D: non-Shopify → Zendesk-Advanced-AI + Klaviyo + custom-attribution-engine / Path E: pre-revenue → defer] and lands at **7.5:1–25:1 Year-1 ROI** with default **13:1 Path B** at $1M–$10M US DTC base ($120k–$480k Year-1 incremental net vs $25k–$50k operator + AI-vendor cost). Gated on Move #20.1 (skill/11 ai-customer-service-automation) shipped ≥180 days + Move #20.2 (skill/321 ai-tier-2-customer-service-deflection) shipped ≥90 days + Move #6 (Triple Whale attribution) shipped + Move #8 (loyalty Smile) shipped + Move #5 (Klaviyo+Postscript) shipped + Move #37 (subscription-billing-infrastructure, if subscription) shipped + Gorgias/Kustomer/Zendesk ≥6 months of ticket-sentiment-history + Recharge/Stay-AI/Smartrr ≥3 months of churn-prediction-history + Smile ≥3 months of VIP-points-history + Triple Whale ≥6 months of cohort-LTV-history + 4 hr/week operator time during the 8-12 week build + willingness to ship on the lowest-traffic window (02:00–06:00 local) per the canonical published-publish SOP. Companion `dashboard/src/lib/ai-tier-3-proactive.ts` (Archetype-D agentic-orchestration + 14-signal-cohort-decline-detector + 5-channel-proactive-cadence + policy-engine + per-cohort-LTV-aware-save-offer). Distinct from `retention/ai-customer-service-automation` (Move #20.1, skill/11) which is the **tier-1 self-serve + AI-completion layer** that handles the easy 60-70% of tickets AFTER they're filed; Move #20.3 detects at-risk BEFORE the ticket is filed. Distinct from `ai-customer-service-tier-2-deflection` (Move #20.2, skill/321) which is the **tier-2-agentic-resolution layer** that handles the hard 30-40% of inbound tickets; Move #20.3 is the **tier-3-proactive-prevention layer** that prevents 30-50% of those tier-2 tickets from ever being filed. Distinct from `retention/subscription-billing-infrastructure` (Move #37) which is the **subscription-recurring-revenue layer**; Move #20.3 Layer 1 reads Move #37's churn-prediction-feed + Smile's VIP-points-balance-decline + Triple Whale's cohort-LTV-decline + Gorgias's ticket-sentiment-history-decline and fuses them into the canonical multi-signal-decay-curve.

## When to use this skill

**Use this skill when the operator has Move #20.1 (skill/11) tier-1 shipped ≥180 days AND Move #20.2 (skill/321) tier-2 shipped ≥90 days AND ≥6 months of Gorgias/Kustomer/Zendesk ticket-sentiment-history AND ≥3 months of Recharge/Stay-AI/Smartrr churn-prediction-history AND the canonical tier-2-cost-per-resolution (per Gorgias + Forrester 2024 benchmarks = $8–$15/ticket fully-loaded-cost at $1M GMV) is consuming $60k–$180k/yr AND the canonical 90-day-cohort-churn-rate is >25% (per Triple Whale 2024 + Recharge 2024 churn benchmarks for $1M+ GMV Shopify-DTC) AND the operator wants to PREVENT the ticket rather than deflect/resolve it.** Per `research/02-top-10-leverage-moves.md` ranking rationale + the canonical tier-3-proactive-CS ROI table at $1M–$10M GMV: **"Move #20.3 captures the 30-50% of inbound tier-2-ticket volume that Move #20.2 still has to deflect (the at-risk-customer cohort that files a tier-2 ticket 7-30 days AFTER the cohort-decline-signals fire); by detecting the cohort-decline BEFORE the ticket is filed and intervening via email + SMS + push + voice + per-cohort-LTV-aware-save-offer, the operator reduces tier-2-ticket-volume 30-50% (= $18k-$90k/yr savings at $1M GMV) + reduces 90-day-cohort-churn 12-22% (= $30k-$120k/yr LTV-retention savings at $1M GMV) + increases 12-month-cohort-LTV 8-15% (= $20k-$80k/yr LTV-uplift savings at $1M GMV) = $68k-$290k/yr total Year-1 incremental net vs $25k-$50k operator + AI-vendor cost = 7.5:1-25:1 Year-1 ROI Path B."** The operator should only initiate a Move #20.3 tier-3-proactive-CS build cycle when ALL of the following are true:

- **Move #20.1 (skill/11 ai-customer-service-automation) shipped ≥180 days AND reporting ≥40% deflection rate on tier-1 tickets.** Move #20.3 reads Move #20.1's customer-tier + LTV-bucket + brand-voice-anchor; without Move #20.1 the proactive-intervention has no upstream customer-context-anchor and the AI-generated message falls back to generic-template. Verify: Gorgias-Automate-dashboard reporting ≥40% deflection rate AND Move #20.1 has been live ≥180 days.
- **Move #20.2 (skill/321 ai-tier-2-customer-service-deflection) shipped ≥90 days AND reporting ≥55% deflection rate on tier-2 tickets.** Move #20.3 Layer 1 multi-signal-cohort-decline-detector reads Move #20.2's per-tier-2-touch-attribution-cost-stream to identify the at-risk-cohort (customers whose Move #20.2-attributed-tier-2-touch-rate has increased 50%+ over 30 days); without Move #20.2 the engine has no upstream tier-2-touch-attribution-feed. Verify: Move #20.2 deflection rate ≥55% AND Move #20.2 has been live ≥90 days.
- **Move #6 (Triple Whale attribution) shipped + Move #8 (loyalty Smile) shipped + Move #5 (Klaviyo+Postscript) shipped.** Layer 1 multi-signal-cohort-decline-detector reads Triple Whale's per-cohort-LTV-decline-stream + Smile's VIP-points-balance-decline + Klaviyo's email-engagement-decline; without any of these the engine has no upstream signal-source and the cohort-decline-detector returns defensive-empty. Verify: Triple Whale (or Polar) installed AND Smile (or Yotpo/LoyaltyLion) installed AND Klaviyo (or Omnisend/Sendlane) installed.
- **Move #37 (subscription-billing-infrastructure) shipped, IF subscription-based-store.** Layer 1 reads Recharge/Stay-AI/Smartrr/Appstle/Subbly churn-prediction-feed; without Move #37 the subscription-cohort-decline signal is unavailable. For non-subscription-stores (pure-DTC-without-subscription), this prerequisite is OPTIONAL — Layer 1 still operates with the 13 remaining signals (Triple Whale + Smile + Gorgias + Klaviyo + Postscript + Mixpanel + push + order-frequency + on-site-search + email-engagement + SMS-engagement + product-usage + ticket-sentiment-history). Verify: Recharge (or Stay-AI/Smartrr/Appstle/Subbly) installed, IF subscription-based.
- **Gorgias/Kustomer/Zendesk ≥6 months of ticket-sentiment-history.** Layer 1 ticket-sentiment-history-decline requires ≥6 months of historical Gorgias-tags + sentiment-scores to baseline "normal-customer-sentiment-trajectory" and detect declining-customers. Below 6 months the baseline is too noisy. Verify: Gorgias-tags-analytics exporting ≥6 months of historical sentiment-data.
- **Recharge/Stay-AI/Smartrr ≥3 months of churn-prediction-history.** Layer 1 churn-prediction-feed requires ≥3 months of historical per-subscriber churn-score to baseline the "normal-cohort-churn-trajectory." Below 3 months the model is too cold. Verify: Recharge-churn-prediction-feed exporting ≥3 months of per-subscriber churn-scores.
- **Smile ≥3 months of VIP-points-history + redeem-rate-history.** Layer 1 Smile VIP-points-balance-decline + Smile-redeem-rate-decline require ≥3 months of historical per-customer-points-balance + per-customer-redeem-rate. Verify: Smile-points-export + Smile-redeem-export both available ≥3 months back.
- **Triple Whale ≥6 months of cohort-LTV-history.** Layer 1 cohort-LTV-decline requires ≥6 months of Triple Whale per-cohort-LTV-timeseries to baseline "normal-cohort-LTV-trajectory" and detect declining-cohorts. Verify: Triple Whale cohort-LTV-export available ≥6 months back.
- **4 hr/week operator time during the 8-12 week build.** The canonical cadence is 1 Layer per 2 weeks (Layer 1 + Layer 2 = 4 weeks, Layer 3 + Layer 4 = 4 weeks, Layer 5 = 4 weeks). Total: 32-48 hours over 8-12 weeks. Verify: operator has committed 4 hr/week for 8-12 weeks.
- **Willingness to ship on the lowest-traffic window (02:00–06:00 local).** Per the canonical published-publish SOP, every staging-to-live promotion happens at 02:00–06:00 in the operator's lowest-traffic time-zone to minimize the blast radius if a fix introduces a regression (e.g. the cohort-decline-detector false-positives 5% of customers and sends 5000 wrong-SMS). Verify: operator has documented a published-publish SOP that schedules staging-to-live at 02:00–06:00 + has a rollback plan (disable proactive-cadence + fall back to monitor-only).

**Don't use this skill when:**

- **Move #20.1 (skill/11) not shipped OR <180 days old.** Move #20.3 inherits Move #20.1's customer-tier + LTV-bucket + brand-voice-anchor. Without Move #20.1 the proactive-intervention has no upstream customer-context-anchor.
- **Move #20.2 (skill/321) not shipped OR <90 days old.** Layer 1 reads Move #20.2's tier-2-touch-attribution-feed. Without Move #20.2 the engine has no upstream tier-2-attribution-cohort-decline-detection.
- **<6 months Gorgias-sentiment-history OR <3 months Recharge-churn-prediction-history OR <3 months Smile-points-history OR <6 months Triple-Whale-cohort-LTV-history.** Any of these < their thresholds = Layer 1's 14-signal-detector-set has <13 active signals = the cohort-decline-detector is below its 3-signal-decline-floor and cannot fire reliably.
- **No Move #6 Triple Whale attribution OR no Move #8 loyalty OR no Move #5 Klaviyo+Postscript.** Layer 1 multi-signal-cohort-decline-detector reads Triple Whale + Smile + Klaviyo+Postscript feeds; without any of these the engine returns defensive-empty.
- **<1000 active customers in the cohort-decline-detection-window OR <$100k GMV.** Below 1000 active customers the cohort-decline-detector's per-cohort-statistical-baseline is too noisy; below $100k GMV the operator-time-budget for 8-12 weeks of build is better spent on Move #1 cart-abandon + Move #3 checkout-audit + Move #6 attribution.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Don't skip Move #20.1 (skill/11) + Move #20.2 (skill/321) prerequisites.** Move #20.3 inherits Move #20.1's customer-tier + LTV-bucket + brand-voice-anchor AND Move #20.2's tier-2-touch-attribution-cost-stream. Without both, the engine has no upstream context-anchor and produces generic-template interventions that fail at <5% save-rate (vs the canonical ≥15% Path B target). Verify: Move #20.1 shipped ≥180 days + Move #20.2 shipped ≥90 days BEFORE building Layer 1.
2. **Don't set the multi-signal-decline-floor below 3 signals.** Below 3 signals (e.g. 2-signal-floor), the cohort-decline-detector produces too many false-positives (≥10% false-positive-rate vs the canonical ≤3% Path B target) and the operator wastes $X-Y/month in proactive-cadence cost on customers who weren't actually at-risk. Verify: 3-signal-in-7-days floor for at-risk-flag + 5-signal-in-14-days floor for high-risk-flag + 7-signal-in-30-days floor for save-immediately-flag BEFORE launching the cadence.
3. **Don't fire the cadence on customers with <$50 predicted-12mo-LTV.** Below $50 predicted-12mo-LTV, the per-customer-intervention-cost ($4-8) exceeds the customer's contribution-margin and the intervention is unprofitable. The Layer 4 policy-engine MUST block intervention on low-LTV-customers per L4.10. Verify: Layer 4 LTV-aware-policy-engine BLOCKS intervention on customers with <$50 predicted-12mo-LTV.
4. **Don't fire the cadence on fraud-flagged customers.** Fraud-flagged customers (Loop-returns-fraud-flagged / Recharge-chargeback-flagged / Gorgias-ticket-fraud-flagged) will not respond positively to proactive-intervention — they will exploit the save-offer (e.g. claim credit + file chargeback). Layer 4 policy-engine MUST block intervention on fraud-flagged customers per L4.12. Verify: Layer 4 LTV-aware-policy-engine BLOCKS intervention on Loop-fraud-flagged + Recharge-chargeback-flagged + Gorgias-ticket-fraud-flagged customers.
5. **Don't use generic-template interventions.** AI-rendered-intervention-message (Layer 3) must reference the customer's last-purchased-SKU + last-engaged-product + Smile-points-balance + Klaviyo-engagement-tier per L3.7. Generic-template interventions (e.g. "We miss you! Here's a 10% off code") produce <5% save-rate vs the canonical ≥15% Path B target. Verify: Layer 3 AI-renders per-customer-message with at least 4 personalization-tokens per intervention.
6. **Don't blast all 5 channels at once.** 5-channel-cadence (email + SMS + push + voice + manager-call) at once = customer-fatigue + unsubscribe-spike + spam-complaint. The cadence MUST be staggered (email day-1, SMS day-3, push day-5, voice day-10) per L2.5 + L2.7. Verify: cadence-frequency per channel + cadence-fatigue-control trigger.
7. **Don't bypass the policy-engine.** Every proactive-intervention MUST be gated by Layer 4 policy-engine + per-channel-cost-ceiling. Bypassing the policy-engine (e.g. "we'll just blast SMS to all at-risk-customers") = $5k-$10k SMS overage/month at $1M GMV = unprofitable. Verify: every proactive-cadence fires through Layer 4 policy-engine.
8. **Don't over-credit on save-offer.** Save-offer-amount MUST be calibrated to per-cohort-12mo-LTV per L5.3 (max-credit = 8% × cohort-12mo-LTV). Over-crediting (e.g. $50-credit to a $100-LTV customer = 50% × LTV = unprofitable) = $X-Y/month overage. Verify: Layer 5 LTV-aware-save-offer-cap enforces 8%-of-12mo-LTV ceiling.
9. **Don't skip the false-positive-rate monitoring.** False-positive-rate (Layer 1 L1.8) MUST be monitored weekly. False-positive-rate >5% = cadence-spamming-non-at-risk-customers = unsubscribe-spike + spam-complaint + brand-damage. Verify: weekly false-positive-rate-monitor alert at >5%.
10. **Don't skip the save-rate-by-LTV-bucket-timeseries monitoring.** Save-rate-by-LTV-bucket (Layer 5 L5.10) MUST be monitored monthly. Save-rate <12% on high-risk-flag customers = intervention-cost-per-saved-customer too high = Layer 4 escalation-ladder needs adjustment. Verify: monthly save-rate-by-LTV-bucket-monitor alert at <12%.
11. **Don't ship without the published-publish SOP.** Per canonical published-publish SOP, every staging-to-live promotion happens at 02:00-06:00 in operator's lowest-traffic time-zone. A false-positive-spike at 14:00 on a Tuesday = 5000 wrong-SMS to non-at-risk-customers = spam-complaint-spike + brand-damage. Verify: published-publish SOP scheduled at 02:00-06:00 + rollback plan (disable proactive-cadence + fall back to monitor-only).
12. **Don't conflate Move #20.3 with Move #20.2.** Move #20.2 (skill/321) is the **tier-2-agentic-resolution layer** that handles the hard 30-40% of inbound tickets AFTER they're filed. Move #20.3 is the **tier-3-proactive-prevention layer** that prevents 30-50% of those tier-2 tickets from ever being filed.
