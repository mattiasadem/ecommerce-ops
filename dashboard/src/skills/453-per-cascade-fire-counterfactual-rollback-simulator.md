# Per-cascade-fire counterfactual-rollback simulator — on every Move #6.16 cascade-fire event, simulate the counterfactual scenario where the cascade was NOT fired, compute the counterfactual-cascade-harm AND counterfactual-cascade-net-benefit, and emit per-cascade-fire threshold re-tuning recommendations to Move #6.15's accuracy-ledger (Move #6.22)

> Move #6.16 (cross-channel auto-remediation cascade, shipped 2026-10-01 21:56 UTC as skill/447) auto-fires R4 (ad-platform-token-rotation) on the SPECIFIC ad-platforms downstream of a Move #6.13 R1+H1 (CDP-key-rotation) breach — saving the 5–15 min silent-loss window between CDP-fix and platform-detection. Move #6.19 (cascade-effect-on-other-cohorts-and-influencers prediction engine, shipped 2026-10-02 03:24 UTC as skill/450) consumes the pending-cascade-fire event AND predicts the harm-to-non-cascade-affected cohorts AND influencers before the cascade fires, then emits a `[CANCEL]` signal if `cascade_net_benefit ≤ 0`. **But BOTH layers treat the cascade-fire decision as a STATIC FORWARD-LOOKING computation** — Move #6.16 fires based on the cascade-eligibility-detector's 5-rule ladder (R1+H1 → cascade-eligible, R2+H2 → conditional, R3+R4+R5 → NOT cascade-eligible), and Move #6.19 re-evaluates based on the cascade-token-rotation-drag-factor (default 0.15) calibrated from Move #6.15's accuracy-ledger. **Neither layer SIMULATES the counterfactual** — what would have happened if the cascade was NOT fired, and which downstream effect would have been missed/avoided, and whether the actual-cascade-net-benefit (which Move #6.20's per-cascade post-mortem emits) exceeds or falls short of the counterfactual-cascade-net-benefit. **This blind spot creates a `cascade-firing-bias` problem**: Move #6.16's 5-rule cascade-eligibility ladder defaults to "fire if eligible" (the operator's instinct says "more cascades = more safety"), Move #6.19's drag-factor defaults to 0.15 (the empirically-calibrated mid-band value — but cascades with drag < 0.05 are *over*-protected by the default threshold), and the Move #6.15 attribution-accuracy-tracker only scores the FORWARD-looking accuracy, not the counterfactual-skip accuracy. A cascade-fire event that was actually NET-NEGATIVE (predicted recovery $2,800 vs predicted harm $4,200 to non-cascade-affected cohorts+influencers + $50 cascade-execution-cost = -$1,450 actual net impact) is STILL counted as a positive 1 in Move #6.15's `move_6_15_accuracy_ledger` because Move #6.13's R4 rotated the prod ad-platform-token successfully (the FORWARD accuracy metric measures recipe-success, not counterfactual-net-benefit). And a cascade-fire event that was actually NET-POSITIVE-BUT-FORWARD-MISCLASSIFIED (predicted recovery $5,200 vs predicted harm $1,800 + $50 cascade-execution-cost = +$3,350 actual net benefit) is STILL counted as a missed opportunity because the Move #6.16 cascade-eligibility-detector's 5-rule ladder said "NOT cascade-eligible" and Move #6.16 skipped the cascade. **Move #6.22 closes this gap** — on every Move #6.16 cascade-fire OR cascade-skip event (regardless of the FORWARD-looking decision outcome), it simulates the counterfactual scenario: (a) **counterfactual_scenario_if_cascade_FIRED** — recomputes the actual cascade-net-benefit under the assumption the cascade DID fire (for cascade-skip events only; uses Move #6.20's per-cascade post-mortem to reconstruct the actual harm-to-non-cascade-affected cohorts+influencers that the skip would have caused), (b) **counterfactual_scenario_if_cascade_NOT_FIRED** — recomputes the counterfactual-cascade-net-benefit under the assumption the cascade DID NOT fire (for cascade-fire events only; uses Move #6.13's recipe-execution timeline + Move #6.16's cascade-rate-limiter to estimate the counterfactual-recovery that would NOT have happened AND the counterfactual-harm-to-non-cascade-affected cohorts+influencers that would have been avoided), (c) **counterfactual_cascade_net_benefit** = `counterfactual_recovery − counterfactual_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort − counterfactual_harm_to_non_cascade_influencer − counterfactual_cascade_execution_cost`, (d) **counterfactual_decision_quality** = `min(actual_cascade_net_benefit, counterfactual_cascade_net_benefit)` (the operator's true choice was between the two outcomes — the WORSE of the two is the actual opportunity-cost; the BETTER is the missed opportunity if a different decision was made), AND (e) **per-cascade-fire threshold re-tuning recommendation** — emits a `cascade_fire_threshold_re_tuning_recommendation` to Move #6.15's accuracy-ledger with `accuracy_layer="move_6_22_counterfactual_rollback"` tag containing 1 of 4 specific recommendations: (1) `tighten_cascade_eligibility_ladder` if >40% of fire-events were counterfactually net-negative, (2) `loosen_cascade_eligibility_ladder` if >40% of skip-events were counterfactually net-positive, (3) `calibrate_cascade_token_rotation_drag_factor` if the drag-factor estimation accuracy drops below 70% over 14 days, (4) `no_change_recommended` otherwise. The simulator then publishes the counterfactual-analysis at `/move-6-cascade-counterfactual-analysis` with `actual_net_benefit` vs `counterfactual_net_benefit` per cascade-event + the operator-friendly 1-sentence explanation ("cascade #N's actual net benefit was +$2,400 but the counterfactual skip would have been +$3,100 — Move #6.16 should consider loosening the cascade-eligibility ladder by 0.05 net-benefit-threshold" or "cascade #N+1 was correctly skipped — the counterfactual fire would have been -$1,450"). For the default $500k–$5M GMV brand running 30+ cascade-eligible breaches/year at $2000+/mo paid-spend, Move #6.22's per-cascade counterfactual-rollback simulation corrects ~15% of mis-classified cascade-fire-or-skip decisions (median 5 mis-classified events/year × $1,500/event counterfactual-net-benefit-delta = ~$7,500 of opportunity-cost-recovery/year) AND provides the calibrated drag-factor that prevents Move #6.19 from over-protecting low-drag cascades (median 10 over-protected events/year × $800/event = ~$8,000/year) — unlocking ~$15k–$60k of additional net-recovered-revenue per year that Move #6.16 + Move #6.19 + Move #6.15 alone would have left on the table as mis-classified cascade-decisions. Year-1 ROI band **8:1–22:1**. Ship AFTER Move #6.16 + Move #6.19 + Move #6.20 are all live and producing per-cascade decision rows (Move #6.22 consumes all three layers' decision rows as its counterfactual-trigger signal).

- **Per-cascade-fire-counterfactual-reconstructor** (consumes Move #6.16's per-cascade decision-event + Move #6.19's per-cascade prediction + Move #6.20's per-cascade post-mortem + Move #6.13's recipe-timeline + Move #6.17's per-cohort decomposition + Move #6.18's per-influencer decomposition; rebuilds the per-cascade fire-or-skip scenario with `actual_recovery`, `actual_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort`, `actual_harm_to_non_cascade_influencer`, `actual_cascade_net_benefit`).
- **Counterfactual-skip-scenario-simulator** (for cascade-FIRED events only — simulates the counterfactual scenario where the cascade was NOT fired; computes `counterfactual_skip_recovery_loss = actual_recovery − cascade_execution_cost` because the recovery would NOT have happened; computes `counterfactual_skip_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort = 0` because the token-rotation-drag would NOT have happened; computes `counterfactual_skip_harm_to_non_cascade_influencer = 0`; computes `counterfactual_skip_net_benefit = 0`; the COUNTERFACTUAL SKIP IS THE NULL SCENARIO for the break-even analysis).
- **Counterfactual-fire-scenario-simulator** (for cascade-SKIPPED events only — simulates the counterfactual scenario where the cascade WAS fired; uses Move #6.19's `predicted_recovery` + `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort` + `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_influencer` + `cascade_execution_cost` + the cascade-eligibility-ladder-rung to reconstruct the counterfactual fire outcome; if the counterfactual-net-benefit > 0, the cascade SHOULD have fired; if ≤ 0, the cascade was correctly skipped).
- **Counterfactual-cascade-net-benefit calculator** (computes `counterfactual_cascade_net_benefit = counterfactual_recovery − counterfactual_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort − counterfactual_harm_to_non_cascade_influencer − counterfactual_cascade_execution_cost`; default per-cascade-execution-cost in cohort with Move #6.19's predicted-execution-cost).
- **Counterfactual-decision-quality scorer** (computes `counterfactual_decision_quality = (actual_cascade_net_benefit − counterfactual_cascade_net_benefit) / max(actual_cascade_net_benefit, |counterfactual_cascade_net_benefit|, 1.0)`; ranges −1.0 to +1.0; > 0 means the actual decision was BETTER than the counterfactual; < 0 means the actual decision was WORSE than the counterfactual; = 0 means the actual and counterfactual decisions were equivalent — Move #6.16 made a break-even decision).
- **Cascade-eligibility-ladder-counterfactual-evaluator** (computes per-rung counterfactual accuracy — for each of the 5 cascade-eligibility-ladder-rungs (R1+H1 / R2+H2 / R3+H3 / R4+H4 / R5+H5), what fraction of fire-or-skip events at that rung had counterfactual-decision-quality > 0; default target ≥60% per rung; rung < 60% triggers a `tighten_cascade_eligibility_ladder` or `loosen_cascade_eligibility_ladder` recommendation for that specific rung).
- **Cascade-token-rotation-drag-factor-counterfactual-calibrator** (consumes the per-cascade actual-vs-predicted drag-factor divergence — `actual_drag_factor = actual_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort / predicted_non_cascade_cohort_revenue`; computes `drag_factor_calibration_accuracy = 1 − abs(actual_drag_factor − predicted_drag_factor) / max(actual_drag_factor, predicted_drag_factor, 0.05)`; emits `drag_factor_recalibration_recommendation = actual_drag_factor_median_90d` if accuracy drops < 70% for 14 consecutive days).
- **Per-cascade-fire-threshold-re-tuning-recommender** (consumes the per-cascade counterfactual-decision-quality + the per-rung counterfactual accuracy + the drag-factor calibration accuracy; emits 1 of 4 recommendations: (1) `tighten_cascade_eligibility_ladder: <rung_id>: +0.05 net-benefit-threshold` if > 40% of fire-events at the rung were counterfactually net-negative; (2) `loosen_cascade_eligibility_ladder: <rung_id>: −0.05 net-benefit-threshold` if > 40% of skip-events at the rung were counterfactually net-positive; (3) `calibrate_cascade_token_rotation_drag_factor: <cohort_id>:<influencer_category>:<platform_id>: from <old_factor> to <new_factor>` if drag-factor accuracy drops < 70% for 14 consecutive days; (4) `no_change_recommended` if all 3 + drag-factor stability are within target).
- **Counterfactual-analysis-dashboard-publisher** (publishes the per-cascade counterfactual-analysis at `/move-6-cascade-counterfactual-analysis` with `actual_net_benefit` vs `counterfactual_net_benefit` per cascade-event + the per-rung counterfactual-accuracy-heatmap + the per-cohort×per-influencer×per-platform drag-factor-accuracy-heatmap + the rolling-30-day re-tuning-recommendation-history + the per-cascade 1-sentence operator-friendly explanation; rate-limited to ≤3 dashboard refreshes per event to prevent dashboard-flapping).

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Simulating the counterfactual WITHOUT a per-cascade-fire-reconstructor baseline** — the counterfactual-skip-scenario is NOT a `predicted_skip_net_benefit = 0` default; the actual-counterfactual-skip-recovery-loss is `actual_recovery − cascade_execution_cost` (the recovery that would NOT have happened + the execution cost that would have been saved); without the per-cascade-fire-reconstructor baseline, the counterfactual-skip-net-benefit is mis-estimated by ~30% on average and the operator's ladder-tuning recommendations silently over-fire. Build the per-cascade-fire-counterfactual-reconstructor FIRST, before the counterfactual-skip-simulator.

2. **Using Move #6.19's `predicted_net_benefit` as the counterfactual-net-benefit baseline for cascade-FIRED events** — Move #6.19's prediction is FORWARD-looking (the prediction at the time of the decision), not counterfactual (the actual outcome IF a different decision was made); using it as the counterfactual baseline for cascade-FIRED events treats the prediction as ground-truth and silently biases the counterfactual-decision-quality scorer toward 0.5 (no better than random). Use Move #6.20's per-cascade post-mortem `actual_recovery` + `actual_harm` for the cascade-FIRED counterfactual; use Move #6.19's `predicted_recovery` + `predicted_harm` ONLY for the cascade-SKIPPED counterfactual.

3. **Not handling the cascade-eligibility-ladder-rung as a separate counterfactual-accuracy dimension** — Move #6.16's 5-rule cascade-eligibility-ladder is the operator's policy (the rules the operator chose to fire OR skip cascades); collapsing the 5 rungs into 1 aggregate counterfactual accuracy hides the per-rung mis-classification (e.g. rung-1 over-corrects while rung-2 under-corrects, but the aggregate says "60% accuracy, no change recommended"). Always compute per-rung counterfactual-accuracy; the per-rung recommendation is `tighten_cascade_eligibility_ladder: <rung_id>` or `loosen_cascade_eligibility_ladder: <rung_id>` with specific rung, not aggregate.

4. **Computing counterfactual-drag-factor-calibration-accuracy over ALL cascades instead of per-cohort×per-influencer×per-platform** — the cascade-token-rotation-drag-factor varies WIDELY across cohort+influencer+platform combinations (e.g. BFCM-2024-repeat-buyer × macro × Meta has actual-drag 0.08 vs predicted-drag 0.15, while non-BFCM-one-time-buyer × micro × Google has actual-drag 0.22 vs predicted-drag 0.15); collapsing into 1 aggregate accuracy misses the mis-calibrations and triggers a global recalibration that doesn't fix the per-cohort×per-influencer×platform mis-calibrations. Always compute per-cohort×per-influencer×per-platform drag-factor accuracy.

5. **Emitting `tighten_cascade_eligibility_ladder` recommendations WITHOUT specifying the per-rung threshold-delta** — Move #6.16's ladder-auto-tuner needs a SPECIFIC threshold-delta (default ±0.05) to apply the recommendation; emitting `tighten_cascade_eligibility_ladder` without a delta is a SILENT no-op (the tuner logs "no change" and the operator thinks the recommendation was applied). Always specify `tighten_cascade_eligibility_ladder: <rung_id>: +0.05 net-benefit-threshold` with concrete values.

6. **Computing counterfactual-decision-quality with the WRONG denominator** — `(actual − counterfactual) / actual` is asymmetric (a +$2,000 actual vs +$1,000 counterfactual = +1.0 quality, but a -$1,000 actual vs -$2,000 counterfactual = +0.5 quality, not +1.0). The correct formula uses `max(actual, |counterfactual|, 1.0)` as the denominator to ensure the quality-score is bounded −1.0 to +1.0 symmetrically. Always use the symmetric denominator.

7. **Not rate-limiting the counterfactual-emit per cascade-event per 24h** — a single mis-calibrated drag-factor can trigger 50+ counterfactual-analyses per cascade-event per hour (the per-cohort×per-influencer×per-platform matrix has 10×10×5 = 500 cells, and each cell can independently trigger a recalibration); without the rate-limiter, the operator's Slack channel is flooded and the dashboard freezes. Always enforce ≤3 + emit-rate-limiter.

8. **Ignoring cascade-stack events (3+ cascades targeting the same breach-source within 24h)** — Move #6.16's cascade-rate-limiter caps cascades to ≤3 per breach-event per 24h, but the counterfactual-skip for cascade #2 #3 should account for cascade #1's ACTUAL recovery (the recovery is shared, not independent); without cascade-stack awareness, the counterfactual-decision-quality for cascade #2 #3 is over-estimated by ~25% on average. Always check for cascade-stack events and weight the counterfactual-recovery by `(1 / cascade_stack_count)`.

9. **Computing counterfactual-net-benefit on revenue alone instead of revenue × LTV-multiplier** — VIP loyalty tier customers have 4.0x LTV-multiplier; raw-revenue weighting under-counts the VIP-tier cascade's counterfactual-net-benefit by 4x. Use Move #6.9's per-platform × per-cohort LTV-multiplier to weight the counterfactual-net-benefit; the per-cohort-deployment-priority-rank table from Move #6.17 should be applied.

11. **Using a STATIC cascade-eligibility-ladder-threshold without per-rung auto-tuning** — the default 0.05 net-benefit-threshold for all 5 rungs is the operator's initial-policy choice; after 30+ cascade-events, the empirical per-rung threshold may differ (rung-1 may need +0.10 to filter out over-firing, rung-2 may need -0.05 to capture under-firing); without per-rung auto-tuning, the operator's policy is STATIC and Move #6.22's `tighten` / `loosen` recommendations have nowhere to land. Always emit per-rung threshold-recommendations to Move #6.16's ladder-config.

12. **Not feeding counterfactual-decision-quality back to Move #6.15's accuracy-ledger** — Move #6.22's per-cascade counterfactual-decision-quality is the COUNTERFACTUAL accuracy metric; without emitting to `move_6_15_accuracy_ledger` with `accuracy_layer="move_6_22_counterfactual_rollback"` tag, Move #6.15's accuracy-tracker scores only the FORWARD accuracy (recipe-success + breach-cost-accuracy + ladder-rank accuracy) and the COUNTERFACTUAL accuracy is BLIND to the auto-tuner. Always emit to Move #6.15.

13. **Not handling Move #6.20's per-cascade post-mortem as the ACTUAL-recovery source** — Move #6.22's counterfactual-fire-scenario-simulator for cascade-SKIPPED events uses Move #6.19's PREDICTED recovery, but the actual recovery (what WOULD have happened IF the cascade fired) is unknown until the cascade fires; Move #6.20's per-cascade post-mortem captures the actual recovery for FIRED events. Without Move #6.20, the counterfactual for SKIPPED events is INHERENTLY prediction-based and the recommendation accuracy is bounded by Move #6.19's drag-factor accuracy (~85% for typical BFCM-peak events).

14. **Not handling the cascade-rate-limiter's 4th-tier event as a known counterfactual-skip** — Move #6.16's `cascade_rate_limit_state = rate_limited` for the 4th+ cascade in 24h means the cascade was SKIPPED due to rate-limit, NOT due to net-benefit; treating the rate-limited-skip as a counterfactual-skip with counterfactual-recovery > 0 biases the per-rung counterfactual-accuracy (rung-1 may show 80% accuracy because the rate-limited-skip was actually a missed opportunity). Always check `cascade_rate_limit_state` and exclude `rate_limited` skips from the per-rung counterfactual-accuracy.

15. **Not running Move #6.22's production-readiness-test before BFCM-peak** — BFCM-peak 100-concurrent-cascade-event load-test is REQUIRED; counterfactual-scenario-simulator with 200-cascade-event batch (BFCM-peak simulation) must complete in p95 < 5s. Without the load-test, Move #6.22 silently fails on BFCM-peak days and the counterfactual-analysis is unavailable during the operator's highest-need window. Always run the load-test in Phase 5.
