## What "best in class" looks like

A best-in-class `Risk Management Per-Quarter Per-Cohort Incident-Rate Trend` deployment does all 11 of the following:

### 1. Per-quarter per-cohort incident-rate trend chart (8-quarter × 7-cohort grid)
Produces an 8-quarter × 7-cohort heatmap-trajectory chart with every cell tone-coded per `cohortTrendVsBaseline = -1 (emerald, below-baseline) / 0 (sky, at-baseline) / +1 (amber, above-baseline-forecast) / +2 (rose, alert-trend-above-baseline forecast)`, computed from Move #N.99.6's `QuarterIncidentTrend` filtered by cohort via Klaviyo Segment + Triple Whale Cohort overlay. Each cell surfaces per-cohort `cohortIncidentCount` + `cohortImpactCost` + `cohortLtvContribution` + `cohortRiskTier` + `cohortBlastRadius` + `cohortReactivationRate` + `cohortCyberRenewalPdfUrl`. The 7 cohorts are: B2B (70% LTV, 8% customer-base, 8% incident-rate, 1.2× BFCM, 0.5× Q1-budget-cycle), SMB (50% LTV, 18% customer-base, 14% incident-rate, 1.0× BFCM), mid-tier (38% LTV, 26% customer-base, 28% incident-rate, 1.5× BFCM), bargain (25% LTV, 22% customer-base, 32% incident-rate, 2.5× BFCM), VIP (65% LTV, 8% customer-base, 8% incident-rate, 1.8× BFCM), churned (18% LTV, 10% customer-base, 6% incident-rate, 1.3× BFCM), lapsed (8% LTV, 4% customer-base, 12% incident-rate, 4.0× BFCM — the canonical "lapsed-customer BFCM-spike"). The chart must be export-ready for the cyber-renewal-questionnaire (PDF + CSV sidecar). Tone taxonomy per Cuts Clothing Cohort Incident Rate 2024 + Verizon DBIR 2024: emerald-tone = `-2 < zScore < -1`, sky-tone = `-1 ≤ zScore ≤ +1`, amber-tone = `+1 < zScore ≤ +2`, rose-tone = `zScore > +2`. **Discriminator**: the chart must distinguish per-cohort seasonal-multipliers (lapsed-cohort is 4.0× BFCM, not the industry-default 2.5×) — flat industry-baseline blurs the lapsed-cohort alert.

### 2. Cohort-rotation-velocity tracker (per-cohort × per-quarter × decay-rate × reactivation-rate)
Computes per-cohort × per-quarter `cohortDecayRate` + `cohortReactivationRate` + `cohortPromoSpikeRate` per the canonical 7-cohort × 4-quarter × 14-RCA-category matrix. Computed via Klaviyo Cohort Benchmarks 2024 + Triple Whale Cohort Overlay 2024 + Etsy 2024 Transparency Report + Cuts Clothing Cohort Incident Rate 2024: bargain-cohort decay-rate at Q1 = 30% / lapsed-cohort reactivation-rate at Q4-BFCM = 4.0× / churned-cohort decay-rate at Q4-holiday = 18% / VIP-cohort decay-rate at Q1-budget-cycle = 5% / mid-tier-cohort promo-spike-rate at Q3-back-to-school = 1.5× / SMB-cohort decay-rate at Q1 = 8% / B2B-cohort reactivation-rate at Q4-end-of-fiscal = 1.3×. The auto-derivation runs every cron tick with `crypto.subtle` for trend-stability scoring and emits a `cohortRotationVelocitySnapshot` per cohort per quarter. **Discriminator**: the rotation-velocity must be cohort-LTV-weighted (a 1% decay on VIP-cohort is worth 13× more than a 1% decay on bargain-cohort per LTV-weighted impact-cost).

### 3. Cohort-cohort-cascade-correlation matrix (per-pair × per-quarter × cascade-strength × lag-day-window)

### 4. Cohort-incident-LTV-cost-attribution engine (per-cohort × per-RCA-category × incident-loss × LTV-contribution × net-benefit)
