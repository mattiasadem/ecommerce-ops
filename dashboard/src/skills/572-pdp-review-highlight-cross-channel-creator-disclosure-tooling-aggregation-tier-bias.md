## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing DTAT-tier (disclosure-tooling-aggregation-tier) with DT-tier (disclosure-tooling-tier) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.44].** DTAT-tier answers WHICH aggregation-cadence the merchant uses (zero-rail-aggregation-pool + annual-rail-aggregation-pool + quarterly-rail-aggregation-pool + monthly-rail-aggregation-pool + daily-rail-aggregation-pool); DT-tier answers WHICH disclosure-tooling (DT1 Meta-Brand-Collabs-Manager + DT2 TikTok-Creator-Marketplace + DT3 YouTube-Paid-Promotion-Badge + DT4 in-house-disclosure-rollup + DT5 MSA-archive-link). The two axes are orthogonal — a DT1 Meta-Brand-Collabs-Manager creator is typically on DTAT1 zero-aggregation (no Meta-rail-rollup-required); a DT5 MSA-archive-link creator is typically forced into DTAT5 daily-rail-aggregation (vintage-disclosure-record-daily-rail-pool). **Fix:** carry DTAT-tier and DT-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung precedence ladders (DTAT1-DTAT2-DTAT3-DTAT4-DTAT5 and DT1-DT2-DT3-DT4-DT5) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-DTAT-tier × per-DT-tier 5×5 cross-axis grid that scores 25 distinct DTAT×DT tooling combinations.

2. **Pitfall — DTAT1 zero-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected annual-rail-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses DTAT1 zero-aggregation pool (Meta-Brand-Collabs-Manager-no-rail-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-border-traffic may have a disclosure-tooling event that crosses USD-EU-border without an annual-rail-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$55k-annual-rail-maint + 1-rollover-per-year on the disclosure-tooling event. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's cross-border-traffic-share + cohort's aggregation-tolerance — DTAT1 score only if cohort-cross-border-share <10% AND cohort-DTAT-tolerance = high; DTAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.

3. **Pitfall — DTAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-JP-cohort fail quarterly-rail-rollup-requirement.** Japan APPI 2022 + Japan APPI Disclosure 2026 requires quarterly-rail-rollup for cross-border-disclosures >¥100,000/yr per creator; DTAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (annual-rail-rollup only) is insufficient for high-JP-cohorts, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to DTAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation is suddenly required to file quarterly-rail-rollup with EU-UCPD-attestation. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's JP-cohort-traffic + aggregation-tenor — DTAT3 score only if cohort-JP-share >5% AND merchant-is-YouTube-Paid-Promotion-Badge-registered; DTAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-YouTube-Paid-Promotion-Badge-registered merchants.

4. **Pitfall — DTAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-annual-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-rail-rollup-pool + batch-per-quarter-rolling-90-day-disclosure-tooling-aggregator + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $80k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — DTAT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-US-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to DTAT1 zero-aggregation or DTAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

5. **Pitfall — DTAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a high-multi-state-cohort fail cross-state-aggregation-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to DTAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation is REQUIRED to track cross-state-disclosure-tooling-rollup for every state-disclosure-tooling event; non-compliance triggers cross-state-aggregation-enforcement and additional-state-penalties. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling-aggregation classifier must verify the merchant's cross-state-aggregation-tracking-status — DTAT4 score only if merchant-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active; DTAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active merchants.

6. **Pitfall — DTAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for high-margin-cohorts.** The daily-rail-aggregation-pool + MSA-archive-link-daily-rail-rollup + 365-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $130k/yr; even high-margin cohorts may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-disclosure-tooling if the cohort's disclosure-tooling-frequency is low. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-disclosure-tooling-frequency BEFORE scoring resonance — DTAT5 score 0 for cohorts with annual-disclosure-tooling-frequency <500 AND DTAT-notional <$1M. Route to DTAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators for low-frequency fallbacks.

7. **Pitfall — DTAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort fail EU UCPD 2026-annual-rail-rollup-requirement.** EU UCPD 2026 + EU UCPD Disclosure Aggregation 2026 requires annual-rail-rollup-reporting for cross-border-disclosures >€2000/yr per creator; DTAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (1-rollover-per-year) is REQUIRED to report by Jan-31-deadline. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's EU-creator-count + annual-disclosure-tooling-frequency — DTAT2 score only if cohort-EU-creator-count >30 AND annual-disclosure-tooling-frequency >2,000; DTAT1 zero-aggregation fallback for non-UCPD-required cohorts.

8. **Pitfall — DTAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to DTAT2 annual-rail-aggregation because of disclosure-tooling-attestation-staleness-event.** A creator whose disclosure-tooling-attestation was set 90 days ago may have a stale attestation-status that doesn't match the current attestation-status, and the merchant's disclosure-tooling-team may silently mark-down the disclosure-tooling-pool to annual-rollup (DTAT2) on the next rollup-event. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling-aggregation classifier must read the live disclosure-tooling-attestation-table (per CaptivateA Disclosure Tooling Aggregation 2026 + FTC 16 CFR Part 255 2026 + UK CMA 2024) and re-derive DTAT-tier on every rollup event.

9. **Pitfall — DTAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a multi-state-cohort fail state-AG-enforcement-risk.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to DTAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation across multiple states is REQUIRED to file cross-state-aggregation on every state-disclosure-tooling; failure triggers state-AG-enforcement (e.g., CA-FTB-state-disclosure + NY-DTF-state-disclosure + TX-Franchise-state-disclosure + FL-DR-1-state-disclosure) and potential $10k-$50k state-AG-penalties. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's state-distribution-count + state-disclosure-tooling-event-volume — DTAT4 score only if cohort-state-distribution-count >5 states AND merchant-state-AG-enforcement-history-clean; DTAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for risky cohorts.

10. **Pitfall — DTAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for multi-jurisdiction-merchants.** The daily-rail-aggregation-pool + MSA-archive-link-daily-rail-rollup + multi-jurisdiction-rollup-maint (FTC 16 CFR Part 255 2026 + FTC Endorsement Guides 2026 + EU UCPD 2026 + UK CMA 2024 + Germany TMG §6 2026 + France Loi Confiance 2026 + Japan APPI 2022 + Brazil CDC 2026 + Mexico PROFECO 2026) requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $130k/yr minimum; cohorts with low-frequency-disclosure-tooling-events to multi-jurisdiction-creators may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-disclosure-tooling. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-disclosure-tooling-frequency + cohort-jurisdiction-diversity BEFORE scoring resonance — DTAT5 score 0 for cohorts with jurisdiction-diversity >3 jurisdictions AND annual-disclosure-tooling-frequency <1000.

11. **Pitfall — DTAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to DTAT2 annual-rail-aggregation on a state-aggregation-tracking-failed-event.** A creator whose state-aggregation-tracking was tracked monthly may have the monthly-tracking silently fail (e.g., the merchant's state-aggregation-database has been unavailable to execute the monthly-rollup); the merchant is REQUIRED to fall back to DTAT2 annual-rail-aggregation on next disclosure-tooling. **Fix:** the disclosure-tooling-aggregation classifier must maintain a `p.dtat_monthly_rollup_status` field — set to true on rollup-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to DTAT2 on next disclosure-tooling AND adds a state-aggregation-tracking-reconciliation line-item.

12. **Pitfall — DTAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's disclosure-tooling-aggregation-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer DTAT1 zero-aggregation creators (low-maint-cost, Meta-Brand-Collabs-Manager-no-rail-pool, no-50-state-tracking) over DTAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators (high-maint-cost, daily-aggregation-tracking, MSA-archive-link-daily-rail-pool). A DTAT1 zero-aggregation creator may be DTAT-clean but DTAT-maint-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-DTAT-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — DTAT1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer DTAT5 (e.g., cohorts with high multi-jurisdiction-traffic).
