## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Skip-Move-#20.6-prerequisite.** Building Move #20.7 multilingual-voice-AI-tier-1-self-serve-deflection without Move #20.6 English-voice-AI-tier-1-self-serve-deflection shipped ≥90 days AND Move #20.5 tier-2-voice-AI-resolution shipped means no upstream voice-AI-infrastructure-shared-cost + no English-baseline-resolution-rate to compare against + no inbound-voice-call-routing + no tier-1-intent-voice-classifier foundation + no per-cohort-LTV-aware-policy-engine. Symptom: per-language-resolution-rate-floor-spike <35% + per-language-cost-per-call-spike >$1.50/call + per-language-CSAT-drop <3.8/5.0. Fix: defer Move #20.7 until Move #20.6 + Move #20.5 + Move #20.1 + Move #20.2 all shipped ≥90 days AND reporting ≥55% English-voice-AI-tier-1-resolution-rate AND ≥35% English-tier-2-voice-AI-resolution-rate.

2. **Below-multilingual-voice-call-volume-floor.** Building Move #20.7 multilingual-voice-AI-tier-1-self-serve-deflection with <250 calls/mo on multilingual-tier-1-voice-intents OR <1500 inbound-calls-per-month total OR <15% multilingual-customer-cohort-share means the per-language-tier-1-classifier training data is too sparse + per-language-pilot-cohort-floors-fail. Symptom: per-language-resolution-rate <35% + per-language-CSAT <3.8/5.0 + per-language-cost-per-call >$1.80/call + per-language-rollback-rate >35%. Fix: defer Move #20.7 until multilingual-call-volume ≥250/mo total AND multilingual-customer-cohort-share ≥15%.

3. **Missing-TCPA-prior-express-consent-with-multilingual-consent-copies.** Building Move #20.7 without TCPA-prior-express-consent-collection-mechanism with separate multilingual-consent-copies in each supported language means the voice-AI-call is a TCPA-violation ($500-$1,500/violation civil penalty per FCC-TCPA-rules). Symptom: per-call-TCPA-violation-rate >5% + class-action-lawsuit-risk + per-language-customer-opt-out-rate-spike. Fix: customer-voice-opt-in-checkbox-at-signup is mandatory in the signup-flow with separate multilingual-consent-copies in each supported language (Spanish/French/German/Italian/Portuguese/Japanese/Korean/Mandarin/Cantonese/Arabic/Hindi/Russian) + per-language-consent-update-mechanism.

4. **Missing-per-jurisdiction-call-recording-consent.** Building Move #20.7 without per-jurisdiction-call-recording-consent-collection-mechanism (CA all-party-consent, FL one-party-consent; Spain-LOPD, France-CNIL, Germany-DSGVO-TTDSG, Italy-Garante, Brazil-LGPD, Japan-PIPA, Korea-PIPA, China-PIPL, Dubai-DIFC-DPL, India-IT-Act, Russia-FZ-152) means the multilingual-voice-AI-call is a recording-violation + cross-border-data-transfer-violation ($1,000-$10,000/violation civil penalty per CA-Penal-Code-632 + per-jurisdiction-penalty-risk + class-action-risk). Symptom: per-jurisdiction-recording-consent-compliance-rate <90% + per-jurisdiction-class-action-risk + per-jurisdiction-data-residency-violation. Fix: per-jurisdiction-call-recording-consent-policy-engine validates every multilingual-voice-AI-call against the applicable jurisdiction + per-jurisdiction-recording-consent-template (12-locale × 12-jurisdiction = 144-templates) + per-jurisdiction-data-residency-routing-decision.
