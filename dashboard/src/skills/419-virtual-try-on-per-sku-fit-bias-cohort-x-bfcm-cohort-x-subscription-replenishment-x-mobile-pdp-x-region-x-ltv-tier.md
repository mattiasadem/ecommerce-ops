5. **7-region collapsed into 2-cell (north-america + ROW) or 4-cell (NA + EU + APAC + ROW) — OR 5-LTV-tier collapsed into 2-tier (high + low) or 3-tier (high + mid + low).** A common shortcut — operators see "international" as a single bucket AND "LTV" as a high-vs-low binary, collapsing the 7-region × 5-LTV-tier taxonomy into 2-cell × 2-tier = 4 cells (NA-high + NA-low + ROW-high + ROW-low). This loses 31-33 region × LTV-tier cells × 8-BFCM-cohort × 12-subscription-cohort × 15-device-cohort = 44,640-47,520 cells of decomposition per per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × product-cohort = 357,120-380,160 cells of decomposition total. This loses the region-specific-size-chart-mismatch + region-specific-payment-friction + region-specific-language-friction + region-specific-cultural-preference signal AND the per-customer LTV distribution signal. The per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × BFCM-cohort × subscription-replenishment × mobile-PDP × region × LTV-tier-leak-rate differential of 32-58% is invisible without per-region × LTV-tier-tag. 7. **EU-VAT-OSS + IOSS collapsed into a single "EU" tax-handling — and LTV-tier collapsed into a single "VIP vs non-VIP" binary.** The EU region has 27 member-states with VAT rates ranging from 17% (Luxembourg) to 27% (Hungary); IOSS is for ≤€150 imports, OSS is for cross-border B2C. Collapsing them into "EU" loses the per-country VAT-differential + per-country-OSS-vs-IOSS-routing signal that Move #74 customs-brokerage surfaces. Similarly, collapsing the 5-LTV-tier taxonomy into a 2-tier "VIP vs non-VIP" loses the per-tier-discount-depth optimization that Move #154 surfaces (whale-LTV-tier = 5%-off / high-LTV-tier = 10%-off / mid-LTV-tier = 15%-off / low-LTV-tier = 20%-off / churn_critical-LTV-tier = suppress). The combination loses 27 EU member-states × 5 LTV-tiers = 135 EU × LTV-tier cells per device-cohort × BFCM-cohort × subscription-cohort.

8. **latin-america-region treated as a single "LATAM" market — AND churn_critical-LTV-tier treated as a single suppression bucket.** LATAM is 33 countries with 3 official languages (Spanish / Portuguese / French-Haiti) + 5 major currencies (BRL / MXN / ARS / CLP / COP / PEN) + 4 payment systems (PIX-Brazil / SPEI-Mexico / OXXO-Mexico / Boleto-Brazil / PSE-Colombia). Collapsing them into "LATAM" loses per-country-language + per-country-payment-friction signal. Per Move #154: churn_critical-LTV-tier is NOT a single suppression bucket — it requires per-region × LTV-tier-cell-level analysis (churn_critical-LTV-tier × north-america-region = re-engagement candidate; churn_critical-LTV-tier × ssa-region = suppress because CAC > LTV). Operators who collapse churn_critical-LTV-tier into a global bucket lose the per-region × LTV-tier-cell-recovery-rate signal and over-suppress the recoverable subset.

9. **mena-region treated as a single "Middle East" market — AND whale-LTV-tier treated as a single high-spend bucket.** MENA has 21 countries with Arabic-RTL + Hebrew-RTL-Israel + Persian-RTL-Iran + Turkish-Latin-Turkey + Kurdish-Latin + Kurdish-Arabic-script. Collapsing into "Middle East" + "high-spend" loses the per-country-RTL-vs-LTR-script signal AND the whale-LTV-tier × mena-region precision-recommendation signal.

12. **ssa-region treated as a single "Africa" market — AND churn_critical-LTV-tier customers routed to global suppression instead of per-region routing.** SSA has 46 countries with English / French / Portuguese / Swahili / Arabic + mobile-money (M-Pesa-Kenya / MTN-Mobile-Money-Uganda / Airtel-Money / Orange-Money / Wave-Senegal / M-Pesa-Tanzania) + cash-on-delivery-prevalence > 60% in Nigeria. 13. **per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × BFCM-cohort × subscription-replenishment × mobile-PDP × region × LTV-tier-rollback-engine firing globally instead of per-cell.** A common shortcut — operators fire the rollback when the global 258,048,000-cell aggregate drops below benchmark. The fix is per-cell rollback: fire ONLY when a single cell completes below its own benchmark-floor for 2 consecutive BFCM-cohorts OR 2 consecutive subscription-cycles OR 2 consecutive device-cohorts OR 2 consecutive regions OR 2 consecutive LTV-tiers. Global rollback drops 258,047,999 healthy cells; per-cell rollback saves the 258,047,999 healthy cells and surgically fixes the 1 broken cell. Per Move #154's Triple-Whale-Cohort-LTV-180-day recommendation: the rollback-engine MUST consider LTV-tier — a churn_critical-LTV-tier × ssa-region × cyber-week-cohort cell at 46% completion is the EXPECTED baseline (not a rollback trigger), but a whale-LTV-tier × north-america-region × pre-BFCM-cohort cell at 84% completion IS a rollback trigger (16% below the 92% whale baseline).
14. **region × LTV-tier-traffic-burst-rollback firing on <60s rate-limit-warning.** A common false-positive: Cloudflare/Fastly/Akamai rate-limit-warnings fire on sub-minute spikes (legitimate BFCM-launch traffic + whale-LTV-tier email-open-spikes from Klaviyo). Firing rollback on every <60s warning drops revenue during legitimate BFCM-cycle-ramp-up + whale-LTV-tier email-campaign-ramp-up. The fix: rate-limit-warning MUST persist for ≥5 minutes AND ≥3 region × LTV-tier cells must hit the threshold simultaneously before rollback fires. Per Move #154's 4-suppression-gates: never score (and thus never rate-limit-warning) a customer who (i) ordered in last 30d, (ii) has 5+ orders lifetime (whale-LTV-tier), (iii) is in an active winback flow, (iv) is on the do-not-suppress list.
15. **region-aware-payment-fallback not wired — OR LTV-tier-aware-discount-fallback not wired — OR LTV-tier-aware-sunset-suppression not wired.** The Move #41.9 layer assumes (a) payment-fallback is region-aware (SADAD + Apple-Pay-MENA + PIX+CFDI-latin-america + UPI-india + M-Pesa-ssa + iDEAL-Netherlands + Klarna-EU + Afterpay-AU/NZ + Alipay-apac + WeChat-Pay-apac + Konbini-apac) AND (b) discount-fallback is LTV-tier-aware (whale-LTV-tier = 5%-off / high-LTV-tier = 10%-off / mid-LTV-tier = 15%-off / low-LTV-tier = 20%-off / churn_critical-LTV-tier = suppress) AND (c) sunset-suppression is LTV-tier-aware (whale + high NEVER sunset). The fix is to fall back to region-aware-payment-fallback based on Move #74 customs-brokerage-region-attribute AND to fall back to LTV-tier-aware-discount-fallback based on Move #154 customer_360.ltv_tier AND to fall back to LTV-tier-aware-sunset-suppression based on Move #154 customer_360.ltv_tier (whale + high are excluded from sunset flow per Move #154's suppression pattern).
