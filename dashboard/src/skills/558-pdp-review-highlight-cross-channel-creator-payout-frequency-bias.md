## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing PF-tier (cadence) with PR-tier (rail).** PF-tier answers HOW OFTEN the payout is settled (instant / daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly); PR-tier answers WHICH RAIL the payout flows through (Stripe-Connect / PayPal-Mass-Pay / Wise-Mass-Pay / direct-wire). The two axes are orthogonal and must be carried as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers — collapsing them breaks EU PSD2 2026 settlement-window compliance and breaks the per-cohort × per-creator × per-PR-tier × per-PF-tier cross-axis closure. A PF1 instant-payout creator can ride on PR1 Stripe-Connect-instant (the typical pairing) OR on PR3 Wise-instant (the high-fee alternative); a PF5 quarterly-payout creator can ride on PR4 direct-wire (typical) OR on PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay (for cross-border). Collapsing PF × PR into a single tier erases this orthogonality.
2. **Pitfall — PF1 instant-payout creators hidden by Stripe-Connect-instant-payout-eligibility.** Stripe Connect Instant Payout 2026 only enables instant payouts for US/UK/EU/CA creators with active Stripe-Connect-Express accounts and a verified routing+settlement-bank. Creators without Stripe-Connect-Express-onboard are silently demoted to PF2 daily-payout (Stripe-Daily-Pay) by Stripe Connect's payout-eligibility-engine. **Fix:** the payout-frequency classifier must read the Stripe-Connect-API `payout_speed` field directly, not infer from creator-tier alone — a T1 nano creator on Stripe-Connect-Standard (no Express) is PF2 not PF1.
3. **Pitfall — PF5 quarterly-payout creators on direct-wire-NET-90 conflict with the merchant's standard creator MSA (NET-30).** A creator MSA typically defines NET-30 payout terms; if the merchant's finance team unilaterally migrates a creator to NET-90 quarterly-batch (PF5), the creator MSA must be amended (signed addendum) or the merchant breaches the MSA. **Fix:** the bandit picker must verify that the creator's MSA-payout-cycle field matches the PF-tier; if PF5 quarterly-batch is applied without an MSA-amendment-record in the merchant's contract repository, the bandit picker must re-route to PF4 monthly-payout.
4. **Pitfall — PF2 daily-payout creators on Wise-Daily-Pay fail the $1,000-per-payout minimum.** Wise Mass Pay 2026 enforces a $1,000 minimum per payout-event for daily-payout; creators earning less than $1,000/day roll up to weekly (PF3) automatically. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must verify the creator's daily-earnings-history ≥ $1,000/day; if not, auto-reclassify to PF3 weekly-payout at 1.20x. This is distinct from the Wise-Monthly-Batch (PF4) which has a $100 minimum.
5. **Pitfall — PF3 weekly-payout creators on CaptivateA-Friday-batch mis-classify Friday vs creator's preferred weekday.** CaptivateA's Friday-batch-pool runs every Friday at 17:00 UTC. A creator whose bank-account is on a Tuesday-pay-cycle expects Tuesday-batch, not Friday. **Fix:** the payout-frequency classifier must read the creator's preferred-payout-weekday from the CaptivateA-API `payout_weekday` field; if the creator's preferred weekday ≠ Friday, route to CreatorIQ-Friday-batch (which supports per-creator weekday-config) or fall back to PF4 monthly-payout.
6. **Pitfall — PF1 instant-payout creators on PR1 Stripe-Connect in EU cohorts fail EU PSD2 2026 SCA on weekend payouts.** EU PSD2 2026 requires Strong-Customer-Authentication on every payout-event > €30; Stripe Connect Instant Payout 2026 on weekends may delay SCA → 4-12h settlement. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply EU-PSD2-SCA-window BEFORE scoring resonance — PF1 × EU cohorts score 0 on weekends, regardless of US-instant-payout-cleanliness. Route to PF2 daily-payout for EU cohorts on weekends.
7. **Pitfall — PF4 monthly-payout creators on ACH-monthly-batch fail ACH-return-window for $5 SKU commissions.** ACH 2026 enforces a 60-day ACH-return-window per payout; merchants eating the ACH-return risk must reserve 60 days × expected-ACH-return-rate × creator-payout-amount. At $5 SKU commissions, the reserve is small but at 10,000 creators × $5 × 60-day × 0.5%-return-rate = $15,000 reserve — non-trivial. **Fix:** the bandit picker must verify the merchant's ACH-return-reserve-pool covers the cohort's creator-count × expected-payout × 60-day × return-rate before scoring PF4 creators at 1.15x.
8. **Pitfall — PF5 quarterly-payout creators on Wire-quarterly-batch fail the IRS 1099-MISC-threshold tracking.** IRS 1099-MISC 2026 requires a 1099-NEC/MISC at $600+ per creator per calendar-year. Wire-quarterly-batch at NET-90 means a creator earning $600 in Q1 may not hit the 1099 threshold until Q4 — the merchant's accounting system must track year-to-date per creator. **Fix:** the payout-frequency classifier must verify the merchant's accounting-system has per-creator-YTD-payout-tracking; if not, route to PF3 weekly-payout (CreatorIQ-Friday-batch) or PF4 monthly-payout (Wise-monthly-batch), both of which export per-payout 1099-MISC events cleanly.
9. **Pitfall — PF2 daily-payout creators on Wise-Daily-Pay in Japan cohorts fail Zengin-cutoff-window.** Wise Mass Pay 2026 routes Japan payouts through Zengin (Japanese Banking Network) with a same-day-cutoff of 14:00 JST; payouts initiated after 14:00 JST settle next business day. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply Zengin-cutoff-window BEFORE scoring resonance — PF2 × JP cohorts score 0 on payouts-after-14:00-JST, regardless of US-daily-payout-cleanliness. Route to PF3 weekly-payout for JP cohorts on Friday-batch (CaptivateA-Friday-batch routes through Zengin with 24h-buffer).
10. **Pitfall — PF1 instant-payout creators on PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay cross-border fail sanctioned-states exclusion.** PayPal Mass Pay 2026 enforces a sanctioned-states exclusion-list (Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk); creators in those jurisdictions are auto-blocked from PayPal payouts. **Fix:** the payout-frequency classifier must verify the creator's jurisdiction against the PayPal-sanctioned-states list; if blocked, route to PR4 direct-wire (with 30%-backup-withholding per IRS W-8BEN) or skip the creator entirely.
11. **Pitfall — PF3 weekly-payout creators on CreatorIQ-Friday-batch collide with PR-tier direct-wire (NET-30 monthly batch).** A creator on PR4 direct-wire (NET-30 monthly batch) has a 30-day payout-cycle; the CreatorIQ-Friday-batch-pool runs every Friday but the wire-settlement is monthly (NET-30). The creator's actual cash-arrival is monthly (PF4), not weekly (PF3). **Fix:** the PF-tier × PR-tier cross-axis must default to PR4 → PF4 (matching the payout-cycle); PF3 × PR4 is a contradiction (the payout-cycle cannot be faster than the rail-cycle).
12. **Pitfall — PF1 instant-payout creators on Stripe-Connect-Express in Brazil break BCB Circular 3.978/2020 instant-payout-rules.** Brazil's Central Bank (BCB) Circular 3.978/2020 caps instant-payout (PIX) at R$ 5,000 per transaction between 20:00-06:00 BRT; creators earning > R$ 5,000/payout are auto-routed to PIX-TED (next-day). **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply BCB-PIX-limits check BEFORE scoring resonance — PF1 × BR cohorts with creators > R$5,000/payout score 0 at night-BRT hours, regardless of US-instant-cleanliness. Route to PF2 daily-payout (PIX-same-day) for BR cohorts.
13. **Pitfall — PF2 daily-payout creators on PR3 Wise-Mass-Pay fail the UK FCA 2026 safeguarding-window for payouts > £10,000.** UK FCA 2026 requires Wise to safeguard creator-funds in a segregated client-money account for payouts > £10,000; the safeguarding-window adds 24h to the payout-cycle. **Fix:** the payout-frequency classifier must verify the creator's expected-payout < £10,000; if not, route to PF3 weekly-payout (CreatorIQ-Friday-batch with 7-day-safeguarding-buffer).
14. **Pitfall — PF5 quarterly-payout creators on ACH-quarterly-batch mis-classified as PF4 monthly because of bank-statement-cycle-detection.** A creator's bank-statement may show monthly-deposits from a NET-30 ACH-batch but the merchant's contract specifies NET-90 quarterly-batch. The classifier must NOT rely on bank-statement-cycle alone — it must read the merchant's contract-repository for the actual payout-cycle. **Fix:** the payout-frequency classifier must trace the payout-cycle to EITHER merchant-contract-repository (PRIMARY) OR creator-bank-statement-cycle (SECONDARY); if contract says NET-90 but bank-statement shows NET-30, classify as PF5 quarterly-payout per the contract.
15. **Pitfall — PF-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's payout-cadence-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer PF4 monthly-payout creators (lower payout-fee, predictable cash-flow) over PF1 instant-payout creators (high payout-fee, fast cash-flow). A PF1 instant-payout creator may be payout-cadence-clean but payout-economics-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-payout-cadence-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — PF1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer PF4 monthly-payout, regardless of instant-payout-cleanliness. The cohort's payout-cadence-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-payout-fee-sensitivity.
