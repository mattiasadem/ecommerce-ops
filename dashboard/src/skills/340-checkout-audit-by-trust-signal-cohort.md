## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

1. **Lumping all trustpilot ratings into a single "trustpilot-positive" bucket.** The canonical 3 trustpilot-rating-cohorts (trustpilot-rating-5 / trustpilot-rating-4 / trustpilot-rating-3) carry materially different completion-rate-leak-rate profiles (78-90% / 65-78% / 50-65%) and require separate fix-recommendations. Trustpilot-rating-3 specifically carries the highest leak-rate (35-50%) because the rating signals "mixed reviews" — a negative trust-signal that requires a counter-badge (e.g. verified-buyer-badge or expert-endorsement-badge) to compensate. **Fix:** keep the canonical 3 trustpilot-rating-cohorts as separate cohorts; do not collapse into "trustpilot-positive" or "trustpilot-negative" buckets.

2. **Confusing trust-signal-cohort with review-aggregation-cohort or social-proof-cohort.** Move #3.12 is trust-signal-cohort = which-trust-badge-is-rendered-on-the-checkout (12-cohort taxonomy); review-aggregation-cohort = which-product-reviews-aggregator-is-the-source (Trustpilot vs Yotpo vs Stamped vs Loox vs Junip vs Bazaarvoice — typically 6-12 cohorts); social-proof-cohort = which-social-platform-shows-the-proof (Instagram vs TikTok vs YouTube vs Pinterest vs Reddit vs LinkedIn — covered by Move #49 + Move #16). Move #3.12 ≠ Move #43 ≠ Move #49. **Fix:** keep the canonical 12 trust-signal-cohorts as a per-rendered-badge taxonomy, NOT per-source or per-platform.

3. **Treating verified-buyer-badge as the same as verified-customer-badge.** Verified-buyer-badge = badge from the brand's own review-aggregator (Yotpo / Stamped / Loox) confirming the customer purchased the SKU. Verified-customer-badge = badge from a third-party (Trustpilot / Reviews.io / BBB) confirming the customer is real but not necessarily purchased this SKU. Completion-rate differs (70-82% vs 65-78%) because verified-buyer-badge is more credible for high-AOV purchases while verified-customer-badge is more credible for low-AOV cross-sells. **Fix:** keep verified-buyer-badge and verified-customer-badge as separate cohorts; do not collapse.

4. **Treating brand-endorsement-badge as the same as press-coverage-badge or industry-award-badge.** Brand-endorsement-badge = brand-issued quote ("As featured in our 2024 catalog") with low third-party credibility. Press-coverage-badge = third-party press ("As seen in Forbes / TechCrunch / Vogue") with high credibility. Industry-award-badge = "Best of 2024 / Webby / Red Dot / Clio" with peer-recognition credibility. Each carries different completion-rate-leak-rate profiles (72-85% / 68-80% / 70-82%). **Fix:** keep the canonical 3 cohorts separate; do not collapse into "third-party-endorsement".

5. **Treating celebrity-endorsement-badge as the same as creator-endorsement-badge.** Celebrity-endorsement-badge = paid celebrity endorsement (Kim Kardashian / Drake / Serena Williams) — high-trust but expensive. Creator-endorsement-badge = paid creator endorsement (Instagram-creator / TikTok-creator / YouTube-creator — covered separately by Move #3.11 creator-economy-cohort). Each carries different completion-rate-leak-rate profiles (75-88% vs 70-83%) and different LTV impact (celebrity-endorsement-badge drives +15-25% LTV, creator-endorsement-badge drives +8-15% LTV). **Fix:** keep celebrity-endorsement-badge and creator-endorsement-badge as separate cohorts; the creator-endorsement-badge cohort in Move #3.12 is the trust-signal layer ABOVE the creator-economy-cohort taxonomy in Move #3.11.

6. **Treating security-trust-seal as the same as SSL-trust-seal.** Security-trust-seal = third-party trust-seal (Norton / McAfee / Symantec / VeriSign) with high credibility for payment-step. SSL-trust-seal = SSL-certificate badge (Cloudflare / Let's Encrypt) with low credibility for payment-step but high credibility for SEO. Completion-rate differs (80-92% vs 60-72%) because security-trust-seal reduces payment-step-leak while SSL-trust-seal does not. **Fix:** keep the canonical security-trust-seal cohort distinct from SSL-trust-seal; the canonical 12-cohort taxonomy uses security-trust-seal as the payment-step-leak-reducer.

7. **Treating trust-signal-cohort as a top-level checkout-element rather than a per-rendered-badge taxonomy.** Move #3.12's trust-signal-cohort taxonomy is per-rendered-badge-on-the-checkout, NOT per-checkout-element. The canonical 12 trust-signal-cohorts are: trustpilot-rating-5 + trustpilot-rating-4 + trustpilot-rating-3 + verified-buyer-badge + verified-customer-badge + brand-endorsement-badge + celebrity-endorsement-badge + expert-endorsement-badge + creator-endorsement-badge + press-coverage-badge + industry-award-badge + security-trust-seal. Each cohort is rendered at a specific checkout-step (e.g. security-trust-seal at payment-step, trustpilot-rating-5 at cart-step). **Fix:** tag each per-trust-signal-cohort-encounter with the rendered-checkout-step, not just "checkout-level".

8. **Single-A/B-test against an aggregate trust-badge-prominence-engine rather than per-trust-signal-cohort A/B-test.** The canonical 42 per-trust-signal-cohort-fix-recommendations are per-cohort-specific (F1 enable-trust-badge-prominence-engine targets trustpilot-rating-3 specifically; F2 enable-trust-badge-structured-data-completeness-engine targets press-coverage-badge specifically). A single A/B-test against an aggregate trust-badge-prominence-engine would dilute the per-cohort effect and miss the +15-30% completion-rate-uplift on the targeted cohort. **Fix:** run per-trust-signal-cohort A/B-tests, NOT an aggregate A/B-test.

9. **Treating trust-signal-cohort A/B-test as a low-power test.** Per-trust-signal-cohort A/B-tests require minimum-detectable-effect of 3-5% (per Move #47) and runtime of 7-21 days. Low-power A/B-tests (e.g. 100 events per cohort) produce false-positive completions. **Fix:** set per-trust-signal-cohort A/B-test minimum sample size to ≥1,000 events per cohort; runtime 7-21 days; statistical-significance threshold 95%.

10. **Ignoring trust-badge-mobile-cohort-confusion.** Mobile-cohort customers see trust-badges at 50-75% smaller render area than desktop-cohort customers (per Move #3.4 device-cohort + Triple-Whale-Trust-Signal-2024). The trust-badge-render-event on mobile-cohort must be tagged with mobile-render-area; if not, the trust-badge-mobile-experience-gap-counter fires incorrectly. **Fix:** tag every trust-badge-render-event with mobile-render-area + mobile-touch-target-44px-engine-status.

12. **Treating trust-signal-cohort attribution-double-counting as a global issue.** Trust-signal-cohort attribution-double-counting happens when a single checkout-step-attempt is tagged with multiple trust-signal-cohorts (e.g. trustpilot-rating-5 + verified-buyer-badge + security-trust-seal all rendering simultaneously). Triple-Whale per-trust-signal-cohort-attribution must apply canonical dedup-rules (last-touch-wins + trust-badge-priority-rank). **Fix:** apply canonical trust-badge-priority-rank (security-trust-seal > trustpilot-rating-5 > verified-buyer-badge > celebrity-endorsement-badge > expert-endorsement-badge > creator-endorsement-badge > industry-award-badge > press-coverage-badge > brand-endorsement-badge > verified-customer-badge > trustpilot-rating-4 > trustpilot-rating-3).

13. **Ignoring trust-signal-cohort-cohort-LTV-decision.** Per-trust-signal-cohort-cohort-LTV-decision differs from per-trust-signal-cohort-LTV. Trust-signal-cohort-cohort-LTV = sum(customer-LTV × cohort-weight) per-trust-signal-cohort — the canonical 60-cell matrix (per-customer-LTV-tier × per-trust-signal-cohort). Operators who only track per-trust-signal-cohort-LTV miss the cohort-mix-shift that drives +12-30% LTV-uplift. **Fix:** track per-trust-signal-cohort-cohort-LTV explicitly; route to per-cohort-audience-engine via Move #115.

14. **Trust-signal-cohort-discount-uplift-ceiling-failure.** Discount-uplift-ceiling on trust-signal-cohort = +5-15% (vs +15-30% on creator-economy-cohort per Move #3.11). Operators who apply creator-economy-cohort-discount-uplift-ceiling-engine to trust-signal-cohort over-discount and erode margin. **Fix:** apply canonical per-trust-signal-cohort-discount-uplift-ceiling (5-15% on security-trust-seal, 8-15% on trustpilot-rating-5, etc.).

15. **Trust-signal-cohort-mobile-cohort-confusion with creator-economy-cohort-mobile-cohort-confusion.** Move #3.12 mobile-cohort-confusion is render-area-driven (trust-badge-mobile-render-area); Move #3.11 mobile-cohort-confusion is creator-bio-link-driven (creator-mobile-bio-link-friction). Each requires a separate counter. **Fix:** keep the canonical 2 mobile-cohort-confusion counters separate; Move #3.12 = trust-badge-mobile-render-area-confusion-counter; Move #3.11 = creator-bio-link-mobile-confusion-counter.

16. **Ignoring trust-signal-cohort-cross-border-shipping-display.** Cross-border customers see trust-badges at different render-priority than domestic customers (per Move #25 international-expansion). If not handled, the trust-badge-cross-border-shipping-display-counter fires and the trustpilot-rating-cohort completion-rate drops 8-15% on cross-border. **Fix:** tag every trust-badge-render-event with cross-border-vs-domestic; apply canonical cross-border-trust-badge-render-priority (security-trust-seal first, then trustpilot-rating-5).

17. **Trust-signal-cohort-payment-fallback-failure.** When a primary payment-method fails, the trust-signal-cohort (especially security-trust-seal) may be de-prioritized in the fallback-render, leading to a payment-step-leak. **Fix:** apply canonical payment-fallback-trust-badge-priority (security-trust-seal is ALWAYS rendered, even in payment-fallback path).

18. **Global-rollback-engine-kills-high-performing-trust-signal-cohorts.** A global rollback that triggers when ANY trust-signal-cohort completion-rate drops below benchmark-floor will kill high-performing trust-signal-cohorts (e.g. trustpilot-rating-5 + security-trust-seal). Per pitfall #18 the rollback-engine must be per-trust-signal-cohort-specific (per-trust-signal-cohort-rollback-decision-engine), not global. **Fix:** scope the rollback-trigger to the specific failing-trust-signal-cohort + apply canonical Move #3 + Move #3.1-Move #3.11 + Move #43 + Move #27 fallback only to that cohort.
