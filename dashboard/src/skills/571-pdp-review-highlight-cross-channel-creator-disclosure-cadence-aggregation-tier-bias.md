## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing DCAT-tier (disclosure-cadence-aggregation-tier) with DC-tier (disclosure-cadence-tier) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.43].** DCAT-tier answers WHICH aggregation-cadence the merchant uses (zero-rail-aggregation-pool + annual-rail-aggregation-pool + quarterly-rail-aggregation-pool + monthly-rail-aggregation-pool + daily-rail-aggregation-pool); DC-tier answers WHICH disclosure-cadence (DC1 real-time-per-post + DC2 batch-per-week + DC3 batch-per-month + DC4 batch-per-quarter + DC5 vintage-disclosed-at-onboard). The two axes are orthogonal — a DCAT1 zero-aggregation creator may use DC1 real-time-per-post (typical, no-rail-rollup-required) or DC2 batch-per-week (rare, no-rail-rollup-required); a DCAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creator is forced into DC5 vintage-disclosed-at-onboard (vintage-disclosed-at-onboard-daily-rail-rollup-pool). **Fix:** carry DCAT-tier and DC-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung precedence ladders (DCAT1-DCAT2-DCAT3-DCAT4-DCAT5 and DC1-DC2-DC3-DC4-DC5) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-DCAT-tier × per-DC-tier 5×5 cross-axis grid that scores 25 distinct DCAT×DC cadence combinations.

2. **Pitfall — DCAT1 zero-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected annual-rail-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses DCAT1 zero-aggregation pool (in-caption-#ad-no-rail-rollup-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-border-traffic may have a disclosure that crosses USD-EU-border without an annual-rail-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$55k-annual-rail-maint + 1-rollover-per-year on the disclosure event. **Fix:** the disclosure-cadence-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's cross-border-traffic-share + cohort's aggregation-tolerance — DCAT1 score only if cohort-cross-border-share <10% AND cohort-DCAT-tolerance = high; DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.

3. **Pitfall — DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-JP-cohort fail quarterly-rail-rollup-requirement.** Japan APPI 2022 + Japan APPI Disclosure 2026 requires quarterly-rail-rollup for cross-border-disclosures >¥100,000/yr per creator; DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (annual-rail-rollup only) is insufficient for high-JP-cohorts, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to DCAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation is suddenly required to file quarterly-rail-rollup with EU-UCPD-attestation. **Fix:** the disclosure-cadence-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's JP-cohort-traffic + aggregation-tenor — DCAT3 score only if cohort-JP-share >5% AND merchant-is-TikTok-Creator-Marketplace-registered; DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-TikTok-Creator-Marketplace-registered merchants.

4. **Pitfall — DCAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-annual-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-rail-rollup-pool + batch-per-quarter-rolling-90-day-disclosure-aggregator + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $80k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the disclosure-cadence-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — DCAT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-US-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to DCAT1 zero-aggregation or DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

5. **Pitfall — DCAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a high-multi-state-cohort fail cross-state-aggregation-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to DCAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation is REQUIRED to track cross-state-disclosure-rollup for every state-disclosure event; non-compliance triggers cross-state-aggregation-enforcement and additional-state-penalties. **Fix:** the disclosure-cadence-aggregation classifier must verify the merchant's cross-state-aggregation-tracking-status — DCAT4 score only if merchant-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active; DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active merchants.

6. **Pitfall — DCAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for high-margin-cohorts.** The daily-rail-aggregation-pool + vintage-disclosed-at-onboard-#ad-daily-rail-rollup + 365-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $130k/yr; even high-margin cohorts may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-disclosure if the cohort's disclosure-frequency is low. **Fix:** the disclosure-cadence-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-disclosure-frequency BEFORE scoring resonance — DCAT5 score 0 for cohorts with annual-disclosure-frequency <500 AND DCAT-notional <$1M. Route to DCAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators for low-frequency fallbacks.

7. **Pitfall — DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort fail EU UCPD 2026-annual-rail-rollup-requirement.** EU UCPD 2026 + EU UCPD Disclosure Aggregation 2026 requires annual-rail-rollup-reporting for cross-border-disclosures >€2000/yr per creator; DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (1-rollover-per-year) is REQUIRED to report by Jan-31-deadline. **Fix:** the disclosure-cadence-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's EU-creator-count + annual-disclosure-frequency — DCAT2 score only if cohort-EU-creator-count >30 AND annual-disclosure-frequency >2,000; DCAT1 zero-aggregation fallback for non-UCPD-required cohorts.

8. **Pitfall — DCAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation because of disclosure-attestation-staleness-event.** A creator whose disclosure-attestation was set 90 days ago may have a stale attestation-status that doesn't match the current attestation-status, and the merchant's disclosure-team may silently mark-down the disclosure-pool to annual-rollup (DCAT2) on the next rollup-event. **Fix:** the disclosure-cadence-aggregation classifier must read the live disclosure-attestation-table (per CaptivateA Disclosure Cadence Aggregation 2026 + FTC 16 CFR Part 255 2026 + UK CMA 2024) and re-derive DCAT-tier on every rollup event.

9. **Pitfall — DCAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a multi-state-cohort fail state-AG-enforcement-risk.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to DCAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation across multiple states is REQUIRED to file cross-state-aggregation on every state-disclosure; failure triggers state-AG-enforcement (e.g., CA-FTB-state-disclosure + NY-DTF-state-disclosure + TX-Franchise-state-disclosure + FL-DR-1-state-disclosure) and potential $10k-$50k state-AG-penalties. **Fix:** the disclosure-cadence-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's state-distribution-count + state-disclosure-event-volume — DCAT4 score only if cohort-state-distribution-count >5 states AND merchant-state-AG-enforcement-history-clean; DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for risky cohorts.

10. **Pitfall — DCAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for multi-jurisdiction-merchants.** The daily-rail-aggregation-pool + vintage-disclosed-at-onboard-#ad-daily-rail-rollup + multi-jurisdiction-rollup-maint (FTC 16 CFR Part 255 2026 + FTC Endorsement Guides 2026 + EU UCPD 2026 + UK CMA 2024 + Germany TMG §6 2026 + France Loi Confiance 2026 + Japan APPI 2022 + Brazil CDC 2026 + Mexico PROFECO 2026) requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $130k/yr minimum; cohorts with low-frequency-disclosure-events to multi-jurisdiction-creators may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-disclosure. **Fix:** the disclosure-cadence-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-disclosure-frequency + cohort-jurisdiction-diversity BEFORE scoring resonance — DCAT5 score 0 for cohorts with jurisdiction-diversity >3 jurisdictions AND annual-disclosure-frequency <1000.

11. **Pitfall — DCAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation on a state-aggregation-tracking-failed-event.** A creator whose state-aggregation-tracking was tracked monthly may have the monthly-tracking silently fail (e.g., the merchant's state-aggregation-database has been unavailable to execute the monthly-rollup); the merchant is REQUIRED to fall back to DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation on next disclosure. **Fix:** the disclosure-cadence-aggregation classifier must maintain a `p.dcat_monthly_rollup_status` field — set to true on rollup-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to DCAT2 on next disclosure AND adds a state-aggregation-tracking-reconciliation line-item.

12. **Pitfall — DCAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's disclosure-cadence-aggregation-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer DCAT1 zero-aggregation creators (low-maint-cost, in-caption-#ad-no-rail-rollup, no-50-state-tracking) over DCAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators (high-maint-cost, daily-aggregation-tracking, vintage-disclosed-at-onboard-daily-rail-pool). A DCAT1 zero-aggregation creator may be DCAT-clean but DCAT-maint-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-DCAT-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — DCAT1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer DCAT5 (e.g., cohorts with high multi-jurisdiction-traffic). The cohort's DCAT-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-DCAT-maint-cost-sensitivity × cohort-multi-jurisdiction-traffic-preference.

13. **Pitfall — DCAT-tier cross-axis with PR-tier silently double-counts aggregation-cost on cross-platform disclosures.** A creator on DCAT1 zero-aggregation with PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay as secondary platform is potentially double-charged aggregation-cost (PayPal's auto-aggregation-generation + merchant's DCAT-rollup on the same disclosure). The disclosure-cadence-aggregation classifier must not double-count aggregation-cost on cross-platform-cohorts. **Fix:** the DCAT-tier × PR-tier cross-axis must apply secondary-platform-DCAT-conditional — DCAT1 × PR2 score uses PayPal-Mass-Pay-DCAT as the secondary; the cohort's primary DCAT-rule is zero-aggregation.

14. **Pitfall — DCAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators mis-classified as DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation because of disclosure-attestation-stale state.** A creator whose disclosure-attestation was set 90 days ago may have a stale attestation that doesn't match the current attestation-status, and the merchant's disclosure-team may silently mark-down the disclosure-pool to annual-rollup (DCAT2) on the next rollup-event. The actual state is "DCAT3-pending — disclosure-attestation-stale". **Fix:** the disclosure-cadence-aggregation classifier must distinguish between DCAT2-confirmed / DCAT3-pending / DCAT3-confirmed states; route DCAT3-pending creators to DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation as a temporary fallback AND flag for disclosure-attestation-refresh.

15. **Pitfall — DCAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's disclosure-cadence-aggregation-residency-preference.** A cohort with US-domestic-only preference (no cross-border-traffic) requires DCAT1 zero-aggregation pool; a cohort with EU-only preference requires DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation pool (or DCAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation for EU-LLCs); a cohort with JP-only preference requires DCAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation pool (or DCAT2 annual-rail-aggregation for JP-individuals); a cohort with BR-only preference requires DCAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation pool — but the DCAT-tier model US-centric-by-default; cohort must use DCAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation as BR-equivalent. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-DCAT-residency-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — DCAT5 × US-domestic-cohort score 0; route to DCAT1 zero-aggregation fallbacks. Add a "DCAT-tier × cohort-jurisdiction" overlay.
