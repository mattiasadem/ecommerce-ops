## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing TF-tier (payout tax form) with PR-tier (payment rail) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.33].** TF-tier answers WHICH payout-tax-form is filed with the IRS (W-9 + W-8BEN + W-8BEN-E + state-1099 + 30%-backup-withholding); PR-tier answers HOW the payment is RAILED (Stripe-Connect + PayPal-Mass-Pay + Wise-Mass-Pay + ACH + Wire). The two axes are orthogonal and must be carried as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers — collapsing them breaks IRS 2026 form-filing compliance AND breaks the per-cohort × per-creator × per-PR-tier × per-TF-tier cross-axis closure. A TF1 W-9-US creator with 1099-NEC can ride on PR1 Stripe-Connect (the typical pairing) OR on PR4 direct-wire (for high-touch brands); a TF5 no-TIN creator with 30%-backup-withholding can ride on PR3 Wise-Mass-Pay (typical) OR on PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay (for low-friction fallbacks). Collapsing TF × PR into a single tier erases this orthogonality.

2. **Pitfall — TF1 W-9-US creators mis-classified as TF2 W-8BEN-individual on a missing-TIN basis.** IRS 2026 requires US individual/entity creators to file W-9 with valid TIN (SSN/EIN). Creators without a valid TIN are NOT eligible for TF1 W-9-US pool and must be routed to TF5 no-TIN (degraded to 30%-backup-withholding). The previous fall-through to TF2 W-8BEN-individual is wrong — TF2 requires a non-US individual with valid foreign-TIN, not a US creator missing TIN. **Fix:** the payout-tax-form classifier must read the creator's tax-residency field (US vs non-US) BEFORE assigning TF-tier — a US creator with missing-TIN is TF5 no-TIN, NOT TF2 W-8BEN-individual.

3. **Pitfall — TF2 W-8BEN-individual creators on 1042-S-only-pool fail IRS Form 1042-S 2026 treaty-rate-claim.** IRS Form 1042-S 2026 requires treaty-rate-claim for foreign-individual creators claiming 0%-NRA-withholding-rate; creators without a valid treaty-claim (e.g., country-no-treaty-with-US) are subject to 30%-NRA-withholding. **Fix:** the payout-tax-form classifier must verify the creator's treaty-claim-state; route to TF5 no-TIN if treaty-claim-invalid.

4. **Pitfall — TF3 W-8BEN-E-entity creators on 1042-S fail IRS Chapter 4 FATCA 2026 entity-classification-rule.** IRS Chapter 4 FATCA 2026 requires W-8BEN-E entity creators to be classified as Active NFFE / Passive NFFE / Exempt Beneficial Owner; mis-classification triggers 30%-withholding-on-US-source-income. **Fix:** the payout-tax-form classifier must verify the entity's FATCA-classification-state; route to TF5 no-TIN if FATCA-classification-missing.

5. **Pitfall — TF4 W-9-with-state-tax creators on state-1099-R fail multi-state-filing-aggregator integration.** State-1099-R 2026 requires filing with EACH state the creator resides in OR earned income in; creators with multi-state-residency require multi-state-filing-aggregator (CA FTB 2026 + NY DTF 2026 + TX Franchise Tax 2026 + FL DR-1 2026 + IL Illinois AG970 2026 + PA Revenue 2026 + OH Ohio IT 2026 + MI Michigan 770 2026). **Fix:** the payout-tax-form classifier must verify the creator's state-residency-list BEFORE scoring resonance — TF4 score 0 for creators with no-state-filing-infrastructure.

6. **Pitfall — TF5 no-TIN creators on 30%-backup-withholding fail IRS-published-rate-2026 24%-withheld-claim.** IRS 2026 30%-backup-withholding is required for any payee without valid TIN; the backup-withholding-rate is 24% (the IRS-published-rate-2026 for the current tax-year), and the merchant must remit the 24% to the IRS via Form 945. **Fix:** the payout-tax-form classifier must verify the merchant's Form-945-filing-infrastructure; route to TF1 W-9-US or TF2 W-8BEN-individual if TIN-collection-infrastructure-missing.

7. **Pitfall — TF1 W-9-US creators in cross-platform-cohorts fail cross-platform-1099-aggregation-conditional.** Stripe-Connect 1099-K 2026 + PayPal Mass Pay 1099-K 2026 + Wise Mass Pay 1099-K 2026 each issue their own 1099-K; creators earning on multiple platforms require cross-platform-1099-aggregation to avoid duplicate-withholding. **Fix:** the payout-tax-form classifier must verify the cohort's cross-platform-1099-aggregation-state; route to TF5 no-TIN if aggregation-failure.

8. **Pitfall — TF2 W-8BEN-individual creators on 1042-S fail EU DAC7 2026 platform-reporting-requirement.** EU DAC7 2026 requires digital-platform-operators to report non-EU-resident-creator-earnings to EU tax-authorities; creators from EU-resident countries on TF2 W-8BEN-individual may be DOUBLE-REPORTED (EU DAC7 + US 1042-S). **Fix:** the payout-tax-form classifier must verify the creator's EU-residency-state; route to TF3 W-8BEN-E-entity for EU-resident-creators with treaty-claim.

9. **Pitfall — TF3 W-8BEN-E-entity creators on 1042-S fail UK RDEC 2026 entity-classification-rule.** UK RDEC 2026 requires UK-resident-entity creators to file P800 + CT600 + iXBRL-tagged-accounts; non-compliance triggers UK-HMRC-penalties. **Fix:** the payout-tax-form classifier must verify the entity's UK-resident-state; route to TF5 no-TIN if UK-resident-without-CT600.

10. **Pitfall — TF4 W-9-with-state-tax creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The state-1099-R 2026 filing-aggregator requires 1 FTE accountant to keep multi-state-filing live + integrate new states + maintain compliance; low-margin cohorts (<$5M annual) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the payout-tax-form classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — TF4 score 0 for cohorts with <$5M annual revenue. Route to TF1 W-9-US creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

11. **Pitfall — TF5 no-TIN creators fail 1-FTE-$120k-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The 30%-backup-withholding-burden 2026 requires 1 FTE tax-compliance to track backup-withholding + file Form 945 + remit 24%-withheld + handle state-varied-backup-withholding + reconcile year-end. **Fix:** the payout-tax-form classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — TF5 score 0 for cohorts with <$5M annual revenue. Route to TF1 W-9-US creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

12. **Pitfall — TF-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's tax-form-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer TF1 W-9-US creators (lower tool-cost, no-backup-withholding, auto-filed-by-Stripe-Connect) over TF5 no-TIN creators (high tool-cost, 30%-backup-withholding, 1-FTE-$120k-maint-cost). A TF1 W-9-US creator may be tax-form-clean but tax-form-cost-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-tax-form-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — TF1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer TF5 no-TIN creators, regardless of 1099-NEC-cleanliness. The cohort's tax-form-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-tax-form-cost-sensitivity × cohort-tax-form-residency-preference.

13. **Pitfall — TF-tier cross-axis with PN-tier silently double-files tax forms.** A creator on TF1 W-9-US with PN2 TikTok-native as secondary platform is double-filed (Stripe-Connect 1099-NEC + TikTok-Creator-Marketplace 1099-K). The payout-tax-form classifier must not double-count tax forms on cross-platform-cohorts. **Fix:** the TF-tier × PN-tier cross-axis must apply secondary-platform-tax-form-conditional — TF1 × PN2 score uses TikTok-Creator-Marketplace-1099-K as the secondary; the cohort's primary tax form is Stripe-Connect-1099-NEC.

14. **Pitfall — TF4 W-9-with-state-tax creators on multi-state-filing-aggregator mis-classified as TF5 no-TIN because of state-filing-failure-state.** A creator on TF4 W-9-with-state-tax whose multi-state-filing has failed (e.g., state-aggregator down, integration broken) is silently demoted to TF5 no-TIN creators. The actual state is "state-filing-failed — state-tax not remitted". **Fix:** the payout-tax-form classifier must distinguish between state-filing-success / state-filing-failed / no-state-filing states; route state-filing-failed creators to TF1 W-9-US as a temporary fallback.

15. **Pitfall — TF-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's tax-residency-preference.** A cohort with US-domestic-only preference (no EU/JP/BR/MX creators) requires TF1 W-9-US or TF4 W-9-with-state-tax pool; a cohort with EU-only preference requires TF2 W-8BEN-individual or TF3 W-8BEN-E-entity pool. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-tax-residency-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — TF2 × US-domestic-cohort score 0; route to TF1 W-9-US or TF4 W-9-with-state-tax fallbacks.
