## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing DMAT-tier (disclosure-mode-aggregation-tier) with DM-tier (disclosure-mode-tier) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.46].** DMAT-tier answers WHICH aggregation-cadence the merchant uses (zero-rail-aggregation-pool + annual-rail-aggregation-pool + quarterly-rail-aggregation-pool + monthly-rail-aggregation-pool + daily-rail-aggregation-pool); DM-tier answers WHICH disclosure-mode (DM1 #ad-disclosure + DM2 bio-link-disclosure + DM3 credentials-disclosure + DM4 aspirational-disclosure + DM5 14-day-cooling-off-reminder-disclosure + DM6 "as seen on" disclosure + DM7 16-locale-creator-relationship-disclosure). The two axes are orthogonal — a DM1 #ad-disclosure creator is typically on DMAT1 zero-aggregation (no disclosure-mode-rail-rollup-required); a DM5 14-day-cooling-off-reminder-disclosure creator is typically forced into DMAT5 daily-rail-aggregation (vintage-mode-record-daily-rail-pool). **Fix:** carry DMAT-tier and DM-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung + 7-rung precedence ladders (DMAT1-DMAT2-DMAT3-DMAT4-DMAT5 and DM1-DM2-DM3-DM4-DM5-DM6-DM7) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-DMAT-tier × per-DM-tier 5×7 cross-axis grid that scores 35 distinct DMAT×DM disclosure-mode combinations.

2. **Pitfall — DMAT1 zero-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected annual-rail-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses DMAT1 zero-aggregation pool (in-caption-#ad-no-rail-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-border-traffic may have a disclosure-mode event that crosses USD-EU-border without an annual-rail-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$55k-annual-rail-maint + 1-rollover-per-year on the disclosure-mode event. **Fix:** the disclosure-mode-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's cross-border-traffic-share + cohort's aggregation-tolerance — DMAT1 score only if cohort-cross-border-share <10% AND cohort-DMAT-tolerance = high; DMAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.

3. **Pitfall — DMAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-JP-cohort fail quarterly-rail-rollup-requirement.** Japan APPI 2022 + Japan APPI Disclosure 2026 + Japan APPI Disclosure Aggregation 2026 + Japan APPI Mode Rollup 2026 requires quarterly-rail-rollup for cross-border-disclosure-modes >¥100,000/yr per creator; DMAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (annual-rail-rollup only) is insufficient for high-JP-cohorts, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to DMAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation is suddenly required to file quarterly-rail-rollup with EU-UCPD-attestation. **Fix:** the disclosure-mode-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's JP-cohort-traffic + aggregation-tenor — DMAT3 score only if cohort-JP-share >5% AND merchant-is-EU-UCPD-registered; DMAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-EU-UCPD-registered merchants.

4. **Pitfall — DMAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-annual-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-rail-rollup-pool + UK-CMA-quarterly-rail-pool + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $80k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the disclosure-mode-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — DMAT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-UK-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to DMAT1 zero-aggregation or DMAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.

5. **Pitfall — DMAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a high-multi-state-cohort fail cross-state-aggregation-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to DMAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation is REQUIRED to track cross-state-disclosure-mode-rollup for every state-disclosure-mode event; non-compliance triggers cross-state-aggregation-enforcement and additional-state-penalties. **Fix:** the disclosure-mode-aggregation classifier must verify the merchant's cross-state-aggregation-tracking-status — DMAT4 score only if merchant-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active; DMAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active merchants.
