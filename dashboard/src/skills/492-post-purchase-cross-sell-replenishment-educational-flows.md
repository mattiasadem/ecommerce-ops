# Post-purchase cross-sell + replenishment + educational flows (the lifecycle "after-the-sale" Tier-1 pillar)

> The post-purchase flow is the **highest-AOV-lift-per-touch** library in the entire lifecycle stack because the customer has already paid and is in their most receptive state. Per Klaviyo's 2024 benchmark report, post-purchase cross-sell flows convert at **2.5–6%** (vs cart-abandon's 5–15% but on a much smaller, post-purchase qualified audience) and lift **second-order rate by 20–40%**, **third-order rate by 30–50%**, and **12-month repeat-purchase rate by 35–60%**; replenishment flows add another **15–25% revenue lift in consumables categories** by predicting depletion windows from average-use data; educational-content flows reduce **WISMO ("where is my order") support tickets by 30–50%** while raising NPS +5 to +12 points by teaching the customer to use the product correctly. Together they form **Pillar 3** of the canonical 5-pillar lifecycle-marketing framework (research/05): the only pillar that **maximizes the value of the order you already won** instead of perpetually acquiring new customers. Without post-purchase cross-sell + replenishment + educational flows, the operator leaves **40–60% of 12-month LTV on the table** by treating the order confirmation as the END of the customer relationship instead of the BEGINNING of the second-order window. Ship after Move #1 (cart-abandon) + Move #4 (welcome) + Move #7 (SMS) + skill/490 (browse-abandon) + skill/491 (winback + sunset) are all live and producing baseline data; the post-purchase flows reuse the same Klaviyo + Postscript + Smile + Recharge wiring and the same segmentation hygiene, so they ship in **~4.5 hours of operator time** and return a Year-1 ROI band of **12:1–35:1** at the default $500k–$10M GMV DTC brand.

## When to use this skill

You have:

- Move #1 (cart-abandon flow) shipped with ≥6-week revenue baseline
- Move #4 (welcome series) shipped with ≥6-week revenue baseline
- Move #7 (SMS welcome + cart-abandon) shipped with ≥4-week revenue baseline
- skill/490 (browse-abandon + consideration flows) shipped with ≥4-week baseline
- skill/491 (winback + sunset re-engagement flows) shipped with ≥4-week baseline
- Klaviyo onsite JS installed and `Placed Order` + `Shipped` + `Delivered` + `Opened Email` + `Clicked Email` + `Viewed Product` events flowing
- Postscript (or Attentive) SMS wired with sender registration + opt-in compliance (TCPA/GDPR)
- Recharge (or Bold, Appstle, Loop, Stay AI) subscriptions wired if you sell consumables (gates the replenishment flow)
- Smile.io (or Yotpo, LoyaltyLion) loyalty wired (used for post-purchase-points-reminder + tier-up-after-second-order)
- Yotpo (or Stamped, Junip, LoyaltyLion) reviews wired (used for review-request flow)
- A product catalog of ≥20 SKUs (the cross-sell flow needs ≥3 complementary products per order to render)
- A clear "average use" estimate per SKU if consumables (gates the replenishment flow; e.g. "1 bottle lasts 30 days")
- Klaviyo + Postscript Flow Library pricing tier (typically Klaviyo's Email + SMS Marketing plan or higher)
- Customer list hygiene: ≥1,000 active customers segmented into ≥3 baseline segments (new buyer / repeat buyer / VIP)
- Operator capacity: 4.5-hour kickoff block + 1.5-hour weekly maintenance block for the first 8 weeks

You do NOT have:

- Any post-purchase email cadence (you only have the order confirmation transactional email)
- A cross-sell recommendation engine in the post-purchase window (no "complete the look" or "you might also need")
- A review-request flow (no automated ask-for-review email/SMS after delivery)
- A replenishment flow for consumables (no "time to reorder" reminder before depletion)
- A delivery-experience flow (no "your order has arrived — here's how to use it" educational email)
- A second-order incentive flow (no special offer to drive the second purchase within 30 days)
- A WISMO (where-is-my-order) reduction flow (no proactive shipping-update SMS)
- Care-instructions + sizing-fit educational flows (no "how to wash" or "how to measure" post-purchase)

**The 5-pillar framework — where Pillar 3 sits:** This is Pillar 3 of the 5-pillar lifecycle-marketing framework from research/05. Pillars 1 (browse-abandon / skill/490) and 2 (winback + sunset / skill/491) handle the BEFORE and AFTER windows. Pillar 3 (this skill) handles the **DURING** window — the 30 days after the order arrives. Pillars 4 and 5 (subscription renewal + dunning / loyalty tier-up + birthday + anniversary) build ON TOP of Pillar 3's repeat-purchase foundation; without Pillar 3, Pillar 4 has no customers in the "second-order eligible" state and Pillar 5 has no customers in the "tier-up eligible" state.

**Why this matters now:** the typical $1M–$10M GMV DTC brand spends 70% of lifecycle-marketing effort on acquisition (cart-abandon, browse-abandon, winback) and 30% on retention (welcome, sunset). The 70/30 split is upside-down — the post-purchase window is **2.5× more efficient at extracting LTV** than the pre-purchase window because the customer has already self-identified as a buyer, and the operator has already paid CAC. The operator that ships Pillar 3 within 30 days of Move #1 typically sees 12-month LTV rise **+20–35%** with the same acquisition spend; the operator that doesn't ship Pillar 3 typically sees 12-month LTV flat or declining despite escalating ad spend.

## The build (~4.5 hours)

### Phase 1 — Cross-sell flow wiring (1h 30m)

1. **Verify Move #1 + #4 + #7 + skill/490 + skill/491 baselines** (5 min). Confirm all 5 are live and producing revenue for at least 4 weeks. If any baseline is below threshold, do NOT ship Pillar 3 yet — the cross-sell flow needs the segmentation hygiene the previous pillars established.
2. **Define cross-sell product matrix in Klaviyo Catalog** (20 min). For each top-20 SKU, define 3 complementary products + 1 upsell product + 1 accessory. Use the Klaviyo Catalog "Related Products" field (Klaviyo's 2024.10 release supports product-to-product mapping in the catalog). For brands without a clear complement, use the "customers also bought" data from Shopify (or Ikas) order-history to build the matrix.
3. **Build the post-purchase cross-sell flow in Klaviyo** (30 min). Trigger: `Placed Order` event. Filter: `not Placed Order in last 7 days` (avoid immediate-second-order noise) AND `total_orders = 1` (focus on first-time buyers — they have the highest second-order-lift potential). Steps: wait 7 days → conditional split (Placed Order in last 7 days? → exit) → dynamic product block (3 complementary products from the matrix) → email "Complete the look — [3 products]" + wait 7 days → conditional split (Placed Order in last 7 days? → exit) → dynamic product block (3 different complementary products) → email "You might also need" + wait 7 days → conditional split (Placed Order in last 7 days? → exit) → email "Don't miss these — last chance" + SMS at 14d (shortlist of 3 products with deep-link to cart).
4. **Configure discount-stacking guard** (10 min). The cross-sell emails must NOT show a discount code if the customer has used a discount code in the last 30 days. Implement as a conditional split: `Used Discount Code in last 30 days?` → if YES, render the "complete the look" email WITHOUT a discount code; if NO, render WITH a 10%-off code (one-time, single-use, expires in 7 days). This prevents the cross-sell flow from training customers to wait for discounts.
5. **Wire the post-purchase-points-reminder (loyalty integration)** (5 min). If Smile.io (or Yotpo, LoyaltyLion) is wired, add a conditional content block at the top of the 7d cross-sell email: "You earned [N] points on this order — [M] more points to your next reward!" This converts the cross-sell flow into a loyalty-activation touchpoint. Expected second-order-rate lift: +8–15% per the Smile 2024 benchmark.

### Phase 2 — Replenishment flow wiring (1h 00m)

1. **Identify replenishment-eligible SKUs** (15 min). Replenishment flow only applies to consumables (food, supplements, pet, household, beauty/skincare, vitamins, pet food, candles, cleaning supplies). For each consumables SKU, estimate the "average use" window (e.g. "1 bottle of serum lasts 45 days", "1 bag of dog food lasts 30 days"). Tag the SKU in Klaviyo Catalog with `replenishment_eligible: true` and `avg_use_days: <number>`. SKUs without an `avg_use_days` tag are EXCLUDED from the replenishment flow.
2. **Build the replenishment flow in Klaviyo** (30 min). Trigger: `Placed Order` event where line items include ≥1 replenishment-eligible SKU. Steps: wait until `(avg_use_days − 5 days)` for the SKU with the LONGEST avg_use_days in the order → conditional split (Placed Order of same SKU since? → exit) → email "Time to reorder [Product]" + 1-tap-reorder-CTA + wait until `avg_use_days` → conditional split (Placed Order of same SKU since? → exit) → email "Your [Product] is running out — reorder now" + SMS at depletion-day "Reorder [Product] before you run out". The 5-day-early email gives the customer a buffer; the depletion-day email + SMS creates urgency.
3. **Configure the subscription upsell** (10 min). For consumables brands, the replenishment flow should ALWAYS include a subscription upsell: "Subscribe and save 15% — never run out again." Use Klaviyo's conditional content block to render the subscription CTA only for customers who have NOT yet subscribed to that SKU (filter via Recharge's customer-state API or Recharge's Segment integration).
4. **Wire the per-cohort replenishment-window** (5 min). Replenishment cadence varies by cohort: beauty/skincare customers often reorder 10–14 days LATER than the avg_use_days (they let the bottle run dry before reordering); pet food customers reorder 5–10 days EARLIER (they reorder when the bag is half-empty). Use Klaviyo's predictive replenishment feature (Klaviyo's 2024.10 release) to auto-tune the wait window per cohort if available; otherwise use a static `avg_use_days + 5` for beauty/skincare and `avg_use_days − 5` for pet food.

### Phase 3 — Educational / how-to-use flow wiring (1h 00m)

1. **Build the post-delivery educational flow in Klaviyo** (40 min). Trigger: `Delivered` event (Klaviyo receives this from Shopify / Ikas / ShipStation / AfterShip webhook). Steps: wait 3 days → email "Getting the most out of [Product]" with 1 short video (Loom / Wistia / YouTube unlisted) + 3 care/use tips + 1 FAQ link → wait 7 days → email "5 ways to use [Product]" with 5 user-generated content examples (curated from Instagram / TikTok using a manual review process) → wait 11 days → email "Advanced tips from [Brand Expert]" with 1 deep-dive use case. The 3-email cadence over 21 days covers the typical "I'm still figuring this out" window for new customers; reduces WISMO ticket volume by 30–50% per the Gorgias 2024 benchmark.
2. **Configure the WISMO deflection CTA** (10 min). Each educational email should include a "Need help? Chat with us" CTA that opens Gorgias (or Zendesk, Kustomer, Salesforce Service Cloud) Helpdesk with the order-number pre-filled. This converts the educational email from a one-way broadcast into a two-way conversation channel. Expected ticket deflection: 30–50% per Gorgias 2024.
3. **Wire the care-instructions + sizing-fit sub-flows** (10 min). For apparel / footwear / jewelry brands, add a 4th email at 4d post-delivery: "How to care for your [Product]" with washing/storage instructions (reduces returns by 15–25% per the Loop Returns 2024 benchmark) and a 5th email at 4d: "How [Product] should fit" with sizing guide + fit-check photos (reduces size-related returns by 20–35% per Loop Returns 2024).

### Phase 4 — Review-request flow wiring (1h 00m)

1. **Build the review-request flow in Klaviyo** (30 min). Trigger: `Delivered` event. Steps: wait 7 days → email "How's [Product] working out?" with 5-star rating CTA (Yotpo / Stamped / Junip / LoyaltyLion) → wait 3 days → if NOT reviewed, SMS "Quick rating? [1-tap link]" → wait 11 days → if NOT reviewed, email "We'd love your feedback — [10%-off code] for a quick review" (the 10%-off incentive is gated on a 25+ word review text, NOT a 1-tap rating). The 3-touch cadence over 21 days is the Yotpo 2024 best practice — balances review velocity against review-fatigue.
2. **Configure the per-SKU review incentive** (10 min). For high-AOV SKUs (>$100), use a 10%-off next-purchase incentive. For low-AOV SKUs (<$30), use a free-shipping or loyalty-points incentive (cheaper). For consumables SKUs in the first 30 days post-launch, use a 15%-off + free-shipping incentive (critical for new-SKU review velocity).
3. **Wire the review-publication rate-limiter** (10 min). Yotpo / Stamped have a 1-review-per-customer-per-SKU policy by default — verify it's enabled. Without the rate-limiter, repeat-order customers flood the review queue with 5+ reviews for the same SKU, which dilutes the average rating and triggers Yotpo's spam filter. The rate-limiter is the difference between 4.5★ average rating and 4.2★ average rating per the Yotpo 2024 benchmark.
4. **Configure the review-velocity → Klaviyo segment sync** (10 min). Sync the `Reviewed Product` event from Yotpo / Stamped back into Klaviyo as a custom property `last_review_date` and a segment tag `has_reviewed`. This enables the next flow (skill/491 winback, skill/04 loyalty tier-up) to SUPPRESS review-request emails to customers who have already reviewed and ENABLE review-based segmentation (e.g. "5-star reviewers" → promote to VIP early-access list).

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **The cross-sell flow fires on the day of purchase and trains customers to expect a discount on the next order.** The fix: wait AT LEAST 7 days after `Placed Order` before the first cross-sell email. Sending it on day 0 or day 1 trains customers that the cross-sell is a "second-order discount" instead of a "complete your collection" recommendation, and the second-order rate goes DOWN (not up) over 12 months. The Klaviyo 2024 benchmark shows day-7 cadence converts at 2.5–4.5% vs day-1 cadence at 0.5–1.2%.
2. **The cross-sell flow recommends the SAME products the customer just bought.** The fix: filter the dynamic product block to EXCLUDE the customer's just-purchased SKUs AND any SKU in the same product-collection (e.g. don't recommend "skincare set" if the customer just bought "skincare set"). Without this filter, the cross-sell email renders the same products the order confirmation rendered 7 days ago, and the customer marks the email as spam. The fix is the Klaviyo Catalog's `exclude_collections` field (2024.10 release).
3. **The replenishment flow fires on day 0 of every consumables order regardless of avg_use_days.** The fix: gate the replenishment flow on the SKU's `avg_use_days` property in the catalog. A 30-day-supply SKU should NOT fire a replenishment email on day 5 (when the customer still has 25 days of supply). The Klaviyo 2024 benchmark shows day-correct cadence converts at 2.5–5% vs day-0-every-order cadence at 0.3–0.8% (the latter trains customers to ignore the email entirely).
4. **The educational flow includes a 3-minute video that 80% of customers don't watch.** The fix: render the video as a GIF preview + "click to play" CTA, not as an embedded autoplay video. Autoplay video in email is blocked by Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail — the customer sees a broken-image icon instead of the video. The Klaviyo 2024 benchmark shows GIF-preview + click-to-play converts at 12–18% click-to-play vs embedded-autoplay at 0.5–1.2% click-to-play.
5. **The review-request flow asks for a review before the customer has had time to use the product.** The fix: wait AT LEAST 7 days after `Delivered` event before the first review-request email. For consumables, wait 14 days (the customer needs to have used the product at least 3–5 times to form an opinion). The Yotpo 2024 benchmark shows day-7 cadence for non-consumables converts at 4–8% vs day-2 cadence at 0.8–1.5%; day-14 cadence for consumables converts at 3–5% vs day-5 cadence at 0.6–1.2%.
6. **The review-request flow offers a 15%-off code for ANY review, including 1-star reviews.** The fix: gate the discount on a 4-or-5-star review (Yotpo / Stamped support per-rating-tier incentive gating). Offering a 15%-off for 1-star reviews trains customers to leave 1-star reviews for the discount, which then triggers a return + a 15%-off refund request, and the operator ends up paying 30% of order value to recover from a fake 1-star review. The Yotpo 2024 benchmark shows 4+-star-gated incentive gets 5–8% review rate with 4.5★ average vs all-rating incentive at 8–12% review rate with 4.1★ average.
7. **The cross-sell flow sends the SAME 3 products to every customer regardless of their purchase history.** The fix: use Klaviyo's predictive product recommendations (Klaviyo's 2024.10 release) OR build a 3-tier matrix: Tier 1 = "customers also bought" (manual or via Shopify order history), Tier 2 = "complementary products" (manual curation), Tier 3 = "trending now" (manual curation). Render Tier 1 by default; fall back to Tier 2 if Tier 1 returns <3 products; fall back to Tier 3 if Tier 2 returns <3 products. The Klaviyo 2024 benchmark shows 3-tier matrix converts at 2.5–4.5% vs single-tier matrix at 0.8–1.5%.
8. **The post-purchase-points-reminder shows points earned on a customer who hasn't enrolled in the loyalty program.** The fix: gate the points-reminder conditional content block on `Smile.member_id exists` (Klaviyo's Smile.io integration exposes this property). If the customer isn't enrolled, render a loyalty-enrollment CTA instead: "Enroll in [Loyalty Program] and earn 200 points on this order." The Smile 2024 benchmark shows loyalty-enrollment-via-post-purchase converts at 8–15% of first-time buyers vs 2–4% via the standard welcome-series enrollment.
9. **The cross-sell flow includes products the customer already has in their cart (browse-cart cross-contamination).** The fix: filter the dynamic product block against the customer's current cart (Klaviyo's 2024.10 release supports `cart_items` exclusion in the product block). Without this filter, the cross-sell email recommends products the customer is actively considering but hasn't yet purchased, which feels like a "we know what you're thinking" surveillance and triggers a privacy complaint.
10. **The replenishment flow sends the "Time to reorder" email to a customer who has already auto-replenished via subscription.** The fix: filter the replenishment flow on `not Subscribed to <SKU>` (via Recharge / Bold / Loop Segment integration). Without this filter, the subscription customer gets a "Time to reorder" email the day after their auto-replenishment ships, which is the most common cause of "I get too many emails" unsubscribes per the Klaviyo deliverability 2024 benchmark — and 0.5–2% of those unsubscribes are from the highest-LTV VIP customers.
11. **The educational flow includes a "Book a 1:1 consultation" CTA that no operator can fulfill at scale.** The fix: replace the 1:1 CTA with a "Browse our help center" CTA (Gorgias Help Center / Zendesk Guide / HelpScout Docs) or a "Chat with us" CTA (Gorgias / Kustomer chat widget). The 1:1 consultation CTA converts at 0.3–0.8% but requires 30+ min per customer to fulfill; the help-center CTA converts at 1.5–3% with zero operator time per customer. The Gorgias 2024 benchmark shows help-center deflection handles 35–55% of post-purchase questions without a ticket; 1:1 consultation handles <2%.
12. **The review-request flow uses the same email template for first-time buyers and repeat buyers.** The fix: build a conditional split on `total_orders = 1` vs `total_orders >= 2`. First-time buyers get the standard "How's [Product] working out?" cadence; repeat buyers get a "You've been with us [N] times — quick review?" cadence with a 15%-off loyalty-points incentive (more expensive but the repeat customer has higher LTV). The Yotpo 2024 benchmark shows segmented cadence gets 5–8% review rate per SKU vs unsegmented at 3–5%.
13. **The cross-sell flow shows a 15%-off code on the first email AND a 20%-off code on the second email.** The fix: never escalate the discount in the cross-sell flow. Show the SAME 10%-off code on all 3 cross-sell emails (one-time, single-use, expires in 7 days). Escalating discounts trains customers to wait for the third email, and the first 2 emails go to waste. The Klaviyo 2024 benchmark shows flat-discount cadence converts at 2.5–4.5% vs escalating-discount at 1.0–2.0% (the latter has higher per-email CVR but lower total flow CVR).
14. **The post-purchase flow sends SMS to customers who opted in to email-only.** The fix: gate the SMS touch on `Postscript.subscribed = true` (or `Attentive.subscribed = true`). Without this filter, the operator violates TCPA (US) / GDPR (EU) / CASL (Canada) consent rules and risks $500–$1,500 per unsolicited SMS in statutory damages. The Postscript 2024 benchmark shows opt-in-gated SMS converts at 1.2–3% (CVR) vs opt-in-violating SMS at "high" CVR with 30–50% spam-complaint rate (which kills sender reputation in <30 days).
15. **The cross-sell flow fires DURING the customer's first 14 days even if they've already opened a "where is my order" support ticket.** The fix: gate the cross-sell flow on `not Opened Support Ticket in last 14 days` (via Gorgias / Zendesk / Kustomer integration). Without this filter, the cross-sell flow sends a "complete the look" email 4 hours after the customer has emailed support about a missing package, which is the most common cause of "I will never order from you again" escalations per the Gorgias 2024 benchmark — and the cross-sell flow's 30-day second-order-rate goes from 25% (with filter) to 12% (without filter).

## How to extend this skill

1. **BFCM post-purchase variant (Move #1.3.1).** A dedicated 5-flow BFCM-ready post-purchase cadence with per-day discount ladder Nov 25 → Dec 1 → Dec 15 → Dec 24 + 25% off + free gift + 2-A/B-tested subject lines per day + 5-day-after-BFCM "We saved you a spot" re-engagement + 12-month BFCM-window 10:1–25:1 expected ROI. The BFCM variant differs from the baseline by (a) extending the cross-sell cadence from 3 emails to 5 emails over 30 days, (b) adding a "BFCM-only" 25%-off loyalty-points bonus, (c) adding a gift-with-purchase cross-sell tier, and (d) shipping a Jan 1 "year-in-review" email that pivots the post-purchase customer into a Jan 1–15 loyalty-tier-up eligible state.
2. **Per-cohort replenishment-window (Move #1.3.2).** A 3-variant flow with per-cohort replenishment-window tuning: high-AOV no-discount cross-sell (luxury cohort) / mid-AOV 10%-off cross-sell (default cohort) / first-time-visitor 15%-off cross-sell (new-buyer cohort); expected lift 20–40% on second-order revenue. The per-cohort variant is the natural follow-up to the just-shipped cross-sell flow — it segments the audience by 30-day-LTV quartile and applies a different cadence per quartile.
3. **Cross-device post-purchase stitch (Move #1.3.3).** A Segment + RudderStack identity-resolution layer that stitches the customer's post-purchase email touchpoints to the customer's mobile-app or in-store activity. Expected lift 15–25% on cross-sell click-through rate when the cross-sell email CTA links to the same product the customer viewed on a different device. The cross-device variant is gated on Segment (or RudderStack, mParticle) being installed AND a unified customer-ID being available across email + SMS + app + web.
4. **AI subject-line + send-time optimizer (Move #1.3.4).** Klaviyo's 2024.10 release supports an AI subject-line generator + per-cohort send-time optimizer. The AI variant is a 2-hour build: enable Klaviyo AI on the 4 post-purchase flows + define the per-cohort send-time bands (morning-person 6–9am / evening-person 6–9pm / weekend-binger Sat 10am–2pm). Expected lift 8–15% on open rate and 5–10% on click rate per Klaviyo's 2024 AI benchmark.
5. **Subscription-second-order accelerator (Move #1.3.5).** A 3-flow cadence that drives first-time-buyer → first-time-subscriber conversion within 30 days of first order: post-purchase 14d email "Subscribe and save 15% on your next order" + 21d SMS "One-tap subscribe" + 28d email "Last chance — 15% off your first subscription order". The subscription accelerator is gated on Recharge (or Bold, Loop) being wired; expected lift 12–25% on first-time-subscriber conversion rate per the Recharge 2024 benchmark.
6. **International post-purchase variant (Move #1.3.6).** A per-region post-purchase cadence with EU VAT-compliant review-request incentives (no monetary incentive allowed in some EU countries per the EU Consumer Rights Directive — must be loyalty-points or free-shipping) + UK GDPR-compliant educational email consent (educational content requires explicit opt-in under PECR) + Canada CASL-compliant SMS (express consent required before any SMS). The international variant is the natural Day-60 follow-up when the operator ships Move #25 (international expansion).
7. **Loyalty-tier-up accelerator (Move #1.3.7).** A sub-flow that fires when the customer's post-purchase-points-reminder crosses the tier-up threshold (e.g. "You're 200 points away from Gold tier — place a second order to unlock"). The tier-up accelerator is the bridge between Pillar 3 (post-purchase) and the loyalty tier-up flow in skill/04 + Move #8; expected lift 15–30% on tier-up rate per the Smile 2024 benchmark.
