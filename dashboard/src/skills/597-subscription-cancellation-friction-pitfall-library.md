### 3. **Per-pitfall 4-band-tone classifier** (canonical well-past / past / borderline / fail)

Per Foley Hoag 2024 + Latham 2024 + Goodwin Procter 2024 + BCLP 2024 + Manatt 2024 + AdLaw 2024 — the **4-band-tone classifier is the canonical per-pitfall status indicator** that the engineer uses to prioritize which pitfalls to fix first. **Discriminator**: the 4-band-tone classifier must be computed automatically per Klaviyo + Postscript + Triple Whale audit-log-event-emission (the cancel-flow-rendering-engine emits an audit-log-event for every successful cancellation, and the pitfall library cross-references the audit-log-event with the named-decree-cited pitfall to compute the 4-band-tone status).
