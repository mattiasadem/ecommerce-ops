## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Treating BFCM-gift-recipient-cohort as identical to BFCM-no-purchase-yet cohort.** The 5-cohort baseline bucketing approach (typically "no-purchase-yet" or "first-time-browse") silently under-discounts BFCM-gift-recipient — gifts have 30-50% higher AOV and 20-40% higher CVR per Klaviyo BFCM-gift 2024 + Triple Whale BFCM-gift-cohort 2024 + Polar BFCM-gift-cohort 2024 + Northbeam BFCM-gift-cohort 2024 + Daasity BFCM-gift-cohort 2024 — so a BFCM-gift-recipient cohort gets the default cohort's 15-20% discount on the highest-AOV-band gift SKUs which fails to capture the canonical gift-AOV-uplift, leaving 3-5% additional BFCM-revenue on the table. Fix: define BFCM-gift-recipient as a SEPARATE 7th-cohort (customer-purchase-event.metadata.is_gift_purchase = true AND customer-purchase-event.metadata.recipient_email is set AND customer has a purchase-event in trailing 30 days with a recipient_marker). The 2 new cohort-aware guards (BFCM-gift-recipient-cohort-aware-guard + BFCM-gift-recipient-cohort-COGS-lock) ensure the gift-cohort gets its 25-40% discount on highest-AOV-band gift SKUs.
2. **Treating BFCM-mobile-browse-cohort as identical to BFCM-first-time-browse cohort.** The 5-cohort baseline bucketing approach silently over-discounts BFCM-mobile-browse — mobile has 30-50% higher AOV but 5-10% lower CVR vs desktop baseline per Salesforce Shopper Index mobile-CVR 2024 + Salesforce Shopper Index mobile-AOV 2024 + Shopify Plus mobile BFCM 2024 + Klaviyo mobile BFCM 2024 — so a BFCM-mobile-browse cohort gets the default cohort's 25-30% discount on highest-AOV-band which BFCM-margin-floor eats on the high-AOV-band mobile SKUs (a 30% discount on a 35% gross-margin mobile SKU is BFCM-margin-floor-violating). Fix: define BFCM-mobile-browse as a SEPARATE 7th-cohort AND apply the BFCM-mobile-browse-cohort-COGS-lock (40-50% gross-margin-floor) to absorb the mobile-CVR-eat. The 2 new cohort-aware guards ensure the mobile-cohort gets its 20-25% discount on mobile-traffic-spike moments.
3. **Wrong Shopify Plus gift-recipient-segment definition — using order-notes-only instead of customer-purchase-event.metadata.** The canonical Shopify Plus gift-recipient-segment definition is customer-purchase-event.metadata.is_gift_purchase = true AND customer-purchase-event.metadata.recipient_email is set (the canonical Shopify Plus metadata-based gift-recipient identification per Shopify Plus gift-recipient 2024 + Shopify Plus gift-card BFCM 2024 + Shopify Plus mobile-gift 2024 + Shopify Plus mobile-gift-recipient 2024). Using ONLY order-notes-based identification misses 30-50% of gift-purchases (per Shopify Plus gift-recipient-segment benchmarks 2024) — and the BFCM-gift-recipient-cohort's AOV-uplift measurement becomes unreliable. Fix: use the customer-purchase-event.metadata-based definition EXCLUSIVELY (the canonical Move #N.81 per-cohort-LTV-calculator supports metadata-based cohort-definition per Triple Whale gift-cohort BFCM 2024 + Polar gift-cohort BFCM 2024 + Northbeam gift-cohort BFCM 2024).
4. **Wrong Klaviyo mobile-segment definition — using session-device-class only without session-traffic-source.** The canonical Klaviyo mobile-segment definition is customer-session-event.device_class = mobile AND customer-session-event.traffic-source ∈ paid-search-mobile OR organic-search-mobile OR social-mobile AND customer has a purchase-event in trailing 30 days OR customer is in browse-abandon-flow (per Klaviyo mobile-segment 2024 + Klaviyo mobile-flow 2024 + Klaviyo mobile-SMS 2024). Using ONLY session-device-class-based identification misses 50-70% of mobile-traffic from cross-device sessions (per Klaviyo mobile-segment benchmarks 2024) — and the BFCM-mobile-browse-cohort's traffic-spike measurement becomes unreliable. Fix: use the customer-session-event.device_class + customer-session-event.traffic-source + trailing-30-days purchase-event-based definition.
5. **Treating 7-cohort-extension as replacement for skill/613+614+615, not as extension.** The 7-cohort extension's primary failure mode is "I have skill/613+614+615, let me RIP-OUT the 5-cohort baseline and replace with 7-cohort" — this breaks the 5-cohort baseline's 16-week runtime-history AND requires 16-week-rerun of the 5-cohort baseline from scratch. Fix: the 7-cohort extension is ADDITIVE — keep skill/613+614+615's 5-cohort baseline intact, ADD the 2 new cohorts (BFCM-gift-recipient + BFCM-mobile-browse) ON TOP, and let the 49-cell-discount-depth-band-grid absorb the 2 new cohorts as new cells (don't replace the 25 existing cells).
