# Per-cohort incident-postmortem auto-generator

> Move #6.17 (per-cohort breach-cost decomposition, shipped 2026-10-01 as skill/448) computes the per-platform × per-cohort × per-recipe breach-cost for the 7 cohort × 5 platform × 5 recipe grid. Move #6.18 (per-influencer breach-cost attribution, shipped 2026-10-01 as skill/449) computes the per-platform × per-influencer × per-recipe breach-cost for the 6 influencer category × 5 platform × 5 recipe grid. Move #6.19 (cascade-effect-on-other-cohorts-and-influencers prediction engine, shipped 2026-10-02 as skill/450) computes the cascade-net-benefit for each Move #6.16 pending-cascade-fire event. **But ALL THREE layers emit only NUMERIC and DECISION-level rows to a dashboard** — the operator (and Move #6.15's attribution-accuracy-tracker + Move #6.17's cohort-protection-priority-rank + Move #6.18's influencer-protection-priority-rank + Move #6.19's cascade-drag-calibration-accuracy auto-tuner) see "BFCM-2024-repeat-buyer cohort: per-cohort breach-cost = $1,200 on Meta, $800 on Google, $400 on TikTok, total $2,400" or "micro-influencer mid-campaign: per-influencer breach-cost = $800 on Meta, $400 on Google, total $1,200" or "Move #6.16 cascade FIRED: predicted_recovery $4,200, predicted_harm $1,800, cascade_net_benefit $2,375" — but they have NO NARRATIVE post-mortem that captures the operator-actionable WHY: WHICH recipe fired, WHICH LTV-multiplier amplified the harm, WHICH cohort was hit hardest, WHICH recovery actions would have changed the outcome, and WHICH future breach-prevention is warranted. **Move #6.20 closes this gap** — on every per-cohort breach-cost computation, it auto-generates a per-cohort incident-postmortem template (delivered to Notion tagged `attribution-move-6.20-cohort-postmortem`); on every per-influencer breach-cost computation, it auto-generates a per-influencer incident-postmortem template (delivered to Notion tagged `attribution-move-6.20-influencer-postmortem`); and on every Move #6.19 cascade-net-benefit computation, it auto-generates a per-cascade incident-postmortem template (delivered to Notion tagged `attribution-move-6.20-cascade-postmortem`). Each post-mortem contains: (a) `cohort_id` OR `influencer_id` OR `cascade_id` + `breach_cost` OR `cascade_net_benefit`, (b) `LTV_multiplier` + `breach_cost_ratio` OR `cascade_harm_to_non_cascade`, (c) `recipe_id` + `protection_priority_rank` OR `cascade_decision` (PROCEED/CANCEL), (d) `recommended_actions_for_cohort_or_influencer_or_cascade_recovery` (the operator-friendly list of WHAT they should change in the next Move #6.13 R1+H1 OR Move #6.16 R4 cascade OR Move #6.19 cascade-fire-threshold to reduce breach-cost or harm-to-non-cascade on the next cascade-event). **The operator sees "BFCM-2024-repeat-buyer cohort breach-cost = $2,400 → recommended: 1) rotate Meta CAPI API key by Tue 14:00 UTC, 2) re-bid BFCM-repeat-buyer micro-influencer cohort on Google at +5% bid-floor, 3) freeze BFCM-2024-one-time-buyer cohort from Meta CAPI cascade for 60 days pending fidelity-review"** instead of just "BFCM-2024-repeat-buyer cohort breach-cost = $2,400" (the numeric-only mode today). For the default $500k–$5M GMV brand running 30+ per-cohort breach-cost events/year × 30+ per-influencer breach-cost events/year × 30+ cascade-net-benefit events/year, Move #6.20's auto-generated post-mortems prevent ~$5k–$15k of operator-time-per-incident (4-hour manual post-mortem × 90 incidents/year × $30/h fully-loaded = ~$10.8k of standing-productivity-loss-per-year) AND prevent ~$20k–$80k of repeat-incident-cost-per-year (the same root-cause firing twice because the recommended-action chain wasn't retained). Year-1 ROI band **5:1–14:1**. Ship AFTER Move #6.17 + Move #6.18 + Move #6.19 are all live and producing per-cohort/per-influencer/per-cascade decision rows (Move #6.20 consumes all three layers' decision rows as its post-mortem-emit signal).

## When to use this skill

You have:

- **Move #6.17 per-cohort breach-cost decomposition live** (skill/448) producing per-platform × per-cohort × per-recipe breach-cost rows with `cohort_id` + `platform_id` + `recipe_id` + `breach_cost` + `LTV_multiplier` + `protection_priority_rank` + `breach_timestamp` at ≥30 cohort-breach-cost events/year (≥1 every ~12 days on average for a BFCM-peak brand, with bursts of 3–5/day during peak).
- **Move #6.18 per-influencer breach-cost attribution live** (skill/449) producing per-platform × per-influencer × per-recipe breach-cost rows with `influencer_id` + `influencer_category` + `platform_id` + `recipe_id` + `breach_cost` + `LTV_multiplier` + `FTC_disclosure_strictness` + `breach_timestamp` at ≥30 influencer-breach-cost events/year.
- **Move #6.19 cascade-effect-on-other-cohorts-and-influencers prediction engine live** (skill/450) producing per-cascade cascade-net-benefit rows with `cascade_id` + `breach_id` + `cascade_target_platforms` + `cascade_decision` (PROCEED/CANCEL) + `predicted_recovery` + `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_cohort` + `predicted_harm_to_non_cascade_influencer` + `cascade_net_benefit` + `cascade_execution_cost` + `cascade_fire_timestamp` at ≥30 cascade-net-benefit events/year.
- **Move #6.13 attribution auto-remediation runbook executor live** (skill/444) — Move #6.20 consumes Move #6.13's `recipe_executed` events to enrich the post-mortem with the actual recipe that fired (R1 / R2 / R3 / R4 / R5).
- **Move #6.12 real-time breach-detector live** — Move #6.20 consumes Move #6.12's `breach_detected` events to enrich the post-mortem with the breach root-cause.
- **Move #6.14 per-event revenue-impact attribution live** (skill/445) — Move #6.20 consumes Move #6.14's per-event breach-cost ledger to enrich the post-mortem with the per-event revenue-impact breakdown.
- **Move #6.15 attribution accuracy tracker + auto-tuning live** (skill/446) — Move #6.20 consumes Move #6.15's `move_6_15_accuracy_ledger` with `accuracy_layer="move_6_20_postmortem_outcome_accuracy"` tag to compute the post-mortem-outcome-accuracy metric (i.e. did the operator actually execute the recommended-action chain within the recommended window?).
- **Per-cohort-incident-postmortem-template-engine** (Jinja2 template library; 7 templates per Move #6.17 cohort + 6 templates per Move #6.18 influencer category + 1 template per Move #6.19 cascade + 1 cross-incident template for cascade-stacks-of-fires).
- **Per-cohort-postmortem-Notion-emitter** (writes the per-cohort post-mortem to the operator's Notion workspace tagged `attribution-move-6.20-cohort-postmortem`; creates a Notion sub-page under the parent `Move #6 Attribution Auto-Cascade Coherence` parent page; rate-limited to ≤1 post-mortem per cohort per recipe per breach-event per 24h).
- **Per-influencer-postmortem-Notion-emitter** (writes the per-influencer post-mortem to Notion tagged `attribution-move-6.20-influencer-postmortem`; rate-limited similarly).
- **Per-cascade-postmortem-Notion-emitter** (writes the per-cascade post-mortem to Notion tagged `attribution-move-6.20-cascade-postmortem`; rate-limited to ≤1 per cascade-event per 24h).
- **Recommended-action-generator** (reads the per-cohort OR per-influencer OR per-cascade `breach_cost_loss_summary` + `LTV_multiplier` + `recipe_id` + `protection_priority_rank` + `cascade_decision` and emits a structured recommended-action-chain with 3-5 specific operator-actionable items; default 3 items per cohort post-mortem, 3 items per influencer post-mortem, 5 items per cascade post-mortem).
- **Recommended-action-prioritizer** (ranks the recommended-action-chain by `(expected_breach_cost_reduction / action_execution_cost) × cohort_or_influencer_or_cascade_priority_weight`; default weights: BFCM-2024-repeat-buyer=3.0x, BFCM-2024-new-buyer=1.5x, BFCM-2024-one-time-buyer=0.5x, non-BFCM-repeat-buyer=2.0x, non-BFCM-new-buyer=1.0x, non-BFCM-one-time-buyer=0.3x, VIP-loyalty-tier=4.0x; macro-influencer=0.8x, mid-tier=1.0x, micro=1.5x, nano=1.2x, affiliate=1.0x, employee-advocate=2.0x).
- **Postmortem-outcome-tracker** (tracks `postmortem_recommended_action_executed = true|false` for each recommended-action item via Notion page-tick + cross-references Move #6.15's `move_6_15_accuracy_ledger` with `accuracy_layer="move_6_20_postmortem_outcome_accuracy"` tag; computes `postmortem_outcome_accuracy = sum(postmortem_recommended_action_executed) / sum(postmortem_recommended_action_emitted)` over a 90-day-rolling-window).
- **Cascade-cross-incident-postmortem-aggregator** (when 3+ Move #6.19 cascade-events fire within 24h targeting the same Move #6.12 breach-source, the aggregator combines them into a single cascade-stack post-mortem tagged `attribution-move-6.20-cascade-stack-postmortem` with cross-incident root-cause-pattern + cumulative-harm-to-non-cascade + cumulative-net-benefit + recommended-action-chain targeting the breach-source not the individual cascades).
- **Postmortem-Slack-thread** (default: `#move-6-postmortems` channel — posts every per-cohort OR per-influencer OR per-cascade post-mortem-Notion-page-URL with a 1-sentence summary + the top-1 recommended-action; rate-limited to ≤5 posts per 24h to prevent alert fatigue).
- **Per-cohort-postmortem-LTV-isolation-test** (enforces `sum(per_cohort_breach_cost_in_postmortem) === total_per_cohort_breach_cost_for_all_cohorts` within ±0.5%; FAIL → post-mortem-emit BLOCKED → Slack alert `postmortem_cohort_breach_cost_isolation_test_failed` + auto-revert to the prior computation).
- **Per-influencer-postmortem-breach-cost-isolation-test** (enforces `sum(per_influencer_breach_cost_in_postmortem) === total_per_influencer_breach_cost_for_all_influencers` within ±0.5%; FAIL → silent rejection + alert + auto-revert).
- **Per-cascade-postmortem-cascade-net-benefit-isolation-test** (enforces `sum(per_cascade_predicted_recovery − predicted_harm) === total_cascade_net_benefit_for_all_cascades` within ±0.5%; FAIL → silent rejection + alert + auto-revert).
- **Postmortem-recommended-action-emit-rate-limiter** (≤3 post-mortems per cohort OR influencer OR cascade per 24h; 4th+ post-mortem in 24h logs `postmortem_emit_rate_limited` and emits only the highest-priority recommended-action items).
- **Move #6.20 runbook drafted** at `playbooks/06.20-per-cohort-incident-postmortem-auto-generator.md` covering the 12-component best-in-class matrix + the 7-cohort-postmortem-template taxonomy + the 6-influencer-postmortem-template taxonomy + the 1-cascade-postmortem-template taxonomy + the 1-cascade-stack-postmortem-template taxonomy + the 5-phase ~16-hour build + the 15-pitfall list + the 6-deployment-scenario benchmark table + the postmortem-outcome-tracker procedure + the recommended-action-prioritizer procedure.
- **Operator has viewed `/move-6-postmortems` dashboard at least once** and confirmed the per-cohort OR per-influencer OR per-cascade post-mortem-Notion-pages render correctly with the recommended-action-chain.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Postmortem-emit-without-recommended-action-chain** — the simplest Move #6.20 bug; if you emit the per-cohort OR per-influencer OR per-cascade post-mortem to Notion with the breach-cost + LTV-multiplier + cascade-decision but WITHOUT a recommended-action-chain, the post-mortem becomes a passive observation log instead of an operator-actionable artifact. The operator opens the post-mortem, reads the breach-cost, but has no operator-actionable next-step → no operator-action chain fires → ~$20k–$80k of repeat-incident-cost-per-year (the same root-cause firing twice because the recommended-action chain wasn't retained). Fix: Move #6.20 MUST emit a recommended-action-chain with 3-5 specific operator-actionable items in EVERY post-mortem; the chain is load-bearing, don't skip it.

2. **Using-static-recommended-action-templates-instead-of-LTV-multiplier-weighted-action-prioritization** — if the recommended-action-prioritizer uses a static recommended-action-priority-rank (e.g. "rotate API key > move member > soft action") across all cohorts/influencers/cascades, Move #6.20 mis-prioritizes actions for cohorts whose LTV-multiplier demands different prioritization (e.g. VIP-loyalty-tier-cohort with 4.0x LTV-multiplier demands "freeze cohort from cascade for 90 days" as the TOP action, but Move #6.20 still treats it as a low-priority and emits "rotate API key" as TOP). Fix: recommended-action-prioritizer (Phase 3) is a HARD requirement; `(expected_breach_cost_reduction / action_execution_cost) × cohort_or_influencer_or_cascade_priority_weight` formula with the 7 cohort + 6 influencer + 1 cascade priority-weights; the weight is load-bearing.

3. **Postmortem-emit-on-recipe-id-only-not-instead-of-cohort_id-or-influencer_id-or-cascade_id-composed** — if Move #6.20 emits post-mortem on `recipe_id` only (i.e. "R1 fired" → post-mortem), the post-mortem is recipe-only without the cohort/influencer/cascade context (BFCM-2024-repeat-buyer cohort breach-cost vs MICRO-INFLUENCER mid-campaign breach-cost vs Move #6.16 cascade-net-benefit all look the same when given the same recipe). Fix: Move #6.20 MUST emit post-mortem with `cohort_id` OR `influencer_id` OR `cascade_id` as the PRIMARY KEY; recipe_id is a SECONDARY context field, not the primary. The cohort/influencer/cascade-key is load-bearing.

4. **No-postmortem-breach-cost-isolation-test** — without the `sum(per_cohort_breach_cost_in_postmortem) === total_per_cohort_breach_cost_for_all_cohorts` ±0.5% verification, a decomposition-arithmetic-bug silently propagates (e.g. floating-point precision loss in pandas groupby OR a `× LTV_multiplier` typo that breaks the cohort-sum). Fix: per-cohort-postmortem-breach-cost-isolation-test is a HARD requirement; every Move #6.20 computation must pass before the post-mortem-emit.

5. **Postmortem-emit-once-per-breach-instead-of-streaming-per-cohort-or-influencer-or-cascade-event** — if Move #6.20 waits for the entire Move #6.12 breach-window to close (5–15 min after Move #6.13 R1+H1 fires) before emitting post-mortem, the post-mortem arrives 5–15 min LATE — by then the operator has already moved to the next breach-event and the recommended-action chain is stale. Fix: Move #6.20 should stream post-mortem incrementally as Move #6.17 OR Move #6.18 OR Move #6.19 emit per-cohort/per-influencer/per-cascade rows; the first post-mortem-row should land within 90s of Move #6.12's breach-detection and the Notion page should be live within 90s.

6. **Not-feeding-Move-#6.20's-postmortem-outcome-back-into-Move-#6.17-or-Move-#6.18-or-Move-#6.19** — if Move #6.20 emits postmortem-outcome-accuracy to a Move-#6.20-only dashboard without wiring it to Move #6.17's per-cohort-protection-priority-rank auto-tuner OR Move #6.18's per-influencer-protection-priority-rank auto-tuner OR Move #6.19's cascade-drag-calibration auto-tuner, Move #6.17/6.18/6.19 keep emitting the same breach-cost numbers that Move #6.20's recommended-action chain proved were wrong (because the postmortem-outcome-accuracy is never computed). Fix: Move #6.20's postmortem-outcome-accuracy MUST be wired to Move #6.15's `move_6_15_accuracy_ledger` with `accuracy_layer="move_6_20_postmortem_outcome_accuracy"` tag; Move #6.15 auto-tunes Move #6.17/6.18/6.19 based on Move #6.20's postmortem-outcome-accuracy.

7. **No-postmortem-emit-rate-limiter** — without a per-cohort OR per-influencer OR per-cascade postmortem-emit rate-limiter (default: ≤3 post-mortems per cohort OR influencer OR cascade per 24h), a single Move #6.12 breach-detection that triggers 5 Move #6.17 per-cohort rows AND 5 Move #6.18 per-influencer rows AND 5 Move #6.19 cascade rows can spam the post-mortem 15 times × 30 cascades/day = 450 post-mortems per breach (operator alert fatigue). Fix: postmortem-emit-rate-limiter is a HARD requirement; 4th+ post-mortem in 24h logs `postmortem_emit_rate_limited` and emits only the highest-priority recommended-action items.

8. **Postmortem-emit-on-breach-cost-in-the-cascade-window-only-instead-of-breach-cost-in-the-pre-cascade-baseline** — if Move #6.20 computes `per_cohort_breach_cost_in_postmortem` from only the breach-window's breach-cost, the breach-cost is biased by the breach itself (low-revenue breach-window artificially reduces breach-cost magnitude). Fix: `per_cohort_breach_cost_in_postmortem` MUST come from the pre-cascade-baseline (breach-cost in the [breach_timestamp − 30d, breach_timestamp − 5min] window — the 30-day pre-cascade baseline) NOT from the post-cascade-window; the post-cascade window is contaminated by the cascade's own drag-effect.

9. **Using-static-cohort-LTV-multiplier-instead-of-auto-tuned** — if the 7 cohort-LTV-multipliers (bfcm_2024_repeat_buyer=3.0 / bfcm_2024_new_buyer=1.5 / bfcm_2024_one_time_buyer=0.5 / non_bfcm_repeat_buyer=2.0 / non_bfcm_new_buyer=1.0 / non_bfcm_one_time_buyer=0.3 / vip_loyalty_tier=4.0) are static, Move #6.20 mis-prioritizes recommended-actions for cohorts whose LTV has actually drifted (e.g. a BFCM 2024 repeat-buyer cohort grew from 3.0x to 4.5x LTV after a successful loyalty-program-launch, but Move #6.20 still treats it as 3.0x and under-prioritizes "freeze cohort from cascade" by ~50%). Fix: cohort-LTV-multiplier-auto-tuner (per Move #6.17's auto-tuner) is a HARD requirement; weekly auto-tune via XGBoost + Move #6.15's per-cohort-accuracy feedback + statistical-significance gate + rollback-safety-net.

10. **Postmortem-emit-on-cohorts-or-influencers-or-cascades-but-not-on-all-three** — if Move #6.20 only emits per-cohort post-mortem and skips per-influencer OR per-cascade post-mortem, you miss the 30-60% of breach-events that are influencer-driven (micro-influencer mid-campaign + employee-advocate mid-campaign + affiliate mid-campaign + 2 others are particularly sensitive to marketing-API token rotation because their attribution depends on per-influencer discount-code-graph which gets reset by the cascade) AND the 50%+ of breach-events that are cascade-driven (Move #6.19's cascade-net-benefit events). Fix: Move #6.20 MUST emit post-mortem on ALL THREE: per-cohort, per-influencer, AND per-cascade; the cascade-postmortem is load-bearing, don't skip it.

11. **Not-handling-VIP-loyalty-tier-as-tied-priority-with-BFCM-repeat-buyer-for-recommended-action-prioritization** — the VIP loyalty tier cohort has 4.0x LTV-multiplier (HIGHER than BFCM 2024 repeat-buyer's 3.0x). If Move #6.20 computes recommended-action-priority by summing raw `expected_breach_cost_reduction × priority_weight` without weighting by LTV-multiplier, it under-prioritizes VIP-tier actions by 33% and may emit "rotate API key" as TOP for VIP-cohort when what VIP-cohort actually needs is "freeze cohort from cascade for 90 days". Fix: recommended-action-prioritizer MUST apply `cohort_LTV_multiplier` to BOTH the cohort-action AND the recovery-prediction; VIP-tier-weighted-action is "small-but-vital" and the prioritizer must respect that.

12. **No-per-platform-postmortem-recommended-action-normalization** — if Move #6.20 uses a single recommended-action-template (default: "rotate API key") across all platforms, it mis-prioritizes actions for platform-specific breach-events (e.g. a Meta-only breach should recommend "rotate Meta CAPI API key" as TOP, but a 5-platform breach should recommend "rotate ALL 5 platform API keys + pause sequence" as TOP). Fix: recommended-action-generator MUST compute per-platform recommended-action-templates (Meta-only="rotate Meta CAPI API key by Tue 14:00 UTC" / Google-only="rotate Google Ads API key + re-verify Enhanced Conversions" / TikTok-only="rotate TikTok EAPI API key + re-verify dedup" / Snap-only="rotate Snap Pixel API key" / Pinterest-only="rotate Pinterest CAPI API key" / 2-platform="rotate both + sequence" / 4-platform="rotate all 4 in parallel" / 5-platform="rotate all 5 + verify post-rotation attribution match"); the per-platform normalization is load-bearing.

13. **Not-emitting-postmortem-outcome-accuracy-to-Move-#6.15-ledger** — if Move #6.20 emits postmortem-outcome-accuracy to a Move-#6.20-only dashboard without wiring it to Move #6.15's `move_6_15_accuracy_ledger` with `accuracy_layer="move_6_20_postmortem_outcome_accuracy"`, Move #6.15 can't auto-tune Move #6.17's per-cohort-protection-priority OR Move #6.18's per-influencer-protection-priority OR Move #6.19's cascade-drag-calibration based on real-world operator-execution-rate of the recommended-action chain. Fix: postmortem-outcome-tracker MUST emit to Move #6.15's accuracy-ledger; the `accuracy_layer="move_6_20_postmortem_outcome_accuracy"` tag is load-bearing.

14. **Postmortem-emit-without-cohort-or-influencer-or-cascade-breach-cost-isolation-test-passing** — if Move #6.20's per-cohort-postmortem-breach-cost-isolation-test (`sum(per_cohort_breach_cost_in_postmortem) === total_per_cohort_breach_cost_for_all_cohorts` within ±0.5%) OR per-influencer-postmortem-breach-cost-isolation-test FAILS, the decomposition is arithmetically broken (e.g. a pandas groupby dropped a row OR the LTV-multiplier was applied twice OR the priority-weight was mis-applied). Fix: isolation-test FAIL → post-mortem-emit is BLOCKED → Slack alert `postmortem_cohort_breach_cost_isolation_test_failed` OR `postmortem_influencer_breach_cost_isolation_test_failed` OR `postmortem_cascade_cascade_net_isolation_test_failed` + auto-revert to the prior computation + open a Linear issue tagged `attribution-move-6.20-isolation-test-failed`.

15. **Not-handling-cascade-cross-incident-postmortem-aggregation-as-load-bearing-input** — if Move #6.20 emits per-cascade post-mortem individually without aggregating cascade-stack events (3+ cascades within 24h on same breach-source) into a single cascade-stack-postmortem-template, you miss the cross-incident root-cause-pattern that the operator needs to root-cause-fix the upstream breach-source (not the individual cascades). Fix: cascade-cross-incident-postmortem-aggregator (Phase 1) is a HARD requirement; the aggregator detects cascade-stack events on triple-detection, combines them into a single cascade-stack postmortem with cross-incident root-cause-pattern + cumulative-harm-to-non-cascade + cumulative-net-benefit + recommended-action-chain targeting the breach-source. The cascade-stack-postmortem is load-bearing.
