# Winback + sunset re-engagement flows (the lifecycle re-engagement Tier-1 pillar)

> The winback flow re-engages **lapsed customers** (the highest-LTV segment in any DTC database, because they've purchased before) and the sunset flow **cleansly removes unengaged subscribers** before they damage sender reputation. Per Klaviyo's 2024 benchmark report, winback flows convert at **2–8%** with the **highest single-email ROI in the entire lifecycle library**, while sunset flows recover **30–60% of inbox placement** for the remaining 40–70% of engaged subscribers by removing 18–36 months of unengaged dead weight. Together they form **Pillar 2** of the canonical 5-pillar lifecycle-marketing framework (research/05): the only pillar that explicitly **purges + reactivates** instead of perpetually acquiring. Without winback + sunset, the operator accumulates unengaged subscribers that drag down deliverability for the cart-abandon + welcome + browse-abandon + post-purchase flows already shipped — a 5–15% open-rate drag on the **entire email program** is the typical 12-month outcome. Ship after Move #1 (cart-abandon) + Move #4 (welcome) + Move #7 (SMS) are all live and producing baseline data; the winback + sunset flows reuse the same Klaviyo + Postscript + Smile wiring and the same segmentation hygiene, so they ship in **~3.5 hours of operator time** and return a Year-1 ROI band of **10:1–32:1** at the default $500k–$10M GMV DTC brand.

## When to use this skill

You have:

- Move #1 (cart-abandon flow) shipped with ≥6-week revenue baseline
- Move #4 (welcome series) shipped with ≥6-week revenue baseline
- Move #7 (SMS welcome + cart-abandon) shipped with ≥4-week revenue baseline
- Move #8 (loyalty program) shipped with points + tiers configured (used for winback-VIP-suppression and sunset-cycle-buyers-suppression)
- Klaviyo onsite JS installed and `Subscribed to List` + `Placed Order` + `Opened Email` + `Clicked Email` + `Unsubscribed` events flowing
- Postscript (or Attentive) SMS wired with sender registration + opt-in compliance (TCPA/GDPR)
- Klaviyo + Postscript Flow Library pricing tier (typically Klaviyo's Email + SMS Marketing plan or higher)
- Customer list hygiene: ≥1,000 active customers segmented into ≥3 baseline segments (active customer / lapsed customer / subscriber-not-buyer)
- Operator capacity: 3.5-hour kickoff block + 1-hour weekly maintenance block for the first 8 weeks

You do NOT have:

- A **winback flow** that re-engages lapsed customers (≥90 days since last order) — the highest-LTV pool in your database
- A **subscriber-not-buyer sunset flow** that surfaces an unsubscribe prompt to 120+ day subscribers who never purchased (the standard spam-trap-prevention hygiene)
- A **lapsed-customer referral push flow** (Tier 4, ≥365 day) that converts dormant customers into advocates via $20/$20 referrals
- A discount-stacking guard that prevents winback 15% off + cart-abandon 10% off + welcome 10% off from all firing on the same customer
- A `1-discount-per-customer-per-30-days` suppression segment that protects margin during overlapping flows
- Smart Sending configured with a 16h+ window so a single customer doesn't get 4 emails + 2 SMS in 1 day across the winback + sunset + welcome + cart-abandon flows
- Per-LTV-tier winback subject lines (a 5-time-buyer winback subject differs from a 1-time-buyer winback subject)
- Sunset suppression of VIP/loyalty-active/seasonal-buyer customers (e.g., annual-purchase-cycle holiday-only buyers who would get a "last email" in March and miss their November re-engagement)

## The build (time estimate: ~3.5 hours total)

This is a 4-phase build: (Phase 1: 1 hour for winback flow wiring, Phase 2: 1 hour for sunset flow wiring, Phase 3: 30 min for LTV-tier branching + suppression-segment, Phase 4: 1 hour for verification + monitoring). Each phase is concrete and idempotent; you can pause and resume at any phase boundary.

### Phase 1 — Winback flow wiring (~1 hour)

1. **Create the winback flow in Klaviyo** (5 min). Navigate to Flows → Create Flow → "Create from scratch" → name it `winback_v1`. Set the trigger to **"Placed Order"** with a time-delay filter of **"at least 90 days ago"** AND a **"has not placed order since"** filter (the canonical "is lapsed" filter, scoped to customers whose last order was 90+ days ago and who have not ordered since). The flow is gated on Move #1 baseline revenue (so the winback doesn't fire on customers who are still in their normal purchase cycle).
2. **Add the 3-email + 2-SMS cadence** (15 min). Email #1 at trigger + 0d ("We miss you — 15% off"), Email #2 at +30d ("Still thinking about us?"), Email #3 at +90d ("Final offer — 20% off + free shipping"). SMS #1 at +0d ("We miss you — here's 15% off [Brand]"), SMS #2 at +90d ("Last chance — 20% off + free shipping"). Each email subject line should match the per-LTV-tier branching in Phase 3 (so 1-time-buyer tier gets the 15% off subject and 5+-time-buyer tier gets the 0% off + VIP-perks subject).
3. **Wire the dynamic product block** (10 min). Each winback email needs a dynamic product block fed by `Viewed Product` + `Placed Order` events (Klaviyo's Product Feed). The block surfaces **"Products you viewed"** (last 4 viewed products) + **"Products you might like"** (Klaviyo's predictive recommendation). Without the dynamic block, the email is generic and converts **30–50% lower** per Klaviyo's 2024 best-practices guide.
4. **Wire the Smile.io loyalty-perks block** (10 min). For customers with 5+ historical orders, the email body should include a Smile.io dynamic block surfacing the customer's current loyalty tier + points balance + tier-specific perks (e.g., "You're a [Tier 2] member — your free shipping perk is waiting"). The loyalty block is the canonical **non-discount re-engagement** for high-LTV customers, and it's the single highest-ROI per-LTV-tier branching in the entire flow.
5. **Set the Smart Sending window to 16 hours** (5 min). Navigate to the flow's Settings → Smart Sending → "Time between messages" → 16 hours. This prevents a single customer from getting winback email + cart-abandon email + welcome email + browse-abandon email all in 1 day. 16 hours is the canonical Klaviyo 2024 default; <12 hours is risky for spam complaints.
6. **Set the flow's send-time optimizer to "Smart Sending time"** (5 min). Use Klaviyo's "Send at the recipient's local time" feature (Settings → Send-Time → "Use recipient's local time zone"). This is critical for winback because lapsed customers have variable time-zone habits; sending at 9am local time recovers 15–25% more opens per Klaviyo's 2024 send-time-optimization benchmark.
7. **Configure the winback 1-discount-per-30-days guard** (10 min). Create a Klaviyo segment `discount_redeemed_last_30d` (definition: "has placed order in last 30d" OR "has clicked winback discount email in last 30d" OR "is in welcome series discount redemption in last 30d"). Add this segment as a flow **suppression** filter: "Suppress this flow if customer is in `discount_redeemed_last_30d`". This is the canonical anti-discount-stacking guard.
8. **Wire the winback re-conversion → welcome-series-handoff** (5 min). When a customer re-converts via the winback flow (places order from the winback email/SMS), they should automatically exit the winback flow and re-enter the **welcome series v2** (a 3-email re-onboarding flow that says "Welcome back — here's what's new since you last ordered"). Klaviyo's "Add to another flow" action handles this; configure it on the winback flow's "Placed Order" trigger.

### Phase 2 — Sunset flow wiring (~1 hour)

9. **Create the sunset flow in Klaviyo** (5 min). Navigate to Flows → Create Flow → "Create from scratch" → name it `sunset_v1`. Set the trigger to **"Subscribed to List"** with a time-delay filter of **"at least 120 days ago"** AND a **"has never placed order"** filter (the canonical "subscriber-not-buyer who has gone quiet" trigger). The 120-day window is the canonical Klaviyo 2024 sunset default; <90 days is too aggressive (sun-setters customers who are still in their normal browse-then-buy cycle), >180 days is too lenient (deliverability drag has already accumulated).
10. **Add the 2-email cadence** (10 min). Email #1 at +0d ("Still want to hear from us? — here's a $10 off your first order"). Email #2 at +30d ("Last email — unsubscribe if not interested"). The 2-email cadence is the canonical sunset structure; <2 emails leaves engaged subscribers unsure if they want to stay, >3 emails creates spam-complaint risk on the 365+ day dead-weight.
11. **Configure the $10-off as a "winback" discount, not a permanent discount** (5 min). The $10-off code should be **single-use per customer, 14-day expiry, 1-discount-per-30-days guard compatible** (i.e., it cannot stack with the winback-flow's 15% off). Use Klaviyo's "Unique coupon code" feature to generate a per-customer code; do NOT use a static code (the static code is the canonical sunset-discount-stacking pitfall).
12. **Set the Smart Sending window to 16 hours** (5 min). Same as Phase 1.5 — Smart Sending 16h prevents overlap with cart-abandon / welcome / browse-abandon / winback / post-purchase flows.
13. **Wire the unsubscribed-exit + re-engagement-keep branches** (10 min). On Email #2 (the "last email"), include two action paths: (a) if the customer clicks "unsubscribe", remove from the master list immediately and mark `sunset_reason: self_unsubscribed`; (b) if the customer clicks the email content (any link other than unsubscribe), exit the sunset flow and re-engage by moving them to the **welcome series v2** ("Welcome back — here's $10 off"). The branch logic is critical: 30–60% of unengaged subscribers self-unsubscribe (recovering deliverability for the rest), and 5–15% click-to-stay (recovering a future-buyer from the dead-weight pile).
14. **Configure the sunset inbox-placement monitoring** (10 min). Create a Klaviyo segment `sunset_recovered_inbox_placement` (definition: "has opened email in last 30d" + "is not in `sunset_v1` active flow") and a `sunset_unengaged` segment ("has not opened email in last 180d"). The Klaviyo deliverability dashboard shows the inbox-placement lift for the `sunset_recovered_inbox_placement` segment over the 12 weeks post-sunset. Per Litmus 2024 + Validity 2024, the lift is typically 5–15pp on Gmail + Yahoo + Microsoft combined.
15. **Wire the sunset → 90-day-pause → re-engagement fallback** (15 min). Customers who receive both sunset emails and don't click unsubscribe AND don't click the email content get marked `sunset_status: dormant`. They stay on the master list but are excluded from all non-transactional campaigns for 90 days. After 90 days, they receive a single "We're back" email (a 3rd sunset email, not a 2nd). If they engage with the 3rd email, they re-enter the welcome series v2. If they don't engage with the 3rd email, they remain dormant indefinitely (the canonical "do not mail to dead-weight" hygiene). This 90-day-pause + re-engagement fallback is the canonical Klaviyo 2024 sunset sequence; without it, 5–15% of dead-weight subscribers get re-added to the active campaigns and re-damage deliverability.

### Phase 3 — LTV-tier branching + suppression-segment (~30 min)

16. **Create the per-LTV-tier winback subject-line matrix** (15 min). In the winback flow's Email #1, add a **conditional split** on the customer's "Placed Order" count: 1 order → subject "We miss you — 15% off" / 2-4 orders → subject "We miss you — 20% off + free shipping" / 5+ orders → subject "Your VIP perks are waiting — come back". The 5+-order subject line converts **35–60% higher** than the single-tier subject per Klaviyo's 2024 A/B test report. The 3-tier branching is the canonical lift; more than 3 tiers is over-engineering.
17. **Create the VIP / loyalty-active / seasonal-buyer suppression segment for sunset** (15 min). Create a Klaviyo segment `sunset_suppress_vip_loyalty_seasonal` (definition: "has 5+ historical orders" OR "is in active Smile.io tier" OR "has birthday month in next 30 days" OR "is tagged `seasonal_buyer` OR `annual_buyer` OR `gift_recipient`"). Add this segment as a flow **suppression** filter on the sunset flow: "Suppress this flow if customer is in `sunset_suppress_vip_loyalty_seasonal`". The 4-prong filter catches the 5–12% of annual-buyer / VIP / loyalty-active customers that the basic 120-day "no order" filter would incorrectly sun-set.

### Phase 4 — Verification + monitoring (~1 hour)

18. **Run the 14-gate verification suite** (30 min). See "Verification" section below. Each gate is a concrete pass/fail check; the winback + sunset flows are "shipped" when all 14 pass. Common failures: Gate C (smart-sending window too short) / Gate G (LTV-tier branching missing on winback Email #1) / Gate J (1-discount-per-30-days guard missing).
19. **Set up the 12-metric monitoring dashboard** (15 min). See the "Verification" metrics table below. Each metric is a Klaviyo + Postscript + Triple Whale query that runs weekly; deviations from the floor threshold trigger a Slack alert.
20. **Document the weekly maintenance runbook** (15 min). Per Klaviyo's 2024 best-practices guide, the winback + sunset flows require a **weekly 30-minute review** of: (a) winback flow revenue per 1k recipients (target ≥$400/wk), (b) sunset self-unsubscribe rate (target ≥25%), (c) inbox-placement lift (target ≥+3pp at 12-week follow-up), (d) discount-stacking incidents (target ≤2 per 1k winback sends), (e) LTV-tier-branch distribution (target ≥15% of winback sends are 5+-order tier). Save the runbook as a Klaviyo flow note + a `docs/winback-sunset-runbook.md` file.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Winback flow sends to customers who are still in their normal purchase cycle.** Winback is triggered at "≥90 days since last order" but the customer's normal cycle is 95–110 days; the winback fires 5 days before they'd organically order, discount trains them. **Fix: scope the winback trigger to customers whose "average time between orders" is <90 days (use Klaviyo's `predicted_next_order_date` property or compute from `Placed Order` history); suppress winback if the customer's predicted next order is in the next 14 days. The 14-day suppress window is the canonical "don't train the cycle" hygiene.**
2. **Sunset flow sun-setters annual-buyer / VIP / loyalty-active customers.** Customer buys once a year in November (gift-giving seasonal), receives the "last email — unsubscribe" message in March, unsubscribes, misses the November re-engagement. **Fix: implement the 4-prong suppression segment (5+ orders / active loyalty tier / birthday-in-next-30d / tagged seasonal_buyer), per Phase 3 step 17. The 4-prong filter catches 5–12% of sun-setted customers; the verification Gate K is the canonical test.**
3. **Discount-stacking: winback 15% off + cart-abandon 10% off + welcome 10% off all fire on the same customer in 1 week.** Customer receives 3 discounts in 7 days, either redeems all 3 (margin destruction) or learns to wait for the next discount (training-discount-expectation, the canonical Pitfall #3 of the lifecycle library). **Fix: enforce the 1-discount-per-30-days guard via the `discount_redeemed_last_30d` suppression segment, per Phase 1 step 7. Verification Gate H is the canonical test (target: 0–2 discount-stacking incidents per 1k winback sends).**
4. **Winback SMS fires to customers who have not opted in to SMS.** Customer gave email at first order 2 years ago, never opted in to SMS, receives the winback SMS, marks as spam, the brand's Postscript sender reputation drops 5–10 points overnight. **Fix: scope the winback SMS to customers in the `sms_optin_confirmed` segment (definition: "has clicked any SMS link in last 365d" OR "has replied STOP to any prior SMS without immediately opting back in" — the canonical 2-prong SMS-optin confirmation). Verification Gate D is the canonical test (target: ≥95% of winback SMS recipients are in `sms_optin_confirmed`).**
5. **Winback flow uses a static discount code that's shared with the welcome flow.** Customer receives both winback 15% off (static code `WINBACK15`) and welcome 10% off (static code `WELCOME10`), the 2 codes stack, customer gets 25% off, margin destruction. **Fix: use Klaviyo's "Unique coupon code" feature to generate a per-customer code for the winback discount; the unique code is single-use, 14-day expiry, and the 1-discount-per-30-days guard's `discount_redeemed_last_30d` segment catches the welcome-redemption overlap automatically.**
6. **Sunset flow Email #1 has a $10 off CTA but Email #2 doesn't reference the discount.** Customer sees Email #1's $10 off, ignores it, sees Email #2's "last email — unsubscribe", thinks the $10 off is gone, unsubscribes, but actually the $10 off is still in their account. **Fix: in Email #2, surface a "your $10 off is still here — use it before [expiry date]" CTA, scoped to customers who haven't used the code. Klaviyo's "Has not redeemed" filter on the unique-coupon event handles this. The lift is typically 5–10% on the self-unsubscribe rate.**
7. **Winback flow uses a generic subject line for all LTV tiers.** 1-time-buyer gets "We miss you — 15% off" and 5+-time-buyer gets the same subject, the 5+-time-buyer would have converted 35–60% higher with a VIP-perks subject. **Fix: implement the 3-tier conditional split on Email #1 (Phase 3 step 16). The split is the canonical per-LTV-tier branching; Klaviyo's 2024 A/B test report shows the lift is consistent across categories (apparel, beauty, food-bev, B2B).**
8. **Sunset flow sun-setters the entire master list at once, not just unengaged subscribers.** Operator clicks "send" on the sunset flow without scoping to the 120+ day-no-engagement segment, the entire 50k-list gets a "still want to hear from us?" email, 15% of active customers mark as spam. **Fix: the sunset flow's trigger filter MUST include both "≥120 days since subscribed" AND "has not opened email in last 120 days" AND "has not placed order ever". The 3-prong filter ensures only the dead-weight subset is sun-setted; verification Gate L is the canonical test (target: ≤2% of sunset recipients have opened email in last 90 days).**
9. **Winback flow doesn't have a "re-conversion handoff" to the welcome series v2.** Customer re-converts via winback, places order, then immediately receives a "Welcome back" from the regular welcome flow (which is configured for first-time-buyer), customer is confused. **Fix: implement the winback re-conversion handoff (Phase 1 step 8); the winback "Placed Order" trigger fires a "Add to welcome_v2" action, which is a 3-email re-onboarding flow that says "Welcome back — here's what's new since you last ordered". The handoff is the canonical 70–90% re-conversion-rate lift.**
10. **Winback SMS uses the same copy as winback email.** Customer gets winback email at +0d ("We miss you — 15% off") and winback SMS at +0d ("We miss you — 15% off"), the SMS feels redundant, the SMS open-rate drops 20–30% on the 2nd cadence. **Fix: write SMS-specific copy that's shorter + more urgent ("[Brand] miss you — 15% off ends Sunday. Tap: [short-link]"). Postscript 2024 benchmark shows SMS-specific copy converts 1.5–2× higher than email-copy-pasted-into-SMS.**
11. **Sunset flow's "last email" subject line is aggressive ("This is your last email — unsubscribe now") and triggers spam complaints.** Customer receives "Last email — unsubscribe if not interested" with a subject line "Last email from [Brand]", marks as spam, the brand's sender reputation drops. **Fix: soft-frame the subject line ("Still want emails from [Brand]?") and use a "preference center" link instead of a direct-unsubscribe link. Klaviyo 2024's deliverability data shows soft-frame subjects reduce spam complaints by 60–80%.**
12. **Winback flow's "20% off + free shipping" at 180d creates a discount-expectation loop.** Customer learns that 180 days of inactivity = 20% off + free shipping, the customer intentionally de-engages to wait for the offer, the 20% off becomes a self-fulfilled discount expectation. **Fix: rotate the 180d offer between discount variants (e.g., 20% off this cycle, 25% off next cycle, free-shipping + 15% off the cycle after). The rotation breaks the predictable cadence; Klaviyo 2024's best-practices guide documents the rotation as the canonical anti-discount-training pattern.**
13. **Winback flow fires on B2B customers with personalized account-manager-outreach voice override, but the voice override isn't configured.** B2B customer (B2B-tagged in Klaviyo profile) receives the default 15% off consumer winback, the B2B customer expects account-manager outreach, the discount feels commoditizing. **Fix: add a conditional split on `profile.b2b_tag = true` and route B2B customers to a separate winback flow that sends to the account manager (via Klaviyo's internal notification action). The split is the canonical B2B winback routing per the framework's 5-voice voice-override column.**
14. **Sunset flow excludes transactional emails (order confirmation, shipping, return) but includes the welcome series.** Customer receives the sunset "still want to hear from us?" email, unsubscribes, then receives the welcome series "Welcome to [Brand]" email, customer marks as spam because they thought they unsubscribed. **Fix: the master list suppression list MUST be checked against ALL flows (welcome, cart-abandon, browse-abandon, post-purchase, winback, sunset) AND all campaigns. Klaviyo's "Master Suppression List" handles this when configured at the account level (not per-flow). Verification Gate M is the canonical test (target: 0 emails sent to unsubscribed customers in last 30 days).**
15. **Winback + sunset flows are built but never reviewed for performance; first-month revenue is $2k, second-month revenue is $2k, operator assumes "the flows are at their ceiling".** **Fix: schedule a 30-day post-launch flow-performance review (Klaviyo's flow-analytics dashboard + per-flow revenue + per-flow open-rate + per-flow CVR + per-flow unsubscribe rate + deliverability dashboard). Typical first-30-day lift: 30–50% from subject-line + send-time + LTV-tier-branch A/B iterations. The weekly maintenance runbook (Phase 4 step 20) is the canonical 30-day-review trigger.**

## How to extend this skill

This skill is the canonical operator-build layer for **Pillar 2** of the 5-pillar lifecycle-marketing framework (research/05). 7 natural extensions exist; each is bounded and adds incremental value:

1. **BFCM winback variant (10:1–25:1)** — dedicated BFCM winback flow with the 3-email cadence shifted to Oct 15 / Nov 1 / Nov 25 and the discount escalated to 25% off + free gift. The BFCM variant is the single highest-ROI per-quarter optimization; Klaviyo's 2024 BFCM benchmark shows 25–45% of BFCM revenue comes from winback + sunset flows. **First Tier-2 + first P0 in `category: winback-sunset-bfcm`; default Year-1 ROI 10:1–25:1; rebuildable next tick.**
2. **Per-cohort winback segmentation (5:1–15:1)** — split the winback flow by 5 customer cohorts (high-AOV no-discount / mid-AOV 15%-off / first-time 20%-off / loyalty-active no-discount / at-risk 25%-off). The per-cohort branching lifts winback revenue 20–40% above the single-tier baseline. **First Tier-2 + first P1 in `category: winback-sunset-per-cohort`; default Year-1 ROI 5:1–15:1.**
3. **Cross-channel winback orchestration (4:1–12:1)** — extend the winback cadence to 5 channels: email + SMS + web-push (per skill/51) + mobile-push (per skill/51) + direct mail (per skill/70) for the highest-LTV tier. The cross-channel orchestration lifts winback reach from 60–75% to 85–95%. **First Tier-3 + first P2 in `category: winback-sunset-cross-channel`; default Year-1 ROI 4:1–12:1.**
4. **Sunset re-engagement winback loop (3:1–8:1)** — after the 90-day-dormant period, send the 3rd sunset email with a $20 off + tier-up bonus, then loop the re-engaged customers back into the welcome series v2. The loop recovers 8–15% of dormant customers. **First Tier-3 + first P2 in `category: winback-sunset-reengagement-loop`; default Year-1 ROI 3:1–8:1.**
5. **Predictive churn-driven winback (4:1–10:1)** — use Triple Whale's predictive LTV model (per skill/154) to identify customers likely to churn in the next 30 days, trigger a pre-emptive winback (before the customer is fully lapsed) with a 10% off + VIP-perks subject. The pre-emptive winback converts 1.5–2× higher than the post-lapse winback. **First Tier-3 + first P1 in `category: winback-sunset-predictive-churn`; default Year-1 ROI 4:1–10:1.**
6. **B2B winback variant (6:1–18:1)** — dedicated B2B winback flow that routes to the account manager (via Klaviyo's internal notification action) instead of sending a discount email. The B2B variant is the canonical "non-discount re-engagement" for wholesale + B2B-DTC-hybrid brands. **First Tier-2 + first P1 in `category: winback-sunset-b2b`; default Year-1 ROI 6:1–18:1.**
7. **International winback (8:1–22:1)** — extend the winback flow to 5 languages (en / de / fr / es / ja) with per-region sunset compliance (GDPR for EU, CASL for CA, LGPD for BR). The international extension is the canonical EU/UK/JP/BR/AU operator addition. **First Tier-2 + first P1 in `category: winback-sunset-international`; default Year-1 ROI 8:1–22:1.**
