## What "best in class" looks like

The 6-pillar framework is built so that each pillar's output composes into the next, and so that the operator's weekly inventory-review + monthly buy-meeting + quarterly OTB-rebalance all run off the same per-SKU-buy-commitment.

### Pillar 1 — Per-SKU buy-commitment engine (the core OTB math)

The per-SKU-buy-commitment engine computes **how many units of each SKU to buy for the next 90 / 180 / 365 days**, gated on the Move #356 hero/halo/filler 3-tier hierarchy + Triple Whale per-SKU-velocity + Recharge per-SKU-replenishment-cadence + Move #96 demand-sensing-signal. Without this engine, OTB is operator-head-only tribal knowledge and the operator over-buys fillers and under-buys heroes (the canonical anti-pattern per Blue-Yonder-OTB-2026 + NetSuite-OTB-2026 + Revionics-OTB-2026 + Move #356 benchmarks).

#### Per-SKU OTB formula (the 7-input math)

```
OTB_units(SKU_X, period_T) = (
  forecast_units(SKU_X, T)          // Pillar 1 from Triple Whale per-SKU-velocity
  + safety_stock_units(SKU_X, T)    // Pillar 1 from velocity-stddev + lead-time
  + BFCM_peak_units(SKU_X, T)       // Pillar 4 from Move #89 peak-multiplier × 8-week-pre-BFCM
  + subscription_cohort_units(SKU_X, T) // Pillar 5 from Recharge per-SKU-replenishment-cadence
  + competitor_stockout_uplift(SKU_X, T) // Pillar 6 from Move #107 competitor-stockout-signal
  − on_hand_units(SKU_X, T)         // Inventory API Locations query
  − on_order_units(SKU_X, T)        // POs + transfers-in-transit
  − dead_stock_clearance(SKU_X, T)  // Move #86 markdown-clearance target
) × tier_multiplier(SKU_X)          // Move #356 hero/halo/filler multiplier
```

Where:

- **forecast_units** is Triple Whale per-SKU-velocity × 90/180/365 (with cohort-affinity-overlay per Move #356 Pillar 3).
- **safety_stock_units** is `Z × σ_30d × √lead_time_days` where Z is the service-level z-score (95% service-level → Z=1.65 for hero, 1.28 for halo, 1.04 for filler).
- **BFCM_peak_units** is `peak_multiplier × forecast_units_8weeks_pre_BFCM` where peak_multiplier is per-SKU (Move #89 BFCM).
- **subscription_cohort_units** is `subscription_active_customers × per-customer-monthly-velocity × period_months`.
- **competitor_stockout_uplift** is `competitor_stockout_pct × forecast_units` from Move #107.
- **tier_multiplier** is 1.4× for hero, 1.0× for halo, 0.6× for filler (per Move #356 Pillar 2).

#### The 5-input rationale

- **forecast_units** is the **commercial signal** — Triple Whale per-SKU-velocity tells the operator how many units they sold in the last 90d; the forecast extrapolates with cohort-affinity-adjustment. Without forecast_units, OTB is operator-head-only.
- **safety_stock_units** is the **resilience signal** — without safety-stock, any supplier-delay or demand-spike causes stockout. Hero-SKUs need 95% service-level (Z=1.65); halo 90% (Z=1.28); filler 85% (Z=1.04).
- **BFCM_peak_units** is the **seasonality signal** — Move #89's per-SKU peak-multiplier (typically 2.5×-4× for hero-SKUs in Q4) must be applied 8-weeks-pre-BFCM. Without this, the operator buys Q3-quantity for Q4 and stockouts on the peak day.
- **subscription_cohort_units** is the **subscription signal** — if 1,000 customers are subscribed to SKU-X with monthly replenishment, the operator needs ≥1,000 × lead-time units on-hand at all times. Without this, subscription-churn-from-stockouts is 30-50% per quarter.
- **competitor_stockout_uplift** is the **competitive signal** — when Move #107 reports top-3-competitors are out-of-stock on SKU-X, the operator can capture 30-50% of competitor-demand if they have inventory. Without this, OTB misses competitive windows.

#### The canonical per-SKU OTB output: the "OTB board"

```
SKU       | Tier   | Forecast 90d | Safety | BFCM Peak | Sub Cohort | Comp Uplift | On-hand | On-order | Dead-stock clr | OTB units | Tier Mult | TOTAL BUY
SKU-001   | HERO   |   600       |  80    |  150      |  100       |  60         |  -200   |  -300    |  -10           |  480      |  1.4×     | 672
SKU-002   | HERO   |   450       |  60    |  120      |   50       |  45         |  -150   |  -200    |   -5           |  370      |  1.4×     | 518
SKU-003   | HALO   |   200       |  30    |   40      |   20       |  20         |  -100   |   -80    |   -8           |  122      |  1.0×     | 122
...
SKU-080   | FILLER |    12       |   5    |    0      |    0       |   0         |   -50   |    -5    |  -45           |  -83      |  0.6×     | -50 (DISCONTINUE)
```

The OTB board is the **single source of truth** for the per-SKU buy-commitment. The operator's weekly inventory-review + monthly buy-meeting + quarterly OTB-rebalance all run off the same board.

### Pillar 2 — 3-pool inventory allocation (on-hand / on-order / pipeline)

Every active SKU's inventory is split across 3 pools, and each pool has a different replenishment-cadence + buffer-policy + visibility-rule.

|| Pool | Definition | % of total per-SKU inventory | Replenishment cadence | Buffer policy | Visibility |
||---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|| **ON-HAND** | Units physically in warehouse | 60-75% | Weekly hero / bi-weekly halo / monthly filler | `days-on-hand = on_hand / forecast_daily_velocity`; target 30-90 days for hero, 60-180 for halo, 90-365 for filler | Real-time Inventory API |
|| **ON-ORDER** | Units on PO + in-transit to warehouse | 15-25% | Weekly review + auto-PO-trigger when on-hand drops below safety-stock | Lead-time × velocity buffer | PO tracker + transfer API |
|| **PIPELINE** | Units not yet ordered but forecast-needed | 5-15% | Monthly forecast-review + quarterly-buy-decision | Quarterly OTB-board drives | OTB-board (this skill) |

**Canonical 3-pool distribution for a healthy $3M-GMV DTC brand:**
- **On-hand:** 60-75% of inventory, with **30-90 days-of-supply for hero**, **60-180 days for halo**, **90-365 days for filler** (per Triple-Whale-Per-SKU-Velocity-2026 + Blue-Yonder-Replenishment-2026 + Manhattan-Replenishment-2026 + Move #29 benchmarks).
- **On-order:** 15-25% of inventory, with **lead-time × velocity** buffer (per Blue-Yonder-Replenishment-2026 + NetSuite-Demand-Planning-2026 + Manhattan-Replenishment-2026 + Move #29 benchmarks).
- **Pipeline:** 5-15% of inventory, with **quarterly OTB-board** driving new POs (per NetSuite-OTB-2026 + Blue-Yonder-OTB-2026 + Revionics-OTB-2026 benchmarks).

**Red-flag distribution:** on-hand <30 days for hero (stockout-risk) OR on-hand >180 days for filler (dead-stock-risk) (per Move #86 dead-stock benchmarks + Blue-Yonder-Replenishment-2026 + Manhattan-Replenishment-2026 benchmarks).

### Pillar 3 — Per-channel warehouse routing + per-region fulfillment pool

Every SKU is routed to the right warehouse(s) and the right region-pool based on Move #25 international + Move #356 per-cohort affinity + Move #107 competitor-stockout-uplift. Without per-channel-warehouse-routing, the operator ships every order from 1-warehouse and east-coast customers wait 5 days vs 2-day-expectation (the canonical anti-pattern per ShipBob-Multi-Warehouse-2026 + LeanBox-Multi-Warehouse-2026 + ShipBob-State-of-DTC-Shipping-2026 + Move #25 benchmarks).

#### Per-warehouse SKU routing rule (LeanBox 2026 + ShipBob 2026 + FBA 2026 benchmark)

|| Warehouse | Hero-SKUs allocated | Halo-SKUs allocated | Filler-SKUs allocated | Routing rule |
||---|---|---|---|---|
|| **US-East (primary)** | 100% of hero-roster | 80% of halo-roster | 60% of filler-roster | Default ship-from for all US orders |
|| **US-West** | 60% of hero-roster | 50% of halo-roster | 30% of filler-roster | Ship-from for CA / OR / WA / AZ orders + East-stockout-fallback |
|| **EU-DE (Frankfurt)** | Top-4 hero-SKUs (per Move #25 DE-preferred) | Top-8 halo-SKUs (DE-preferred) | None | Ship-from for all DE/NL/AT orders |
|| **EU-UK (London)** | Top-3 hero-SKUs (UK-preferred) | Top-6 halo-SKUs (UK-preferred) | None | Ship-from for UK orders |
|| **CA (Toronto)** | 60% of hero-roster (US-shipping-tariff-avoidance) | 30% of halo-roster | None | Ship-from for CA orders |
|| **AU (Sydney)** | Top-2 hero-SKUs | Top-4 halo-SKUs | None | Ship-from for AU orders |

**Per-region fulfillment-pool** ensures that the **on-hand + on-order units for SKU-X in warehouse-W** matches the **forecast-units for region-W**. Without this, the operator's DE-warehouse-runs-out-of-SKU-Y-while-US-warehouse-has-300-units-of-SKU-Y-sitting (the canonical "no-cross-warehouse-balance" anti-pattern per ShipBob-Multi-Warehouse-2026 + LeanBox-Multi-Warehouse-2026 + Move #25 benchmarks).

### Pillar 4 — BFCM peak-multiplier + safety-stock + cash-flow-lock

Move #357 ships with a **BFCM-cohort-peak-engine** that applies a per-SKU peak-multiplier (typically 2.5×-4× for hero-SKUs in Q4) **8 weeks pre-BFCM** + locks-in cash-flow + safety-stock for the BFCM window. Without this engine, the operator buys Q3-quantity for Q4 and stockouts on the peak day (the canonical "we-ran-out-of-our-top-3-SKUs-on-Black-Friday" anti-pattern per Move #89 BFCM + Blue-Yonder-BFCM-2026 + Manhattan-BFCM-2026 + Move #107 benchmarks).

#### BFCM 8-week-pre-window buy-decision matrix

|| Week pre-BFCM | Action | Output |
||---|---|---|---|
|| **Week -8 to -6** | Re-score per-SKU forecast with Q4-cohort-affinity-overlay (Move #356 C1 + C2 + C5 cohorts) | BFCM-aware forecast |
|| **Week -6 to -4** | Apply per-SKU peak-multiplier (2.5× hero / 2.0× halo / 1.3× filler) + safety-stock (Z=2.33 for BFCM, 99% service-level) | BFCM-aware OTB |
|| **Week -4 to -2** | Issue POs for the BFCM-window delta (OTB_units − on_hand − on_order) | BFCM POs issued |
|| **Week -2 to 0** | Receive + stage BFCM inventory in priority warehouses (US-East / US-West / EU-DE) | BFCM inventory ready |
|| **Week 0 (BFCM week)** | Monitor sell-through daily; trigger cross-warehouse-transfer if stockout-risk on hero-SKU | BFCM live-monitoring |

### Pillar 5 — Subscription-cohort replenishment integration

If Move #11 subscription-replenishment is live, Move #357's OTB-engine **integrates with Recharge/Loop per-SKU-replenishment-cadence**. Without this integration, subscription-SKU stockouts cause 30-50% subscription-churn-per-quarter (the canonical anti-pattern per Recharge-Replenishment-2026 + Loop-Replenishment-2026 + Move #11 benchmarks).

#### Subscription-cohort OTB formula

```
subscription_OTB_units(SKU_X, T) = (
  active_subscribers(SKU_X, T)            // Recharge API
  × monthly_replenishment_velocity(SKU_X) // per-customer-units-per-month
  × period_months(T)
  × cohort_growth_multiplier(SKU_X, T)    // Triple Whale cohort-LTV × Smile-tier
  × subscription_churn_buffer(SKU_X, T)   // 10-15% buffer for stockout-driven-churn
)
```

The subscription-cohort-OTB is **layered on top of** the base-forecast-OTB (NOT a replacement). The operator runs both numbers and takes the max.

### Pillar 6 — Competitor-stockout-uplift + per-cohort-affinity-velocity-overlay

Move #357's OTB-engine **integrates with Move #107 competitive-price-intelligence-engine** to capture competitor-stockout-uplift, AND with Move #356 Pillar 3 per-cohort-affinity-matrix to apply per-cohort-velocity-overlay. Without these integrations, OTB misses competitive windows and per-cohort-demand-uplifts.

#### Competitor-stockout-uplift formula

```
competitor_uplift(SKU_X, T) = (
  competitor_stockout_pct(top-3 competitors, SKU_X, T)  // Move #107
  × forecast_units(SKU_X, T)
  × competitive_capture_rate(SKU_X, T)                  // 30-50% per Move #107
)
```

#### Per-cohort-affinity-velocity-overlay formula

```
cohort_adjusted_forecast(SKU_X, T, cohort_C) = (
  base_forecast(SKU_X, T)
  × cohort_affinity_score(SKU_X, C)  // Move #356 Pillar 3
  × cohort_size(C, T)                // Triple Whale cohort-LTV
)
```

The per-cohort-affinity-overlay adjusts the **per-warehouse-OTB** (not the total OTB), because each warehouse serves a different cohort-mix.

## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

1. **Ship-without-Pillar-1-per-SKU-OTB-formula** — operator picks buy-quantities by gut. Over-buys fillers + under-buys heroes. Stockout-rate on hero-tier stays at 15-25%. **Fix:** ship Pillar 1 first; the OTB-formula is the single source of truth.

2. **No safety-stock in the OTB-formula** — operator buys forecast-units-only. Any supplier-delay or demand-spike causes stockout. Hero-SKU stockout-rate stays at 10-20%. **Fix:** safety-stock with Z=1.65 for hero (95% service-level) + 1.28 for halo + 1.04 for filler.

3. **No BFCM-peak-multiplier in the OTB-formula** — operator buys Q3-quantity for Q4. Stockouts on Black Friday. Stockout-cost = 3× the discount-collected. **Fix:** BFCM-peak-multiplier 2.5× hero / 2.0× halo / 1.3× filler applied 8-weeks-pre-BFCM (per Move #89).

4. **No Move #356 tier-multiplier in the OTB-formula** — operator buys the same quantity for hero + halo + filler. Hero-tier stockouts + filler-tier overstock. **Fix:** tier-multiplier 1.4× hero / 1.0× halo / 0.6× filler (per Move #356).

5. **No Move #11 subscription-cohort-integration** — subscription-SKUs stockout, subscription-churn-rate spikes to 30-50% per quarter. **Fix:** subscription-cohort-OTB layered on top of base-forecast-OTB (max-wins).

6. **No Move #107 competitor-stockout-uplift** — top-3-competitors out-of-stock on SKU-X, operator doesn't capture the 30-50% competitor-demand-uplift. **Fix:** competitor-uplift = competitor-stockout-pct × forecast-units × 30-50%-capture-rate (per Move #107).

7. **No Move #25 per-region-fulfillment-pool** — operator ships DE-orders from US-warehouse. DE-shipping-time = 7-12 days vs 2-3-day-local-warehouse-expectation. DE-CVR drops 50%. **Fix:** per-warehouse-SKU-routing-rule + per-region-fulfillment-pool.

8. **Pillar 2 3-pool-allocation too on-hand-heavy** — operator puts 90% in on-hand, 10% in on-order, 0% in pipeline. Working-capital-tied-up in inventory + dead-stock-risk. **Fix:** 60-75% on-hand / 15-25% on-order / 5-15% pipeline.

9. **Pillar 2 3-pool-allocation too pipeline-heavy** — operator forecasts but never buys. Stockout-rate stays at 15-25%. **Fix:** the OTB-board drives the buy-decision; pipeline is the forecast, not the buy.

10. **Pillar 3 per-warehouse-routing-too-shallow** — operator only routes hero-SKUs to multi-warehouse, halo + filler stay in 1-warehouse. Halo-CVR drops 30-45% in non-primary-warehouse-regions. **Fix:** 80% of halo-roster in US-East + 50% in US-West + per-region-pool for EU/CA/AU.

11. **Pillar 3 no-cross-warehouse-balance** — DE-warehouse-runs-out-of-SKU-Y while US-warehouse-has-300-units-of-SKU-Y-sitting. **Fix:** weekly-cross-warehouse-balance-check + auto-transfer-trigger when stockout-risk detected.

12. **Pillar 4 BFCM-peak-multiplier-too-conservative** — operator applies 1.5× peak-multiplier when actual BFCM-peak is 3.5× for hero-SKUs. Stockouts on Black Friday. **Fix:** per-SKU-peak-multiplier calibrated from prior-year-BFCM-data + cohort-affinity-overlay (per Move #89).

13. **Pillar 4 cash-flow-not-locked-for-BFCM** — operator has BFCM-OTB-board but doesn't reserve cash. PO-issuance delayed by 2 weeks → stockouts on Black Friday. **Fix:** cash-flow-lock at Week -6 pre-BFCM (PO-issuance-window).

14. **Pillar 5 subscription-cohort-growth-multiplier-ignored** — subscription-cohort-grows-20%-per-quarter, OTB-engine-buys-flat-quantity. Subscription-SKU stockouts at month 4. **Fix:** cohort-growth-multiplier from Triple Whale cohort-LTV × Smile-tier.

15. **Pillar 5 subscription-churn-buffer-too-low** — operator uses 5% buffer, actual stockout-driven-churn is 15%. **Fix:** 10-15% subscription-churn-buffer.

16. **Pillar 6 per-cohort-affinity-overlay-ignored** — operator buys flat-forecast. DE-cohort-demands-SKU-Y more than US-cohort, but OTB-engine-buys-uniformly. DE-stockouts + US-overstock. **Fix:** per-cohort-affinity-overlay applied per-warehouse (DE-warehouse-OTB-uses-DE-cohort-overlay).

17. **Move #357 ships without Move #356** — OTB-engine has no tier-assignment to gate hero/halo/filler buy-quantity. Over-buys fillers. **Fix:** ship Move #356 first; OTB-engine consumes Move #356 3-tier-hierarchy.

18. **Move #357 ships without Move #29** — OTB-engine has no inventory-forecasting-signal. Operator can't compute days-of-supply + safety-stock + lead-time-buffer. **Fix:** ship Move #29 first; require Triple Whale per-SKU-velocity + Recharge per-SKU-replenishment-cadence configured.
