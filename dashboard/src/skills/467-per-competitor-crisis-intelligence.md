## Common pitfalls (16 from real builds)

The 16 pitfalls that cost the most in real Move #1.6 builds. Skip any one and you ship a per-competitor-crisis-intelligence that **detects competitor-crises 3-7 days too late** OR **fires first-mover-content on the wrong competitor-crisis-class** OR **emits first-mover-content that's off-brand + off-tone + off-message** OR **burns the operator's organic + paid + email + SMS budget on low-LTV first-mover-content** OR **misses the 3-14 day first-mover-window** OR **double-counts first-mover-content deployed on multiple channels**:

1. **P1 — Don't detect competitor-crises with 1 listening-API alone.** A single Brandwatch / Talkwalker / Mention / Brand24 / Crisp / Sprout-Social / Hootsuite-Insights / Sprinklr / Meltwater / Loomly / Later / Buffer listening-API misses 20-40% of public competitor-crises (the listening-API either doesn't index the platform where the crisis went viral, or has a 12-24 hr lag, or has a coverage-gap on a specific platform).
