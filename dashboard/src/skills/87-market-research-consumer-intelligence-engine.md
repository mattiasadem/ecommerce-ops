## When to use this skill

Use this skill when **any** of the following is true:

1. You're entering a new category, a new vertical, or a new geo and need to size the prize (TAM / SAM / SOM) **before** allocating $50k+ of inventory capital.
2. You're about to launch a new product line and the operator cannot articulate the per-vertical WTP band, the per-vertical demand elasticity, or the per-vertical white-space gap within 60 seconds.
3. You're being out-competed by a brand that appeared in the last 6 months and you can't articulate **why** in terms of per-SKU positioning, per-channel pricing, or per-cohort sentiment share.
4. You're scaling a channel (Meta, TikTok, Amazon) past $50k/mo spend and you need per-vertical-category-share-trend + per-vertical-Trend-Velocity-Score to know whether to double-down or pivot.
5. You're preparing a board update, a Series A deck, or an LP report and the investor is asking "what's the market" — and you need a defensible per-vertical-market-sizing + per-vertical-market-share + per-vertical-TAM-SAM-SOM number.
6. You're considering an acquisition, a wholesale partnership, or a marketplace expansion and you need a competitor due-diligence MRD + competitive pricing matrix + per-SKU positioning map.
7. You're hearing a new category trend ("Stanley cups", "magnesium glycinate", "at-home red-light therapy") on TikTok and need to validate demand-signal-velocity before allocating capital.
8. You want to back your CFO's forecast with a bottoms-up per-vertical-WTP × per-vertical-target-cohort × per-vertical-cohort-conversion-rate model.

Do **not** use this skill if your brand is pre-revenue (skip to Move #4 welcome series + Move #6 attribution). Skip if you're a single-product brand with one SKU and one channel — the matrix adds more overhead than value.

## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

1. **Building a market-research pipeline that doesn't drive a decision.** "We have a beautiful TAM-SAM-SOM dashboard but we don't act on it." **Fix:** Tie every Move #87 output to a downstream Move (#42 BFCM, #60 product-launch, #29 inventory-forecast, #94 voice, #90 AI-orchestration). If a metric has no downstream consumer in 30 days, kill it.
2. **Buying GWI + Nielsen + Circana without first asking "what decision will this answer?".** GWI Foundation is $30k/yr — for a $1M GMV brand that's 3% of GMV. **Fix:** Start with Similarweb + SEMrush + Ahrefs + Triple-Whale-cohort-overlay + free Google-Trends + free Apify free tier. Add GWI when you cross $5M GMV. Add Nielsen/Circana only when you're in CPG/wholesale and need retailer-grade data.
3. **Surveying 200 customers when the brand has 5,000 customers.** Sample-size-error is real: a 200-person survey has ±7% margin of error at 95% confidence. **Fix:** Use Qualtrics + Prolific panel for the PMF survey (n=400+ for ±5% MoE), but pull the **existing-customer-segmented** NPS + Van-Westendorp from Klaviyo + Postscript (n=500+) every quarter at near-zero incremental cost.
4. **Conflating trend with fad.** A Trend-Velocity-Score of 3× for 14 days = Stanley-cup-boom. A Trend-Velocity-Score of 1.5× for 180 days = magnesium-glycinate. **Fix:** Apply the 90-day-velocity AND 365-day-velocity AND cohort-LTV-overlay (Triple-Whale cohort). A fad has high 90-day-velocity + low 365-day-velocity + low cohort-LTV. A trend has high 90-day-velocity + high 365-day-velocity + high cohort-LTV.
5. **Sentiment-mining without grounding.** Using GPT-4o to "summarize Amazon reviews" produces hallucinated features that aren't in the actual corpus. **Fix:** Use a real RAG pipeline: ingest all reviews into pgvector/Pinecone, retrieve the top-k for the query, then prompt the LLM with the retrieved corpus as context. Verify with a held-out 200-review test set; the answer must cite a verbatim review phrase 90%+ of the time.
6. **PMF = 40% means you can raise prices 20%.** It doesn't. PMF measures retention-and-word-of-mouth; price-sensitivity is a separate dimension. **Fix:** Run Van-Westendorp-PSM + per-vertical-conjoint-analysis + per-vertical-Willingness-To-Pay max-diff alongside PMF. The right price is the intersection of PMF and WTP, not PMF alone.
7. **TAM = "$50B global market for our category" is a useless number.** Nobody converts the global market at 100%. **Fix:** TAM-SAM-SOM triangulation: TAM = global category size (Euromonitor) + SAM = addressable cohort × WTP × frequency (bottom-up) + SOM = realistic 3-year share at current CAC + brand strength. The bottom-up delta from the top-down should be <20%; if it's >40%, your cohort definitions are off.
8. **Per-vertical-cohort-persona based on the founder's gut, not on Numerator + GWI + Circana panel data.** "Our customer is a 28-year-old urban professional woman." That's not a persona; that's a stereotype. **Fix:** Run K-means on actual purchase + survey data. The cluster output is the persona. Then validate against Numerator + GWI panels. The persona should match the panel in 4+ of 5 dimensions.
9. **Skipping the per-vertical-SWOT + per-vertical-porters + per-vertical-PESTEL because "we know our category".** You don't. **Fix:** Quarterly refresh. PESTEL in particular changes fast (regulatory: EU-CBAM-2026, UK-CBAM-2027, California-SB-253/261, EU-CSDDD, EU-AI-Act all hit 2024–2026 — a Q1 2025 PESTEL is materially different from a Q1 2026 PESTEL).
10. **The MRD is 200 pages and nobody reads it.** **Fix:** The MRD has a **1-page exec summary** with the 5 critical numbers (TAM-SAM-SOM + Trend-Velocity-Score + PMF + NPS + WTP-optimum-price-point) and a 5-page "decision-tree" appendix. The full 200 pages live in the appendix; the operator only reads the exec summary.
11. **Trend-Velocity-Score measured on the wrong keyword set.** Searching "skincare" instead of "niacinamide serum" or "magnesium glycinate". The category head term is too noisy. **Fix:** Pick 10–15 long-tail keywords (per-vertical-product-keyword × per-vertical-feature-keyword × per-vertical-benefit-keyword). Measure each separately; weight by search-volume.
12. **Per-vertical-competitor-pricing scraper hits the bot-detection wall within 48 hours.** **Fix:** Rotate residential proxies (Bright-Data + Oxylabs + Zyte), use headless-browser with stealth (Apify + Browse-AI + Octoparse), respect robots.txt + rate-limits, never hit Amazon + Walmart + Sephora directly from a single IP. Use a scraper that mimics human scroll + mouse-movement. Cache the result for 24–48 hours; competitor pricing rarely changes intra-day.
13. **Pricing-research done with "would you pay $X" hypothetical questions.** Customers lie 30–60% of the time on hypothetical WTP. **Fix:** Use Van-Westendorp-PSM with real price-anchored questions + per-vertical-conjoint-analysis with real attribute-tradeoffs + actual cart-abandon price-A/B test (Move #47 growth-experimentation) as the ground truth. The Van-Westendorp + conjoint is the survey-side; the A/B test is the live-side.
14. **No kill criteria on the substrate.** You're paying $42k/yr for GWI + Similarweb + SEMrush + Ahrefs + Apify + Bright-Data. Is each one earning its place? **Fix:** Per-vendor attribution analysis quarterly: per-vendor-touchpoint → per-vendor-decision-influenced → per-vendor-decision-driven-revenue. If a vendor contributes <5% of decisions, drop it.
15. **Conflating "consumer-sentiment" with "brand-sentiment".** Sentiment-mining of reviews measures **product sentiment**, not **brand sentiment**. A 4.5-star product can have a 2-star brand. **Fix:** Two pipelines: per-vertical-product-sentiment-mining (RAG over reviews) + per-vertical-brand-sentiment-mining (RAG over social + Reddit + TikTok + news + press). Surface both on the dashboard.
16. **Survey fatigue.** Bi-annual Sean-Ellis + NPS + Van-Westendorp + conjoint + max-diff = 5 surveys × 200 customers = 1,000 survey-responses/yr. Your best customers get tired. **Fix:** Combine into ONE 12-question survey that takes 4 minutes. PMF (Q1) + NPS (Q2) + Van-Westendorp (Q3–Q6) + max-diff (Q7–Q10) + open-text (Q11–Q12). 4-minute completion rate ≥ 70% vs the canonical 30-second 5-question 40% completion.
17. **No "what does the competitor see" view.** You have your MRD, but you don't have your competitor's MRD. **Fix:** Reverse-engineer competitor moves via per-vertical-competitor-monitoring + per-vertical-competitor-pricing + per-vertical-competitor-ad-spend (Meta Ad Library + TikTok Creative Center + Google Ads Transparency + SEMrush). The competitor's pricing + ad-spend + launch cadence + new-SKU velocity is their MRD walk.
18. **Move #87 outputs decay without a refresh cadence.** A 12-month-old MRD is materially wrong on TAM-SAM-SOM (the category grew) + Trend-Velocity-Score (the trend shifted) + competitor-pricing (they repriced) + WTP (inflation). **Fix:** Refresh cadence: daily scrape + weekly rollup + bi-weekly competitor-pricing + monthly sentiment + quarterly MRD + bi-annual survey + annual PESTEL. Anything older than 6 months should trigger a "data is stale" warning on the dashboard.
