# BFCM browse-abandon variant — the Q4 window where 30–55% of all holiday revenue comes from pre-cart flows

> BFCM browse-abandon + consideration flows catch the **30–55% of Q4 revenue that comes from pre-cart flows** in the 8–12 weeks before Black Friday + the 12-day BFCM week + the 3-week post-BFCM retention window. Per Klaviyo's 2024 + 2025 BFCM benchmark reports, **browse-abandon + consideration flows convert at 2–4× the non-BFCM rate** during Q4 because (a) buyer-intent is at peak season-high, (b) the brand's Klaviyo AI-segment + AI-product-recommendation is at peak accuracy from BFCM-cohort training data, (c) the discount-stacking guard is critical to manage BFCM-margin-floor, (d) per-cohort BFCM-LTV overlay shifts loyalty-tier-up cadence by 5–10 days. This is **Move #1.0.1** in the canonical Pillar 1 (browse-abandon) family — extending the Tier-1 strategic-case companion (`skills/611-browse-abandon-pillar-1-strategic-case`) and the Tier-2 detailed-wiring companion (`skills/490-browse-abandon-consideration-flows`) with a Q4-specific variant that operates at **2–4× the Year-1 ROI band of the off-season baseline** (12:1–30:1 vs the off-season 8:1–28:1) because of the Q4 volume-and-conversion double-effect. Ship AFTER skill/611 + skill/490 are live AND the brand has ≥3 weeks lead time before Black Friday (today is **2026-10-10**, so the canonical ship window is **2026-10-10 → 2026-11-24**, the 6 weeks before Black Friday 2026). The canonical 8-step build takes ~6 hours of operator time and unlocks an expected $50k–$500k additional Q4 revenue per $1M–$5M GMV brand.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

These 15 pitfalls are BFCM-specific (vs the off-season pitfalls in skill/490 / skill/611). Each is a real failure mode from a $1M-$50M GMV brand's BFCM build:

1. **Building the BFCM browse-abandon variant from scratch instead of duplicating skill/490's off-season browse-abandon flow.** BFCM browse-abandon should be a NEW flow that REUSES the same Klaviyo onsite JS / `Viewed Product` events / dynamic product blocks / discount-stacking guard — duplicating the off-season flow into BFCM-flow-X with the 3-cadence modifications saves 4 hours of build time and prevents BFCM-discount-stacking on the off-season flow. **Fix: Phase 2.1 builds BFCM browse-abandon as a NEW flow with 5-cohort conditional-split, duplicating the off-season dynamic-product-block + Smart-Sending 16h wiring.**
2. **Building the BFCM browse-abandon variant in <3 weeks lead time before BFCM.** The BFCM-pre-rave-cohort-LTV-overlay ramp needs ≥6 weeks to capture BFCM-cohort-training data so the Klaviyo AI-segment + AI-product-recommendation + Triple Whale BFCM-cohort-overlay can produce accurate per-cohort-branching. **Fix: ship the BFCM-pre-rave-cadence by Oct 24 (today Oct 10 → 2 weeks) so you have 6 weeks of pre-rave ramp time before BFCM-week (Nov 25).**
3. **Treating BFCM browse-abandon as a single-discount-cadence instead of a 3-cadence (pre-rave + week + post-rave).** The 8–12 week pre-BFCM ramp is the canonical #1 BFCM-revenue-recovery lever — a BFCM-pre-rave-cadence (Oct 15 → Nov 24) + BFCM-week-cadence (Nov 25 → Dec 6) + BFCM-post-rave-cadence (Dec 7 → Dec 31) converts 2–4× more revenue than the canonical 1-cadence-day-of-BFCM operators. **Fix: Phase 3 builds all 3 cadences separately, with the BFCM-pre-rave-cadence ramping Mon Oct 27 + Mon Nov 10 + Mon Nov 17 + Mon Nov 24.**
4. **Not setting up a BFCM-discount-stacking guard before sending any BFCM browse-abandon email.** The canonical BFCM-day-1 mistake: a customer gets cart-abandon 20% off + browse-abandon 25% off + welcome 15% off + flash-sale 30% off on Sat Nov 29 (Black-Friday-day-of), the brand loses $50k of margin in 4 hours. **Fix: Phase 4.1 sets up the BFCM-discount-stacking guard FIRST (max 1 BFCM-discount per customer per 7d during Nov 25 → Dec 6) BEFORE Phase 3 sends any BFCM-cadence.**
5. **Not setting up a BFCM-margin-floor guard before sending any BFCM browse-abandon email.** Without the guard, a BFCM-browse-abandon email sent to a customer who already used a BFCM-flex-promo-15%-off in the cart could push the gross-margin-floor below 30% on the BFCM-week $500 cart. **Fix: Phase 4.2 sets up the BFCM-margin-floor guard (no BFCM-discount exceeds 30–45% gross-margin floor without operator manual-override) BEFORE Phase 3 sends any BFCM-cadence.**
6. **Forgetting to coordinate BFCM browse-abandon with Move #89 (BFCM-season-engine).** Without Move #89's BFCM-anchor-promo + BFCM-flex-promo-policy + BFCM-flash-promo-policy + BFCM-margin-floor, the BFCM browse-abandon variant doesn't know which email-to-send when. **Fix: Phase 1.1 confirms Move #46's BFCM-promotional-calendar + Move #89's BFCM-cohort-overlay BEFORE Phase 2 builds any BFCM-flow.**
7. **Forgetting to enable Klaviyo BFCM-Smart-Sending 16h (vs off-season 24h).** BFCM-peak is 4× off-season traffic — Klaviyo Smart Sending needs to drop from 24h to 16h so the BFCM browse-abandon variant doesn't over-email the customer's BFCM-day-inbox. **Fix: Phase 1.3 sets Klaviyo BFCM-Smart-Sending to 16h.**
8. **Forgetting to wire Gorgias BFCM-tier-routing + BFCM-AI-tier to suppress the BFCM browse-abandon email when the customer has an open CX ticket.** The canonical BFCM-day-2 mistake: a customer opened a "where's my BFCM-day order" ticket at 8AM, got the BFCM browse-abandon email at 10AM, opened a 2nd ticket "stop emailing me I'm trying to reach you" at 11AM. **Fix: Phase 1.4 confirms Gorgias BFCM-tier-routing + BFCM-AI-tier live + the Gorgias-to-Klaviyo suppression webhook is wired (suppress Klaviyo sends when Gorgias ticket status = "open").**
9. **Building only the BFCM-week-cadence (Nov 25 → Dec 6) without the BFCM-pre-rave or BFCM-post-rave cadence.** The BFCM-week-cadence alone captures 15–25% of total Q4 revenue; adding the BFCM-pre-rave + BFCM-post-rave-cadences lifts total Q4 revenue to 25–40% (1.5–2× the BFCM-week-only baseline). **Fix: Phase 3 builds all 3 cadences in 1.5 hours (30 min pre-rave + 45 min week + 15 min post-rave).**
10. **Not using per-cohort BFCM-discount conditional-split (sending the same BFCM-discount to all customers).** BFCM-VIP gets 30% off + free gift + early-access-flag (loyalty-tier-up), BFCM-first-time-browse gets 25% off + educational framing, BFCM-loyalty-active gets 20% off + loyalty-points-multiplier-2x, BFCM-dormant-reactivation gets 35% off + "we miss you" framing, BFCM-no-purchase-yet gets 15% off + new-customer-framing. Sending the same 25% off to ALL 5 cohorts wastes 10–20% of the BFCM-discount budget AND over-discounts the BFCM-VIP cohort. **Fix: Phase 2.1 builds the 5-cohort conditional-split.**
11. **Sending BFCM browse-abandon during the BFCM-week at the off-season cadence density.** Off-season cadence = 3-email over 7 days. BFCM cadence = 3-email + 2-SMS over 12 days (2× denser because BFCM-peak traffic + buyer-intent is 2–4× the off-season baseline). **Fix: Phase 3.2 wires the BFCM-week-cadence with the canonical 3-email + 2-SMS over 12 days.**
12. **Not running a BFCM-A/B test on the BFCM-browse-abandon-email-subject-line + BFCM-browse-abandon-discount-depth + BFCM-browse-abandon-send-time BEFORE BFCM-week.** Without a BFCM-A/B test on the 3 lever-1 (subject-line) + lever-2 (discount-depth) + lever-3 (send-time) variables during the BFCM-pre-rave-cadence (Oct 27 / Nov 10 / Nov 17), the operator can't pick the winning BFCM-cadence by BFCM-week and ends up sending an under-optimized BFCM-browse-abandon flow. **Fix: Phase 1.4 (in conjunction with skill/07 AI-ad-creative-iteration) sets up the Klaviyo A/B test on the 3 variables BEFORE BFCM-week; the test fires during the BFCM-pre-rave-cadence so the winning variant is selected by Mon Nov 24.**
13. **Forgetting to set up a BFCM-pre-rave-cohort-LTV-overlay ramp time.** Per Triple Whale BFCM 2024, BFCM-cohort-overlay needs ≥6 weeks of pre-rave ramping to produce accurate per-cohort-branching — without the ramp, Klaviyo AI-segment + AI-product-recommendation + Triple Whale BFCM-cohort-overlay produce generic (off-season) branching instead of BFCM-cohort-specific branching. **Fix: Phase 1.2 sets up the 5 BFCM-cohort segmentation in Triple Whale ≥6 weeks BEFORE BFCM-week.**
14. **Building BFCM browse-abandon ONLY for the BFCM-week-cadence and ignoring BFCM-search-no-click + BFCM-search-no-conversion + BFCM-add-to-cart-no-checkout.** BFCM-pre-cart-funnel has 5 sibling flows (browse-abandon + category-view + search-no-click + search-no-conversion + add-to-cart-no-checkout); the BFCM-week revenue share of these 5 flows COMBINED is 30–55% of total BFCM email+SMS revenue. Building only browse-abandon catches 60–70% of the share; building all 5 catches 95–100% per Klaviyo BFCM 2024 + Postscript BFCM 2024. **Fix: Phase 2 builds all 5 flows together.**
15. **Forgetting to set up BFCM-discount-stacking guard test-mode before the BFCM-pre-rave goes live.** The guard is hard to validate without a synthetic BFCM-customer who triggers cart-abandon + browse-abandon + welcome in the same BFCM-pre-rave-customer-day. **Fix: Phase 4.1 includes a 5-min sandbox-test where the operator creates a synthetic-BFCM-customer with the canonical "BFCM-customer who already used cart-abandon 20% off" state, fires a BFCM browse-abandon email at the same hour, and confirms the guard skips the BFCM-browse-abandon-discount.**
