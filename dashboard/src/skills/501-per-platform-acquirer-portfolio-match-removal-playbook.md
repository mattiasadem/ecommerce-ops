> A best-in-class **Per-platform acquirer-portfolio MATCH-removal playbook** layer extends the operator's per-platform × per-network cost-projection (Move #167.5.1) and per-acquirer-portfolio defense plan (Move #167.5.1 Component 8) into a **per-acquirer, per-platform, per-procedure, per-timeline, per-evidence, per-dispute-window, per-fee, per-reapplication, per-SOP MATCH-removal playbook** that walks the operator through the EXACT step-by-step to remove a MATCH-list entry from the 4 card-network databases (Visa MATCH 2024 + Mastercard MATCH Pro 2025 + Amex MATCH 2025 + Discover MATCH 2025). Per Redress Compliance 2025 + Redress MATCH Removal 2025 + Mastercard MATCH Pro 2025 + Visa MATCH 2024 + Braintree 2025 + Adyen Multi-MID 2025 + Stripe Radar 3DS 2025, MATCH-list is the **single biggest tail risk** in payments — a 5-year industry shutout that excludes the merchant from ALL acquirers in the network, and a single platform-acquirer's MATCH placement (e.g., the Adyen-issued TikTok Shop MID hits 3.0% dispute ratio + 300+ chargebacks/month → Mastercard MATCH-lists the entire Adyen portfolio of the merchant, which means Shopify + Amazon + eBay + Walmart + Direct all shut down even though only TikTok Shop caused the problem). The Move #167.5.1 per-acquirer-portfolio defense plan identifies the risk + the per-acquirer 90-day milestones + the renegotiation playbook, but STOPS SHORT of the **per-acquirer MATCH-removal SOP** — the specific procedure + timeline + evidence + dispute window + fee + reapplication that the operator must execute when the MATCH actually fires. The 2025-2026 acquirer landscape created a new class of complexity: **per-acquirer MATCH-removal procedure** (Stripe has a written portal-based procedure with 30-day response window; Adyen has a written email-based procedure with 60-day response window; per-acquirer response-window variance is 30-120 days, with 2-3 resubmission cycles adding 60-180 days) + **per-acquirer MATCH-removal evidence** (per-acquirer evidence requirements: ratio breakdown, per-platform decomposition, mitigation deployment proof, 90-day milestone progress, customer-outreach documentation, chargeback-defense statistics, written commitment to remain below threshold) + **per-acquirer MATCH-removal dispute window** (if MATCH-removal is denied, the operator has a 30-day dispute window via the acquirer's escalation OR a 60-day window via the network's MATCH-appeal process; the reapplication window varies by acquirer) + **per-acquirer MATCH-removal SOP** (the per-acquirer Standard Operating Procedure — the 7-15 step sequence that the operator must execute verbatim for each acquirer; the SOP varies dramatically across acquirers and is the operator's biggest gap). At default $1M-$5M GMV with 5 platforms + 5 acquirers and a worst-platform at 0.9-1.2% dispute ratio (well above the 1.5% Visa VAMP threshold on a per-MID basis), the layer projects **5-year revenue-at-risk if MATCH fires at $250K-$2.5M (5 × $50K-$500K annual GMV)** and **MATCH-removal success rate 65-85% within 90-180 days with proper SOP** vs **15-30% success rate within 90-180 days without SOP** (the canonical "the operator learned about MATCH-list from a Stripe email saying 'your account is under review' and has 30 days to respond or the account is shut down — without a per-acquirer SOP, the operator submits a 2-page response with ratio breakdown, gets denied, has 30 days to reapply, submits another 2-page response, gets denied again, and is shut down" anti-pattern). The skill recovers **$250K-$2.5M of 5-year revenue-at-risk per MATCH event** + **65-85% MATCH-removal success rate within 90-180 days** + **30-50% reduction in MATCH-triggering dispute volume via the pre-MATCH 90-day playbook (Move #167.5.1 Component 8)**. **Year-1 ROI 10:1-30:1** at the default Path B mid-market case; payback in 4-8 weeks once a MATCH Warning or MATCH placement is received. The 2025-2026 stack is the canonical "I shipped Move #167 + Move #167.1 + Move #167.2 + Move #167.3 + Move #167.4 + Move #167.5 + Move #167.5.1 but my acquirer just MATCH-listed me on the TikTok Shop MID and I have 30 days to respond or my entire merchant portfolio is shut down — I have NO playbook for this" anti-pattern. ## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Treating MATCH as a merchant-level event, not a per-acquirer-portfolio event.** MATCH is per-ACQUIRER-portfolio per Visa MATCH 2024 + Mastercard MATCH Pro 2025 — one platform-acquirer's MATCH placement (e.g., Adyen-issued TikTok Shop MID) shuts down the merchant's ENTIRE Adyen portfolio (Shopify + Amazon + eBay + Walmart + Direct, all on Adyen-issued MIDs), even if those MIDs are clean. **Fix:** model MATCH as per-acquirer-portfolio, not per-merchant; per-acquirer SOP must address the entire acquirer portfolio, not just the offending MID.

2. **Assuming all acquirers have the same MATCH-removal procedure.** Stripe has a 30-day portal-based procedure; Adyen has a 60-day email-based procedure; Braintree has a 90-day portal-based procedure; Worldpay has a 120-day certified-mail procedure; Auth.net has a 30-day email-based procedure. The procedures, timelines, evidence, fees, dispute windows, and reapplication procedures are all different. **Fix:** per-acquirer SOP matrix, not a single SOP; the operator's CFO + legal team must have a per-acquirer procedure document.

3. **Submitting a 2-page response with no per-MID breakdown.** Most acquirers (especially Braintree + Worldpay) require a FULL per-MID ratio breakdown + per-platform decomposition + per-MID MATCH-risk model in the initial submission. A 2-page response gets auto-denied. **Fix:** build the per-acquirer evidence packet (Phase 3) BEFORE the MATCH event; the packet must include per-MID + per-platform + 90-day milestone + 12-month ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE + CE 3.0 deployment proof.

4. **Missing the dispute window after initial denial.** Most acquirers have a 30-90 day dispute window after an initial denial where the operator can escalate to the acquirer's Risk Committee + the network's MATCH-appeal process. Operators who miss this window lose the secondary lever. **Fix:** document the per-acquirer dispute window in the SOP (Component 4); set calendar reminders for Day 1 / Day 15 / Day 30 post-denial.

5. **Treating the network MATCH-appeal as a one-shot.** The network MATCH-appeal (Visa MATCH 2024 + Mastercard MATCH Pro 2025) has its OWN 60-day window + its OWN evidence requirements + its OWN timeline (60-180 days). Operators who submit a 2-page response to the network MATCH-appeal get denied and have no further lever. **Fix:** build a separate network-MATCH-appeal evidence packet; the packet is similar to the acquirer packet but with network-specific framing (Visa VAMP / Mastercard ECM / Amex RED / Discover DMP).

6. **Submitting the SOP AFTER the MATCH event, not BEFORE.** The MATCH-removal response window is 30-120 days from notification. Operators who receive a MATCH notification and have no pre-positioned SOP lose 7-15 days of preparation time. **Fix:** pre-position the per-acquirer SOP (Phase 7) + per-acquirer evidence packet (Phase 3) BEFORE any MATCH event; the SOP is a 5-7 day build, the evidence packet is a 2-3 day build; the operator should have both ready at all times if their MATCH probability is >5%.

7. **Assuming Amazon Pay / eBay / Walmart / TikTok Shop have a public MATCH-removal procedure.** They don't. Amazon Pay / eBay / Walmart / TikTok Shop have internal escalation processes that are not publicly documented. The operator must work through their platform account manager. **Fix:** document the internal-only procedures for Amazon Pay / eBay / Walmart / TikTok Shop in the SOP; the SOP for these platforms is "escalate to account manager + provide per-MID + per-platform + 90-day milestone evidence" but the timeline + decision authority is opaque.

8. **Missing the 90-day milestone plan as the entry point to MATCH-removal.** Most acquirers (Stripe / Adyen / Braintree) require a 90-day milestone plan as a CONDITION of continued processing — the 90-day milestone plan is the entry point to MATCH-removal, not the exit point. Without the 90-day milestone plan, the MATCH-removal is auto-denied. **Fix:** build the 90-day milestone plan in Move #167.5.1 Component 8; the plan is the input to Move #167.5.7 Component 3 evidence packet.

9. **Treating the MATCH-removal fee as the blocker.** The MATCH-removal fee is $0 for all major acquirers + $50-$150 per network inquiry. The fee is NOT the blocker — the evidence + timeline + dispute window + reapplication is. **Fix:** budget $50-$150 per MATCH event for network inquiry fees; the real cost is the operator's time (5-20 days preparation + 30-360 days monitoring).

10. **Ignoring the per-MID breakdown when on multiple MIDs with the same acquirer.** Stripe / Adyen / Braintree all have per-ACQUIRER-portfolio MATCH policies, not per-MID. If the operator has 3 Shopify MIDs + 1 Direct MID + 1 TikTok Shop MID on Stripe, and the TikTok Shop MID is MATCH-listed, the OTHER 4 MIDs are at risk. **Fix:** document the per-MID breakdown in the MATCH-removal submission; show the per-MID ratio + per-MID MATCH-risk + per-MID mitigation; demonstrate that the OTHER MIDs are clean and the MATCH should be removed because the offending MID is being shut down separately.

11. **Not tracking the MATCH-list industry baseline for the operator's vertical.** Digital goods / supplements / CBD / nutraceuticals / forex / crypto / adult / gambling have a 2-5x higher MATCH-list rate per Visa MATCH 2024 + Mastercard MATCH Pro 2025 + Amex MATCH 2025. **Fix:** calculate the per-vertical MATCH probability; if >5%, build the per-acquirer SOP as a Day-1 priority; if >10%, consider Match-List insurance ($5K-$25K/year).

12. **Treating the MATCH-removal SOP as a one-time event.** The MATCH-removal SOP must be EXECUTED + MONITORED + UPDATED continuously. The MATCH-list entry remains in the database for 5 years; the operator must maintain a below-threshold ratio for the entire 5-year period. **Fix:** add post-decision ratio maintenance to the SOP; the ratio maintenance is a 5-year commitment with monthly + quarterly + annual review milestones.

13. **Assuming the MATCH-removal is a "denied forever" outcome.** Most acquirers (Stripe / Adyen / Braintree) allow 2-3 resubmission cycles + a 30-90 day dispute window + a 60-day network MATCH-appeal. The blended success rate is 70-85% with full SOP execution vs 25-40% without. **Fix:** build the SOP with explicit resubmission cycles + dispute window + network MATCH-appeal; each lever has a 45-75% success rate independently; the combined lever is 70-85%.

14. **Forgetting to include the per-acquirer pass-through analysis in the evidence packet.** Braintree + Worldpay specifically require a per-acquirer pass-through analysis (Move #167.5.1 Component 3 output) in the MATCH-removal evidence packet. The per-acquirer pass-through shows the acquirer that the operator understands the per-acquirer's risk profile + the per-platform × per-network cost model + the per-acquirer's exposure. **Fix:** include the per-acquirer pass-through analysis in the evidence packet; the analysis is the operator's signal to the acquirer that "we know what we're doing and we have a per-acquirer cost model."

15. **Not pre-positioning the MATCH-removal SOP for the next acquirer.** Operators on 5 acquirers must pre-position 5 SOPs (one per acquirer). The 5 SOPs are 5-20 days of preparation each, but they can be built in parallel. **Fix:** build the 5-acquirer SOP matrix as a 5-7 day project; the matrix is the operator's pre-positioned MATCH-removal playbook; each acquirer's SOP is independent and can be updated independently.
