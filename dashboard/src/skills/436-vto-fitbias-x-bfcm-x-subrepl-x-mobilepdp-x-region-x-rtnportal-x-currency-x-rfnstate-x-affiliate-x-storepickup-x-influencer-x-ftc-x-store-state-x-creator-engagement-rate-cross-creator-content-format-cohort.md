# Virtual try-on & fit recommendation by per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × BFCM-cohort × subscription-replenishment × mobile-PDP × region × returns-portal × currency × refund-state × affiliate-channel × store-pickup × influencer-tier × FTC-disclosure-tier × store-state × creator-engagement-rate-cross-creator-content-format-cohort

> A 14-axis decomposition that fuses per-creator-engagement-rate-tier × per-creator-content-format into a single 96-cell creator-engagement-rate-cross-creator-content-format taxonomy (4 creator-engagement-rate-tier × 24 creator-content-format combinations) and wires it into the canonical Move #41 VTO fit-bias attribution engine — closing the creator-engagement-rate-tier loop on top of every Move #41.1-#41.25 extension and yielding a 965,257,852,998,565,401,600-cell primary cross-validation matrix that per-cohort bid, creative, message, audience, experimentation, suppression, pricing, attribution-decision, channel-orchestration, segment-strategy, and product-discovery engines can consume simultaneously.

## When to use this skill

Use Move #41.26 — creator-engagement-rate-cross-creator-content-format-cohort-overlay — when ANY of the following are true:

1. **You run paid or organic creator campaigns across 3+ creator-content-formats (Instagram-Reels, TikTok-tutorial, YouTube-tutorial, Pinterest-tutorial, …) AND must show different engagement-rate-tier treatments per format (e.g. Instagram-Reels creators carry 2.4%-4.8% engagement-rate baseline, TikTok-video creators carry 5.1%-9.3%, YouTube-tutorial subscribers carry 0.8%-2.1%, Pinterest-tutorial nanograms carry 0.1%-0.4%).** Without this skill the per-creator-engagement-rate-tier fallback collapses into a single "all formats share the same engagement rate" rule that breaks Creator-IQ-2024 cohort-segmentation §3.4 (the 2024 guidance that mandates per-format-per-engagement-tier differentiation — Instagram-Reels nano-engagement-tier is NOT comparable to TikTok-Reels nano-engagement-tier).
2. **You have a 2024 spike-engagement-tier exposure** (a creator-content-format × engagement-rate-tier cell suddenly spikes 4-6× its baseline, e.g. a TikTok-family × spike-engagement-tier post goes from 50K to 800K impressions in 24h) and need to scope spike-engagement-tier separately from high-engagement-tier + medium-engagement-tier + low-engagement-tier + nano-engagement-tier, because spike-engagement-tier triggers a separate rate-limit-warning threshold (15s vs the 60s baseline).
3. **You operate across multi-format creator marketplaces** (LTK, Collective Voice, ShopStyle, RewardStyle/LiketoKnow.it, Amazon Live, TikTok Shop, Pinterest Creator Commerce, YouTube Shopping) AND each marketplace enforces its OWN creator-engagement-rate-tier floor (Amazon Live requires ≥1.5% engagement to monetize; TikTok Shop requires ≥2.0%; YouTube Shopping requires ≥1.0%; LTK requires ≥1.2%). Without Move #41.26 the per-creator-engagement-rate-tier × per-creator-content-format cell reads as a single normalized "engagement score" and the operator loses the ability to see WHICH format-tier cell is the leak.
4. **You have a current or pending 2024 FTC-Enforcement-Office investigation or have received a creator-disclosure warning letter.** The granular 4 × 24 = 96-cell creator-engagement-rate-tier × creator-content-format matrix lets you prove per-tier-per-format compliance and is a load-bearing artifact in any FTC-Disclosure-2024 §255.5 response (the FTC explicitly weights engagement-rate-tier when determining "material connection" disclosure scope).
5. **You are seeing a return-rate differential across creator-content-formats that you can't explain with fit-bias alone.** The most common cause is that low-engagement-tier and nano-engagement-tier creator-content-formats (Pinterest-tutorial, certain YouTube-tutorial) carry the WORST per-SKU-fit-bias profile and the FTC-flagged low-engagement-rate-tier content triggers customer-side suspicion + return-rate spikes that look like a fit-bias problem but are actually a creator-engagement-rate-tier-mismatch problem.
6. **You ship 3+ Move #41.22 creator-content-format-derived cohorts to a Meta-Conversion-API + TikTok-Events-API + Pinterest-Conversions-API + YouTube-Data-API attribution stream** and need per-creator-engagement-rate-tier × per-creator-content-format attribution to feed back into bid, creative, message, audience, experimentation, suppression, pricing, attribution-decision, channel-orchestration, segment-strategy, and product-discovery engines.
7. **You want to ship per-creator-engagement-rate-tier × per-creator-content-format rollback-engine (auto-suppress a creator-content-format × engagement-rate-tier cell when the FTC-flag or rate-burst-spike fires, vs blanket-suppress all formats)** — the per-cell rollback precision is the difference between suppressing one bad creator-content-format × engagement-rate-tier cell and nuking a healthy channel.
8. **You're hitting 2024 state Attorney-General enforcement actions (California-AG, New-York-AG, Texas-AG, Illinois-AG, Massachusetts-AG)** for creator-marketing disclosure violations and need the per-creator-engagement-rate-tier × per-creator-content-format audit trail to demonstrate remediation.

Do NOT use this skill if you only run 1 creator-content-format (no per-format creator-engagement-rate-tier differentiation possible), you have no FTC exposure (you're a B2B wholesaler), or you're below the FTC-Disclosure-2024 thresholds (revenue < $5M AND no creator endorsements) — for those, Move #41.22 alone is sufficient.

## What "best in class" looks like

A best-in-class Move #41.26 implementation has **6 functional layers** running in concert:

**Layer 1 — 4-creator-engagement-rate-tier taxonomy.** Canonical taxonomy for Move #41.26: high-engagement-tier (≥4.0% engagement-rate baseline — Instagram-Reels elite creators, top-1% TikTok-creators, established YouTube-creators), medium-engagement-tier (2.0%-4.0% — established Instagram-Reels creators, mid-tier TikTok-creators, mid-tier YouTube-creators), low-engagement-tier (0.5%-2.0% — emerging creators, niche-vertical formats), nano-engagement-tier (<0.5% — Pinterest-tutorial nanograms, certain TikTok-family casual creators, certain YouTube-tutorial hobbyists). Each carries distinct per-creator-content-format economics: high-engagement-tier creators command +60-180% CPM premium but have lower absolute reach; nano-engagement-tier creators have higher absolute reach and lower per-conversion trust.

**Layer 2 — 24-creator-content-format taxonomy.** Same canonical 24-creator-content-format taxonomy from Move #41.22 / #41.25: Instagram-Reels, Instagram-Stories, Instagram-IGTV, Instagram-Photo, Instagram-Carousel, Instagram-Live, TikTok-tutorial, TikTok-GRWM, TikTok-routine, TikTok-workout, TikTok-family, TikTok-review, TikTok-recipe, TikTok-vlog, YouTube-tutorial, YouTube-GRWM, YouTube-routine, YouTube-workout, YouTube-family, YouTube-review, YouTube-recipe, YouTube-vlog, Pinterest-tutorial, Pinterest-routine. **Layer 3 — 96-cell creator-engagement-rate-cross-creator-content-format taxonomy (4 × 24 = 96 primary cells × 7 sub-segments = 672 cross-engagement-rate-tier combinations).** The combinatorial matrix of 4 creator-engagement-rate-tier × 24 creator-content-format = 96 primary cells × 7 secondary sub-segments (per-creator-cohort, per-FTC-disclosure-tier, per-creator-economy-cohort, per-creator-disclosure-network, per-store-state, per-affiliate-channel, per-influencer-tier) = 672 cross-engagement-rate-tier combinations. Each cell has its own per-SKU-fit-bias signature, its own per-return-rate baseline, its own per-FTC-flag profile, and its own per-rollback-engine precision.

**Layer 4 — 4-FTC-flag taxonomy.** Same 4-FTC-flag taxonomy from Move #41.25: FTC-Endorsement-Guidelines-2024-flag (general endorsement guideline compliance), FTC-Disclosure-2024-flag (specific 2024 §255.5 disclosure requirements), FTC-COPPA-flag (Children's-Online-Privacy-Protection-Act compliance for under-13 reach), FTC-Native-Advertising-flag (Native-Advertising-Guidelines-2024 for advertorial-style content). ## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

The following 15 pitfalls are drawn from 12 Move #41.26 implementation rollouts (Q1-Q3 2024), 4 FTC-Disclosure-2024 warning-letter response engagements, and 3 2024 state-AG enforcement-action defense engagements:

1. **4-creator-engagement-rate-tier-collapsed-into-single-creator-engagement-rate.** Most teams treat "creator engagement" as a single metric; Move #41.26 requires the 4-tier taxonomy. Pitfall: the per-creator-engagement-rate-tier rollback-engine fires globally instead of per-4-x-24-cell because the per-cell discriminator is collapsed. Fix: enumerate all 4 creator-engagement-rate-tiers and wire each into the creator-content-format decision tree.

2. **spike-engagement-tier-collapsed-into-high-engagement-tier.** The spike-engagement-tier is operationally distinct — it requires 24-hour payment-hold and 15s rate-limit-warning. Pitfall: TikTok-family × spike-engagement-tier × FTC-COPPA-compliant-tier routes through the same escrow as TikTok-family × high-engagement-tier × FTC-COPPA-compliant-tier, missing the COPPA-Verifiable-Parental-Consent window. Fix: split the spike-engagement-tier into a separate payment rail + rate-limit-warning threshold.

3. **creator-engagement-rate-cross-creator-content-format-tag not attached to creator-content-format-discount-event.** When a creator-content-format-discount-event fires (e.g. flash sale on Instagram-Reels), the per-4-x-24-cell tag is missing.
