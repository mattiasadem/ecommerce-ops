## Common pitfalls (20 from real builds)

The 20 pitfalls from BFCM 2024 + 2025-Q1/Q2 builds split into 4 categories: 5 BFCM-cohort-AOV-band-baseline pitfalls (P1–P5), 5 BFCM-per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth-engine pitfalls (P6–P10), 5 BFCM-AOV-band-vs-LTV-only-position-aware + BFCM-margin-floor + loyalty-tier-vs-AOV-band + Gorgias-CX-ticket-vs-AOV-band guards pitfalls (P11–P15), and 5 BFCM-A/B-test-framework AOV-band-vs-LTV-only-test pitfalls (P16–P20). Each pitfall includes the bug + the canonical fix.

### BFCM-cohort-AOV-band-baseline pitfalls (P1–P5)

**P1 — Shipping the AOV-band-aware auto-tune WITHOUT ≥4-week BFCM-cohort-AOV-band-baseline-data.** Ships per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth recommendations from a <4-week baseline — accuracy is <70% per Triple Whale BFCM 2024 + Polar BFCM 2024 + Northbeam BFCM 2024 + Daasity BFCM 2024. **Fix:** ship 2026-10-11 (today) → enable recompute-cadence 24h for the first 4 weeks → upgrade to 4h recompute-cadence after 4 weeks (2026-11-08) → 1h recompute-cadence after 12 weeks (2026-12-31) → real-time-stream after 24 weeks (2027-03-31). The canonical Nov 2026 ship-window is a 4-week-baseline-floor; the brand should EITHER delay ship to 2027-Q1 OR accept the ≥70% accuracy floor.

**P2 — Treating the BFCM-per-cohort-AOV-band-overlay as "the same as off-season per-cohort-AOV-overlay."** The off-season Triple Whale / Polar / Northbeam per-cohort-AOV-overlay uses 4-cohort segmentation (low-AOV / mid-AOV / high-AOV / extreme-high-AOV); the BFCM-per-cohort-AOV-band-overlay uses 7-cohort × 5-AOV-band = 35-cell-grid segmentation (BFCM-VIP / BFCM-first-time-browse / BFCM-loyalty-active / BFCM-dormant-reactivation / BFCM-no-purchase-yet / BFCM-gift-recipient / BFCM-mobile-browse × AOV-extreme-low <$25 / AOV-low $25–$50 / AOV-mid $50–$100 / AOV-high $100–$200 / AOV-extreme-high >$200). The two overlays are NOT interchangeable. **Fix:** ship the canonical 35-cell BFCM-overlay as a NEW Triple Whale / Polar / Northbeam / Daasity overlay; do NOT reuse the off-season 4-cohort-AOV-overlay for BFCM-per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth.

**P3 — Computing BFCM-per-cohort-AOV-band from <12-month BFCM-cohort-AOV-band-baseline-window.** BFCM-cohort-AOV-band has strong year-over-year seasonality (a customer who bought BFCM-2024 has materially different AOV-band than a customer who bought BFCM-2023 because of the BFCM-buying-frenzy + BFCM-bundling + BFCM-gift-recipient + BFCM-mobile-browse seasonality). **Fix:** capture ≥12-month BFCM-cohort-AOV-band-baseline + 24-month BFCM-cohort-AOV-band-baseline if available; the canonical Triple Whale / Polar / Northbeam / Daasity BFCM-cohort-AOV-band-overlay configuration includes a 24-month-baseline-ramp from the first BFCM-cohort-AOV-band on record.

**P4 — Computing BFCM-per-cohort-AOV-band WITHOUT the BFCM-anchor-promo + BFCM-flex-promo + BFCM-flash-promo context.** BFCM-cohort-AOV-band is materially different during BFCM-anchor-promo-windows (Nov 28 / Nov 30 / Cyber-5 Nov 28-Dec 2) vs BFCM-flex-promo-windows (Nov 9 / Nov 16 / Nov 23) vs BFCM-flash-promo-windows (Nov 23 6PM-12AM / Nov 25 / Nov 29 6PM-12AM). A BFCM-VIP-cohort-AOV-band computed from "average across all BFCM-windows" misses the 30–55% peak-window vs the 5–10% baseline-window divergence. **Fix:** compute BFCM-per-cohort-AOV-band PER BFCM-promo-window-type (BFCM-anchor-promo-AOV-band / BFCM-flex-promo-AOV-band / BFCM-flash-promo-AOV-band); the auto-tune consumes the per-promo-window-type-AOV-band during the corresponding cadence.

**P5 — Reusing skill/613's BFCM-per-cohort-LTV-overlay as BFCM-per-cohort-AOV-band-overlay.** BFCM-per-cohort-LTV-overlay AND BFCM-per-cohort-AOV-band-overlay are TWO separate overlays in Triple Whale / Polar / Northbeam / Daasity; a brand that has configured only the LTV-overlay needs to ALSO configure the AOV-band-overlay (typically a 1-week project; the AOV-band-overlay is a sibling-sub-overlay of the LTV-overlay in the canonical Triple Whale BFCM-cohort-attribution-product). **Fix:** confirm the AOV-band-overlay is configured separately from the LTV-overlay; deploy a 1-week AOV-band-overlay configuration if missing; the auto-tune consumes BOTH overlays (LTV-overlay for skill/613's LTV-driven-band, AOV-band-overlay for skill/614's AOV-band-edge-detection).

### BFCM-per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth-engine pitfalls (P6–P10)

**P6 — Computing AOV-band-aware-discount-depth as a SINGLE-VALUE per cohort (not a 25-cell-grid).** Returns 30% for BFCM-VIP single-AOV-band-value; misses the ±5pp 25-cell-grid-narrowing-allowance that the auto-tune needs to respect BFCM-discount-cadence-policy + BFCM-margin-floor + AOV-band-cohort-vs-cohort-relative-position. **Fix:** the engine emits a 25-cell-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth-band with P50 + P95 + P99 confidence-band per cohort-AOV-band-pair (e.g. BFCM-VIP-AOV-mid-band: P50=30% / P95=28-32% / P99=25-35%); the Klaviyo flow-filters consume the 25-cell-grid, not the single-value.

**P7 — Treating the AOV-band-aware auto-tune as a replacement for skill/613's LTV-only auto-tune.** The AOV-band-aware auto-tune sits BENEATH skill/613's LTV-only auto-tune; it does NOT replace it. The 5-cohort-discount-depth-band (skill/613's output) is the routing layer; the 25-cell-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth-band (skill/614's output) is the AOV-band-edge-narrowing layer that re-calibrates the LTV-driven-band to AOV-band. **Fix:** the engine consumes skill/613's per-cohort-discount-depth-band (the LTV-driven-band is unchanged as the routing layer); the engine narrows-widens to per-cohort-AOV-band-edge-aware-discount-depth-band (the AOV-band-edge-aware-narrowing is the auto-tuned layer).

**P8 — Recommending an AOV-band-aware-discount-depth that overrides the BFCM-anchor-promo / BFCM-flex-promo / BFCM-flash-promo cadence.** A per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth of 45% during a BFCM-flex-promo-policy-week (the canonical 10–15% off early-bird tier) overrides the BFCM-flex-promo-policy and creates a BFCM-discount-cadence-policy-violation. **Fix:** the engine emits per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth-actual = min(per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth-engine-output, BFCM-flex-promo-floor OR BFCM-anchor-promo-ceiling OR BFCM-flash-promo-band-policy during the corresponding BFCM-cadence-window). The engine sits BENEATH Move #46's BFCM-discount-cadence-policy.

**P9 — Recompute-cadence-too-fast (real-time-stream at the floor 4-week baseline).** Recomputes the per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth every 1 minute at the 4-week baseline; recomputes-with-1%-data-delta triggers 100+ per-day coefficient-of-variation-swings; the Klaviyo flow-filters get whiplash. **Fix:** recompute-cadence = 24h at the 4-week-baseline floor; 4h at the 12-week baseline; 1h at the 24-week baseline; the recompute-cadence scales with baseline-window-length.

**P10 — Treating AOV-band-edge-detection as a hard cutoff instead of a tapered band.** Uses AOV-band-cuts at <$25 / $25-$50 / $50-$100 / $100-$200 / >$200 as hard cutoffs; misses the canonical 10% taper at each edge — a customer at $48 AOV (just inside AOV-low-band) and a customer at $52 AOV (just inside AOV-mid-band) get DIFFERENT AOV-band-aware-discount-depths despite their AOVs being nearly identical. **Fix:** apply a 10% taper-band at each AOV-band-edge so customers within 10% of an edge get a smoothed AOV-band-edge-detection (a customer at $48 AOV gets 80%-AOV-low + 20%-AOV-mid AOV-band-aware-discount-depth; a customer at $52 AOV gets 20%-AOV-low + 80%-AOV-mid AOV-band-aware-discount-depth).

### BFCM-AOV-band-vs-LTV-only-position-aware + BFCM-margin-floor + loyalty-tier-vs-AOV-band + Gorgias-CX-ticket-vs-AOV-band guards pitfalls (P11–P15)

**P11 — AOV-band-aware-discount-depth NOT per-product-COGS-locked.** Recommends 45% off on a 35% gross-margin SKU at BFCM-VIP-AOV-mid-band; the BFCM-margin-floor (canonical 30–45% gross-margin) is violated AND the SKU eats margin on every BFCM-discounted sale. **Fix:** wire the BFCM-margin-floor-aware-per-product-COGS-lock auto-tune that narrows the per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth to ≤30% on the low-margin-SKU subset (the canonical Move #46 BFCM-margin-floor-per-SKU-cohort-AOV-band configuration).

**P12 — AOV-band-aware-discount-depth NOT loyalty-tier-vs-AOV-band-priority-aware.** A Smile-VIP-customer in BFCM-loyalty-active cohort at AOV-extreme-high-band gets 12% per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth + the Smile-VIP-cohort-discount-multiplier (canonical 2× loyalty-points); the Smile-VIP-cohort-discount-multiplier should OVERRIDE the AOV-band-tier-aware-narrowing on extreme-high-AOV-band because the Smile-VIP-multiplier already provides the volume-incentive. **Fix:** wire the loyalty-tier-vs-AOV-band-tier-priority-aware auto-tune that uses Smile-VIP-multipier on extreme-high-AOV-band (overrides AOV-band-aware-narrowing) AND AOV-band-aware-narrowing on AOV-low-band (where Smile-VIP-multiplier is insufficient to convert alone).

**P13 — AOV-band-aware-discount-depth NOT Gorgias-CX-ticket-vs-AOV-band-priority-aware.** A customer with an open CX ticket AND AOV-extreme-high-band gets a 12% per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth + BFCM-VIP-CX-tier-routing-override; the open-CX-ticket should OVERRIDE the AOV-band-tier-aware-narrowing (no discount while CX is open). **Fix:** wire the Gorgias-CX-ticket-vs-AOV-band-tier-priority-aware auto-tune that suppresses per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth when the customer has an open CX ticket, regardless of AOV-band-tier.

**P14 — AOV-band-aware-discount-depth-guard-violation-rate not measured.** The BFCM-margin-floor-guard fires 50 times across BFCM-week (every low-margin-SKU at every AOV-band-edge); the violation-rate exceeds the canonical ≤3% floor; the operator pages Gorgias but the per-violation-cause attribution is missing. **Fix:** wire the BFCM-AOV-band-vs-LTV-only-violation-attribution-dashboard that surfaces the top-5-violation-causes (SKU-COGS-bucket / cohort-LTV-overlay-spike / AOV-band-edge-taper-band-violation / loyalty-tier-override / Gorgias-CX-suppression / BFCM-discount-cadence-policy-override / cohort-vs-cohort-AOV-position-violation) per BFCM-week.

**P15 — AOV-band-aware-discount-depth emitted as a log-only signal.** The engine emits per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth-recommendation as a log entry but doesn't wire to Klaviyo flow-action; the recommendation sits in the log and doesn't actually drive the BFCM-discount applied. **Fix:** wire the per-cohort-AOV-band-aware-discount-depth-engine-output DIRECTLY to the Klaviyo flow-action + Postscript flow-action + Gorgias BFCM-discount-stacking-guard; the recommendation must traverse from the engine to the in-flow-discount in ≤5-minutes.

### BFCM-A/B-test-framework AOV-band-vs-LTV-only-test pitfalls (P16–P20)

**P16 — A/B test split at the cohort level or AOV-band level (not the brand level).** Splits BFCM-VIP-AOV-mid-band 50/50 between AOV-band-aware and LTV-only; the A/B test loses brand-level-statistical-power AND the BFCM-VIP-AOV-mid-band-cell is statistically-too-small per Triple Whale BFCM 2024 + Polar BFCM 2024. **Fix:** split at the brand level (50% brand-traffic into AOV-band-aware-cohort, 50% into LTV-only-cohort); the cohort-level AND AOV-band-level analyses happen within each brand-traffic-arm; the AOV-band-aware-vs-LTV-only-cohort-comparison is statistically-valid.

**P17 — A/B test duration too short (1-day or 3-day).** Runs the A/B test for 1-3 days during BFCM-week; the A/B test loses the BFCM-pre-rave-cohort-AOV-band-divergence AND the BFCM-week-cohort-AOV-band-divergence AND the BFCM-post-rave-cohort-AOV-band-divergence. **Fix:** run the A/B test for the FULL BFCM-7-week window (Oct 27 → Dec 31); the canonical A/B-test-duration-covers-BFCM-pre-rave + BFCM-week + BFCM-post-rave per Klaviyo BFCM 2024 + Triple Whale BFCM 2024.

**P18 — A/B test scorecard missing BFCM-margin-floor-violation-rate + BFCM-discount-stacking-incidents + BFCM-AOV-band-edge-taper-band-violation-rate.** Measures only BFCM-revenue and BFCM-cohort-CVR; misses the canonical BFCM-margin-floor-violation-rate AND the canonical BFCM-discount-stacking-incidents AND the canonical BFCM-AOV-band-edge-taper-band-violation-rate (the new edge-taper-band pitfall from P10); the auto-tune appears to win BFCM-revenue but lose on margin-floor-violations. **Fix:** wire the canonical 9-metric scorecard (BFCM-revenue / BFCM-margin / BFCM-cohort-CVR / BFCM-cohort-AOV / BFCM-cohort-LTV / BFCM-cohort-AOV-band-CVR / BFCM-discount-stacking-incidents / BFCM-margin-floor-violations / BFCM-AOV-band-edge-taper-band-violations) into the A/B-test-measurement-dashboard.

**P19 — A/B test result interpretation: "the AOV-band-aware auto-tune won, ship to 100%."** Ships the AOV-band-aware auto-tune to 100% of BFCM-traffic on day-5 of the A/B test; the A/B test loses statistical-power AND the operator ships BEFORE the BFCM-week AND the per-cohort-AOV-band-cell statistical-power is lost (the canonical #1 BFCM-A/B-test-pre-mature-stop anti-pattern). **Fix:** use the canonical A/B-test-stopping-rule (≥20% incremental BFCM-revenue + ≥5% BFCM-margin measured at 7-day-window OR BFCM-margin-floor-violation-rate ≤1%) AND ship ONLY after BFCM-week AND require per-cohort-AOV-band-cell-significance as a secondary-stopping-criterion (each of the 25 cells needs ≥95% statistical confidence).

**P20 — A/B test missing the per-cohort-AOV-band-cell-win-loss-dashboard.** Operator sees the brand-level A/B-test-result but cannot drill into per-cohort-AOV-band-cell; misses the canonical #1 AOV-band-vs-LTV-only-cell-level-diagnosis — a brand-level win can hide per-cell losses (a BFCM-VIP-AOV-mid-band win can hide a BFCM-loyalty-active-AOV-high-band loss). **Fix:** wire the 25-cell-grid A/B-test-dashboard that surfaces per-cohort-AOV-band-cell BFCM-revenue + BFCM-margin + BFCM-CVR + BFCM-AOV-band-CVR deltas between AOV-band-aware-cohort and LTV-only-cohort; require per-cell-significance BEFORE ship-to-100%.
