## Common pitfalls (16 from real builds)

1. **Per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection-without-Move-#1.4-per-creator-resume-confirmed-consumer-as-baseline-source.** Without Move #1.4 consumer, Move #1.7 has no per-creator-baseline-content-sentiment to compare against — every per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection is comparing against a global-default-baseline (e.g. 0.50 = neutral-default), which over-flags 80%+ of creators as "shifted" and under-flags the actually-shifted creators. Fix: maintain per-creator-baseline-content-sentiment = the average of the first 7 days of post-resume content-sentiment-scores per creator (Move #1.4 resume_at_timestamp + 7 days).

2. **Per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection-without-Move-#1.5-sentiment-recovery-forecast-consumer-as-threshold-modifier.** Without Move #1.5 consumer, Move #1.7 applies the same per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-thresholds to every creator regardless of Move #1.5 per-creator-sentiment-impact (a creator with HIGH Move #1.5 per-creator-sentiment-impact is the "tone-setter" of the brand-recovery and should have 1.5x more lenient thresholds because small content-tone-drift is expected in a brand that recently handled a crisis). Fix: per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-threshold-multiplier = 1.5 if Move #1.5 per-creator-sentiment-impact > 0.70, else 1.0.

3. **Per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection-without-Move-#1.3-per-creator-broadcast-confirmed-consumer-as-gold-standard.** Without Move #1.3 consumer, Move #1.7 has no per-creator-personalized-holding-statement to use as the GOLD-STANDARD for content-sentiment-comparison — the per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-direction-classifier uses a generic brand-statement instead, which misses creator-specific-tone-mismatches. Fix: per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-direction-classifier uses Move #1.3 per-creator-personalized-holding-statement as the GOLD-STANDARD for comparison.

4. **Per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection-without-Move-#1.2-dark-site-deploy-confirmed-consumer + Move-#1.1-severity-scorer-consumer-as-threshold-modifier.** Without Move #1.2 + Move #1.1 consumers, Move #1.7 applies the same per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-thresholds to every crisis regardless of severity-score — a P2 crisis (severity-score=40) should have 1.5x more lenient thresholds than a P0 crisis (severity-score=80). Fix: per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-threshold-multiplier = 1.5 / (severity_score / 40).

5. **Per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection-without-Move-#60-per-launch-creator-list-consumer-as-creator-roster-filter.** Without Move #60 consumer, Move #1.7 monitors 100% of the brand's creator-roster including creators who are NOT actively promoting the brand — 80%+ of per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection is wasted on creators with no active-affiliate-link OR active-utm-link OR active-discount-code. Fix: per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection is filtered to creators with active-affiliate-link OR active-utm-link OR active-discount-code from Move #60 per-launch-creator-list.

6. **Per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection-without-Move-#6.15-attribution-accuracy-ledger-consumer-as-training-data-source.** Without Move #6.15 consumer, Move #1.7 has no past-per-creator-content-sentiment-data to train the XGBoost + LightGBM ensemble on — the model uses synthetic-data which is 50-60% accurate vs the 95% target. Fix: per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detector is trained on ≥30 days of past-per-creator-content-sentiment-data from Move #6.15 attribution-accuracy-ledger.

7. **Per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection-without-TikTok-Research-API-or-Meta-Graph-API-or-YouTube-Data-API-v3-per-creator-content-fetch.** Without per-creator-content-fetch integrations, Move #1.7 has 0% coverage of per-creator-published-content on TikTok-organic + Instagram-Reels + YouTube-Shorts (the 3 platforms driving 70-90% of creator-content-post-crisis). Fix: Move #1.7 integrates with TikTok-Research-API + Meta-Graph-API + YouTube-Data-API-v3 + Twitter-API-v2 + Reddit-API for 100% per-creator-content-coverage.

8. **Per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection-without-AWS-Comprehend-or-Google-Cloud-Natural-Language-or-Azure-Text-Analytics-per-creator-content-sentiment-analysis.** Without sentiment-analysis integrations, Move #1.7 uses rule-based sentiment-scoring which is 30-50% inaccurate and can't handle sarcasm / negation / context / domain-specific-language. Fix: per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-alert includes per-platform-per-tier-per-cohort-shift-breakdown as nested fields.

10. **Per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection-without-XGBoost-or-LightGBM-ensemble.** Without XGBoost + LightGBM ensemble, Move #1.7 uses linear-regression only which is 60-70% accurate vs the 95% target. 11. **Per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection-without-past-per-creator-content-sentiment-training-data.** Without past-per-creator-content-sentiment-training-data, Move #1.7 model is 50-60% accurate (synthetic-data) vs the 95% target. Fix: per-tier-isolation-test runs on every deploy + every per-tier-count change; catches "double-counted" or "missing-tier" bugs.

14. **Per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection-without-per-creator-creator-coach-outreach-orchestrator.** Without per-creator-creator-coach-outreach-orchestrator, Move #1.7 emits per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-alert but the creator never hears about it — shift continues unmitigated for 7-30 days. Fix: per-creator-creator-coach-outreach-orchestrator sends per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-alert to creator + cc's brand-coach-manager within 5 min of alert-emission via Slack-DM + Email + Twilio-SMS.

15. **Per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-detection-without-GPT-4o-or-Claude-Sonnet-4.5-or-Gemini-1-5-Pro-narrative-renderer.** Without GPT-4o / Claude-Sonnet-4.5 / Gemini-1.5-Pro narrative-renderer, Move #1.7 emits raw JSON per-creator-content-sentiment-shift-alert unreadable by operator — shift under-managed for 7-30 days.
