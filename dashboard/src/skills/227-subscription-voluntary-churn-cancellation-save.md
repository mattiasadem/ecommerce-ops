# Subscription voluntary churn prevention + cancellation save-flow (pause-as-a-recovery-strategy)

> Move #14.2.3 is the canonical **voluntary-churn-prevention + cancellation-save-flow layer** every DTC subscription brand needs AFTER Move #14.2 (post-charge dunning) AND Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) AND Move #14.2.2 (cross-border dunning) are live: deploy a **Recharge + Stay AI + Skio + Bold Subscriptions + Loop Subscriptions + Smartrr + Appstle + Okendo Subscriptions + Klaviyo + Postscript + Triple Whale** pause-as-a-recovery-strategy engine that converts **20–35% of would-be voluntary cancellations into retained subscribers** (the canonical Recharge 2024 + ProfitWell 2024 + Stay AI 2024 voluntary-churn-save benchmark — driven primarily by pause-instead-of-cancel + skip-instead-of-cancel + swap-instead-of-cancel + discount-instead-of-cancel + multi-tier 30d / 60d / 90d pause option + automated pause-then-resume nurture + cancel-flow-survey-driven personalization), handles the **65–72% of subscription churn that is voluntary vs 28–35% involuntary** (the canonical Recharge 2024 + ProfitWell 2024 voluntary-vs-involuntary-churn split), and converts voluntary churn into **retained MRR of 8–18% of monthly subscription revenue** BEFORE the cancellation completes. **Stay AI Pause + Recharge Pause + multi-tier 30d / 60d / 90d + automated pause-then-resume nurture** is the canonical Path A pick for Shopify-DTC; **Skio smart-cancellation + Recharge cancel-flow-survey + Klaviyo pause-then-resume flow** is the canonical Path B pick for $1M+ GMV brands with Klaviyo-native stack; **Chargebee + Recurly + custom-cancel-flow + Triple Whale cohort-LTV feedback-loop** is the canonical Path C pick for $5M+ GMV multi-platform brands; **Bold Subscriptions + custom-built pause-and-save-flow + Triple Whale + Postscript SMS-first** is the canonical Path D pick for $100k–$500k GMV brands with tight operator capacity. Year-1 ROI band **9:1–32:1** with a default **18:1** at $1M subscription GMV Path A. Ship AFTER Move #14.2 (post-charge dunning) shipped ≥90 days AND Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) shipped ≥60 days AND Move #14.2.2 (cross-border dunning) shipped ≥30 days AND ≥300 active subscribers AND a baseline voluntary-churn-rate exists AND a baseline MRR-dashboard exists, in a 4-phase build over 14–28 days. Companion artifact scope: this skill synthesizes the canonical 6-pillar voluntary-churn-prevention + cancellation-save-flow framework (Pillar 1 pre-cancellation-engagement-+-save-the-sale-engine / Pillar 2 pause-as-a-recovery-strategy-+-multi-tier-pause-option / Pillar 3 skip-+-swap-+-change-frequency-alternative-offers / Pillar 4 discount-+-loyalty-reward-+-winback-ladder / Pillar 5 cancel-flow-survey-+-cancel-reason-segmentation-+-per-reason-route / Pillar 6 pause-then-resume-+-winback-after-cancellation automation) for $300+ subscriber DTC subscription brands. Distinct from `subscription-dunning` (Move #14.2 — failure-RECOVERY layer AFTER charge fails; Move #14.2.3 is the failure-PREVENTION layer BEFORE cancellation completes), from `subscription-ai-failure-prediction` (Move #14.2.1 — AI failure-prediction on card-update data; Move #14.2.3 is voluntary churn where the customer decides to cancel vs Move #14.2.1's involuntary churn where the card fails), from `subscription-cross-border-dunning` (Move #14.2.2 — multi-currency + cross-border dunning layer; Move #14.2.3 layers cancel-flow-survey + multi-language pause + per-locale copy on top), from `subscription-replenishment` (Move #11 — replenishment-cycle-focused on consumable SKU-cadence; Move #14.2.3 fires on the cancel-click moment), and from `lifecycle-flow-library` (Move #14.1 — 20-flow email/SMS library across all stages; Move #14.2.3 is the cancel-flow-specific library).

## When to use this skill

**Use this skill when the operator has shipped Move #14.2 (post-charge dunning) ≥90 days AND Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) ≥60 days AND Move #14.2.2 (cross-border dunning) ≥30 days AND is observing that voluntary churn (customer clicks cancel) accounts for ≥60% of monthly subscriber churn AND voluntary-churn-rate exceeds involuntary-churn-rate AND the operator wants to lift voluntary-churn-save-rate from the baseline 10–20% to the best-in-class 35–55% (per Recharge 2024 + Stay AI 2024 + ProfitWell 2024 voluntary-churn-save benchmarks).** Voluntary churn prevention is structurally distinct from Move #14.2's post-charge dunning (which fires AFTER the charge fails) and from Move #14.2.1's AI failure prediction (which fires BEFORE the charge attempts). Move #14.2.3 fires at the cancel-click moment AND in the 7–30 days BEFORE the cancel-click via pre-cancellation-engagement (engaged-subscriber-LTV-uplift / per-subscriber-cohort save-targeting / cancel-risk-prediction). Move #14.2.3 is the **voluntary-churn-prevention + cancellation-save-flow layer** — the operator should only initiate it when ALL of the following are true:

You have:
- **Shopify (or Ikas / BigCommerce / WooCommerce / headless) DTC store** with admin API access AND a subscription app with pause-instead-of-cancel + skip-instead-of-cancel + swap-instead-of-cancel support (Recharge / Stay AI / Skio / Bold Subscriptions / Loop Subscriptions / Appstle / Smartrr / Seal Subscriptions / Okendo Subscriptions / Subbly / Chargebee / Recurly / Stripe Billing).
- **≥300 active subscribers** with at least 60+ days of recurring-billing history — below this threshold, voluntary-churn segmentation data is too sparse to train a reliable save-flow-targeting engine; deferred manual pause-reminder + ad-hoc winback emails suffice; the platform-fee overhead is not amortized.
- **Move #11 (subscriptions) shipped ≥180 days** AND a baseline MRR-dashboard exists (Recharge Analytics / ProfitWell / Baremetrics / Stripe Sigma / Triple Whale) — without a baseline, the operator cannot measure voluntary-churn-rate OR attribute retained MRR to specific save-flows.
- **Move #14.2 (post-charge dunning) shipped ≥90 days** AND ≥3 baseline Klaviyo segments defined (active_subscriber / past_due_subscriber / cancelled_subscriber / at_risk_subscriber / paused_subscriber / skip_next_cycle_subscriber) — the pause-as-a-recovery-strategy depends on Klaviyo + Postscript as the cross-channel reminder substrate AND on Move #14.2's voluntary-vs-involuntary churn segmentation as the data substrate.
- **Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) shipped ≥60 days** AND the failure-risk-scoring-engine is fed voluntary-cancel-flow-survey data — without this, Move #14.2.3 cannot predict which subscribers are at risk of voluntary cancellation BEFORE the cancel-click; the pre-cancellation-engagement engine depends on Move #14.2.1's ML substrate.
- **Move #14.2.2 (cross-border dunning) shipped ≥30 days** (only required for brands with ≥10% non-US subscribers) AND ≥6 baseline Klaviyo segments including per-currency and per-language segments — the cross-border cancel-flow-survey depends on Move #14.2.2's per-locale copy substrate.
- **≥$30k MRR / ≥$360k ARR** AND ≥$30 AOV AND ≥12 months of historical voluntary-cancellation data in the subscription-app data-warehouse — below these thresholds, the subscriber base is too thin + history too shallow to train a reliable save-flow-targeting engine; Recharge-native smart-cancellation + manual pause-reminder are sufficient without a custom build.
- **Documented voluntary-churn-prevention decision** — save-flow choice (Stay AI Pause vs Skio smart-cancellation vs Recharge native vs custom-built) + cancel-flow-survey choice (Recharge native vs Typeform vs Klaviyo conditional survey) + pause-tier choice (single 30d vs multi-tier 30d/60d/90d vs unlimited pause) + skip/swap/discount/loyalty ladder + pause-then-resume cadence + winback-after-cancellation cadence. Without this, the operator is reacting to voluntary cancellations one at a time without a save-flow-targeting engine.

You do NOT have:
- A baseline voluntary-churn-rate (the canonical "we don't know what % of our churn is voluntary vs involuntary" gap)
- A pre-cancellation-engagement flow at T-30d / T-14d / T-7d before the cancel-click (the canonical "we only learn the subscriber wants to cancel when they click cancel" gap)
- A cancel-flow-survey with per-reason segmentation (the canonical "all voluntary churn looks the same to us" gap)
- A pause-as-a-recovery-strategy with multi-tier pause option (the canonical "we only offer one 30d pause option" gap)
- A pause-then-resume automated nurture flow (the canonical "subscribers pause and we never contact them again" gap)
- A winback-after-cancellation flow with discount-ladder (the canonical "cancelled subscribers never hear from us again" gap)
- A voluntary-churn-save-rate metric tracked weekly (the canonical "we don't know what % of would-be-cancellations we're saving" gap)

**Voluntary churn costs 2–4× what involuntary churn costs at the LTV layer.** Per ProfitWell 2024 voluntary-churn-benchmark, voluntary churn is **65–72% of total subscription churn** while involuntary is **28–35%**. Saving 1 voluntary cancellation retains the full subscriber LTV (typically 12–36 months × AOV × gross margin). Saving 1 involuntary cancellation retains the same LTV minus the recovery cost. Net: a brand with $1M MRR + 6% monthly voluntary churn has **$720k of annual voluntary-churn-MRR exposed**; lifting save-rate from 20% to 45% retains **$180k/yr of voluntary-churn-MRR** (25 percentage points × $720k). Compounding with Move #14.2 + Move #14.2.1 + Move #14.2.2 involuntary-churn recovery, the total retained MRR can lift from median 25% to best-in-class **65–78%** — a **40–53 percentage point lift** that translates to **$300k–$500k/yr in total retained subscription MRR** at $1M MRR.

## The build (4-phase 14–28 day build)

### Phase 1 — Diagnose + baseline (Days 1–5)

**Goal:** Quantify the voluntary churn problem before fixing it. Establish the baseline that Move #14.2 + Move #14.2.1 + Move #14.2.2 will be combined with.

1. **Export 90-day voluntary-cancellation history from Recharge / Stay AI / Skio / Chargebee / Recurly / Stripe.** Capture: cancel-click timestamp + cancel-reason (if cancel-flow-survey is live) + cancel-flow-step (where they abandoned) + LTV at cancel-click + cohort + tier + last-order-amount + pause-history + skip-history + swap-history + discount-history.
2. **Calculate baseline voluntary-churn-rate vs involuntary-churn-rate** — Recharge Analytics → Churn Report OR ProfitWell → Cohort Analysis → Voluntary vs Involuntary Churn. Baseline median: voluntary 65–72% / involuntary 28–35%. Confirm Move #14.2's voluntary-vs-involuntary segmentation is correctly tracking both rates.
3. **Identify top-10 cancel-reasons** from cancel-flow-survey data (if survey is live) OR from Klaviyo `subscription_cancellation_requested` webhook events (if no survey). Group into: too-expensive / not-using-enough / product-not-working / switching-to-competitor / moving / temporary-break / duplicate-order / gift-expiration / other. Each reason maps to a different save-flow-targeting rule.
4. **Calculate baseline voluntary-churn-save-rate** — what % of cancel-clicks converted to pause / skip / swap / discount / loyalty-reward vs went to actual cancellation. Baseline median: 10–20% (no save-flow) to 18–28% (Recharge native smart-cancellation). Best-in-class: 45–55%.
5. **Calculate baseline pause-then-resume conversion** — what % of paused subscribers resume within the pause window. Baseline median: 22–35% (no resume cadence) to 50–62% (basic resume email). Best-in-class: 65–78% (3-touch resume cadence + per-tier-discount).
6. **Calculate baseline winback-after-cancellation** — what % of cancelled subscribers return within 90 days. Baseline median: 4–8% (no winback) to 12–20% (single-email winback). Best-in-class: 18–28% (3-email + 2-SMS winback + per-tier-discount-ladder).
7. **Capture baseline MRR at risk** — multiply voluntary-churn-rate × MRR × 12 months to estimate annual voluntary-churn-MRR exposed. This is the baseline Move #14.2.3 retains.
8. **Snapshot baseline** — capture the 5 metrics above (voluntary-churn-rate / voluntary-churn-save-rate / pause-then-resume-rate / winback-after-cancellation-rate / MRR-at-risk) per cohort + per tier as the Move #14.2.3 baseline.

### Phase 2 — Pre-cancellation-engagement + cancel-flow-survey (Days 6–12)

**Goal:** Predict which subscribers are at risk of voluntary cancellation BEFORE the cancel-click + instrument the cancel-flow-survey to capture cancel-reason per cancel-click.

1. **Build pre-cancellation-engagement cancel-risk-prediction model.** Use Move #14.2.1's failure-risk-scoring-engine substrate + add 12 new voluntary-churn features: declining-engagement-score (email-open-rate / SMS-reply-rate / login-frequency / page-view-frequency over 30d) + declining-replenishment-cadence (gap between orders growing) + pause-history (frequent-pauser is at-risk) + skip-history (frequent-skipper is at-risk) + swap-history (frequent-swapper is at-risk) + cohort-voluntary-churn-rate (cohorts with high voluntary churn predict at-risk individuals) + LTV-decline-trajectory (LTV-trajectory declining subscriber is at-risk) + price-sensitivity (subscriber on highest discount tier is at-risk) + support-ticket-history (recent-complaint is at-risk) + product-not-working-flag (recent-NPS-detractor-flag is at-risk) + switching-to-competitor-flag (engagement-with-competitor-content is at-risk) + temporary-break-signal (moving / vacation / seasonal-flag is at-risk). Score each subscriber T-30d / T-14d / T-7d before potential cancel-click.
2. **Build pre-cancellation-engagement flow** for high-risk subscribers — Klaviyo 3-email + Postscript 2-SMS cadence at T-30d / T-14d / T-7d with per-cohort engagement-discount + loyalty-reward-prompt + "we miss you" personal-message. Conversion target: 5–10% of high-risk subscribers re-engage without canceling.
3. **Build cancel-flow-survey.** Single-question-survey with 10 cancel-reasons (too-expensive / not-using-enough / product-not-working / switching-to-competitor / moving / temporary-break / duplicate-order / gift-expiration / prefer-one-time-purchase / other). Recharge native cancel-flow-survey is the canonical pick; Typeform embed is the alternative for $5M+ GMV brands. Survey completion rate target: 78–88%.
4. **Build cancel-reason segmentation** in Klaviyo — `cancel_reason_too_expensive`, `cancel_reason_not_using_enough`, `cancel_reason_product_not_working`, `cancel_reason_switching_to_competitor`, `cancel_reason_moving`, `cancel_reason_temporary_break`, `cancel_reason_duplicate_order`, `cancel_reason_gift_expiration`, `cancel_reason_prefer_one_time_purchase`, `cancel_reason_other`. Each segment routes to a different save-flow-targeting rule.
5. **Wire survey response → save-flow-targeting engine.** When a cancel-reason is captured, route the subscriber to the per-reason save-flow (e.g., `cancel_reason_too_expensive` → discount-ladder / `cancel_reason_temporary_break` → pause-option / `cancel_reason_not_using_enough` → skip-option + replenishment-cadence-reset).
6. **Build per-reason save-flow-targeting accuracy dashboard** — Triple Whale or Looker dashboard showing per-reason save-flow-conversion-rate. Save-flow-targeting accuracy target: 78–88%.

### Phase 3 — Pause-as-a-recovery-strategy + skip/swap/discount alternatives (Days 13–19)

**Goal:** Build the 4 save-flow alternatives (pause + skip + swap + discount) + the multi-tier pause-option that converts 38–48% of would-be cancellations into retained subscribers.

1. **Configure multi-tier pause-option** in Recharge / Stay AI / Skio / Bold / Loop / Chargebee — 4 tiers (30d / 60d / 90d / unlimited-pause) with per-tier messaging (e.g., 30d = "We'll save your subscription + reminder before resume" / 60d = "Pause longer with 10% off your next order" / 90d = "Pause for the season + 15% off your next order" / unlimited = "Pause as long as you need — we'll save your subscriber discount"). Pause-instead-of-cancel rate target: 38–48%.
2. **Configure skip-instead-of-cancel-option** — Recharge Skip or Stay AI Skip with optional 1-time-discount. Skip-instead-of-cancel rate target: 12–18%.
3. **Configure swap-instead-of-cancel-option** — Recharge Swap with cheaper-variant-suggestion (e.g., cancel of premium-subscription → swap to standard-subscription at 30% lower price). Swap-instead-of-cancel rate target: 8–14%.
4. **Configure discount-instead-of-cancel-ladder** — 3-step discount ladder (10% / 20% / 30% off next order OR 1 / 2 / 3 months free) with per-cancel-reason discount-rules (`cancel_reason_too_expensive` → 20% off next order + 30% off if 2-month-commit / `cancel_reason_not_using_enough` → 1 month free + replenishment-cadence-reset / `cancel_reason_product_not_working` → free product-replacement OR full-refund). Discount-instead-of-cancel rate target: 8–14%.
5. **Build pause-then-resume automated nurture flow** in Klaviyo — 3-touch resume cadence at T-7d-before-pause-end / T-3d-before-pause-end / T-1d-before-pause-end with per-tier-resume-discount (e.g., 30d tier → 10% off resume / 60d tier → 15% off resume / 90d tier → 20% off resume) + per-cohort-resume-rule + personal-message-from-founder. Pause-then-resume conversion target: 65–78%.
6. **Build skip-then-resume cadence** — 1-touch re-engagement email 7 days after skip with replenishment-cadence-reset option. Skip-then-resume conversion target: 45–58%.
7. **Wire all 4 save-flows to Move #14.2's voluntary-vs-involuntary-churn segmentation** — confirm Move #14.2's churn-rate tracking correctly attributes save-flow conversions to retained MRR (not to involuntary recovery).

### Phase 4 — Winback-after-cancellation + verification (Days 20–28)

**Goal:** Convert 18–28% of cancelled subscribers back to active within 90 days + verify all 7 gates.

1. **Build winback-after-cancellation flow** in Klaviyo — 3-email + 2-SMS cadence at T+3d / T+14d / T+30d / T+60d / T+90d after cancellation with per-tier-discount-ladder (e.g., 15% off return at T+3d / 25% off return at T+14d / 35% off return at T+30d) + per-cancel-reason-discount (`cancel_reason_too_expensive` → deeper discount / `cancel_reason_temporary_break` → resume-friendly copy / `cancel_reason_product_not_working` → product-replacement-offer). Winback-after-cancellation rate target: 18–28%.
2. **Build re-subscription-nudge** for high-LTV cancelled subscribers — Triple Whale cohort-LTV signal flags subscribers with above-median LTV; nudge them with 1:1 founder-email + personal-discount-code. Re-subscription rate target: 25–38%.
3. **Wire winback to Move #14.2's voluntary-churn-rate tracking** — confirm Move #14.2's voluntary-churn-rate correctly excludes winback-conversions (a winback-conversion is NOT a churn event; it's a re-subscription event).
4. **Build voluntary-churn-save-rate dashboard** — Triple Whale or Looker dashboard showing: voluntary-churn-rate / voluntary-churn-save-rate / pause-then-resume-conversion / winback-after-cancellation-rate / per-cancel-reason-save-rate / per-tier-save-rate / per-cohort-save-rate. Refresh weekly.
5. **Build 30-day post-launch verification** — capture the 5 metrics above per cohort + per tier + per cancel-reason and verify against the Phase 1 baseline. Target lift: voluntary-churn-save-rate +20–30pp / pause-then-resume-conversion +30–45pp / winback-after-cancellation-rate +10–18pp.
6. **A/B test per-tier-discount-ladder** — compare 10/20/30% ladder vs 15/25/35% ladder vs 20/30/40% ladder. Optimize for 90-day voluntary-churn-save-rate × per-tier-discount-cost. Hold ladder constant for 30 days before changing.
7. **No-regression checks** — confirm Move #14.2 (post-charge dunning) recovery-rate unchanged; Move #14.2.1 (AI failure prediction) precision/recall unchanged; Move #14.2.2 (cross-border dunning) non-US recovery-rate unchanged; Move #11 (subscription replenishment) replenishment-cadence unchanged.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real subscription save-flow builds)

1. **Single-tier pause-only option (no multi-tier + no skip + no swap + no discount).** Median impact: voluntary-churn-save-rate stuck at 18–28% (Recharge-native only); Top-quartile impact: stuck at 28–35% (multi-tier pause + skip + swap); Stretch impact: 45–55% save-rate unlocked. **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 — implement multi-tier pause-option (30d / 60d / 90d / unlimited) + skip-instead-of-cancel + swap-instead-of-cancel + 3-step discount-ladder. Single-tier pause alone caps save-rate at 28–35%; multi-tier + skip + swap + discount ladder unlocks 45–55%.

2. **No cancel-flow-survey (subscriber clicks cancel without reason).** Median impact: cannot route to per-reason save-flow; voluntary-churn-save-rate stuck at 18–24%; Top-quartile impact: 32–42% with single-question-survey; Stretch impact: 45–55% with survey + per-reason routing. **Fix:** Phase 2 Step 3 + 4 — implement Recharge native cancel-flow-survey OR Typeform embed with 10 cancel-reasons. Survey-completion-rate target 78–88%.

3. **No pre-cancellation-engagement (only fire on cancel-click).** Median impact: cancel-clicks convert at 18–28% save-rate vs Top-quartile 35–45% with pre-engagement; Stretch impact: 50–60% save-rate with pre-engagement + loyalty-reward + per-cohort-engagement-discount. **Fix:** Phase 2 Step 1 + 2 — build cancel-risk-prediction model using Move #14.2.1's failure-risk-scoring-engine substrate + 12 new voluntary-churn features (declining-engagement / declining-replenishment-cadence / pause-history / skip-history / swap-history / discount-history / cohort-voluntary-churn-rate / LTV-decline-trajectory / price-sensitivity / support-ticket-history / product-not-working-flag / switching-to-competitor-flag) + pre-engagement-flow at T-30d / T-14d / T-7d.

4. **Discount-ladder too aggressive (40%+ off next order).** Median impact: subscriber takes the discount + cancels anyway within 90 days (discount-skipping); Top-quartile impact: 3-step ladder (10% / 20% / 30%) balances retention vs margin; Stretch impact: per-cancel-reason-discount-rules optimize margin. **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 4 — implement 3-step discount-ladder (10% / 20% / 30% off) + per-cancel-reason-discount-rules. Discount > 40% off is margin-destructive; subscribers take the discount + cancel within 90 days at 50–65% rate.

5. **Discount-ladder too low (5% off next order).** Median impact: discount-instead-of-cancel rate stuck at 2–4% (subscribers see 5% as not-worth-staying); Top-quartile impact: 8–14% with 10/20/30% ladder; Stretch impact: 18–25% with per-reason discount + per-tier discount. **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 4 — minimum 10% off next order; 20% off 2-month-commit; 30% off 3-month-commit. 5% off is too low to be motivating; subscribers see 5% as not-worth-staying.

6. **No pause-then-resume automated nurture (subscriber pauses and never hears from us).** Median impact: pause-then-resume-conversion stuck at 22–35%; Top-quartile impact: 50–62% with single-resume-email at end-of-window; Stretch impact: 65–78% with 3-touch resume cadence + per-tier-resume-discount. **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 5 — build 3-touch resume cadence at T-7d / T-3d / T-1d before pause-end with per-tier-resume-discount. Without resume cadence, 50–65% of paused subscribers churn instead of resuming.

7. **Single-tier pause (only 30d pause, no 60d / 90d / unlimited).** Median impact: pause-instead-of-cancel-rate stuck at 12–18%; Top-quartile impact: 28–38% with multi-tier pause; Stretch impact: 45–58% with multi-tier pause + per-tier-discount + per-tier-resume-nurture. **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 1 — implement 4-tier pause-option (30d / 60d / 90d / unlimited-pause) with per-tier messaging + per-tier-discount + per-tier-resume-nurture. Single-tier 30d alone caps pause-instead-of-cancel at 18%.

8. **No skip-instead-of-cancel option (subscribers must pause or cancel).** Median impact: skip-instead-of-cancel-rate stuck at 2–5%; Top-quartile impact: 12–18% with single-skip-option; Stretch impact: 25–35% with skip + 1-time-discount + per-cohort-skip-rule. **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 2 — implement Recharge Skip or Stay AI Skip with optional 1-time-discount (10% off next non-skipped order). Skipping is the canonical save-flow for "not-using-enough" cancel-reason.

9. **No swap-instead-of-cancel option (subscribers must cancel instead of downgrading).** Median impact: swap-instead-of-cancel-rate stuck at 1–3%; Top-quartile impact: 8–14% with single-swap-option; Stretch impact: 18–25% with swap + cheaper-variant-suggestion + per-cohort-swap-rule. **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 3 — implement Recharge Swap with cheaper-variant-suggestion (e.g., cancel of premium-subscription → swap to standard-subscription at 30% lower price). Swapping is the canonical save-flow for "too-expensive" cancel-reason with LTV-retention upside.

10. **No winback-after-cancellation flow (cancelled subscribers never hear from us again).** Median impact: winback-after-cancellation-rate stuck at 4–8% (no winback) or 8–14% (single-email winback); Top-quartile impact: 18–28% with 3-email + 2-SMS cadence + per-tier-discount-ladder; Stretch impact: 35–48% with per-cancel-reason-discount + per-tier-discount + per-cohort-discount + founder-email. **Fix:** Phase 4 Step 1 — implement 3-email + 2-SMS winback cadence at T+3d / T+14d / T+30d / T+60d / T+90d with per-tier-discount-ladder + per-cancel-reason-discount.

11. **Cancel-flow-survey too long (5+ questions).** Median impact: survey-completion-rate stuck at 30–45%; Top-quartile impact: 78–88% with single-question-survey; Stretch impact: 92–98% with single-question-survey + animated-survey + per-locale-translated. **Fix:** Phase 2 Step 3 — single-question-survey with 10 cancel-reasons as radio buttons + optional-text-field. Multi-question-surveys drop completion-rate by 30–50pp.

12. **No save-flow-targeting accuracy tracking (cannot optimize which save-flow works for which cancel-reason).** Median impact: per-reason-save-flow-conversion stuck at 30–45% (no ML targeting); Top-quartile impact: 78–88% with Stay AI + Triple Whale cohort-LTV feedback-loop; Stretch impact: 92–98% with custom-ML + per-cohort-save-targeting. **Fix:** Phase 2 Step 6 — build per-reason-save-flow-targeting-accuracy dashboard in Triple Whale or Looker showing per-reason-save-flow-conversion-rate. Without accuracy tracking, you cannot optimize save-flow-targeting rules.

13. **Pause-window too short (7-day pause only).** Median impact: pause-then-resume-conversion stuck at 35–50%; Top-quartile impact: 50–62% with 30-day pause; Stretch impact: 65–78% with 30d/60d/90d multi-tier pause + per-tier-resume-nurture. **Fix:** Phase 3 Step 1 — minimum 30d pause; 60d / 90d / unlimited tiers for long-term break subscribers. 7-day pause is too short for most use-cases (vacation / moving / temporary-break / seasonal); subscribers churn instead of using pause.

14. **Cancel-flow-survey in English only (no per-locale translation for cross-border brands).** Median impact: cross-border survey-completion-rate stuck at 35–55% (English-only); Top-quartile impact: 78–88% with per-locale-translation; Stretch impact: 92–98% with per-locale-translation + per-locale-tone-of-voice + native-flag-icon. **Fix:** Phase 2 Step 3 — per-locale-translated survey for brands with ≥10% non-US subscribers (Move #14.2.2 prerequisite); English + German + French + Spanish + Italian + Dutch minimum. Without per-locale translation, cross-border survey-completion drops 30–50pp.

15. **Re-subscription path too friction-heavy (must re-enter card + re-confirm address + re-confirm tier).** Median impact: winback-conversion stuck at 8–14%; Top-quartile impact: 22–32% with 1-click-re-subscription; Stretch impact: 35–48% with 1-click + saved-payment-method + saved-shipping-address + saved-tier-pre-selection. **Fix:** Phase 4 Step 1 — implement 1-click re-subscription path with saved-payment-method (tokenized) + saved-shipping-address + saved-tier-pre-selection + per-tier-discount-applied. Re-subscription friction >3 steps drops winback-conversion by 15–25pp.
