## When to use this skill

Use this skill **after Move #6 Triple Whale attribution is live AND Move #6.5/6.6/6.7/6.8/6.9 attribution-quality-audit + drift-unification + unified-dashboard are all shipped** (the MMM engine requires clean attribution substrate or it inherits the same drift), **after Move #10 lifecycle flow library has identified ≥1,000 subscribers OR ≥100 customers** (the cohort-overlay on MMM needs signal-to-noise), **after Move #53 financial-operations-unit-economics is live** (the marginal-ROAS decision needs COGS context), **after Move #62 people-operations-org-design has ≥1 paid-media manager OR agency partner** (the weekly re-estimation loop needs an owner), and **before Move #6.11 next-move-in-the-attribution-stack + Move #76 contact-center + Move #81 multichannel-product-feed + Move #118 per-cohort-pricing + Move #120 per-cohort-channel-orchestration**. It is the right move when the operator CANNOT answer "if I shift $5k/mo from Meta to TikTok, what's my expected revenue delta with 80% confidence?", when the channel-budget-split is by-gut-feel (e.g. "60% Meta + 25% Google + 15% TikTok"), when the operator does not know the **marginal ROAS** per channel (only the average ROAS), when the operator cannot distinguish **base vs incremental** revenue (so 80% of the attributed revenue would have happened anyway), when the operator has no **saturation curves** (so they keep pouring spend into a channel past the inflection point), when the operator has no **carry-over** (so they underestimate the 14-day tail of Meta brand campaigns), when the operator has no **cross-channel halo** (so they cut Brand Search budget that was actually driving Meta performance), or when GeoLift/holdout-tests are not running quarterly (so the operator learns about incrementality only after they've wasted $50k+).

You have:

- **A live Shopify (or comparable) DTC storefront with at least 12 months of order history** (gives the MMM engine a year of weekly-or-daily channel-spend time-series to estimate saturation curves + carryover + halo).
- **≥$2k/mo paid-media spend across ≥3 channels** (Meta + Google + TikTok minimum; ≥5 channels is better for cross-channel-halo estimation).
- **≥6 months of weekly-or-daily channel-spend time-series in Triple Whale or comparable** (the MMM engine needs at least 26 weekly data points to fit a stable Bayesian hierarchical model).
- **Move #6 Triple Whale attribution shipped (all 7 gates pass) + Move #6.5/6.6/6.7/6.8/6.9 shipped** (the MMM engine builds on clean attribution substrate).
- **At least one GeoLift-able geo-region in operation** (US states + EU countries + CA provinces + AU states — the operator needs to be able to suppress spend in ≥1 region for ≥2 weeks to measure incremental lift).
- **At least one of:** in-house data-engineer OR agency-partner with MMM-execution-capability OR Triple Whale MMM OR Northbeam MMM subscription OR Marketing Evolution subscription (Path A: Triple Whale MMM + Northbeam MTA; Path B+: Northbeam Pro + Recast + Lifesight; Path C: Meridian + LightweightMMM + Robyn + PyMC-Marketing).
- **At least one of:** willingness-to-run-quarterly-GeoLift-tests OR willingness-to-run-holdout-tests OR willingness-to-run-ghost-ads OR access-to-Marketing-Evolution-or-Northbeam-incrementality-module (the incrementality-test-engine is the canonical "ground truth" calibration for the MMM).
- **A documented channel-budget-allocation-decision-policy** (e.g. "we rebalance channels weekly based on marginal-ROAS-with-confidence-intervals"; without this, the MMM output is informational not actionable).
- **A willingness to act on the MMM output** (this is the load-bearing artifact — every weekly paid-media decision should reference it).

Do **not** use this skill when:

- The operator has <$2k/mo paid-media spend (the MMM ROI is sub-1:1 at <$1k/mo; below $2k/mo the cost of the MMM-tool + operator-time exceeds the marginal-ROAS uplift).
- The operator has <3 paid-media channels (MMM is about cross-channel-allocation; with 1-2 channels the marginal-ROAS is just "spend more or less").
- The operator has <6 months of weekly channel-spend data (the MMM needs at least 26 weekly data points for stable Bayesian inference).
- The operator has not shipped Move #6 Triple Whale attribution (MMM without clean attribution substrate is MMM-on-garbage-in-garbage-out).
- The operator is OK with the "60% Meta + 25% Google + 15% TikTok gut-feel split" and the operator's marginal-ROAS is already 5×+ (rare but possible for hyper-efficient niche brands).
- The operator has no GeoLift-able geo-region (no US/EU/CA/AU footprint, or all geo-regions are too small for statistical power).

The **4 canonical outputs** every "best in class" MMM stack must surface weekly:

1. **Per-channel marginal ROAS with 80% confidence interval** ("with 80% confidence, Meta will return $3.50 ± $0.80 marginal revenue per $1 incremental spend next week").
2. **Per-channel saturation curve** ("Meta is at 65% of its saturation ceiling; the next $1k/mo will return $2.80 marginal ROAS vs the current $4.50 average ROAS").
3. **Per-channel carryover** ("TikTok ads have a 14-day tail; 38% of attributed TikTok revenue comes from days 8–14 after the spend").
4. **Cross-channel halo coefficients** ("for every $1 spent on Brand Search, Meta returns an additional $0.42 in halo revenue that Meta's last-click attribution under-counts").

Without all 4, the operator is flying blind on marginal-budget-allocation and will over-spend into the saturation zone + under-spend on halo-leveraging channels.

- Validate: every channel has daily-spend + daily-revenue + daily-orders + daily-cohort-LTV.
- Validate: organic + email + SMS + direct + brand-search + non-brand-search are all separated.

**Day 2: MTA engine wiring**
- Install Northbeam MTA OR Triple Whale MTA OR Marketing Evolution MTA.
- Configure Shapley-value attribution (default) OR Markov-chain attribution (for high-volume channels).
- Validate: per-channel-attribution-percentage-sum = 100% across all channels (no double-counting).

**Day 3: MTA-coverage check**
- Verify MTA covers ≥95% of touchpoints (post-iOS-14.5 the touchpoint-coverage caps at ~70-80% for Meta, so the MTA needs to be calibrated against MMM).
- Document the MTA-vs-last-click delta per channel (this is the foundation for the "incremental-revenue-under-counted-by-MTA" overlay).

### Phase 2 — MMM baseline + first GeoLift (Days 4–14)

**Days 4–7: First Bayesian MMM fit**
- Install Triple Whale MMM OR Northbeam MMM OR Marketing Evolution OR Meridian OR LightweightMMM OR Robyn.
- Configure Hill-saturation per channel + Adstock-carryover per channel + cross-channel-halo.
- Fit on 12 months of weekly data + 6 months of daily data (if available).
- Validate: per-channel marginal-ROAS confidence interval is finite (not [0, ∞]).
- Validate: R² ≥ 0.70 on holdout test (last 4 weeks).

**Days 8–10: First GeoLift test**
- Pick 1 major channel (Meta OR Google OR TikTok) + 1 geo-region (CA-state + TX-state for US; or DE + FR for EU).
- Suppress spend in the test region for 14 days; measure incremental lift vs control region.
- Validate: lift is statistically significant at p<0.10.
- Validate: GeoLift-estimated mROAS is within ±20% of MMM-estimated mROAS (this is the canonical "calibration check").

**Days 11–14: First budget-rebalancing-engine**
- Build a weekly Looker/Metabase dashboard that surfaces:
  - Per-channel marginal ROAS + 80% confidence interval
  - Per-channel saturation-curve position (% of ceiling)
  - Per-channel carryover (% of attributed revenue from days 8+)
  - Cross-channel halo coefficients
  - Recommended weekly rebalance: "shift $X from Channel A (mROAS=2.1×) to Channel B (mROAS=5.4×)"

### Phase 3 — Weekly re-estimation + quarterly GeoLift cadence (Days 15–28)

**Days 15–21: Weekly re-estimation cron**
- Wire the MMM to re-fit every Sunday at 02:00 with prior week's data.
- Validate: re-estimation runtime <4 hours.
- Validate: marginal-ROAS doesn't shift >30% week-over-week (large shifts = model instability).

**Days 22–28: Quarterly GeoLift cadence**
- Schedule 4 GeoLifts/yr (1/quarter) on the largest channels by spend.
- Validate: each GeoLift validates the MMM within ±20% (if not, the MMM needs recalibration).
- Validate: GeoLift-results are pushed to the operator-dashboard-overlay within 24h of test completion.

### Phase 4 — Incrementality-test-engine + holdout-tests (Days 29–42)

**Days 29–35: Ghost-ads + holdout-tests**
- Configure ghost-ads (PSA-test) on Meta + Google for the top 2 audiences.
- Validate: holdout-test sample size ≥10k impressions per arm.
- Validate: holdout-test runtime ≥14 days for statistical power.

**Days 36–42: Switchback-tests + Bayesian-structural-time-series**
- For TikTok + Snap + Pinterest (high-variance channels), configure switchback-tests (alternate spend ON/OFF every 24h or 48h).
- Validate: switchback-test sample size ≥5k impressions per arm.

### Phase 5 — Cross-channel-halo + saturation-curve-position dashboard (Days 43–56)

**Days 43–49: Halo-coefficient surfacing**
- For every channel pair (Meta↔Google, Meta↔TikTok, Google↔Brand-Search, etc.), surface the halo coefficient in the MMM output.
- Validate: top-5 channel-pairs by halo-coefficient are documented in the operator-dashboard.
- Validate: brand-search halo is being credited back to brand-search (not Meta) when the operator cuts Meta spend.

**Days 50–56: Saturation-curve dashboard**
- For every channel, plot the Hill-saturation curve on the operator-dashboard with current spend position.
- Validate: if any channel is at >80% of saturation ceiling, the dashboard surfaces a "saturation-warning" alert.

## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

**Pitfall #1 — Running MMM without clean attribution substrate.** The single most common pitfall. Move #6 Triple Whale must be at overall_passed: true + Move #6.5/6.6/6.7/6.8/6.9 attribution-quality-audit + drift-unification + unified-dashboard all shipped before MMM is run. **Fix:** Run `python3 scripts/triple_whale_attribution_check.py` + `python3 scripts/attribution_quality_audit.py` + `python3 scripts/tiktok_attribution_audit.py` + `python3 scripts/snap_pinterest_attribution_audit.py` + `python3 scripts/attribution_cross_platform_rollup.py` FIRST and confirm all 21 prior-tick gates pass. MMM on garbage attribution is MMM-on-garbage-in-garbage-out.

**Pitfall #2 — Confusing MMM marginal-ROAS with MTA last-click ROAS.** The MTA last-click ROAS is ~2–3× higher than the MMM marginal-ROAS because last-click gives 100% credit to the last touchpoint and ignores base revenue. Operators who don't understand this distinction will think the MMM is broken when it returns mROAS=2.1× for Meta that the last-click ROAS says is 5.4×. **Fix:** Document the MMM-vs-MTA delta per channel in the operator-dashboard; set the operator's mental model to "MMM marginal-ROAS is the truth, MTA last-click ROAS is the upper bound".

**Pitfall #3 — Not running GeoLift quarterly, so MMM is never calibrated.** Bayesian MMM without GeoLift-calibration is a sophisticated curve-fit, not a causal measurement. Operators who skip the quarterly GeoLift will have MMM outputs that drift over time as the channels saturate + halo shifts + iOS updates break attribution. **Fix:** Schedule 4 GeoLifts/yr in advance (1/quarter); wire the GeoLift-result back into the MMM as a calibration-check; if the GeoLift-estimated mROAS is outside ±20% of the MMM-estimated mROAS, escalate to Path C/D.

**Pitfall #4 — Using only weekly data instead of weekly + daily.** With only weekly data, the MMM can't estimate intra-week patterns (e.g. Meta's Saturday-Sunday dip) and the carryover estimate is biased. **Fix:** Use daily data for the past 90 days + weekly data for the past 12 months (the Bayesian model can ingest both granularities simultaneously).

**Pitfall #5 — Forgetting to model cross-channel-halo, so cutting Brand-Search collapses Meta.** When the operator cuts Brand-Search budget to "save money", Meta performance collapses 30–60% within 14 days because Brand-Search was driving upper-funnel Meta audience growth. MMM without cross-channel-halo coefficients won't see this. **Fix:** Always include Meta↔Brand-Search + Meta↔TikTok + Google↔Meta halo coefficients in the MMM; surface them in the operator-dashboard.

**Pitfall #6 — Ignoring carryover, so TikTok budget cuts are premature.** TikTok ads have a 14-day carryover (38% of attributed TikTok revenue comes from days 8–14 after the spend). Operators who cut TikTok budget based on a 3-day ROAS dip are making a premature cut. **Fix:** Always include Adstock-transformation in the MMM; surface the per-channel carryover in the operator-dashboard; only rebalance based on ≥14-day rolling mROAS.

**Pitfall #7 — Treating MMM output as a deterministic recommendation, not a probabilistic one.** MMM outputs marginal-ROAS with a confidence interval (e.g. "Meta mROAS = $3.50 ± $0.80 at 80% confidence"). Operators who treat the point estimate as deterministic will over-react to noise. **Fix:** Always surface the 80% confidence interval in the operator-dashboard; only rebalance if the upper-bound of one channel exceeds the lower-bound of another (i.e. "Meta 80%-CI = [$2.70, $4.30] + TikTok 80%-CI = [$3.20, $5.60]" → shifting budget from Meta to TikTok has high confidence; "Meta 80%-CI = [$2.70, $4.30] + YouTube 80%-CI = [$3.10, $3.90]" → too much overlap, don't rebalance).

**Pitfall #8 — Fitting the MMM on 6 months of data instead of 12+ months.** With 6 months of weekly data, the MMM can't reliably estimate year-over-year seasonality (BFCM + Q1 + summer-dip + back-to-school). The result is the operator shifts budget into TikTok in mid-November because the MMM thinks TikTok is high-mROAS, but it's just seasonal noise. **Fix:** Always use 12+ months of weekly data (or 18+ months for brands with strong BFCM); document the seasonality-adjustment-method in the MMM config.

**Pitfall #9 — Not separating base revenue from incremental revenue.** If 80% of Meta-attributed revenue would have happened anyway (organic search + brand-direct + email), then Meta's true incremental contribution is 20% of what the last-click attribution says. Operators who don't model base-vs-incremental will over-credit Meta and under-allocate to channels that drive incremental conversions (Snap + Pinterest + Reddit often look bad on last-click but are 60-80% incremental). **Fix:** Always include a base-revenue term in the MMM; document the per-channel-incremental-share in the operator-dashboard.

**Pitfall #10 — Saturating a channel past its Hill-ceiling, wasting 30-50% of spend.** Every channel has a Hill-saturation curve ("the more you spend, the less marginal revenue per dollar"). Operators who don't see the saturation-curve position will pour budget into Meta past the 80% saturation mark and waste 30-50% of marginal spend. **Fix:** Always plot the Hill-saturation curve per channel in the operator-dashboard; surface a "saturation-warning" alert when any channel is at >80% of its ceiling.

**Pitfall #11 — Treating GeoLift-test as a one-time calibration, not a quarterly check.** GeoLift-results drift over time as the channels evolve + the audience composition changes + new competitors enter. Operators who run 1 GeoLift in Year 1 and never again will have a stale MMM by Year 2. **Fix:** Schedule 4 GeoLifts/yr (1/quarter) as a load-bearing operational cadence; the operator-dashboard surfaces "last GeoLift was 95 days ago" as a health metric.

**Pitfall #12 — Using only point-estimate attribution (no Shapley/Markov), so channel-multi-touch is invisible.** Last-click attribution gives 100% credit to the last touchpoint, which systematically over-credits bottom-funnel channels (Brand-Search + Google Shopping + retargeting) and under-credits top-funnel channels (Meta + TikTok + YouTube). Operators who use only last-click will under-invest in top-funnel channels and over-invest in bottom-funnel channels, eventually starving the funnel. **Fix:** Always include Shapley-value OR Markov-chain MTA in addition to last-click; surface the per-channel-Shapley-attribution in the operator-dashboard; set the channel-budget-split based on Shapley-attribution, not last-click.

**Pitfall #13 — Modeling promotional spikes (BFCM + Prime Day) as organic growth, breaking the MMM.** The MMM treats a BFCM revenue spike as "paid spend is highly efficient" and the operator increases budget 50% in mid-November thinking BFCM-economics will persist. **Fix:** Always include BFCM + Prime Day + seasonal-promotional indicators as covariates in the MMM; document the per-channel-BFCM-uplift vs non-BFCM-uplift in the operator-dashboard.

**Pitfall #14 — Mistaking MTA-coverage drop for revenue drop (iOS-17/18 privacy updates).** When Apple ships a privacy update (e.g. iOS-17 Link Tracking Protection), the MTA-coverage can drop from 80% to 60% overnight, and the operator panics thinking revenue is dropping. **Fix:** Always distinguish MTA-coverage-drop from revenue-drop in the operator-dashboard; treat MTA-coverage as a separate health-metric from revenue; re-baseline the MMM after major iOS updates.

**Pitfall #15 — Choosing the wrong tool for the brand-size (Path A on a $25M GMV brand = wasted year).** Path A (Northbeam MMM + Triple Whale MTA + 1 GeoLift/quarter) is for sub-$1M GMV operators. A $25M GMV operator using Path A will get a sub-optimal MMM and waste 6-12 months before realizing they need Path C (Meridian + LightweightMMM + Robyn + Haus + Marketing Evolution + weekly re-estimation + 12 GeoLifts/yr). **Fix:** Use the GMV-tier decision-matrix in Path A/B+/B/C/D before selecting the MMM-tool; revisit the tool-selection annually.

**Pitfall #16 — Running MMM without documenting the model-spec, so future operators can't validate.** Bayesian MMM has dozens of hyperparameters (Hill-coefficient prior, Adstock-decay prior, halo-coefficient prior, base-revenue prior). Operators who don't document the model-spec end up with an MMM that nobody can validate or improve. **Fix:** Always document the model-spec in a `mmm-spec.yaml` file (or equivalent); include every hyperparameter + every prior + every covariate; version-control the spec; review the spec annually.

**Pitfall #17 — Confusing Marketing Evolution / Recast / Haus / Lifesight / Triple Whale MMM with a "real" MMM.** These tools are MMM-as-a-service (the vendor runs the model and gives you a dashboard). They are 80% as good as a custom Meridian + LightweightMMM + Robyn implementation for most operators, BUT they don't give the operator the model-spec to inspect + customize. Operators who need bespoke channel-mix logic (e.g. B2B2C + retail-media + marketplace) should use Path C/D. **Fix:** Default to Triple Whale MMM + Northbeam MMM + Marketing Evolution for sub-$10M GMV; use Meridian + LightweightMMM + Robyn + PyMC-Marketing for $10M+ GMV with bespoke needs.

**Pitfall #18 — Not wiring the MMM output to the budget-execution layer (Meta Ads + Google Ads API), so rebalancing is manual.** Operators who generate the MMM-output as a PDF/CSV every week but don't wire it to the Meta Ads API + Google Ads API will spend 2-4 hours per week manually adjusting budgets. **Fix:** Wire the MMM-output to the Meta Ads API + Google Ads API + TikTok Ads API via Triple Whale + Northbeam + Marketing Evolution automated-bidding; the MMM recommends, the API executes, the operator reviews the weekly summary in 15 minutes.
