# Lifecycle celebratory flows — birthday + anniversary + loyalty-points-expiry + seasonal-gift-guide (the lifecycle "brand-equity" Tier-1 pillar, Move #1.5)

> The lifecycle-celebratory pillar is the **highest brand-equity compounding layer** in the entire lifecycle-marketing library: it converts the operator's existing customer data (birthday field + first-purchase date + Smile.io points balance + Q4 season) into **4 customer-specific emotional touchpoints** (birthday celebration, purchase anniversary, points-expiry rescue, seasonal gift-guide) that fire on the most personal moments in the customer's lifecycle. Per Klaviyo 2024 + Smile.io 2024 + Baymard benchmarks, the 4 celebratory flows together convert at **6–18%** (vs the canonical 2–5% of cart-abandon + welcome + browse-abandon), lift **12-month LTV by 30–60%** for the touched cohort, redeem **30–55% of at-risk loyalty-points-expiry** + **35–55% of birthday-discount codes** + **12–22% of anniversary-discount codes**, and lift Q4 revenue **8–25%** via the seasonal-gift-guide. Together they form **Pillar 5** of the canonical 5-pillar lifecycle-marketing framework (research/05): the only pillar that compounds **brand-equity** (the emotional connection that drives word-of-mouth + UGC + repeat-purchase-rate) instead of acquiring new buyers or re-engaging lapsed ones. Without birthday + anniversary + loyalty-points-expiry + seasonal-gift-guide, the operator captures only the 12–22% of the brand-equity-compounding revenue potential — every customer whose birthday field defaults to the account-creation date (the canonical pitfall) misses the **highest-ROI single touchpoint in the database** (35–55% redemption rate, 18–35% AOV uplift, $400–$1,200 revenue per 1k birthday emails), every customer with >365 days since first purchase misses the "thanks for being a customer" anniversary moment, every Smile-points-issued customer with a 30-day-expiring-points balance churns to a competitor before the operator notices, and every Q4 holiday season passes without a per-customer-segmented gift-guide cadence that lifts Q4 revenue 8–25%. Ship after Move #1 (cart-abandon) + Move #4 (welcome) + Move #7 (SMS) + Move #8 (loyalty program) + skill/490 (browse-abandon) + skill/491 (winback-sunset) + skill/492 (post-purchase) + skill/493 (loyalty engagement) are all live and producing baseline data; the 4 celebratory flows reuse the same Klaviyo + Postscript + Smile + Baymard/Q4-calendar wiring, so they ship in **~4 hours of operator time** and return a Year-1 ROI band of **8:1–28:1** at the default $500k–$10M GMV DTC brand.

## When to use this skill

You have:

- Move #1 (cart-abandon flow) shipped with ≥6-week revenue baseline
- Move #4 (welcome series) shipped with ≥6-week revenue baseline
- Move #7 (SMS welcome + cart-abandon) shipped with ≥4-week revenue baseline
- Move #8 (loyalty program) shipped with Smile.io (or Yotpo / LoyaltyLion) points + tiers + VIP tier + birthday-field + points-expiry configured
- Skill/490 (browse-abandon + consideration flows) shipped with ≥4-week revenue baseline
- Skill/491 (winback + sunset) shipped with ≥4-week revenue baseline
- Skill/492 (post-purchase cross-sell + replenishment + educational) shipped with ≥4-week revenue baseline
- Skill/493 (loyalty engagement flows: tier-up + tier-down + points-expiry + birthday + anniversary + NPS-detractor) shipped with ≥4-week revenue baseline (tier-up + NPS-promoter amplification + tier-down rescue data available as gating signal)
- Klaviyo onsite JS installed and `Placed Order` + `Subscribed to List` + `Fulfilled` + `Opened Email` + `Clicked Email` events flowing
- Smile.io events flowing into Klaviyo (via Smile's Klaviyo integration: `points_earned` + `tier_changed` + `vip_status_changed` + `points_expiring` + `birthday` + `anniversary` events)
- Postscript (or Attentive) SMS wired with sender registration + opt-in compliance (TCPA/GDPR)
- Customer-profile `birthday` field populated (or a "fill in your birthday for $5 off" capture flow is live per skill/493's birthday-collect flow)
- Customer-profile `first_purchase_date` or equivalent (Klaviyo custom property `Placed Order` first-occurrence captured) — required for the anniversary flow
- Q4 calendar + shipping-deadline calendar loaded into the operator's project-management tool (Nov 1 first-gift-guide email → Dec 24 last-shipping-date email cadence)
- Customer list hygiene: ≥1,000 active customers segmented into ≥3 baseline segments (active customer / lapsed customer / subscriber-not-buyer) + per-tier segments (Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3 / VIP) + per-celebration-state segments (birthday-this-month / anniversary-this-month / points-expiring-30d / holiday-gift-shopper)
- Operator capacity: 4-hour kickoff block + 1-hour weekly maintenance block for the first 8 weeks (the seasonal-gift-guide is a Q4-only flow but requires August-October build lead time)

You do NOT have:

- A **birthday flow** that emails + SMS's the customer on their birthday month with a personalized gift / discount (the canonical "happy birthday — $20 off" + 7-day-pre-birthday teaser moment)
- An **anniversary flow** that emails the customer on the 1-year / 2-year / 5-year / 10-year anniversary of their first purchase (the canonical "it's been [N] years — thanks for being a customer" moment)
- A **loyalty-points-expiry warning flow** that surfaces a 30-day-prior warning when Smile.io points are about to expire (the canonical "use your [N] points before [date]" rescue, distinct from skill/493's loyalty-engagement variant which fires on tier-threshold crossings)
- A **seasonal-gift-guide flow** that emails the customer 4 times over Q4 (Nov 1 gift-guide / Dec 1 last-shipping-dates / Dec 15 stocking-stuffers / Dec 22 final-week) with per-customer-segmented product recommendations
- A **per-celebration-state Klaviyo segment** (birthday-this-month / anniversary-this-month / points-expiring-30d / holiday-gift-shopper) that the 4 flows trigger on
- A **birthday-field-validation filter** in the birthday flow trigger (else "happy birthday" fires on the account-creation date for customers who never filled in their birthday)
- A **1-discount-per-30-days guard** that prevents birthday 20% off + anniversary 15% off + loyalty-points-expiry 10% off + seasonal-gift-guide 15% off + cart-abandon 10% off + welcome 10% off + winback 15% off + browse-abandon 10% off + tier-up 15% off + NPS-promoter 10% off from all firing on the same customer in 30 days
- A **Q4-shipping-deadline calendar** wired into the seasonal-gift-guide cadence (else the Dec 22 "final week" email ships after the carrier's cut-off, customer is angry)
- A **per-cohort branching** in the seasonal-gift-guide (high-AOV gets luxury gift concierge / mid-AOV gets product-bundle / first-time-buyer gets "under $50" entry point)
- A **gift-recipient-via-shipping-address capture** in the seasonal-gift-guide (else gift-guide is generic; the canonical "ship directly to recipient with gift-wrap" requires a recipient-address-capture step)
- A **CDP-stitched identity layer** (Segment / RudderStack / Klaviyo Data Platform) that resolves the same customer across Klaviyo + Postscript + Smile + Shopify customer-profile (else the birthday email fires but the birthday SMS doesn't, or the anniversary discount code fires but isn't tied to the customer's loyalty-points balance)

## The build (time estimate: ~4 hours total)

**Phase 1 — Birthday flow wiring (1 hour).** Create the Klaviyo date-triggered flow `birthday_celebratory_v1`: trigger = `Profile.birthday` reaches today's date. Audience filter = `Profile.birthday` is not empty + customer has not received a birthday email in the last 365 days + suppress if customer in `discount-redeemed-segment` in last 30d (the 1-discount-per-30-days guard). Email #1 at birthday-7d: subject "Your birthday gift is coming on [date]" with Klaviyo dynamic block `{% customer.first_name %}` + `$20 off your next order` code. Email #2 at birthday-day: subject "Happy birthday, {% customer.first_name %}! Your gift is here" with birthday-gift-curation block (3-5 product recommendations from the operator's "birthday-gift" metafield-tagged SKUs). Email #3 at birthday+7d: subject "Last chance — your birthday gift expires [date]" with countdown-timer block + 15% off (vs the 20% at birthday-day). SMS at birthday-day (via Postscript) = "Happy birthday, {% customer.first_name %}! Your $20 off gift is waiting: [URL]. Reply STOP to opt out." Per-cohort branching: high-AOV customers get "your curated gift concierge" (no discount, personal-stylist-CTA) instead of the 20% off; first-time customers (Placed Order = 0) get 25% off first-order + 30-day-extended-birthday-window. Wire Smile.io's `birthday` event into Klaviyo via the Smile-Klaviyo integration (the canonical "points-issued-on-birthday" trigger for the loyalty-tier-up variant per skill/493). Wire Gorgias ticket creation for any birthday-flow email reply (the canonical "I never got my gift" routing).

**Phase 2 — Anniversary flow wiring (45 minutes).** Create the Klaviyo date-triggered flow `anniversary_celebratory_v1`: trigger = `Placed Order` first-occurrence anniversary (1-year / 2-year / 5-year / 10-year). Use Klaviyo's `Date of first order` custom property OR a dedicated `first_purchase_date` property. Email at anniversary-1-year: subject "It's been 1 year — thanks for being a customer" with `$15 off` + `review-request` dual-CTA. Email at anniversary-2-year: subject "2 years! Here's $20 off + a curated gift" with the curated-gift block + personal-note from founder. Email at anniversary-5-year: subject "5 years! ## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Birthday flow fires on the account-creation date for customers who never filled in their birthday field.** Klaviyo's default `Profile.birthday` field falls back to the customer's account-creation date when the field is empty, and the operator ships "happy birthday — $20 off" in a random month to every customer who signed up in that month. The customer is confused, the email goes to spam, the operator assumes "birthday flow doesn't work." **Fix: enforce a `Profile.birthday is not empty AND Profile.birthday is not the same as Profile.created_at` filter; add a "fill in your birthday for $5 off" capture form at account creation + first-order + loyalty-program-enrollment to populate the field. Klaviyo's date-triggered flow's "filter" feature is the canonical guard.**
2. **Anniversary flow fires on the wrong date because the operator uses the customer's last-purchase date instead of first-purchase date.** Customer who purchased 3 years ago and again last week receives a "3-year anniversary" email within days of their most recent order, the timing feels off, the customer is confused. **Fix: use Klaviyo's `Date of first order` custom property (auto-populated by Klaviyo's `Placed Order` event with the FIRST-occurrence timestamp) OR a dedicated `first_purchase_date` property populated by the operator's Shopify/ZenCommerce webhook. The 1-year / 2-year / 5-year / 10-year anniversary is a FIRST-purchase-date celebration, NOT a last-purchase-date celebration.**
3. **Loyalty-points-expiry warning fires the day-of-expiry (not 30 days before).** Smile.io's default `points_expiring` webhook fires T-0 (the day the points expire), the customer has 0 time to act, the points lapse, the customer blames the brand. **Fix: configure Smile.io's `points_expiring` webhook to fire T-30 (30 days before expiry); the canonical 4-email cadence is 30d + 14d + 7d + 1d-before. The 1d-before final-warning is the highest-converting single email in the cadence (12-22% CVR per Smile 2024).**
4. **Seasonal-gift-guide Q4 calendar is built in November (not August).** Operator realizes Q4 starts in 6 weeks, rushes to build 4 emails + 1 SMS in 2 weeks, ships 2 of the 4 with broken product recommendations + 1 with a wrong shipping-deadline date, Q4 revenue lift is +3% (vs the 8-25% target). **Fix: build the Q4 calendar in AUGUST (4 months lead time); 4 calendar-reminders: Aug 1 (build kickoff) / Oct 1 (build complete) / Nov 1 (first-email sent) / Dec 22 (final-email sent). The Baymard 2024 Q4-gift-guide benchmark shows 8-25% revenue lift only when the cadence is built + tested + segmented ahead of time.**
5. **Birthday flow uses the same `$20 off` code for high-AOV and low-AOV customers; high-AOV customers feel "discounted" and unsubscribe.** Customer with $300 AOV receives "your $20 off gift" and feels the brand is commoditizing itself; the canonical Sephora / Starbucks / Amazon Prime approach is no-discount + curated gift for high-LTV customers. **Fix: per-cohort branching: high-AOV customers (top 20% by AOV) get "your curated gift concierge" (no discount, personal-stylist-CTA) instead of the 20% off; first-time customers get 25% off first-order + 30-day-extended-birthday-window; mid-AOV get the standard 20% off. The 3-branch template is the canonical best-in-class.**
6. **Anniversary flow's review-request + referral-CTA fire on the same email, customer only clicks one.** Operator stacks 3 CTAs in the 1-year-anniversary email (review / referral / discount-code), the email's primary CTA gets 1.2% CTR (vs 4-8% best-in-class) because the 3 CTAs compete for attention. **Fix: split the 1-year anniversary into 2 emails — Email #1 at anniversary-day (thanks + $15 off) + Email #2 at anniversary+7d (review-request OR referral-CTA, but not both). The 2-email sequence lifts 30-day reorder rate by 8-15% vs the 3-CTA single-email approach.**
7. **Loyalty-points-expiry warning emails a customer who has 0 redeemable points (balance is 0).** Smile.io's `points_expiring` webhook can fire when the customer's balance has already dropped to 0 (e.g., they used the points but the webhook fired on the original expiry), the customer is confused ("I don't have any points?"). **Fix: filter the flow's audience on `Smile-points-balance > 0` at the time of the email send (NOT at the time of the webhook trigger); the canonical 4-email cadence gates the first email on a positive balance check, skips the customer if balance is 0. Klaviyo's flow filter `Profile.smile_points_balance > 0` is the canonical guard.**
8. **Seasonal-gift-guide ships the same product grid to every customer; the "I already bought this" anti-pattern fires.** Customer who bought the operator's flagship sweater last month receives the gift-guide email with that sweater as the top recommendation, the customer is annoyed ("you should know I just bought this"), unsubscribes. **Fix: use Klaviyo's dynamic product block + a `bought_in_last_90d` exclusion filter; the canonical best-in-class product grid excludes any SKU the customer has purchased in the last 90 days AND any SKU in the customer's "already-viewed" browse history. The Klaviyo Product Feed + dynamic block is the canonical engine.**
9. **Birthday + anniversary + points-expiry + gift-guide + cart-abandon + welcome + winback + browse-abandon + tier-up + NPS-promoter ALL fire on the same customer in 30 days, customer gets 4-5 emails in 1 week.** Customer is overwhelmed, marks the operator as spam, sender reputation drops 5-15% across the entire flow library. **Fix: enforce a 1-discount-per-customer-per-30-days rule via Klaviyo's "suppress this flow if customer is in discount-redeemed-segment in last 30d" filter; the canonical guard applies to ALL discount-bearing flows (birthday + anniversary + points-expiry + gift-guide + cart-abandon + welcome + winback + browse-abandon + tier-up). For the celebratory-flow stack specifically, the canonical cadence is 1 celebratory email per customer per 30 days (with the other 3 celebratory flows suppressed for that 30-day window).**
10. **Seasonal-gift-guide ships the "last shipping dates" email on Dec 1 but the operator's carrier cut-off is Dec 18 (not Dec 22).** Customer orders on Dec 20 expecting delivery by Dec 24, package arrives Dec 28, customer is angry, the operator has to issue a refund + pay for return-shipping. **Fix: the canonical last-shipping-date email must reflect the operator's ACTUAL carrier cut-offs (USPS / UPS / FedEx / DHL per-zone); build the cut-off table in August with the carrier's published holiday schedule, NOT the customer's expected cut-off. The Dec 22 final-week email is the LAST email of the season — after Dec 22 the gift-guide flow stops sending (else the customer is promised delivery they can't get).**
11. **Loyalty-points-expiry warning emails are sent from a no-reply@brand.com address; customer with a question (e.g., "can I combine these with my birthday discount?") can't reply.** Customer replies to the warning email, gets a bounce-back, the operator's CS team never sees the question, the customer churns. **Fix: all celebratory emails must be sent from a reply-enabled address (`hello@brand.com` or a CS-rep's name + title); wire Gorgias (or Help Scout / Zendesk) to convert the email reply into a ticket. The canonical "no-reply vs reply-enabled" choice is the same as the NPS-detractor routing in skill/493.**
12. **Anniversary flow's 5-year and 10-year emails use the same copy + CTA as the 1-year email; the 5-year customer feels under-celebrated.** Customer with 5 years of $2,000+ LTV receives "thanks + $20 off" — the same as a 1-year customer — and feels the brand doesn't recognize their loyalty. **Fix: per-tier branching on the anniversary email: 1-year gets $15 off + review-request, 2-year gets $20 off + curated gift, 5-year gets a premium-product-shipped (the operator's most-LTV-driving SKU) + personal note from founder, 10-year gets a luxury gift + personal call from CS-rep. The 4-tier branching is the canonical best-in-class.**
13. **Birthday flow's email #3 (birthday+7d "last chance") fires BEFORE the customer has had time to use the gift from email #2.** Customer receives the birthday-day email at 9am, doesn't open it until 8pm, email #3 fires the next morning at 9am (24 hours later), customer feels rushed, doesn't redeem. **Fix: enforce a minimum 7-day window between email #2 (birthday-day) and email #3 (birthday+7d) AND a `last_email_opened = false` check on email #3 (if the customer hasn't opened email #2, skip email #3 to avoid the spam-flagging anti-pattern). Klaviyo's flow filter + smart-sending window is the canonical guard.**
14. **Seasonal-gift-guide's "ship directly to recipient with gift-wrap" CTA links to a non-existent Shopify gift-wrap product; the CTA fails to convert.** Operator's gift-guide email says "ship directly to recipient" but the Shopify checkout doesn't have a gift-wrap add-on product, the customer abandons, the operator has to apologize. **Fix: BUILD the Shopify gift-wrap add-on product + recipient-address-capture field BEFORE the Q4 email cadence launches (Aug-Oct build window); the canonical product is `[Brand] Gift Wrap — $[X]` with a recipient-name + recipient-address custom-property. Without the underlying product, the CTA is broken.**
15. **Birthday + anniversary + points-expiry + gift-guide flows are built in isolation (not connected to the existing loyalty + post-purchase + winback flows).** The 4 celebratory flows fire on personal moments but the customer has no context for why the brand knows their birthday (no loyalty-enrollment email, no post-purchase cross-sell in the prior 60 days, no NPS-survey sent in the last 90 days). **Fix: the 4 celebratory flows are the FINAL layer of a connected lifecycle stack — they depend on Move #1 (cart-abandon) + Move #4 (welcome) + Move #7 (SMS) + Move #8 (loyalty program) + skill/490 (browse-abandon) + skill/491 (winback-sunset) + skill/492 (post-purchase) + skill/493 (loyalty engagement) all being live first. The canonical "Pillar 5 ships last" rationale is that birthday + anniversary + points-expiry + gift-guide only compound when the customer has already had 4+ prior touchpoints with the brand in the last 12 months. Without the prior pillars, the celebratory moments feel random.**
