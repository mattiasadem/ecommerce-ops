## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Measuring ROAS as last-click-attribution only** — the canonical iOS-14.5+-era + post-cookie-deprecation hit where Meta + TikTok + Google's last-click attribution over-credits view-through conversions from low-LTV cohorts and under-credits high-LTV cohort conversions from brand-search or post-purchase-email triggers. A creative with `lastClickROAS: 2.5×` is often `cohortLTVROAS: 1.1×` per Move #6.5 + Move #6.10 + iOS-14.5+ benchmarks. **Fix:** Move #10.5 MANDATES cohort-LTV-overlay as the canonical-attribution-substrate — surface both `lastClickROAS` AND `cohortLTVROAS` AND `lastClickVsCohortLTVDelta` for every per-creative × per-cohort × per-daypart cell.

2. **Skipping the Move #6.5 attribution-quality baseline check** — without `gateOverallPassed === true` AND `lastAuditTimestamp >= window`, cohort-LTV-overlay is unreliable (per Move #6.5 benchmarks). **Fix:** Move #10.5 auto-kill-criteria includes `attribution-quality-degraded-kill-criteria` — kill if Move #6.5 attribution-quality-gate-failed AND cohort-LTV-overlay reliability drops below 0.85. Move #10.5 auto-scale-criteria includes `attribution-quality-degraded-scale-protection` — DO-NOT-SCALE if Move #6.5 attribution-quality-gate-failed.

3. **Auto-killing creative at low TRL (time-resolved-lifetime)** — a creative launched <7 days ago may have artificially-low cohort-LTV-overlay because cohort-LTV-tracker needs time to mature. **Fix:** Move #10.5 auto-fire-quiet-period of 7d × cohort-LTV-overlay-confidence-band > 0.95. Never auto-fire within ≤7d of creative-launch unless cohort-LTV-overlay-confidence-band > 0.95.

4. **Auto-killing creative on cohort-LTV-overlay cold-start noise** — cohort-LTV-overlay has cold-start noise during the first 3-7 days of a creative's lifecycle. **Fix:** Move #10.5 auto-fire-quiet-period of 7d × `attribution-event-count > 30 cohort × $30 effective-spend` per Move #6.10 + Move #6.11 benchmarks.

5. **Auto-scaling creative to budget without surfacing calibration** — a creative with `cohortLTVROAS: 1.6×` may be at the edge of the calibration window. **Fix:** Move #10.5 auto-scale-criteria includes `scale-to-1.5×` (the canonical calibration-tier) + `scale-to-2×` (the canonical aggressive-tier) + `scale-to-3×` (the canonical aggressive-tier-with-cohort-LTV-delta > +30% vs 7d-baseline). Always start at scale-to-1.5× first.

6. **Auto-scaling creative to budget when LTV-illusion-trap fires** — a creative with `lastClickROAS: 3.0×` but `cohortLTVROAS: 1.4×` is a LTV-illusion-trap per Move #6.5 benchmarks. **Fix:** Move #10.5 auto-scale-criteria includes `cohort-LTV-illusion-trap-scale-protection` — DO-NOT-SCALE if `lastClickROAS > cohortLTVROAS × 1.5` (the canonical LTV-illusion-trap surface).

7. **Not surfacing the canonical per-creative × per-cohort × per-daypart × 3-lookback-window LTV-overlay table to the operator** — the operator needs to see ALL 3,150 per-creative-cohort-daypart-window cells (7 cohorts × 5 dayparts × 3 lookback-windows × 30 AI-creative-variants = 3,150 cells) on a single dashboard. **Fix:** Move #10.5 dashboard (Phase 3 above) renders all 3,150 cells in the canonical-per-creative-LTV-overlay-table with sortable + filterable + demo-mode + URL-param routing.
