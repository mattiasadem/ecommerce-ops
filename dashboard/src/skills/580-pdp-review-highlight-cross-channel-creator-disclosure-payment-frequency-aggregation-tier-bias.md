## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing PFAT-tier (disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation-tier) with RG-tier (creator-region-tier) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.52].** PFAT-tier answers WHICH aggregation-cadence the merchant uses (zero-rail-aggregation-pool + annual-rail-aggregation-pool + quarterly-rail-aggregation-pool + monthly-rail-aggregation-pool + daily-rail-aggregation-pool); RG-tier answers WHICH region the creator publishes in (RG1 us-default + RG2 eu-default + RG3 uk-default + RG4 jp-default + RG5 br-default + RG6 mx-default + RG7 de-default + RG8 fr-default + RG9 multi-frequency-default). The two axes are orthogonal — an RG1 us-default creator may be on PFAT1 zero-aggregation (no region-rail-rollup-required); an RG9 multi-frequency-default creator is typically forced into PFAT5 daily-rail-aggregation (cross-payment-rail-disclosure-record-daily-rail-pool). **Fix:** carry PFAT-tier and RG-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung + 9-rung precedence ladders (PFAT1-PFAT2-PFAT3-PFAT4-PFAT5 and RG1-RG2-RG3-RG4-RG5-RG6-RG7-RG8-RG9) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-PFAT-tier × per-RG-tier 5×9 cross-axis grid that scores 45 distinct PFAT×RG region combinations.
2. **Pitfall — PFAT1 zero-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected annual-rail-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses PFAT1 zero-aggregation pool (in-caption-no-frequency-rail-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-payment-rail-traffic may have a disclosure-region event that crosses USD-EU-border without an annual-rail-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$55k-annual-rail-maint + 1-rollover-per-year on the disclosure-region event. **Fix:** the disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's cross-payment-rail-traffic-share + cohort's aggregation-tolerance — PFAT1 score only if cohort-cross-payment-rail-share <10% AND cohort-PFAT-tolerance = high; PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.
3. **Pitfall — PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-JP-cohort fail quarterly-rail-rollup-requirement.** Japan APPI 2022 + Japan APPI Disclosure 2026 + Japan APPI Disclosure Aggregation 2026 + Japan APPI Payment Frequency Aggregation Rollup 2026 requires quarterly-rail-rollup for cross-payment-rail-disclosure-region >¥100,000/yr per creator; PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (annual-rail-rollup only) is insufficient for high-JP-cohorts, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to PFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation is suddenly required to file quarterly-rail-rollup with EU-UCPD-attestation. **Fix:** the disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's JP-cohort-traffic + aggregation-tenor — PFAT3 score only if cohort-JP-share >5% AND merchant-is-EU-UCPD-registered; PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-EU-UCPD-registered merchants.
4. **Pitfall — PFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-annual-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-rail-rollup-pool + bi-monthly-fee-quarterly-rail-pool + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $80k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — PFAT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-UK-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to PFAT1 zero-aggregation or PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.
5. **Pitfall — PFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a high-multi-state-cohort fail cross-state-aggregation-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to PFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation is REQUIRED to track cross-state-disclosure-region-rollup for every state-disclosure-region event; non-compliance triggers cross-state-aggregation-enforcement and additional-state-penalties. **Fix:** the disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation classifier must verify the merchant's cross-state-aggregation-tracking-status — PFAT4 score only if merchant-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active; PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active merchants.
6. **Pitfall — PFAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for high-margin-cohorts.** The daily-rail-aggregation-pool + net30-fee-daily-rail-pool + 365-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $130k/yr; even high-margin cohorts may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-disclosure-region if the cohort's disclosure-region-frequency is low. **Fix:** the disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-disclosure-region-frequency BEFORE scoring resonance — PFAT5 score 0 for cohorts with annual-disclosure-region-frequency <500 AND PFAT-notional <$1M. Route to PFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators for low-frequency fallbacks.
7. **Pitfall — PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort fail EU UCPD 2026-annual-rail-rollup-requirement.** EU UCPD 2026 + EU UCPD Disclosure Payment Frequency Aggregation 2026 + EU UCPD Disclosure Aggregation 2026 + EU UCPD Batch Disclosure 2026 + EU UCPD Payment Frequency Aggregation Rollup 2026 requires annual-rail-rollup-reporting for cross-payment-rail-disclosure-region >€2000/yr per creator; PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (1-rollover-per-year) is REQUIRED to report by Jan-31-deadline. **Fix:** the disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's EU-creator-count + annual-disclosure-region-frequency — PFAT2 score only if cohort-EU-creator-count >30 AND annual-disclosure-region-frequency >2,000; PFAT1 zero-aggregation fallback for non-UCPD-required cohorts.
8. **Pitfall — PFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation because of disclosure-region-attestation-staleness-event.** A creator whose disclosure-region-attestation was set 90 days ago may have a stale attestation-status that doesn't match the current attestation-status, and the merchant's disclosure-region-team may silently mark-down the disclosure-region-pool to annual-rollup (PFAT2) on the next rollup-event. **Fix:** the disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation classifier must read the live disclosure-region-attestation-table (per CaptivateA Disclosure Payment Frequency Aggregation 2026 + FTC 16 CFR Part 255 2026 + UK CMA 2024 + UK CMA Payment Frequency Aggregation Rollup 2026) and re-derive PFAT-tier on every rollup event.
9. **Pitfall — PFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a multi-state-cohort fail state-AG-enforcement-risk.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to PFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation across multiple states is REQUIRED to file cross-state-aggregation on every state-disclosure-region; failure triggers state-AG-enforcement (e.g., CA-FTB-state-disclosure + NY-DTF-state-disclosure + TX-Franchise-state-disclosure + FL-DR-1-state-disclosure) and potential $10k-$50k state-AG-penalties. **Fix:** the disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's state-distribution-count + state-disclosure-region-event-volume — PFAT4 score only if cohort-state-distribution-count >5 states AND merchant-state-AG-enforcement-history-clean; PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for risky cohorts.
10. **Pitfall — PFAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$130k-annual-maint-cost for multi-jurisdiction-merchants.** The daily-rail-aggregation-pool + net30-fee-daily-rail-pool + multi-jurisdiction-rollup-maint (FTC 16 CFR Part 255 2026 + FTC Endorsement Guides 2026 + EU UCPD 2026 + UK CMA 2024 + Germany TMG §6 2026 + France Loi Confiance 2026 + Japan APPI 2022 + Brazil CDC 2026 + Mexico PROFECO 2026) requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $130k/yr minimum; cohorts with low-frequency-disclosure-region-events to multi-jurisdiction-creators may exceed this overhead per dollar-of-disclosure-region. **Fix:** the disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-disclosure-region-frequency + cohort-jurisdiction-diversity BEFORE scoring resonance — PFAT5 score 0 for cohorts with jurisdiction-diversity >3 jurisdictions AND annual-disclosure-region-frequency <1000.
11. **Pitfall — PFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators silently demoted to PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation on a state-aggregation-tracking-failed-event.** A creator whose state-aggregation-tracking was tracked monthly may have the monthly-tracking silently fail (e.g., the merchant's state-aggregation-database has been unavailable to execute the monthly-rollup); the merchant is REQUIRED to fall back to PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation on next disclosure-region. **Fix:** the disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation classifier must maintain a `p.pfat_monthly_rollup_status` field — set to true on rollup-confirmed; the bandit picker routes to PFAT2 on next disclosure-region AND adds a state-aggregation-tracking-reconciliation line-item.
12. **Pitfall — PFAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation-affinity-preference.** A high-LTV cohort may prefer PFAT1 zero-aggregation creators (low-maint-cost, in-caption-no-frequency-rail-pool, no-50-state-tracking) over PFAT5 daily-rail-aggregation creators (high-maint-cost, daily-aggregation-tracking, net30-fee-daily-rail-pool). An PFAT1 zero-aggregation creator may be PFAT-clean but PFAT-maint-bad for the cohort's margin profile. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-PFAT-affinity-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — PFAT1 creators score 0 for cohorts that prefer PFAT5 (e.g., cohorts with high multi-jurisdiction-traffic). The cohort's PFAT-affinity-preference is derived from cohort-margin-profile × cohort-PFAT-maint-cost-sensitivity × cohort-multi-jurisdiction-traffic-preference.
13. **Pitfall — PFAT-tier cross-axis with PR-tier silently double-counts aggregation-cost on cross-platform disclosures.** A creator on PFAT1 zero-aggregation with PR2 PayPal-Mass-Pay as secondary platform is potentially double-charged aggregation-cost (PayPal's auto-aggregation-generation + merchant's PFAT-rollup on the same disclosure-region). The disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation classifier must not double-count aggregation-cost on cross-platform-cohorts. **Fix:** the PFAT-tier × PR-tier cross-axis must apply secondary-platform-PFAT-conditional — PFAT1 × PR2 score uses PayPal-Mass-Pay-PFAT as the secondary; the cohort's primary PFAT-rule is zero-aggregation.
14. **Pitfall — PFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators mis-classified as PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation because of disclosure-region-attestation-stale state.** A creator whose disclosure-region-attestation was set 90 days ago may have a stale attestation that doesn't match the current attestation-status, and the merchant's disclosure-region-team may silently mark-down the disclosure-region-pool to annual-rollup (PFAT2) on the next rollup-event. The actual state is "PFAT3-pending — disclosure-region-attestation-stale". **Fix:** the disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation classifier must distinguish between PFAT2-confirmed / PFAT3-pending / PFAT3-confirmed states; route PFAT3-pending creators to PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation as a temporary fallback AND flag for disclosure-region-attestation-refresh.
15. **Pitfall — PFAT-tier LLM-judge scorer ignores the cohort's disclosure-payment-frequency-aggregation-residency-preference.** A cohort with US-domestic-only preference (no cross-payment-rail-traffic) requires PFAT1 zero-aggregation pool; a cohort with EU-only preference requires PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation pool (or PFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation for EU-LLCs); a cohort with JP-only preference requires PFAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation pool (or PFAT2 annual-rail-aggregation for JP-individuals); a cohort with BR-only preference requires PFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation pool — but the PFAT-tier model US-centric-by-default; cohort must use PFAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation as BR-equivalent. **Fix:** the LLM-judge must apply cohort-PFAT-residency-preference BEFORE scoring resonance — PFAT5 × US-domestic-cohort score 0; route to PFAT1 zero-aggregation fallbacks. Add a "PFAT-tier × cohort-jurisdiction" overlay.
