## Common pitfalls (21)

1. **P1 — Don't wire per-cohort-affinity-loyalty-engine-version telemetry against the wrong loyalty-event-stream.** Smile.io-Loyalty emits to a different event-stream than Yotpo-Loyalty / LoyaltyLion-Loyalty / Friendbuy-Loyalty; wire to the engine-version's specific event-stream endpoint (e.g. Smile.io webhook endpoint vs Yotpo events endpoint vs LoyaltyLion profile-update endpoint). A wire-test verifies ≥ 1 event/100-checkout-attempts per loyalty-engine-version.
2. **P2 — 33 NEW quinque-centesimal-axis event-streams must be wired FIRST.** Don't instrument funnel-telemetry AFTER dashboards are built — telemetry freshness is required for layer-1 to be load-bearing. Each of the 33 quinque-centesimal-axis dimensions must emit ≥ 1 value per 100 checkout-attempts cross-cohort, cross-LTV-tier.
3. **P3 — Subscription-engine-version-stale is a billing-cycle risk, not just a leak-rate risk.** Recharge-Subscription vs Bold-Subscription vs Appstle-Subscription vs Loop-Subscription emits billing-cycle events with different schema; mis-wiring can cause hidden dunning-cycle-billing-failures → involuntary-churn. Wire-test verifies ≥ 1 billing-cycle-event/100-checkout-attempts per subscription-engine-version with a per-engine-version schema-validator (per-cohort-affinity-billing-cycle-event-schema-version-stale is a SEPARATE cause-diagnosis D346).
4. **P4 — Customer-journey-engine-version vs journey-orchestration-engine-version vs journey-step-engine-version are THREE different sub-cell-variants, NOT the same.** Iterable-Customer-Journey emits different event-streams for /journey vs /orchestration vs /step endpoints; merging them loses per-cohort-affinity-customer-journey-engine-version-resolution. Wire-test verifies per-endpoint funnel-telemetry with separate event-stream IDs.
5. **P5 — Loyalty vs Rewards vs Referral vs Affiliate are FOUR different engines.** Smile.io-Loyalty vs Smile.io-Rewards vs Friendbuy-Referral vs Refersion-Affiliate are NOT interchangeable — a "loyalty" applies to points/tiers/vip, a "rewards" applies to redemptions/catalog, a "referral" applies to friend-invite-program, an "affiliate" applies to external-publisher-commission. Confusing them causes over-reward or under-reward leak-cells.
6. **P6 — Lifecycle-message and lifecycle-trigger events are EVENT-DRIVEN, not POLLING-DRIVEN.** Klaviyo-Lifecycle / Iterable-Lifecycle / Braze-Lifecycle emit per-message-send events; Klaviyo-Lifecycle-Trigger / Iterable-Lifecycle-Trigger / Braze-Lifecycle-Trigger emit per-trigger-fire events. Polling-driven funnel-telemetry loses per-event freshness; use event-stream subscriber.
7. **P7 — Don't conflate attribution-engine-version with experimentation-engine-version.** Triple-Whale-Attribution / Northbeam-Attribution / Rockerbox-Attribution is the ATTRIBUTION decision (which channel/source drove this conversion), NOT the EXPERIMENTATION engine (Optimizely-Experimentation / VWO-Experimentation / LaunchDarkly-Experimentation). The Move #3.46 quinque-centesimal-axis covers BOTH; attribution-engine is a Move #3.46 attribution-engine-version dimension and experimentation-engine is a separate Move #3.46 experimentation-engine-version dimension.
8. **P8 — Cohort-evolution-engine-version vs cross-cohort-engine-version are TWO different engines.** Amperity-Cohort-Evolution emits different event-streams for /cohort-evolution vs /cross-cohort endpoints; merging them loses per-cohort-affinity-cohort-evolution-engine-version-resolution. Wire-test verifies per-endpoint funnel-telemetry with separate event-stream IDs.
9. **P9 — LLM-engine-version-rollout bypasses Move #3.46 loyalty-engine-version during BFCM-peak → 60-80% loyalty-event-loss.** When the operator rolls out a new Smile.io-Loyalty engine-version during BFCM-peak, the Smile.io-Loyalty telemetry stream takes 4-8 hours to stabilize; checkout-attempts during this window emit empty `c.cohort_affinity_loyalty_engine_version` values → funnel-telemetry blank-cells → completion-rate-counter under-reports by 60-80%. Mitigation: Move #3.46's Layer-6 26-engine rollback-stack includes a per-cohort-affinity-loyalty-engine-version-rollout-window-guard that forces fallback to the previous stable loyalty-engine-version for 4-8 hours during a rollout.
10. **P10 — Global per-cohort-affinity-quinque-centesimal-axis-trait-many-rollback kills high-performers.** Rolling back ALL 33 quinque-centesimal-axis-trait-many simultaneously reverts to a previous-funnel-state that may have a LOWER completion-rate on a non-leaking cell. The fix is per-trait rollback: only rollback the specific loyalty-engine-version that's leaking, NOT all 33 axes.
11. **P11 — Don't reach for Yotpo-Loyalty as a 1:1 substitute for LoyaltyLion-Loyalty or Smile.io-Loyalty.** Each loyalty-engine has different reward-tier-coverage (points vs tiers vs VIP vs paid-membership). A merchant with mixed-loyalty-strategy needs ALL loyalty-engines instrumented, not one. Wire-test verifies per-reward-tier-event emission per loyalty-engine-version.
