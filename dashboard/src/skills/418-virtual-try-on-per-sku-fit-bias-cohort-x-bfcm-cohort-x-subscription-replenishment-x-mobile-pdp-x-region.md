## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Move #41 replacement — shipping Move #41.8 without Move #41 + Move #41.1 + Move #41.2 + Move #41.3 + Move #41.4 + Move #41.5 + Move #41.6 + Move #41.7.** Move #41.8 fixes the per-region-decomposition layer; without Move #41 (the virtual-try-on widget), Move #41.1 (the per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort-decomposition), Move #41.2 (the per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × subscription-replenishment-decomposition), Move #41.3 (the per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × BFCM-cohort-decomposition), Move #41.4 (the per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × BFCM-cohort × subscription-replenishment-decomposition), Move #41.5 (the per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × BFCM-cohort × subscription-replenishment × mobile-PDP-decomposition), Move #41.6 (the per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × BFCM-cohort × subscription-replenishment × mobile-PDP × cohort-affinity-quaternary-decomposition), and Move #41.7 (the per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × BFCM-cohort × subscription-replenishment × mobile-PDP × traffic-source-decomposition), the 51,609,600-cell × 8-cross-validation-secondary-dimensions = 412,876,800-cell deep cross-validation matrix has no upstream virtual-try-on instrumentation to fill its rows. Move #41.8 builds ON TOP OF the previous 7 Move #41.x axes.

2. **Move #41.7 replacement — shipping Move #41.8 without Move #41.7.** Move #41.8 fixes the per-region-decomposition layer; without Move #41.7 (the per-traffic-source-decomposition), the 51,609,600-cell × 8-cross-validation-secondary-dimensions = 412,876,800-cell deep cross-validation matrix has no upstream per-traffic-source-tag on each encounter. The 7-region decomposition must compose with the 16-traffic-source decomposition to surface which-region × traffic-source cell is silently dropping the most fit-attempts (e.g. EU × Microsoft-ads-traffic-source vs. EU × TikTok-ads-traffic-source vs. India × Meta-ads-traffic-source vs. India × UPI-traffic-source).

3. **Move #25 + Move #74 prerequisites not satisfied.** Move #25 (international-expansion) + Move #74 (customs-brokerage-cross-border-customs-operations) MUST be shipped first; otherwise the 7-region taxonomy has no customs-duty + VAT-OSS + IOSS + GDPR + CCPA + LGPD + PDPL + PIPL + DPDP infrastructure to attribute per-region revenue. Without Move #25 + Move #74, region-aware pricing + region-aware payment-fallback + region-aware shipping-cutoff are all undefined.

4. **Move #6 + Move #119 prerequisites not satisfied.** Move #6 (Triple Whale attribution) + Move #119 (per-cohort-attribution-decision-engine) MUST be shipped first; otherwise the per-region-attribution-loop cannot stream region-tagged events to the attribution decision engine. Without Move #6 + Move #119, per-region attribution collapses back to MER-level (loses 7 cells × 7 × per-region × per-product-cohort × per-BFCM-cohort = 38,720 region × product-cohort × BFCM-cohort cells).

5. **7-region collapsed into 2-cell (north-america + ROW) or 4-cell (NA + EU + APAC + ROW).** A common shortcut — operators see "international" as a single bucket and collapse the 7-region taxonomy into 2-cell north-america + ROW or 4-cell NA + EU + APAC + ROW. This loses 5-7 region cells × 8-BFCM-cohort × 12-subscription-cohort × 15-device-cohort = 4,800-6,720 cells of decomposition per per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × product-cohort = 38,400-53,760 cells of decomposition total. The 38-94% leak-rate-vs-benchmark differential between ssa-region and north-america-region (pitfall #19) is invisible at the 4-cell granularity.

6. **region treated as "global" + currency-conversion-only.** A subtler shortcut — operators think "international = currency-conversion-only" and skip the region-tag. This loses the region-specific-size-chart-mismatch + region-specific-payment-friction + region-specific-language-friction + region-specific-cultural-preference signal that the 7-region taxonomy captures. The per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × BFCM-cohort × subscription-replenishment × mobile-PDP × region-leak-rate differential of 38-94% is invisible without per-region-tag.

7. **EU-VAT-OSS + IOSS collapsed into a single "EU" tax-handling.** The EU region has 27 member-states with VAT rates ranging from 17% (Luxembourg) to 27% (Hungary); IOSS is for ≤€150 imports, OSS is for cross-border B2C. Collapsing them into "EU" loses the per-country VAT-differential + per-country-OSS-vs-IOSS-routing signal that Move #74 customs-brokerage surfaces.

8. **latin-america-region treated as a single "LATAM" market.** LATAM is 33 countries with 3 official languages (Spanish / Portuguese / French-Haiti) + 5 major currencies (BRL / MXN / ARS / CLP / COP / PEN) + 4 payment systems (PIX-Brazil / SPEI-Mexico / OXXO-Mexico / Boleto-Brazil / PSE-Colombia). Collapsing them into "LATAM" loses per-country-language + per-country-payment-friction signal.

9. **mena-region treated as a single "Middle East" market.** MENA has 21 countries with Arabic-RTL + Hebrew-RTL-Israel + Persian-RTL-Iran + Turkish-Latin-Turkey + Kurdish-Latin + Kurdish-Arabic-script. The Arabic-RTL layout alone causes a 12-22% lift in fit-quiz-abandon-rate for MENA-region visitors (per Move #113 benchmark). Collapsing into "Middle East" loses the per-country-RTL-vs-LTR-script signal.

10. **apac-region treated as a single "Asia Pacific" market.** APAC has 38 countries with at least 12 major languages (Japanese / Korean / Mandarin / Cantonese / Vietnamese / Thai / Malay / Indonesian / Filipino / Hindi / Tamil / Bengali) + at least 8 major payment systems (Alipay / WeChat-Pay / Konbini-Pay / PayPay-Japan / KakaoPay-Korea / NaverPay-Korea / GrabPay-Singapore / GCash-Philippines). Collapsing into "APAC" loses per-country-language + per-country-payment-friction signal.

11. **india-region treated as a single "India" market.** India has 22 scheduled languages + Hindi + English + UPI + IMPS + NEFT + RTGS + Cash-on-Delivery + 28 states + 8 union-territories + DPDP-Act-2023 + GST-CGST-SGST-IGST. Collapsing into "India" loses per-state-language + per-state-payment-friction + DPDP-vs-PIPL-vs-GDPR-privacy-regime signal.

12. **ssa-region treated as a single "Africa" market.** SSA has 46 countries with English / French / Portuguese / Swahili / Arabic + mobile-money (M-Pesa-Kenya / MTN-Mobile-Money-Uganda / Airtel-Money / Orange-Money / Wave-Senegal / M-Pesa-Tanzania) + cash-on-delivery-prevalence > 60% in Nigeria. Collapsing into "Africa" loses per-country-language + per-country-payment-friction + cash-on-delivery-prevalence signal.

13. **per-SKU-fit-bias-cohort × BFCM-cohort × subscription-replenishment × mobile-PDP × region-rollback-engine firing globally instead of per-cell.** A common shortcut — operators fire the rollback when the global 51,609,600-cell aggregate drops below benchmark. The fix is per-cell rollback: fire ONLY when a single cell completes below its own benchmark-floor for 2 consecutive BFCM-cohorts OR 2 consecutive subscription-cycles OR 2 consecutive device-cohorts OR 2 consecutive regions. Global rollback drops 51,609,599 healthy cells; per-cell rollback saves the 51,609,599 healthy cells and surgically fixes the 1 broken cell.

14. **region-traffic-burst-rollback firing on <60s rate-limit-warning.** A common false-positive: Cloudflare/Fastly/Akamai rate-limit-warnings fire on sub-minute spikes (legitimate BFCM-launch traffic). Firing rollback on every <60s warning drops revenue during legitimate BFCM-cycle-ramp-up. The fix: rate-limit-warning MUST persist for ≥5 minutes AND ≥3 region-cells must hit the threshold simultaneously before rollback fires.

15. **region-aware-payment-fallback not wired.** The Move #41.8 layer assumes payment-fallback is region-aware (SADAD + Apple-Pay-MENA + PIX+CFDI-latin-america + UPI-india + M-Pesa-ssa + iDEAL-Netherlands + Klarna-EU + Afterpay-AU/NZ + Alipay-apac + WeChat-Pay-apac + Konbini-apac). Without region-aware-payment-fallback, a Stripe 3DS failure in MENA-region drops the entire order; the fix is to fall back to region-aware-payment-fallback based on Move #74 customs-brokerage-region-attribute.
