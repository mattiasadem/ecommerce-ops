# Browse-abandon as Pillar 1 — the strategic case for the pre-cart 5–10×-volume pool

> Browse-abandon + consideration flows catch the **80–95% of site visitors who never reach the cart** — by far the largest pool of pre-purchase intent on any DTC site. Per Klaviyo's 2024 + 2025-Q1/Q2 benchmark reports, browse-abandon converts at **0.6–1.5%** (vs cart-abandon's 5–15%), but absolute volume is **5–10× larger** because the browse pool is 5–10× the cart pool, so total revenue from browse-abandon + consideration flows often matches or **exceeds** cart-abandon revenue. This is **Pillar 1** of the canonical 5-pillar lifecycle-marketing framework (research/05) — the only pillar that catches visitors **before they signal cart intent**, and the canonical pre-requisite to Move #14 (lifecycle marketing expansion) because it captures the **30–50% of site visitors who show any signal of purchase intent**, vs the **5–20%** that Move #1 (cart-abandon) captures alone. Ship after Move #1 (cart-abandon), Move #4 (welcome), and Move #7 (SMS welcome + cart-abandon) are all live and producing baseline data — the consideration flows reuse the same Klaviyo + Postscript + Smile wiring and the same segmentation hygiene, so they ship in **~4 hours of operator time** and return a Year-1 ROI band of **8:1–28:1** at the default $500k–$10M GMV DTC brand. The companion operator-build skill is `skills/490-browse-abandon-consideration-flows.md` (the Tier-2 detailed-wiring companion); this strategic-case skill is the Tier-1 "why ship this and how to position it" companion that the senior operator reads before approving the ~4-hour build sprint.

## When to use this skill

You have:

- Move #1 (cart-abandon flow) shipped with ≥6-week revenue baseline + KPI dashboard
- Move #4 (welcome series) shipped with ≥6-week revenue baseline + open-rate dashboard
- Move #7 (SMS welcome + cart-abandon) shipped with ≥4-week revenue baseline
- Klaviyo onsite JS installed and `Viewed Product` + `Searched Site` + `Added to Cart` + `Placed Order` + `Category Viewed` events flowing
- Postscript (or Attentive) SMS wired with sender registration + opt-in compliance (TCPA/GDPR/CASL/LGPD)
- Klaviyo + Postscript Flow Library pricing tier (typically Klaviyo's Email + SMS Marketing plan or higher)
- Triple Whale or Polar attribution installed (so the operator can quantify browse-abandon revenue per traffic source)
- ≥$2,000/mo paid-media spend (the brand has a steady inflow of new visitors to "browse")
- Operator capacity: 1-hour strategic-alignment block + 4-hour build block + 1-hour weekly maintenance block for the first 8 weeks
- ≥1,000 active customers segmented into ≥3 baseline segments (active customer / lapsed customer / subscriber-not-buyer)
- C-suite / VP-buy-in alignment on the "pre-cart pillar" framing (the build costs ~4 hours of operator time but creates a 30–50%-volume lift — the CFO must understand the framing)
- Site has ≥5,000 weekly `Viewed Product` events (the volume threshold below which browse-abandon emits <100 emails/wk and ROI is too thin to justify the build)

You do NOT have:

- A browse-abandon flow that catches the 80–95% who browse-only and never add-to-cart (the **single largest unmonetized pool** on any DTC site)
- A category-view flow that catches visitors who browse a category but don't view a product (catches category-curious visitors before they self-segment away from your hero SKUs)
- A search-no-click or search-no-conversion flow that catches visitors who search but don't engage (the **highest-intent pool of the library** — they typed their need into your search bar)
- An add-to-cart-no-checkout flow that catches the 5–15% who add to cart but abandon before checkout-start (the bridge between cart-abandon and browse-abandon — higher conversion than browse-only but lower volume)
- AI/SMS so you're leaving the bulk of pre-purchase intent on the table
- Per-product browse-abandon with dynamic product blocks (Klaviyo's `Viewed Product` event feeds the dynamic block; without the dynamic block, the email is generic and converts 30–50% lower)
- Discount-stacking guards (browse-abandon 10% off + cart-abandon 10% off + winback 15% off + sunset 20% off all firing on the same customer = margin destruction or discount-trained-customer)
- Smart Sending guard on browse-abandon so a single visitor doesn't get 4 emails + 2 SMS in 1 day
- A `Viewed Product → Added to Cart` window configured for ≥4 hours (the canonical "browse-then-buy" meditation window; <2 hours silently skips 60–70% of intent signals)
- Per-traffic-source browse-abandon subject lines (organic-search visitors convert with educational framing; paid-social visitors convert with offer framing — a single subject line captures <60% of the per-source lift)

## The build (time estimate: ~4 hours total)

This is a 5-phase build: (Phase 1: 30 min for strategic-alignment + success-metric definition, Phase 2: 1.5 hours for browse-abandon flow wiring, Phase 3: 1 hour for category-view + search-no-click + search-no-conversion + add-to-cart-no-checkout, Phase 4: 30 min for stock-back + price-drop + low-stock events, Phase 5: 30 min for verification + monitoring). Each phase is concrete and idempotent; you can pause and resume at any phase boundary. The **detailed operator-build steps** live in the companion skill `skills/490-browse-abandon-consideration-flows.md` (Tier-2 detailed-wiring) — this skill focuses on the **strategic alignment** + **Pillar-1 framing** + **how to defend the build cost to leadership**.

### Phase 1 — Strategic-alignment + success-metric definition (~30 min)

1. **Document the current state** (10 min). Export the prior 90 days of Klaviyo + Postscript flow performance: cart-abandon open-rate / CTR / CVR, welcome-series revenue per 1k recipients, total email+SMS revenue share. The export is the **baseline** that the browse-abandon build will be measured against (per the verification Gate A). Common baseline: cart-abandon = 60–80% of email revenue, browse-abandon = 0% (because the flow doesn't exist yet).
2. **Define the post-launch success metric** (10 min). Pick 2-3 KPIs the team will be measured on: (a) **browse-abandon flow revenue per 1,000 `Viewed Product` events per week** (target $300–$800/wk floor / $800–$1,500/wk stretch), (b) **browse-abandon flow share of total email+SMS revenue** (target ≥25% floor / ≥45% stretch), (c) **12-month incremental revenue per $1 pillar program cost** (target 8:1 floor / 28:1 stretch). Document the 3 KPIs in a `docs/browse-abandon-success-metrics.md` file so the team has a single source of truth.
3. **Get C-suite / VP alignment** (10 min). Walk through the 3 KPIs + the build-cost (~4 hours operator time + ~$100–$400/mo incremental tooling cost) + the Year-1 ROI band (8:1–28:1). The framing matters: "Pillar 1 captures the **30–50% of site visitors who show any signal of purchase intent** vs the 5–20% Move #1 alone captures" is the canonical strategic narrative. Get explicit budget approval before Phase 2 starts.

### Phase 2 — Browse-abandon flow wiring (~1.5 hours)

The detailed 8-step recipe lives in `skills/490-browse-abandon-consideration-flows.md` Phase 2. The high-level outline:

4. **Create the browse-abandon flow in Klaviyo** (5 min). Flows → Create → "Create from scratch" → `browse_abandon_v1`. Trigger: `Viewed Product` event with a `Viewed Product → Added to Cart` meditation window of **≥6 hours** (the canonical "browse-then-buy" window — Klaviyo's 2024 default is 4h, best-in-class is 6–12h).
5. **Add the 3-email + 2-SMS cadence** (30 min). Email #1 at +4h ("Saw this?" + dynamic product block), Email #2 at +24h ("Still thinking about it?" + 1-product dynamic block), Email #3 at +72h ("Last chance — 10% off"). SMS #1 at +24h ("You left [Product] in your cart" + short-link), SMS #2 at +72h ("10% off if you order today"). 3+2 is the canonical cadence; <3 emails misses the meditation window, >4 emails creates spam-complaint risk.
6. **Wire the per-LTV-tier + per-traffic-source branching** (15 min). Add a **conditional split** on Email #1: 1-time-buyer → "Saw this?" + 10% off / 2-4-time-buyer → "Saw this?" + free shipping / 5+-time-buyer → "Saw [Product]? Your VIP perks are waiting" / no-LTV signal (subscriber-not-buyer) → "Saw this? Here's 15% off your first order". Add a second conditional split on per-traffic-source: organic-search → educational framing, paid-social → offer framing, direct → personalized framing. The 3-tier × 3-traffic-source lift is the canonical 35–60% revenue lift.
7. **Wire the dynamic product block** (10 min). Each email needs a dynamic block fed by the `Viewed Product` event (Klaviyo's Product Feed). The block surfaces **"Products you viewed"** (last 4 viewed products) + **"Products you might like"** (Klaviyo's predictive recommendation). Without the dynamic block, the email is generic and converts 30–50% lower.
8. **Set the Smart Sending window to 16 hours** (5 min). Settings → Smart Sending → "Time between messages" → 16h. This prevents a single customer from getting browse-abandon email + cart-abandon email + welcome email + winback email all in 1 day. 16h is the canonical Klaviyo 2024 default; <12h is risky for spam complaints.
9. **Set the flow's send-time optimizer to "Smart Sending time"** (5 min). Klaviyo's "Send at the recipient's local time" feature. Critical for browse-abandon because new site visitors have variable time-zone habits; sending at 9am local time recovers 15–25% more opens.
10. **Wire the 1-discount-per-30-days guard** (10 min). Create a Klaviyo segment `discount_redeemed_last_30d` (definition: "has placed order in last 30d" OR "has clicked browse-abandon discount email in last 30d" OR "is in welcome series discount redemption in last 30d" OR "has redeemed a winback discount in last 30d"). Add as flow suppression filter. This is the canonical anti-discount-stacking guard — without it, 9–20% of browse-abandon sends overlap with cart-abandon / welcome / winback / sunset discounts.

### Phase 3 — Consideration-event flows (~1 hour)

The same detailed-operator-build recipe is in `skills/490-browse-abandon-consideration-flows.md` Phase 3.

11. **Category-view flow** (15 min). Flows → Create → `category_view_v1`. Trigger: `Category Viewed` event with a `Category Viewed → Viewed Product` meditation window of ≥12 hours. 1 email at +24h ("Still browsing [Category]? Here's what's trending"). Lower volume than browse-abandon but converts 30–50% of category-curious visitors.
12. **Search-no-click flow** (15 min). Flows → Create → `search_no_click_v1`. Trigger: `Searched Site` event with a `Searched Site → Viewed Product` meditation window of ≥4 hours AND `Searched Site → Added to Cart` of ≥24 hours. 1 email at +24h ("Didn't find what you were looking for? Here are [Category]'s top picks"). Catches 0-result searches (content-gap signal) + ≥1-result-no-click searches (relevance-gap signal).
13. **Search-no-conversion flow** (10 min). Flows → Create → `search_no_conversion_v1`. Trigger: `Searched Site` event with a `Searched Site → Viewed Product` of <4 hours AND `Searched Site → Placed Order` of ≥7 days. 1 email at +7d ("Still thinking about [search terms]? Here's a $10 off your first order"). Higher intent than search-no-click.
14. **Add-to-cart-no-checkout flow** (10 min). Flows → Create → `add_to_cart_no_checkout_v1`. Trigger: `Added to Cart` event with an `Added to Cart → Started Checkout` meditation window of ≥1 hour AND `Added to Cart → Placed Order` of ≥4 hours. 1 email at +4h ("Your cart is waiting — [Product]"). The bridge between browse-abandon and cart-abandon.
15. **Wire all 4 consideration flows to the same Smart Sending 16h + 1-discount-per-30d guard** (10 min). All 4 flows share the same Settings → Smart Sending + suppression segment.

### Phase 4 — Stock-back + price-drop + low-stock event flows (~30 min)

16. **Stock-back notification flow** (15 min). Flows → Create → `stock_back_v1`. Trigger: `Viewed Product` event for out-of-stock SKU + inventory-restock event for the same SKU within 30 days. 1 email at restock ("It's back — [Product]"), 1 SMS at restock ("[Product] is back in stock"). The highest-purchase-intent pool in the entire library — visitors who came back to check have intent already.
17. **Price-drop notification flow** (10 min). Flows → Create → `price_drop_v1`. Trigger: `Viewed Product` event + product-price-update event to a lower price within 30 days. 1 email at price-drop ("[Product] just dropped to $[X]").
18. **Low-stock notification flow** (5 min). Flows → Create → `low_stock_v1`. Trigger: `Viewed Product` event + inventory-low-stock event for the same SKU within 7 days. 1 email at low-stock ("[Product] is almost out — only [N] left").

### Phase 5 — Verification + monitoring (~30 min)

19. **Run the 14-gate verification suite** (15 min). See "Verification" section below. Each gate is a concrete pass/fail check; the Pillar 1 flows are "shipped" when all 14 pass.
20. **Set up the 12-metric monitoring dashboard** (10 min). The 12 metrics below each have a Klaviyo/Postscript/Triple Whale query + Slack alert.
21. **Document the 30-day post-launch flow-performance review** (5 min). Save a `docs/browse-abandon-30-day-review.md` checklist. Per Klaviyo 2024's best-practices guide, the Pillar 1 flows require a **30-day post-launch review** for: (a) per-flow CVR vs floor, (b) per-flow revenue per 1k recipients, (c) per-traffic-source branch distribution, (d) per-LTV-tier branch distribution, (e) discount-stacking incidents, (f) inbox-placement lift.

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Browse-abandon flow emits on every page view with no `Viewed Product → Added to Cart` meditation window.** The flow triggers on first page view, emails the visitor 4 hours later, the visitor has already added to cart and converted organically, the email is "stale" relative to the visitor's actual state. **Fix: the trigger MUST include both `Viewed Product` AND a meditation filter of `Viewed Product → Added to Cart` ≥ 4–6 hours (canonical Klaviyo default is 4h, best-in-class is 6–12h). The meditation window is the canonical "the visitor didn't convert because they left, not because they were interrupted" filter; <2h silently skips 60–70% of intent signals because the visitor is still mid-session. Verification Gate B is the canonical test.**
2. **Category-view flow fires on every category-view including the homepage and the "About" page.** Visitor lands on the homepage, fires `Category Viewed`, receives a "Still browsing [Category]?" email, marks as spam. **Fix: the category-view trigger MUST exclude the homepage / About / Contact / FAQ / Cart / Checkout pages. Klaviyo's "Viewed Category" property has a `category_id` field; the trigger should scope to `category_id EXISTS AND category_id NOT IN (homepage, about, contact, faq, cart, checkout)`. Verification Gate C is the canonical test (target: 0% of category-view recipients landed on a non-product-category page in last 24h).**
3. **Search-no-click flow treats 0-result searches and ≥1-result-no-click searches the same.** Visitor searches "purple shoes", gets 0 results, receives "Didn't find what you were looking for? Here are [Category]'s top picks" — the email is irrelevant because the category's top picks don't include purple shoes. **Fix: split the search-no-click flow into 2 branches — (a) 0-result search → "We don't have [search terms] — be the first to know when we do" + a back-in-stock subscription CTA, (b) ≥1-result-no-click → "Here are [search terms] results you might like". The 2-branch structure catches both content-gap (0-result) and relevance-gap (≥1-result-no-click) cases. Verification Gate D is the canonical test (target: each branch has its own CVR metric, with 0-result branch converting >0.2% and ≥1-result-no-click branch converting >0.5%).**
4. **Add-to-cart-no-checkout flow fires on every add-to-cart including ones that immediately progress to checkout.** Visitor adds to cart, opens checkout in another tab, the flow fires 4 hours later, the visitor has already completed checkout. **Fix: the add-to-cart-no-checkout trigger MUST include the meditation filter `Added to Cart → Started Checkout` < 1 hour (catch the "added but bounced before checkout" signal) AND `Added to Cart → Placed Order` of ≥4 hours. Without the second filter, the flow fires on every visitor who added to cart regardless of whether they converted. Verification Gate E is the canonical test (target: 0 add-to-cart-no-checkout recipients placed order in last 4 hours).**
5. **Browse-abandon flow's 72h "Last chance — 10% off" fires on every visitor, including ones who added-to-cart-then-removed.** Visitor adds to cart, removes, the cart-abandon flow's `Added to Cart` event is now stale (no added-to-cart event in last 4h), the browse-abandon flow's `Viewed Product` event is still in the meditation window, both flows fire on the same visitor. **Fix: implement a per-flow `added_to_cart_in_last_4h` exclusion on the browse-abandon flow (suppress browse-abandon if visitor has an added-to-cart event in the last 4 hours — they're being handled by cart-abandon). The cross-flow exclusion is the canonical "no double-fire" hygiene.**
6. **Stock-back notification fires for low-velocity SKUs that the visitor didn't view in the last 30 days.** Inventory system fires a stock-back event for a low-velocity SKU, the flow emails the entire customer base matching "viewed this SKU in last 30d", the email is irrelevant because the visitor's intent was 60+ days ago. **Fix: scope stock-back trigger to customers who viewed the SKU in the **last 14 days** AND who haven't received a stock-back email for any SKU in the last 7 days (the "stock-back-fatigue" suppression). The combined filter is the canonical "stock-back intent signal hasn't decayed" hygiene.**
7. **Browse-abandon flow uses a single subject line for all visitor LTV tiers.** A 1-time-buyer and a 5+-time-buyer receive the same "Saw this?" subject, the 5+-time-buyer would have converted 35–60% higher with a VIP-perks subject. **Fix: implement the 3-tier LTV conditional split on Email #1 (Phase 2 step 6). The split is the canonical per-LTV-tier branching; Klaviyo's 2024 A/B test report shows the lift is consistent across categories (apparel, beauty, food-bev, B2B).**
8. **Browse-abandon flow uses a single subject line for all traffic sources.** An organic-search visitor and a paid-social visitor receive the same "Saw this? — 10% off" subject, the organic-search visitor would have converted 35–65% higher with an educational subject like "Here's how to choose the right [Category]". **Fix: implement the per-traffic-source branching (Phase 2 step 6 — organic-search / paid-social / direct / referral / email). The split is the canonical per-source branching; Triple Whale's 2024 attribution dashboard shows the per-source lift.**
9. **Discount-stacking: browse-abandon 10% off + cart-abandon 10% off + welcome 10% off all fire on the same customer in 1 week.** Customer receives 3 discounts in 7 days, either redeems all 3 (margin destruction) or learns to wait for the next discount (training-discount-expectation). **Fix: enforce the 1-discount-per-30-days guard via the `discount_redeemed_last_30d` suppression segment, per Phase 2 step 10. Verification Gate H is the canonical test (target: 0–2 discount-stacking incidents per 1k browse-abandon sends).**
10. **Browse-abandon flow uses a static discount code that's shared with the cart-abandon and welcome flows.** Customer receives browse-abandon 10% off (static code `BROWSE10`) AND cart-abandon 10% off (static code `CART10`) AND welcome 10% off (static code `WELCOME10`), the 3 codes stack, customer gets 27% off, margin destruction. **Fix: use Klaviyo's "Unique coupon code" feature to generate a per-customer code for the browse-abandon discount; the unique code is single-use, 14-day expiry, and the 1-discount-per-30-days guard's `discount_redeemed_last_30d` segment catches the welcome-redemption overlap automatically.**
11. **Browse-abandon SMS uses the same copy as browse-abandon email.** Customer gets browse-abandon email at +4h ("Saw this? — [Product]") and browse-abandon SMS at +24h ("Saw this? — [Product]"), the SMS feels redundant, the SMS open-rate drops 20–30% on the 2nd cadence. **Fix: write SMS-specific copy that's shorter + more urgent ("[Brand] saw you browsing [Product] — 10% off ends Sunday. Tap: [short-link]"). Postscript 2024 benchmark shows SMS-specific copy converts 1.5–2× higher than email-copy-pasted-into-SMS.**
12. **Search-no-click flow emails all searchers including ones who DID find what they were looking for and converted.** Visitor searches "purple shoes", clicks a result, converts, the search-no-click flow's `Searched Site → Viewed Product` meditation window isn't long enough to exclude converters, the visitor receives "Didn't find what you were looking for?" 24h after purchase, marks as spam. **Fix: extend the search-no-click meditation window to `Searched Site → Placed Order` of ≥7 days. The visitor who converted within 7 days is excluded; the visitor who searched but didn't convert in 7 days is the canonical search-no-click audience.**
13. **Browse-abandon flow fires on B2B customers with default consumer voice.** B2B customer (B2B-tagged in Klaviyo profile) browses the B2B wholesale catalog, receives the default 10% off consumer browse-abandon, the B2B customer expects a quote or account-manager outreach, the discount feels commoditizing. **Fix: add a conditional split on `profile.b2b_tag = true` and route B2B customers to a separate browse-abandon flow that sends to the account manager (via Klaviyo's internal notification action). The split is the canonical B2B routing per the framework's 5-voice voice-override column.**
14. **Stock-back notification flow sends a generic "this product is back" email without surfacing the customer's specific viewed-product history.** Customer viewed 3 SKUs in the last 30 days, all 3 happen to be back in stock, the email says "It's back!" without naming which products, the customer can't tell which product the email is about. **Fix: wire the stock-back email to a per-viewed-product dynamic block that surfaces "Products you viewed that are back in stock" (filtered to the customer's last 30 days of `Viewed Product` events that intersect with the inventory-restock event). The dynamic block is the canonical "make the email about the customer's actual intent" hygiene; without it, open-rate drops 30–50%.**
15. **Browse-abandon + consideration flows are built but never reviewed for performance; first-month revenue is $5k, second-month revenue is $5k, operator assumes "the flows are at their ceiling".** **Fix: schedule the canonical 30-day post-launch flow-performance review (Klaviyo's flow-analytics dashboard + per-flow revenue + per-flow open-rate + per-flow CVR + per-flow unsubscribe rate + deliverability dashboard + per-traffic-source branch performance). Typical first-30-day lift: 30–50% from subject-line + send-time + LTV-tier-branch + per-traffic-source-branch A/B iterations. The weekly maintenance runbook (Phase 5 step 21) is the canonical 30-day-review trigger.**

## How to extend this skill

This skill is the **strategic-alignment + Pillar-1 framing layer** for the canonical 5-pillar lifecycle-marketing framework (research/05). 7 natural extensions exist; each is bounded and adds incremental value. The first 4 are already partially covered by the companion Tier-2 skill `skills/490-browse-abandon-consideration-flows.md`; the last 3 are extensions:

1. **BFCM browse-abandon variant (12:1–30:1)** — dedicated BFCM browse-abandon flow with the 3-email cadence shifted to Oct 15 / Nov 1 / Nov 25 and the discount escalated to 15% off + free gift + BFCM-expectation-set. The BFCM variant is the single highest-ROI per-quarter optimization; Klaviyo's 2024 BFCM benchmark shows 30–55% of BFCM revenue comes from browse + consideration flows.
2. **Per-cohort browse-abandon segmentation (5:1–15:1)** — split the browse-abandon flow by 5 customer cohorts (high-AOV browse-then-buy / mid-AOV browse-then-save / first-time-browse / loyalty-active browse / at-risk-no-browse-yet). The per-cohort branching lifts browse-abandon revenue 20–40% above the single-tier baseline.
3. **Cross-channel browse-abandon orchestration (4:1–12:1)** — extend the browse-abandon cadence to 5 channels: email + SMS + web-push (per skill/51) + mobile-push (per skill/51) + direct mail (per skill/70) for the highest-LTV tier. The cross-channel orchestration lifts browse-abandon reach from 60–75% to 85–95%.
4. **Predictive churn-driven browse-abandon (5:1–14:1)** — use Triple Whale's predictive LTV model (per skill/154) to identify customers likely to browse-then-leave (no `Added to Cart` event in 7d from a high-intent `Viewed Product` event), trigger a pre-emptive browse-abandon with a 10% off + per-traffic-source framing before the customer is fully lost. The pre-emptive browse-abandon converts 1.5–2× higher than the post-browse-window browse-abandon.
5. **AI-personalized per-product recommendation extension (6:1–18:1)** — use Klaviyo's AI-product-recommendation + Iterable's AI-channel + Dynamic Yield's AI-personalization to feed per-recipient per-product dynamic blocks that surface the customer's **predicted next-purchase product** instead of just "products you viewed". The AI-personalized extension lifts browse-abandon revenue 25–50% above the viewed-product-baseline.
6. **International browse-abandon (8:1–22:1)** — extend the browse-abandon flow to 5 languages (en / de / fr / es / ja) with per-region meditation windows (EU visitors typically browse-then-buy in 6–12h; JP visitors in 24–48h; US visitors in 4–8h) and per-region browse-event compliance (GDPR for EU, CASL for CA, LGPD for BR, APPI for JP). The international extension is the canonical EU/UK/JP/BR/AU operator addition.
7. **Browse-pool capture-rate optimizer (4:1–10:1)** — add a measurable browse-pool-capture-rate dashboard (browse-pool-emailed / browse-pool-total) that tracks how much of the browse pool is actually being mailed to. The capture-rate is the canonical "how much of the unmonetized pool are we capturing" KPI. Best-in-class capture-rate is 60–80%; median is 30–50%; worst-in-class is 10–25% (because the operator's onsite JS isn't capturing all `Viewed Product` events or Klaviyo's site-tracking pixel isn't installed site-wide).
