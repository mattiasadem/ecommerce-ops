## What "best in class" looks like

The 6-pillar Move #358.5.10 portfolio-AI-agent-engine framework sits on top of Move #358.5.9 + Move #358.5.8 + Move #358.5.7 + Move #358.5.6 + Move #358.5.5 + Move #358.5.4 + Move #358.5.3 + Move #358.5.2 + Move #358.5.1 + Move #93 + Move #127 + Move #144. The framework ships in 6 functional pillars:

**Pillar 1 — Portfolio-AI-agent-portfolio-decomposition-engine.** Decomposes the trailing-≥2-quarter historical Move #358.5.6 Pillar 1 cell matrix with the portfolio-tier dimension (≥6 cohort-tiers simultaneously tuned) replacing the per-cohort-pair-per-week dimension. The engine maintains per-portfolio × per-cohort-tier × per-supplier-pair × per-rollover-pressure-scenario × per-CCD1-CCD6-pattern × per-CRO1-CRO6-rebalancing × per-region × per-SKU cell coverage ≥95% Tier-1. Each cell carries the portfolio-CRO-tuning-outcome (Pillar 2 8-dim portfolio-portfolio-CRO-tuning-portfolio-resilience-vector) AND the per-cohort-pair-baseline (the Move #358.5.6 Pillar 6 6-tuple actual-savings-vector for the same cohort-tier WITHOUT portfolio-optimization) AND the portfolio-tuning delta = portfolio-tuning - per-cohort-pair-baseline. The decomposition engine publishes the cell matrix to Move #182 AI-agent-trust-recovery-engine weekly-Wednesday-8-AM rollup and to the Move #358.5.7 Q2 COHORT-PAIR-BREAKDOWN portfolio-breakdown generator weekly-Tuesday-2-PM and to the Move #358.5.7 Q8 NEXT-QUARTER-RECOMMENDATIONS portfolio-tuning-recommendations generator weekly-Thursday-10-AM.

**Pillar 2 — Portfolio-portfolio-CRO-tuning-portfolio-resilience-vector 8-dim.** Publishes per-portfolio × per-cohort-tier × per-supplier-pair × per-rollover-pressure-scenario `portfolio_pass_rate_pct` (computed as the ratio of portfolio-tuning-cycles where MR1-MR6 successfully fired the expected recommendation-band under portfolio-tuning-pressure to total portfolio-tuning-cycles) + `portfolio_recommendation_band_drift_pct` (the % of portfolio-tuning-cycles where MR3 PROPOSE-CRO-REWEIGHT fires a different recommendation-band under portfolio-tuning vs the per-cohort-pair-baseline recommendation-band; a high drift_pct signals the joint-CRO-weight solver is over-tuning) + `portfolio_rr_firing_rate_pct` (the % of portfolio-tuning-cycles where Move #358.5.6 RR1-RR6 rollback-engine fires under portfolio-tuning; expected to be HIGHER under portfolio-tuning vs per-cohort-pair-baseline as portfolio-tuning is riskier) + `portfolio_avoided_loss_under_portfolio_usd` (computed as the Pillar 6 6-tuple portfolio-avoided-loss-savings-vector dim 1 with portfolio-tuning applied) + `portfolio_ltv_preservation_under_portfolio_pct` (Pillar 3 6-dim dim 3 with portfolio-tuning) + `portfolio_compute_cost_usd` (joint-CRO-weight-solver-compute + sandbox-isolation + cross-cohort-tier-transfer-learning cost) + `portfolio_classification_drift_pct` (the % of portfolio-tuning-cycles where Move #358.5.4 Pillar 1 CCD1-CCD6 classification shifts under portfolio-tuning vs per-cohort-pair-baseline; a high drift_pct signals the cross-cohort-classifier is portfolio-fragile) + **portfolio_cross_cohort_contention_pct** (a NEW 8th-dim measuring the % of CRO-weight-budget contention between ≥2 cohort-tiers; high contention_pct = joint-CRO-weight-solver is forcing trade-offs that hurt at least one cohort-tier; this dim is the CRITICAL distinguisher from Move #358.5.6 Pillar 2). Each dim populated per-portfolio-tuning-cycle and rolled up to per-portfolio × per-cohort-tier × per-quarter.

**Pillar 3 — Portfolio-portfolio-LTV-preservation-vector 6-dim.** Publishes per-portfolio × per-cohort-tier × per-supplier-pair × per-rollover-pressure-scenario `portfolio_ltv_pre_usd` (LTV measured 30d before the portfolio-tuning-cycle start, with portfolio-tuning applied) + `portfolio_ltv_post_usd` (LTV measured 30d after the portfolio-tuning-cycle end, with portfolio-tuning applied) + `portfolio_ltv_degradation_pct` ((portfolio_post - portfolio_pre) / portfolio_pre × 100; a NEGATIVE degradation = portfolio-tuning eroded LTV) + `portfolio_decile_rank_drop` (decile rank drop within all cohort-tiers post-portfolio-tuning) + `portfolio_decile_rank_drop_vs_singleton` (portfolio_decile_rank_drop − per_cohort_pair_decile_rank_drop; positive = portfolio-tuning caused additional decile-rank-drop beyond the per-cohort-pair-baseline) + **portfolio_high_performer_loss_pct** (a NEW 6th-dim measuring the % LTV loss in the top-decile cohort-tier under portfolio-tuning vs per-cohort-pair-baseline; a high value signals the joint-CRO-weight solver is HURTING the highest-LTV cohort-tier to help lower-LTV cohort-tiers — P16 pitfall specifically guards against this). Each dim populated per-portfolio-tuning-cycle.

**Pillar 4 — Portfolio-AI-agent-engine 8-stage PT1-PT8.** Generates the portfolio-tuning-cycle in 8 stages: PT1 DEFINE-PORTFOLIO-SCOPE (define the portfolio scope per quarter: ≥6 cohort-tiers with cross-cohort-tier-affinity ≥40% to enable transfer-learning; uses convex-optimization or Bayesian-optimization with portfolio_pass_rate_pct as the objective function and portfolio_cross_cohort_contention_pct as the regularization term; portfolio-CRO-tuning-proposal follows Move #181 Pillar 2 firing-quota and Move #182 Pillar 3 confidence-band gating) → PT4 PORTFOLIO-MR1-OBSERVE (verify MR1 OBSERVE-ROLLOVER correctly observes the portfolio-wide rollover activity across ≥6 cohort-tiers; failures propagate to PR4 AUTO-REGENERATE) → PT8 COMPUTE-PORTFOLIO-ROLLUP (compute portfolio-rollups for Move #358.5.7 Q2 COHORT-PAIR-BREAKDOWN portfolio-breakdown + Move #358.5.7 Q8 NEXT-QUARTER-RECOMMENDATIONS portfolio-tuning-recommendations + Move #127 portfolio-rollup-engine + Move #93 portfolio-rebalancing-engine comparison).

## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

**P1. Ship without Move #358.5.9 Pillar 6 stress-test-AI-agent-cost-amortization-engine integration.** The portfolio-engine must read Move #358.5.9 Pillar 6 6-tuple stress-test-savings-vector to compute the portfolio-vs-stress-test resilience delta; shipping without this read means PT7 PORTFOLIO-MR6-COMMIT cannot validate against stress-test-baseline and PR3 fires spuriously. **Fix:** Pillar 1 cell matrix must join on Move #358.5.9 Pillar 6 6-tuple by portfolio × cohort-LTV-tier × supplier-pair × rollover-pressure-scenario-id; portfolio-tuning-cycle with missing stress-test-baseline is rejected pre-publish.

**P2. Ship without ≥6 LTV-cohort-tiers AND ≥4 simultaneous-rollover-pressure scenarios per quarter.** The portfolio-engine must have a portfolio to optimize AND rollover-pressure to apply; shipping without ≥6 cohort-tiers means the joint-CRO-weight-solver has no portfolio; shipping without ≥4 simultaneous-rollover-pressure scenarios means PT2 ENUMERATE-CROSS-COHORT-TUNING-CONSTRAINTS returns 0 constraints and the engine ships as a static per-cohort-pair playbook. **Fix:** Move #358.5.10 prerequisite #16 enforces both ≥6 LTV-cohort-tiers AND ≥4 simultaneous-rollover-pressure scenarios per quarter; portfolio-engine with <6 cohort-tiers or <4 scenarios available is REJECTED at the prereq gate.

**P3. No PT7 portfolio-MR6-commit confidence-band gating + per-cohort-tier-margin-of-safety enforcement.** The portfolio-engine can propose portfolio-tuning-cycles that violate Move #182 Pillar 3 confidence-band floor or Move #181 Pillar 2 firing-quota OR consume >85% of per-cohort-tier-margin-of-safety; shipping without PT7 gating means a portfolio-tuning-cycle that "MR6 would have committed under portfolio-tuning with 100% confidence" but violates Move #181 quota OR strips the per-cohort-tier-margin-of-safety below 15% gets accepted as evidence. **Fix:** PT7 must cross-check Move #182 + Move #181 + Move #358.5.6 Pillar 5 multi-supplier-rollover-tuning-confidence-band + per-cohort-tier-margin-of-safety ≥15%; portfolio-tuning-cycle that violates any of the four is rejected at PT7 with a PR4 AUTO-REGENERATE.

**P4. No 7-sub-rule PR1-PR7 rollback-coverage validation.** Without PR1-PR7, a portfolio-tuning-cycle that drifts to T5 confidence-band or triggers a CRITICAL-RISK classification or fires an RR1-RR6 rollback or hits portfolio_cross_cohort_contention_pct > 40% can silently propagate to Move #358.5.7 Q2 + Move #358.5.7 Q8 + Move #358.5.6 MR1; bad portfolio-tuning-baselines corrupt next-quarter portfolio-tuning. **Fix:** Pillar 6 7-sub-rule PR1-PR7 fire on (PR1 PT4-portfolio-MR1-observe-failure / PR2 PT5-portfolio-MR2-classification-drift / PR3 PT6-portfolio-MR3-recommendation-band-drift > 20% / PR4 PT7-portfolio-MR6-commit-margin-of-safety-violation / PR5 CRITICAL-RISK-classification / PR6 portfolio_cross_cohort_contention_pct > 40% / PR7 operator-portfolio-rollback-request).

**P5. No confidence-band on portfolio-tuning-cycle.** All portfolio-tuning-cycles treated as equally-trustworthy. **Fix:** Pillar 5 5-tier confidence-band on every portfolio-tuning-cycle (T1 ≥95% portfolio-validity / T2 85-95% / T3 70-85% / T4 50-70% / T5 <50% REJECT); portfolio-tuning-cycle confidence-band is the AGGREGATE of PT7 validation-confidence × Move #358.5.6 actual-confidence-band × portfolio-tuning-cycle-consistency-check × portfolio_cross_cohort_contention_pct floor.

**P6. No Move #358.5.8 Pillar 4 counterfactual-validation-feedback integration.** The portfolio-engine feeds back to Move #358.5.8 CFS7 VALIDATE-AGAINST-PRIOR-CONSENSUS as portfolio-coverage evidence; shipping without this loop means the counterfactual-simulator doesn't learn from portfolio-evidence (e.g., "MR-agent portfolio-tuned under 8 portfolio-tuning-cycles with pass_rate 95% — this evidence should boost Move #358.5.8 CFS7 prior-consensus-confidence for any counterfactual that proposes portfolio-tuning conditions"). **Fix:** wire C7 to Move #358.5.8 CFS7 with quarterly-batch feedback (Q-end + 7d SLA; same as Move #358.5.9 Pillar 5 C6).

**P7. No Move #358.5.7 Pillar 4 Q2-PORTFOLIO-BREAKDOWN-integration.** The portfolio-engine must publish to Move #358.5.7 Q2 as a portfolio-breakdown; shipping without this integration means portfolio-tuning-cycle outcomes stay siloed in the portfolio-engine and never reach the CFO + cohort-owners as portfolio-breakdown intelligence. **Fix:** Pillar 5 C4 channel is mandatory for every portfolio-tuning-cycle (T1-T5); portfolio-tuning-cycle not published to Move #358.5.7 Q2 is a PR7 AUTO-REGENERATE-AND-REGENERATE trigger.

**P8. No Move #89 BFCM-peak-multiplier integration.** Portfolio-tuning-cycle of historical events that include BFCM-quarter data must include Move #89 BFCM-peak-multiplier context (BFCM-week portfolio-tuning-pressure is multiplicative, not additive); shipping without BFCM-multiplier means portfolio-tuning-cycle under-reports BFCM-driven portfolio-fragility. **Fix:** Pillar 4 PT3 JOINT-CRO-WEIGHT-SOLVER must apply Move #89 BFCM-peak-flag when the portfolio-tuning-cycle date falls in BFCM-week (Black-Friday → Cyber-Monday + 7d) with portfolio-tuning-pressure-multiplier 1.5×.

**P9. **P10. No C6 MOVE-#358.5.6-MR-AGENT-PORTFOLIO-FEEDBACK-LOOP integration.** The portfolio-tuning-cycle feeds back to Move #358.5.6 MR1-MR6 as a new portfolio-baseline; shipping without this loop means the MR-agent doesn't learn from portfolio-evidence (e.g., "MR-agent was portfolio-fragile under cross-cohort-tier-cascade portfolio-tuning-cycle with pass_rate 45% — this evidence should retune MR3 PROPOSE-CRO-REWEIGHT to be MORE conservative under cross-cohort-cascade pressure"). **Fix:** wire C6 to Move #358.5.6 TUN1 OBSERVE stream with weekly-batch feedback (each Wednesday morning, deliver trailing-quarter Pillar 2 8-dim + Pillar 3 6-dim + portfolio_pass_rate_pct as new portfolio-baseline).

**P11. Shipping without this discrimination means portfolio-engine overstates portfolio-tuning-premium. **Fix:** Pillar 2 dim 1 portfolio_pass_rate_pct must be normalized to the same per-cohort-pair-baseline-pressure level (covariate-adjusted portfolio-tuning-premium calculation); portfolio-tuning-cycle with portfolio-pressure < per-cohort-pair-baseline-pressure is excluded from the premium calculation.

**P12. No portfolio_cross_cohort_contention_pct 8th-dim.** The portfolio-engine ships only 7 dims in Pillar 2 instead of the canonical 8-dim; portfolio_cross_cohort_contention_pct (the % of CRO-weight-budget contention between ≥2 cohort-tiers) is the CRITICAL distinguisher from Move #358.5.6 Pillar 2 7-dim. Without this dim, the operator cannot tell whether the joint-CRO-weight-solver is forcing trade-offs that hurt at least one cohort-tier. **Fix:** Pillar 2 dim 8 is mandatory; portfolio-tuning-cycle with missing dim 8 is rejected pre-publish with a Pillar 6 schema-error counter increment.

**P13. No Triple-Whale 48-field-schema coverage validation.** Downstream consumers (Move #93, Move #113, Move #115, Move #119, Move #127, Move #144, Move #154, Move #180, Move #181, Move #182, Move #185, Move #186) silently fail when fields like `portfolio_tuning_cycle_id`, `portfolio_cross_cohort_contention_pct` are missing. **Fix:** schema-validation gate fires pre-publish; events missing >2 fields are dropped with a Pillar 6 schema-error counter increment.

**P14. No Q-end + 7d portfolio-tuning-SLA enforcement.** CFO-board-pack-portfolio-tuning-appendix must reach the CFO within 7d post-quarter-end (same SLA as Move #358.5.9 CFO-board-pack-stress-test-appendix — both share the Q-end + 7d distribution SLA). **P15. No portfolio-engine-cost-amortization tracking.** Portfolio-engine profitability unknown; the operator can't tell whether Move #358.5.10 is generating ROI or is a cost center. **Fix:** Pillar 6 6-tuple publishes `portfolio_engine_net_resilience_value_usd` per portfolio per quarter; CFO-board-pack-meeting review checks `net_resilience_value > 0` for ≥80% of portfolios per quarter.

**P16. No portfolio_high_performer_loss_pct 6th-dim protection against hurting top-decile cohort-tier.** The portfolio-engine may optimize for total-portfolio-LTV-preservation while HURTING the top-decile cohort-tier (e.g., shifting CRO-weight-budget from high-LTV cohort-tier to mid-LTV cohort-tier); without portfolio_high_performer_loss_pct 6th-dim, the operator cannot detect this anti-pattern. **Fix:** Pillar 3 dim 6 is mandatory; portfolio-tuning-cycle where top-decile cohort-tier loses >10% LTV vs per-cohort-pair-baseline is flagged as ANTI-PATTERN + PR5 AUTO-ESCALATE fires (a portfolio-engine that hurts top-decile to help mid-decile is a NET NEGATIVE for the operator).
