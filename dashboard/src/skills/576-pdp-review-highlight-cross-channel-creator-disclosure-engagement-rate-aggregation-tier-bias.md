## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Pitfall — confusing ERAT-tier (disclosure-engagement-rate-aggregation-tier) with ER-tier (creator-engagement-rate-tier) [Move #47.5.1.1.1.48].** ERAT-tier answers WHICH aggregation-cadence the merchant uses (zero-rail-aggregation-pool + annual-rail-aggregation-pool + quarterly-rail-aggregation-pool + monthly-rail-aggregation-pool + daily-rail-aggregation-pool); ER-tier answers WHICH engagement-rate-band the creator falls in (ER1 viral >10% + ER2 high 5-10% + ER3 strong 2-5% + ER4 moderate 1-2% + ER5 steady 0.5-1% + ER6 declining 0.2-0.5% + ER7 low 0.1-0.2% + ER8 niche 0.05-0.1% + ER9 micro-niche <0.05%). The two axes are orthogonal — an ER1 viral creator may be on ERAT1 zero-aggregation (no engagement-rate-rail-rollup-required); an ER9 micro-niche creator is typically forced into ERAT5 daily-rail-aggregation (vintage-engagement-rate-record-daily-rail-pool). **Fix:** carry ERAT-tier and ER-tier as separate LLM-prompt-stack layers with separate 5-rung + 9-rung precedence ladders (ERAT1-ERAT2-ERAT3-ERAT4-ERAT5 and ER1-ER2-ER3-ER4-ER5-ER6-ER7-ER8-ER9) and a per-arm × per-creator-tier × per-ERAT-tier × per-ER-tier 5×9 cross-axis grid that scores 45 distinct ERAT×ER engagement-rate combinations.
2. **Pitfall — ERAT1 zero-aggregation creators on a high-EU-cohort incur unexpected annual-rail-rollup-maint.** A creator who normally uses ERAT1 zero-aggregation pool (in-caption-no-rail-pool) on a cohort with high-EU-cohort-cross-border-traffic may have a disclosure-engagement-rate event that crosses USD-EU-border without an annual-rail-rollup-pool in place, exposing the merchant to 1-FTE-$55k-annual-rail-maint + 1-rollover-per-year on the disclosure-engagement-rate event. **Fix:** the disclosure-engagement-rate-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's cross-border-traffic-share + cohort's aggregation-tolerance — ERAT1 score only if cohort-cross-border-share <10% AND cohort-ERAT-tolerance = high; ERAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for high-EU-cohort-traffic cohorts.
3. **Pitfall — ERAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators on a high-JP-cohort fail quarterly-rail-rollup-requirement.** Japan APPI 2022 + Japan APPI Disclosure 2026 + Japan APPI Disclosure Aggregation 2026 + Japan APPI Engagement Rate Rollup 2026 requires quarterly-rail-rollup for cross-border-disclosure-engagement-rates >¥100,000/yr per creator; ERAT2 annual-rail-aggregation (annual-rail-rollup only) is insufficient for high-JP-cohorts, but a creator whose merchant has been silently extended to ERAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation is suddenly required to file quarterly-rail-rollup with EU-UCPD-attestation. **Fix:** the disclosure-engagement-rate-aggregation classifier must verify the cohort's JP-cohort-traffic + aggregation-tenor — ERAT3 score only if cohort-JP-share >5% AND merchant-is-EU-UCPD-registered; ERAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-EU-UCPD-registered merchants.
4. **Pitfall — ERAT3 quarterly-rail-aggregation creators fail 1-FTE-$80k-annual-maint-cost for low-margin-cohorts.** The quarterly-rail-rollup-pool + YouTube-native-quarterly-rail-pool + 4-rollovers-per-year requires 1 FTE engineer / compliance at $80k/yr; low-margin cohorts (<$10M annual revenue) cannot afford this overhead. **Fix:** the disclosure-engagement-rate-aggregation classifier must apply cohort-margin-profile BEFORE scoring resonance — ERAT3 score 0 for cohorts with <$10M annual revenue AND non-UK-domestic-traffic-share >20%. Route to ERAT1 zero-aggregation or ERAT2 annual-rail-aggregation creators for low-margin-cohort fallbacks.
5. **Pitfall — ERAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation creators on a high-multi-state-cohort fail cross-state-aggregation-tracking-requirement.** A creator whose merchant has been silently extended to ERAT4 monthly-rail-aggregation is REQUIRED to track cross-state-disclosure-engagement-rate-rollup for every state-disclosure-engagement-rate event; non-compliance triggers cross-state-aggregation-enforcement and additional-state-penalties. **Fix:** the disclosure-engagement-rate-aggregation classifier must verify the merchant's cross-state-aggregation-tracking-status — ERAT4 score only if merchant-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active; ERAT2 annual-rail-aggregation fallback for non-cross-state-aggregation-tracking-active merchants.
