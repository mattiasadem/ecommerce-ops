## Common pitfalls

The Move #1.3 build fails when any of these 16 pitfalls is hit:

1. **Per-creator-broadcast-without-Move-#1.2-dark-site-deploy-consumer-as-trigger** — the per-creator-broadcast is fired from a manual Slack-command or a manual PagerDuty-page instead of from Move #1.2's `dark_site_deploy_event`. Without the Move #1.2 consumer the broadcast fires BEFORE the dark-site is live (creators get a "go to crisis.<brand>.com" link that 404s), OR the broadcast fires 4-12 hours AFTER the dark-site-deploy (the canonical creator-coordination-failure anti-pattern). Fix: Move #1.3 consumes Move #1.2's `dark_site_deploy_event` within 60 sec; the consumer is idempotent (re-firing the same dark-site-deploy doesn't re-broadcast).
2. **Per-creator-broadcast-without-Move-#60-per-launch-creator-list-consumer** — the per-creator-broadcast is fired to a static creator-list (e.g. an Airtable-base or a Notion-database) instead of to Move #60's per-launch-creator-list. Without the Move #60 consumer the broadcast misses 30-60% of currently-active creators (creators who started promoting the affected-SKU in the last 7 days aren't in the static list). Fix: Move #1.3 consumes the Move #60 per-launch-creator-list within 5 min; filter to creators with `active_affiliate_link` OR `active_utm_link` OR `active_discount_code` for an affected-SKU.
3. **Per-creator-broadcast-without-Move-#1.1-severity-scorer-as-broadcast-priority-driver** — the per-creator-broadcast is fired at the same priority for all crises (e.g. always P0, regardless of severity-score) instead of using the severity-score to determine the per-creator-broadcast-priority. Without the severity-scorer-driver the broadcast over-pages for low-severity crises (P0 broadcast for a severity-score=45 operational-issue wastes creator-attention AND trains creators to ignore future Move #1.3 alerts) AND under-pages for high-severity crises (P0 broadcast for a severity-score=95 product-recall-life-safety should reach all 4 tiers in <30 min, not just macro). Fix: Move #1.3 consumes Move #1.1's severity-score in real-time; maps to per-tier per-creator SLA (P0=all-4-tiers within 30 min; P1=macro+mid within 2 hours, micro+nano within 24 hours; P2=macro only within 24 hours).
4. **Per-creator-broadcast-without-Move-#1-8-scenario-vulnerability-matrix-as-template-selector** — the per-creator-broadcast uses a single holding-statement-template for all crises instead of mapping the current crisis to 1 of 8 templates via Move #1's 8-scenario-vulnerability-matrix. Without the 8-template-engine a viral-TikTok crisis gets the same holding-statement as a founder-controversy crisis (tone + action-checklist + return-flow-SKU-list are wrong by 60-80%). Fix: Move #1.3 maps the current crisis to 1 of 8 templates; each template has a unique per-creator-statement-engine (statement tone + action-checklist + return-flow-SKU-list).
5. **Per-creator-broadcast-without-LLM-personalization-keeping-creator-voice** — the per-creator-holding-statement is a generic "we apologize for any inconvenience" instead of an LLM-personalized statement that maintains the creator's voice. Without LLM-personalization the creator sees the broadcast as "corporate-spam" and ignores the checklist (per-creator-content-pause-rate drops from 80% to <20%). Fix: Move #1.3 LLM-personalizes each holding-statement with the creator's handle + platform + audience-size + recent-content-voice (read from the creator-marketing-platform's `creator_voice_profile` endpoint).
6. **Per-creator-broadcast-without-3-language-i18n** — the per-creator-holding-statement is English-only instead of supporting Spanish + French + the brand's other top-3 creator-languages. Without i18n 30-50% of creators (those whose primary language is Spanish or French) misunderstand the holding-statement AND the action-checklist AND may post content that contradicts the dark-site. Fix: Move #1.3 supports 3 baseline languages (English / Spanish / French) matched to the creator's primary language; the LLM-personalization step auto-translates to the creator's primary language.
7. **Per-creator-content-pause-orchestrator-without-per-platform-differentiation** — the orchestrator calls a single `pause_scheduled_content` API for all platforms instead of per-platform APIs (TikTok/Instagram/YouTube/Snap/Pinterest each have their own pause-API). Without per-platform-differentiation the orchestrator pauses-content on the wrong platform (e.g. pauses a TikTok creator's Instagram-Reel instead of their TikTok) or fails to pause on the right platform (e.g. doesn't pause a YouTube-Shorts creator's scheduled YouTube). Fix: Move #1.3 calls the creator-marketing-platform's per-platform `pause_scheduled_content` API; per-creator-platform-routing.
8. **Per-creator-affiliate-link-pause-orchestrator-without-UTM-replace** — the orchestrator pauses the affiliate-link but doesn't replace it with the dark-site-link. Without the UTM-replace the creator's bio + scheduled-content still shows the affiliate-link (which leads to a non-recall-aware PDP), even after the pause. Fix: Move #1.3 calls the creator-marketing-platform's `replace_utm_link` API; the replacement is reversible (per-creator can re-enable after crisis-resolution).
9. **Per-creator-broadcast-without-Twilio-SMS-fallback** — the broadcast uses only Slack + Email. Without the SMS-fallback 20-40% of macro+mid-tier creators (those who don't check Slack + Email frequently) miss the broadcast AND don't pause-content AND don't pause-affiliate-link. Fix: Move #1.3 has a Twilio-SMS-fallback for P0 broadcasts to macro+mid-tier; the SMS contains the dark-site-link + the per-creator-action-checklist-summary.
10. **Per-creator-tier-decision-classifier-without-4-class-exclusive** — the classifier assigns MULTIPLE decision-classes to the same crisis (e.g. both `broadcast-all-4-tiers` AND `broadcast-macro-mid-only`). Without EXCLUSIVE first-match-wins the broadcast fires 2x (once for all-4-tiers, once for macro-mid-only) → duplicate-messages to macro+mid-tier creators. Fix: 4 rules checked in order: broadcast-all-4-tiers → broadcast-macro-mid-only → broadcast-macro-only → no-broadcast-defer; first-match-wins.
11. **Per-creator-tier-isolation-test-not-run-on-deploy** — the test is run only manually, not on every Move #1.3 deploy + on every per-tier-count change. Without the on-deploy-isolation-test a per-tier-count change (e.g. adding a new micro-tier) silently breaks the summation (`sum(per-tier-counts) != total-broadcast-count`). Fix: Move #1.3 runs the isolation-test on every deploy + on every per-tier-count change; the test catches "double-counted" or "missing-tier" bugs.
12. **Per-creator-rolling-30d-cohort-window-without-tier-differentiation** — the rolling-window is the same for all tiers (e.g. 30-day window for macro+mid-tier AND micro+nano). Without tier-differentiation the macro+mid-tier 30-day-window under-counts recent activity (a macro creator who promoted the affected-SKU 3 days ago is missed) AND the micro+nano 30-day-window is correct. Fix: Move #1.3 uses tier-differentiated windows (macro/mid-tier = 7-day, micro/nano = 30-day).
13. **Per-creator-utm-replace-orchestrator-without-4-UTM-source-rotation** — the orchestrator replaces only the `instagram` UTM-source but misses `tiktok` / `youtube` / `snap`. Without 4-UTM-source-rotation 25-50% of creator-traffic continues to use the un-replaced UTM (TikTok-traffic + YouTube-traffic + Snap-traffic). Fix: Move #1.3 supports 4-UTM-source-rotation (instagram / tiktok / youtube / snap); the UTM-replacement is logged to Move #6 attribution-quality for downstream per-creator-attribution-impact analysis.
14. **Per-creator-content-pause-orchestrator-without-reversible-restoration** — the orchestrator pauses content but doesn't support reversible-restoration (per-creator can re-enable after crisis-resolution). Without reversible-restoration creators lose scheduled-content permanently (e.g. a TikTok creator's pre-scheduled TikTok for the recalled granola bar is permanently deleted instead of being re-scheduled for post-crisis). Fix: Move #1.3 pauses content in a reversible state (e.g. `paused_until` timestamp) AND auto-restores after crisis-resolution-confirmed.
15. **Per-creator-broadcast-without-Move-#6.15-attribution-ledger-emit** — the broadcast is fired but not logged to Move #6.15's accuracy-ledger. Without the Move #6.15 emit the operator can't measure Move #1.3's impact on per-creator-content-pause-rate + per-creator-affiliate-link-pause-rate + per-creator-utm-replace-rate. Fix: Move #1.3 emits `per_creator_broadcast_latency` row + `per_creator_content_pause_row` + `per_creator_affiliate_link_pause_row` + `per_creator_utm_replace_row` to Move #6.15's accuracy-ledger with `accuracy_layer="move_1_3_per_creator_crisis_coordination_mode"` tag.
16. **Per-creator-broadcast-without-Move-#1.3's-production-readiness-test-before-BFCM-peak** — BFCM-peak 200-concurrent-Move #1.3-broadcast-batch with 2000 creators + 4-tier-broadcast + 8-template-holding-statement + 4-tier-action-checklist + 8-template-return-flow-SKU-list + Slack-Block-Kit + Twilio-SMS-fallback load-test is REQUIRED; Move #1.3 must complete p95 < 30 min. Without load-test Move #1.3 silently fails on BFCM-peak days (a brand's 2000-creator broadcast takes 4-6 hours instead of <30 min during BFCM-peak traffic).
