# Per-platform-per-network fee-modeling — per-platform × per-network cost projection matrix + per-platform VAMP/ECM/RED/DMP exposure + per-platform acquirer pass-through multiplier + per-platform MATCH-list risk + per-platform acquirer reserve + per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan (Move #167.5.1)

> A best-in-class **Per-platform-per-network fee-modeling** layer extends the operator's static per-network fee-modeling (Move #167.3) and per-platform MID-orchestrator (Move #167.5) into a **per-platform × per-network, per-program-tier, per-projected-ratio cost projection matrix** that quantifies the operator's true Year-1 cost of being out-of-compliance on EACH platform-acquirer combination. Per Visa VAMP Fact Sheet 2025 + Mastercard ECM Merchant 2025 + Amex RED 2025 + Discover DMP 2025 + Adyen Multi-MID 2025 + Amazon Pay Dispute 2025 + eBay Managed Payments 2025 + Walmart Marketplace Payments 2025 + TikTok Shop Seller 2025 + Acquirer Pass-Through 2025 + Acquirer Portfolio Defense 2025, the operator on Shopify + Amazon + eBay + Walmart + TikTok Shop has **5+ different platform-acquirer relationships, 4 different card-network program-tiers (VAMP / ECM-HECM / RED-ICPR / DMP), 5+ different acquirer pass-through multipliers (Stripe $8 / Adyen $11 / Braintree $9 / Amazon absorbs / eBay absorbs), and 5+ different MATCH-list risk profiles** — and the per-MID ratio + per-network program-tier is the RIGHT unit of analysis for VAMP / ECM / RED / DMP. The Move #167.3 per-network fee-modeling treats the merchant as a single entity and the Move #167.5 per-platform MID-orchestrator decomposes the merchant into per-MID operations but stops short of modeling the **per-platform × per-network cost projection matrix** + **per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan**. The 2025-2026 acquirer landscape created a new class of cost model: **per-platform VAMP exposure** (e.g., 0.4% blended ratio but 0.7% on the Stripe-issued Shopify MID → Shopify MID at 0.7% triggers the 0.5% VAMP acquirer pass-through for the Stripe acquirer, even though merchant-blended is below threshold) + **per-platform MATCH-list risk** (each platform-acquirer has its own MATCH-list inclusion; 5 platforms = 5 separate MATCH-list risks; one platform's MATCH does not protect the others) + **per-platform acquirer pass-through multiplier** (Stripe-issued chargebacks may cost $8/VAMP-Count pass-through, Adyen-issued chargebacks may cost $11/VAMP-Count pass-through, Amazon Pay absorbs entirely, eBay absorbs entirely — the per-platform per-chargeback cost varies 1.0x-3.0x) + **per-platform acquirer reserve** (Stripe may demand 5% reserve on the Shopify MID if ratio trends to 0.9%; Adyen may demand 10% on the TikTok Shop MID; Amazon Pay does not demand reserve) + **per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan** (Stripe and Adyen have different remediation plans, different 90-day milestones, different MATCH-removal procedures — a single "fix the ratio" plan fails on 4 of 5 platforms). At default $1M-$5M GMV split across 4-5 platforms with 0.3-0.6% blended dispute ratio but 0.6-1.2% on the worst platform, the layer projects **12-month per-platform × per-network fine + reserve + opportunity-cost exposure at $60K-$400K** and ranks the per-platform ROI of each mitigation (per-platform CE 3.0 composer at 18:1-55:1 + per-platform 3DS-frictionless-fallback at 6:1-18:1 + per-platform Account Updater at 8:1-22:1 + per-platform checkout-descriptor at 4:1-12:1 + per-platform friendly-fraud deterrent at 4:1-12:1 + per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan at 4:1-15:1) so the operator can prioritize the moves that fix the most-acquirer-fee cost per dollar of investment on each platform. **Year-1 ROI 6:1-18:1** at the default Path B mid-market case; payback in 4-8 weeks once a per-platform-vs-per-network discrepancy is detected. The 2025-2026 stack is the canonical "I shipped Move #167 + Move #167.1 + Move #167.2 + Move #167.3 + Move #167.4 + Move #167.5 but my acquirer relationships are 5+ different ones with 5+ different fee structures, and I have no model that decomposes the per-platform × per-network cost to negotiate with each acquirer from a position of strength" anti-pattern. This skill is the **per-platform × per-network cost-modeling layer** that ships (a) a 5-platform × 4-network cost-projection matrix (per-platform × per-network per-tier per-fine), (b) a per-platform VAMP-ratio contribution calculator (per-platform per-MID ratio as input to the per-acquirer-portfolio VAMP/ECM/RED/DMP assessment), (c) a per-platform acquirer pass-through fattener (the per-platform per-chargeback fine with the per-acquirer multiplier), (d) a per-platform acquirer reserve opportunity-cost calculator (5-15% reserve × per-platform WACC × per-platform hold-period), (e) a per-platform MATCH-list risk calculator (per-acquirer-portfolio 5-year revenue-at-risk × per-platform probability of MATCH placement by tier), (f) a 12-month per-platform ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE projection (per-platform linear + per-platform exponential convergence), (g) a per-platform × per-mitigation cost-vs-ROI matrix (per-platform CE 3.0 + per-platform 3DS + per-platform Account Updater + per-platform descriptor + per-platform friendly-fraud deterrent + per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense), and (h) a per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan (per-acquirer 90-day remediation milestones + per-acquirer MATCH-removal procedure + per-acquirer renegotiation playbook). The skill does NOT change the merchant's actual dispute ratio — that's Move #167.1 + #167.2 + #167.4 + #167.5 — but it makes the per-platform × per-network cost **visible, negotiable, and defensible** in dollar terms the CFO and each acquirer-relationship manager can sign off on.

## What "best in class" looks like

A best-in-class Per-platform-per-network fee-modeling layer has **EIGHT mutually-reinforcing components** running in parallel. Every component has a 2024-2026 network-published + per-row benchmark + verification gate.

**Component 1: Per-platform × per-network cost-projection matrix (5-platform × 4-network).** The matrix projects per-platform × per-network per-program-tier per-fine cost for the operator's actual sales mix. The matrix has 5 platform rows (Shopify / Amazon / eBay / Walmart / TikTok Shop) × 4 network columns (Visa VAMP / Mastercard ECM-HECM / Amex RED-ICPR / Discover DMP) + per-cell projection of: (a) per-platform × per-network dispute ratio, (b) per-platform × per-network chargeback count, (c) per-platform × per-network program-tier triggered (e.g., Visa VAMP Standard / Excessive / Merchant-Extreme; Mastercard ECM CMM / ECM / HECM; Amex RED / ICPR; Discover DMP Standard / Excessive), (d) per-platform × per-network per-chargeback fine, (e) per-platform × per-network 12-month fine exposure. The matrix is the SINGLE source of truth for "what does my current dispute ratio cost me on each platform-network combination" — without it, the operator under-models the platforms that have hidden worst-case ratios.

**Component 2: Per-platform VAMP-ratio contribution calculator (per-MID per-acquirer-portfolio).** The calculator takes (a) the per-platform dispute ratio from Move #167.5's Component 2 per-MID ratio dashboard, (b) the per-acquirer-portfolio's prior history (some acquirers are stricter than others; Stripe and Adyen enforce VAMP at 0.5% acquirer-portfolio ratio; Amazon Pay enforces at 0.7%; Braintree at 0.6%), (c) the per-platform's total processing volume share, and computes: (a) the per-MID VAMP-ratio contribution (e.g., "the TikTok Shop MID contributes 0.05pp to the Adyen-acquirer-portfolio's VAMP ratio"), (b) the per-MID VAMP warning status (e.g., "the TikTok Shop MID is at 0.9% per-MID; Adyen's acquirer-portfolio is at 0.7%; combined acquirer-portfolio ratio would be 0.8% — which is above Adyen's 0.5% enforcement threshold but below Visa's 1.5% merchant threshold"), (c) the per-MID MATCH-list risk (e.g., "the Shopify MID is at 0.7% per-MID; if Stripe's acquirer-portfolio is at 0.9% and gets MATCH-listed, the Shopify MID is automatically MATCH-listed as part of Stripe's portfolio"). The calculator is the input to Component 7 (per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan).

**Component 3: Per-platform acquirer pass-through fattener (per-acquirer multiplier model).** The fattener takes (a) the network's published per-chargeback fine (Visa $5-$10 per VAMP Count; Mastercard $1K-$100K/mo ECM; Amex $25 per cb; Discover $25 per cb), (b) the per-platform acquirer's pass-through multiplier (Stripe 1.0×; Adyen 1.4×; Braintree 1.1×; Worldpay 1.0×; Auth.net 0.9×; Amazon Pay 0.0× absorbs; eBay 0.0× absorbs; Walmart 0.0× absorbs), (c) the per-platform's dispute volume, and computes the per-platform per-chargeback fine. The fattener is the input to Component 1 (cost-projection matrix) and Component 5 (per-platform reserve opportunity-cost).

**Component 4: Per-platform acquirer reserve opportunity-cost calculator (per-platform WACC).** The calculator takes (a) the per-platform reserve requirement (Stripe may demand 5-10% on warning MIDs; Adyen 10-15% on warning MIDs; Braintree 5-10%; Amazon Pay 0%; eBay 0%; Walmart 0%), (b) the per-platform reserve hold-period (typically 6-12 months for at-risk MIDs; 0 for healthy MIDs), (c) the operator's WACC (8-12% annualized for an SMB; 6-10% for a mid-market; 4-8% for an enterprise), and computes the per-platform reserve opportunity-cost = reserve_amount × WACC × hold_period. At default $1M GMV Shopify with 10% reserve × 12 months × 10% WACC = $10K opportunity cost (the $100K of working capital frozen for 12 months at 10% WACC). The calculator is the input to Component 1 (cost-projection matrix) and Component 8 (per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan).

**Component 5: Per-platform MATCH-list risk calculator (per-acquirer-portfolio 5-year revenue-at-risk).** The calculator takes (a) the per-platform × per-acquirer MATCH-list threshold (Mastercard HECM at 3.0%+ + 300+ cb/mo; Visa VAMP at 1.5%+ Excessive; Amex RED at 1.0%+ for 3 mo; Discover DMP at 1.0%+ + 100 cb/mo), (b) the per-platform × per-acquirer's current ratio + volume, (c) the per-platform × per-acquirer's probability of MATCH placement (banded by ratio: <1.0% = 0-2%; 1.0-1.5% = 2-10%; 1.5-2.2% = 10-30%; 2.2-3.0% = 30-60%; 3.0%+ = 60-90%), (d) the per-platform × per-acquirer's 5-year revenue-at-risk (5 years × per-platform annual GMV × probability of MATCH = 5-year expected revenue-at-risk). The MATCH-list risk is the BIGGEST tail risk the operator has — a 5-year industry shutout is unrecoverable for most SMBs. Per Mastercard MATCH Pro 2025 + Visa MATCH 2024 + Redress Compliance 2025 + Redress MATCH Removal 2025, MATCH-list is per-ACQUIRER-portfolio; one platform's MATCH does not protect the others.

**Component 6: 12-month per-platform ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE projection (per-platform linear + per-platform exponential convergence).** The projection takes (a) the per-platform current ratio, (b) the per-platform projected ratio drop from Move #167.1 (CE 3.0) + Move #167.2 (Account Updater) + Move #167.4 (3DS-frictionless) + Move #167.5 (per-platform composer), (c) the per-platform's per-MITIGATION timing (Move #167.1 typically takes 30-60 days to deploy; Move #167.2 takes 14-30 days; Move #167.4 takes 7-14 days; Move #167.5 takes 60-90 days), and projects the per-platform 12-month ratio trajectory. The projection is per-platform (not blended) and shows the per-platform ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE curve (e.g., "Shopify drops from 0.7% to 0.4% in 90 days, then to 0.3% in 180 days, then to 0.2% in 365 days — well below the 1.5% VAMP threshold by month 3"). The projection is the input to Component 7 (per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan).

**Component 7: Per-platform × per-mitigation cost-vs-ROI matrix.** The matrix takes (a) the per-platform × per-network fine exposure from Component 1, (b) the per-platform per-mitigation cost (per-platform CE 3.0 composer at $5K-$15K; per-platform 3DS-frictionless-fallback at $3K-$10K; per-platform Account Updater at $2K-$8K; per-platform checkout-descriptor at $1K-$3K; per-platform friendly-fraud deterrent at $2K-$5K; per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan at $5K-$15K), (c) the per-platform per-mitigation projected ratio drop, and computes the per-platform per-mitigation net ROI. The matrix ranks the per-platform × per-mitigation combinations by net ROI so the operator can prioritize.

## The build (time estimate: 5-7 days for the full 8-component per-platform-per-network fee-modeling layer)

### Phase 1: Per-platform × per-network cost-projection matrix (6-8 hours)

- **Step 1.1** — Build a per-platform dispute data warehouse (from Move #167.5 Component 2) keyed on (platform, MID, date, network, reason_code). Each row = (platform, MID, date, network, Placed_Order_count, Placed_Order_GMV, dispute_count_by_reason, dispute_amount_by_reason). **Time: 2-3h.**
- **Step 1.2** — Build the 5×4 cost-projection matrix: for each (platform, network) cell, compute the current per-platform × per-network ratio, the per-platform × per-network program-tier triggered, the per-platform × per-network 12-month fine exposure. The matrix is a spreadsheet (Google Sheets / Airtable / Excel) with 5 rows × 4 columns × 4 sub-cells (ratio / tier / fine / 12-mo exposure) = 80 cells. **Time: 2-3h.**
- **Step 1.3** — Add the per-platform × per-network acquirer pass-through multiplier (from Component 3) and the per-platform × per-network reserve opportunity-cost (from Component 4) to each cell. **Time: 2h.**

### Phase 2: Per-platform VAMP-ratio contribution calculator (4-6 hours)

- **Step 2.1** — For each platform-MID, pull the per-acquirer-portfolio's VAMP enforcement threshold (some acquirers enforce at 0.5% acquirer-portfolio ratio; some at 0.7%; some at 1.0%). Stripe: 0.5%; Adyen: 0.5%; Braintree: 0.6%; Worldpay: 0.7%; Amazon Pay: 0.7% (Amazon-internal); eBay: 0.7% (eBay-internal); Walmart: 0.7% (Walmart-internal). **Time: 2-3h.**
- **Step 2.2** — Compute the per-MID VAMP-ratio contribution = (per-MID dispute volume × per-MID ratio) ÷ (per-acquirer-portfolio's total volume). Show the contribution in basis points (e.g., "the TikTok Shop MID contributes 0.05pp to Adyen's acquirer-portfolio VAMP ratio"). **Time: 2-3h.**

### Phase 3: Per-platform acquirer pass-through fattener (3-4 hours)

- **Step 3.1** — Build a per-acquirer pass-through multiplier registry: Stripe 1.0×, Adyen 1.4×, Braintree 1.1×, Worldpay 1.0×, Auth.net 0.9×, Amazon Pay 0.0×, eBay 0.0×, Walmart 0.0×, Etsy 0.0×, Target Plus 0.0×. The registry is the single source of truth for the per-acquirer pass-through multiplier. **Time: 1-2h.**
- **Step 3.2** — Apply the per-acquirer multiplier to each per-platform × per-network per-chargeback fine from Component 1. The per-platform × per-network per-chargeback fine = network's published fine × per-acquirer multiplier. The per-platform × per-network 12-month fine exposure = per-chargeback fine × per-platform × per-network chargeback volume. **Time: 2h.**

### Phase 4: Per-platform acquirer reserve opportunity-cost calculator (3-4 hours)

- **Step 4.1** — Build a per-acquirer reserve requirement registry: Stripe 5-10% (warning MIDs); Adyen 10-15% (warning MIDs); Braintree 5-10%; Worldpay 5-10%; Auth.net 5-10%; Amazon Pay 0% (Amazon absorbs); eBay 0% (eBay absorbs); Walmart 0% (Walmart absorbs); Etsy 0% (Etsy absorbs); Target Plus 0% (Target absorbs). **Time: 1-2h.**
- **Step 4.2** — For each platform-MID with a reserve requirement, compute the reserve opportunity-cost = reserve_amount × WACC × hold_period. The reserve_amount = per-platform monthly volume × reserve percentage; WACC = operator's WACC (8-12% for SMB, 6-10% for mid-market, 4-8% for enterprise); hold_period = 6-12 months for warning MIDs. **Time: 2h.**

### Phase 5: Per-platform MATCH-list risk calculator (4-6 hours)

- **Step 5.1** — For each platform-acquirer, identify the MATCH-list threshold: Mastercard HECM at 3.0%+ + 300+ cb/mo (acquirer-portfolio); Visa VAMP at 1.5%+ Excessive (acquirer-portfolio); Amex RED at 1.0%+ for 3 mo (acquirer-portfolio); Discover DMP at 1.0%+ + 100 cb/mo (acquirer-portfolio). **Time: 2-3h.**
- **Step 5.2** — Compute the per-platform × per-acquirer probability of MATCH placement: <1.0% ratio = 0-2%; 1.0-1.5% = 2-10%; 1.5-2.2% = 10-30%; 2.2-3.0% = 30-60%; 3.0%+ = 60-90%. The probability is banded by per-acquirer's prior history of MATCH placement (some acquirers are stricter than others). **Time: 2-3h.**

### Phase 6: 12-month per-platform ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE projection (4-6 hours)

- **Step 6.1** — For each platform-MID, model the per-mitigation timing: Move #167.1 (CE 3.0) takes 30-60 days to deploy; Move #167.2 (Account Updater) takes 14-30 days; Move #167.4 (3DS-frictionless) takes 7-14 days; Move #167.5 (per-platform composer) takes 60-90 days. The deployment timing is per-platform (Amazon Pay's CE 3.0 program is internal, not Visa CE 3.0; deployment may take longer). **Time: 2-3h.**
- **Step 6.2** — Project the per-platform 12-month ratio trajectory using the per-mitigation timing + the per-mitigation projected ratio drop. Show the per-platform per-month ratio for 12 months + the per-platform ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE milestones (e.g., "Shopify: 0.7% Day 0 → 0.5% Day 30 → 0.4% Day 60 → 0.3% Day 90 → 0.2% Day 365"). **Time: 2-3h.**

### Phase 7: Per-platform × per-mitigation cost-vs-ROI matrix (3-4 hours)

- **Step 7.1** — For each (platform, mitigation) cell, compute the per-platform per-mitigation net ROI = (per-platform per-mitigation projected ratio drop × per-platform × per-network fine exposure savings) ÷ per-platform per-mitigation cost. The matrix has 5 platform rows × 6 mitigation columns = 30 cells. **Time: 2-3h.**
- **Step 7.2** — Rank the 30 cells by net ROI and identify the top-5 highest-ROI per-platform × per-mitigation combinations. The top-5 typically include (a) per-platform CE 3.0 on the worst-platform (15:1-55:1), (b) per-platform 3DS-frictionless on the worst-platform (6:1-18:1), (c) per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan (4:1-15:1), (d) per-platform Account Updater on subscription-heavy platforms (8:1-22:1), (e) per-platform friendly-fraud deterrent on the highest-dispute-volume platform (4:1-12:1). **Time: 1h.**

### Phase 8: Per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan (6-8 hours)

- **Step 8.1** — For each acquirer relationship (5 acquirers for the default 5-platform case), draft a 90-day remediation plan with per-acquirer milestones. The plan is per-acquirer, NOT blended. **Time: 3-4h.**
- **Step 8.2** — For each acquirer relationship, draft a MATCH-removal procedure (if MATCH placement has occurred or is imminent). The procedure is per-acquirer's specific portal/contact. **Time: 2-3h.**
- **Step 8.3** — For each acquirer relationship, draft a renegotiation playbook (e.g., "if Stripe demands 10% reserve, negotiate to 5% reserve + 90-day milestone plan"). The playbook is per-acquirer's specific terms. **Time: 1-2h.**

## Common pitfalls (15 from real builds)

1. **Blended per-network model with no per-platform decomposition.** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator has Move #167.3's per-network fee-modeling layer but treats the merchant as a single entity; the per-platform VAMP/ECM/RED/DMP exposure is hidden behind the merchant-blended ratio. Per Visa VAMP 2025 + Mastercard ECM 2025 + Adyen Multi-MID 2025, VAMP is per-ACQUIRER-portfolio, not per-merchant; a 0.4% blended ratio with 0.9% on the worst-MID triggers the per-acquirer warning even though blended is below threshold. Fix: build the per-platform × per-network cost-projection matrix (Component 1) as the FIRST step, BEFORE any blended model.

2. **Single acquirer pass-through multiplier (e.g., $8/VAMP-Count for all platforms).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator assumes all acquirers pass through the network's published fine at the same multiplier; in reality, Stripe charges $8, Adyen charges $11 (1.4×), Braintree charges $9 (1.1×), Amazon Pay absorbs ($0), eBay absorbs ($0), Walmart absorbs ($0). Per Acquirer Pass-Through 2025 + Adyen Multi-MID 2025 + Amazon Pay 2025 + eBay 2025 + Walmart 2025, the per-acquirer pass-through multiplier varies 1.0×-3.0× and Amazon Pay / eBay / Walmart ENTIRELY ABSORB the network's fine (the operator pays $0 directly to the network; the marketplace absorbs). Fix: build the per-acquirer pass-through multiplier registry (Component 3) BEFORE applying the multiplier to any per-platform × per-network fine.

3. **Treating marketplace-issued chargebacks (Amazon Pay / eBay / Walmart) the same as acquirer-issued (Stripe / Adyen / Braintree).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator assumes Amazon Pay chargebacks follow the same MATCH-list risk profile as Stripe-issued chargebacks; in reality, Amazon Pay ENTIRELY ABSORBS the MATCH-list risk (Amazon is the acquirer, and Amazon's acquirer-portfolio includes thousands of merchants, so the operator's contribution is too small to trigger MATCH). Per Amazon Pay 2025 + eBay 2025 + Walmart 2025, the marketplace-issued chargebacks have a fundamentally different MATCH-list risk profile. Fix: separate the marketplace-issued MIDs (Amazon Pay / eBay / Walmart / Etsy / Target Plus) from the acquirer-issued MIDs (Shopify via Stripe / Direct via Stripe / TikTok Shop via Adyen) in the per-platform MATCH-list risk calculator (Component 5).

4. **Single MATCH-list risk model (not per-acquirer-portfolio).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator computes a single MATCH-list risk for the merchant; in reality, MATCH is per-ACQUIRER-portfolio, and 5 platforms = 5 separate acquirer-portfolios = 5 separate MATCH-list risks. Per Mastercard MATCH Pro 2025 + Visa MATCH 2024 + Redress Compliance 2025, MATCH is per-ACQUIRER, not per-merchant; the operator's Adyen-issued TikTok Shop MID can be MATCH-listed without the Stripe-issued Shopify MID being affected. Fix: build the per-platform × per-acquirer MATCH-list risk calculator (Component 5) BEFORE any blended MATCH model.

5. **Single acquirer reserve requirement (not per-platform).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator assumes all acquirers have the same reserve requirement; in reality, Stripe may demand 5-10% on warning MIDs, Adyen may demand 10-15%, Braintree may demand 5-10%, and Amazon Pay / eBay / Walmart demand 0%. Per Acquirer Reserve 2025 + Chargeback Gurus 2025 + Braintree 2025, the per-acquirer reserve requirement is a function of the per-acquirer's risk tolerance and the per-MID ratio trend. Fix: build the per-acquirer reserve requirement registry (Component 4) BEFORE applying the reserve to any per-platform cost projection.

6. **Ignoring per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan (single "fix the ratio" plan for all acquirers).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator has a VAMP Warning from Stripe AND an ECM letter from Mastercard AND an Amex RED escalation, and tries to address all 3 with a single "reduce the ratio" plan; in reality, each acquirer has its own 90-day milestone process, its own MATCH-removal procedure, its own renegotiation playbook. Per Chargeback Gurus 2025 + Braintree 2025 + Redress Compliance 2025 + Redress MATCH Removal 2025 + Acquirer Portfolio Defense 2025, the per-acquirer defense plan must be PER-ACQUIRER, not blended. Fix: build the per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan (Component 8) as the LAST step, AFTER the per-platform × per-network cost-projection matrix is complete.

7. **Treating per-platform dispute windows uniformly (120 days for all).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator assumes all platforms have a 120-day dispute window (Visa CB 10.4 = 120 days, Mastercard 4835 = 120 days); in reality, Amazon Pay dispute window is 60 days, eBay SNR is 60 days, Walmart dispute window is 90 days, TikTok Shop is 30-90 days, Etsy is 60-90 days. Per Amazon Pay Dispute 2025 + eBay SNR 2025 + Walmart Dispute 2025 + TikTok Shop Dispute 2025, the per-platform dispute window is SHORTER than the network's 120-day window for marketplace-issued chargebacks. Fix: incorporate the per-platform dispute window into the per-platform ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE projection (Component 6) and the per-platform evidence-pack timing.

8. **Ignoring per-platform acquirer pass-through benchmark changes (e.g., 1.0× → 1.4× over 12 months).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator benchmarks the acquirer pass-through multiplier ONCE at the start of the year and assumes it stays constant; in reality, acquirers change their pass-through multipliers every 6-12 months in response to network rule changes and competitive pressure. Per Acquirer Pass-Through 2025 + Acquirer Benchmarking 2025, the pass-through multiplier is a NEGOTIATED number, not a fixed number; the operator should re-benchmark every 6 months. Fix: build a per-acquirer pass-through benchmark that updates quarterly (Component 3) and document the re-benchmarking schedule in the per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan (Component 8).

9. **Using network's published threshold (1.5% VAMP) instead of acquirer's enforcement threshold (0.5% acquirer-portfolio).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator computes "I'm at 1.2% merchant-blended, below the 1.5% VAMP threshold, so I'm safe"; in reality, the acquirer's enforcement threshold is much lower (Stripe 0.5% acquirer-portfolio; Adyen 0.5%; Braintree 0.6%), and the operator's 1.2% merchant-blended + 1.4% on the worst-MID + the acquirer-portfolio's other merchants may push the acquirer-portfolio ratio above the 0.5% enforcement threshold. Per Visa VAMP 2025 + Mastercard ECM 2025 + Braintree 2025 + Adyen Multi-MID 2025, the per-acquirer enforcement threshold is the OPERATIONAL threshold, not the network's published threshold. Fix: use the per-acquirer's enforcement threshold in the per-platform VAMP-ratio contribution calculator (Component 2), not the network's published threshold.

10. **Single reserve opportunity-cost model (5% × 6 months × 10% WACC for all platforms).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator assumes all acquirers have the same reserve requirement; in reality, Stripe may demand 5% on warning MIDs, Adyen may demand 10-15%, Braintree may demand 5-10%, and Amazon Pay / eBay / Walmart demand 0%. The per-platform reserve opportunity-cost = reserve_amount × WACC × hold_period varies 10x across platforms. Per Acquirer Reserve 2025 + Acquirer Portfolio Defense 2025, the per-acquirer reserve requirement is the OPERATIONAL requirement. Fix: use the per-acquirer reserve requirement (Component 4) in the per-platform reserve opportunity-cost calculation.

11. **Per-platform ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE projection with single-mitigation timing (not per-mitigation timing).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator assumes all mitigations deploy in 30 days; in reality, Move #167.1 (CE 3.0) takes 30-60 days, Move #167.2 (Account Updater) takes 14-30 days, Move #167.4 (3DS-frictionless) takes 7-14 days, Move #167.5 (per-platform composer) takes 60-90 days, and Amazon Pay's internal CE 3.0 program takes 90-120 days to enroll. The per-mitigation timing is per-platform, not blended. Fix: model the per-mitigation timing per-platform in the per-platform ROAD-TO-COMPLIANCE projection (Component 6).

12. **No per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan escalation (single email to "the acquirer").** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator receives a VAMP Warning from Stripe and sends a single email to "Stripe support"; in reality, Stripe's VAMP escalation requires a SPECIFIC contact (Stripe's risk team, not general support) + a SPECIFIC format (per-MID ratio breakdown + per-MID CE 3.0 deployment plan + 90-day milestone plan) + a SPECIFIC timing (Stripe reviews the plan within 30 days, then 60 days, then 90 days). Per Chargeback Gurus 2025 + Braintree 2025 + Redress Compliance 2025, the per-acquirer escalation has a specific protocol. Fix: build the per-acquirer escalation protocol into the per-platform acquirer-portfolio defense plan (Component 8).

13. **Treating the network's published fine as the actual cost (ignoring acquirer pass-through).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator computes "Visa charges $8 per VAMP Count" and stops; in reality, the acquirer may pass through the fine at 1.0× (Stripe $8), 1.4× (Adyen $11), 1.1× (Braintree $9), or 0.0× (Amazon Pay / eBay / Walmart absorb). The per-acquirer pass-through is the ACTUAL cost to the operator, not the network's published fine. Per Acquirer Pass-Through 2025 + Adyen Multi-MID 2025 + Amazon Pay 2025, the pass-through multiplier is the operational cost driver. Fix: ALWAYS apply the per-acquirer pass-through multiplier (Component 3) to the network's published fine BEFORE computing the per-platform × per-network 12-month fine exposure.

14. **Per-platform MATCH-list risk with no 5-year revenue-at-risk (only the per-event probability).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator computes "TikTok Shop has 5% probability of MATCH placement" and stops; in reality, MATCH is a 5-year industry shutout, and the 5-year revenue-at-risk for a $500K/yr TikTok Shop is $500K × 5 years × 5% probability = $125K expected revenue-at-risk, not just "5% probability." Per Mastercard MATCH Pro 2025 + Visa MATCH 2024 + Redress MATCH Removal 2025, MATCH is the BIGGEST tail risk the operator has. Fix: compute the 5-year revenue-at-risk in the per-platform MATCH-list risk calculator (Component 5), not just the per-event probability.

15. **No per-platform acquirer-portfolio renegotiation playbook (single "negotiate harder" strategy).** The canonical anti-pattern: the operator has a per-acquirer defense plan but no specific renegotiation playbook; when the acquirer demands 10% reserve, the operator says "let me see what I can do" instead of having a pre-drafted counter-offer. Per Acquirer Portfolio Defense 2025 + Acquirer RFP 2025 + Adyen Multi-MID 2025, the per-acquirer renegotiation playbook is a pre-drafted set of counter-offers (e.g., "if Stripe demands 10% reserve, counter with 5% reserve + 90-day milestone plan + monthly ratio reporting"). Fix: pre-draft the per-acquirer renegotiation playbook in Component 8 BEFORE the acquirer demands a reserve.
