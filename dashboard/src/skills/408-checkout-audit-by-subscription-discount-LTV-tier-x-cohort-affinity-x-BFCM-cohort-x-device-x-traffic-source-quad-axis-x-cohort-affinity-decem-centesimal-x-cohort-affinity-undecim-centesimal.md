## Common pitfalls (23 numbered, P1-P23)

1. ship-from-warehouse-engine-version-stale is a warehouse-availability-cache-drift risk, not just a leak-rate risk. Move #3.52's per-cohort-affinity-ship-from-warehouse-engine-version ladder reads the BFCM-cohort-window freshness stamp; if the merchant's Shopify-FulfillmentNetwork-undecim-centesimal hasn't been re-synced in 7+ days, BFCM-week-1 first-ship-discount cohort may be served stale warehouse-availability-cache that COMPLETES but ships from a 0-stock warehouse (overselling). Always wire `warehouse_availability_last_refreshed_at` freshness stamp alongside the leak-rate counter.
2. ship-from-store-engine-version-stale is an in-store-inventory-mismatch risk. Per-cohort-affinity-ship-from-store-engine-version tracks the canonical Shopify-POS-Fulfillment-undecim-centesimal vs Square-POS-Ship-undecim-centesimal; if the in-store-inventory-stream was last refreshed pre-BFCM, BFCM-peak-cohort buy-online-pickup-in-store (BOPIS) cohort sees in-store-stock=0 and the per-cohort-affinity leak-rate counter falsely reads 90% when the real cause is a 6h-stale in-store-inventory-stream.
3. ship-from-supplier-engine-version-stale is a supplier-stockout-mismatch risk.
