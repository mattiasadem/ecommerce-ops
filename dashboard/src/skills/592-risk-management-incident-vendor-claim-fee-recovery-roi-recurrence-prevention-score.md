## What "best in class" looks like

A best-in-class Risk Management Per-vendor Claim-fee-vs-recovery ROI Chip + Per-vendor Recurrence-prevention-Score implementation has **8 distinguishing primitives**:

1. **Per-vendor claim-fee-vs-recovery ROI chip** — every per-incident vendor-claim from Move #N.99.1's `incidentLedger` gets summarized into a per-vendor × per-RCA-category × per-claim-fee × per-recovered-amount × per-win-rate × per-recovery-ROI-per-dollar-filed chip that auto-generates after every vendor-claim resolution. The chip is a single-row card per vendor × per-RCA-category with: **vendor logo + name** · **RCA-category label** (e.g. `chargeback-representment`, `ad-account-reinstatement`, `3PL-pallet-loss`, `email-deliverability-recovery`, `cyber-insurance-claim`) · **`+$X,XXX`** recovered amount (emerald) · **`- $X,XXX`** total-fees-filed amount (muted) · **`X%`** win-rate (4-band tone per Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024: emerald ≥60% · sky 40-59% · amber 20-39% · rose <20%) · **`$X.XX`** ROI-per-dollar-filed (the canonical operator-question answer: "for every $1 I spent filing with this vendor, how many dollars did I recover?") · **`#N`** total-claims count · **`last-filed-at`** timestamp · **"Open vendor-portfolio →"** link to the per-vendor deep-dive page. The chip is the canonical "spend-the-least-on-vendor-X-to-claim-the-most-from-vendor-X" optimizer per Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024 + Rothy's Chargeback Spike 2024. Example rendered chip: `**Stripe** · chargeback-representment · +$7,200 · -$345 (23 @ $15) · **38%** win-rate · **$20.87** ROI/$1 · 23 claims · last-filed 2024-09-12`. With a sibling chip below: `**Shop Pay** · chargeback-representment · +$4,800 · -$240 (12 @ $20) · **50%** win-rate · **$20.00** ROI/$1 · 12 claims · last-filed 2024-08-30` — so the operator sees the side-by-side comparison in one glance and decides to push the next 10 chargebacks to Shop Pay.

2. **Per-vendor 7-band fee-drivers taxonomy** — every per-vendor claim-fee gets categorized into exactly one of the 7 canonical fee-drivers (per Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024):
   - **`per-claim-flat`** — flat fee per claim filed (e.g. Stripe's $15 dispute-fee, Shop Pay's $20 dispute-fee, Etsy marketplace-seller-protection's $0 fee)
   - **`per-recovered-pct`** — percentage of recovered amount (e.g. some 3PL-claim processors charge 15% of recovery, debt-collection agencies charge 25% of recovery)
   - **`per-month-recurring`** — recurring monthly fee per vendor-relationship (e.g. some cyber-insurance carriers charge $50-$200/mo per active-incident-tracker subscription)
   - **`per-tier-banded`** — tier-banded by claim-amount (e.g. crypto-fraud-recovery firms charge $500 for claims <$10k, $1,500 for $10-50k, $5,000 for $50-250k)
   - **`per-incident-capped`** — capped per-incident (e.g. some cyber-insurance policies cap payout-fee-recovery at $5k/incident)
   - **`per-claim-step`** — step-function (e.g. Chargeback Gurus charge $9 per claim for the first 100, $7 per claim for 100-500, $5 per claim for 500+)
   - **`per-volume-banded`** — volume-banded monthly fee (e.g. some 3PL warehouse-claim aggregators charge 0.05% of monthly 3PL-vendor-spend)

   The taxonomy is intentionally COMPREHENSIVE — every fee-model in the canonical 11-vendor-coverage-list maps to one of the 7 bands. The taxonomy is the load-bearing first-step of the per-vendor ROI computation — without fee-driver classification the operator cannot answer "what's the marginal cost of filing one more chargeback-representment with Stripe?" (per-claim-flat = exactly $15 = straightforward) vs. "what's the marginal cost of filing one more 3PL-claim with vendor-X?" (per-recovered-pct = 15% × $X-recovered, increases with claim-size = not straightforward). Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024 + Beazley Breach Response 2024.

3. **Per-vendor win-rate-floor with 4-band tone-class** — every vendor × RCA-category combination gets a canonical `win-rate-floor` against which the current 18-month win-rate is measured (per Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + Cutwater Capital Ecommerce Risk 2024 + Coalition Cyber Claims 2024):
   - **`emerald`** — current win-rate ≥win-rate-floor + 10pp (e.g. Shop Pay chargeback win-rate is 50% vs floor 40% → emerald; signal "above-floor, continue current volume")
   - **`sky`** — current win-rate within ±10pp of win-rate-floor (e.g. Stripe chargeback win-rate is 38% vs floor 40% → sky; signal "at-floor, investigate why win-rate is dropping")
   - **`amber`** — current win-rate is win-rate-floor-10pp to win-rate-floor-20pp below (e.g. Stripe chargeback win-rate is 30% vs floor 40% → amber; signal "below-floor, escalate to vendor's higher-tier per the escalation-rule")
   - **`rose`** — current win-rate is win-rate-floor-20pp+ below (e.g. Stripe chargeback win-rate is 20% vs floor 40% → rose; signal "well-below-floor, auto-trigger vendor-forensics-audit")

   The win-rate-floors are vendor-specific (NOT a one-size-fits-all). Stripe's chargeback-representment floor is 40% (their AI-dispute-system averages 35-50% per Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024); Shop Pay's chargeback-representment floor is 40% (similar); Meta's ad-account-reinstatement floor is 90% (their appeal-system averages 92-95% per Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024); Coalition's cyber-insurance claim-approval floor is 60%; At-Bay's is 55%; 3PL-claim-approval-vendor floor is 50%. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024.

4. **Per-vendor recovery-curve-decay with half-life** — every vendor × RCA-category gets a canonical `recovery-curve-half-life` measured in months-since-claim-filed × per-month-recovery-rate (per Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + PagerDuty Ecommerce Incident Data 2024):
   - **`half-life-fast (≤3 months)`** — Stripe chargeback representment: ~85% of recoveries arrive in days 7-21 with the remaining ~15% in days 22-90. Stripe's recovery is back-loaded on first-cycle (most wins arrive day-7 to day-21 per Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024), so the decay half-life is ~14 days.
   - **`half-life-moderate (4-6 months)`** — cyber-insurance claim (Coalition / At-Bay / Beazley): 30% of recoveries arrive in months 1-3, 50% in months 4-6, 20% in months 7-12 per Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Woodruff Sawyer Cyber Claims 2024. Half-life ~4 months.
   - **`half-life-slow (7-12 months)`** — marketplace-seller-protection (Amazon / eBay / Etsy): most recover in 30-90 days but the long-tail appeals can run 6-12 months per Etsy 2024 transparency report. Half-life ~7 months.
   - **`half-life-extreme (12+ months)`** — regulatory-fine-recovery (GDPR / CCPA / state-attorney-general): 90% recovers in 12-24 months with appeals running 24+ months per Coalition Cyber Claims 2024. Half-life ~14 months.

   The half-life is the canonical pre-deployment predictor: an operator with $50k of pending Stripe chargeback-representment today can predict ~$42.5k will arrive in 14 days + ~$7.5k will arrive in 14-90 days. An operator with $50k of pending cyber-insurance-claim can predict ~$15k in 0-3 months + $25k in 4-6 months + $10k in 7-12 months. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + Woodruff Sawyer Cyber Claims 2024 + Marsh Cyber Insurance 2024 + PagerDuty Ecommerce Incident Data 2024.

5. **Per-vendor 5-tier escalation-rule auto-builder** — every vendor × RCA-category gets a canonical escalation-rule that fires automatically (per Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024):
   - **Tier-0: self-service-portal** — every vendor's default filing path (Stripe's dashboard / Shop Pay's portal / Meta's appeal-portal / 3PL's customer-success-email). No escalation needed; ROI per dollar-filed is best at this tier for low-severity ($0-$1k) claims.
   - **Tier-1: chargeback-recovery-firm / 3PL-claim-specialist** — for medium-severity ($1k-$25k) claims, the operator escalates to a third-party specialist (e.g. Chargeback Gurus / Chargebacks911 / MidMetrics for chargebacks; TopShelf / 3PL-claim-aggregators for 3PLs). ROI per dollar-filed is best at this tier for medium-severity.
   - **Tier-2: vendor's Risk-Team / dedicated-account-manager** — for high-severity ($25k-$100k) claims, the operator escalates within the vendor to the dedicated risk/account-management team. ROI per dollar-filed is best at this tier for high-severity (Stripe's Risk-Team has a 60-70% win-rate per Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 vs the 38% Tier-0 win-rate; Meta's dedicated-account-manager has 95%+ reinstatement-rate vs 50-70% Tier-0 per Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024).
   - **Tier-3: arbitration / legal-action / regulatory-complaint** — for critical-severity ($100k+) claims, the operator escalates to Visa / Mastercard arbitration, FTC complaint, or state-attorney-general complaint. ROI per dollar-filed is best at this tier for critical-severity (Visa arbitration has a 75% operator-win-rate vs 38% Tier-0 per Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Cutwater Capital 2024).
   - **Tier-4: vendor-forensics-audit + carrier-grade-evidence-pack** — for vendor-recurrence-prevention (2+ same-vendor same-RCA-category in 24 months), the operator escalates to a formal vendor-forensics-audit with the vendor's engineering / risk team + attaches the cyber-insurance carrier-grade evidence-pack. The audit produces a written recurrence-prevention-commitment-letter that the carrier counts as preventive-control-evidence.

   The auto-builder picks the starting-tier based on the incident-severity + ROI-per-tier data: an incident with $50k RecoverableLoss automatically starts at Tier-2 (dedicated-account-manager), saving the operator the time-cost of escalating up from Tier-0. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024.

6. **Per-vendor recurrence-prevention-score with auto-trigger vendor-forensics-audit** — every vendor × RCA-category gets a canonical 0-100 recurrence-prevention-score computed from the 2nd-gen-incident-tracker (Move #N.99.3) × the 5-action-item-type corrective-action verification-gate (Move #N.99.3) × the canonical 3-tier forensics-audit trigger (per Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024):
   - **score 90-100** — no recurrence in 24 months; corrective-actions verified-shipped day-90; vendor is `emerald`-band; signal "keep filing at current volume".
   - **score 70-89** — 1 recurrence in 24 months; corrective-actions verified-shipped day-60; vendor is `sky`-band; signal "monitor, do not escalate yet".
   - **score 50-69** — 1 recurrence in 12 months OR 2 in 36 months; corrective-actions verified-shipped day-30 but not day-60; vendor is `amber`-band; signal "auto-trigger Tier-2 escalation (dedicated-account-manager) + Tier-1 forensics-audit request".
   - **score <50** — 2+ recurrences in 24 months OR 3+ in 36 months; corrective-actions verified-shipped day-30 BUT recurrence-prevention-evidence lacking; vendor is `rose`-band; signal "auto-trigger Tier-4 vendor-forensics-audit + add to cyber-insurance-renewal-questionnaire Section G 'vendor-recurrence-explainability' + flag next year's reserve allocation".

   The auto-trigger fires the vendor-forensics-audit when the score crosses <50 — the operator gets a Slack + e-mail + dashboard notification: "**Stripe chargeback-representment recurrence-prevention-score has dropped to 47/100 (rose-band) — 3 chargeback-spike recurrences in 24 months from cohort-LTV-tier-1 — auto-filing Tier-4 vendor-forensics-audit request with Stripe's Risk-Team + auto-filing cyber-insurance-coverage-appeal to flag Stripe's process as a contributing-factor.** The auto-trigger is the canonical operator-question answer: "when does a vendor's pattern of recurrences become CAUSE FOR ACTION, not just cause for note?" — answer: when the score drops to <50. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024 + Verizon DBIR 2024.

7. **Per-vendor RCA-category-stacked recurrence-ratchet** — when 2+ same-RCA-category recurrences fire in 24 months, the recurrence-ratchet auto-stacks the per-RCA-category factor into the per-vendor recurrence-prevention-score AND auto-upgrades the corrective-action from `criterion` (sprint-contract-row) to `rule` (`.claude/rules/.md`) so the operator ships a documented recurrence-prevention-rule (per TruPath Labs Postmortem Discipline 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024):
   - **1st recurrence** — recurrence-ratchet fires `criterion`-type corrective-action (sprint-contract-row: "Holdout chargeback-spike rate < 0.3% verified by test_X"); recurrence-prevention-score drops by 10-15 points (e.g. 92 → 78).
   - **2nd recurrence** — recurrence-ratchet fires `criterion` + `rule`-type corrective-action (`.claude/rules/.md`: "Pre-checkout alert threshold is 0.3% chargeback-spike, not 0.5%; alert fires below threshold; carrier-grade evidence-pack is auto-attached"); recurrence-prevention-score drops by 15-25 points (e.g. 78 → 56, amber-band).
   - **3rd recurrence** — recurrence-ratchet fires `rule` + `hook` + `test`-type corrective-action (pre-commit-hook blocks any commit touching alert-thresholds without verification + test_re_raises.py verifies the new threshold fires); recurrence-prevention-score drops by 20-30 points (e.g. 56 → 28, rose-band); auto-trigger Tier-4 vendor-forensics-audit.

   The ratchet is the canonical mechanism for OPERATIONALIZING the recurrence-prevention-score — without it, the score is just a number; with it, each recurrence auto-tightens the preventive-control-layer. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + TruPath Labs Postmortem Discipline 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024.

8. **Per-vendor savings-projection chip + next-move auto-CTA** — every per-vendor ROI chip includes a `savings-projection` field that computes "if operator pushes the next $X in claims to this vendor's higher tier + switches vendors per the ROI-per-dollar-filed ranking, the operator saves $Y over the next 24 months" + a `next-move` auto-CTA per Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024:
   - **`switch-vendor-CTA`** — "Stripe's 38% win-rate costs ~$8k/yr more than Shop Pay's 50% — switch 30 of your next 80 chargebacks to Shop Pay to recover an additional $3.6k/yr"
   - **`escalate-tier-CTA`** — "Stripe's at-floor win-rate auto-triggers Tier-2 Risk-Team — file the next 5 disputes via Stripe Risk-Team-tier to recover an additional 22pp win-rate"
   - **`forensics-audit-CTA`** — "Meta's 3rd recurrence in 18 months triggers Tier-4 vendor-forensics-audit — file the audit to recover a written recurrence-prevention-commitment-letter that the carrier counts as preventive-control-evidence + raises your renewal approval probability by 10-15%"
   - **`halt-vendor-CTA`** — "3PL-X's 0% recovery-rate over 8 attempts is below-floor — halt new 3PL-X claims + auto-route to 3PL-Y which has 40% recovery-rate; projected savings $5k/yr"

   The chip is the canonical "what action should I take right now to recover more with this vendor?" optimizer. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Verizon DBIR 2024.

## Common pitfalls (16 from real builds)

1. **Don't ship the per-vendor chip WITHOUT the 4-tone win-rate-floor comparison.** A chip that just shows "Stripe: 38% win-rate" without the floor (40% per Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024) and the 4-tone (sky/amber/rose) leaves the operator guessing whether 38% is good, bad, or at-floor. Always show the floor + the band + the delta-vs-floor. The chip's value is the comparison, not the raw number. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024.

2. **Don't accept fee-driver tagging as manual — auto-tag from the vendor-rule-table on first-claim.** The 7-band fee-drivers taxonomy has a canonical `vendorRuleTable` that maps every canonical vendor to its dominant fee-driver (Stripe → `per-claim-flat` $15; Shop Pay → `per-claim-flat` $20; Meta → `$0 (waived on appeal)`; 3PL-X → `per-recovered-pct` 15%; Coalition → `per-month-recurring` $50-$200; Klaviyo BAA → `per-tier-banded` $0.50-$2k/yr). Forcing the operator to manually tag every fee-driver invites errors. Auto-tag from the table on first-claim, fall back to manual override on vendor-not-in-table. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024 + Hiscox Breach Response 2024.

3. **Don't compute the recurrence-prevention-score against the operator's CURRENT vendor × RCA-category only — include ALL vendors in the last 24 months.** A vendor-recurrence at 24 months is computed against the vendor × RCA-category 24-month window, NOT against the operator's total incident-history. The vendor-recurrence-prevention-score measures whether THIS VENDOR's process / fee-model / dispute-system is breaking, independent of which OTHER vendors are also failing. Use separate keys: `ecom-ops:vendor-rca-category-recurrence-ratchet:v1` (per-vendor) and `ecom-ops:second-gen-incident-tracker:v1` (per-RCA-category across all vendors). Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Verizon DBIR 2024.

4. **Don't merge the 4-band tone (emerald/sky/amber/rose) into a single "ok/bad" binary** — keep the bands distinct. A vendor at sky-band ("at-floor, investigate") requires a different action (escalate-to-tier-2) than a vendor at amber-band ("below-floor, audit"). Don't simplify to "good/bad" — operators need the canonical 4-band to know what action to take. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024.

5. **Don't compute the recovery-curve-decay without a multi-month history** — require ≥6 months of claim history per vendor × RCA-category. A vendor with 1 claim in the last 30 days gives a 100% win-rate but the half-life projection is unreliable. Force the operator to either wait for more history OR set the half-life to "insufficient-data" + show the projection as "needs ≥6 months to project". Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + Woodruff Sawyer Cyber Claims 2024.

6. **Don't auto-route incidents to the 5-tier escalation-rule WITHOUT consulting per-tier-ROI baseline** — the auto-builder should pick starting-tier based on incident-severity × per-tier-ROI-baseline × per-tier-cost-to-escalate. An incident with $5k RecoverableLoss with Stripe's Tier-2 has $X-tier-cost-to-escalate of ~$50 + projected-ROI of 60% × $5k = $3k; auto-routing to Tier-2 makes sense. An incident with $500 RecoverableLoss with Stripe's Tier-2 has same $50 cost-to-escalate + projected-ROI of 60% × $500 = $300 < $50 → auto-routing to Tier-2 LOSES MONEY; route to Tier-0 instead. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024.

7. **Don't fire the Tier-4 vendor-forensics-audit auto-trigger WITHOUT cyber-insurance-carrier-grade evidence-pack attachment.** A vendor-forensics-audit request without the carrier-grade evidence-pack (Move #N.99.1's 12-artifact pack + Move #N.99.2's 18-artifact combined-pack) is a weak escalation that the vendor can dismiss as "operator-error, not vendor-issue". The auto-trigger fires the audit-request + auto-attaches the carrier-grade evidence-pack + auto-sends the audit-request to the vendor's Risk-Team + copies the cyber-insurance carrier's broker. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024 + Woodruff Sawyer Cyber Claims 2024.

8. **Don't compute the RCA-category-stacked recurrence-ratchet's tier-2 `rule`-type corrective-action WITHOUT a `.claude/rules/.md` file template.** The ratchet auto-tightens "alert threshold" → `criterion` (sprint-contract-row); "alert threshold" → `criterion + rule` (`.claude/rules/.md` named `recurrence-prevention-<vendor>-<rcaCategory>.md`). The template must be auto-generated from the ratchet auto-builder; the operator only needs to edit 2-3 lines and commit. Without the template, the operator ends up writing `.claude/rules/.md` files from scratch and the ratchet is dead-on-arrival. Reference: TruPath Labs Postmortem Discipline 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024.

9. **Don't ship the per-vendor savings-projection chip WITHOUT the operator's `recoverableLedger` baseline.** A savings-projection of "$3,600/yr by switching 30 chargebacks to Shop Pay" needs the operator's CURRENT volume on Stripe (a 30-chargeback projection × $5k-avg-disputed × 38% win-rate × $5,700 recovered on Stripe = projected Switch-recovery $7,500) — without the baseline the projection is wrong. Always require `recoverableLedger: { vendor: string; rcaCategory: string; claimCount: number; recovered: number }` per vendor × RCA-category before projecting. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024.

10. **Don't accept vendor-portfolio as static — the canonical 11-vendor list evolves.** New vendors appear (e.g. Stripe launched "Chargeback Protection" auto-defender in 2024; Adyen launched "Dispute Management" in 2024; Klarna launched "Buyer Protection Fee" $20/claim). The vendor-portfolio should auto-update from a vendor-rule-table fetched quarterly; the operator's stored vendors get tagged "deprecated" when the canonical list no longer has them. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024 + Beazley Breach Response 2024.

11. **Don't fire the auto-CTA without confirming the incident is not "in-cooldown" — a chip that auto-suggests "switch to Shop Pay" while the operator has 23 unresolved Stripe chargebacks in flight is premature.** Always check `unresolvedClaimsByVendor: Map<vendor, count>` before firing the switch-vendor-CTA: if unresolvedClaimsByVendor ≥ 5, surface amber "you have 5+ unresolved Stripe claims in flight — finish those before switching". Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Rothy's Chargeback Spike 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024.

12. **Don't conflate per-vendor recurrence-prevention-score with per-RCA-category recurrence-prevention-score** (sklearn'd from Move #N.99.3's pitfall #6). The vendor-level score measures whether THIS VENDOR's process is broken. The RCA-category-level score measures whether the same RCA-category (across all vendors) is recurring. A vendor at 50/100 with a single RCA-category may indicate vendor-specific issue (the vendor's process); an RCA-category at 50/100 with 3 vendors indicates RCA-category issue (not vendor-specific). Use separate keys. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024.

13. **Don't accept "fee waived" or "no fee" as the absence of fee — model the implicit-cost.** Many "fee-waived" vendor-processes have an implicit cost (operator's time + tool-cost + carrier-grade-evidence-pack-cost). For example, Meta's ad-account-reinstatement is "$0 fee" but costs ~$2k in operator-time to file a 6-hour-appeal + $250 in cyber-insurance-carrier-grade-evidence-pack attachments. Model the implicit-cost separately from the explicit-fee. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024.

14. **Don't compute the recovery-curve-decay against the per-month history ONLY** — also compute against per-cohort × per-RCA-category. The same vendor can have a fast-half-life for B2B-cohort chargebacks but a slow-half-life for SMB-cohort chargebacks because SMB customers don't respond to dispute-emails as reliably. The recovery-curve should be per-vendor × per-RCA-category × per-cohort. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + PagerDuty Ecommerce Incident Data 2024 + Stripe Fraud Disputes 2024 + Rothy's Chargeback Spike 2024.

15. **Don't fire the Tier-4 forensics-audit auto-trigger on a vendor-recurrence-prevention-score that includes <3 incidents** — small samples produce unreliable scores. A vendor with 1 incident × 0 recurrences has a 100/100 score (vacuously good); but the score is unreliable. Require ≥3 incidents OR ≥24 months of data before computing the score; otherwise flag as "insufficient-data". Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + Cuts Clothing 2024 + Athletic Greens Meta Double Incident 2024.

16. **Don't ship Move #N.99.4 without wiring the per-vendor ROI chip + per-vendor recurrence-prevention-score to Move #N.99.8 (cyber-insurance-renewal-readiness-pack).** The chip + score auto-attach to the renewal-readiness-pack — the carrier sees "per-vendor ROI on chargeback-representment: 38% at $20.87 ROI/$1 · per-vendor ROI on ad-account-reinstatement: 100% at $0 fee · per-vendor recurrence-prevention-score for Stripe chargeback-representment: 47/100 (rose-band, auto-triggered Tier-4 audit)". Without this wire, the carrier asks "what's your per-vendor ROI?" and the operator has no answer — the renewal-premium goes up 15-25%. Reference: Coalition Cyber Claims 2024 + At-Bay Incident Response 2024 + Woodruff Sawyer Cyber Claims 2024 + Marsh Cyber Insurance 2024.
