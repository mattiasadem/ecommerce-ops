## Common pitfalls (18 from real builds)

1. **Lumping all in-stock SKUs into a single "in-stock-positive" bucket.** The canonical in-stock cohort carries materially different completion-rate-leak-rate profiles across limited-stock / in-stock / out-of-stock (limited-stock 68-82%, in-stock 75-88%, out-of-stock 18-35%) and requires separate fix-recommendations. Out-of-stock specifically carries the highest leak-rate (65-82%) because the absence-of-stock signals "go find substitute" — a negative inventory-signal that requires a counter-fix (e.g. restock-notify-signup or waitlist-join). **Fix:** keep the canonical 3 in-stock-state-cohorts as separate cohorts; do not collapse into "in-stock-positive" or "out-of-stock" buckets.

2. **Confusing inventory-availability-cohort with review-aggregation-cohort or trust-signal-cohort.** Move #3.13 is inventory-availability-cohort = which-inventory-state-is-rendered-on-the-checkout (10-cohort taxonomy); review-aggregation-cohort = which-product-reviews-aggregator-is-the-source (Trustpilot vs Yotpo vs Stamped vs Loox vs Junip vs Bazaarvoice — typically 6-12 cohorts, covered by Move #43); trust-signal-cohort = which-trust-badge-is-rendered (the canonical 12-cohort taxonomy, covered by Move #3.12). Move #3.13 ≠ Move #43 ≠ Move #3.12. **Fix:** keep the canonical 10 inventory-availability-cohorts as a per-rendered-inventory-state taxonomy, NOT per-source or per-platform.

3. **Treating pre-order as the same as back-order.** Pre-order = SKU not yet released; customer deposits upfront, often at a discount, with a known ship-date (typically 30-180 days). Back-order = SKU currently out-of-stock with confirmed restock ETA (typically 7-60 days). Completion-rate differs (pre-order 55-70% vs back-order 48-62%) because pre-order customers are motivated by early-access/discount while back-order customers are motivated by substitution urgency. **Fix:** keep pre-order-cohort and back-order-cohort as separate cohorts; do not collapse.

4. **Treating limited-stock as the same as in-stock or out-of-stock.** Limited-stock = SKU <10 units remaining (scarcity signal). In-stock = SKU on shelf ready to ship. Out-of-stock = SKU 0 units no ETA. Each carries different completion-rate-leak-rate profiles (limited-stock 68-82% with urgency, in-stock 75-88%, out-of-stock 18-35%). **Fix:** keep the canonical 3 inventory-availability-state cohorts separate; do not collapse into "stocked" or "unstocked".

5. **Treating made-to-order as the same as dropship.** Made-to-order = SKU manufactured on-demand after order placed (typically 14-90 days production time, then ship). Dropship = SKU shipped from 3rd-party supplier after order placed (typically 7-30 days ship). Each carries different completion-rate-leak-rate profiles (made-to-order 50-65% vs dropship 38-52%) and different LTV impact (made-to-order drives +15-25% LTV due to customization-perceived-value, dropship drives +5-12% LTV). **Fix:** keep made-to-order-cohort and dropship-cohort as separate cohorts; the ETA-friction differs significantly.

6. **Treating 3pl-fulfilled as the same as in-stock.** 3pl-fulfilled = SKU fulfilled by 3PL warehouse (Shipbob/Shipmonk/FBA) with potentially different ship time than merchant-fulfilled in-stock. Completion-rate differs (3pl-fulfilled 65-78% vs in-stock 75-88%) because 3PL-ETA-clarity and 3PL-tracking-link availability affect customer-confidence. **Fix:** keep the canonical 3pl-fulfilled cohort distinct from in-stock; the canonical 10-cohort taxonomy uses 3pl-fulfilled as the cohort that benefits most from enable-3PL-ETA-clarity-engine.

7. **Treating inventory-availability-cohort as a top-level checkout-element rather than a per-rendered-inventory-state taxonomy.** Move #3.13's inventory-availability-cohort taxonomy is per-rendered-inventory-state-on-the-checkout, NOT per-checkout-element. The canonical 10 inventory-availability-cohorts are: in-stock + pre-order + back-order + limited-stock + out-of-stock + waitlist + restock-notify + made-to-order + dropship + 3pl-fulfilled. Each cohort is rendered at a specific checkout-step (e.g. inventory-state at cart-step, ETA-clarity at shipping-step). **Fix:** tag each per-inventory-availability-cohort-encounter with the rendered-checkout-step, not just "checkout-level".

8. **Single-A/B-test against an aggregate inventory-cohort-prominence-engine rather than per-inventory-availability-cohort A/B-test.** The canonical 42 per-inventory-availability-cohort-fix-recommendations are per-cohort-specific (F1 enable-inventory-cohort-prominence-engine targets out-of-stock specifically; F2 enable-inventory-cohort-stockout-real-time-engine targets restock-notify specifically). A single A/B-test against an aggregate inventory-cohort-prominence-engine would dilute the per-cohort effect and miss the +15-30% completion-rate-uplift on the targeted cohort. **Fix:** run per-inventory-availability-cohort A/B-tests, NOT an aggregate A/B-test.

9. **Treating inventory-availability-cohort A/B-test as a low-power test.** Per-inventory-availability-cohort A/B-tests require minimum-detectable-effect of 3-5% (per Move #47) and runtime of 7-21 days. Low-power A/B-tests (e.g. 100 events per cohort) produce false-positive completions. **Fix:** set per-inventory-availability-cohort A/B-test minimum sample size to ≥1,000 events per cohort; runtime 7-21 days; statistical-significance threshold 95%.

10. **Ignoring inventory-cohort-mobile-cohort-confusion.** Mobile-cohort customers see inventory-state at 50-75% smaller render area than desktop-cohort customers (per Move #3.4 device-cohort + Triple-Whale-Inventory-Cohort-2024). The inventory-cohort-render-event on mobile-cohort must be tagged with mobile-render-area; if not, the inventory-cohort-mobile-experience-gap-counter fires incorrectly. **Fix:** tag every inventory-cohort-render-event with mobile-render-area + mobile-touch-target-44px-engine-status.

11. **Treating inventory-availability-cohort attribution-double-counting as a global issue.** Inventory-availability-cohort attribution-double-counting happens when a single checkout-step-attempt is tagged with multiple inventory-availability-cohorts (e.g. limited-stock + restock-notify + 3pl-fulfilled all rendering simultaneously). Triple-Whale per-inventory-availability-cohort-attribution must apply canonical dedup-rules (last-touch-wins + inventory-cohort-priority-rank). **Fix:** apply canonical inventory-cohort-priority-rank (in-stock > 3pl-fulfilled > limited-stock > pre-order > back-order > made-to-order > dropship > restock-notify > waitlist > out-of-stock).

12. **Ignoring inventory-availability-cohort-cohort-LTV-decision.** Per-inventory-availability-cohort-cohort-LTV-decision differs from per-inventory-availability-cohort-LTV. Inventory-availability-cohort-cohort-LTV = sum(customer-LTV × cohort-weight) per-inventory-availability-cohort — the canonical 50-cell matrix (per-customer-LTV-tier × per-inventory-availability-cohort). Operators who only track per-inventory-availability-cohort-LTV miss the cohort-mix-shift that drives +12-30% LTV-uplift. **Fix:** track per-inventory-availability-cohort-cohort-LTV explicitly; route to per-cohort-audience-engine via Move #115.

13. **Inventory-availability-cohort-discount-uplift-ceiling-failure.** Discount-uplift-ceiling on inventory-availability-cohort = +5-15% (vs +15-30% on creator-economy-cohort per Move #3.11). Operators who apply creator-economy-cohort-discount-uplift-ceiling-engine to inventory-availability-cohort over-discount and erode margin. **Fix:** apply canonical per-inventory-availability-cohort-discount-uplift-ceiling (5-15% on in-stock, 8-15% on limited-stock, etc.).

14. **Inventory-availability-cohort-mobile-cohort-confusion with creator-economy-cohort-mobile-cohort-confusion.** Move #3.13 mobile-cohort-confusion is render-area-driven (inventory-cohort-mobile-render-area); Move #3.11 mobile-cohort-confusion is creator-bio-link-driven (creator-mobile-bio-link-friction). Each requires a separate counter. **Fix:** keep the canonical 2 mobile-cohort-confusion counters separate; Move #3.13 = inventory-cohort-mobile-render-area-confusion-counter; Move #3.11 = creator-bio-link-mobile-confusion-counter.

15. **Ignoring inventory-availability-cohort-cross-border-shipping-display.** Cross-border customers see inventory-state at different render-priority than domestic customers (per Move #25 international-expansion). If not handled, the inventory-cohort-cross-border-shipping-display-counter fires and the 3pl-fulfilled cohort completion-rate drops 8-15% on cross-border. **Fix:** tag every inventory-cohort-render-event with cross-border-vs-domestic; apply canonical cross-border-inventory-cohort-render-priority (in-stock first, then 3pl-fulfilled with ETA).

16. **Inventory-availability-cohort-payment-fallback-failure.** When a primary payment-method fails, the inventory-availability-cohort (especially 3pl-fulfilled + dropship) may be de-prioritized in the fallback-render, leading to a payment-step-leak. **Fix:** apply canonical payment-fallback-inventory-cohort-priority (inventory-state is ALWAYS rendered, even in payment-fallback path).

17. **Inventory-availability-cohort-stockout-real-time-staleness.** Inventory-cohort-state from Shopify-Inventory-API may be stale by 30-300 seconds due to webhook-delivery-lag. A customer who adds an out-of-stock SKU to cart may complete checkout only to receive an apology email 60 seconds later — a per-checkout-step-leak. **Fix:** wire real-time inventory-cohort-state validation at checkout-step-entry via Shopify-Inventory-API + webhook-delivery-monitoring + 30-second-staleness-threshold.

18. **Global-rollback-engine-kills-high-performing-inventory-availability-cohorts.** A global rollback that triggers when ANY inventory-availability-cohort completion-rate drops below benchmark-floor will kill high-performing inventory-availability-cohorts (e.g. in-stock + limited-stock + 3pl-fulfilled).
